JANUARY 16, 2019
PORT ST. JOE, FLORIDA
SPECIAL MEETING
The Gulf County Board of County Commissioners met this date in special session with
the following members present: Chairman Sandy Quinn, Jr., Vice Chairman Jimmy
Rogers, and Commissioners David C. Rich, Ward McDaniel, and Phil McCroan.
Others also present were: County Attorney Jeremy Novak, Clerk Rebecca L. Norris,
Clerk Director of Finance & Management Rhonda Woodward, Deputy Clerk Leanna
Roberts, County Administrator Michael L. Hammond, County Assistant Administrator
Warren Yeager, Jr., Deputy Administrator Kari Summers, Staff Assistant–E.D.C. Johanna
White, G.I.S. Coordinator Jessica Susich, Information Technology Dennis Barfield, Sr.,
Office of Management & Budget Director Sherry Herring, Assistant Mosquito Control
Director Austin Horton, Public Works & Mosquito Control Director Mark Cothran, Sheriff

S.H.I.P. Administrator Joe Paul.
Chairman Quinn called the meeting to order at 5:00 p.m., E.T.
Chairman Quinn led in the Pledge of Allegiance to the Flag.
BEACON HILL PARK / F.E.M.A. PUBLIC ASSISTANCE
County Assistant Administrator Yeager discussed working with F.E.M.A. regarding
temporary housing at Veterans’ Memorial Beacon Hill Park; presenting the first layout that
F.E.M.A. proposed <copy was not provided to the Clerk for the record>. He then
presented a second proposed layout <copy was not provided to the Clerk for the record>;
reporting that F.E.M.A. is requesting 2.94 acres and will install the infrastructure, water,
power and sewer. County Assistant Administrator Yeager reported that F.E.M.A. is
requesting a twelve (12) to fifteen (15) month lease with a possible six (6) month
extension; stating this temporary housing will be for displaced residents. He requested a
motion of the Board giving authorization to move forward and for the Chairman to sign
the contract (agreement). After discussion by County Attorney Novak, County Assistant
Administrator Yeager reported the agreement has to be submitted to F.E.M.A. by January
18, 2019. Commissioner McDaniel motioned to move forward. Commissioner Rich
seconded the motion. Chairman Quinn called for public comment. Cheryl Ploegstra, of
Beacon Hill appeared before the Board to discuss her concerns regarding high density,
additional traffic, noise, lights and trash. She requested that the contract with F.E.M.A.
state that after the contract expires, then F.E.M.A. will remove the improvements.
Charmon Henderson, of Beacon Hill appeared before the Board to discuss her concerns
regarding the proposed temporary housing. Amy Corzine, of Beacon Hill appeared before
the Board to discuss her concerns regarding the increase of bears due to the increase in
trash; requesting a commitment from F.E.M.A. and the County that the Park will revert

back to the Veterans’ Park. Jennifer Corbin, of Beacon Hill appeared before the Board to
discuss her concerns for policing the temporary housing area and the landscape. She
requested that at the end of the F.E.M.A. tour that the property be returned to a park, not
an RV (recreational vehicle) Park. Chairman Quinn called for public comment. Karen
Johnson, of St. Joe Beach appeared before the Board to inquire whether a temporary
caution flashing light will be installed due to the increase in traffic. She also requested at
the end of the lease, that the area revert back. Randall Copeland, of Beacon Hill appeared
before the Board to request that the County put it in writing that when the leasing terms
expire, it will not be a RV (recreational vehicle) Park and that it will return to its natural
intent. Jim McKenzie, of Beacon Hill appeared before the Board to request the Board’s
commitment to return this property to the people when the emergency need is over. He
discussed is concerns regarding safety and traffic; requesting fencing and pedestrian
walkways. Gretchen Mayes, of Beacon Hill appeared before the Board to ask how far into
Bay County will families be residing in the temporary housing. County Assistant
Administrator Yeager stated that the majority of the people will be Gulf County residents,
but there may be a few displaced families from Mexico Beach. Ms. Mayes inquired as to
how many parking spaces will be available for each pad. County Assistant Administrator
Yeager stated that most of the lots are 60 feet deep and minimum width is 35 feet, so
there will be room for two (2) vehicles per pad. Upon inquiry by Ms. Mayes, County
Assistant Administrator Yeager reported that there will be a management team, which will
include security. Ms. Mayes stated that she wants to see Veterans’ (Memorial-Beacon
Hill) Park go back to what it was originally intended for and it not turn into Beacon Hill RV
(recreational vehicle) Park. Margaret Clayton, of Beacon Hill appeared before the Board
to inquire about the utilities; suggesting the County view an area on County Road 386
that would be more beneficial. Brian Cahill, of Beacon Hill appeared before the Board to
discuss the Honor Walk Project. Chairman Quinn called for public comment. There being
no further public comment and discussion by members of the Board, the motion then
passed unanimously.
PRIVATE PROPERTY DISASTER RECOVERY RESOLUTION
County Attorney Novak presented a proposed resolution <copy provided to the Clerk for
the record> and then read the proposed resolution by title for the Board to consider. He
reported that the letter, signed by the Chairman, supporting the legal authority in which to
make this request, the supporting documents <copy provided to the Clerk for the record>,
and the Resolution will be submitted to F.E.M.A. Commissioner McCroan so moved.
Commissioner Rogers seconded the motion. Chairman Quinn called for public comment.
Lorinda Gingell, of Oak Grove appeared before the Board to inquire about obtaining right-
of-entry from out-of-town property owners. County Attorney Novak reported that the
Board set the January 31, 2019 deadline for property owners to get their debris to the
right-of-way; stating that the issue tonight is specifically regarding private roads and
leaning trees. After discussion by County Attorney Novak, County Administrator
Hammond reported that the proposed resolution today is dealing with leaning trees with
the right-of-entry program for yard debris to be addressed at Tuesday’s regular Board
meeting. Following discussion, Pat Hardman, Coastal Community Association President
appeared before the Board to discuss the right-of-entry program. Chairman Quinn called
for public comment. Connie French, of Port St. Joe appeared before the Board to discuss

the yard debris in their backyard; requesting assistance with debris removal. County
Administrator Hammond stated he would review; discussing Samaritans Purse
volunteering within the County. Mrs. French reported that she has the right-of-entry form
signed and ready to submit. Chairman Quinn called for additional public comment. There
being no further public comment, the motion passed unanimously, as follows:
RESOLUTION NO. 2019-03
A RESOLUTION OF THE BOARD OF COUNTY COMMISSIONERS OF
GULF COUNTY, FLORIDA, AUTHORIZING THE COUNTY CHIEF
ADMINISTRATOR TO PROVIDE FOR THE REMOVAL OF DEBRIS
RESULTING FROM HURRICANE MICHAEL FROM PRIVATE
PROPERTY WHERE SUCH DEBRIS REPRESENTS AN IMMEDIATE
THREAT TO LIFE, PUBLIC HEALTH, OR SAFETY, OR TO THE
ECONOMIC RECOVERY OF THE COMMUNITY AT LARGE, UPON
AUTHORIZATION FROM THE PROPERTY OWNER; AUTHORIZING
THE COUNTY CHIEF ADMINISTRATOR TO INITIATE A REQUEST FOR
REIMBURSEMENT FROM F.E.M.A. FOR PRIVATE PROPERTY DEBRIS
REMOVAL; AND PROVIDING AN EFFECTIVE DATE.
*Complete Resolution on file with Clerk*
