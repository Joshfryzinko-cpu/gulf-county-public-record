– Adoption of Statewide Mutual Aid Agreement (Disaster Response
Services), as follows:
RESOLUTION NO. 2019-15
WHEREAS, the State of Florida Emergency Management Act, Chapter 252,
authorizes the State and its political subdivisions to provide emergency aid and
assistance in the event of a disaster of emergency; and
WHEREAS, the statutes also authorize the State to coordinate the provision of any
equipment, services, or facilities owned or organized by the State of it political
subdivisions for use in the affected area upon the request of the duly constituted authority
of the area; and
WHEREAS, the Resolution authorizes the request, provision, and receipt of
interjurisdictional mutual assistance in accordance with the Emergency Management Act,
Chapter 252, among political subdivisions within the State; and
NOW, THEREFORE, be it resolved by Gulf County Board of County Commission
that in order to maximize the prompt, full and effective use of resources of all participating
governments in the event of an emergency or disaster we hereby adopt the Statewide
Mutual Aid Agreement which is attached hereto and incorporated by reference.
(End)
2012 COUNTY HELD TAX CERTIFICATES
Clerk Norris discussed the County Held Tax Certificates valued under $5,000.00 for 2012
that are set to expire at the end of May. Requesting Board direction, she offered the
following three (3) options; Option 1: Do not apply for tax deeds; allowing the certificates
to expire; Option 2: Apply for tax deeds on all parcels; directing County Attorney to make
applications for tax deed to the Tax Collector prior to May 25, 2019; or Option 3: Apply
for a tax deed on Certificate #673, Parcel ID #02949-001R; directing County Attorney to
make application for tax deed to the Tax Collector prior to May 25, 2019, and allow all
other certificates to expire. County Administrator Hammond recommended Option 3. After
discussion, Commissioner McDaniel motioned to approve Option 3. Commissioner
Rogers seconded the motion. Chairman Quinn called for public comment. There being
no public comment, the motion passed unanimously.

AWARD BID #1819-24 – COMMERCIAL FIRE TRUCK TOP MOUNT PUMPER /
HIGHLAND VIEW FIRE DEPARTMENT
County Administrator Hammond reported the Highland View Fire Department is
recommending to award Bid #1819-24 for a Commercial Fire Truck Top Mount Pumper
to Hall-Mark RTC / REV Technical Center, sole-bidder, in the amount of $387,606.55.
Stephanie Strayer, Highland View Fire Department Secretary appeared before the Board
to discuss the fire truck and the award. Upon inquiry by Commissioner McCroan, County
Administrator Hammond recommended they go with Highland View Fire Department’s
recommendation; reporting that it will take a while to get this truck built; noting the County
did not bid financing. He stated that 2/3 of the money is in their budget (Highland View
Fire Department) and a new budget beginning October 1, 2019. He stated they may look
at financing, if needed. He recommended awarding in the amount of $387,606.55 and to
get the truck ordered so they can start building it. Chris Thwaits, of REV Technical Center
appeared before the Board to discuss the financial options. County Administrator
Hammond stated that the County will pay the bid price upon delivery. Following
discussion, Commissioner McCroan so moved. Commissioner Rogers seconded the
motion. Chairman Quinn called for public comment. There being no public comment, the
motion passed unanimously.
