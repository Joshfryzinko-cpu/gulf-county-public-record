Chairman Quinn called the meeting to order at 5:02 p.m., E.T.
2019-20 BUDGET OVERVIEW
Office of Management & Budget Director Herring appeared and stated the following
regarding the 2019-20 budget: 1) the budget book has been provided to each member
of the Board; 2) the tentative aggregate millage rate is 7.4946, which is 4.98% below the
current year aggregate roll-back rate of 7.8873; 3) the tentative aggregated millage rate
includes the following millage rates: Countywide at 7.1000 mills and each Fire District
are at 0.5000 mills; and 3) the tentative M.S.T.U. voted debt service millage rates are: a)
Gulfside Beachfront at 1.1547; b) Gulfside Interior at 0.8664, and c) Bayside at 0.9016.
She reported that the stated millages are the maximum millage rates, but the Board may
adopt a less rate at tonight’s hearing by a majority vote. OMB Director Herring then
reported the following: 1) the Port St. Joe D.R.A. obligation is $175,000.00 based upon
the 2019 Interlocal Agreement with the first payment due in December, 2019, which takes
the place of the final TIFF payment for the 1990 base area; 2) there is no additional funds
due for the 2010 expansion area due to a decrease in values; 3) General Fund Budget
includes Cash Carry Forward of $9.5 million as a source of funding for FY2019-20 Budget,
and $4.3 million of the Cash Carry Forward is restricted and not available for general
operation use; and 4) the tentative budget has been provided to each Commissioner,
posted on the County’s website two (2) days prior to this hearing, as required, and
advertised in the newspaper. She requested Board approval of the following amendments
for the Final Budget: 1) a decrease of $1,935.00 in Fund 103; 2) increase of $21,800.00
in Fund 107; 3) decrease of $10,000.00 in Fund 126; 4) increase of $94,233 in Fund 128;

5) increase of $7,814.00 in Fund 205; 6) increase of $38,500.00 in Fund 301; and 7) line
item adjustment in Fund 001, General Fund, in order to true-up the Tax Collector’s funding
transfers for the revised T.D.C. revenue estimates. Commissioner McDaniel motioned to
move forward with these changes. Commissioner Rogers seconded the motion.
Chairman Quinn called for public comment. There being no public comment, the motion
passed unanimously. County Administrator Hammond discussed working on the budget;
reporting that the County was able to give back the Constitutional Officer’s their money
after the Triumph funding, but have continued to freeze the Board budget and outside
agencies. After discussion by County Administrator Hammond, Chairman Quinn called
for public comment. There being no public comment, Chairman Quinn closed to the
public. OMB Director Herring reported that the Countywide Millage is 7.1000, with the
Aggregate Millage Rate at 7.4946, which is less than Aggregate Roll-back Rate of 7.8873
by 4.98%.
