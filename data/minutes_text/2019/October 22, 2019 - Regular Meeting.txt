the Flag.

IOLA ROAD
County Administrator Hammond discussed concerns regarding Iola Road; reporting that
every time is rains the ‘mudders’ tear-up the road, costing the County $8 to $10K to fix it.
He stated the Board needs to decide if the County is going to no longer fix the road, close
the road, or start putting people in jail for tearing up the County’s road; reporting that these
are not children, but adults that are doing this. He reported the County does not have the
resources to fix (Iola) road every time; requesting the Board to make a decision regarding
this matter. Public Works Director Cothran discussed the damage to Iola Road and
Muscogee Road during that last two (2) rainfalls. Commissioner Rich stated that it is going
to take a culture change and education of our participates; requesting help from Sheriff
Harrison. Sheriff Harrison appeared before the Board to discuss the problem at Iola Road;
reporting that this is not an easy fix, but if the Board wants to prosecute, then his office
will do the best they can moving forward. Commissioner Rich inquired of the timeline to
pave Iola Road. County Administrator Hammond stated that it is in the process, waiting
on approval. Discussion followed regarding Iola Road being a short-cut for commuters
and the damage made by the ‘mudders’. Commissioner Rich discussed an alternative
place for the ‘mudders’ to go, Tri-State Off-Road Park in Calhoun County; stating they do
not need to do it on public roads. Commissioner McCroan inquired whether
Commissioner Rich wanted to make a motion to temporarily close the road (Iola Road).
Commissioner Rich stated that the Board does not need to close it temporarily, but needs
to enforce the law and make them not tear up public property. County Administrator
Hammond stated that he will work with the Sheriff and the next time it rains, they will
monitor it more closely and will start putting people in jail for tearing up the road; reporting
that signs and remote cameras will be installed. After further discussion regarding the
repairs made to Iola Road, Chairman Quinn suggested installing signs that they will be
prosecuted; agreeing to place remote cameras in the area.
