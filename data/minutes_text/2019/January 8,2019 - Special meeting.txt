ROADSIDE DEBRIS
County Administrator Hammond stated that staff’s recommendation is for the Board to
set January 31, 2019 as the last day for the public to place anything on the public or
private right-of-way for F.E.M.A. to pick up. He stated that the entire project (all the
roadside haul-off) has to be completed by March 6, 2019, unless it is extended. Following
discussion, County Assistant Administrator Yeager discussed meeting with Metric
Engineering regarding debris pick up and problem areas; stating that there is an end date
and F.E.M.A. will not reimburse after a certain length of time. County Assistant
Administrator Yeager discussed leaning trees within the County; reporting that Metric
Engineering must follow specific rules for F.E.M.A. guidelines and reimbursements. He
stated that Metric Engineering can only trim the top of these trees that generate on private
property which are leaning over the road; therefore, Metric is suggesting the County start
the right-of-entry process which will provide the opportunity for contractors and F.E.M.A.
to go on private property for owners without insurance and having no way to get their
debris to the right-of-way. Commissioner McDaniel motioned to set the cut-off date to
January 31, 2019. Commissioner Rogers seconded the motion. Chairman Quinn called
for public. Pat Hardman, of Coastal Community Association appeared before the Board
to discuss her concerns regarding the deadline date and the lack of manpower to get the

debris to the right-of-way. After discussion, Commissioner McCroan inquired if a request
to extend the deadline can be added to the motion. After discussion by County Attorney
Novak and County Administrator Hammond, Chairman Quinn called for public comment.
Lissa Dulany, of Port St. Joe appeared before the Board to discuss the deadline in her
area. Kim Milton, of Simmons Bayou appeared before the Board to request that the
deadline be noticed on utility bills and property tax bills; asking if the public could obtain
assistant in getting debris to the right-of-way by the deadline. Commissioner McCroan
motioned to extend time to speak. Commissioner Rich seconded the motion, and it
passed unanimously. Ms. Milton inquired about code enforcement or penalties imposed
on property owners that leave there homes after the deadline. County Administrator
Hammond stated that the state and county ditches will be cleaned of debris. Upon inquiry
by Chairman Quinn, County Administrator Hammond reported that the property tax bills
have already been mailed out and the County does not mail out utility bills; stating that
the notice has been in The Star as a potential date. He reported that the County can place
it on the website, T.D.C. site, and email chain, and notify the Chamber of Commerce. Joe
Mizereck, of St. Joe Beach appeared before the Board to inquire as to what will be the
penalty if residents don’t put their debris on the roadside or if they put it out after the
deadline. County Administrator Hammond stated that technically it is a felony if anyone
places debris on the right-of-way after the deadline. After discussion, Chairman Quinn
called for public comment. There being no further public comment, the motion passed
unanimously.
BEACON HILL PARK / F.E.M.A.
County Assistant Administrator Yeager discussed the need for housing for misplaced
residents (due to Hurricane Michael); stating that F.E.M.A. is trying to do what they can
regarding temporary housing. He reported that F.E.M.A. has approached the County
about placing RV (recreational vehicle) pads at Veterans’ Memorial Beacon Hill Park and
negotiating with the County to provide funding for the use of this area. T.D. Dargan,
F.E.M.A. representative of Region 10 (ten) appeared before the Board to discuss locating
pads for temporary housing; reporting that due to the flood plains, it has made been
difficult to find temporary housing locations for residents. He reported that F.E.M.A. has
a demand for 42 (forty-two) pads; providing a proposed design for the Veterans’ Memorial
Beacon Hill Park location <copy provided to the Clerk for the record>. Mr. Dargan reported
that the proposed plan has 51 pads to address shortfalls for Mexico Beach residents. He
discussed the bi-monthly rate for use of the property with F.E.M.A. doing the construction
and the utilities; reporting that the end of the housing mission is April 2020. Following
discussion, and upon inquiry by Commissioner McDaniel, Mr. Dargan stated that F.E.M.A.
recommends the County carry insurance for the location and bill F.E.M.A. as part of the
lease agreement for that insurance. After discussion by County Assistant Administrator
Yeager, Commissioner McDaniel motioned to go with the recommendation of (Veterans’
Memorial) Beacon Hill Park. After discussion by Chairman Quinn, Commissioner
McCroan seconded the motion. After discussion by members of the Board, County
Administrator Hammond requested for clarification if it is the Board intent in the motion to
proceed, allowing County Assistant Administrator Yeager to negotiate the contract, have
the County Attorney draft the agreement and present it to the Board for consideration at

their next Board meeting. Chairman Quinn was in agreement, and then called for public
comment. There being no public comment, the motion passed unanimously.
