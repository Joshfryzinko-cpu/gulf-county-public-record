AUGUST 30, 2019
EMERGENCY MEETING
PORT ST. JOE, FLORIDA
The Gulf County Board of County Commissioners met this date in emergency session
with the following members present: Chairman Sandy Quinn, Jr., Vice Chairman Jimmy
Rogers, and Commissioners David C. Rich, Ward McDaniel, and Phil McCroan.
Others present were: Clerk Director of Finance & Management Rhonda Woodward,
Deputy Clerk Leanna Roberts, County Administrator Michael L. Hammond, County
Assistant Administrator Warren Yeager, Jr., Emergency Management Director Marshall
Nelson, Emergency Management Staff Assistant Rachel Jackson, Dewberry/County
Engineer Clay Smallwood, III, Grant Administrator Kaci Rhodes, Office of Management
& Budget Director Sherry Herring, Assistant Mosquito Control Director Austin Horton,
Public Works & Mosquito Control Director Mark Cothran, Assistant Public Works Director
Raymond Hart, Sheriff Mike Harrison, and T.D.C. Executive Director Kelli Godwin.
Chairman Quinn called the meeting at 9:30 a.m., E.T.
Chairman Quinn opened the meeting in prayer and led the Pledge of Allegiance to the
Flag.
HURRICANE DORIAN
County Administrator Hammond presented the Board with a proposed seven (7) day
emergency declaration for consideration; calling for Emergency Management Director
Nelson to provide an update. Emergency Management Director Nelson appeared before
the Board to provide printed material regarding Hurricane Dorian <copy provided to the
Clerk for the record>; reporting that it is still a very dangerous hurricane. He discussed
the tract and the hurricane force winds; reporting that staff will continue to monitor. County
Administrator Hammond discussed the rainfall totals and the potential of river flooding.
He then recommended that the Board adopt the seven (7) day Local State of Emergency
(due to Hurricane Dorian). Chairman Quinn called for a motion. Commissioner McCroan
so moved. Commissioner Rogers seconded the motion. Chairman Quinn called for public
comment. There being no public comment, the motion passed unanimously. County
Administrator Hammond reported that Public Works has made some provisions, but have
not put sand or bags out yet. Public Works Director Cothran reported that Public Works
has a small amount of sandbags leftover from the last storm, but a load of sand and
shovels can be placed outside the fence for residents. County Administrator Hammond
reported that the sand will not be placed until the County receives a better timeline; stating
if the need arises, then Public Works will provide what is on hand.

There being no further business, and upon motion by Commissioner McCroan, second
by Commissioner Rogers seconded the motion, and unanimous vote, the meeting did
then adjourn at 9:35 a.m., E.T.
SANDY QUINN, JR.
