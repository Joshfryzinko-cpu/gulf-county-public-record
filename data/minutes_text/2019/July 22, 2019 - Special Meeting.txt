Chairman Quinn called the meeting to order at 9:01 a.m., E.T.
Chairman Quinn led in the Pledge of Allegiance to the Flag.
TENTATIVE 2019-20 BUDGET
County Administrator Hammond discussed this being the first for him with the County
going into budget session with a fixed millage rate; stating this is also the first year with
the new budget officer under County Administration. He reported that this proposed
budget includes a 3% (three percent) pay raise and insurance adjustments. County
Administrator Hammond requested a vote of the Board to accept the Countywide
Millage (Rate) of 7.100. Upon inquiry by Chairman Quinn, County Administrator
Hammond reported that the County has approximately (12) twelve vacancies with nine
being in Public Works; reporting there are no newly created positions in this proposal.
Commissioner McDaniel motioned to maintain the millage rate at 7.100 for Fiscal Year
2019-20. Commissioner McCroan seconded the motion. Chairman Quinn called for
public comment. There being no public comment, the motion passed unanimously.
Upon request by County Administrator Hammond, Commissioner McCroan motioned to
adopt ½ mill for each of the following Fire Control Districts: 1. St. Joseph; 2. Tupelo; 3.
Overstreet; and 4. Howard Creek. Commissioner Rogers seconded the motion.
Chairman Quinn called for public comment. There being no public comment, the motion

passed unanimously. County Administrator Hammond reported that the combined total
net reduction in ad valorem taxes from these funds is $825,961.00.
County Administrator Hammond discussed the (MSTU) debt service at the Cape (San
Blas); recommending millage rates of 1.1547 for Gulfside, 0.8664 for Gulfside Interior,
and 0.9016 for Bayside. County Commissioner McDaniel motioned to approve.
Commissioner Rich seconded the motion. After discussion, Chairman Quinn called for
public comment. There being no public comment, the motion passed unanimously.
County Administrator Hammond then discussed the proposed tentative budget by each
line item for the Board’s consideration. Office of Management & Budget Director Herring
also discussed various line items and projects within the proposed tentative budget.
After the overview, County Administrator Hammond asked the Board for comments,
suggestions, or changes (to the proposed tentative budget). Commissioner McDaniel
discussed the progress at the Dead Lakes Park; stating that it should triple in revenue
once operations commence.
Upon inquiry by Commissioner McCroan about filling the fire coordinator position,
County Administrator Hammond stated this position will need to be filled at some point,
but currently the County does not have the funds for a full-time position.
Commissioner McDaniel discussed his concerns regarding the agreement with Bay
County for E.M.S. Services; requesting that Gulf County let Bay County get back on
their feet a little bit and then approach them in the future to request a little bump
(increase) for this service. County Administrator Hammond discussed the ambulance
service within Bay County.
Chairman Quinn inquired about the time remaining for workers from CareerSource (Gulf
Coast). County Administrator Hammond stated until the money runs out (F.E.M.A. Grant
Funds due to Hurricane Michael); reporting that the County currently has 35 (thirty-five)
workers employed under this grant funding.
County Administrator Hammond discussed optional dates for the required public budget
meetings (hearings). Commissioner McDaniel motioned to hold the First Budget
Hearing on September 5, 2019 after 5:00 p.m., E.T. Commissioner Rich seconded the
motion. Chairman Quinn called for public comment. There being no public comment, the
motion passed unanimously.
After discussion by County Administrator Hammond and Office of Management &
Budget Director Herring regarding proposed hearing dates, Commissioner McCroan
motioned to hold the Final Budget Hearing on September 24, 2019 at 5:01 p.m., E.T.
Commissioner Rogers seconded the motion. Chairman Quinn called for public
comment. There being no public comment, the motion passed unanimously.

Office of Management & Budget Director Herring reported that the County is waiting on
funding from the State that will affect the General (101) and Hospital Funds (124);
stating that some cash carry forward estimates that will need to be recalculated. She
discussed adding a fund for the Hurricane Housing Recovery Program. Office of
Management & Budget Director Herring requested approval to make these changes and
other necessary changes before finalizing the tentative budget book. Commissioner
Rogers so moved. Commissioner Rich seconded the motion. Chairman Quinn called for
public comment. There being no public comment, the motion passed unanimously.
County Administrator Hammond requested permission to change the numbers
regarding the pay raise after completion of the employee evaluation process; reporting
the State has not published the Elected Officials rates (of pay) and once established
these numbers will be trued-up. Commissioner Rich so moved. Commissioner Rogers
seconded the motion. Chairman Quinn called for public comment. There being no public
comment, the motion passed unanimously.
ADMINISTRATIVE REPORT & UPDATE
County Administrator Hammond reported that the developer for the Highland View
project (Annex to the City * Old Highland View School Site) withdrew his request. He
suggested the Board schedule a workshop with the City (of Port St. Joe) to discuss
future annexations and the fact that the County is opposed to high density
developments.
Clerk Norris reported that Tax Collector Jenkins sends her regrets that she could not
attend today’s meeting due to training with DMV (Department of Motor Vehicle).
County Administrator Hammond reported that the County is adding a couple of
handicap parking spaces to the front of the (Gulf County) Courthouse; stating that
complaints were received regarding the current number of spaces being inadequate.
There being further business, and upon motion by Commissioner McDaniel, second by
Commissioner Rogers, and unanimous vote, the meeting did then adjourn at 10:10
a.m., E.T.
