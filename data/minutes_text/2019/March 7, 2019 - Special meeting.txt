MARCH 7, 2019
SPECIAL MEETING
PORT ST. JOE, FLORIDA
The Gulf County Board of County Commissioners met in this date in special session with
the following members present: Chairman Sandy Quinn, Jr., Vice Chairman Jimmy
Rogers, and Commissioners David C. Rich, Ward McDaniel, and Phil McCroan.
Others present were: County Attorney Jeremy Novak, Clerk Director of Finance &
Management Rhonda Woodward, Deputy Clerk Leanna Roberts, County Administrator
Michael L. Hammond, County Assistant Administrator Warren Yeager, Jr., Deputy
Administrator Kari Summers, Building Official Brad Bailey, Staff Assistant–E.D.C.
Johanna White, Emergency Management Director Marshall Nelson, Emergency
Management Staff Assistant Rachel Jackson, Dewberry/County Engineer Clay
Smallwood, III, G.I.S. Coordinator Jessica Susich, Office of Management & Budget
Director Sherry Herring, Assistant Mosquito Control Director Austin Horton, Parks &
Recreational Director Billy Traylor, County Planner Lee Collinsworth, Public Works &
Mosquito Control Director Mark Cothran, Assistant Public Works Director Raymond Hart,
Sheriff’s Department Captain Chris Buchanan, T.D.C. Executive Director Kelli Godwin,
Veterans' Service Officer & S.H.I.P. Administrator Joe Paul, and VSO/S.H.I.P./Central
Services Director Lynn Lanier.
Sheriff’s Department Captain Buchanan called the meeting to order at 10:00 a.m., E.T.
Chairman Quinn opened the meeting with prayer, and led in the Pledge of Allegiance to
the Flag.
TRIUMPH APPLICATION
Assistant Administrator Yeager discussed the application to be submitted to Triumph for
the loss of ad valorem taxes on behalf of the County, City, and School Board. Upon
request by Assistant Administrator Yeager, Commissioner McDaniel motioned to approve
the submission of this application and allow the Chairman to sign the application.
Commissioner Rogers seconded the motion. Chairman called for public comment. There
being no public comment, the motion passed unanimously.
INTERLOCAL AGREEMENT – CITY OF PORT ST. JOE
County Administrator Hammond provided a copy of the proposed interlocal agreement
with the City of Port St. Joe to the Board for review <copy was provided to the Clerk for
the record>. After discussion by County Administrator Hammond, he requested direction
from the Board on whether to continue negotiations or withdraw the offer. Following
discussion and upon inquiry by Chairman Quinn, County Administrator Hammond stated
he would like the Board to withdraw the offer or give the City (of Port St. Joe) a week to
vote on this proposed interlocal agreement. He requested Board approval to codify the

change (to the proposed interlocal agreement) on page 4 (four), paragraph 4 (four). After
discussion, Commissioner McDaniel motioned to give the City (of Port St. Joe) one week
to accept what was presented (today) or it will be pulled off the table. Chairman Quinn
requested that Commissioner McDaniel hold his motion until the Board votes on the
change to the proposed interlocal agreement. Commissioner McDaniel was in agreement.
Commissioner McDaniel motioned to accept the change to number 4 (four) (to the
proposed interlocal agreement). Commissioner McCroan seconded the motion.
Chairman Quinn called for public comment. There being no public comment, the motion
passed unanimously. Commissioner McDaniel motioned to give the City of Port St. Joe
one (1) week to accept the County’s offer under this proposed interlocal agreement.
Commissioner Rich seconded the motion. Chairman Quinn called for public comment.
City Mayor Bo Patterson appeared before the Board to discuss the proposed interlocal
agreement and the DRA (Downtown Redevelopment Agency). Chairman Quinn called for
additional public comment. There being no additional public comment, the motion passed
unanimously. County Administrator Hammond stated that if the City (of Port St. Joe) does
not accept the proposed interlocal agreement, then he would like an authorization vote
for him and the County Attorney to take all legal necessary steps to block the extension.
The Board agreed to put this option off until the City (of Port St. Joe) makes their decision.
BID #1819-19 – PUBLIC ASSISTANCE CONSULTANT SERVICES
Upon recommendation by County Administrator Hammond, Commissioner McCroan
motioned to amend the advertisement and extend for two more weeks to allow additional
submittals for Bid #1819-19, Public Assistance Consultant Services (long-term).
Commissioner Rogers seconded the motion. Chairman Quinn called for public comment.
There being no public comment, the motion passed unanimously.
BID ADVERTISEMENT – HIGHLAND VIEW FIRE DEPARTMENT FIRE TRUCK
Following discussion by County Administrator Hammond, Commissioner Rogers
motioned to advertise to receive sealed bids for the purchase of a new fire truck for the
Highland View Fire Department. Commissioner McDaniel seconded the motion. After
discussion, Chairman Quinn called for public comment. There being no public comment,
the motion passed unanimously.
DEPARTMENT OF AGRICULTURE CONTRACT AMENDMENT
Assistant Mosquito Control Director Horton requested Board approval and for the
Chairman to sign the amended agreement with Florida Department of Agriculture.
Commissioner McDaniel so moved. Commissioner McCroan seconded the motion.
Chairman Quinn called for public comment. There being no public comment, the motion
passed unanimously.
PURCHASE REQUEST FOR T.D.C. MOWERS
Assistant Mosquito Control Director Horton discussed replacing the mowers that were
damaged by Hurricane Michael; reporting the new cost for these mowers exceed the
allotted purchase threshold. He requested Board approval for an exception to the Bid
Policy to purchase these mowers locally. Commissioner McCroan so moved.

Commissioner Rich seconded the motion. Chairman Quinn called for public comment.
There being no public comment, the motion passed unanimously.
C30B BRIDGE
Public Works Director Cothran provided an update regarding C30B (Indian Lagoon)
Bridge; reporting that the proposal from Ferrovial Services to temporary repair the bridge
is $23,150.00. Commissioner McCroan made the motion (to accept Ferrovcial Services
proposal). Commissioner Rogers seconded the motion. After discussion regarding Money
Bayou Bridge and C30B Bridge, Chairman Quinn called for public comment. Pat
Hardman, President of the Coastal Community Association appeared before the Board
to discuss her concerns regarding the culvert on Marnie Island Drive. County
Administrator Hammond reported that this road is State (owned) and it is on their list.
Chairman Quinn called for additional public comment. There being no additional public
comment, the motion passed unanimously.
PURCHASE REQUEST FOR T.D.C. MOWERS
County Attorney Novak requested that the Board add to the motion to state that the
County will purchase the mowers on State contract price and there is only one vendor in
the County that sells that piece of equipment. Commissioner McCroan included it in his
motion. Upon request by Chairman Quinn calling for a motion to add that language to the
first motion. Commissioner McCroan so moved. Commissioner Rich seconded the
motion. Chairman Quinn called for public comment. There being no public comment, the
motion passed unanimously.
INTERLOCAL AGREEMENT – CITY OF PORT ST. JOE
City Mayor Bo Patterson appeared before the Board to report that the City of Port St. Joe
will be holding a meeting on Monday at 12:00p.m., E.T. to consider adoption of the
proposed interlocal agreement.
C.B.R.A. – 40 ACRES
Coastal Community Association President Pat Hardman appeared before the Board to
discuss the 40 acres removed from C.B.R.A. (Coastal Barrier Resource Act); stating that
all that is needed now is the President’s (Trump) signature. She requested an update on
the remaining acres. County Administrator Hammond provided an update.
DEBRIS REMOVAL – HURRICANE MICHAEL
Pat Hardman, Coastal Community Association President inquired as to the deadline for
placing storm debris from Hurricane Michael on the roadside. County Administrator
Hammond reported the deadline is March 15, 2019, and it will be monitored.
TYNDALL AIR FORCE BASE
Commissioner McDaniel discussed the meetings held in Tallahassee; reporting that
Senator Rubio knows Gulf County and has assured us that Tyndall Air Force Base is
sound.

There being no further business, and upon motion by Commissioner Rogers, second by
Commissioner Rich, and unanimous vote, the meeting did then adjourn at 10:38 a.m.,
