COMMUNICATION SERVICE
E-911 Coordinator Guthrie appeared before the Board to present Rob Lavielle, of First
Net AT&T. Mr. Lavielle appeared to discuss communications; requesting permission from
the Board to place a temporary site behind the E.O.C. Building, at no cost to the County,
while they draft a build plan to present to the County.
Upon request by Commissioner McDaniel, E-911 Coordinator Guthrie gave an update on
the radio tower system, stating the County is negotiating with a company for the use of a
radio tower located in Wewahitchka. Commissioner McDaniel discussed his concerns
regarding this matter.
Upon inquiry by Commissioner Rich, County Administrator Hammond stated that he
would not recommend allowing First Net to setup a trailer behind the E.O.C. Building.
County Administrator Hammond reported that he has signed a lease agreement with
Duke Energy (use of radio tower on South end of the County) on the North Tower; stating
that the only thing left is to obtain a building (to house the County’s equipment).
Upon inquiry by Commissioner McCroan, County Administrator Hammond stated the
County is trying to get out of the radio tower business, and he doesn’t think it is in the best
interest of the County to have it located on site (First Net’s trailer and tower). Upon inquiry
by Commissioner McCroan regarding any advantage for the County to have this onsite,
E-911 Coordinator stated the advantage of becoming a First Net facility, the County will
receive Federal assets and a satellite truck in the event of another disaster. Mr. Lavielle
reported that it is a 25 x 25 footprint, cart on wheels that will cover this complex; discussing
the coverage under the proposed five (5) year agreement. Commissioner Rich requested
that Mr. Lavielle present his sales pitch to the County Administrator. After discussion,
Commissioner McCroan discussed the advantage being better coverage for AT&T.
Chairman Quinn requested that at the time Mr. Lavielle presents his sales pitch to County
Administrator Hammond, that he email the Board material to review. County Administrator
Hammond asked whether the Board is directing him to negotiate with First Net. Chairman
Quinn stated that he was fine with him sitting down and talking to Mr. Lavielle. County
Administrator Hammond stated that he will meet with Mr. Lavielle and bring back a
recommendation before the Board at their next meeting. Upon inquiry by Commissioner
McDaniel regarding alternate locations, Mr. Lavielle discussed the landfill site; stating that

it would not benefit this complex due to the distance and trees. Discussion continued
regarding other location sites within the County.

VARIANCE REQUEST – JOAN LOVELACE
Planner Collinsworth presented a variance request from Joan Lovelace, Parcel ID
#03895-000R; reporting the P.D.R.B. recommended, by vote of 3-0, to approve this
variance. Chairman Quinn called for comment to anyone wishing to speak regarding this
variance <no one came forward>. He then asked if there were any objections to the
waiving the Quasi-Judicial Hearing. There being none, Commissioner McDaniel motioned
to waive of the Quasi-Judicial Hearing. Commissioner Rogers seconded the motion.
Chairman Quinn called for public comment. There being no public comment, the motion
passed unanimously. County Attorney Novak stated that if the Board has no question,
then he would recommend the Board review Section 34 (of the Public Hearing and Review
Form). Chairman Quinn called for public comment. There being none, the Chairman
closed comment to the public. Upon inquiry by Commissioner McCroan, Planner
Collinsworth discussed the variance request and possible setback issues regarding this
parcel. After discussion, Chairman Quinn inquired of Planner Collinsworth’s
recommendation. Planner Collinsworth stated he does not have an issue with it going out
(variance), but is concerned on the next one (second variance request on same parcel)
coming forward because the Board has not normally granted variances on two sides at
the same time on the same piece of property. County Attorney Novak reported that the
P.D.R.B. found that the granting of the variance request will not confer on the applicant
any special privilege that is denied by these regulations to other lands, structures or
buildings in the same district (fourth criteria). Commissioner Rogers motioned to accept
the P.D.R.B. recommendation on the fourth criteria. Commissioner Rich seconded the
motion. Chairman Quinn called for public comment. There being no public comment, the
motion passed unanimously. County Attorney Novak stated that the following findings for
consideration: 1. accepts the Planning Department’s statement of completeness for the
variance; 2. deems the records submitted as competent and substantial evidence to
support the decision; 3. accepts the Planning Department’s statement and representation
that proper notice of Public Hearing was provided in accordance within the provisions

under special exceptions of the Gulf County’s LDR’s for variances; 4. shall make a finding
that the requirements regarding hardship relief have been met by the applicant for a
variance; 5. shall further make a finding that the granting of the variance will be in harmony
with the general purpose and intent of the regulations, and not injurious to the
neighborhood; and 6. under no circumstances shall the Board grant a variance to allow a
use not permissible under the terms of these regulations in the district involved.
Commissioner Rogers so moved. Commissioner Rich seconded the motion. Chairman
Quinn called for public comment. There being no public comment, the motion passed
unanimously. Following discussion by County Attorney Novak, Commissioner Rich
motioned to move forward on this variance. Commissioner Rogers seconded the motion.
Chairman Quinn called for public comment. There being no public comment, the motion
passed unanimously.
