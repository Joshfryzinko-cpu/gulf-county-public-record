a special meeting early to mid-February to open these bids.

OPENING BID #1819-15 – S.H.I.P. DISASTER FUNDING ACCESSORY FUNCTIONS

best qualified bidder to continue with these projects. Commissioner Rogers so moved.

There being no public comment, the motion passed unanimously.
AWARD BID #1819-10 – EXCAVATION BEACH SAND / HONEYVILLE BORROW PIT

bidder for Bid #1819-10 (Excavation Beach Sand at Honeyville Borrow Pit); requesting
the Board to award this bid to Roberts and Roberts, Inc. and allow him to negotiate and
execute the contract. Commissioner McCroan so moved. Commissioner Rich seconded
the motion. Chairman Quinn called for public comment. There being no public comment,
the motion passed unanimously.
AWARD BID #1819-05 – SOLID WASTE SERVICES
County Administrator Hammond recommended to award Bid #1819-05 for the Solid
Waste Services to BCC Waste Solutions; requesting permission to negotiate a contract
with this low bidder. Commissioner McDaniel so moved. Commissioner McCroan
seconded the motion for discussion. After discussion, Chairman Quinn called for public
comment. Kathy Jones, of Cape San Blas appeared before the Board to inquire whether
this service included yard debris pickup. County Administrator Hammond confirmed that
it does include yard debris pickup. Chairman Quinn called for additional public comment.
There being no public comment, the motion passed unanimously. Upon request by
County Administrator Hammond, Commissioner Rogers motioned to allow the County
Attorney to submit notice (of termination) to the current carrier (Waste Pro of Florida, Inc.).
Commissioner McDaniel seconded the motion. Chairman Quinn called for public
comment. There being no public comment, the motion passed unanimously.
PURCHASE TRANSFER STATION – WASTE PRO

with Waste Pro regarding the purchase of the Transfer Station and to allow him to sign
the contract, contingent upon an agreement. Commissioner Rich so moved.
Commissioner Rogers seconded the motion. Chairman Quinn called for public comment.
There being no public comment, the motion passed unanimously.

LEASE OFFICE SPACE – SUPERVISOR OF ELECTIONS BUILDING
County Assistant Administrator Yeager reported that the County has received interest in
office space located at the Supervisor of Elections; stating that it was previously used as
the investigators office by the Sheriff’s Department. Upon request by County Assistant
Administrator Yeager, Commissioner McCroan motioned to allow County Assistant
Administrator Yeager to negotiate the lease terms for this building. Commissioner Rich
seconded the motion. Chairman Quinn inquired whether the lease would include the
building located in the back. County Assistant Administrator Yeager said that is
negotiable; discussing the small building in the back that has been used in the past for
storage. Chairman Quinn stated that the Supervisor of Elections has approval to use this
building for storage. Chairman Quinn called for public comment. Jill Bebee, of Port St.
Joe appeared before the Board to inquire about the current use of this building for the
Gulf County Master Gardeners and the Extension Office. Members of the Board reported
that the office space up for lease is behind the building used for the Master Gardeners.
Chairman Quinn called for additional public comment. There being no further public
comment, the motion passed unanimously.

RESOLUTION NO. 2019-05
A RESOLUTION OF THE BOARD OF COUNTY COMMISSIONERS OF
GULF COUNTY, FLORIDA, AUTHORIZING THE COUNTY CHIEF
