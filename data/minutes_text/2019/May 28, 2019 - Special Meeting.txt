Chairman Quinn called the meeting to order at 8:32 a.m., E.T.
DISASTER HOUSING PLAN
Central Services Director Lanier discussed the proposed Disaster Affordable Housing
Program (D.A.H.P.); presenting the proposed plan to the Board for consideration <copy
provided to the Clerk for the record>. County Administrator Hammond discussed the
different pots of money funding this project; reporting that the first set of funds would come
from the (Hurricane) Irma Pot. He stated that once this program is funded, staff will come
back before the Board to look at advertisements for potential lots; reporting the County
would not buy lots in advance, but it would be a simultaneous closing once the applicant
qualifies. County Administrator Hammond discussed a point system that benefits first
responders and essential personnel. Central Services Director Lanier discussed the
eligibility requirements and ranking criteria; presenting this to the Board for consideration
<copy provided to the Clerk for the record>. County Administrator Hammond requested
a motion from the Board to approve this plan with the eligibility requirements and the
proposed resolution by title. County Attorney Novak then read the proposed resolution by
title. Commissioner Rich so moved. Commissioner McCroan seconded the motion. After
discussion by Chairman Quinn, he then called for public comment. E.D.C. Director
McKnight appeared before the Board to discuss this program; stating this will benefit our
workforce. After discussion, Chairman Quinn called for additional public comment. There
being no further public comment, the motion then passed unanimously, as follows:
RESOLUTION NO. 2019-18

A RESOLUTION ESTABLISHING THE GULF COUNTY DISASTER
AFFORDABLE HOUSING PROGRAM (DAHP), AFFIRMING THE
ELIGIBILITY REQUIREMENTS AND RANKING CRITERIA IN ORDER TO
CREATE AFFORDABLE HOUSING USING FUNDS RECEIVED FROM
THE U.S. DEPARTMENT OF HOUSING AND URBAN DEVELOPMENT
(HUD).
*Complete Resolution of file with Clerk*
(End)
County Administrator Hammond stated that this program will be geared more toward the
workforce, not the very low or low income. He reported that S.H.I.P. funds were previously
increased to $70,000.00 that assist the elderly and the disabled who are in the very low
or low income scale. County Administrator Hammond stated that the next plan will be to
present to the Board, based on funding from the State primarily, an expansion of the
S.H.I.P. program that is currently $350,000.00 annually to several million per year to re-
do roofs and home rehabilitation.
ADMINISTRATIVE REPORT & UPDATE
County Administrator Hammond stated that he will give the administrative report and
update during the regular Board meeting to follow.
There being no further business, and upon motion by Commissioner McCroan, second
by Commissioner McDaniel, and unanimous vote, the meeting did then adjourn at 8:50
a.m., E.T.
