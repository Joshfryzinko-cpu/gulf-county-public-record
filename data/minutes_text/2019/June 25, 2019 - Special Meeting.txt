Chairman Quinn called the meeting to order at 8:32 a.m., E.T.
HOUSING PLAN
S.H.I.P. Director Paul appeared before the Board to report that five (5) contractors
submitted applications for the S.H.I.P. Program; requesting Board approval for all five (5)
contractors, as follows: 1. Casey Civil, LLC; 2. Jerry’s Framing Crew; 3. LMK
Construction, Inc.; 4. Gulf to Bay Construction; and 5. Raymond Driesbach. He stated
that all five (5) contractors met the requirements, including the required insurance
according to the S.H.I.P. Program. Commissioner McDaniel motioned to accept these
fine (5) contractors. Commissioner Rogers seconded the motion. Chairman Quinn called
for public comment. There being no public comment, the motion passed unanimously.
S.H.I.P. Director Paul reported that through the Hurricane Housing Recovery Program,
the State of Florida allocated $65 million to (this program); stating that Gulf County was
awarded $5.915 million. He also reported that Florida Housing put together a funding
agreement, which will be brought before the Board for consideration in August, 2019.
S.H.I.P. Director Paul discussed the proposed agreement; requesting that the
administration fee be reduced to $190,000.00, giving the County an extra $400,000.00 to
put into a fund to help with home repairs or whatever is needed. He then provided a status
report regarding S.H.I.P. Projects. S.H.I.P. Director Paul requested the Board submit a
letter to Florida Housing asking they waive the requirements in the agreement; requesting

$4 million for moderate income levels and create a strategy like the D.H.A.P. strategy
program for moderate income workforce housing, with the remaining $1.915 million to be
used to create homes for the very low, and low income levels. After discussion, County
Administrator Hammond recommended the Board request a good chunk of this money
(administration fee) to go toward that goal. After discussion by County Administrator
Hammond, Commissioner McCroan discussed his concerns regarding the site-built home
project. After further discussion regarding site-built homes and mobile homes,
Commissioner McDaniel motioned to submit a letter requesting $4 million. Commissioner
McCroan seconded the motion. County Administrator Hammond requested to add in the
motion that the County will cap the administrative cost to $190,000.00. Commissioner
McDaniel added this to his motion. Chairman Quinn called for public comment. Pat
Hardman, of the Long-Term Recovery Committee appeared before the Board to discuss
the houses that have not been repaired (due to Hurricane Michael) and the estimate for
repairs to these homes and rebuilt homes. Chairman Quinn called for additional public
comment. There being no further public comment, the motion passed unanimously.
County Administrator Hammond stated that the next workshop the Board holds, staff
needs to talk about managing the C.D.B.G. money coming and possibly contracting this
service out.
ADMINISTRATIVE REPORT & UPDATE
Upon inquiry by Chairman Quinn, County Administrator Hammond stated that he will
