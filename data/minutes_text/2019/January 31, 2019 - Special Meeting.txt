JANUARY 31, 2019
PORT ST. JOE, FLORIDA
SPECIAL MEETING
The Gulf County Board of County Commissioners met this date in special session with
the following members present: Chairman Sandy Quinn, Jr., Vice Chairman Jimmy
Rogers, and Commissioners David C. Rich, Ward McDaniel, and Phil McCroan.
Others present were: County Attorney Jeremy Novak, Clerk Rebecca L. Norris, Clerk
Director of Finance & Management Rhonda Woodward, Deputy Clerk Leanna Roberts,
County Administrator Michael L. Hammond, Deputy Administrator Kari Summers,
Building Official Brad Bailey, Gulf County E.D.C. Director Jim McKnight, Staff Assistant–
E.D.C. Johanna White, Emergency Management Director Marshall Nelson, E9-1-1
Coordinator Ben Guthrie, G.I.S. Coordinator Jessica Susich, Office of Management &
Budget Director Sherry Herring, Assistant Mosquito Control Director Austin Horton,
County Planner Lee Collinsworth, Public Works & Mosquito Control Director Mark
Cothran, Assistant Public Works Director Raymond Hart, Sheriff Mike Harrison, Veterans'
Service Officer & S.H.I.P. Administrator Joe Paul, and VSO/S.H.I.P./Central Services
Director Lynn Lanier.
Chairman Quinn called the meeting to order at 10:00 a.m., E.T.
Chairman Quinn opened the meeting with prayer, and led int eh Pledge of Allegiance to
the Flag.
PUBLIC ASSISTANCE CONSULTANT
County Attorney Novak discussed the contract for Public Assistance with Horne, LLP (Bid
#1819-02 amend * Disaster Recovery Consultant Services); reporting the contract will
expire soon. He stated that the County is going out to receive sealed bids for a long-term
public assistance consultant. Upon request by County Attorney Novak to approve a
change order, Commissioner McDaniel motioned to extend the contract (with Horne, LLP)
for thirty (30) days. Commissioner Rogers seconded the motion. Chairman Quinn called
for public comment. There being no public comment, the motion passed unanimously.
LANDFILL STATUS – DISPOSAL AND REPORT
Public Works Director Cothran discussed the need for a dozier; recommending the Board
purchase a 2019 Caterpillar D6KS (Dozier) tractor on State Contract, in the amount of
$184,564.00, to be paid out of Capital Project Fund. County Administrator Hammond
discussed placing a request in the next Agenda to adjust the Capital Projects Fund and
then make the purchase out of the Capital Project Fund. After discussion, County
Administrator Hammond requested a motion to: 1. purchase the tractor off State
Contract; 2. agree to adjust the Capital Projects Fund to put this purchase in the Capital
Fund and; 3. purchase the tractor from the Capital Projects Fund. Commissioner

McCroan so moved. Commissioner McCroan seconded the motion. Chairman Quinn
called for public comment. There being no public comment, the motion passed
unanimously.
STATUS OF COUNTY CLEANUP EFFORTS
County Administrator Hammond reported that the County has not received an extension
(for storm debris to be placed on the right-of-ways); requesting Board approval to allow
staff to set the timetable based on what Secretary Gainer says later today. Commissioner
Rogers motioned to give County Administrator Hammond the authority to set an extension
if and when the County receives the call (from Secretary Gainer). Commissioner McCroan
seconded the motion for discussion. After discussion, Chairman Quinn called for public
comment. There being no public comment, the motion passed unanimously. Alex
Yarbrough, of Horne LLP appeared before the Board to provide a status update to the
Board regarding the storm debris removal due to Hurricane Michael.
OTHER ADMINISTRATIVE UPDATE & REPORTS
County Attorney Novak discussed the need for a F.E.M.A. Integrated Public Alert Warning
System application and a MOA (Memorandum of Agreement) authorizing the Chairman,
Emergency Management Director Nelson, or E9-1-1 Coordinator Guthrie to sign the
application and the agreement. Commissioner McDaniel so moved. Commissioner
Rogers seconded the motion. Chairman Quinn called for public comment. There being
no public comment, the motion passed unanimously.
County Administrator Hammond requested permission from the Board to submit the
County’s Legislative request, as: 1. revenue shortfall request direct appropriation from
the Legislator and; 2. additional funds, in the amount of $15 million for beach
renourishment. Commissioner McCroan made the motion. Commissioner Rich seconded
the motion. Chairman Quinn called for public comment. There being no public comment,
the motion passed unanimously.
Commissioner McCroan discussed his concerns regarding Triumph. After discussion,
County Administrator Hammond addressed the need for a letter from the Board re-
requesting the funds for the ad valorem tax relief for the City of Port St. Joe, School Board,
and the County. Commissioner McDaniel motioned to submit a letter. Commissioner Rich
seconded the motion. Chairman Quinn called for public comment. There being no public
comment, the motion passed unanimously.
County Administrator Hammond discussed the Board giving Don Butler (pervious Chief
Administrator) the authority to negotiate with St. Joe (Company) on purchasing the
property around the Honeyville sand site (borrow pit); reporting that there is a hand-shake
agreement on the price. He reported that the County is ready to move forward on the
purchase of the southern portion, approximately 53 acres, and that he has been given the
authority and it will be concluded in the next couple of weeks. Commissioner McCroan so
moved. Commissioner Rogers seconded the motion. After discussion, Chairman Quinn
called for public comment. There being no public comment, the motion passed
unanimously.

County Administrator Hammond reported that the Board gave him permission to negotiate
with Roberts & Roberts, Inc. on the sand project (Bid #1819-10 * Excavate Beach Sand
at the Honeyville Borrow Pit) and has agreed to allow Roberts & Roberts, Inc. to take out
100,000 yards; a buy-sell agreement. He stated that the County Attorney is drafting the
agreement and he will execute this agreement within the next seven (7) days.
STONE MILL CREEK FIRE DEPARTMENT
Commissioner McDaniel discussed his concerns regarding the Stone Mill Creek Fire
Station (Bid #1718-14). Building Official Bailey appeared before the Board to discuss the
bid project and the contractor (Cathey Construction). After discussion, County Attorney
Novak advised the Board should they wish to stop and take a step back, he recommends
they terminate the award. Commissioner McDaniel motioned to null and void the contract
with Cathey Construction (Bid #1718-14 * Stone Mill Creek Fire Station). County Attorney
Novak stated the motion would be just the award. After discussion, Commissioner
McDaniel amended his motion to terminate the award (there is no executed contract).
Commissioner Rich seconded the motion. After discussion by members of the Board and
County staff, Chairman Quinn called for public comment. Joe Paul, of Stone Mill Creek
appeared before the Board to discuss his concerns regarding this project. Chairman
Quinn called for additional public comment. There being no further public comment, the
motion passed 4 to 1, with Commissioner McCroan voting no.
LAND CONVEYANCE – ROAD 12
Following discussion by Clerk Norris, Commissioner Ward motioned to accept the Quit-
Claim Deed from Deseret Ranches of North Florida, LLC for Road 12. Commissioner Rich
seconded the motion. Chairman Quinn called for public comment. There being no public
comment, the motion passed unanimously.
ROAD 12 EASEMENT AGREEMENT – DESERET RANCHES
After discussion by Clerk Norris, Commissioner McCroan motioned to accept an
Easement Agreement with Deseret Ranches of North Florida, LLC on Road 12.
Commissioner Rogers seconded the motion. Chairman Quinn called for public comment.
There being no public comment, the motion passed unanimously.
OTHER ADMINISTRATIVE UPDATE & REPORTS
City of Port St. Joe Mayor Patterson appeared before the Board to thank them for their
work in the rebuilding of the County after the storm (Hurricane Michael). Jason Shoaf, of
Port St. Joe appeared before the Board to discuss the Triumph money and the need to
rebuild.

There being no further business, and upon motion by Commissioner McCroan, second
by Commissioner Rogers, and unanimous vote, the meeting did then adjourn at 11:13
a.m., E.T.
SANDY QUINN, JR.
CHAIRMAN
