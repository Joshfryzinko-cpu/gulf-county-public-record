MARCH 13, 2019
SPECIAL MEETING
PORT ST. JOE, FLORIDA
The Gulf County Board of County Commissioners met this date in special session with
the following members present: Chairman Sandy Quinn, Jr., Vice Chairman Jimmy
Rogers, and Commissioners David C. Rich, Ward McDaniel, and Phil McCroan.
Others present were: County Attorney Jeremy Novak, Clerk Rebecca L. Norris, Clerk
Director of Finance & Management Rhonda Woodward, Deputy Clerk Leanna Roberts,
County Administrator Michael L. Hammond, County Assistant Administrator Warren
Yeager, Jr., Building Official Brad Bailey, Emergency Management Director Marshall
Nelson, E9-1-1 Coordinator Ben Guthrie, Emergency Management Staff Assistant Rachel
Jackson, Gulf County E.M.S. Director Houston Whitfield, G.I.S. Coordinator Jessica
Susich, Human Resource Director Brett Lowry, Office of Management & Budget Director
Sherry Herring, Assistant Mosquito Control Director Austin Horton, Public Works &
Mosquito Control Director Mark Cothran, and Assistant Public Works Director Raymond
Hart.
Chairman Quinn called the meeting to order at 4:00 p.m., E.T.
Chairman Quinn led in the Pledge of Allegiance to the Flag.
DEAD LAKES PARK
County Administrator Hammond reported that Dead Lakes Park should be up and running
in two (2) to three (3) weeks out; requesting Board approval to allow T.D.C. to run and
manage this facility, as well as set the rates. He reported that the County wants to go to
a cashless system; allowing T.D.C. to purchase the necessary equipment, under the bid
limit, for this service. Commissioner McDaniel discussed the park; reporting that this park
is self-sufficient. After discussion, Commissioner McDaniel inquired about the
maintenance building for the workcrews. County Administrator Hammond stated that it is
the County’s intention to keep this building for that purpose. After further discussion,
Commissioner Rogers so moved. Commissioner McCroan seconded the motion.
Chairman Quinn called for public comment. There being no public comment, the motion
passed unanimously.
RIGHT OF ENTRY PROGRAM
County Administrator Hammond stated that D.O.T. (Florida Department of
Transportation) informed the County that they are not going to pick up the bill for right of
entry on private property for the County; reporting that the County has been pre-approved
by F.E.M.A. (Federal Emergency Management Agency) and D.E.M (FL Department of
Emergency Management). He reported the County received thirty (30) applications thus
far and suggested they end accepting applications today; requesting approval of the

Board to allow Public Works Director Cothran to go through the applications deemed
eligible and fill them (to cut leaning trees) by using county labor, and the help of private
contractors like Kim Hunter if needed; then submit requests for reimbursement. Following
discussion, Commissioner McDaniel motioned to set the deadline to submit right of entry
applications for Friday (March 15, 2019). Commissioner Rich seconded the motion.
Chairman Quinn called for public. There being no public comment, the motion passed
unanimously. Upon discussion by County Administrator Hammond, Commissioner
McDaniel motioned to allow the County, on a case by case basis, to do the work and hire
a professional, like Kim Hunter and others, to cut the trees. After discussion,
Commissioner Rogers seconded the motion. Chairman Quinn called for public comment.
There being no public comment, the motion passed unanimously.
D.O.T. ROADWORK/ROADS
County Administrator Hammond discussed his concerns regarding the roadwork in
Highland View; reporting there was a wreck on top of the bridge yesterday. He suggested
the Board submit a letter to D.O.T. (Florida Department of Transportation) requesting live
maintenance of traffic during business hours on Highway 98 until the job is completed.
Commissioner McCroan requested that County Administrator Hammond include in the
letter his concerns regarding the road conditions at the Cape. County Administrator
Hammond agreed to include this matter in the letter. Commissioner McCroan motioned
to submit a letter (to D.O.T.). Commissioner Rich seconded the motion. Following
discussion, Chairman Quinn called for public comment. There being no public comment,
the motion passed unanimously.
Upon request by County Administrator Hammond, Commissioner Rogers motioned to
submit a letter to D.O.T. (Florida Department of Transportation) requesting them to
resurface the Country Club Road. Commissioner McDaniel seconded the motion.
Chairman Quinn called for public comment. There being no public comment, the motion
passed unanimously.
County Administrator Hammond discussed the damage to secondary roads due the
debris haulers; requesting the Board allow Public Works to do an inventory and submit
the list to F.E.M.A. Chairman Quinn requested that alleyways be added to this list. County
Administrator Hammond stated that the list will consist of Countywide and Citywide.
Commissioner Rogers so moved. Commissioner Rich seconded the motion. Chairman
Quinn called for public comment. There being no public comment, the motion passed
unanimously.
MARVIN AVENUE PROPERTY ACQUISITION
County Administrator Hammond discussed a proposed contract from Barbara Whitfield
<copy provided to the Clerk for the record> regarding the purchase of a piece of property
on the corner of 10th Street and Marvin Avenue; stating that these two (2) lots would be
an addition to the 10th Street Park as a parking area. After discussion, Commissioner
McCroan so moved. Commissioner Rich seconded the motion. County Administrator
Hammond requested they include in the motion to allow him to sign the contract and to
close on the property. Commissioner McCroan agreed to include this in his motion.

Chairman Quinn called for public comment. Port St. Joe City Mayor Patterson appeared
before the Board to discuss the 10th Street Park. Chairman Quinn called for additional
public comment. There being no further public comment, the motion passed unanimously.
STONE MILL CREEK FIRE DEPARTMENT DIRECT PURCHASE ORDER
County Attorney Novak requested Board approval of Direct Purchase Orders for the
concrete and the metal building (for the Stone Mill Creek Fire Department), suspending
the procurement policy. Office of Management & Budget Director Herring stated that the
first request is for the County purchase of the metal building from Bigbee Steel Buildings,
Inc., in the amount of $45,986.00 with the exemption to the bidding requirements due to
the immediate availability of the material. Commissioner McDaniel so moved.
Commissioner Rich seconded the motion. Chairman Quinn called for public comment.
There being no public comment, the motion passed unanimously. Upon request by Office
of Management & Budget Director Herring, Commissioner McDaniel motioned to approve
Change Order #1 with Fisher Construction for the Direct Purchase of the concrete,
estimated to be $25,000.00, the roll-up doors, in the amount of $10,800.00 and to allow
County Administrator Hammond to sign this change order and Direct Purchase Order,
and any future ones, for this project. Commissioner Rich seconded the motion.
Discussion followed. Chairman Quinn called for public comment. There being no public
comment, the motion passed unanimously.
ADMINISTRATION UPDATE AND REPORTS
County Administrator Hammond discussed the need of a letter from the Chairman
recommending Helping Hands to assist in the tornado relief in Lee County, Alabama.
Commissioner Rogers so moved. Commissioner Rich seconded the motion. Chairman
Quinn called for public comment. There being no public comment, the motion passed
unanimously.
County Administrator Hammond discussed the wishes of the Board for submitting an
extension request regarding the deadline for placing storm debris on the right-of-ways;
stating that he does not believe D.O.T. (Florida Department of Transportation) will grant
the request and would not recommend submitting this request. He requested direction
from the Board regarding this matter. After discussion, Commissioner McCroan motioned
for the County Attorney to draft a letter requesting the extension from both F.E.M.A.
(Federal Emergency Management Agency) and D.O.T. (FL Department of
Transportation), and fully funded. Commissioner Rogers seconded the motion.
Discussion followed regarding time. Commissioner Rich requested that the County ask
for a 90 (ninety) day extension. After discussion, Chairman Quinn called for public
comment. There being no public comment, the motion passed unanimously.
SPECIAL ELECTION
Commissioner McDaniel discussed the Special Election; reporting that the primary is in
April and the General Election is in June; reporting that the Florida Division of Elections
approved the two (2) Super-precinct sites.

STORM DEBRIS – UTILITY LINES
Chairman Quinn discussed his concerns regarding storm debris pick up in the alleyways
and the downed lines (cable); inquiring whether utility companies will return to collect
these lines (cable). He discussed calling Mediacom to request they send workers back
out to collect these lines. County Administrator Hammond stated that staff will make
contact with them. City Mayor Patterson appeared before the Board to report that
Mediacom stated they will start picking up the down lines this week.
PAINTED PONY ROAD
Commissioner McCroan inquired of Public Works on finding shell for Painted Pony Road.
Public Works Director Cothran discussed the project.
DRY DOCK PROJECT – OLD MILL SITE
County Administrator Hammond discussed the log operation; suggesting a tour. He
reported that GAC Contractors is now mobilized on the Old Mill Site for the improvements,
and the Eastern Shipbuilding Warehouse is going out for bids.
There being no further business, and upon motion by Commissioner Rogers, second by
Commissioner Rich, and unanimous vote, the meeting did then adjourn at 4:38 p.m., E.T.
