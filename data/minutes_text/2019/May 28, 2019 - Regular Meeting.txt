UPDATE – ST. JOE COMPANY
Jorge Gonzalez, of St. Joe Company appeared before the Board to provide an update
regarding various projects within the County; discussing the St. Joe Company’s
commitment to economic development and working with an energy company
(BioCarburante Company * biorefinery for fuel). He reported that Eastern Shipbuilding is
getting the site ready for the Port Project. Mr. Gonzalez stated that St. Joe Company is
not going to stop trying to redevelop The Port and bring jobs to the area. He discussed
the Airport Project; commending the Board for moving forward with the feasibility study.
Mr. Gonzalez stated that St. Joe Company is willing to work with the County under a
public private partnership (P3). He reported that St. Joe Company is committed to
rebuilding the Marina; possibly expanding the project by providing a state-of-the-art dry
storage facility, hotel lodging, and shopping. Mr. Gonzalez discussed ongoing
construction at WindMark Beach; reporting that 94 (ninety-four) additional lots are being
built upon. After discussion, County Administrator Hammond requested approval from the
Board to allow County Attorney Novak to work with the St. Joe Company on the P3 for
the Airport Property. Commissioner Rogers so moved. Commissioner Rich seconded the
motion. Chairman Quinn called for public comment. There being no public comment, the
motion passed unanimously.

UPDATE – GULF CORRECTIONAL INSTITUTION
Gulf Correctional Institution Warden Scott Payne appeared before the Board to provide
an update regarding the reopening of the prison in Stone Mill Creek (GCI); reporting that
the schedule is to open four or five (4-5) dorms at the Main Unit starting the end of June
or first of July, 2019. He discussed the reconstruction plan of the Annex Unit.
Commissioner McDaniel discussed his concerns regarding the 2020 Census; reporting
that Gulf County is down by 20% (twenty percent) on population. County Administrator
Hammond reported that the census count has been delayed until April 1, 2020; requesting
the County be notified soon if the Annex is not going to be up and running. Warden Payne
discussed the need for staffing; reporting that the goal is to get both facilities back up and
running.

SENIOR MANAGEMENT RETIREMENT
County Administrator Hammond reporting that the State changed the rules for Senior
Management Retirement; stating personnel in the position when the change was made,
are eligible to get retroactive benefit for the difference of 1.6 verses 2.0. He reported that
the County has two employees, Lee Collinsworth (in the amount of $19,250.85) and Mark
Cothran (in the amount of $6,075.68) that had this change; recommending that the Board
pay the difference to make it whole, paying out of their budgets and address this matter
later on with a budget amendment. Commissioner McCroan so moved. Commissioner
Rogers seconded the motion. Chairman Quinn called for public comment. There being
no public comment, the motion passed unanimously.
