AUGUST 21, 2019
WORKSHOP
PORT ST. JOE, FLORIDA
The Gulf County Board of County Commissioners met this date in a workshop session
with the following members present: Chairman Sandy Quinn, Jr., Vice Chairman Jimmy
Rogers, and Commissioners David C. Rich, Ward McDaniel, and Phil McCroan.
Others present were: County Attorney Jeremy Novak, Deputy Clerk Leanna Roberts,
County Administrator Michael L. Hammond, County Assistant Administrator Warren
Yeager, Jr., Deputy Administrator Kari Summers, Building Official Brad Bailey, Code
Enforcement Officer Trevor Seay, Emergency Management Director Marshall Nelson,
E9-1-1 Coordinator Ben Guthrie, Assistant Mosquito Control Director Austin Horton,
County Planner Lee Collinsworth, Public Works & Mosquito Control Director Mark
Cothran, Assistant Public Works Director Raymond Hart, Veterans' Service Office
Director & S.H.I.P. Director Joe Paul, and VSO/S.H.I.P./Central Services Director Lynn
Lanier.
Chairman Quinn called the meeting to order at 3:33 pm, E.T.
Veterans' Service Office Director & S.H.I.P. Director Paul opened the meeting with prayer,
and Chairman Quinn led in the Pledge of Allegiance to the Flag.
HOUSING PLAN
S.H.I.P. Director Paul appeared before the Board to discuss the proposed Hurricane
Housing Recovery Program Funding Agreement (H.H.R.P.); presenting a packet to the
Board for review <copy provided to the Clerk for the record>. He reported that H.H.R.P.
funds must be used for hurricane housing recovery but was told by Florida Housing that
if a resident moves to Gulf County for workforce, then funds could be used from the
demolition reconstruction strategy to build a house for them. S.H.I.P. Director Paul
discussed the proposed agreement; reporting that Florida Housing would like the
agreement to be submitted by September 15, 2019. He requested that the Board consider
approving the presented funding agreement; discussing the limits for moderate and
median income and the demolition reconstruction strategy. S.H.I.P. Director Paul stated
that most of the houses under review cannot rehab with $70,000.00 because the house
is worth $60,000.00 and it is over 50% destroyed (from Hurricane Michael), so the only
choice is to figure a way to tear it down (demolish) and build a house within the
$100,000.00 limit. Discussion followed regarding house built options. S.H.I.P. Director
Lanier reported that the County has two (2) years to encumber and spend this money;
stating that her concern is that if the County does all site-built homes, then there will not
be enough time to spend all of the funding. Chairman Quinn discussed the bid process
requirements. S.H.I.P. Director Paul discussed the bid currently out for mobile and
modular homes; stating that the site-built homes will be bid out. Discussion followed

regarding the proposed H.H.R.P. funding agreement. County Administrator Hammond
discussed amending the LHAP (Local Housing Assistance Plan) and his concerns
regarding the lack of auditing. He discussed looking at calling the mortgage, if the person
dies, or it be built in a land trust. County Administrator Hammond requested that more
restrictions be added, if the Board agrees to raise the dollar amount; reporting that under
the current LHAP it is a ten (10) year forgiveness. Discussion following regarding building
on family property and the inherited family member not being eligible for S.H.I.P. County
Administrator Hammond discussed options to be put in place in order for the County to
get the property back, especially if the County purchases the land. Upon inquiry by
Chairman Quinn, S.H.I.P. Director Paul stated that he is going to a symposium next week
to attend a class regarding community land trust. Discussion followed regarding land trust
and housing authority. Commissioner McCroan inquired to County Administrator
Hammond as to what needs Board approval is the 30/30/30 (presented H.H.R.P. funding
agreement). S.H.I.P. Director Paul stated that in addition to this agreement, there is one
other item for the Board to consider; a technical revision to the LHAP, changing the
purchase assistance from $30,000.00 to $80,000.00. Chairman Quinn discussed his
concerns regarding the plan; requesting staff to reach out to other Counties to find out
what they are doing. S.H.I.P. Director Paul stated that there is a State-wide workshop
next week, and upon return from this workshop, he will request the Board to call a special
meeting to discuss the proposed plan for the Board to consider. After discussion by
Commissioner McDaniel and S.H.I.P. Director Paul, S.H.I.P. Director Lanier discussed
Bid #1819-41 for the purchase of modular and mobile homes; reporting that three (3) bids
were received, but would like to reject and readvertise with modified bid specifications.
S.H.I.P. Director Paul stated that the Consent (agenda) has already been done for the
Board’s next meeting, so he will be asking County Administrator Hammond to bring up
the bid to request that the Board reject and re-bid with clarification (to the bid specs).
County Administrator Hammond stated that at the special meeting we need to discuss
the remaining disaster housing funds, explain where the County is in the current year
S.H.I.P., and checking into what other Counties in Northwest Florida are doing. County
Administrator Hammond discussed his concerns regarding auditing, housing authority,
and proof of homeowner’s insurance. After discussion, County Administrator Hammond
stated that the County needs checks and balances to make sure the applicants are
following the rules. After discussion regarding homeowner’s insurance coverage,
Chairman Quinn called for public comment. Steve Deputy, General Manager of Sales and
Marketing for Little Custom Homes of Florida, Inc. appeared before the Board to report
that the company is leasing a facility in Carrabelle and intend to build systemized home
for moderate, low, and workforce housing. He stated they are building workforce and
affordable housing, and traditional stick-built homes. Mr. Deputy discussed the company
and the building process; stating that he would like to get-in on the bidding process.
County Administrator Hammond discussed the two (2) year deadline; stating that he will
bring it up to the County’s delegation next week. Pat Hardman, of Cape San Blas
appeared before the Board to discuss her concerns regarding the property taxes for these
homes. S.H.I.P. Paul stated that there is a possibility of an extension, but there is a
deadline to request an extension from Florida Housing.

There being no further discussion, Chairman Quinn adjourned the meeting at 4:51 pm,
ET.
