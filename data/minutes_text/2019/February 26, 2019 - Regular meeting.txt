BID #1819-21 – PUBLIC SAFETY FACILITY FINANCING
Upon recommendation by County Administrator Hammond, Commissioner McCroan
motioned to award Bid #1819-21 for the Public Safety Facility Financing (Gulf County
Sheriff’s Office Building * 418 Cecil G Costin) to the low-bidder, People’s South Bank, at
a rate of 3.6% and no closing fee. Commissioner Rich seconded the motion. County
Administrator Hammond requested the Board to include in the motion to allow the
Chairman to sign all the necessary documents to close. Commissioner McDaniel had no
objection to adding this to his motion. Chairman Quinn called for public comment. There
being no public comment, the motion passed unanimously (4-0). County Administrator
Hammond discussed holding a meeting with the U.S. Treasury; reporting that the funds
(for this purchase) have been approved and will be release early April, 2019.
BID #1819-19 – PUBLIC ASSISTANCE CONSULTANT SERVICES
County Administrator Hammond recommended the Board table the bids received for Bid
#1819-19, Public Assistance Consultant Services (Long Term). Commissioner McDaniel
motioned to table. Commissioner Rich seconded the motion. Chairman Quinn called for
public comment. There being no public comment, the motion passed unanimously (4-0).
County Administrator Hammond reported that a recommendation will be provided to the
Board later next week to either accept one of the received bids, or rebid.

DOWNTOWN REDEVELOPMENT AGENCY – CITY OF PORT ST, JOE
County Administrator Hammond provided a timeline regarding the DRA (Downtown
Redevelopment Agency). Upon inquiry by Commissioner McDaniel, County Administrator
Hammond stated that the vote is to extend for another thirty (30) years, but the County is
working on an interlocal agreement with the City of Port St. Joe; regarding a commitment
that they will not seek to extend, or do anymore DRA’s, for a period of twenty (20) years.
Following discussion, County Commissioner McCroan reported that the Martin L. King
DRA is totally separate from the DRA on Reid Avenue. County Administrator Hammond
discussed the boundary lines in the beginning of the DRA. He stated that if the City of
Port St. Joe approves the proposed interlocal agreement, then the County will need to
hold a special meeting on Thursday of next week.
FUNDS – TRIUMPH BOARD

fund; calling it Hurricane Michael Major Disaster Recovery Program, in the amount of $15
million for loss of ad valorem taxes. Assistant Administrator Yeager discussed completing
the application and submitting it to the Board at a later meeting for Board approval.

ELECTION UPDATE
Gulf County Supervisor of Elections John Hanlon appeared before the Board to provide
a handout <copy provided to the Clerk for the record>; discussing the upcoming Special
Election. He reported that the County will have to pay for these elections upfront, out of
pocket, but the State will reimburse the County in two (2) to three (3) years. Supervisor
of Elections Hanlon discussed the cost options for the Special Election. Following
discussion, Supervisor of Elections Hanlon requested a draft from the Board, in the
amount of $76,000.00 to start the process. County Administrator Hammond discussed
his concerns regarding the unfunded mandate by the State; stating that the Board needs
to send a resolution to the Governor requesting the use of super precincts and a resolution
requesting the State to pay for it. Upon inquiry by Commissioner Rich, County
Administrator Hammond recommended that the Board submit a resolution to the
Governor asking for the two super precincts and that the State pick up the tab. After
discussion, County Administrator Hammond requested approval to give Supervisor of
Elections Hanlon $45,000.00 today, and if the Super Precincts are not approved, then the
Board provide the difference. Commissioner McDaniel motioned to give $45,000.00 to
Gulf County Supervisor of Elections (for the cost of the Super Precincts Special Election).
Commissioner McCroan seconded the motion. Commissioner McCroan asked whether
this motion included the resolution. Commissioner McDaniel agreed to add the resolution
to his motion. Chairman Quinn called for public comment. There being no public
comment, the motion passed unanimously (4-0), as follows:
