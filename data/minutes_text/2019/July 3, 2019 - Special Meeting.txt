JULY 3, 2019
SPECIAL MEETING
PORT ST. JOE, FLORIDA
The Gulf County Board of County Commissioners met this date in special session with
the following members present: Chairman Sandy Quinn, Jr., Vice Chairman Jimmy
Rogers, and Commissioners David C. Rich, Ward McDaniel, and Phil McCroan.
Others present were: County Attorney Jeremy Novak, Clerk Rebecca L. Norris, County
Administrator Michael L. Hammond, Deputy Administrator Kari Summers, Code
Enforcement Officer Trevor Seay, Gulf County E.D.C. Director Jim McKnight, Emergency
Management Director Marshall Nelson, E9-1-1 Coordinator Ben Guthrie, Gulf County
E.M.S. Director Houston Whitfield, Dewberry/County Engineer Clay Smallwood, III, Grant
Administrator Kaci Rhodes, Human Resource Director Brett Lowry, Office of Management
& Budget Director Sherry Herring, Assistant Mosquito Control Director Austin Horton,
Parks & Recreational Director Billy Traylor, County Planner Lee Collinsworth, Public
Works & Mosquito Control Director Mark Cothran, Assistant Public Works Director
Raymond Hart, Sheriff Mike Harrison, T.D.C. Executive Director Kelli Godwin, and
VSO/S.H.I.P./Central Services Director Lynn Lanier.
Chairman Quinn called the meeting to order at 9:01 a.m., E.T.
Chairman Quinn led in the Pledge of Allegiance to the Flag.
FDEP – ST. JOSEPH PENINSULA BEACH NOURISHMENT PROJECT 19GU2
Office of Management & Budget Director Herring requested Board approval to amend the
agreement (with FL Department of Environmental Protection) for the beach nourishment
project grant to add $2 million for the section of beach for the State. Commissioner
McCroan so moved. Commissioner Rogers seconded. Chairman Quinn called for public
comment. There being no public comment, the motion passed unanimously.
RFQ #1819-33 – HVAC
County Administrator Hammond reported that the County received three (3) responses
to Bid #1819-33 for the HVAC Services for County Departments & Jail (Detention Facility);
recommending the Board accept all three (3) as qualified contractors; 1. Philco
Mechanical Services, LLC; 2. GW Services Heating & Air; and 3. Miller Heating & Air
Conditioning. Commissioner Rich so moved. Commissioner McDaniel second. Chairman
Quinn called for public comment. There being no public comment, the motion passed 4
to 0, with Commissioner McCroan abstaining due to him having an employment
relationship (Philco Mechanical Services, LLC) <Form 8B on file with the Clerk>.

STATE INFRASTRUCTURE
County Administrator Hammond reported that the County received a $900,000.00 State
appropriation through D.E.O. (Department of Economy Opportunity) for infrastructure in
the County due to Hurricane Michael. He requested Board approval to accept (funding)
and to go through the procedures to implement this grant and to priorities two (2) items:
1. Transfer Station, setting aside $500,000.00; and 2. Ambulance and equipment
purchase, setting aside $180,000.00. Commissioner McDaniel so moved. Commissioner
Rogers seconded. Chairman Quinn called for public comment. There being no public
comment, the motion passed unanimously.
Office of Management & Budget Director Herring discussed the proposed grant
agreement; reporting the work period will start July 1st; requesting Board approval to allow
the Chairman to sign documents as the scope of work comes about. Commissioner
Rogers so moved. Commissioner McCroan seconded. Chairman Quinn called for public
comment. There being no public comment, the motion passed unanimously.
Upon request by County Administrator Hammond, Commissioner McDaniel motioned to
allow E.M.S. Director Whitfield to advertise for an ambulance, once the County received
the documentation. Commissioner Rich seconded the motion. Chairman Quinn called for
public comment. There being no public comment, the motion passed unanimously.
HORNE LLP – INVOICE #1
Upon recommendation by County Administrator Hammond, Commissioner McDaniel
motioned to pay an invoice from Horne, in the amount of $150,000.00 (for Disaster
Recovery Consulting Services * Bid #1819-02 amend). Commissioner Rich seconded the
motion. Chairman Quinn called for public comment. There being no public comment, the
motion passed unanimously.
EXTENSION GRANT – VIVA FL LANDSCAPE GRANT
Office of Management & Budget Director Herring requested Board approval to ratify a
Viva Florida Grant application for an extension grant for landscaping at the Sheriff’s
Office, both Extension Offices, and Dead Lakes Park. Commissioner McCroan made the
motion. Commissioner Rogers seconded the motion. Chairman Quinn called for public
comment. There was no public comment. Office of Management & Budget Director
Herring reported that the grant amount is only $3,000.00 with an equal match and the
match will be used as in-kind services. Upon inquiry by Chairman Quinn, Commissioner
McCroan stated his motion stands. The motion then passed unanimously.
BEACH MAINTENANCE
Commissioner McCroan discussed his concerns regarding the beach raking service.
Parks & Recreational Director Traylor appeared before the Board to discuss the increase
of public on Gulf County Beaches; reporting that the Sea Turtle Patrol is holding up the
contractor’s ability to start early enough to finish raking before visitors enter the beach.
He requested the Board submit notice to the Turtle Patrol. After discussion,
Commissioner McCroan discussed providing notice to the Turtle Patrol when the beaches
will be raked so that they can patrol the beach earlier. Chairman Quinn directed Parks

and Recreational Director Traylor to contact the Turtle Patrol regarding the dates that the
beach will be raked. Upon inquiry by Commissioner Rich regarding the need for a motion,
Commissioner Rogers so moved. Commissioner Rich seconded the motion. Chairman
Quinn called for public comment. There being no public comment, the motion passed
unanimously.
TRANSFER STATION EMERGENCY PROCUREMENTS
County Administrator Hammond requested permission from the Board to waive the Bid
rules due to the timing; declaring an emergency, allowing him to go out to procure the
materials for the Transfer Station under the Emergency Bid Procedure. Commissioner
McDaniel motioned to waive the procedure. Commissioner McCroan seconded the
motion. Chairman Quinn called for public comment. There being no public comment. The
motion passed unanimously.
ADMINISTRATIVE REPORT & UPDATE
Upon recommendation by County Administrator Hammond, Commissioner McCroan
motioned to donate $500.00 to the Port St. Joe Girls Dixie Youth Softball Team for
expenses to travel to the State tournament <copy provided to the Clerk for the record>.
Commissioner Rich seconded the motion. County Commissioner McDaniel discussed his
concerns regarding the donation; inquiring if this would open the door for every team,
setting a precedent. He offered to personally donate to this ball team. County
Administrator Hammond stated that each request would be an individual decision of the
Board on a case by case basis. Commissioner McCroan discussed his concerns; offering
to also contribute personally. After discussion by Chairman Quinn, Clerk Norris discussed
her concerns with donating to specific teams and this meeting a public purpose overall;
also offering to donate personally and to deliver the individual donations. County Attorney
Novak discussed prior practices of the Board and public purposes having been stated in
the past. Commissioner Rich discussed this concerns with contributing to individual teams
with taxpayers’ money; noting given the opportunity to donate it may be better to
contribute personally or through one’s business. Chairman Quinn discussed each
Commissioner donating $100.00, totaling $500.00, with the Clerk contributing too. The
motion was then withdrawn.
County Administrator Hammond discussed a request from Sunset Village Owners
Association regarding a speed limit change <copy provided to the Clerk for the record>;
reporting the County will have to write a letter to FL D.O.T. (Department of
Transportation). He noted that he had no objection to the request. Following discussion,
Commissioner McCroan stated he is not in favor of a speed reduction for this area.
Commissioner Rogers discussed his concerns; stating that he agrees with Commissioner
McCroan.
County Administrator Hammond reported that South Gulf Fire Department is ready to
start doing Jaws of Life responses; requesting Board permission to be added to the list.
He stated that if the Board does not have an objection, then the County will proceed and
notify the Sheriff’s Office to start dispatching them. Commissioner McCroan discussed
the new truck and the training.

Jason Tunnell, President of BCC Waste Solutions appeared before the Board to provide
an update regarding the solid waste services and the Transfer Station. Commissioner
McCroan stated there have been some transitional issues but once they are smoothed
out, he (Jason Tunnell) will do a great job. Commissioner Rogers echoed Commissioner
McCroan’s comments; noting Mr. Tunnell is working well with staff. Commissioner Rich
reported that there are still Waste Pro cans on the side of the road. County Administrator
Hammond stated that Public Works will need to finish their round-up (collection of the
Waste Pro cans) throughout the County. Commissioner McDaniel discussed cans in his
District; reporting that his calls have dropped off.
Public Works Director Cothran reported that he is negotiating with someone to take all
the (wood) chips from the (Five Points) Landfill; requesting permission to negotiate. Upon
recommendation by County Administrator Hammond, Commissioner Rogers motioned to
allow County staff to negotiate the removal of the (wood) chips from (Five Points) Landfill.
Commissioner McCroan seconded the motion. Upon inquiry by Commissioner McDaniel,
Public Works Director Cothran reported that the gentleman is from Georgia. After
discussion by Commissioner Rogers, Chairman Quinn called for public comment. There
being no public comment, the motion passed unanimously.
County Administrator Hammond reported that the County closed on the Golf Course on
Monday and started operations yesterday. T.D.C. Director Godwin recommended to
purchase the Jonas System (software); reporting that the first time fee is under the bid
limit and an annual fee will come after that. Commissioner Rich so moved. Commissioner
Rogers seconded the motion. Chairman Quinn called for public comment. There being
no public comment, the motion passed unanimously. County Administrator Hammond
reported that the majority of the full-time employees are being paid from the grant through
CareerSource Gulf Coast; stating that he may have to hire some part-time employees
that the County will have to pay, specifically weekends, to run the restaurant and bar until
the transition (Bid #1819-36 * Country Club Golf Course Restaurant & Bar Management
Services).
County Administrator Hammond reminded the Board that the budget meeting is July 22,
2019 at 9:00 a.m., E.T.
County Administrator Hammond reported that the County is out for bids (Bid #1819-39)
for internet and phone service; requesting permission for a short-term extension (with the
current provider, Mediacom), if needed. Commissioner Rich so moved. Commissioner
McDaniel seconded the motion. Chairman Quinn called for public comment. There being
no public comment, the motion passed unanimously.
South Gulf County Fire Department Chief Vince Bishop appeared before the Board to
discuss his concerns regarding the sale of fireworks considering the dry conditions and
downed trees in the County.

Mr. Bishop reported that four (4) volunteers have gone through the Jaws of Life training;
requesting that South Gulf Fire Department take over as primary for South Gulf County
and be a backup for the City (of Port St. Joe). <No action taken by the Board>
Upon inquiry by Chairman Quinn regarding the burn ban, County Administrator Hammond
reported that he allowed the ban to expire once the County started getting measurable
rain; stating that he does not think the County is at a point where a reinstatement is
needed at this time. He also reported that the conditions are being regulated through the
State (Division of Forestry).
There being no further business, and upon motion by Commissioner Rogers, seconded
Commissioner Rich, and unanimous vote, the meeting did then adjourn at 9:46 a.m., E.T.
