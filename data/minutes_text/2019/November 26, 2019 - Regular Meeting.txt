INTERLOCAL AGREEMENT – CALHOUN COUNTY
County Administrator Hammond introduced Commissioner Gene Bailey from Calhoun
County, requesting he come before the Board. Calhoun County Commissioner Bailey
appeared before the Board to report that currently their building inspector is out sick and
requested this Board consent to an Interlocal agreement between the Counties to help
