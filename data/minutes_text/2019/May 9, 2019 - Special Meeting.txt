HIGHLAND VIEW ANNEXATION REQUEST
County Administrator Hammond read aloud a portion of the 2005 Interlocal Agreement
between the City of Port St. Joe and the County regarding annexations and future
annexations; reporting that the City of Port St. Joe has advertised for a land use change
(parcel located in Highland View; old Highland View Elementary School site) without
holding a workshop with the County nor performing a referendum. He stated that the
developer is a seeking 60 (sixty) foot high development and somewhere in the
neighborhood 176 units on a nine (9) acre parcel. County Administrator Hammond stated
that he sees no way around the Board objecting to the annexation based on this (2005)
Interlocal; reporting that the County has a subsequent Interlocal that does not strike this
portion of the original Interlocal. County Attorney Novak discussed the 2005 and 2013
Interlocal Agreements; reporting that paragraph four (4) from the 2005 Interlocal was not
affected regarding the requirements the City and County should go through. Upon inquiry
by County Commissioner McDaniel, County Administrator Hammond reported that the
City (of Port St. Joe) has been approached by a developer and real estate agent that
wants to do a high-density development on this property, and the County does not allow
for that, so the City has been approached as annexing therefore allowing the developer
to obtain higher density and higher heights. He discussed his concerns regarding the
density and traffic with this proposed development; stating the County is bound, unless
the Board agrees to waive the requirement of the referendum. Commissioner Rogers
suggested meeting with the City of Port St. Joe regarding this matter before a decision is
made. County Administrator Hammond stated that he can request that the City Manager
(Jim Anderson) contact the City Mayor (Bo Patterson) and call a workshop. After
discussion, County Administrator Hammond stated that the Board needs to officially notify
the City of Port St. Joe that the County is objecting, on the short-term, to this annexation.
After discussion, County Administrator Hammond recommended that the Board give
notice to the City of Port St. Joe that there is an agreement that stated they cannot do it
(annex this parcel). County Attorney Novak discussed County Administrator Hammond’s
recommendation; stating the City of Port St. Joe needs to know where the Board is on
this issue. Upon inquiry by Chairman Quinn regarding the need for a motion to allow
County Attorney Novak to work with the City regarding this matter, County Attorney Novak
discussed the will of the Board to provide direction as to the Board’s consent or in
opposition to the annexation at this time. County Administrator Hammond requested a
motion to oppose the annexation at this time based on the current Interlocal Agreement
with the City (of Port St. Joe) and offer to have a sit-down workshop with the City in the
near future to see if details can be worked out. Commissioner Rogers so moved.
Commissioner McDaniel seconded the motion. Chairman Quinn called for public
comment. Pat Hardman, of Port St. Joe appeared before the Board to discuss her
concerns regarding the density and developers coming in to maximize their investment.
Chairman Quinn called for additional public comment. There being no further discussion,
the motion passed unanimously.
