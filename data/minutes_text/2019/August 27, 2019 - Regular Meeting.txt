LITIGATION REPORT
County Attorney Novak provided a status report regarding a lawsuit filed against the City
of Port St. Joe and Gulf County regarding the 10th Street Park; reporting that the County
has a stipulation agreement for the voluntary dismissal. He then read portions of the
stipulation agreement to the Board for consideration; reporting the County filed a motion
to dismiss, since the majority of the complaint was against the City (of Port St. Joe)
regarding what it wanted to do or did not do with that land. County Attorney Novak stated
that the only thing the County did was pledge the T.D.C. 5th Cent (Penny) toward assisting
the City (of Port St. Joe) in funding the improvement (of the 10th Street Park). He
discussed the use of these funds for parks and recreation in Gulf County; stating that it is
all within Statute. County Attorney Novak requested a vote by the Board to authorize the
Chairman and himself to execute the stipulation of dismissal, submit to Judge Gay, and
have this lawsuit removed from the court system. Commissioner McDaniel so moved for
the motion. Commissioner Rich seconded the motion. Chairman Quinn called for public
comment. Robert Branch, of Port St. Joe appeared before the Board to state that the
lawsuit is still rolling, it is not over. Chairman Quinn directed the Clerk to freeze Mr.
Branch’s time (to speak). County Attorney Novak advised his client (the Board) that they
can listen to Mr. Branch under the speaking law, but Mr. Branch is represented by an
attorney. Chairman Quinn directed the Clerk to start Mr. Branch’s time. As Mr. Branch
began to speak, Chairman Quinn directed the Clerk to pause his time; inquiring if Mr.
Branch was appearing to speak on what the County Attorney just discussed. Mr. Branch
stated that he signed up to speak on the topic of the Golf Course. Chairman Quinn stated
that the Board is not to that point in the meeting. After discussion, Chairman Quinn stated
that Mr. Branch can speak on the Golf Course at the end (of the meeting). Chairman
Quinn called for any public comment regarding the motion. There being no further public
comment, the motion passed unanimously.

MORATORIUM – ACCESSORY STRUCTURE / TOURIST CORRIDOR
Commissioner McCroan discussed accessory structures on lots, then making a motion to
place a moratorium on anything other than a primary structure on a lot. He stated that
one must have a primary structure on a lot to have an accessory structure or anything
else on that lot in the tourist corridor. Commissioner McDaniel seconded the motion.
Commissioner McCroan stated that it puts a moratorium on anything, unless there is a
primary structure. County Administrator Hammond asked for clarity on what the Board
means by tourist corridor; asking if the Board wants to follow the same open water that
the County did prior, which would include the Beaches, Beacon Hill, excluding WindMark,
the City (of Port St. Joe), Jones Homestead, or Simons Bayou, but would include the
Cape and Indian Pass. He inquired if the Board wanted to follow the CCCL Line and open
water, but not the Bay. Commissioner McCroan said yes, at this time, placing a
moratorium until the Board can figure out a permanent solution. Commissioner McCroan
stated yes when County Administrator Hammond asked whether the Board wanted staff
to bring back a recommendation on a proposed ordinance. Chairman Quinn called for
public comment. There being no public comment, the motion passed unanimously.
County Administrator Hammond stated that he will do his best to have a draft by next
Thursday’s special meeting.

PUBLIC HEARING – AMENDING BUILDING CODE ORDINANCE
Pursuant to advertisement to consider adoption of a proposed ordinance amending the
building code, County Attorney Novak read the proposed ordinance by title and some of
the highlights. County Administrator Hammond reported that the only individuals that
would be able to enforce the arrest in this matter would be the Gulf County Sherriff’s
Office. Discussion followed. County Attorney Novak discussed ‘after the fact’ variances;
stating, for the record, if a property owner did not apply for the permit, then the process
would not be met, therefore; they would not receive the power permit. After discussion by
County Administrator Hammond, Chairman Quinn called for public comment. Debra

Mayes, of Palm Breeze Way appeared before the Board to support the proposed
ordinance; discussing her concerns regarding there being no mention of RVs
(recreational vehicles) within the ordinance. She requested the Board include that a
recreational vehicle permit application be filed with the Building Department so the
penalties would apply to RVs (recreational vehicles), as well as other buildings and mobile
homes. County Administrator Hammond stated they are included; allowing staff to set the
fees and applications as a part of this (ordinance). He reported the only thing mentioned
that is not in this (ordinance) and the County will not enforce, is covenants and restrictions
in subdivisions. Following discussion, Ms. Mayes stated, for the record, that she is happy
that RVs (recreational vehicles) are implied, even if it is not written. Mike Holcombe, of
Palm Breeze Way appeared before the Board to discuss his concerns regarding an RV
(recreational vehicle) on blocks with no power. County Administrator Hammond reported
that they are in violation and have been cited. Following discussion, Mr. Holcombe
requested this ordinance to be enforced to resolve the matter in his neighborhood.
Chairman Quinn called for public comment. Larry Bronson, of Palm Breeze Way
appeared before the Board to state that he fully supports the comments from Mr.
Holcombe; presenting a photo to the Board to review <copy provided to the Clerk for the
record>. After discussion by County Administrator Hammond, Planner Collinsworth stated
that RVs (recreational vehicles) are not addressed in a building code because they are
not regulated by the Florida building code, they are regulated by the Florida Department
of Motor Vehicle. He discussed his visits to the site mentioned; reporting that it has been
turned over to code enforcement. Commissioner McCroan motioned to extend Mr.
Bronson’s time to speak. Commissioner Rogers seconded the motion, and it passed
unanimously. Chairman Quinn directed the Clerk to give three (3) more minutes. Mr.
Bronson discussed his concerns regarding another piece of property, 133 Palm Breeze
Way, with a RV (recreational vehicle) being closed in with a barn structure; requesting
that the County look into this one also. County Administrator Hammond stated that they
will send the Building Department to review it. Commissioner McDaniel discussed cutting
power to those not in compliance. Chairman Quinn called for public comment. Glenn
Combs, of Wewahitchka appeared before the Board to discuss his concerns regarding
hardship cases. Commissioner McCroan stated this will be on a case by case basis.
County Attorney Novak read the language; stating that it will be a case by case evaluation
by the Administrator. Discussion followed by members of the Board. Chairman Quinn
called for public comment. Debra Mayes, of Palm Breeze Way appeared before the Board
to inquire of Planner Collinsworth’s comment regarding RVs (recreational vehicles).
Commissioner McCroan stated that Planner Collinsworth statement was that the Florida
Building Code does not apply to RVs (recreational vehicles); reporting that the County is
not amending the actual building code, but the enforcement. After discussion by County
Administrator Hammond, Chairman Quinn called for public comment. There being no
further public comment, Chairman Quinn closed to the public. County Administrator
Hammond discussed having some discretion on hardship cases and enforcing grievous
violations. Commissioner McCroan motioned to adopt the following Ordinance.
Commissioner Rogers seconded the motion. Chairman Quinn called for public comment.
There being no public comment, the motion passed unanimously, as follows:
