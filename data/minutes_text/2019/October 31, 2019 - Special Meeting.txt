OCTOBER 31, 2019
SPECIAL MEETING
PORT ST. JOE, FLORIDA
The Gulf County Board of County Commissioners met this date in special session with
the following members present: Chairman Sandy Quinn, Jr., Vice Chairman Jimmy
Rogers, and Commissioners David C. Rich, Ward McDaniel, and Phil McCroan.
Others present were: Deputy Clerk Leanna Roberts, County Administrator Michael L.
Hammond, County Assistant Administrator Warren Yeager, Jr., Deputy Administrator Kari
Summers, Code Enforcement Officer Trevor Seay, Human Resource Director Brett
Lowry, Mosquito Control Director Austin Horton, Public Works Director Mark Cothran,
Assistant Public Works Director Raymond Hart, Tax Collector Shirley Jenkins, Veterans'
Service Office Director & S.H.I.P. Director Joe Paul, and VSO/S.H.I.P./Central Services
Director Lynn Lanier.
Chairman Quinn called the meeting to order at 3:02 p.m., E.T.
Chairman Quinn led in the Pledge of Allegiance to the Flag.
BID #1819-50 – RE-NEW MOBILE & MODULAR HOMES
S.H.I.P. Director Paul appeared before the Board to recommend the award of Bid #1819-
50 for re-new mobile and modular homes (S.H.I.P./H.H.R.P./Disaster Funding) to the
following low bidders: (1) Showcase Homes Direct for the two bed/two bath mobile home,
three bed/two bath mobile and four bed/two bath mobile home; (2) Timberline
Construction Group for one bed/one bath mobile home; (3) Ironwood Homes for one
bed/one bath handicap mobile home, two bed/one bath handicap mobile home, two
bed/two bath handicap mobile home, and three bed/two bath handicap mobile home; and
(4) Life Homes for the two bed/one bath modular home and three bed/two bath modular
home. Chairman Quinn called for a motion to accept these bids. Commissioner McCroan
so moved. Commissioner Rich seconded the motion. After discussion regarding total set
up cost, Chairman Quinn called for public comment. There being no public comment, the
motion passed unanimously.
H.H.R.P. FUNDING APPLICATIONS
S.H.I.P. Director Paul reported that for the extremely low and very low-income level the
County will offer rehabilitation, in the amount of $20,000.00 or a trailer (one bed/one bath)
for $40,000.00; for the low-income level the County will do rehabilitation with a maximum
of $25,000.00 or down payment assistant for a maximum of $50,000.00, not to exceed
50% (fifty percent) of the cost of the home; for the moderate income level the County will
not be doing rehabilitation, only purchase assistance at a maximum amount of
$60,000.00, not to exceed 50% (fifty percent) of the home. He stated that all funding is
through H.H.R.P. (Hurricane Housing Recovery Program). S.H.I.P. Director Paul

discussed the process of the lottery; reporting that the County is expecting two (2) other
pots of money. Upon inquiry by Chairman Quinn, S.H.I.P. Director Paul reported that once
the names are pulled, applicants have thirty (30) days to get all the required missing
documentation into their office. County Administrator Hammond stated that applicants will
be disqualified when it is found that they have lied on their application and they are not
eligible, or for failure to get a subsequent loan from the bank for the moderate-income
level. After discussion regarding the program levels, S.H.I.P. Director Lanier and Gulf
County Tax Collector Jenkins appeared before the Board to assist in the lottery drawing.
Tax Collector Jenkins pulled numbers by each district and by each income category.
S.H.I.P. Director Paul reported that an updated list will be provided to the Board according
to the numbers that were drawn. County Administrator Hammond discussed the spending
requirements; stating that going forward the Board will need to decide how to move these
monies around to get the set-aside (30/30/40) and how to handle the implementation of
the program going forward. He stated that the County will need to decide on how they
plan to manage this program; split it up or do it all in-house. County Administrator
Hammond discussed holding a special meeting prior to the next regular Board meeting
to make these decisions. Chairman Quinn stated the Board needs to talk about the need
for additional contractors for the rehabilitations at this special meeting.
ADMINISTRATIVE REPORT AND UPDATE
County Administrator Hammond presented a request <copy provided to the Clerk for the
record> to the Board to increase the stipend by $25.00 per month, as recommended by
the Health Insurance Committee. Commissioner McDaniel so moved. Commissioner
Rogers seconded the motion. Chairman Quinn called for public comment. There being
no public comment, the motion passed unanimously.
County Administrator Hammond requested approval from the Board to submit the
County’s formal C.I.B.R. (Community Issue Budget Request): $100,000.00 to complete
Beacon Hill Park and continued drainage and debris cleanup of ditches, in the amount of
$2 million. Commissioner Rogers so moved. Commissioner Rich seconded the motion.
Chairman Quinn called for public comment. There being no public comment, the motion
passed unanimously.
Assistant Administrator Yeager requested the Board approve a waiver of the Flow Control
Ordinance to D.E.P. (Department of Environmental Protection), as they are cleaning out
some of the inland waterways (due to Hurricane Michael). Commissioner McCroan so
moved. Commissioner McDaniel second the motion. Chairman Quinn called for public
comment. There being no public comment, the motion passed unanimously.
County Administrator Hammond noted the need to coordinate with the Chairman to
schedule a special meeting prior to the Board’s next regular meeting. Chairman Quinn
had no objections.

There being no further business, and upon motion by Commissioner McDaniel, second
by Commissioner Rogers, and unanimous vote, the meeting did then adjourn at 4:04 p.m.,
E.T.
