SEPTEMBER 5, 2019
BUDGET HEARING
PORT ST. JOE, FLORIDA
The Gulf County Board of County Commissioners met this date in special session with
the following members present: Chairman Sandy Quinn, Jr., Vice Chairman Jimmy
Rogers, and Commissioners David C. Rich, Ward McDaniel, and Phil McCroan.
Others present were: County Attorney Jeremy Novak, Clerk Rebecca L. Norris, Clerk
Director of Finance & Management Rhonda Woodward, Deputy Clerk Leanna Roberts,
County Administrator Michael L. Hammond, County Assistant Administrator Warren
Yeager, Jr., Building Official Brad Bailey, Gulf County E.M.S. Director Houston Whitfield,
Office of Management & Budget Director Sherry Herring, Assistant Mosquito Control
Director Austin Horton, Parks & Recreational Director Billy Traylor, Property Appraiser
Mitch Burke, Property Appraiser Office IT Tech Dennis Barfield, Jr., Public Works &
Mosquito Control Director Mark Cothran, Assistant Public Works Director Raymond Hart,
Supervisor of Elections John Hanlon, Tax Collector Shirley Jenkins, and T.D.C. Executive
Director Kelli Godwin.
Chairman Quinn called the meeting to order at 5:10 p.m., E.T.
The meeting then did recess at 5:10 p.m., E.T.
The meeting did reconvene at 5:11 p.m., E.T.
TENTATIVE 2019-20 OVERVIEW
County Administrator Hammond reported that the Sheriff has changed (health) insurance
groups, but he has agreed to include them in the match program, up to maximum $50.00
per month (HSA contribution). Office of Management & Budget Director Herring
addressed the Board; providing the following information: (1) the proposed aggregate
millage rate is 7.4946, which is 4.98% below the current year aggregate rolled back rate
of 7.8873; (2) the county-wide proposed tentative millage rate is 7.1000; (3) the
Dependent Fire Districts; St. Joseph, Tupelo, Overstreet, and Howard Creek are all at
0.5000 mills; and (4) the proposed MSTU Debt Service millage rates are: Gulfside Beach-
front at 1.1547, Gulfside Interior at 0.8664, and Bayside at 0.9016. She reported that the
Port St. Joe DRA (Downtown Redevelopment Agency) obligation for this year is
$175,000.00, and the first payment will be due December, 2019. OMB Director Herring
stated that the General Fund Budget includes Cash Carried Forward of $9,509,603.00 as
a source of funding, and $4,316,603.00 is restricted and not available for general
operating. She reported that the tentative budget has been provided to each
commissioner <copy provided to the Clerk for the record>, and posted on the County’s
website as required for this Public Hearing. OMB Director Herring requested
authorization, by motion of the Board, to make line item adjustments for expenditures

within each fund prior to the final hearing due to recent changes from open enrollment.
<No motion was given.> Chairman Quinn opened the floor for any Constitutional Officer
wishing to come before the Board to speak. None stepped forward. County Administrator
Hammond discussed the proposed budget; reporting there are numerous vacant
positions, and some will be filled during the year. OMB Director Herring requested a
motion from the Board allowing her to make line item adjustments as previously
mentioned. Commissioner Rogers so moved. Commissioner McCroan seconded the
motion. Chairman Quinn called for public comment. There being no public comment, the
motion passed unanimously. OMB Director Herring stated that the millage rates are
unchanged from what is presented in the proposed budget, which is a Countywide
General Fund millage rate of 7.1000; the aggregate millage rate of 7.4946, which is less
than aggregate rolled-back rate of 7.8873 by 4.98%. Chairman Quinn called for public
comment. There was no public comment.

FINAL BUDGET HEARING – SEPTEMBER 24, 2019
County Administrator Hammond reported that the Final Budget Hearing will be held in the
afternoon, after the regular Board meeting on September 24th at 5:01 p.m., E.T. He
thanked the Constitutional Officers for working with the County on this year’s budget;
thanking the Board for issuing a pay raise and for the healthcare stipend for employees.
There being no further business, upon motion by Commissioner McCroan, second by
Commissioner Rogers, and unanimous vote, the meeting did then adjourn at 5:58 p.m.,
