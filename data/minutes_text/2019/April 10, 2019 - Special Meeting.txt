APRIL 10, 2019
SPECIAL MEETING
PORT ST. JOE, FLORIDA
The Gulf County Board of County Commissioner met this date in special session with the
following members present: Chairman Sandy Quinn, Jr., Vice Chairman Jimmy Rogers,
and Commissioners David C. Rich, Ward McDaniel, and Phil McCroan.
Others present were: County Attorney Jeremy Novak, Clerk Rebecca L. Norris, Clerk
Director of Finance & Management Rhonda Woodward, Deputy Clerk Leanna Roberts,
County Administrator Michael L. Hammond, County Assistant Administrator Warren
Yeager, Jr., Deputy Administrator Kari Summers, Building Official Brad Bailey, Staff
Assistant–E.D.C. Johanna White, Emergency Management Director Marshall Nelson,
E9-1-1 Coordinator Ben Guthrie, Emergency Management Staff Assistant Rachel
Jackson, Gulf County E.M.S. Director Houston Whitfield, Dewberry/County Engineer Clay
Smallwood, III, G.I.S. Coordinator Jessica Susich, Human Resource Director Brett Lowry,
Information Technology Dennis Barfield, Sr., Assistant Mosquito Control Director Austin
Horton, County Planner Lee Collinsworth, Sheriff Mike Harrison, T.D.C. Executive
Director Kelli Godwin, and VSO/S.H.I.P./Central Services Director Lynn Lanier.
Chairman Quinn called the meeting to order at 4:00 p.m., E.T.
Chairman Quinn led in the Pledge of Allegiance to the Flag.
AWARD BID #1819-19 AMEND – PUBLIC ASSISTANCE CONSULTANT SERVICES
Upon recommendation by the interview committee and County Administrator Hammond,
Commissioner McCroan motioned to award Bid #1819-19 amend for Public Assistance
Consultant Services (long-term) to CDR Maguire. Commissioner Rich seconded the
motion. County Administrator Hammond requested permission to allow him to execute
the contract; stating that this contractor is already doing business with Bay County and
the City of Parker. Upon inquiry by Chairman Quinn, Commissioner McCroan had no
objection to adding this to his motion. Chairman Quinn called for public comment. There
being no public comment, the motion passed unanimously.
LETTER OF SUPPORT – SCHOOL BOARD
Upon discussion by County Administrator Hammond, Commissioner McDaniel motioned
to submit two (2) letters of support for triumph application for Wewahitchka High School
welding education program and agri-science education program. Commissioner Rogers
seconded the motion. Chairman Quinn called for public comment. There being no public
comment, the motion passed unanimously.

BEACH RESTORATION
Upon request by Assistant County Administrator Yeager, Commissioner McCroan
motioned to proceed with shorebird monitoring services (with MRD Associates, Inc.) and
allow the Chairman to sign the agreement. Commissioner Rogers seconded the motion.
Chairman Quinn called for public comment. There being no public comment, the motion
passed unanimously.
EASTERN PROJECT
Assistant County Administrator Yeager discussed a change order (with GAC
Contractors), with an increase of $72,893.74 (for the Eastern Shipbuilding Site Work * Bid
#1819-12); reporting that this will be paid from the $6 million appropriation from Legislator.
Commissioner McCroan so moved. Commissioner McDaniel second the motion.
Chairman Quinn called for public comment. There being no public comment, the motion
passed unanimously.
NUISANCE BEARS
County Administrator Hammond discussed receiving complaints regarding nuisance
bears within the County; requesting to submit a Resolution to FWC (Fish and Wildlife

submit a Resolution (to FWC). Commissioner McDaniel seconded the motion. Chairman
Quinn called for public comment. There being no public comment, the motion passed

ADMINISTRATIVE REPORT & UPDATE
County Attorney Novak reported that payment terms with Langton Associates has to be
changes based on instructions from U.S. Treasury and Langton has agreed to consult in
additional grant projects; stating that staff needs permission from the Board to engage
them on a separate agreement for consulting services on other grant projects, in the
amount of $1,000.00 per month. He requested permission from the Board to approve the
change order in order to add this additional service. After discussion by Assistant
Administrator Yeager, Commissioner Rogers so moved to approve the Change Order
with Langton Associates. Commissioner Rich seconded the motion. Chairman Quinn
called for public comment. There being no public comment, the motion passed
unanimously.
County Administrator Hammond discussed the fuel company (The BioCarburante
Company); reporting that the fuel can be pumped directly into vehicles. He stated that a
meeting is scheduled for May 6, 2019 to discuss the acquisition of the property and move
forward. County Administrator Hammond also reported that for the first two (2) years, the
company will make 60 million gallons (of fuel) per year.

County Administrator Hammond reminded the Board of the Budget Committee meeting
to be held on Wednesday, April 17, 2019 at 9:00 a.m., E.T.; stating that the Constitutional
Officers will be in attendance.
County Administrator Hammond reported that he has a meeting tomorrow at 2:00 p.m.,
E.T. to discuss recreational acquisition and will have something next week to move
forward.
Chairman Quinn called for public comment. Pat Hardman, of Coastal Community
Association appeared before the Board to discuss her concerns regarding debris on the
right-of-way illegally; inquiring if the County is looking to place a penalty for the illegal
dumping. County Administrator Hammond reported that the County is keeping an eye on
the situation and a new code enforcement officer has been hired. He stated that people
should call Public Works to report illegal dumping. County Administrator Hammond
reported that the State is refusing to pick up tires; stating that he has instructed the County
to pick up the tires, using the credit remaining with Waste Pro to dispose of these tires.
After discussion by Ms. Hardman, County Administrator Hammond stated that if it is a
contractor illegally dumping, to call the Sherriff’s Office. Members of the Board discussed
their concerns regarding illegal dumping.
Sheriff Harrison appeared before the Board to report that James Jenson’s funeral
services will be held tomorrow at 4:00 p.m., C.T. at Westside Baptist Church.
Upon request by County Administrator Hammond, Commissioner Rogers motioned to
accept a grant extension for a beautification grant at Veterans’ Memorial Beacon Hill from
Department of Transportation, extending through June 30, 2020. Commissioner
McDaniel seconded the motion. Chairman Quinn called for public comment. There being
no public comment, the motion passed unanimously.
There being no further business, and upon motion by Commissioner McDaniel, second
by Commissioner Rogers, and unanimous vote, the meeting did then adjourn at 4:26 p.m.,
E.T.
