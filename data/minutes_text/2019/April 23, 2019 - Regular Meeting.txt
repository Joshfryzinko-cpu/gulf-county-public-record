SMALL SCALE MAP AMENDMENT – W. J. GILFORD

SMALL SCALE MAP AMENDMENT – CINNAMON HILL, LLC
Planner Collinsworth presented a small scale map amendment from Cinnamon Hill, LLC
(Thad Williams) * Parcel ID #03501-181R; stating this request is changing a 1.76 acre
parcel from Residential to Mixed Commercial/Residential. He reported that these
meetings were properly noticed, the P.D.R.B. met and the vote died for a lack of a second
on the motion; stating that there was no approval made from the P.D.R.B. regarding this
application. Chairman Quinn called for public comment. Chairman Quinn called for any
objection to the waiving of the Quasi-Judicial Hearing. There being no objection,
Commissioner McDaniel motioned for the Board to waive (the Quasi-Judicial Hearing).
Commissioner McCroan seconded the motion. Chairman Quinn called for public
comment. There being no public comment, the motion passed unanimously. Charles
Brake, of Overstreet appeared before the Board to discuss his concerns regarding this
small scale map amendment application; stating that he is in opposition to any land use
change. Chairman Quinn called for additional public comment. Patrick Lango, of
Overstreet appeared before the Board to oppose this application. Chairman Quinn called
for additional public comment. There being no further public comment, Chairman Quinn
closed the hearing to public comment. Upon inquiry by Chairman Quinn, County Attorney
Novak reported that the P.D.R.B. did not make a recommendation to approve as it failed
for a lack of a second on the application. He stated that staff needs the action of the Board

with regarding to this application. Commissioner McCroan motioned to deny the
application. Commissioner Rich seconded the motion. Chairman Quinn called for public
comment. After discussion by Commissioner Rogers, County Attorney Novak requested
approval to the waiving of the full findings of the Board under a Public Hearing/Quasi-
Judicial Hearing and to include the following in the motion: the Board has reviewed and
accepts the P.D.R.B. statement of completeness for the map land use change, that the
Board deems the records submitted is competent and substantial evidence; the Board
accepts the P.D.R.B.’s statement and representation that proper notice of today’s Public
Hearing was provided with accordance with the Gulf County LDR’s and Comprehensive
Plan, and following that, that the provision apply to substantial and competent evidence
presented to the Board, and the Board incorporate this form and the Chairman’s
signature. Chairman Quinn inquired of Commissioner McCroan in regards to adding this
to his motion. Commissioner McCroan agreed. The motion then passed unanimously.

LAWSUIT – COUNTY WEBSITE
County Administrator Hammond discussed the County being sued regarding the
handicap accessibility via the County’s website; reporting that staff places a lot of
information on the website for the public’s information. After discussion by County
Administrator Hammond, Clerk Norris reported that some of the documents received
come from outside sources and cannot be manipulated to make them ADA compliant;
stating there may be some items that can be removed, as they are not required to be

posted. County Attorney Novak discussed his concerns regarding the effect of public
records laws have on small rural Counties.
SETBACKS AND NEW CODES
Chairman Quinn called Barbara Pickels to appear. Barbara Pickels, of St. Joe Beach
appeared before the Board to discuss a variance and setback request; asking the County
to allow a variance in order to rebuild back their original footprint. County Administrator
Hammond reported that there are hundreds (100’s) of houses that are not allowed to
rebuild to exact footprint; stating that the newest law that would affect Ms. Pickels’
situation is twenty-five (25) years old. He discussed the process for an applicant to plead
their case and the guidelines regarding setbacks. Planner Collinsworth reported that there
are several property owners in this same situation regarding fire separations per Florida
Building Code; stating that the lot can be built on, just not like what Ms. Pickels had
previously. After discussion by Planner Collinsworth, Ms. Pickels reported that her
neighbor was given a variance before the storm (Hurricane Michael). County
Administrator Hammond discussed looking at this further; stating that he is not aware of
a variance given on a side setback that would affect another property owner, mainly due
to fire codes.
REPEAL – RECREATIONAL VEHICLE ORDINANCE

in 2018; stating that residents are permitted to have one (1) RV per parcel in
unincorporated areas in Gulf County, as long as you meet the LRD’s (Land Development
Regulations) setbacks, and requirements.
