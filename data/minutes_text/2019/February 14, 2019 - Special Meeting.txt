INTERLOCAL AGREEMENT
County Administrator Hammond discussed a proposed Interlocal agreement with the
City of Port St. Joe <copy provided to the Clerk for the record>; requesting the Board to
approve this Interlocal agreement to submit to the City (of Port St. Joe). Commissioner
McCroan so moved. Commissioner Rich seconded the motion. Upon inquiry by
Commissioner McDaniel, County Administrator Hammond reported that the adoption of
this agreement needs to be quick as the current D.R.A. (Downtown Redevelopment
Agency) ends at the end of the year; stating that based on this Interlocal agreement
there will not be an extension or a new D.R.A. for a period of twenty (20) years.
Chairman Quinn called for public comment. There being no public comment, the motion
passed unanimously.
RFQ – QUALIFIED CONTRACTORS
County Administrator Hammond discussed working through Horne, LLP to do a
prequalified bidder process for the repairs needed to the County buildings due
Hurricane Michael. He requested Board approval to advertise to receive sealed bids for
qualified contractors. Commissioner Rogers so moved. Commissioner McDaniel
seconded the motion. Chairman Quinn called for public comment. There being no public
comment, the motion passed unanimously.

GRANT ACCEPTANCE – APPROVALS
County Assistant Administrator Yeager reported that a grant from Visit Florida, a

Commissioner Rogers motioned to accept the grant award. Commissioner McCroan
seconded the motion. Chairman Quinn called for public comment. There being no public
comment, the motion passed unanimously.

REJECT BID #1819-16 – ¾ TON PICKUP TRUCK
E9-1-1 Coordinator Guthrie appeared before the Board to request the Board reject Bid

County Administrator Hammond reported that the private road right-of-entry is
approved, but the private property right-of-entry has not yet been approved. Alex
Yarbrough, with Horne, LLP appeared before the Board to provide an update regarding
the storm debris removal on private roads and working on obtaining approval on private
properties. County Administrator Hammond reported that F.E.M.A. is requesting that the
private property sites be pre-vetted. Upon inquiry by Commissioner Rich, County
Administrator Hammond stated that Metric will be pre-vetting for the private property
right-of-entry program. Discussion followed regarding legal dumping. County
Administrator Hammond stated that he does not expect any more extensions for placing
debris on the roadside.
County Administrator Hammond discussed an email from U.S. Treasury regarding the
purchase of the bank building for the Sheriff’s Office <copy provided to the Clerk for the
record>; reporting that they are not anticipating to make the award prior to February 28,
2019. He stated that the seller (Richard Hinds) does not want to extend; requesting the
Board advertise for sealed bids for short-term financing up to $550,000.00 up to 180
days to complete this purchase; waiving the bid requirements and do a seven (7) day,
under an emergency situation. Administrator Hammond noted the interest on a short-
term long would be cheaper than the lease payments. Following further discussion on
the history of this matter, Commissioner Rich made the motion. Commissioner
McDaniel seconded the motion. Chairman Quinn called for public comment. County
Administrator Hammond requested for Commissioner Rich add to his motion to allow
the Chairman to execute any required loan documents as needed. Commissioner Rich
had no objection. Chairman Quinn called for public comment. There being no public
comment, the motion passed unanimously.
County Administrator Hammond discussed the Honeyville Borrow Pit sand excavation
project; reporting that the Contractor (Roberts & Roberts, Inc. * Bid #1819-10) is going
to put a dredge in within the next few days.
County Administrator Hammond addressed the Boards need to decide on what to apply
for (projects) through Triumph. County Assistant Administrator Yeager reported that the
Triumph Board approved a $15 million set-aside for ad valorem tax loss within the four

(4) County region due to impact of Hurricane Michael (Bay, Gulf, Franklin, and Wakulla).
He stated that Wakulla County is not going to lose any (ad valorem tax), Franklin
County will lose no more than $1 million, and Bay County (excluding municipalities)
would prefer not to use their Triumph money for ad valorem tax loss. County Assistant
Administrator Yeager reported that Gulf County has submitted a pre-application for the
ad valorem tax loss; stating the request was for over three (3) years, including all three
(3) taxing authorities, in the amount of $20 million. He stated that he will provide further
information at the next regular meeting; reporting that the discussion has been for Gulf
County to maintain the County’s current millage rate this year and whatever that loss is,
that this does not generate, will be the dollar amount asked for from Triumph. Following
discussion, County Administrator Hammond recommended, at a minimum, the Board
back off the last year, and ask for the current and the year starting October 1, which
would be $12 to $15 million and with no loan. After discussion, Commissioner McCroan
motioned to ask for $15 million (from the Triumph Board for ad valorem tax loss for the
County, the School Board and Cities). Commissioner McDaniel seconded the motion.
Chairman Quinn called for public comment. There being no public comment, the motion
passed unanimously.
County Administrator Hammond reported that the Pot III’s (the environmental pot)
number one project is septic to sewer; discussing the Beaches Sewer Plant at Gulf Aire.
He suggested the Board reply to the City’s (of Port St. Joe) request to redirect these
funds, stating these funds are not allowed to be spent on rehabbing lift stations in the
City of Port St. Joe for routine maintenance; noting these funds are for environmental
enhancements. County Assistant Yeager discussed the original plan was to use these
funds to do away with the Beaches (Gulf Aire) Sewer Plant. Commissioner McCroan
stated he had no objection to the City (of Port St. Joe) taking that system offline;
discussing his concerns regarding the City of Port St. Joe obtaining any more debt.
County Administrator Hammond discussed a second option would be to suggest to the
City of Port St. Joe that the County would pay to take that plant offline and the owner
would work out an agreement with the City of Port St. Joe to pay (using Pot III Money)
for the affluent to be treated in the City of Port St. Joe. Following discussion, County
Administrator Hammond suggested holding a workshop with the Municipalities to
discuss authorized project options available under Pot III (environmental) money. After
further discussion, County Administrator Hammond requested a motion of the Board to
meet with the City (Port St. Joe) and make them this offer; to just take the affluent; in
addition to that no cost, and potentially have a workshop with both municipalities in the
next month or so. Commissioner Rogers so moved. Commissioner McCroan seconded
the motion. Chairman Quinn called for public comment. There being no public comment,
the motion passed unanimously.
