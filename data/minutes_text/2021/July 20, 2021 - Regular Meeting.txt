County Attorney Novak presented a proposed ordinance before the Board <copy

Novak stated it would not, as this park is preexisting, and the County has the authority to
make it exclusive to this ordinance. Chairman Quinn called for public comment. Fran

East 30A in the County’s ordinance for RV pole barns and mobile homes. In response,

for a motion to move forward. Commissioner McCroan so moved. Commissioner Farrell

PUBLIC HEARING – RECREATIONAL VEHICLES ORDINANCE
County Attorney Novak reported that this is the second Public Hearing, then read the
proposed ordinance regarding the Gulf County recreational vehicles by title for the Board
to consider. He reported the Public Hearing has been advertised; stating the affected
areas would be the open coastal waters of Gulf County, essentially Cape San Blas, Indian
Pass and St. Joe Beach. Chairman Quinn called for public comment. Attorney Patrick
Floyd, representing residents of Indian Pass area appeared before the Board to thank the
Board for their efforts regarding this proposed ordinance. He discussed concerns
regarding RVs (recreational vehicles) as habitable structures, asking the Board to be in
support of a vote for this proposed ordinance. Chairman Quinn called for additional public
comment. Tamatha Stienwalks, of St. Joe Beach appeared before the Board to discuss
her concerns regarding the grandfather clause, zoning, and excluded areas within the
County. Charles Messer, of St. Joe Beach appeared to discuss the advertisement placed
in the newspaper with the July 27, 2021 hearing date; stating the meeting was changed
to July 20, 2021. He then discussed his concerns regarding the proposed ordinance and
the grandfather clause. County Attorney Novak reported that the Public Hearing was
originally advertised for July 27, 2021, but the Board voted to move the meeting to the
20th of July, therefore staff readvertised the notice, in addition to being on the County
website, YouTube and posted on the County buildings (Courthouse and Administration)
doors. He stated the County has met the statutory requirements; discussing the penalties
listed within the proposed ordinance. County Attorney Novak reported that if a property is
owned by a current landowner, the County’s ordinance is not restricting someone’s ability
going forward from living in these areas, stating if you have an established use and you
use your property for that RV (recreational vehicle), then you can continue to live in that.
County Attorney Novak stated that there are three (3) ways the grandfather clause goes
away, the parcel owner; (1) passes away, (2) sale of the property, or (3) by violating the
mandatory evacuation; noting that Administration and Code Enforcement will deal with
enforcement, and it will be the property owner that will be in violation if the RVs are not
removed during a mandatory evacuation. County Administrator Hammond discussed
concerns regarding recent problems with advertisements in the newspapers. Chairman
Quinn called for public comment. Robert Griffin, of Oak Grove appeared before the Board
to inquire about what happens to the lot after the grandfather clause is lost. County
Attorney Novak stated that it is an exceptional use and if you are not grandfathered in,
you are no longer in Gulf County allowed to pull an RV onto a parcel and live in a
recreational vehicle in these zones. County Administrator Hammond reported that Mr.
Griffin is not in the zone, therefore his property is not affected by the proposed ordinance.
Upon inquiry by Mr. Griffin, County Attorney Novak stated that the grandfather (clause) is
for the immediate owner. Greg Setrich, of St. Joe Beach appeared to discuss concerns
regarding mandatory evacuation for RVs owners, property rights, and this proposed
ordinance. Following discussion, Dan Hauversburk, of Beacon Hill appeared before the
Board to discuss his concerns regarding the proposed ordinance and RVs in his
neighborhood. Lynn Wilkinson, of Beacon Hill appeared before the Board to speak in
opposition to the proposed ordinance. Upon motion by Commissioner Rich, second by
Commissioner Farrell, and unanimous vote, the Board extended Mr. Wilkinson’s time to

speak. Mr. Wilkinson’s discussed the Beacon Hill area and the grandfather clause;
requesting that the Board vote not to adopt this proposed ordinance. Following additional
discussion, Chairman Quinn called for public comment. Debra Neel, of Beacon Hill
appeared before the Board to state that she is against this proposed ordinance. County
Attorney Novak discussed Section 6 of the proposed ordinance regarding the restrictions
and exceptions of RVs. Tim Finnerty, of Beacon Hill appeared to state that he is in support
of this proposed ordinance, making some suggestions regarding the influx of RVs on U.S.
Highway 98. He presented photos of RVs on U.S. Highway 98 <copies provided to the
Clerk for the record>; discussing concern regarding property values and decreased new
construction. Upon motion by Commissioner McCroan, second by Commissioner Farrell,
and unanimous vote, the Board extended Mr. Finnerty’s time to speak. Mr. Finnerty
voiced concerns regarding tax assessment and loss revenue to the County. He
recommended to include no RVs along U.S. Highway 98 and the grandfather Section 6-
B1 to be retroactively changed to October 1, 2018, before Hurricane Michael. Coastal
Community Association President Pat Hardman appeared before the Board in support of
this proposed ordinance; providing an assessment report from Property Appraiser Burke
<copy provided to the Clerk for the record> for review. Trell Neel, of Indian Pass appeared
before the Board in support of this proposed ordinance. Commissioner Farrell motioned
to allow Mr. Neel additional time to speak. Commissioner McCroan seconded the motion,
and it passed unanimously. Mr. Neel continued to discuss his support of passing this
proposed ordinance. Pat Kavanaugh, of Indian Pass appeared before the Board in
support of the proposed ordinance, thanking the Board for moving forward. Butch Kline
appeared in support of this proposed ordinance. Ben Pridgeon, of St. Joe Beach
appeared before the Board to discuss his concerns regarding the proposed ordinance;
stating the County should make the proposed ordinance for those areas that want it, south
beach, Indian Pass and the Cape <copy of his letter was provided to the Clerk for the
record>. Scott Taranto, of Cape San Blas appeared to discuss his property on Cape San
Blas Road and his concerns regarding this proposed ordinance; requesting the Board
allow those that purchased property to finish their project. Ken Wadleigh, of Beacon Hill
appeared to discuss his concerns regarding the proposed ordinance, stating he is not in
support of this proposed ordinance. Fran Vellanti, of Cape San Blas appeared before the
Board. County Attorney Novak requested a recess from the Chairman after Ms. Vellanti
speaks. Ms. Vellanti discussed her concerns regarding easy evacuation routes,
requesting that 30A be included in the proposed ordinance.
The meeting did recess at 10:47 a.m., E.T.

HURRICANE LITIGATION MITIGATION PROGRAM – HURRICANE MICHAEL
County Attorney Novak requested Board authority to approve an affidavit that will be
signed by all qualified applicants upon receiving the H.L.M.P. (Hurricane Litigation
Mitigation Program) funds and recorded in the Official Records. He requested a vote of
the Board to require each H.L.M.P. applicant to sign this affidavit and acknowledgement.
Commissioner Farrell so moved. Commissioner McCroan seconded the motion.
Chairman Quinn called for public comment. There being no public comment, the motion
passed unanimously.
PUBLIC SAFETY – U.S. HIGHWAY 98
Tim Finnerty, of Beacon Hill appeared before the Board to discuss his concerns regarding
crossing U.S. Highway 98 going to the beach. He provided some solutions to this issue:
1. inquiring if it could be labeled as a pedestrian yield of right-of-way; 2. certain designated
areas for pedestrians are expected to cross; 3. signage with a report tool for those
vehicles that do not slow down; 4. moving U.S. Highway 98 back for commercial traffic
and leaving ‘old 98’ as a residential area only; and 5. labeling the second highway as a
hurricane evacuation route. Commissioner McCroan motioned to extend Mr. Finnerty’s
time to speak. Motioned failed for a lack of a second. <Commissioner Rich left the meeting
at 11:47 a.m., E.T.>
PUBLIC SAFETY – 30A EAST
Fran Vellanti, of Cape San Blas appeared before the Board to report there is a blind spot
at the new construction area on County Road 30A East entering the County line,
discussing her concerns regarding the speeding vehicles and recent car accident at this
site. She discussed concerns regarding a new strain of heartworms growing; stating that
the contractor should be made to install storm drains. <Commissioner Rich returned to
the meeting at 11:49 a.m., E.T.> Ms. Vellanit reported that she has called the Health
Department and they are working on it. She also discussed her concerns regarding the
debris in the ditches from commercial businesses in this area.
RECREATIONAL VEHICLE PARK – BEACON HILL
Christine Malorano, of Beacon Hill appeared before the Board in inquire about the
recently tabled RV Park at Veterans’ Memorial Beacon Hill Park. County Administrator
Hammond stated when the Board wants to bring it back up, then it will come back up.
Following discussion, she then inquired how this RV Park will be grandfathered in with
the newly adopted Ordinance. County Attorney Novak stated that there is language in the
Ordinance that speaks to preexisting parks in the County, and they will be exempt from
this Ordinance as long as they are in compliance with it first, also the Board has the
authority to create parks, recreational and pickle ball courts under the Land Bureau
Management grant awarded to the County that allows the Board to put these items in
their parks in this corridor.

LOTTERY DRAWING – H.H.R.P. FUNDING ROUND 2
S.H.I.P. Directors Joe Paul and Lynn Lanier, along with Gulf County Tax Collector Shirley
Jenkins appeared before the Board to draw applicant names for the H.H.R.P. Funding
Round 2 (Hurricane Michael). S.H.I.P. Director Paul reported that names will be drawn by
District and by income level. Tax Collector Jenkins then pulled numbers by each District
and by each income category, while S.H.I.P. Director Lanier called aloud the names
assigned to each number pulled.
There being no further business, and upon motion by Commissioner McCroan, second
by Commissioner Farrell, and unanimous vote, the meeting did then adjourn at 12:05
p.m., E.T.
