PRISON UPDATE
County Administrator Hammond discussed the need to send letters to Representative

CITY OF PORT ST. JOE ANNEXATION
County Administrator Hammond stated that he doesn’t think the development is possible
at this location (site near County Public Works Building); discussing his concerns
regarding the traffic dumping out on a congested sub-standard County road. He stated
the biggest problem is not necessarily the density, it is the wetland credit; reporting that if
the City (of Port St. Joe) would have agreed to the County’s density and County wetland
transfer, which is one to five, they could get approximately 88 units on that property, now
the City will get somewhere between 350-500 units and there is no way 10th Street can
take that traffic, even if some come up Knowles (Avenue). County Attorney Novak
reported that time is running out for the Board to challenge the annexation; stating he will
need a vote of the Board to challenge it. Commissioner McDaniel discussed the joint
workshop held today; expressing his concerns regarding the annexation, density,
wetlands, and access to the property. Commissioner McCroan stated he willing to
compromise with eight units per acre instead of four, but cannot support fifteen; noting
the need to work together and not have to litigate this. Commissioner Farrell discussed
his concerns regarding the density deal; wishing the County and City could come to an
agreement. Chairman Quinn discussed the deadline to challenge and trying to
compromise on density; stating the County needs to try to find a compromise before the
deadline to challenge it. County Attorney Novak stated this is the County’s only
opportunity to evaluate and examine the issues; being limited in their challenges to the
issue of municipal annexation, the County having asked the questions and not receiving
the answers regarding the future and growth of this annexation. Chairman Quinn stated
it is not that the County does not want affordable housing, the issue is density of the
property. County Administrator Hammond reported that Gateway Manor was supposed
to be workforce housing but ended up being low-income housing. Upon inquiry by
Chairman Quinn regarding a recommendation, County Administrator Hammond noted he
does not get a vote; stating his order from the Board is to oppose any annexation that are
not mutually agreed upon by the City of Wewahitchka or the City of Port St. Joe. He stated
this is the Board’s last opportunity to stop the annexation; discussing his concerns with
the City’s annexation, the developers, and high density. Following discussion,
Commissioner McDaniel placed a motion on the floor that the Board challenge this
annexation. Commissioner Rich seconded the motion. After additional discussion,
Chairman Quinn called for public comment. There was no public comment. County
Attorney Novak requested a motion that would include the Chairman’s authority to sign a
Resolution and authorizing the County staff to take those legal steps; noting he will
prepare the Resolution and provide it to the Clerk. Commissioner McDaniel included
Attorney Novak’s recommendation in the motion. Commissioner Rich stated his second
stands. Chairman Quinn called for public comment. There being none, the motion then
passed unanimously.

GULF COUNTY WATER DEPARTMENT UPDATE
County Consultant Butler appeared before the Board to provide a status report <copy
provided to the Clerk for the record> regarding the Gulf County Water Department. Upon
inquiry by Chairman Quinn, County Administrator Hammond discussed the confusion
regarding the boil water notice; reporting the boil water notice was not for all of Gulf
County, just for the Gulf County Water Department (LUCI-Lighthouse Utilities Company,
Inc.). Upon inquiry by Commissioner McCroan, Josh Baxley, of Dewberry appeared
before the Board to discuss the timetable of the boil water notice; reporting that the County
is waiting to receive the lab results from the water testing from D.E.P. (Florida Department
of Environmental Projection). Discussion followed regarding water customers registering
for Alert Gulf and putting the registration information on customers’ water bill. County
Administrator Hammond thanked County Consultant Butler for his service in working on
the Water Department. Pat Hardman, of Coastal Community Association appeared before
the Board to state that information needs to get back out that the boil water notice is just
for Lighthouse Utilities; asking when customers will know that the notice is lifted. County
Administrator Hammond stated they will be notified through a press release and a notice
will be posted on the County’s website. Ms. Hardman requested that this notice be posted
on the Fire Department’s website; reporting that Coastal Community Association will also
get the word out.
