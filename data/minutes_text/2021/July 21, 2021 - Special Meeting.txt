JULY 21, 2021
SPECIAL MEETING
PORT ST. JOE, FLORIDA
The Gulf County Board of County Commissioners met this date in special session with
the following members present: Chairman Sandy Quinn, Jr., Vice Chairman Phil
McCroan, and Commissioners David C. Rich, Ward McDaniel, and Patrick Ferrell.
Others present were: County Attorney Jeremy Novak, Clerk Rebecca L. Norris, Director
of Finance & Management Amy Ludlam, Deputy Clerk Leanna Roberts, County
Administrator Michael L. Hammond, Deputy Administrator Kari Summers, Building Official
Brad Bailey, Emergency Management Director/Fire Coordinator Matt Herring, Gulf
County E.M.S. Director Jody Daniels, Grant Administrator Kaci Rhodes, Grant
Administrator Maegen Conners, Human Resource Director Brett Lowry, Information
Technology Dennis Barfield, Sr., Office of Management & Budget Director Sherry Herring,
County Planner Lee Collinsworth, Property Appraiser Mitch Burke, Property Appraiser
Office IT Tech Dennis Barfield, Jr. Public Works Director Mark Cothran, Assistant Public
Works Director Raymond Hart, Sheriff Mike Harrison, Sheriff's Department Captain Chris
Buchanan, Supervisor of Elections John Hanlon, and T.D.C. Executive Director Kelli
Godwin.
Chairman Quinn called the meeting to order at 9:01 a.m., E.T.
Chairman Quinn opened the meeting with the Pledge of Allegiance to the Flag.
ADMINISTRATIVE UPDATE & REPORT
County Administrator Hammond presented a memorandum recommending the Board
approve the $15.00 increase (County contribution) to HSA (Health Savings Account).
Commissioner McCroan so moved. Commissioner McDaniel seconded the motion.
Chairman Quinn called for public comment. There being no public comment, the motion
passed unanimously.
County Administrator Hammond discussed receiving Bid #2021-30 for Workers’
Compensation, Property and Casualty Insurance Coverage; stating that staff wants the
option to go back to the County’s original $2 million liability policy for general liability and
public officers’ risk. Dwight Van Lierop, of Acentria Public Risk appeared before the Board
to discuss the policy plan. Human Resource Director Lowry appeared before the Board
to recommending to the Board, if it is a slight increase, going to the $2 million for cyber
(security coverage). County Administrator Hammond reported this bid does not include
replacement for windstorm on docks and dune walkovers, etc. Human Resource Director
confirmed that that is correct; noting if the County has a vessel that hits a County dock,
then the County would be covered, but any kind of windstorm event is not covered. He
reported if the Board desires to have this coverage, it will be over $50,000.00. County

Administrator Hammond stated that if the Board is good with not having the windstorm on
the docks and accessories, but going to the $2 million on the cyber, staff would
recommend the low bid of Acentria Public Risk, through Florida County’s Trust. Chairman
Quinn called for a motion to go with the low bidder. Commissioner Rich so moved.
Commissioner McCroan seconded the motion. Chairman Quinn called for public
comment. There being no public comment, the motion passed unanimously.
TENTATIVE 2021-22 BUDGET
County Administrator Hammond provided the Board with an overview of the tentative
2021-22 budget. He then provided highlights throughout the tentative budget by line item.
Discussion followed regarding park expenses. Chairman Quinn stated that all members
of the Board play a part in Veterans’ Memorial Beacon Hill Park flags; suggesting that the
$15,000.00 (cost for the flags) be split between the five (5) Districts. County Administrator
Hammond completed the highlights of remaining budgeted line item. OMB Director
Herring discussed the balanced budget for the new year; reporting the Revenues and
Expenditures by line item will still change as the County is still waiting on some revenue
estimates that are provided by the State. She requested the Board allow staff to make
Cash Carry Forward related adjustments, Revenue Estimating Conference (REC)
revenue changes, line item adjustments for the various expenditures, and other changes
that do not affect ad valorem.
The meeting recess at 9:39 a.m., E.T.
The meeting did then reconvene at 9:44 a.m., E.T.
OMB Director Herring requested permission from the Board to make line item
adjustments that do not affect ad valorem between now and the first hearing for the
tentative budget. Chairman Quinn called for a motion to move forward with that.
Commissioner McCroan so moved. Commissioner Rich seconded the motion. Chairman
Quinn called for public comment. There being no public comment, the motion passed
unanimously. OMB Director Herring requested the Board set the Countywide mileage rate
at 6.8, the four (4) Special Dependent Fire Districts at .5 mills, and the Gulf Front M.S.T.U.
at .8537, Interior at .7065, and Bayside at .7115 mills. County Administrator Hammond
requested the Board wait (on the vote) to allow the Constitutional Officer’s time to speak
on their budgets.
Clerk Norris first introduced Amy Ludlam as the new Finance Director for the Clerk. She
stated the Clerk’s budget request includes COLA (cost of living) increases, and retirement
and insurance adjustments; respectfully requesting the Board’s approval.
On behalf of Tax Collector Shirley Jenkins, who was not able to attend, Clerk Norris stated
the Tax Collector has the same budget request (which includes COLA increases,
retirement, and insurance adjustments).
Supervisor of Elections Hanlon appeared before the Board to state that his budget is good
as it is set for now.

Property Appraiser Burke appeared before the Board to report that his budget had a small
increase in their EDP software, in the amount of $3,500.00.
Sheriff Harrison appeared before the Board to request two (2) positions; discussing the
current staffing of officers and his concerns regarding eligibility for grant funding due to
the matching funds needed.
Chairman Quinn called for public comment. Gretchen Mayes, of Beacon Hill appeared
before the Board to support Sheriff Harrison’s budget request for at least two (2) positions;
requesting the Board to please find the funding. She discussed concerns regarding the
E.M.S. services, requesting the Board support funding these two departments. Chairman
Quinn called for additional public comment. There was no further public comment.
OMB Director Herring requested the Board to approve setting these mileage rates.
Commissioner McCroan discussed concerns regarding the increase traffic in the County.
Discussion followed regarding traffic, law enforcement, and E.M.S. Upon inquiry by
Chairman Quinn, OMB Director Herring stated that the Countywide Mileage is 6.800, the
four (4) fire districts are at .5000 mils each, Gulf front M.S.T.U. is .8537, M.S.T.U. Interior
is .7065, and M.S.T.U. Bayside is .7115 mils. She reported that last year the mileage
(rate) was 7.100 (Countywide). Discussion followed regarding last year’s M.S.T.U. rates.
Chairman Quinn called for a motion. Commissioner McDaniel moved to set the mileage
rate at 6.800, the fire districts staying the same, and follow the beach rates. Commissioner
Farrell seconded the motion. Chairman Quinn called for public comment. There being no
public comment, the motion passed unanimously.
OMB Director Herring requested the Board set the dates for the Public Hearings for the
first hearing to be held on September 8, 2021 at 5:01 p.m., E.T. Commissioner Farrell so
moved. Commissioner McCroan seconded the motion. Chairman Quinn called for public
comment. There being no public comment, the motion passed unanimously. OMB
Director Herring requested permission to hold the second and final hearing on September
20, 2021 at 5:01 p.m., E.T. Commissioner McCroan so moved. Commissioner Farrell
seconded the motion. Chairman Quinn called for public comment. There being no public
comment, the motion passed unanimously. OMB Director Herring reported that the Board
will receive a tentative budget book prior to the first hearing, notice will be posted and
advertised according to TRIM guidelines.
ADMINISTRATIVE UPDATE & REPORT
County Administrator Hammond discussed looking at avenues to fund the fuel center if
the C.D.B.G.R. or Hazard Mitigation grant appeal is denied.

There being no further business, and upon motion by Commissioner Farrell, second by
Commissioner McCroan, and unanimous vote, the meeting did then adjourn at 10:11
a.m., E.T.
