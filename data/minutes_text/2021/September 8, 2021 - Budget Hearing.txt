SEPTEMBER 8, 2021
BUDGET HEARING
PORT ST. JOE, FLORIDA
The Gulf County Board of County Commissioner met this date in special budget session
with the following members present: Chairman Sandy Quinn, Jr., Vice Chairman Phil
McCroan, and Commissioners David C. Rich, Ward McDaniel, and Patrick Ferrell.
Others present were: Director of Finance & Management Director Amy Ludlam, Deputy
Clerk Leanna Roberts, County Administrator Michael L. Hammond, County Assistant
Administrator Clay Smallwood, III, Deputy Administrator Kari Summers, Building Official
Brad Bailey, Gulf County E.D.C. Director Jim McKnight, Grant Administrator Kaci
Rhodes, Office of Management & Budget Director Sherry Herring, Mosquito Control
Director Austin Horton, Public Works Director Mark Cothran, Sheriff Mike Harrison, and
Supervisor of Elections John Hanlon.
Chairman Quinn called for the meeting to order at 5:01 p.m., E.T.
TENTATIVE 2021-22 OVERVIEW
OMB Director Herring noted that each member of the Board had been provided a copy of
the proposed budget book and it was also posted to the County’s website, as required by
TRIM <copy provided to the Clerk for the record>. She reported the following information
to the Board: 1. proposed aggregate millage rate is 7.1929, which is 5.32% above the
current year aggregate rolled back rate of 6.8293; 2. County-wide proposed tentative
millage rate is 6.8000; 3. Dependent Fire Districts: St. Joseph, Tupelo, Overstreet and
Howard Creek are all set at 0.5000 mills; and 4. Proposed M.S.T.U. Debt Service millage
rates are: Gulfside Beachfront at 0.8537, Gulfside Interior at 0.7065 and Bayside at
0.7115. OMB Director Herring stated that this is the maximum millage rate that the Board
can adopt, then provided reasons the Ad Valorem Tax Revenues are increasing, as
follows: 1. Sheriff’s Office; 2. FRS retirement cost; 3. health insurance cost; 4. pay raises;
5. capital outlay; 6. IT Department; 7. Building and Planning Departments; and 8. EMS
Services. She provided additional information to the Board, as follows: 1. page 4 provides
the balanced totals of each fund; 2. Insurance settlement funds have been carried forward
and reserved until those expenditures are incurred due to the unknown timing of many
repair projects; 3. Growth and stability of reserves are necessary to maintain and improve
our credit worthiness on current and future bond issues; 4. Must continue to cash flow the
anticipated reimbursements for insurance deductible and improvements, not covered by
F.E.M.A. or insurance with the reserves. OMB Director Herring noted the Board may
amend the tentative budget at tonight’s Hearing, but any increases in expenditures will
require equivalent reductions specified by line-item. She stated that she has a two-change
request: 1. The book the Board received actually has four (4) amended pages from what
is posted on the County’s website, being an adjustment on pages 7, 9, 11 and 12 to
balance the transfers in and out between funds. OMB Director Herring requested Board

approval to this correction which increased General Fund transfer out by $12,495.00 and
decreased Reserve for Contingencies by $12,495.00. Chairman Quinn called for a motion
to move forward, approving these corrections. Commissioner McCroan motioned to
approve. Commissioner Farrell seconded the motion. Chairman Quinn called for public
comment. There being no public comment, the motion passed unanimously. 2. (second
request) allow staff, between now and the Final Budget Hearing, to make line-item
adjustments to expenditures within each fund that do not change the overall fund totals.
Chairman Quinn called for a motion to move forward. Commissioner McDaniel so moved
for the line-item adjustments. Commissioner McCroan seconded the motion. Chairman
Quinn called for public comment. There being no public comment, the motion passed
unanimously.
PUBLIC COMMENT
Chairman Quinn called for public comment. There being no public comment, Chairman
Quinn closed the Public Hearing to the public for comment.
TENTATIVE 2021-22 OVERVIEW
OMB Director Herring restated the County’s millage rates for Countywide at 6.8000; with
the aggregate millage rate being 7.1929, which is greater than the rollback rate of 6.8293
by 5.32%.

FINAL BUDGET HEARING – SEPTEMBER 27, 2021
OMB Director Herring reminded the Board that the Final Budget Hearing is set for
Monday, September 27, 2021 at 5:01 p.m., E.T. She stated that it will be advertised in the
newspaper, as required by TRIM.
TENTATIVE 2021-22 BUDGET
County Administrator Hammond stated that this is the first time the County has been able
to cut taxes since the economic meltdown; reporting that had the County been able to
settle with the County’s insurance company (PRM) the County may have been able to
fund more things like the Sherriff’s Office and Supervisor of Elections Office. He reported
the County was unable to settle in mediation, therefore it looks like the County will be
going to court; stating that the insurance company owes the County $2.6 million.
LOCAL STATE OF EMERGENCY – TROPICAL SYSTEM MINDY
County Administrator Hammond reported that according to Emergency Management
Director Matt Herring Tropical System Mindy will hit between 2:00 a.m. and 4:00 a.m.,
expecting another six (6”) inches of rain. He recommended, out of an abundance of
caution, that the Board declare a Local State of Emergency. County Administrator
Hammond stated that at this time it is unknown what the School System will do. Chairman
Quinn called for a motion to declare a Local State of Emergency. Commissioner McCroan
so moved. Commissioner Farrell seconded the motion. Chairman Quinn called for public
comment. There being no public comment, the motion passed unanimously.

Commissioner McCroan, and unanimous vote, the meeting did then adjourn at 5:16 a.m.,
