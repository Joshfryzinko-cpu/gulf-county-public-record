OCTOBER 27, 2021
WORKSHOP
PORT ST. JOE, FLORIDA
The Gulf County Board of County Commissioners met this date in a workshop with the
following members present: Chairman Sandy Quinn, Jr., Vice Chairman Phil McCroan,
and Commissioners Ward McDaniel and Patrick Ferrell <Commissioner David C. Rich
was not present>.
Others present were: County Attorney Jeremy Novak, Clerk Rebecca L. Norris, County
Administrator Michael L. Hammond, County Assistant Administrator Clay Smallwood, III,
Deputy Administrator Kari Summers, Gulf County E.D.C. Director Jim McKnight, Grant
Administrator Kaci Rhodes, Grant Administrator Maegen Conners, and Office of
Management & Budget Director Sherry Herring.
Also present were: Bryan Crowe and Doug Maggio of Fresenius, Representatives from
Franklin County Chairman Ricky Jones and Administrator Michael Moron, David Walker
the CEO of Weems Memorial Hospital, Port St. Joe City Mayor Rex Buzzett, Ascension
Sacred Heart President Henry Stovall and Vice President of Nursing Leigha Thursby
<both appearing by phone>, Ray Roton and Charles Perry of Cypress Development, and
Dan Velazques of St. Joe Company.
E.D.C. Director McKnight called the meeting to order at 1:00 p.m., E.T.
DIALYSIS CENTER
E.D.C. Director McKnight announced the attendees present, thanking all those in
attendance. He reported that the dialysis center (Davita Dialysis Center) that was located
in Port St. Joe for ten years closed down March 31, 2021. Doug Maggio, of Fresenius
discussed the possibility of opening a dialysis center in Gulf County and read Fresenius’
mission statement. Discussions followed regarding the Fresenius facility and quality of
life for patients. Upon inquiry by Commissioner McDaniel, Charles Perry, of Cypress
Development discussed the building space, previously used by Davita, stating that they
are in discussion with Fresenius about renting this space for a dialysis facility. Discussion
followed regarding marketing options for the center. County Administrator Hammond
asked how Gulf County could help to get this facility online. Mr. Maggio discussed that
the goal would be to get the hospitals involved, looking to sign a five-year lease with five-
year option, and getting the area doctors to refer patients. Discussion followed regarding
financial support and the need for partnerships. Scared Heart Hospital President Henry
Stovall stated that the hospital is willing to have conversations with Fresenius regarding
a joint venture. Discussion followed regarding transportation for dialysis patients. Mr.
Maggio stated that they will go back and run the numbers and present a proposal within
the next few weeks.

There being no further discussion, the workshop did adjourn at 1:41 p.m., E.T.
