SEPTEMBER 8, 2021
SPECIAL MEETING
PORT ST. JOE, FLORIDA
The Gulf County Board of County Commissioners met this date in special session with
the following members present: Chairman Sandy Quinn, Jr., Vice Chairman Phil
McCroan, and Commissioners David C. Rich, Ward McDaniel, and Patrick Ferrell.
Others present were: Director of Finance & Management Director Amy Ludlam, Deputy
Clerk Leanna Roberts, County Administrator Michael L. Hammond, County Assistant
Administrator Clay Smallwood, III, Deputy Administrator Kari Summers, Building Official
Brad Bailey, Gulf County E.D.C. Director Jim McKnight, Grant Administrator Kaci
Rhodes, Office of Management & Budget Director Sherry Herring, Mosquito Control
Director Austin Horton, Public Works Director Mark Cothran, and Sheriff Mike Harrison.
Sheriff Harrison called the meeting to order at 4:30 p.m., E.T.
Chairman Quinn opened the meeting with the Pledge of Allegiance to the Flag.
HIGHLAND VIEW SCHOOL STORMWATER PIPE
Public Works Director Cothran discussed the privately owned property (Tripletail RV
Resort, LLC * Parcel ID #04272-000R) located at the old Highland View School site,
stating there is a catch-box on Red Fish Street that takes a pipe underground on the north
side of this property all the way over to the ditch. He reported that the County does not
have an easement, but the Developer has agreed to a fifteen-foot (15”) easement to allow
the County to install new pipe from Red Fish Street all the way to the big ditch. Public
Works Director Cothran recommended the Board suspend the Bid Policy (purchase is
over the bid limit) to allow staff to order the pipe, stating the County will install the pipe.
After discussion regarding this project, Public Works Director Cothran stated the County
is looking at $26,000.00 worth of pipe. Upon inquiry by Commissioner Rich regarding a
timeline, Public Works Director Cothran discussed the delay in finding a vendor with the
pipe in stock, but if they can order it tomorrow, the County will be looking at a six (6) week
project. County Administrator Hammond requested the motion include that this purchase
be paid from Secondary Road and Bridge fund. Chairman Quinn called for a motion to
suspend the Bid Policy. Commissioner McCroan so moved. Commissioner Farrell
seconded the motion. Chairman Quinn called for public comment. There being no public
comment, the motion passed unanimously. Chairman Quinn called for a motion to move
forward and pay for the pipe out of Secondary Road and Bridge. Commissioner Farrell so
moved. Commissioner Rich seconded the motion. Chairman Quinn called for public
comment. There being no public comment, the motion passed unanimously.

C.D.B.G.-DR PRIORITIES
County Administrator Hammond provided a proposed list of priorities for the C.D.B.G.-DR
(Community Development Block Grant-Disaster Recovery) Round 2 Projects <copy
provided to the Clerk for the record>, recommending the Board make the White City
Water Plant Project as their top priority and the dredging project as a possible add-on to
the list. He reported that the dredging project is on the Triumph Board’s (agenda) for
Monday’s meeting; noting that it looks positive. Following discussion regarding the
proposed list, County Administrator Hammond recommended the Board turn in the
dredge project but make the water project number one (#1). Chairman Quinn stated the
proposed list ranking as follows: 1. White City Water Plant Project; 2. Channel Dredging;
3. Advanced Wastewater Treatment Facility Project; and 4. Airport Runway Paving
Project. County Administrator Hammond stated that he does not think it matters how the
County ranks them after #1 (number one), but the #1 project would be White City Water
Plant Project. He stated the water plant and wastewater will rank higher because the of
specifics in the grading criteria. Commissioner McCroan inquired about the commitment
of the dredging project from the Triumph Board. Matt Terry, of Port St. Joe and member
of the Triumph Board appeared to say he cannot speak for the other board members, but
he has been involved and this project looks very favorable from his standpoint. Upon
inquiry by Chairman Quinn, County Administrator Hammond recommended that the
Board motion to move forward on four projects: water plant, dredging, wastewater, and
airport; ranking the water project as number one (#1). Chairman Quinn then called for a
motion. Commissioner McDaniel so moved. Commissioner McCroan seconded the
motion. Chairman Quinn called for public comment. There being no public comment, the
motion passed unanimously.
ADMINISTRATIVE UPDATE & REPORT
County Administrator Hammond stated that, due to the deadline, the County applied for
dredging money through the Resilient Florida Grant, recommending the Board approve
these projects. Grant Administrator Rhodes requested the Board ratify the projects
submitted for the Resilient Florida Grant Program, as follows: 1. White City Water Plant;
2. Road and Stormwater Project; 3. Advanced Wastewater Project; 4. Airport Runway
Paving Project; 5. Jail Improvements Project; and 6. Channel Dredging Project. Chairman
Quinn called for a motion to ratify these 6 (six) projects. Commissioner Farrell so moved.
Commissioner Rich seconded the motion. Chairman Quinn called for public comment.
There being no public comment, the motion passed unanimously.
Public Works Director Cothran discussed surplus items that the County has tried to get
rid-of for the last six (6) to eight (8) months; reporting the County has advertised twice
(Bid #2021-21 and Bid #2021-38 * sale of various vehicles and equipment) and the
County still has a lot of items left. He stated that he spoke with County Attorney Novak
regarding how to go from here, and his recommendation was to suspend the Bid Policy
and allow the highest and best bid. Public Works Director Cothran stated he would
announce it here, if the Board is okay with it, then would run with it; noting as an example
they would begin taking bids immediately and go through 4:00 p.m. E.T. next Thursday
or Friday, then open the bids on Monday; giving whoever is listening, an opportunity
without doing an advertisement in the newspaper. He further stated that whatever is left,

it would be on a first come-first serve basis, offering whatever, with Public Works letting
it go for that. Upon inquiry by Commissioner McDaniel, Public Works Director Cothran
stated that each item will be broken down (not sold as a lot). County Administrator
Hammond stated the next option would be the Pick-n-Pull, or the dump. Chairman Quinn
called for a motion to suspend the Bid Policy and allow to go out for the highest and best
bid. Commissioner McDaniel so moved. Commissioner Farrell seconded the motion.
Chairman Quinn called for public comment. There being no public comment, the motion
passed unanimously. Public Works Director Cothran requested the Board to allow him to
go ahead and give a date right now so it can be out there and be placed on the website.
Chairman Quinn directed Public Works Director Cothran to move forward. Public Works
Director Cothran stated that it will start immediately and go through Friday September 24,
2021 at 4:00 p.m., E.T., the same way they do it at the Clerk’s Office, and posting the
information on the website. He stated that bids would be opened on Monday, September
27, 2021 and after that, it will be on a first come-first serve basis for whatever (items) do
not get sold. The Board had no objections.

County Administrator Hammond discussed the need to have a sit-down (meeting) with
Deseret Ranches and Northwest Florida Water Management District to discuss
stormwater run-off. He stated that between Hurricane Michael and the change of land
conditions going from a tree growing operation to a cattle operation, it has inadvertently
caused a lot of stormwater run-off to County ditches, County roads, Wetappo Creek, Bay
County, and Sandy Creek. County Administrator Hammond stated that if the Board has
no objections, he will send an invitation and get a meeting scheduled. Commissioner
Farrell stated that he would like to attend this meeting once scheduled to discuss the

stormwater run-off in St. Joe Beach and GulfAire. County Administrator Hammond stated
that if the Board has no objections, he will reach out and then call a workshop. The Board
had no objection.
