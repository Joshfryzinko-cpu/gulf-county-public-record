UPDATE – C.D.B.G.-DR
Chris Gouras, of Gouras and Associates appeared before the Board to provide a status
update regarding the last round of C.D.B.G.-DR (Community Development Block Grant –
Disaster Recovery) funding. He reported that the County has published notice regarding
the next round of funding, which was held yesterday, and discussed the grant funding
and projects. Mr. Gouras reported that in Round 2 Funding the projects being submitted
for approval to D.E.O. are: 1. Water Treatment Plant in White City that will connect the
water source to the lines to the Cape; 2. Application for a Wastewater Project; and 3.
Airport. Chairman Quinn called for a motion to extend Mr. Gouras time to speak. Upon
motion by Commissioner McCroan, second by Commissioner Farrell, and unanimous
vote, the motion passed unanimously to allow Mr. Gouras additional time to speak. Mr.
Gouras added there would be a potential application for airport improvements and may
apply for dredging. Discussion followed regarding the denial of the Port Project in the
Round 1 funding. Upon motion by Commissioner McCroan, second by Commissioner
Farrell, and unanimous vote, the Board approved Mr. Gouras additional time to speak.
Discussion followed regarding the denial in Round 1 Funding. County Administrator
Hammond discussed his concerns regarding the percentage of funds being distributed
throughout the Counties, stating that Gulf County is only at 1% (one precent) and Gulf
and Bay were the two Counties most effected by Hurricane Michael. Following discussion,
Mr. Gouras requested the Board to notify him of the priority list for Round 2, as the
deadline to submit applications is September 13th.

VARIANCE REQUEST – PRESNELL’S VACATION RESORT & RV PARK
Planner Collinsworth presented a variance request from Presnell’s Vacation Resort and
RV Park, Parcel ID #06252-000R. Chairman Quinn called for anyone wishing to speak
on this issue to step forward. No one stepped forward. Planner Collinsworth stated that
the P.D.R.B. voted 4-0 to approve the exemption but noted that there was no F.E.M.A.
exemption provided by the applicant at the time of the P.D.R.B. meeting. Upon inquiry by
Chairman Quinn, Planner Collinsworth stated that the applicant is requesting an
exemption for flood relief to build below flood, but a F.E.M.A. exemption was not provided,
only ten (10) items the applicant thinks they qualify for. Upon inquiry by Chairman Quinn
regarding options, County Administrator Hammond stated he did not attend the P.D.R.B.
meeting, but the TAC met and denied the request based on evidence not provided. He
stated if the Board wants to pass this, then the Board needs to do the same as before,
get F.E.M.A. and the State to say this is allowable exemption. Planner Collinsworth stated
that one of the stipulations on the one the Board did approve was the applicant (St. Joe
Shrimp) had to go to the same size that was previously there prior to the storm (Hurricane
Michael); the same footprint. He stated this applicant did mention to the P.D.R.B. that
they wanted to enlarge the size of this new building. Planner Collinsworth stated if this is
approved, then one of the stipulations should be that this applicant get treated the same;
they must build to the same size that was there before. Chairman Quinn called for a
motion to waive the formal reading. Commissioner McCroan so moved. Commissioner
Farrell seconded the motion. Chairman Quinn called for public comment. There being no
public comment, the motion passed unanimously. Commissioner McDaniel made a
motion to deny this and have the applicant reapply once they get their ducks in order.
Commissioner Rich seconded the motion. Chairman Quinn called for public comment.
There being no public comment, the motion passed unanimously.
