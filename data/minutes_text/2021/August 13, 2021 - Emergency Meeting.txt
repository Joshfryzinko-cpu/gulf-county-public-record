AUGUST 13, 2021
EMERGENCY MEETING
PORT ST. JOE, FLORIDA
The Gulf County Board of County Commissioners met this date in special session with
the following members present: Chairman Sandy Quinn, Jr., Vice Chairman Phil
McCroan, and Commissioner David C. Rich <Commissioners Ward McDaniel and Patrick
Ferrell appeared via telephone>.
Others present were: Clerk Rebecca L. Norris, Finance & Management Director Amy
Ludlam, County Administrator Michael L. Hammond, Deputy Administrator Kari
Summers, Emergency Management Director/Fire Coordinator Matt Herring, Gulf County
E.M.S. Director Jody Daniels, Mosquito Control Director Austin Horton, Public Works
Director Mark Cothran, Sheriff Mike Harrison, and Sheriff's Department Captain Chris
Buchanan.
Chairman Quinn called the meeting to order at 3:51 p.m, E.T.
TROPICAL SYSTEM FRED
County Administrator Hammond stated that Emergency Management Director/Fire
Coordinator Herring has an emergency order, out of abundance of caution (for the Board
to consider). He reported that school has been canceled and hopes problems are limited
to the Indian Pass Road. County Administrator Hammond stated that it is projected to hit
Gulf County as a tropical storm, not a hurricane. Chairman Quinn called for a motion to
declare a Local State of Emergency. Commissioner McDaniel so moved. Commissioner
McCroan seconded the motion. Chairman Quinn called for public comment. There being
no public comment, the motion passed unanimously. County Administrator Hammond
requested the Board recess and if needed will monitor and be available for the call in the
E.O.C. Building.
The meeting did recess at 3:51 p.m., E.T.
The meeting did then reconvene on August 24, 2021 at 8:55 a.m., E.T.
