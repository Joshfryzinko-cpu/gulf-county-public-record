SEWER SYSTEM – BEACON HILL
Commissioner McDaniel discussed receiving a call from a resident regarding Mexico
Beach no longer adding taps to the sewer system in Beacon Hill. County Administrator
Hammond reported that the only thing that was approved in Beacon Hill, which was in
2003 through a C.D.B.G. grant, was a sewer line for the Beacon Hill Assistant Living
Facility which also included the subdivision in that area. He stated that since that time,
the City of Port St. Joe and Gulf County entered into a Interlocal Agreement, where the
City of Port St. Joe would run sewer eventually, when funds became available, to the
remaining residents of Beacon Hill up to the (Bay) County line. County Administrator
Hammond continued to state that over the next 13-15 years, without approval or objection
from the County, some residents from the Beacon Hill area along U.S. Highway 98 from
the Park North to the Bay County line have hooked on (to the City of Mexico Beach’s
sewer system) and more hooked onto this line after Hurricane Michael. He reported that
the County was notified a month ago from the City of Mexico Beach that this line is over
capacity and will not be able to hook on anymore residents. County Administrator
Hammond stated that he believes the City of Port St. Joe now has funds to run a line,
reporting that Gulf Aire will have to be off-line, and the County will need to have
discussions with the City of Port St. Joe regarding this project.
