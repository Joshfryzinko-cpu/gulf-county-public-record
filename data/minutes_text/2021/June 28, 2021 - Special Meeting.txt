BID #2021-29 – BEACON HILL VETERANS’ MEMORIAL PARK CAMPGROUND
CONTRACT
County Administrator Hammond discussed negotiations of the bid (#2021-29 *
Administrative Services for Veterans' Memorial Beacon Hill Recreational Vehicle Park),
stating that a contract is being presented to the Board to consider today, but the #1
(number one) ranked bidder wishes to withdraw (their bid). Ralph Rish, of Gulf Ventures,
LLC appeared before the Board to discuss his concerns regarding comments posted on
Facebook and the negotiated proposed contract; reporting that the County was going to
get 28% off the top. He stated that he would like to pull it (his bid) and that he does not
want to do the others either (Bid #2021-03 * Dead Lakes Park and Howard Creek Lower
Landing Campground). Chairman Quinn stated that Mr. Rish is out of all parks, asking if
there were any recommendations from members of the Board. He discussed the
possibility to negotiate with the second ranked bidder, Presnells’ Bayside Marina RV
Resort, and Old Salt RV Park. Discussion followed regarding the other bidders and bid
specifications. Commissioner McDaniel motioned to go with the second bidder/second
proposal and negotiate with them. Commissioner Rich seconded the motion. Chairman
McCroan stated he will vote no because he feels the County needs to run it in-house.
Chairman Quinn called for public comment. Rob Lapham, of Beacon Hill appeared before
the Board to discuss his concerns regarding this project, requesting that the profits not be
spread across the County, but used in this area. Commissioner Rich motioned to extend

Mr. Lapham’s time to speak. Commissioner McCroan seconded the motion, and it passed
unanimously. Mr. Lapham stated that he speaks for himself and his neighbors, requesting
again that the Board make the funds (profit) available to the uses and beneficiaries to that
particular Park (Veterans’ Memorial Beacon Hill Park). Tom Semmes, of Wewahitchka
appeared before the Board to present a copy of a land patent for this Park for Board
review; stating that the County cannot transfer title to anyone else. He stated that the RV
(recreational vehicle) Park should be removed because it violates this patent the County
signed and agreed to. Alan Wetzstein, of Port St. Joe appeared before the Board to
discuss his concerns regarding Veterans’ Memorial Beacon Hill Park, stating the County
should keep it the way it is and look at this project again in a few years. Ted Lakey, of
Gulf Aire appeared before the Board to request that the Board hold a public meeting
before the final vote, and he would like to see the site plan for this project. Debra Neel, of
Beacon Hill appeared before the Board to discuss her concerns regarding this Park;
stating it is underutilized, and she is in support of this project if the park is kept to a higher
standard. Chairman Quinn directed County Administrator Hammond to work with the
contractor to hold a public meeting to discuss the site plan. County Administrator
Hammond stated the plan was to hold a meeting for public comment once a contract was
executed, discussing the project, site plan, and the use of this Park. He stated that he will
try and have a recommendation before the Board at their next regular meeting. The
motion then passed 4 to 1, with Commissioner McCroan voting no.
ADMINISTRATIVE UPDATE & REPORT
OMB Director Herring requested the Board to accept grant award through the State of
Florida E911 Spring 2021 Rural County Grant (#21-04-10), in the amount of $37,292.99.
Commissioner Farrell so moved to approve. Commissioner McCroan seconded the
motion. Chairman Quinn called for public comment. There being none, the motion passed
unanimously.
Assistant County Administrator Smallwood discussed the project on 386 (County Road
386/Overstreet Road); recommending Board approval of Change Order #1 for the CEI
(construction engineering and inspection) Services for County Road 386 (Overstreet
Road) (Bid #1920-30), to be paid from the liquidated damages collected from the
contractor (GAC Contractors * Bid #1920-29). After discussion regarding the project,
Chairman Quinn called for a motion to move forward with Assistant County Administrator
Smallwood recommendation. Commissioner Farrell so moved. Commissioner McCroan
seconded the motion. Chairman Quinn called for public comment. There being none, the
motion passed unanimously.
County Administrator Hammond presented the Insurance Committee recommendations
for the employee benefit renewals for 2021-22 <copy provided to the Clerk for the record>.
Chairman Quinn called for a motion to stick with it. Commissioner McDaniel so moved.
Commissioner Rich seconded the motion. Chairman Quinn called for public comment.
There being none, the motion passed unanimously.

County Administrator Hammond reported that the City (of Port St. Joe) passed an
Emergency Ordinance and again has dipped into the County’s area and a letter will have
to be sent. County Consultant Butler appeared before the Board and provided a copy of
Port St. Joe Water Service Area and Lighthouse Utilities Company Water Service Area
Maps before the purchase <copy provided to the Clerk for the record>. He discussed the
area the City of Port St. Joe dipped down into on the Lighthouse Utility Company, Inc.
service area; reporting the County bought Lighthouse Utility and their service area.
County Consultant Butler suggested the Board notify the City (of Port St. Joe) that the
County knows what has been done. After discussion regarding the water service areas,
he recommended the Board put it in writing to the City (of Port St. Joe) that they are
encroaching the County Service Area. Commissioner McDaniel discussed his concerns
regarding the encroachment. County Administrator Hammond discussed allowing County
Consultant Butler to draft a letter and he will get the Chairman to sign it. After further
discussion, Chairman Quinn called for a motion to send a letter to the City (of Port St.
Joe). Commissioner McDaniel so moved to send a letter. Commissioner Farrell seconded
the motion. Chairman Quinn called for public comment. There being none, the motion
passed unanimously.
County Attorney Novak discussed a letter of support needed for Bay Savers, reporting

Chairman Quinn called for a motion to send a letter of support. Commissioner McDaniel

Quinn called for public comment. There being none, the motion passed unanimously.
