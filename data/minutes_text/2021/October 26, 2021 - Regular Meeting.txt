PUBLIC HEARING – STATE REVOLVING FUND / GULF COUNTY WATER
DEPARTMENT IMPROVEMENTS RESOLUTION
Philip Jones, of Dewberry Engineers appeared before the Board to present a facility’s
plan that was produced for the water system at the Cape <copy provided to the Clerk for
the record>; stating this is part of the process for the D.E.P. State Revolving Fund along
with holding this Public Hearing. Commissioner McCroan discussed concerns regarding
additional debt to this water system and keeping the rates low. County Administrator
Hammond stated that staff is recommending a change in fees and requiring a deposit,
but the water rate will not change. He discussed the water usage and additional accounts
added to the system; reporting the biggest customer is the State Park and it will be coming
back online in the Spring 2022 which will be a huge taxing on North end. County
Administrator Hammond reported that Northwest Florida Water Management District is
putting up one-half of the money and then some will be forgiven, maybe as much as 46%,
but the remaining balance will be borrowed at 0.12% based on the revolving loan fund.
He stated that he does not see the need to increase rates. Upon inquiry by Commissioner
McDaniel, Mr. Jones stated that the booster station will be located on the North end of
State land, on the Troy Deal property. Following discussion, Chairman Quinn opened the
Public Hearing to the public, calling for public comment. There being no public comment,
Chairman Quinn then closed the Public Hearing for comment. County Administrator
Hammond discussed passing a resolution. County Attorney Novak then read the
proposed resolution by title. Chairman Quinn then called for a motion to move forward
with this Resolution. Commissioner McDaniel so moved. Commissioner Farrell seconded
the motion. Chairman Quinn called for public comment. There being no public comment,
the motion passed unanimously, as follows:

DECEMBER HOLIDAY SCHEDULE
County Administrator Hammond recommended Board approve the change to the
December Christmas Holiday Schedule <copy provided to the Clerk for the record>.
Chairman Quinn requested County Administrator Hammond to read the recommendation
aloud for the Elected Officials present. County Administrator Hammond stated that
currently the Board’s policy is that if the holiday falls on Saturday, then staff takes the
preceding day and if the holiday falls on Sunday, then you take the day after. He stated
this recommendation would have the Courthouse closed on Friday and Monday, instead
of Thursday and Friday. Clerk Norris reported that the Court holidays have gone out and
she will have to observe those days for the Court Department, which will be closed on
Thursday and Friday and open on Monday. After Commissioner McDaniel’s inquiry from
Tax Collector Jenkins and Property Appraiser Burke regarding any objection, Chairman
Quinn called for a motion to move forward. Commissioner McDaniel so moved.
Commissioner McCroan seconded the motion. Chairman Quinn called for public
comment. There being no public comment, the motion passed unanimously.
MASK REQUIREMENTS – GULF COUNTY COURTHOUSE
County Administrator Hammond stated that the Board had invited to the Constitutional
Officers to attend today’s meeting to discuss the masks in the Courthouse but believes
this matter has resolved itself. Clerk Norris reported that this matter has resolved itself,
stating that there were some concerns. She stated the Clerk’s Office followed the
Judiciary’s recommendation in the Courtroom and as the Clerk’s Office is very public and
for the protection of employee’s and citizens who visit, continued during the last variant

in requiring masks in the office, but the common areas within the Courthouse were mask
free. She noted that all of this has resolved itself and there are currently no mask
requirements.
VACCINE MANDATE – SCARED HEART EMPLOYEES
County Administrator Hammond inquired if anyone from Scared Heart Hospital was
present, noting that Mr. (Henry) Stovall was having a procedure and could not attend
today’s meeting. He reported that the County has been contacted by several employees
of Sacred Heart Hospital regarding the threat of being fired because they will not take the
vaccine, noting that the Municipalities and the County fund the hospital a good bit of
money to subsidize it. He discussed concerns regarding locals being fired, and the need
to discuss this further with the hospital maybe at the next meeting. Hospital Board
Member Rex Buzzett appeared before the Board to discuss the hospital, reporting that
this was not a local decision, it came from the CEO at the corporate level. Commissioner
McCroan stated for the record that he has received several calls and employees have
filed for religious exemption and have been denied. He stated that it should be a personal
choice, a business or organization should not be able to hold employment over their head
to enforce something not of the employees’ choice. After discussion by Commissioner
McDaniel, County Administrator Hammond suggested to the Board to invite Mr. Stovall
to the next regular meeting. Commissioner McDaniel discussed the shortage in nursing
staff already; stating that people don’t need to be threatened with their jobs.

BID #2021-41 ADMINISTRATIVE SERVICES – DEAD LAKES PARK AND HOWARD
CREEK LOWER LANDING CAMPGROUND
Assistant County Administrator Smallwood reported that the County come to some terms
with the contractor (Madrid CPWG * Bid #2021-41 * Administrative Services for Dead
Lakes Park and Howard Creek Lower Landing Campground); providing the highlights to
the Board for consideration. He stated that: 1. Dead Lakes Park will have 25-30 sites
open within 60 days of the executed agreement; 2. more sites at Dead Lakes Park will be
added over time; 3. Howard Creek, within 18 months, will be up and running to full
capacity; 4. there will be a store at both locations; 5. County will maintain the boat ramps
at both locations; 6. County will maintain the bathroom and playground at Dead Lakes
Park; 7. Contractor will maintain the grass cutting and utilities; 8. agreement will be a 10
year lease term with a guarantee 5 year term option and beyond that will be two 5 year
optional terms to be agreed upon by both parties; 9. $1,500.00 per month or 10% of the
gross rental revenue; 10. payments for Howard Creek will start at the 18 month time or if
the contractor starts renting before the 18 months, then the County collect 10%; and 11.
any improvements the contractor makes, the County will prorate the value of those
improvements over a 10 year period. Assistant County Administrator Smallwood
requested Board approval. Following discussion, Chairman Quinn called for a motion to
approve it and move forward. Commissioner Rich so moved. Commissioner Farrell
seconded the motion. Chairman Quinn called for public comment. There being no public
comment, the motion passed unanimously. Upon inquiry by County Administrator
Hammond, Assistant County Administrator Smallwood reported that the contractor is
CPWG; stating that they will probably have a different business entity name that they will
form for this park management.
