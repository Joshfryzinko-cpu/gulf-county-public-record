MAY 17, 2021
SPECIAL MEETING
PORT ST. JOE, FLORIDA
The Gulf County Board of County Commissioners met this date in special session with
the following members present: Chairman Sandy Quinn, Jr., Vice Chairman Phil
McCroan, and Commissioners David C. Rich, Ward McDaniel, and Patrick Ferrell.
Others present were: County Attorney Jeremy Novak, Deputy Clerk Leanna Roberts,
County Administrator Michael L. Hammond, Deputy Administrator Kari Summers,
Emergency Management Director/Fire Coordinator Matt Herring, County Engineer Clay
Smallwood, III, Grant Administrator Kaci Rhodes, Grant Administrator Maegen Conners,
Office of Management & Budget Director Sherry Herring, Mosquito Control Director Austin
Horton, Public Works Director Mark Cothran, Assistant Public Works Director Raymond
Hart, and Sheriff Mike Harrison.
Sheriff Harrison called the meeting to order at 3:30 p.m., E.T.
Chairman Quinn led in the Pledge of Allegiance to the Flag.
CITY ANNEXATION RESOLUTION
County Attorney Novak presented a draft interlocal agreement <copy provided to the
Clerk for the record> and then read the draft into the record. Commissioner McDaniel
stated this is a good interlocal agreement and that both parties can work with this, which
may lead to future annexation in this same form. He also stated that he is in support of
this agreement. County Attorney Novak stated for the record that this proposed interlocal
agreement applies solely to those two (2) parcels in City Ordinance 584 and nothing to
do with any future annexations. Commissioner McCroan motioned to approve.
Commissioner Farrell seconded the motion. Chairman Quinn called for public comment.
There being none, the motion passed unanimously (5-0).
H.H.R.P.
County Administrator Hammond discussed each Commissioner receiving an email <copy
not provided to the Clerk for the record> from S.H.I.P. Director Lanier regarding the mobile
homes. He stated that the mobile homes are now a little over 500 square foot and the
total will be $54,000.00. S.H.I.P. Director Lanier appeared before the Board to report the
mobile homes will be $51,875.00 which will include delivery, setup, skirting, steps, A/C
and dirt pad. She also reported that it will include a new power pole, if one is needed.
County Administrator Hammond inquired if the Board is in agreement with these changes.
Commissioner Farrell discussed his concerns regarding the square footage compared to
the price of the mobile home. Upon inquiry by Chairman Quinn, S.H.I.P. Director Lanier
reported that there are eight (8) applications for Round 1 Funding waiting for a mobile
home. Upon inquiry by Commissioner McDaniel, S.H.I.P. Director Lanier stated that the

last set of mobile homes were 709 square feet for $49,350.00. Discussion followed
regarding the price increase and options to assist these applicants. Commissioner
McCroan requested that the Board table this matter and to bring back a recommendation
at next Tuesday’s Board meeting. County Administrator Hammond requested ideas from
the Board for staff to review and bring back a recommendation. He discussed the option
of relaxing the ‘double gift’ allowing those that have has a S.H.I.P. loan to be eligible for
H.H.R.P. Funding. Chairman Quinn inquired of the number of applications received for
Round 2 Funding. S.H.I.P. Lanier stated that as of today, forty (40) applications have
been received for Round 2 Funding. After discussion, Chairman Quinn called for a motion
to table. Commissioner McDaniel so moved to table. Commissioner Rich seconded the
motion. Chairman Quinn called for public comment. There being none, the motion passed
unanimously (5-0).
ADMINISTRATIVE UPDATE & REPORT
County Administrator Hammond reported that staff is unable to settle with the County’s
insurance claim and have agreed to mediation. He requested authorization from the
Board to allow him to settle with the consult of the Chairman. County Administrator
Hammond stated that staff needs the Board on standby, or authority to settle under the
best interest for the County and then bring it back before the Board for ratification. He
stated that he is asking for the authority to settle with the Chairman’s consult and bring it
back before the Board for ratification. After discussion by County Attorney Novak meeting
with the Commissioners individual, Chairman Quinn called for a motion to move forward
with it. Commissioner McCroan so moved. Commissioner Farrell seconded the motion.
Chairman Quinn called for public comment. There being none, the motion passed
unanimously (5-0).
County Administrator Hammond discussed the work needed at the White City dock;
recommending that the Board do it, but at one-half of the length originally built. Public
Works Director Cothran reported that the dock close to the bridge was rotten and has
been torn out, recommending rebuilding it at half-length and make repairs to the second
dock. County Administrator Hammond recommended the Board cap it at $25,000.00.
Commissioners Rich and Farrell discussed the need for an immediate fix; stating fixing
half now and half later when funds become available. Discussion followed regarding fixing
the docks in two phases. Commissioner McDaniel so moved to cap it a $25,000.00.
Commissioner Farrell seconded the motion. Chairman Quinn called for public comment.
There being none, the motion passed unanimously (5-0).
Grant Administrator Rhodes stated that it is the recommendation of staff for the Board to
reject Bid #2021-16; requesting Board approval to readvertising this bid for the Eastern
Shipbuilding site concrete slab project. Chairman Quinn called for a motion to rebid it.
Commissioner McCroan so moved. Commissioner Rich seconded the motion. Chairman
Quinn called for public comment. There being none, the motion passed unanimously (5-
0).

Upon request by Grant Administrator Rhodes, Commissioner McCroan so moved to ratify
the advertisement (to receive sealed bids) for the removal of the Donna Kay (derelict
vessel * Bid #2021-24) at Cape San Blas. Commissioner Farrell seconded the motion.
Chairman Quinn called for public comment. There being none, the motion passed
unanimously (5-0).
County Administrator Hammond reported that the County Attorney submitted the
proposed interlocal agreement with the City of Port St. Joe, attaching the recorded
agreement as an exhibit. He stated that the first reading will be held at next Tuesday’s
Board meeting.
There being no further business, and upon motion by Commissioner Farrell, second by
Commissioner McCroan, and unanimous vote, the meeting did then adjourn at 4:06 p.m.,
E.T.
