APRIL 13, 2021
JOINT WORKSHOP
WARD RIDGE, FLORIDA
The Gulf County Board of County Commissioners held a joint workshop with the City of
Port St. Joe. The following Board members were present: Chairman Sandy Quinn, Jr.,
Vice Chairman Phil McCroan, and Commissioners David C. Rich, Ward McDaniel, and
Patrick Ferrell.
City Commission Board members present were: Mayor Rex Buzzett, and Commissioners
Scott Hoffman, Brett Lowry, David Ashbrook, and Eric Langston.
Also present were: County Attorney Jeremy Novak, Deputy Clerk Leanna Roberts,
County Administrator Michael L. Hammond, County Assistant Administrator Clay
Smallwood, III, Deputy Administrator Kari Summers, Emergency Management
Director/Fire Coordinator Matt Herring, Grant Administrator Kaci Rhodes, Office of
Management & Budget Director Sherry Herring, Mosquito Control Director Austin Horton,
Public Works Director Mark Cothran, and Sheriff Mike Harrison.
City Staff members present were: City Clerk Charlotte Pierce, City Attorney Clinton
McCahill, and City Manager Jim Anderson.
City Mayor Buzzett opened the meeting at 4:01 p.m., E.T.
VARIOUS TOPICS
County Chairman Quinn asked for a status update regarding the density behind the (Gulf
County) Public Works site. City Mayor Buzzett reported the City (of Port St. Joe) went
through the requirements of the State to annex the property and then submitted a large-
scale plan amendment to D.E.O. (Department of Economic Opportunity) for approval for
zoning changes. After discussion regarding the proposed zoning change, City
Commissioner Ashbrook discussed the need for affordable housing. County Chairman
Quinn stated that he thought, in a previous meeting with himself and City Mayor Buzzett,
that a number was agreed upon regarding the density that both the City (Port St. Joe)
and County could agree on. City Mayor Buzzett said no (there was no agreed number).
Discussion followed regarding the need for affordable housing and the City codes
regarding density. County Commissioner McCroan discussed the proposed development
in Highland View and the outpour of residents against this development. City Mayor
Buzzett reported that the PACE Foundation has been approved for the second phase
(Gateway Manor) for affordable housing. Discussion followed regarding the City and
County working together, the County applying for grant funds and the City providing the
land (site near Gulf County Public Works Building), concerns with traffic flow, and access
to this property and wetlands. City Commissioner Hoffman discussed ideas for this
property; stating the first step is to annex this property into the City. City Commissioner

Hoffman inquired if the County would push for reduced density at the “Field of Dreams”
(vacant property near the College), if the City (of Port St. Joe) decided to build on that
parcel. County Chairman Quinn stated this property is already annexed into the City.
County Commissioner Farrell discussed concerns regarding density; referring to the
proposed development at Highland View and now the development off Butler Bay Road.
He stating the City and County need to look to the future on density. County Attorney
Novak discussed the time frame for the County to take action regarding the annexation
(property near Gulf County Public Works Building). City Commissioner Ashbrook asked
the County to trust the City; stating the City Commission is going to do what is in the best
interest of the residents of Port St. Joe. City Mayor Buzzett stated all the City did was
annex the property; reporting no plans have been made to develop that property as of
yet. Discussion followed regarding density, trying to find some medium ground, the City’s
zoning requirements, and the City going out for RFQs (request for qualifications) to see
what a developer would like to do with this property. County Attorney Novak noted that
this is not a matter of trust, but concerns regarding density; reporting the window is closing
for the County to take action. City Attorney McCahill reported the property has been
annexed and sent to D.E.O.; inquiring what the County wants. County Attorney Novak
discussed the meeting between the County Chairman and City Mayor to agree on a
compromise to the density. City Mayor Buzzett discussed the need to move forward and
meet after it is done to make it a special district.
City Mayor Buzzett reported that he instructed City (of Port St. Joe) staff to look at “Field
of Dreams” (vacant property located near the College) for ballfields; stating his plan is to
start small and work off that to make it better. He discussed his concerns regarding the
ballpark at 10th Street Park; stating the City has infrastructure problems under the fields.
Upon inquiry by County Chairman Quinn, City Mayor Buzzett stated the 10th Street Park
has outgrown its usefulness. County Chairman Quinn discussed his concerns regarding
the location of the “Field of Dreams” and the length of time it will take to complete this
project. Discussion followed regarding the location and children getting to the ballfield.
County Chairman Quinn stated that something needs to be done now at 10th Street Park;
reporting he would be willing to donate his $5,000.00 parks funds to build a dugout each
year. County Commissioner McCroan discussed his concerns regarding the condition of
10th Street Park; agreeing with the Chairman that something needs to be done now. He
stated he would be willing to put up his $5,000.00 park funds. Discussion continued
regarding the “Field of Dreams” and 10th Street Park.
City Commissioner Hoffman discussed the bathrooms at the Washington Gym; stating
the County was willing to give $75,000.00 with the City matching funds, and now finds out
the money was in grant funds. He inquired about these grants and officially asked the
County to make a pledge regardless if the grant is awarded. County Chairman Quinn
stated he is not sure where $75,000.00 came from, but the County has applied for a grant
and waiting to hear back. He stated that if this grant is not approved, then the County will
apply for a different grant, in the amount of $550,000.00 in hopes to help with the
Washington Gym. City Commissioner Hoffman reported that the City pledged 1/3 of the
funds needed for the Senior Citizens Building (roof), as asked of the County; stating they
found the money and explained it wasn’t contingent upon securing grant funding.

City Mayor Buzzett stated that before the next meeting, in three (3) months, a more
informative agenda will be provided.
There being no further discussion, City Mayor Buzzett then adjourned the meeting at 4:58
p.m., E.T.
