ADVERTISE FOR SEALED BIDS – PROPERTY LOT / CAPE SAN BLAS
As a staff recommendation, County Administrator Hammond requested to list the lot (property)
for sale that the County purchased prior to the original beach renourishment project at Cape
San Blas for access purposes. He also recommended a minimum bid of $400,000.00; keeping
a four foot (4’) access for the public; stating this property is located just north of the Stumphole.
Upon inquiry by Commissioner McCroan regarding the parking location, County Administrator
Hammond reported that the parking is at the (Troy) Deal site. Following discussion regarding
the lot history, the buildable section of the lot being Gulf front, the erosion control line, and
access considerations, Chairman Quinn called for a motion to put it (the lot) up for sale
(receiving sealed bids). Commissioner McDaniel so moved. Commissioner Rich seconded
the motion. Chairman Quinn called for public comment. Richard Fletcher, of Cape San Blas
appeared before the Board to report that Cape Island Preserve, Phase I already has a four
foot (4’) easement that is adjacent to his property (3625 Cape Sand Blas Road). He requested
the Board investigate this easement and make sure it is still viable. County Administrator
Hammond stated that he believes this easement is for the interior lots, not on the Bay, just for
this subdivision. He reported that if everyone inside this subdivision agrees to give the
easement to the County, then he would think that would satisfy the County’s need, but
presently the County has to have a County owned easement to meet our agreement with
D.E.P. for the beaches and shores renourishment credit. Following further discussion
regarding the property and easement concerns, Chairman Quinn called for further public
comment. There being no additional public comment, the motion passed unanimously.
STATUS REPORT – GULF CORRECTIONAL INSTITUTE
GCI Warden Scott Payne appeared before the Board to provide a status report regarding Gulf
Correctional Institute; stating that all correctional officer supervised workcrews will be
temporary suspended; allowing those employees to work on the compound due to a storage
in staffing. He stated that the non-supervised workcrews will continue. County Administrator
Hammond discussed his concerns regarding the cut and the paid workcrews going to Bay
County; stating that Gulf County should be first priority for any workcrews that goes out as the
County gave the State the property for GCI and was promised workcrews first inline. He
reported that DOC (Department of Corrections) is only taking one (1) inmate from the County
Jails at a time; stating at this rate, DOC will never get there count up. Warden Payne stated

that State Institutes are legally obligated to give inmates to those paid contracts. Discussion
followed regarding staff shortages causing the closure of dorms at the Main Unit and Forestry
Camp. Warden Payne then provided an inmate count report for workcrews. Commissioner
McDaniel discussed his concerns regarding the low number of inmates per workcrews; stating
the taxpayers cannot afford one inmate to one supervisor ratio. Warden Payne discussed his
concerns regarding staff and the much needed help from the Legislature.

MORATORIUM – RECREATIONAL VEHICLE PARKS / CAPE SAN BLAS
Commissioner McCroan stated he has received a lot of calls regarding RVs (recreational
vehicles) on the South end, especially commercial RV Parks and the safety on the roads;
reporting there is one way in and out at the Cape (San Blas) and Indian Pass. He inquired if
the County Attorney had any suggestions on how the County could pump the breaks on this.
County Attorney Novak discussed emergency actions options and staff recommendations
after looking at the issue, but stated if there is a concern of the Board, or Commissioner
McCroan, it would be his recommendation to place a ninety (90) day moratorium on
recreational vehicle park applications on anything that is not pending or currently being
submitted. After discussion regarding areas of concern, Commissioner McCroan then
motioned to put a moratorium in place on the Cape Road and Indian Pass, from where you
turn at the Raw Bar down to the boat ramp. County Attorney Novak clarifying the motion for
any pending applications for recreational vehicle commercial property. Commissioner
McCroan said yes. Commissioner Farrell seconded the motion. Discussion followed regarding
possible minimum size limit requirements for permitted RV parks. County Attorney Novak
noted for the record that one of the basis for this moratorium is you can explore accessibility
for emergency evacuations; stating the one way in and one way out is unique. Chairman
Quinn called for public comment. Sam Amerson, Jr., of White City appeared before the Board
to discuss concerns regarding commercial recreational vehicle parks in White City; requesting
the Board add White City to the moratorium. County Administrator Hammond discussed the
zoning difference between the Cape and White City. Chairman Quinn called for additional
public comment. There being none, the motion passed unanimously.

PUBLIC RIGHT-OF-WAY ABANDONMENT – BUTLER BAY ROAD REVIEW
County Attorney Novak discussed the topic of Butler Bay Road that was tabled last month;
stating that it has been placed on today’s Agenda. He stated, based on his understanding of
discussions during the last meeting were, that 2/3 of that road not being abandoned, the road
being resurfaced, the bridge being closed and the County would receive a 10’ (ten foot)
ingress/egress access to the water in perpetuity for all of Gulf County to have access down
to the water with the ability to park along the right-of-ways. He reported that the application
has been paid in full; stating if the Board is in agreement, then an advertisement for a public
hearing to consider a proposed resolution will be held at the next regular meeting. Chairman
Quinn called for public comment. Edward (Buddy) Wood, of Port St. Joe appeared before the
Board to request the Board not abandon any of the road; offering to furnish the pipe to repair
the bridge. Chairman Quinn called for additional public comment. Curtis Brown, representing
Barefoot Cottages Homeowners Association appeared before the Board to discuss their
concerns regarding the proposed abandonment; asking the County not to give up that view.
Chairman Quinn called for further public comment. Clifford Knauer, representing Lagoon
Manor LLC appeared before the Board to discuss the project; reporting that the property
owner has received development approval from the City of Port St. Joe and needs to obtain
access to the property. He stated that the property owner has agreed to build and maintain
the bridge, also giving access to the public with a 10’ (ten foot) easement to the water. Shawn
(last name not given) of Lagoon Manor Development appeared before the Board to discuss
the property and the development project. Discussion followed regarding parking, space for
a turn-around and the bridge. Planner Collingsworth discussed the County’s right-of-way, the
annexed property and access to the property. After discussion, County Attorney Novak
inquired whether the road resurfacing from U.S. Highway 98 up to the bridge was proposed
from the applicant. Mr. Knauer stated that is correct. County Attorney Novak discussed the
proposed 10’ (ten foot) easement to the water for public access. Sam Amerson, Jr., of White
City appeared before the Board to discuss his concerns regarding the proposed roadway
abandonment; stating that he is not in agreement to the abandonment of this road. After
discussion by Commissioner McDaniel, Edward (Buddy) Wood of Port St. Joe appeared to
report that he has plenty of culvert pipe to replace the bridge; discussing the ownership of
property on the other side of the bridge. Upon inquiry by Chairman Quinn, Planner
Collinsworth stated the County owns the right-of-way, but if there is a piece of beach there
then yes the public can get out, but he would need to see a survey and the State would own

the mean high-water line <Commissioner Rich left the meeting at 10:53 a.m., E.T.>.
Discussion followed regarding the mean high-water line. Commissioner McDaniel inquired as
to whether the County has to repair the bridge. County Attorney Novak stated there can be
legal challenges to egress and ingress to their property; reporting that it would be a City issue
and a County issue <Commissioner Rich returned to the meeting at 10:55 a.m, E.T.>. He
stated that property owners have an expectation and there is an ability to challenge and
demand it through a declaratory action. Discussion followed regarding public access and
possible alternatives to access the property. County Administrator Hammond asked the
developer if the development planned today, other than the gate, is in the County right-of-way
or is it 100% on the lot that was purchased on the other side. Mr. Knauer stated that none is
in the County right-of-way. He confirmed, upon County Administrator Hammond’s inquiry, that
if the County did not abandon anything, it would not change what they are building (their
development), other than the gate. County Administrator Hammond stated the question today
is not to abandon this road or not, the question today is whether to advertise the abandonment
for a later meeting and what the offer is. Following discussion, Commissioner McDaniel
moved to abandon the right-of-way beyond the bridge with the County maintaining the right-
of way up to the bridge. Commissioner Rich seconded the motion. Chairman Quinn stated
the need to have an agreement to include that the property owner will take the bridge, and
they will repair the road from U.S. Highway 98 up to the bridge with County Administrator
Hammond adding that there will be a 10’ (ten foot) easement access. Chairman Quinn stated
there would be public access and parking spots for six (6) vehicles. The Developer stated it
would include a turn-around. Chairman Quinn noted the need to make sure all that is included.
County Administrator Hammond stated before the vote, just to clarify, today the Board is not
abandoning anything, what he assumes the Board is doing is agreeing on terms so that the
County can advertise a Public Hearing for the abandonment process. He stated with three
votes to move forward, then staff will draft an agreement with the Developer and advertise it
in the paper for the next regular meeting to have an abandonment process. Chairman Quinn
called for Commissioner McDaniel to pull his motion. Commissioner McDaniel stated he would
amend the motion so the County can move forward with the abandonment. Chairman Quinn
clarified this to mean to advertise for the abandonment of the road. Chairman Quinn called
for public comment. There being no public comment, the motion passed 3 to 2, with
Commissioners McCroan and Farrell voted no.
The meeting did then recess at 11:11 a.m., E.T.
