APRIL 19, 2021
SPECIAL MEETING
PORT ST. JOE, FLORIDA
The Gulf County Board of County Commissioners met this date in special session with
the following members present: Chairman Sandy Quinn, Jr., Vice Chairman Phil
McCroan, and Commissioners David C. Rich, Ward McDaniel, and Patrick Ferrell.
Others present were: County Attorney Jeremy Novak, Deputy Clerk Leanna Roberts,
County Administrator Michael L. Hammond, County Assistant Administrator Clay
Smallwood, III, Deputy Administrator Kari Summers, Code Enforcement Officer Trevor
Seay, Grant Administrator Kaci Rhodes, Grant Administrator Maegen Conners, Office of
Management & Budget Director Sherry Herring, Mosquito Control Director Austin Horton,
Assistant Public Works Director Raymond Hart, and Sheriff Mike Harrison.
Sheriff Harrison called the meeting to order at 11:30 a.m., E.T.
Chairman Quinn led in the Pledge of Allegiance to the Flag.
CITY OF PORT ST. JOE ANNEXATION
County Attorney Novak discussed the joint workshop held last week with the City of Port
St. Joe that was followed by the County’s special meeting to authorize Administration and
the Legal Department to look into the procedural review and any potential conflict
resolution or challenge with the City of Port St. Joe’s annexation. After meeting with
Administration and members of the Board individually, County Attorney Novak stated that
Gulf County will go through a conflict assessment review; reporting this is a forty-five (45)
day process whereby the County will notify the City (of Port St. Joe) of its intent to
challenge the adoption of the (City) Ordinance and the grounds for which the County is
challenging it. He noted his previous advisement to the Board of the phases of this
process; the first (1st) step being to hold a public meeting to adopt a resolution that initiates
conflict assessment and presenting a certified letter that is required to be submitted to the
City of Port St. Joe. County Attorney Novak presented and read a proposed resolution for
the Board to consider <copy provided to the Clerk for the record>. He then gave a
summary of the timeframe, procedural, and statutory requirements. Chairman Quinn
called for a motion to accept the resolution. Commissioner Farrell so moved.
Commissioner Rich seconded the motion. Chairman Quinn called for public comment.
There being none, the motion passed unanimously, as follows:
RESOLUTION NO. 2021-16
A RESOLUTION OF THE BOARD OF COUNTY COMMISSIONER OF
GULF COUNTY, FLORIDA AUTHORIZING, APPROVING AND

ADMINISTRATIVE UPDATE AND REPORT
OMB Director Herring submitted a request <copy provided to the Clerk for the record> for
an extension regarding the H.H.R.P. Round 2 Funding application period; accepting
applications through June 15, 2021. Chairman Quinn inquired about the number of
applications that have been submitted. OMB Director Herring reported approximately 25
(twenty-five) applications have been turned in. Chairman Quinn called for a motion to
extend the application process. Commissioner McCroan so moved. Commissioner
McDaniel seconded the motion. Chairman Quinn called for public comment. There being
none, the motion passed unanimously. County Administrator Hammond stated that he
hopes to have a status update regarding H.H.R.P. Round 1 Funding to share with the
Board at the regular meeting. He stated the County may have to re-advertise for Round
1 due to the lack of applications, but a recommendation will be presented to the Board for
consideration soon. Commissioner Rich stated he knows that District I received most of
the money from Round 1, but the County has a desire to help as many people as possible.
He stated that if it can’t balance out, then the funds need to go where there is the most
need. After discussion by Commissioner McCroan regarding his concerns of using the
money were most needed, County Administrator Hammond reported that Calhoun County
did sign-off on the “piggy-back” bid, but the County has not contacted the contractor to
see if they will honor this price (purchase mobile homes with H.H.R.P. Funding).
Discussion followed regarding the requirement to qualify for this funding.
County Administrator Hammond reported that the Senate took the prison closure off the
table; stating that Gulf County appears to be safe for another year. He stated that the
State is “moth-balling” everything that will not remain open, including workcrews.
Commissioner McDaniel reported that Gulf County received 18 (eighteen) inmates on
Thursday. County Administrator Hammond stated the County’s Lobbyist and State
Representatives fought hard.
County Administrator Hammond reported that the appropriations for the airport was
funded at $500,000.00 (requested $975,000.00). He stated that he has directed the
County Attorney to start the P3 (public private partnership) process with St. Joe Company
to get this project off the ground.
County Administrator Hammond stated that he may be bringing a tether ordinance before
the Board to consider in the process of amending the (Animal Control) Ordinance (#2008-
20). He discussed the need of another animal control officer; stating the Humane Society
will be reaching out to the Municipalities to fund a portion of this part-time employee. He
stated that if the Municipalities (City of Port St. Joe and City of Wewahitchka) agree, then
he will be asking the County to pay a third (3rd) for this part-time person.

County Administrator Hammond discussed inviting someone from the Senior Citizens
Association; reporting the City (of Port St. Joe) put up their third (3rd) (to repair the Senior
Citizens Building roof). He stated the building belongs to the Senior Citizens Associations,
located on County property (land) leased to them and they have let their insurance lapse.
He stated that the Senior Citizens need to obtain insurance coverage and name the
County under the liability policy. County Administrator Hammond reported that after this
is completed and upon the County Attorney’s review, he will bring this before the Board
to consider. Discussion followed regarding the need to carry insurance coverage.
Chairman Quinn discussed conversations with the City of Port St. Joe regarding the
County helping the City with the dug-outs at 10th Street Park and the Washington Gym.
He requested, if the Board agrees, to submit a Memorandum of Understanding to the City
of Port St. Joe stating the County would take work, take the lead on the dug-outs, the
County would hire the contractors, and the County will get it done; requesting the City to
sign a letter allowing the County to take the lead. Chairman Quinn called for any objection
to sending a letter of memorandum of understanding. Commissioner McCroan made the
motion. Commissioner Farrell seconded the motion. After discussion by Commissioner
McDaniel, Chairman Quinn called for public comment. There being none, the motion
passed unanimously.
Pat Hardman, President of Coastal Community Association appeared before the Board
to inquire of any updates to the boil water notice. County Administrator Hammond
reported that it has been lifted as of today and Emergency Management Director Herring
will have an update on Alert Gulf.
There being no further business, and upon motion by Commissioner McDaniel, second
by Commissioner McCroan, and unanimous vote, the meeting did then adjourn at 12:00
p.m., E.T.
