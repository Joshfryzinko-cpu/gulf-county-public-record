PUBLIC HEARING – RECREATIONAL VEHICLES ORDINANCE
County Attorney Novak reported that this is the first Public Hearing advertised and a
second Public Hearing will be held on July 27, 2021. He then read the proposed
recreational vehicles ordinance by title; stated that the map is attached. Upon inquiry by
Commissioner McCroan, County Attorney Novak stated that page 4 has structures
defined. Chairman Quinn called for public comment. County Attorney Novak read Section
4, defining the restrictive Coastal Recreational Vehicle areas for the record. Chairman
Quinn called for public comment. Attorney J. Patrick Floyd, representing Indian Pass
property owners, appeared before the Board to ask the Board to consider adding the main
purpose, which is that recreational vehicles are prohibited in this Coastal Construction
Zone unless and except set forth in the grandfather and exemption section. County
Attorney Novak reported that Section 3 has a revision that incorporates those comments.

clients. County Attorney Novak stated that Mrs. Jones’ questions are under Section 6
Subsection B2. Commissioner McCroan added for clarity that lots not permitted (prior to

lot with intent is not covered. County Attorney Novak agreed, stating that the defined use

regular meeting will be moved to July 20, 2021. Commissioner McCroan motioned to

POLICY – H.H.R.P. FUNDING
County Administrator Hammond discussed a proposed amendment <copy provided to
the Clerk> to the H.H.R.P. Funding Policy, stating the following highlights:
ROUND 1 ($5,915,000.00): 1. very low rehabs (rehabilitation) increase from $20,000.00
to $40,000.00; 2. very low purchase assistance, if possible, allow a purchase-option of
$50,000.00, up to 100% of the purchase price, with the same requirements as the low
category for the applicant to carry insurance and occupy for ten (10) year before
ownership; 3. remove the mobile home purchase option; 4. allow moderates receiving
Round 2 funding in Districts III and V to be funded with Round 1 funding on a ready-to-
close basis to try to fill-out the rest of the moderates in Round 1; (moving folks that applied
in the second round in District III and District V moderates to the first round), with
unfunded moderates in District III and V that are not funded in Round 1 going back to
Round 2. Mr. Hammond explained that the Board voted early on to allow Districts III and
V which were under-represented in the first rounds to get priority on the moderates; and
5. propose drawing for the new money be held at the July 20, 2021 meeting.

ROUND 2 ($1,820,000.00): 1. Increase very low rehabs to $40,000.00; 2. very low
purchase assistance, the same as Round 1, at $50,000.00; 3. remove the mobile home
purchase option; 4. increase the low rehab to $50,000.00; 4. increase the low purchase
assistant to $60,000.00 with all the same requirements as before; and 5. increase the
moderate up to $75,000.00 in Round 2, with the same requirements. Commissioner
Farrell so moved. Commissioner McCroan seconded the motion. Chairman Quinn called
for public comment. There being no public comment, the motion passed unanimously.
