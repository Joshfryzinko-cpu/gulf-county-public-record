SEPTEMBER 25, 2025
WORKSHOP
WHITE CITY, FLORIDA
The Gulf County Board of County Commissioners met this date in workshop session at
the White City Fire Department with the following members present: Chairman Sandy
Quinn, Jr., and Commissioners Christopher McLemore, and Randy Pridgeon
<Commissioners Phil McCroan and Jack Husband, III were absent>.
Others present were: Chief Deputy Leanna Cooper, Assistant County Administrator Clay
Smallwood, III, Deputy Administrator Kay Summers, Emergency Management
Director/Fire Coordinator Matt Herring, G.I.S. Coordinator Clay Kennedy, Grant
Administrator Laura Beach-Armstrong, Office of Management & Budget Director Sherry
Herring, and T.D.C. Executive Director Silvia Williams.
Representing Gulf County Fire Departments were: Mike Barrett, Tripp Barrier, Sarah
Freer, Jim Irwin, Jarrod McCardle, Dan Mobley, Billy Ray Moore, Wesley Newsome, Lisa
Pridgeon, and Jimmy Rodgers.
Chairman Quinn called the meeting to order at 7:00 p.m., E.T.
Chairman Quinn opened the meeting in prayer and Chief Barrett led in the Pledge of
Allegiance to the Flag.
VOLUNTEER FIRE FIGHTER INCENTIVE PROGRAM
Chairman Quinn noted Commissioners McCroan and Husband are not present due to a
scheduling conflict. Fire Coordinator Herring reported that the County purchased two fire
trucks in the last month, noting a tanker truck for South Gulf Fire Department WAS
purchased using budget funds and a pumper truck at Stone Mill Creek Fire Department
funded through State Appropriations. He stated the White City Fire Department completed
a State safety inspection and was awarded a grant from the State Fire Marshal for ten
(10) S.C.B.A. sets and one bottle, noting Howard Creek Fire Department also received
grant funds to purchase S.C.B.A.s. Fire Coordinator Herring reported that the County
received $100,000.00 in grant funds for White City training academy equipment. He
discussed the opening of 50/50 matching grants available, encouraging fire departments
to work with the County grant department to apply. Fire Coordinator Herring reported there
are 129 fire volunteers countywide, forty (40) Firefighter I & II, with three (3) in the
academy. Commissioner Pridgeon discussed reviewing the incentive pay packets and
hearing any concerns from the firefighters. He stated the County has requested $1.2
million in State Appropriations, discussing goals in training new volunteers and reaching
the high school students. White City Fire Chief Rodgers discussed ladder testing that was
completed this week which was needed for certification. He reported that the White City
Fire Department received air packs. Fire Coordinator Herring discussed the ISO

(Insurance Services Office) ratings, stating the County’s two largest Fire Departments,
White City and Beaches, dropped to fours. Chief Barrett of South Gulf Fire Department
discussed incentive pay ideas, as follows: 1) $2,500.00 per year for certified Firefighter I
& II; 2) $1,000.00 per year for support staff; and 3) paying once a year on December 1st.
He suggested using benchmarks that Fire Chiefs would be responsible for tracking,
keeping up with paperwork and attending monthly training. Chief Barrett stated Fire
Chiefs should be paid the same, not accepting the pay bonus. He discussed implementing
a County wide documented training program, noting South Gulf Firefighters are required
to have incident command training, ICS 100, 200, 700 and 800 online courses. Chief
Barrett recommended multi-department training to train on hose evolutions, ladders,
smokehouse, etc., noting this would assist in teamwork and ISO ratings. Chief Barrett
continued discussing training ideas. He also discussed contacting FAA (Federal Aviation
Administration) to propose building a fire training tower at the airport. Chief Barrett
reported that blitz line monitor equipment is needed at each fire department. Upon inquiry
by Chairman Quinn, Chief Barrett stated due to scheduling conflicts it is difficult but would
like to schedule multi-department training scheduled once a month. Stone Mill Creek Fire
Chief Newsome thanked the grants department, Fire Coordinator Herring and
Commissioners for the new fire truck. He reported that he has not received any complaints
regarding the firefighter incentive pay. Chief Newsome requested that if a structure fire is
called that at least three (3) fire departments respond, more so during the day to ensure
proper manpower. Chief Moore of Howard Creek Fire Department discussed the need to
page out three (3) fire departments, especially due to the distance. He requested financial
assistance with hose, pump and ladder testing for the smaller fire stations. Commissioner
Pridgeon noted Howard Creek Fire Department needs an emergency generator. Fire
Coordinator Herring reported installation of a generator is on the list along with an LP tank
for Howard Creek Fire Department. Fire Coordinator Herring discussed his concerns
regarding the smaller fire departments’ budgets. O.M.B. Director Herring noted each fire
department has a minimum of $10,000.00 in the Repair and Maintenance of Equipment
Fund and $5,000.00 in Repair and Maintenance of Building Fund. Dalkeith Fire Chief
Barrier discussed his concerns regarding the Wewahitchka Fire Department paging. Fire
Coordinator Herring discussed dispatch separation from County Fire Department and the
City of Wewahitchka Fire Department. Discussion followed. Chief Barrier discussed the
need for younger volunteers to join the fire departments. Commissioner Pridgeon
discussed working together to sell the training program at the high schools. Chairman
Quinn stated he is in support of reaching out to the school system and the students.
Discussion followed. Chief Newsome discussed reaching out to the senior class a few
years ago to recruit. Discussion followed regarding the schooling cost and the incentive
program. Chief McCardle discussed setting up a sand pipe system on the outer areas
and the possibility of using Deseret Ranches’ ponds as a water supply. Discussion
followed regarding dispatching fellow fire departments to fire calls. Beaches Fire
Department Assistant Irwin discussed the increase in rental property elevator entrapment
calls and issues with obtaining elevator keys, recommending the power be cut to the
elevator and homeowners place the elevator key near the fuse panels. Commissioner
Pridgeon noting this matter will be discussed at the Board meeting in November. Assistant
Irwin discussed his concerns regarding volunteer shortage, noting experienced
volunteers are aging out. He recommended implementing a strategic five-year plan and

an incentive program for businesses who employees volunteer firefighters, allowing for a
flexible policy for serious fire calls. Chief Moore discussed the need for a mechanic that
can work on fire trucks. Commissioner Pridgeon discussed the blitz line monitor
equipment that has been ordered for various fire departments. Discussion followed.
Commissioner Pridgeon discussed drafting written protocols for each fire station.
Discussion followed regarding using Active Alert and E-dispatch to communicate between
fire departments. Sarah Freer, of South Gulf Fire Department discussed her concerns
regarding obtaining access to gated communities. She stated there needs to be an
ordinance where there is a standard access code for emergency services. Discussion
followed regarding calls for car accidents. Dan Mobley discussed his concerns regarding
the incentive plan, recruiting volunteers and the budget to pay firefighters. He
recommended going to a one County station with four staffing similar to Bay County. Mr.
Mobley noted Mexico Beach has budgeted $1 million for four (4) firefighters. He discussed
his concerns regarding the lack of water and hydrants within the County. Chief Moore
stated most firefighters volunteer to help, not for the money.
There being no further discussion the meeting did then adjourn at 8:17 p.m., E.T.
