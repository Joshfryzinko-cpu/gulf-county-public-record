FEBRUARY 12, 2025
WORKSHOP
HONEYVILLE, FLORIDA
The Gulf County Board of County Commissioners met this date in a joint workshop with
the City of Wewahitchka with the following members present: Chairman Sandy Quinn,
Jr., Vice Chairman Phil McCroan, and Commissioners Christopher McLemore, Lloyd Jack
Husband, III, and Randy Pridgeon.
City of Wewahitchka City Commissioners and staff present were: City Mayor Philip
Gaskin, City Commissioners Brian Cox, Johnny Paul and Charles Pettis <Ralph Fisher
was not present>, and City Manager Michael Gortman.
Others present were: Deputy Clerk Leanna Cooper, County Administrator Michael L.
Hammond, County Assistant Administrator Clay Smallwood, III, Code Enforcement
Officer Robert Knowles, Emergency Management Director/Fire Coordinator Matt Herring,
Gulf County E.M.S. Director Jody Daniels, G.I.S. Coordinator Clay Kennedy, Human
Resource Director Kaci Rhodes, Office of Management & Budget Director Sherry
Herring, Mosquito Control Director Austin Horton, Public Works Director Mark Cothran,
Sheriff Mike Harrison, and T.D.C. Executive Director Silvia Williams.
Chairman Quinn called the meeting to order at 8:01 a.m., C.T.
Chairman Quinn then opened the meeting with prayer and led in the Pledge of Allegiance
to the Flag.
COUNTY AND CITY ISSUES
LIGHTING – T L JAMES PARK
City Mayor Gaskin noted that City Commissioner Fisher is not present due to sickness.
He then thanked the Board for holding this workshop. City Mayor Gaskin discussed the
need for new lighting at T L James Park, in the amount of $200,000.00, noting this cost is
for materials only. He discussed the usage of the park being County residents, not just
City kids.
FIRE STATION – CITY OF WEWAHITCHKA
City Mayor Gaskin then thanked the Board for their assistance with finishing up the City
of Wewahitchka Fire Station building while the State was holding funds.
ROAD BOND
County Commissioner McLemore discussed the road bond, noting the County will need
a road paving list from the City of Wewahitchka. County Administrator Hammond reported
that the Local Option 1 ends in 2031, noting the City of Wewahitchka received 10%, City
of Port St. Joe received 21% and the County 66%. Discussion followed regarding the

road bond and creating a road paving list. County Administrator Hammond noted if the
Municipalities agree to a road bond, then the old road bond will have to be paid off, the
new bond will be for twenty years, and the Local Gas Option Tax will be extended.
Discussion followed regarding State grants to pave roads within the City limits.
LIGHTING – T L JAMES PARK
Commissioner Husband discussed the lights at T L James Park needing repairs and the
recent joint project of the new pickle ball courts at this park. Discussion followed regarding
the $50,000.00 per year pledge from the County to the City of Wewahitchka. City Mayor
Gaskin discussed obtaining a loan for the project and the $50,000.00 per year pledge
from the County. Discussion followed regarding the loan interest, the quote for the lighting
project at the park, and County funds to the City of Wewahitchka. County Administrator
Hammond discussed pledging the full amount of $200,000.00 upfront. After further
discussion, it was noted that the $50,000.00 pledge was used last year for the new pickle
ball courts. County Administrator Hammond discussed a four-year agreement committing
$200,000.00 upfront to the City of Wewahitchka for the lights at T L James Park.
OLD GULF COUNTY COURTHOUSE WEWAHITCHKA
Commissioner Husband discussed the Old Gulf County Courthouse needing an end use
(user), noting the City of Wewahitchka could use this facility as a City Hall and green
space for a farmer’s market. City Mayor Gaskin expressed his support of City use of this
facility. Ann Morris-Johnson, of Wewahitchka discussed creating a town square area. City
Mayor Gaskin discussed tearing out the old jail portion of the building. Commissioner
McCroan discussed his concerns regarding the means for the City of Wewahitchka to
maintain the building, noting the possibility of grant funds. Discussion followed regarding
grants and grant writing. Mrs. Morris-Johnson discussed a research paper performed by
an FSU student regarding the Old Gulf County Courthouse and its history, noting she will
provide a copy to the Board to review. Commissioner McCroan closed by noting that the
City of Wewahitchka need to decide what they want in the building and direction.
FIRE TRAINING FACILITY – WHITE CITY
Commissioner Pridgeon discussed using the old White City Fire Station as a training
facility for new firefighters obtaining certifications. He discussed his concerns regarding
members aging out and the need for younger volunteers. Wesley Newsome discussed
his support in holding a training facility in White City. City Mayor Gaskin agreed that fire
departments need young people to serve. Sharon Gaskin, of Wewahitchka discussed
getting this information out to the students as young as 10th grade, noting the students
would be eager to hear more about training to be a firefighter. Discussion followed
regarding involving high school students.
100 YEAR CELEBRATION – GULF COUNTY
Assistant County Administrator Smallwood discussed the upcoming 100-year celebration
of Gulf County, noting T.D.C. Director Williams is working on the celebration event.

CITY ROADS – FLOODING
Sharon Gaskin, of Wewahitchka discussed her concerns regarding City roads and the
flooding on Second Street. City Mayor Gaskin stated the City is working on the matter.
MEETING SCHEDULE – MUNICIPALITIES
Commissioner Husband discussed the County meeting with the City every six months
and inviting the School Board to meet, noting today was a good meeting.
ROAD BOND
David Brown inquired about the list of roads to be paved. Commissioner Husband
discussed creating a road paving list and the City receiving a percentage of the road bond
money to pave roads withing the City limits. Mr. Brown discussed his concerns regarding
Corn Griffin Road, noting it is a dirt road and there are culvert issues.
There being no further discussion, the meeting did then adjourn at 8:58 a.m., C.T.
