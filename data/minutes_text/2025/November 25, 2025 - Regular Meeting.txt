the following members present: Chairman Sandy Quinn, Jr., Vice Chairman Phil
McCroan, and Commissioners Christopher McLemore, Lloyd Jack Husband, III, and
Randy Pridgeon.

Deputy Clerk Leanna Cooper, Deputy Clerk Casi Campbell, County Administrator Michael

Kari Summers, Deputy Administrator Silvia Williams, Building Official Brad Bailey, Gulf

Kennedy, Grant Administrator Laura Beech-Armstrong, Human Resource Director Kaci

Flag.
The meeting did then recess at 9:20 a.m., E.T.
The meeting did reconvene at 9:50 a.m., E.T.
CONSENT AGENDA
Chairman Quinn called for public comment regarding the Consent Agenda. Robert
Branch, of Port St. Joe appeared before the Board, referencing to page 16 of the Consent,
to inquire of the road name regarding a D.O.T. grant agreement for design and permitting
of a roadway in White City. County Assistant Administrator Smallwood reported the
design and permitting project is for West Beatty Avenue, Lilac Street, and Road 20 loop.
Chairman Quinn called for any additional public comment regarding the Consent Agenda.
There being no further comment, Chairman Quinn called for a motion to accept the
Consent Agenda as printed. Commissioner Pridgeon made the motion. Commissioner
McCroan seconded the motion. Chairman Quinn called for public comment. There being
no public comment, the motion passed unanimously, as follows:

1. Approval of Checks & Warrants for October 2025 which are incorporated herein
by reference, pursuant to Chapter 136.06 F.S.

ADVERTISE TO RECEIVE SEALED BIDS – AIRPORT ACCESS ROAD
From page 1 of the Administrative Update and Report packet, <copy provided to the Clerk
for the record>, O.M.B. Director Herring requested Board permission to advertise to
receive sealed bids for the construction of the airport access road. Upon inquiry by
Commissioner Pridgeon regarding the funding source, O.M.B. Director Herring reported
that this project is D.O.T. funding through L.T.P. (Local Transportation Program).
Commissioner Pridgeon inquired if an FAA (Federal Aviation Administration) number has
been assigned. County Consultant Yeager appeared before the Board to discuss the
highlights of the County airport project, noting the County is at 30% complete design of
the airport. Discussion followed. Commissioner Pridgeon noted he is not in support of

building this road when the County has not yet received FAA approval or the
$50,000,000.00 in funding. County Administrator Hammond noted FAA has cut the air
space, reporting that it is restricted air space. Discussion followed. Commissioner
McLemore made the motion. Commissioner Husband seconded the motion.
Commissioner McCroan inquired of the motion. Commissioner Husband noted the motion
is to advertise to receive sealed bids. Chairman called for public comment. Sara Hodgson,
of St. Joe Beach appeared before the Board to inquire why Gulf County has not been
notified who is in the flight path that FAA has carved out, noting her home will be in this
flight path. County Consultant Yeager noted all this information was in the presentation
provided during a previous workshop. County Administrator Hammond stated this
information is available. Discussion followed. Christy McElroy, of Port St. Joe appeared
before the Board to inquire if this project is being funding with the State appropriations
from Senator Corey Simon. County Administrator Hammond stated no, this funding is a
$1 million appropriation, noting Senator Simon is in support. Ms. McElory inquired if the
Board would amend the motion to include a cost estimate to pave the road at Costin
Airport. County Administrator Hammond noted the Board tried and was told no, reporting
Costin Airport is privately owned with plans to build a (90’) ninety-foot hanger for an
airship. Ms. McElroy stated this is a spoken public records request, requesting to see all
the feasibility studies as well as the FAA circle. Upon inquiry by Chairman Quinn, Clerk
Norris stated that public records requests do not have to be in writing, except court related
requests. She discussed the new portal and the benefits of putting a request in writing.
Ms. McElroy clarified the request to any feasibility study, FAA. Clerk Norris inquired if the
request was regarding any feasibility study that the County has ever had. Ms. McElroy
discussed one from 2008 and the most recent study and the airspace. Commissioner
Husband discussed the possibility of using this road, that D.O.T. is funding, to access the
Industrial Park and the future location of the Emergency Operations Center. Robert
Branch, of Port St. Joe appeared before the Board to discuss the recent workshop
regarding E.M.S. and his concerns regarding the shortfall in funding for emergency
services with talks of the County spending funds for a feasibility study for an airport.
Deborah Mays, of Indian Pass appeared before the Board to inquire that the only way St.
Joe Company will donate the 1,000 acres is if there is an airport, noting the County would
be building a road for St. Joe Company. Commissioner Husband discussed the possibility
of the land being sold to an industrial partner, noting this area and the adjoining parcels
are zoned industrial therefore, this road would be an asset to the County. Discussion
followed. Commissioner McCroan noted that D.O.T. allocates funds annually, reporting
these funds cannot be used for E.M.S. services. The motion passed 4 to 1, with
Commissioner Pridgeon voting no.

manmade events. Chairman Quinn called for motion to extend time. Upon motion by

Pridgeon inquired if the point of the Bay is closing in. Mr. Todd noted the Bay could close
in over a period of time, but it does not affect the amount of water moving. Commissioner
Pridgeon inquired of any runoff test sites and if the commercial and recreational fisherman
work together. Mr. Todd discussed differences between the commercial fishermen and
recreational fishermen. Discussion followed. Commissioner McCroan noted the County
has an Ordinance that regulates what property owners can do on their lot. Mr. Todd
discussed his concerns regarding land clearing before building. County Administrator
Hammond stated that the County has a Land Clearing Ordinance (#2017-03) for parcels
along the coast. He stated that fecal matter was found in the Bay and a massive sewer
leak under the Highland View Bridge, discussing his concerns regarding the wastewater
treatment plant. Commissioner Pridgeon encouraged the commercial and recreational
fishermen to come together in a united front. Mr. Todd discussed his concerns regarding
saving the Bay using preventative maintenance.

ECONOMIC DEVELOPMENT UPDATE
Gulf County E.D.C. Director McKnight appeared before the Board to discuss the layoffs
at Eastern Shipbuilding, noting it was due to a Coastguard contract and most of the layoffs
will take place at the Nelson Street Shipyard in Bay County. He noted Eastern is not the
only business with layoffs, reporting that Amazon laid off 1,500 people. E.D.C. Director
McKnight discussed the future boat line up at Eastern Shipbuilding. He encouraged the
Board to keep working on the floating dry dock project to bring in boat repair jobs. E.D.C.
Director McKnight discussed various grants applied, touring the Port in Mobile, Alabama,
and a Maritime Academy. He noted the County is continuing moving forward with
workforce housing. E.D.C. Director McKnight reported that the Gulf County School Board
has applied for a $6.8 million digital tools grant for internet technology, AI and Google
workspace, noting the school system is training teachers to incorporate this technology
into the curriculum. Commissioner Pridgeon noted his support in the digital tools, and how
maritime will follow in the school system. He discussed building up the school welding
department for Eastern Shipbuilding jobs. E.D.C. Director McKnight discussed a major
building company that is interested in building workforce housing in Wewahitchka.
Chairman Quinn noted students are also obtaining energy certification through the school
system.
