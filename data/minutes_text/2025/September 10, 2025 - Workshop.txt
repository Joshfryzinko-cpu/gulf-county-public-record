SEPTEMBER 10, 2025
WORKSHOP
PORT ST. JOE, FLORIDA
The Gulf County Board of County Commissioners met this date in a workshop with the
following members present: Chairman Sandy Quinn, Jr., Vice Chairman Phil McCroan,
and Commissioners Christopher McLemore, Lloyd Jack Husband, III, and Randy
Pridgeon.
Present representing the Port St. Joe Port Authority were: Port Authority Vice Chairman
Zach Childs, Port Authority Secretary Jessica Rish, and Port Authority Treasurer Christian
Johnson. Port Authority Chairman Guerry Magidson appeared by telephone.
Others present were: County Attorney Jeremy Novak, Director of Finance & Management
Leo Bebeau, Deputy Clerk Leanna Cooper, County Administrator Michael L. Hammond,
County Assistant Administrator Clay Smallwood, III, Deputy Administrator Kari Summers,
Building Official Brad Bailey, Code Enforcement Officer, Gulf County E.D.C. Director Jim
McKnight, G.I.S. Coordinator Clay Kennedy, Grant Administrator Erin Kennedy, Human
Resource Director Kaci Rhodes, Office of Management & Budget Director Sherry
Herring, County Planner Doug Crane, and T.D.C. Executive Director Silvia Williams.
Chairman Quinn called the meeting to order at 11:24 a.m., E.T.
PORT AUTHORITY
County Administrator Hammond discussed the no interest loan from the County, noting
that the loan has been extended to allow the Port Authority operating capital. He stated
this meeting is to discuss future goals, floating dry dock and maritime academy.
Commissioner Pridgeon discussed touring a dry dock in Mobile, Alabama, noting the
desire to protect the (St. Joseph) Bay and tourism. He stated there are mechanisms in
place where the Bay will be monitored by State agencies with regards to the floating dry
dock. Members of the Board discussed more jobs for a diverse economy and workers
coming in from surrounding Counties. Commissioner Pridgeon discussed the need for
training and opportunity for businesses to come to Gulf County. He inquired if the Port
Authority has been approached regarding the Gulf to Gadsden Freight Logistics Zone
(FLZ). E.D.C. Director McKnight discussed the proposed FLZ project. He discussed major
employers like the hospital (Ascension Sacred Heart Gulf) and Eastern Shipbuilding and
looking into touring the floating dry dock in Mobile. Discussion followed regarding logistics
zone. Port Authority Chairman Magidson stated that he worked on the logistics project
and is supportive to the change of the Port Authority board only if it brought more funding
to the Port, finished the dredging and fixed the rail (road). County Administrator Hammond
discussed the success for inland Counties affiliated with a nearby Port, noting the
surrounding ports are at capacity. He stated whatever the outcome is of the Port Authority
board, the majority of the members need to be from Gulf County. E.D.C. Director

McKnight discussed the cost of a port and opening the railroad. Discussion followed
regarding various Ports within the State. Commissioner Pridgeon discussed his concerns
regarding putting it across four Counties and the growing of a port. Commissioner
McCroan stated he is in support of the floating dry dock and maritime academy. Port
Authority Chairman Magidson inquired if anyone has asked St. Joe Company their
thoughts on the change to the Port Authority board. E.D.C. Director McKnight noted no
one has asked St. Joe Company. Upon suggestion by Port Authority Chairman Magidson,
E.D.C. Director McKnight stated he would reach out to St. Joe Company on the matter.
Chairman Quinn expressed his support of the floating dry dock and the maritime
academy. Commissioner McCroan stated he is not here to oppose the Senator or
Representative, noting he wants to find common ground. Commissioner Pridgeon
discussed Triumph and various projects through Triumph funds. Discussion followed.
County Administrator Hammond noted this is a joint application between the Port Authority
and the County, stating the Port Authority needs a revenue stream and potentially have a
paid secretary/grant writer. Port Authority Vice Chairman Childs stated he supports the
floating dry dock and the maritime academy. After discussion by E.D.C. Director McKnight
regarding a revenue stream for the Port Authority, Commissioner Pridgeon discussed the
potential of the Port Authority leasing buildings near the floating dry dock as a revenue
stream. Members of the Board continued to discuss starting the project with the floating
dry dock. Port Authority Secretary Rish inquired how the County would be working with
the Port Authority regarding the floating dry dock, noting the lack of staff. County
Administrator Hammond discussed the proposal of a percentage of co-ownership of what
was bought, discussing Eastern Shipbuilding renting which would provide a revenue
stream to the Port Authority. Upon inquiry by Port Authority Secretary Rish regarding who
would oversee this project, County Administrator Hammond discussed joint ownership
between the Port Authority and County and to lease it to Eastern Shipbuilding, reporting
that there will be reporting requirements for at least ten (10) years. He noted that the
County has assisted the Port Authority in grant writing. County Administrator Hammond
suggested the Port Authority not charge Eastern Shipbuilding, noting that once buildings
are on site, leasing the buildings could be the Port Authority’s revenue steam. Port
Authority Secretary Rish inquired if Eastern Shipbuilding would be doing the dredging of
the dry dock. County Administrator Hammond stated it would be a County project due to
Triumph funding, noting the permit is issued to Eastern Shipbuilding. Discussion followed
regarding the floating dry dock project. County Attorney Novak discussed meeting with
the City of Port St. Joe, noting the potential lawsuit regarding a parcel of land on the
Industrial Road adjacent to the proposed floating dry dock site. He requested that the Port
Authority advocate on behalf of the County to request the receding of the opposition letter
from the City of Port St. Joe regarding the floating dry dock and ask the Port Authority to
support the County regarding the parcel adject to the floating dry dock site. Chairman
Quinn discussed the desire to resolve the conflict assessment with the City of Port St.
Joe. County Administrator Hammond stated the County needs the Port Authority’s input
and attendance at the Triumph meeting when the application is placed on the agenda. He
discussed Attorney Gibson and County Attorney Novak drafting an operational agreement
to present to the Board and the Port Authority for consideration. Discussion followed
regarding attending the Triumph meeting and the growth a floating dry dock could bring
to Gulf County.

There being no further discussion, the meeting did then adjourn at 12:18 p.m., E.T.
