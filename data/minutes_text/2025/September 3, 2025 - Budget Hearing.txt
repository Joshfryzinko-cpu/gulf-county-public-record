TENTATIVE 2025-26 OVERVIEW
County Administrator Hammond presented to the Board a highlight overview of the
tentative 2025-26 budget, noting additional copies are available for the public to follow
along <copy provided to the Clerk for the record>. He noted the major change is the
addition to the road bond money (bank loan). County Administrator Hammond stated that
not all the payroll adjustments have been implemented, including the Constitutional
Officers. OMB Director Herring reported the proposed aggregate millage rate of 5.9851,
noting it is 2.02% above the current aggregate rolled back rate of 5.8665. She stated the
proposed aggregate millage rate is: 1) County-wide millage at 5.6; and 2) four (4)
Dependent Fire Districts at .5 mills each, noting the County had no proposed M.S.T.U.
Voted Debt Service millage rates this year. OMB Director Herring reported this is the
maximum millage rates, noting the Board may not exceed the proposed millage rate. She
provided an overview of the reasons for the increase in Ad Valorem tax dollars, as follows:
1) Sheriff’s Office; 2) four (4) Constitutional Officers; 3) Gulf County E.M.S.; 4) County
Jail; 5) firefighter incentives; 6) insurance cost; 7) pay raises for employees; 8) park
renovations; and 9) Port St. Joe Downtown Redevelopment (2010 CRA NPSJ). OMB
Director Herring noted the tentative budget has been posted to the County’s website for
public review, stating the proposed budget keeps the General Fund and Public Works
operating expenditures in line with the annual revenue estimate. She stated the Board

may amend the budget but any increases in expenditures will require equivalent reduction
by specified line-item. OMB Director Herring requested the following changes: 1)
decrease General Fund by $7,280.00 for revised revenue estimates and offsetting
adjustments to reserves and expenditures; 2) increase Secondary Road & Bridge Fund
by $6,235.00 for revised revenue estimates and offsetting adjustments to reserves and
expenditures; 3) increase General Grants Fund by $19,316.00 for revised revenue
estimates and offsetting adjustments to reserves and expenditures; 4) decrease E-911
Fund by $80,000.00 for revised revenue estimates and offsetting adjustments to reserves
and expenditures; 5) increase Gas Tax Debt Service Fund by $19,190.00 for revised
revenue estimates and offsetting adjustments to reserves and expenditures; 6) all funds
make line-item adjustments to revenues and expenditures within each fund prior to the
final hearing to account for the recent changes to personnel, contracts, revised state
estimates, elected official salaries, and other necessary adjustments prior to the final
hearing. She noted the changes will not change the total by fund or ad valorem taxes
adopted today. Chairman Quinn called for a motion to approve the recommended
changes. Commissioner Husband made the motion. Commissioner Pridgeon seconded
the motion. Chairman Quinn called for public comment. There being no public comment,
the motion passed unanimously. County Administrator Hammond discussed the high
points, noting the employee count paid by the County budget. He discussed the parks
renovation project and the paving road list. Commissioner Pridgeon discussed the parks
renovation project. Upon inquiry by Chairman Quinn, County Administrator Hammond
noted if the funds are split evenly, the total with the match would be $250,000.00 for each
district. Commissioner Husband inquired if the funds are not spent within the budget year
it could be rolled over. County Administrator Hammond said yes. Following discussion
regarding the two (2) separate funds, the Board had no objection to splitting the funding
evenly. Members of the Board expressed their support in the proposed budget. Chairman
Quinn opened the hearing to the public, calling for public comment. There being no public
comment, Chairman Quinn closed the hearing to the public.

SPECIAL MEETING – EGLIN AIR FORCE SITE
County Administrator Hammond requested to speak on a matter not budget related,
noting the matter is timely. He requested permission from the Board to invite
Congressman Dunn to Gulf County to attend a special meeting to visit the Eglin site
(beach) and demand Eglin Air Force to open the beaches immediately. The Board had no
objection. County Administrator Hammond noted he would draft a letter to submit to
Congressman Dunn tomorrow.
