MONEY BAYOU
County Administrator Hammond noted the County previously opposed and he does not
believe it will move forward with permitting through the Corp of Engineers. Stephen
Czarnik, of Money Bayou appeared before the Board to discuss his concerns regarding
a neighboring property owner filing with D.E.P. (Department of Environmental Protection)
and the Corp of Engineers permits to divert Money Bayou right across his property, details
of his plan, and remediation. Upon motion by Commissioner McCroan, second by
Commissioner Husband, and unanimous vote, the Board extended Mr. Czarnik time to
speak. Mr. Czarmik requested that the Board oppose this plan and forward their

opposition to the Army Corp of Engineers and D.E.P. He also noted that the Corp of
Engineers had already granted the permit, but it has expired. Mr. Czarnik reported that a
recent permit was filed with D.E.P., and the homeowner will need to obtain the rights to
the submerged lands located in front of their property in order to proceed. He asked the
Board to oppose this permit as well. Commissioner McCroan noted he is completely
against this project. County Administrator Hammond discussed Money Bayou, noting the
County has never supported cutting-off beach access to the public, pedestrian or driving,
to straighten the water flow. He stated the Corp of Engineers has not notified the County
that they have any intention of issuing a permit. County Administrator Hammond stated
the County has already opposed it and will continue to oppose it. He then noted if the
Board wants to backup staff’s previous opposition, that would be satisfactory.
Commissioner McCroan inquired of County Attorney Novak if the Board needed a
resolution. County Attorney Novak discussed the Board making a formal vote in
opposition to it. County Administrator Hammond noted the Corp already has the County’s
objection and it has been documented. Commissioner McCroan made the motion to adopt
a Resolution. Commissioner Pridgeon seconded the motion. Commissioner Pridgeon
recommended that the Resolution state the reasons why the Board is in opposition.
Commissioner McCroan directed Assistant County Administrator Smallwood to submit the
Resolution to all necessary agencies, including D.E.P. and the Corp of Engineers.
Chairman Quinn inquired for clarification if the motion included the recommendation by
Commissioner Pridgeon. Commissioner McCroan stated yes. After discussion by
members of the Board, Chairman Quinn called for public comment. Elliot Barnett, of
Indian Pass appeared before the Board to thank the Board for being in opposition, noting
the Corp permit states that someone will need to request a Public Hearing, wondering if
the Board would submit the request. He reported that he, personally, has requested a
Public Hearing. County Administrator Hammond noted if the Corp of Engineers does not
issue the permit, there is no need for a Public Hearing. Chairman Quinn called for any
additional public comment. Jacklyn Mickle, of Cape San Blas appeared before the Board
to state she is in support of this plan or another plan because of what is happening in
Money Bayou. She discussed her concerns regarding wetlands being filled in and two
houses being built, noting the Bayou has shifted more west and is now a large lake.
Following discussion, Debra Mayes, of the Gulf County Citizens Coalition appeared
before the Board to note the Board has already received her email <copy not provided to
the Clerk for the record> regarding this matter, inquiring if the Corp of Engineers does
permit this project, does Gulf County have any power to stop someone from putting a big
ditch across the roadway (drivable beach access). County Administrator Hammond noted
he does not think it will get to that point, but yes, the main argument by the County is that
public funds have been placed on the beach for emergency berms and customary use.
He reported it is a public beach that the County puts public funds to protect, and the
County has beach driving. Ms. Mays discussed the channel plan and her concerns
regarding the proposed plan. Chairman Quinn called for public comment. Rodney
Dupree, of Money Bayou appeared before the Board to discuss the waterway at Money
Bayou and thanked the Board for their support. The motion then passed unanimously, as
follows:
