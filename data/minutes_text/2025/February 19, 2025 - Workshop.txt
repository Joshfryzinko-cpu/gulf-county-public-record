FEBRUARY 19, 2025
WORKSHOP
PORT ST. JOE, FLORIDA
The Gulf County Board of County Commissioners met this date in a workshop session
with the following members present: Chairman Sandy Quinn, Jr., Vice Chairman Phil
McCroan, and Commissioners Christopher McLemore, Lloyd Jack Husband, III, and
Randy Pridgeon.
Others present were: County Attorney Jeremy Novak, Director of Finance & Management
Leo Bebeau, Deputy Clerk Leanna Cooper, County Administrator Michael L. Hammond,
County Assistant Administrator Clay Smallwood, III, Deputy Administrator Kari Summers,
Building Official Brad Bailey, Gulf County E.M.S. Director Jody Daniels, County
Consultant Warren Yeager, Jr., G.I.S. Coordinator Clay Kennedy, Grant Administrator Erin
Kennedy, Human Resource Director Kaci Rhodes, Office of Management & Budget
Director Sherry Herring, Mosquito Control Director Austin Horton, County Planner Doug
Crane, Public Works Director Mark Cothran, and Assistant Public Works Director
Raymond Hart.
Chairman Quinn called the meeting to order at 9:00 a.m., E.T.
Chairman Quinn led in the Pledge of Allegiance to the Flags.
CHA/AIRPORT
County Administrator Hammond announced staff will provide an overview of the airport
project. County Consultant Yeager appeared before the Board to introduce consultants
from CHA Consulting, noting CHA has a presentation to provide to the Board regarding
the proposed airport design. Paul Puckli, CHA Vice President appeared before the Board
to provide the agenda of today’s presentation <copy provided to the Clerk for the record>,
discussing the eight steps to building an airport. He reported the team has completed five
of the eight steps of the program thus far. CHA Program Manager Jose Blanoc appeared
before the Board to discuss the benefits of a general aviation airport. He then discussed
other benefits to the County, one being an emergency operations center at the airport and
the economic impact that an airport provides. Fred Loeffler, CHA Project Manager,
appeared before the Board to discuss the airport design, noting the 30% design would be
ready by July 2025. Josh Baxley of Dewberry appeared before the Board to discuss the
roadway design and entranceway into the airport site. County Administrator Hammond
stated his intent would be to hold off on the turn lanes until the construction of the airport.
Mr. Puckli returned before the Board inquiring of any questions from the Board.
Commissioner Pridgeon inquired about how the County found CHA Consulting. County
Administrator Hammond stated the County procured CHA through an RFQ (Request for
Qualifications), noting this group has done extensive work at the Panama City Airport.
Commissioner Pridgeon discussed the eight steps, inquiring about the element of the

stakeholder buy-in. Mr. Blanco noted CHA is assisting the County with this element piece.
Upon discussion regarding Tyndall Air Force Base, County Administrator Hammond
reported Tyndall Air Force Base did not file a letter of objection and the County has
received a letter of no objection from the FAA. Discussion followed regarding Tyndall Air
Force and the proposed runway design of the airport and customer usage of the airport.
Mr. Puckli stated the feasibility study is focusing on finding new airport customers, not to
take away from a neighboring airport facility. Commissioner Husband inquired about how
the County will address the surrounding parcels access to their property. Mr. Blanco
discussed the need for realignment of the road. Discussion followed regarding the
roadway design and the turn lane. Upon inquiry by Commissioner McCroan, County
Consultant Yeager discussed past efforts to partner with Franklin County to grow
economic development at their present airport and unfortunately Franklin County was not
in support. Following discussion, Chairman Quinn then called for public comment. Christy
McElroy, of Port St. Joe appeared before the Board to discuss the relocation of the
Panama City Airport. She noted that the County does not need a $50 million airport to
land a helicopter. Chairman Quinn called for public comment. Debroah Mays, of the Gulf
County Citizens Coalition appeared before the Board to discuss their concerns regarding
the County’s downpayment, the land donation from St. Joe Company, and inquired who
will be operating the airport. County Administrator Hammond stated the County has a
public private partnership with St. Joe Company, noting St. Joe Company will have the
lease and believes St. Joe Company will be sublease operations. Ms. Mays expressed
concerns regarding the reason behind the need for an airport, noting that the County
wants the airport for seaport, industrial land, and railway but none of these industries
exist. David Smith, of White City appeared before the Board to discuss his concerns
regarding the proposed airport, noting he is opposed to the current proposed location.
Discussion followed regarding the location and the runway design. Chairman Quinn again
called for additional public comment. There were no further public comments.
There being no further discussion, the meeting did then adjourn at 10:17 a.m., E.T.
