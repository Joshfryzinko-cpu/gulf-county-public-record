AUGUST 19, 2025
SPECIAL MEETING
PORT ST. JOE, FLORIDA
The Gulf County Board of County Commissioners met this date in special session with
the following members present: Chairman Sandy Quinn, Jr., Vice Chairman Phil
McCroan, and Commissioners Christopher McLemore, Lloyd Jack Husband, III, and
Randy Pridgeon.
Others present were: County Attorney Jeremy Novak, Director of Finance & Management
Leo Bebeau, Deputy Clerk Leanna Cooper, County Administrator Michael L. Hammond,
County Assistant Administrator Clay Smallwood, III, Deputy Administrator Kari Summers,
Building Official Brad Bailey, Code Enforcement Officer Robert Knowles, Gulf County
E.M.S. Director Jody Daniels, Grant Administrator Erin Kennedy, Grant Administrator
Laura Beech-Armstrong, Human Resource Director Kaci Rhodes, Office of Management
& Budget Director Sherry Herring, Mosquito Control Director Austin Horton, County
Planner Doug Crane, Public Works Director Mark Cothran, Assistant Public Works
Director Raymond Hart, Sheriff Mike Harrison, and T.D.C. Executive Director Silvia
Williams.
Chairman Quinn called the meeting to order at 3:30 p.m., E.T.
Chairman Quinn opened the meeting with prayer and led in the Pledge of Allegiance to
the Flag.
2026 LEGISLATIVE PRIORITIES
County Administrator Hammond discussed accepting the Board members’ Legislative
priority list, noting staff can add to it, if needed. Commissioner Pridgeon presented a
proposal <copy provided to the Clerk for the record> from Monolith regarding the Fire
Training Facility in White City, discussing the State Fire Marshal grant to fund the
equipment for the facility. He noted the location of the fire training facility will be located
at the Old White City Fire Department Station. Commissioner Pridgeon stated, if the
Board has no objection, he is ready to add this project to the State appropriations request.
Chairman Quinn stated he has no objection, expressing his support for this project.
Following discussion, members of the Board expressed their support. Commissioner
Pridgeon discussed the marine training facility, noting members of the Board need to tour
a nearby facility and begin talks with Tom P. Haney Technical Center. Upon inquiry by
Commissioner Pridgeon, County Administrator Hammond noted that the County has a
draft agreement that has been presented to the Port Authority for consideration.
Discussion followed regarding the proposed marine training facility. Members of the Board
discussed their support and traveling to Alabama to tour a facility. Commissioner Pridgeon
noted it’s not really an ask for a Legislative appropriation but wants to get it on their radar
for upcoming years. He discussed his concerns regarding the “Back Beach Road” project

(Gulf to Bay Highway) and the traffic in the St. Joe Beach area, noting the County needs
to make the Legislative Delegation aware. Discussion followed regarding traffic and
ambulances traveling to Panama City. County Administrator Hammond reported this
project will have to be in the 5-year plan (Back Beach Road Project). Commissioner
Pridgeon discussed the living shoreline (in Highland View), noting the money is already
there and water is already on the list. Upon discussion, County Administrator Hammond
noted the ask will be water project/reuse. Commissioner Husband discussed the airport.
County Administrator Hammond reported that staff have received a price on the turn lanes
and there is enough funding for the road-in but will need to ask for $2 million for the turn
lanes. Commissioner Husband noted he is unsure if he wants to add the Old Gulf County
Courthouse in Wewahitchka to the list just yet due to staff still looking into grant funding
resources but inquired if the Board had any objections to adding it to the list. Chairman
Quinn inquired if Commissioner Husband has received notice from Congressman Dunn’s
office on the community project. Following discussion, Chairman Quinn inquired about
the time the Delegation meeting was changed to. County Administrator Hammond noted
it has been moved to 10:30 a.m. Chairman Quinn noted he may not be able to attend.
County Administrator Hammond recapped the items on the (priorities) list, as follows: 1)
fire training academy; 2) airport and road; 3) road projects; 4) fire truck and; 5) water
projects/sewer reuse. Members of the Board inquired of who is getting a fire truck. Fire
Coordinator Herring appeared before the Board to report there is a memorandum going
in the Consent Agenda for Board approval, noting in the five-year fire department plan,
year four would be Overstreet and Howard Creek is set for year five (to receive a fire
truck). County Administrator Hammond requested Board approval of the initial request,
noting the items listed can be discussed at the delegation meeting. Chairman Quinn
called for a motion to move forward with those legislative priorities request. Commissioner
Husband so moved. Commissioner Pridgeon seconded the motion. Chairman Quinn
called for public comment. Casey Plouffe, of Cape San Blas appeared before the Board
to inquire where she could obtain a copy of what the Board is reviewing. Chairman Quinn
noted today’s discussion was topics that members of the Board presented, inquiring if the
Clerk sends out the Agenda and Information packets the Thursday before the regular
meeting. Deputy Clerk Cooper noted the Agenda and Information Packets are typically
completed the Thursday before the regular meeting. Following discussion, Ms. Plouffe
inquired if there are documents to review for special meetings. Chairman Quinn noted
members of the Board may have items that each member will discuss, but there are some
cases the Board will have a small packet, but it probably will not be emailed out.
Commissioner Husband noted there is a notice section on the County website also.
Chairman Quinn called for any additional public comment. There being none, the motion
passed unanimously.
RATIFY V.A.B. CHAIRMAN & VICE CHAIRMAN
Chairman Quinn called for a motion to appoint himself to serve as Chairman of the Value
Adjustment Board for 2025 and Commissioner McCroan serve as Vice Chairman.
Commissioner Husband so moved. Commissioner Pridgeon seconded the motion.
Chairman Quinn called for public comment. Chairman Quinn called for public comment.
There being no public comment. The motion passed unanimously.

ADMINISTRATIVE UPDATE & REPORT

LITIGATION CITY OF PORT ST. JOE – ANNEXATION
County Attorney Novak discussed attending a hearing before Judge Collier on a partial
motion for summary judgment and the lawsuit with the City of Port St. Joe regarding
annexation (Parcel ID #04571-000R). He reported the Judge granted the Summary
Judgment for the County and a date will be set regarding the damages for the County.
County Attorney Novak discussed going back before the Judge next week to argue the
legality of what the City of Port St. Joe did. He noted that he has provided the City of Port
St. Joe with the County’s opposition to the newly annexation (Parcel ID #04571-000R)
and provided the recently adopted Resolutions and the notice of the Conflict Assessment
Meeting. Following discussion, Commissioner Pridgeon noted this annexation is directly
across the street from where the County is talking about placing the marine academy.
Discussion followed regarding the annexation and the location. County Administrator
Hammond noted the intent of the City of Port St. Joe is to approve a letter to rescind their
letter of support for the floating dry dock, encouraging Members of the Board to encourage
City Commissioners to not approve to rescinding their letter of support. County Attorney
Novak noted once a date is set, an advertisement of the Conflict Assessment Meeting will
be posted.
There being no further business, and upon motion by Commissioner Husband, second
by Commissioner McCroan, and unanimous vote, the meeting did then adjourn at 4:04
p.m., E.T.
