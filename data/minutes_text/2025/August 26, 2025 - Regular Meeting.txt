TIPPING FEES – FIVE POINTS LANDFILL
Mosquito Control Director Horton noted the tipping fees have remained without an
increase over the past several years, but unfortunately expenses are increasing. He
requested permission from the Board to potentially increase the tipping fees by a small
amount, if deemed necessary. County Administrator Hammond discussed that the
amount of flow has decreased due to the decrease in construction, noting the overhead
remains the same. He reported that the County will lose funds at the Wetappo Transfer
Station, but the County cannot afford to lose money at Five Points. County Administrator
Hammond stated he may come back before the Board to request a three (3) to four (4)
percent COLA (cost of living adjustment) increase if there is no improvement, noting the
County has not changed the rates since the County took the transfer station back over
from BCC Waste Solutions.

QUASI-JUDICIAL HEARING – P.D.R.B.
PLANNED DEVELOPMENT PROJECT AMENDMENT – SALT PINES
Planner Crane presented a planned development project amendment application from
68V Salt Pines, LLC, for Parcel ID #03019-00R and #03040-002R, Salt Pines
Subdivision. He noted the original plan development project was approved by the Board
in July 2024 with the Ordinance adopted in September. Planner Crane reported that the
Board approved 100 townhomes, noting the amendment would reduce the project to
eighty (80) detached single-family homes with 68 homes in the County and 12 inside the
city limits. Chairman Quinn called for anyone wishing to speak to step forward. Applicant
Grant Rish appeared before the Board to state he is present to answer any questions
regarding the amendment request. Chairman Quinn inquired if Mr. Rish had any objection
to waiving the Quasi-Judicial Hearing. With no objection, Chairman Quinn called for a
motion to waive. Commissioner McCroan so move. Commissioner Husband seconded
the motion. Chairman Quinn called for public comment. There being no public comment,
the motion passed unanimously. Planner Crane reported the P.D.R.B. recommended, by
4-0 vote, the Board approve this planned development project amendment. Upon inquiry
by Chairman Quinn, Mr. Rish discussed changing the project from 100 townhomes to 68

single family homes, noting the change will provide more green space. Commissioner
McLemore inquired if this amendment is in compliance with County rules. Planner Crane
discussed the LDR’s (Land Development Regulations) and the rules regarding P.D.P.
(Planned Development Project). Discussion followed regarding the amended planned
development project and side setbacks. Chairman Quinn called for a motion to waive the
formal reading. Commissioner McCroan so moved. Commissioner Husband seconded
the motion. Chairman Quinn called for public comment. There being none, the motion

BRIDGE REPLACEMENT – BUDDY FLOORE BRIDGE
County Administrator Hammond discussed the proposed plan to replace Buddy Floore
Bridge by the developer, requesting the Board to approve the bridge plan and the bypass
plan. Tyler Marsh, of Southeastern Consulting Engineers, Inc. appeared before the Board
to discuss the bridge replacement project, providing a site plan to the Board for review
<copy provided to the Clerk for the record>. Following discussion, Commissioner
Pridgeon discussed his concerns regarding the upcoming controlled burn and an
additional access route. County Administrator Hammond requested the Board to approve
the following: 1) developer will do the bridge; 2) the County will do the abutments; 3)
developer will pave the road; and 4) the developer will maintain the bypass until the
project is complete. Mr. Marsh reported the bypass will be completed in four (4) months.
Discussion followed regarding residents using Jarrott Daniels Road. County Administrator
Hammond noted if the Board approves this request, then staff will withdraw the rehab
project. Commissioner McCroan motioned to approve. Commissioner McLemore
seconded the motion. Commissioner Pridgeon requested staff notify E.M.S. and dispatch

of the alternate route. Chairman Quinn called for a clarification of the motion, inquiring if
the motion is to move forward and allow the developer to build the bridge, but also in the
motion for the County to withdraw the funds for the rehab of the bridge. County
Administrator Hammond noted he will bring the rehab project up separately. Chairman
Quinn then called the motion to allow the developer to take care of everything. Chairman
Quinn called for public comment. There being none, the motion passed unanimously (4-
0), with Commissioner Husband abstaining due to work on the project <FORM 8B on file
with the Clerk>. County Administrator Hammond noted the County provided an offer to
pave the road and the developer pay for paving, noting the County is only paying for the
abutments of the bridge (Buddy Floore).
