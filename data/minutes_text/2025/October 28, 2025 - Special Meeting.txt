INSURANCE
Reid Torgersen, of Torgersen Consulting, appeared before the Board to discuss the health
insurance renewal <copy provided to the Clerk for the record>, stating the claims have
not been favorable therefore, there is a higher renewal. He noted United Healthcare has
an HMO (health Maintenance organization) platform called NHP (Neighborhood Health
Program) that narrows the number of physicians and facilities, but it would be a more
manageable price point for the County and employees. Mr. Torgersen noted staff
recommendation is to offer $900.00 towards employees’ premium, noting any surplus
would be applied to dental, vision, or voluntary plans. He reported the dental renewal will
have a 3.5% increase and there is no increase to vision or life. Mr. Torgersen discussed
the Board going out to receive sealed bids for the voluntary benefits (Bid #2526-01),
noting County Administration recommend the Board award to AFLAC. He reported there
is a 45% increase in the rates for the new year. After Mr. Torgersen discussed the
insurance plan options, Commissioner Husband inquired if the deductible counts towards
the maximum out of pocket. Mr. Torgersen confirmed. Commissioner Husband noted HSA
(health savings account) does roll over year to year. Upon inquiry by Commissioner
Husband, Mr. Torgersen stated that preexisting conditions were covered. Discussion
followed regarding the health plan options. Members of the Board discussed increasing
the County’s contribution. Commissioner Husband discussed his concerns regarding the

three healthcare plans. Commissioner Pridgeon discussed continuing with last year’s
plan and increasing the contribution to cover pharmacy. Discussion followed regarding
the County contribution. Commissioner Husband discussed the County providing the
annual County contribution to the employee’s HSA account January 1, 2025. Discussion
followed regarding high claims and budgeting premiums. Upon inquiry by Commissioner
Husband, Mr. Torgersen stated only the previous year’s claims are factored into the plan
rates. Discussion followed regarding the United Healthcare rewards program, gym
membership, and high claims over the last two years. Commissioner McCroan discussed
his concerns regarding the increase and the plans presented. Discussion followed
regarding the cost of the family plan. Commissioner Husband made the motion that the
Board accept these three plans with the caveat that the County funds the HSA accounts
for each employee with the County’s portion of the funds on January 1, 2026, and Reid
Torgersen is giving the Board his word that he will go through with every single employee,
and spend as much time as possible to help every employee find a better family plan.
County Administrator Hammond inquired if the Board was choosing the $1,035.07 plan,
noting this the plan with no copay. Commissioner Husband said yes. Discussion followed.
Mr. Torgersen discussed keeping the two HSA plans as they are and adding the plan on
the left doing away with the prescription copay. Commissioner Husband added that to his
motion. Commissioner McCroan seconded the motion for discussion. After further
discussion, Commissioner Husband added to his motion to fund $1,000.00 per employee
towards insurance premiums. Chairman Quinn called for public comment. There being
no public comment, the motion passed unanimously.
Referring to pages 7 and 8 of the packet, Mr. Torgersen reported there is a 3% increase
in the dental premiums. Discussion followed regarding the dental plans provided.
Commissioner Pridgeon made the motion. Upon inquiry by Chairman Quinn for
clarification of the motion, Commissioner Pridgeon stated his motion is to renew the two
current plans. Commissioner Husband seconded the motion. Chairman Quinn called for
public comment. There being none, the motion passed unanimously.
Mr. Torgersen stated County Administration’s recommendation is to award (Bid #2526-
01) for voluntary benefits to AFLAC, noting these benefits will be individual plans available
to employees. Commissioner Husband motioned to approve. Commissioner McLemore
seconded the motion. Chairman Quinn called for public comment. There being no public
comment. The motion passed unanimously.

There being no further business and upon motion by Commissioner McCroan, seconded
by Commissioner McLemore, and unanimous vote. The meeting did then adjourn at 9:07
a.m., E.T.
