FWC UPDATE
FL Fish & Wildlife Director Jon Creamer appeared before the Board to provide a handout
<copy provided to the Clerk for the record> and discussed red tide in the area. He
discussed the uniqueness of the St. Joseph Bay and the difficulties of red tide exiting the
Bay. FWC Director Creamer stated FWC is working with the University of South Florida
regarding red tide forecast models. He reported that FWC is seeing a decrease in
numbers of red tide in the area, noting water samples were taken at the White City bridge,
and research is underway to see how the red tide reached Lake Wimico. Commissioner
McCroan discussed concerns with the amount of grass found in Lake Wimico. FWC
Director Creamer stated the grass should die out with the colder weather, noting FWC is
monitoring the matter. Upon inquiry by Commissioner McCroan, FWC Director Creamer
stated researchers don’t believe higher amounts of grass was the cause of red tide in
Lake Wimico. Commissioner Pridgeon inquired of the timeframe for the (chemical)
disbursement in South Florida. FWC Director Creamer noted the program is in the
development phase but could provide the Board with more information from the research
center on the subject. Commissioner Pridgeon noted he would like to add this topic to the
January retreat (workshop). Upon inquiry by Commissioner Pridgeon, FWC Director
Creamer discussed adding sampling sites in White City. Discussion followed. Upon
inquiry by Commissioner McLemore regarding how often the sample results are posted,

FWC Director Creamer stated website postings are updated as samplings results
received, noting samples are taken weekly. County Administrator Hammond thanked
FWC Director Creamer for the funding to clean up the aftermath of the red tide.
County Administrator Hammond discussed the (low water crossing) project on Sauls
Creek Road, noting the steepness of road seems to have leveled out. He stated the
County will know more in how high the water will be in the road during the next flood
event.
County Administrator Hammond requested an update on bears. FWC Director Creamer
reported the harvest number for bears will be reported at the end of the season, noting
there are a few harvests in the Panhandle. Discussion followed.
FWC Director Creamer thanked the Board and public regarding Sauls Creek Road, noting
that FWC will continue to monitor this road.
Chairman Quinn inquired if FWC was able to trap an injured bear near the Bridgeport
area. FWC Director Creamer noted a report was issued regarding an injured bear, stating
he will follow up with the Board on the outcome.
NOTICE OF APPLICATION – TAX DEED #2025-019
Upon discussion by County Administrator Hammond, Clerk Norris noted there is a tax
deed sale coming up for auction that is adjacent to County property. County Administrator
Hammond inquired whether the Board has a desire to bid on this parcel, noting the
property is located on U.S. Highway 22A (Parcel ID #01834-000R) and abuts the Gulf
County Health Department parcel. Clerk Norris reported the sale is scheduled for January
7, 2026, at 11:00 a.m., ET. Upon inquiry by Chairman Quinn, Clerk Norris stated it is
approximately 20+ acres. Discussion followed. Commissioner Husband requested that
County Attorney Novak conduct a title search on the parcel. Clerk Norris stated the
property information report is available for reviewing. County Attorney Novak stated he
would run a title search. Commissioner Husband discussed the possibility of any liens on
the property and bidding a reasonable amount. Discussion followed. Commissioner
Husband motioned, with the stipulation due to this property being adjoined by County
owned property, the County will make a reasonable offer during the online auction through
the Clerk’s Office. Commissioner McLemore seconded the motion. Chairman Quinn
called for public comment. There being none, the motion passed unanimously.

ADVERTISE TO RECEIVE SEALED BIDS – DISASTER RECOVERY & DEBRIS
REMOVAL SERVICES
From page 3 of the packet, Emergency Management Director Herring requested
permission to advertise to receive sealed bids for disaster recovery and debris removal
services. After discussion by Commissioner Pridgeon, Emergency Management Herring
discussed the bid procedure from the last bidding for these services, noting a committee
ranked and the top three bidders appeared before the Board for an interview before the
Board awarded the bid. County Administrator Hammond noted this procedure is not how
the County typically awards, inquiring how the Board would like to proceed. He discussed
options for the Board to consider: 1) staff rank and bring a bid award recommendation to
the Board for approval or 2) rank, then have the top three bidders appear before the Board
for an interview. After discussion by members of the Board, the Board had no objection
to the ranking and interviewing process for this bid. County Administrator Hammond
stated for clarification that staff will rank and then the Board will pick from the top three.
Discussion followed regarding the need to bid for the monitoring services (found on page
2 of the packet). Commissioner Pridgeon made the motion. Commissioner McCroan
seconded the motion. Chairman Quinn called for public comment. There being none, the
motion passed unanimously.
