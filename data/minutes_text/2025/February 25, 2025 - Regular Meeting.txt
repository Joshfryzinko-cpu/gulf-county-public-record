WHITE CITY BOAT RAMP
Commissioner Pridgeon discussed the White City Boat Ramp in need of repair and past
structures that were utilized at this site. He discussed working towards creating a fishing
area that is handicap accessible and making repairs to the docks. County Administrator
Hammond stated the County received this site from the Corps of Engineers, proposing to
line up the seawall where the old building once stood and possibly place a pavilion in the
future. County Administrator Hammond requested a motion to proceed with the plan.
Commissioner Pridgeon motioned to approve. Commissioner McLemore seconded the
motion. Commissioner McCroan inquired if this will go out for bids. County Administrator
Hammond noted it will all be done in-house. Chairman Quinn called for public comment.
Commissioner Husband inquired if the pavilion was part of the plan. County Administrator
Hammond stated he is currently asking permission to make sure permits are in order,
remove the existing seawall that is leaning into the Canal, and the County will cut the
bank back and slope it to the Canal to make it in line with both the east and west side.
Chairman Quinn called again for public comment. Charles Gathers, of Port St. Joe
appeared before the Board to inquire if the Board has considered the cost for this project.
Chairman Quinn noted the project will be done in-house. Following discussion regarding
cost, County Administrator Hammond noted, other than labor and County use of
equipment, the only thing the County may have to purchase is riprap, but he thinks the
County has enough in-house for this project. He then stated the cost will come when the
pavilion is installed. Sam Anderson, of White City appeared before the Board to inquire if

the rental fees for the boat slots could help offset the cost of this project. Chairman Quinn
again called for public comment. Christy McElroy, of Port St. Joe appeared before the
Board to inquire if OMB Director Herring has any information regarding the balance of the
parks revenue and if there will be any permits for this project. Commissioner Pridgeon
discussed the first step is to ensure the County has the permits in order. Following
additional discussion, Chairman Quinn called again for public comment. David Smith, of
White City appeared before the Board to discuss his concerns regarding the White City
Park, noting repairs have been made to the bathrooms. He asked the Board not to forget
the rest of the repairs needed at the park, noting his concerns regarding the pile of dirt
near the park entrance and an unsightly nearby grinder pumping station. Chairman Quinn
called for public comment. Discussion followed by Commissioner McLemore and Mr.
Smith regarding the park. Chairman Quinn then called for a motion to allow Mr. Smith
more time to speak. Commissioner Pridgeon made the motion. The motion failed for a
lack of a second. Chairman Quinn called for public comment. Mark Merrill, of White City
appeared before the Board to note the park needs more work than just a seawall, noting
it is not welcoming. He suggested the County offer a swimming area, stating there is no
access for swimming or kayaking. Chairman Quinn again called for any further public
comment. There being no further public comment, the motion passed unanimously.
Commissioner McCroan reported that the lift station belongs to the City of Port St. Joe.
County Administrator Hammond discussed the concerns stated regarding the sightly and
unsightly items near the park. He reported that the County has a good relationship with
D.O.T. (Florida Department of Transportation), noting that all of the parking at the White
City Boat Ramp is on D.O.T. property. Commissioner McCroan noted D.O.T. stages items
at Highland View also. County Administrator Hammond stated that staff will reach out to
the City of Port St. Joe regarding securing the lift station area.
