PROPOSED WHITE CITY AIRPORT & TYNDALL AIR FORCE BASE MISSION
County Administrator Hammond stated that Colonel Niebuhr, of Tyndall Air Force Base
canceled (on the agenda to speak), inquiring if the Board wanted to discuss pages 22
and 30 that were pulled from the Consent Agenda. Chairman Quinn had no objection,
calling anyone from the public wishing to speak on item 11, proposed White City Airport
and Tyndall Air Force Base mission. Commissioner Pridgeon noted the letters in the
Agenda from Tyndall. Upon inquiry by Commissioner Pridgeon, Deputy Administrator
Summers stated an email was sent regarding the cancellation but would reschedule at a
later date. Commissioner Pridgeon discussed the airspace and Tyndall’s operations
moving towards Gulf County. Discussion followed regarding the Tyndall Air Force Base.
Chairman Quinn discussed working with Tyndall and encouraged each member of the
Board to have a sit-down meeting with Colonel Niebuhr. Chairman Quinn called for public
comment. Brandon Murphy, of Cape San Blas appeared before the Board to discuss his
support of the Gulf County Airport and the economic and medical benefits. Major General
Don Ralph, of the Air Force appeared before the Board to express his support for the
airport project. Grant Rish, of Rish Real Estate Group, appeared before the Board to
discuss the surrounding air force bases that also have international airports, noting the
airport would be an asset to the County. Sarah Hodgson, St. Joe Beach appeared before
the Board to state she is not in support of the airport, discussing her concerns regarding
her home being in the flight path. Chairman Quinn called for any additional public
comment. Deborah Mayes with the Gulf County Citizens Coalition appeared before the
Board to discuss her concerns regarding the proposed international airport without flight
control and duplicating Franklin County’s airport. She stated she is in agreement with the
County talking with Tyndall Air Force Base before moving forward. Paul Puckli, of CHA
Consulting, appeared before the Board to discuss the airport proposal, noting there is a
way to work with Tyndall regarding the airspace concerns. He stated Gulf County is not
trying to duplicate what is going on in Apalachicola (Franklin County). Discussion followed
regarding the proposed airport and Tyndall Air Force Base. County Administrator
Hammond inquired if the comment period ended for the FAA in 2024, stating the County
received a letter of no objection or comment from Tyndall and Eglin. He reported that Gulf
County is currently not in Tyndall’s flight path nor in any restricted area, noting Gulf County
is 24 miles from Tyndall’s flight line. Upon inquiry by County Administrator Hammond
regarding a request to meet with Tyndall Air Force Base for two years, County Consultant
Yeager appeared before the Board to state a request to meet was after the first response.
He noted the initial draft was provided to Tyndall with a request to forward it to Eglin for
review. He stated that six weeks after the submittal, the County received Tyndall’s
response and a request to meet began in March 2024; discussing their efforts to set up a
meeting with Tyndall Air Force Base. Commissioner McCroan noted he has reached out
to Tyndall to request a meeting, and one has not been set yet, discussing the comments
made by Major General Ralph. He encouraged each member of the Board to meet with
Tyndall. Chairman Quinn agreed with a sit-down with Tyndall and inquired if FAA will
provide funding if Gulf County and Tyndall could not come to an agreement. CHA
Consultant Puckli stated, based on the airspace, he feels confident that the proposed
airport could work with the airspace environment. Commissioner Husband discussed his
concerns regarding the airport discussions, noting the County is in a feasibility study and
it must be completed before any environmental permits are issued. He discussed the

funding for the proposed airport. Johnathon Hand, of CHA Consultant, appeared before
the Board to state he is pro-airport, noting he is an aviation planner and commercial pilot,
and it would do nothing to diminish what the Air Force does. He stated CHA came today
prepared to talk with the Tyndall representative regarding their concerns with the
airspace. Commissioner Pridgeon discussed Tyndall and funding.

APPRAISAL REQUEST – LISTER LANDING
County Administrator Hammond discussed the meeting with Mr. Whitfield, requesting
Board approval for obtaining an appraisal on the Lister Landing piece (Parcel ID # 01037-
050R). Chairman Quinn called for a motion to allow County Administrator Hammond to
go out for an appraisal on that property. Commissioner McLemore made the motion.
Commissioner Husband seconded the motion. Chairman Quinn called for public
comment. There being none, the motion passed unanimously.
SCHEDULING – JUNE MEETING
County Administrator Hammond reminded everyone that the June meeting was
rescheduled to the Monday before. He stated in the event that something comes up and
needs a special meeting, then the bids will be open during the special, but otherwise
those bids coming in next week will be opened at the June meeting. Commissioner
Pridgeon inquired if a special meeting will be called if Tallahassee finalizes the
appropriations. County Administrator Hammond stated only if there is a crisis will there
be a special meeting called, noting staff is monitoring it daily.
BREAKWATER PROJECT
Cissy Mynatt of Save the Cape appeared before the Board to inquire on the Breakwaters
Project and discuss what the committee can do to assist with the breakwaters and sand.
She discussed the waiver during turtle season and the desire to go out for bids. County
Administrator Hammond stated he received permission from the Board to advertise to
receive sealed bids once funding is received, noting the County is $10 million short.
Discussion followed regarding advertising the bid. Ms. Mynatt discussed her concerns
regarding the erosion and inquired what they can do to help.
DRY DOCK GRANT
Deborah Mayes, of Gulf County Citizens Coalition appeared before the Board to discuss
the dry dock grant, noting the $15 million from the Port Authority is not going to be rolled
over into the County’s dry dock project and the marine academy has been removed from
the (Triumph) grant. She discussed her concerns with the marine academy not being
included. She continued to discuss the lease agreements, bonds, and the project,
requesting the Board hold a public meeting before going to Triumph with the amended
application. Commissioner Pridgeon discussed the comments from Triumph regarding
the dredge funding for the Port Authority and his concerns regarding the education
element. County Administrator Hammond discussed going to the Triumph Board, noting
the Port Authority is willing to transfer the $15 million for their dredging project to the dry
dock project and marine academy. Following discussion, E.D.C. Director McKnight
appeared before the Board to discuss the Triumph application and Eastern Shipbuilding.
BEACH RAKING
John Ehrman, of Cape San Blas appeared before the Board to inquire if FWC is aware of
the beach raking and depositing at the base of the dunes. Commissioner Husband noted
the deposit is on the landward tow of the dune. Discussion followed regarding the smell.

There being no further business, and upon motion by Commissioner Husband, second
by Commissioner McCroan, and unanimous vote, the meeting did adjourn at 12:09 p.m.,
