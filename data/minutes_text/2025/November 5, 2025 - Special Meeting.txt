NOVEMBER 5, 2025
SPECIAL MEETING
PORT ST. JOE, FLORIDA
The Gulf County Board of County Commissioners met this date in regular session with
the following members present: Chairman Sandy Quinn, Jr., Vice Chairman Phil
McCroan, and Commissioners Christopher McLemore, Lloyd Jack Husband, III, and
Randy Pridgeon.
Others present were: County Attorney Jeremy Novak, Chief Deputy Clerk Leanna
Cooper, Deputy Clerk Casi Campbell, County Administrator Michael L. Hammond, County
Assistant Administrator Clay Smallwood, III, Deputy Administrator Kari Summers, Deputy
Administrator Silvia Williams, Emergency Management Director/Fire Coordinator Matt
Herring, Gulf County E.M.S. Director Jody Daniels, G.I.S. Coordinator Clay Kennedy,
Grant Administrator Erin Kennedy, Office of Management & Budget Director Sherry
Herring, Mosquito Control Director Austin Horton, County Planner Doug Crane, Public
Works Director Mark Cothran, and Sheriff Mike Harrison.
Chairman Quinn called the meeting to order at 2:00 p.m., E.T.
Chairman Quinn led in the Pledge of Allegiance to the Flag.
COUNTY LITIGATION
County Attorney Novak reported that the County has received a signed Order from the
Judge regarding the oyster litigation (Indian Lagoon).
County Attorney Novak discussed a lawsuit against the City of Port St. Joe regarding the
annexation of 115 acres in the Industrial Park. He reported the County has received
favorable rulings from the Courts with regards to previous annexation lawsuits, stating the
County is working with the City of Port St. Joe to settle this matter. County Attorney Novak
noted the City of Port St. Joe called a special executive session November 4, 2025
whereby voting to accept the settlement agreement in their regular meeting. He requested
Board ratify the settlement agreement with the City of Port St. Joe, reading aloud the
terms of the settlement agreement regarding annexation of Parcel ID #04269-000R.
County Attorney Novak stated once the settlement agreement is fully executed, a
stipulation will be filed with the Court with prejudice. After discussion by members of the
Board, County Attorney Novak requested Board approval to allow the Chairman to sign
the settlement agreement with the City of Port St. Joe. Commissioner McCroan so moved.
Commissioner Pridgeon seconded the motion. Chairman Quinn called for public
comment. There being no public comment, the motion passed unanimously.

County Administrator Hammond stated the Board authorized him to negotiate and draft a
contract to purchase a small parcel on Industrial Road (Parcel ID #04571-000R),
requesting direction from the Board regarding the price offer. County Attorney Novak
discussed mediation held with the City of Port St. Joe regarding the annexation (Parcel
ID #04571-000R) and spot zoning. He discussed the conflict resolution, mediation,
obtaining an appraisal, and the offers to purchase the land, requesting direction from the
Board in how to proceed with the purchase price (#04571-000R) as the property owner
has made a counteroffer. Commissioner Husband stated he does not want to go higher
than the County’s last best offer, noting if the County purchases this parcel, he wants the
parcel to remain in the County. Discussion followed regarding the parcel remaining in the
County, litigation with the City of Port St. Joe and the County’s offer to purchase the
property. Commissioner McCroan motioned that the County has given the landowner our
highest and best offer, providing the property owner until noon Friday to accept the offer.
County Attorney Novak requested the time be changed to an hour early. Commissioner
McCroan had no objection to amending his motion to change the time to 11:00 a.m., E.T.
Commissioner Husband seconded the motion for discussion. After further discussion,
Commissioner McCroan included in his motion that if the County purchases this parcel it
will remain in the County. County Administrator Hammond discussed proposed stipulation
agreement, inquiring if there would be any proposed conflicts. County Attorney Novak
requested permission for the Board to speak with County Administrator Hammond
privately. The Board had no objection.
HIGHLAND VIEW REHAB FACILITY PROPOSAL
Commissioner Pridgeon discussed the concerns of a possible drug rehabilitation facility
utilizing the Outlook apartments in Highland View, noting a public meeting was held at the
Highland View Fire Station and a petition was drafted in opposition. He noted that the City
of Port St. Joe is reviewing City policies to address the matter. County Attorney Novak
discussed the first reading by the City of Port St. Joe of a proposed ordinance to restrict
sober homes within the City limits. Commissioner Pridgeon inquired if the County needs
to mirror the City’s proposed ordinance. County Attorney Novak discussed drafting a
proposed ordinance to advertise to hold a public hearing, noting in the interim the County
could adopt a proposed resolution on the matter. Doug Kelly, of Highland View appeared
before the Board to inquire if the Board could count the RV (recreational vehicle) park in
the proposed ordinance as a park. County Attorney Novak noted that it is considered a
recreational vehicle (RV) park. Mr. Kelly then inquired if the County has State regulations
regarding the distance from a park or church. County Attorney Novak discussed following
State guidelines. Discussion followed regarding State guidelines and the concerns of the
proposed drug rehabilitation facility in Highland View. Chairman Quinn discussed
reviewing the proposed language from surrounding Cities’ proposed ordinances.
Commissioner Pridgeon inquired if the Board needs to adopt a proposed resolution today.
County Attorney Novak stated no, noting he will draft a proposed ordinance then seek
Board approval to advertise to hold a public hearing over the holidays. Commissioner
Husband discussed the need for an impact study before an impact fee can be imposed,
noting he is not in support of any residential impact fee. Discussion followed. County
Attorney Novak discussed reaching out to the property owner regarding the intent, noting
whether the property owner’s moves forward, the County needs to address the topic.

COUNTY LITIGATION
County Administrator Hammond discussed his concerns regarding the motion, noting the
City of Port St. Joe has a deadline to accept the County’s settlement agreement. He
reported the proposed agreement would place a 5 (five) year moratorium, but the City of
Port St. Joe would be able to annex the parcel (#04571-000R). County Administrator
Hammond noted if the County does not purchase this property there is still a pending
agreement between the County and City of Port St. Joe that the Board would need to vote
on, noting the pending settlement agreement with the City of Port St. Joe is in conflict with
the motion on the floor. After discussion by Commissioner McCroan, County Attorney
Novak recommended the Board vote on the land purchase first, and then he will provide
a recommendation to the Board regarding the lawsuit with the City of Port St. Joe.
Chairman Quinn stated the motion on the floor is the highest best with a deadline of 11:00
a.m., E.T. County Attorney Novak noted the recommendation by Commissioner Husband
was if the County purchased the property, then it would remain in the County. He stated
the annexation is the issue and if the parcel goes into the City, then he would request
flexibility of the Board to allow the County Administrator Hammond to negotiate and bring
back a recommendation to the Board that the County maintain jurisdiction over the parcel
with regards to regulator, inspections, permitting, and etc. if the parcel is annexed into the
City of Port St. Joe or it will remain in the County, noting staff requested Board flexibility.
Commissioner Husband had no objection. Chairman Quinn called for public comment.
There being none, the motion passed unanimously. County Attorney Novak reported that
if the County does not have a resolve with the City by the provided deadline or County
purchase, then the County will take next steps to have the City of Port St. Joe served.
ADMINISTRATIVE UPDATE & REPORT
Fire Coordinator Herring appeared before the Board to present a recommendation to the
Board from page 1 from the Administrative Update and Report Packet <copy provided to
the Clerk for the record> allowing him to purchase two (2) extrication/JAWS of life tools
for Highland View Fire Department, noting the purchase price is $19,220.00, plus freight
from sole-source vendor, Ten-8 Fire & Safety. He noted the purchase request follows the
allowable exceptions to the bid process being sole-source vendor and exception
purchase due to the compatibility of existing goods and services. Discussion followed
regarding training needs and the provided quote. Chairman Quinn called for a motion to
move forward with purchasing. Commissioner Pridgeon made the motion. Commissioner
McLemore seconded the motion. Chairman Quinn called for public comment. There being
none, the motion passed unanimously.
WHITE CITY FIRE TRUCK
Fire Coordinator Herring requested Board approval to allow him to purchase a fire truck
for the White City Fire Department <page 4 of the packet>, reporting the funds are in their
budget. He recommended the Board allow the White City Fire Department to maintain the
same model fleet with the allowable exception to purchase sole-source due to availably
of goods and services with a delivery date on twelve (12) months upon signing.
Commissioner Pridgeon made the motion. Chairman Quinn called the motion to approve
it. Commissioner Husband seconded the motion. Chairman Quinn called for public
comment. There being no public comment, the motion passed unanimously.

ADMINISTRATIVE UPDATE & REPORT

County Administrator Hammond reported that the County submitted the Legislative
appropriations requests, noting the committee hearings will be held next month.
County Administrator Hammond stated he will be placing a letter in the upcoming
information packet regarding a property owner that had to tear their house down to the
pilings and restart construction due to a private provider not following the rules (Red Tape
Florida).
County Administrator Hammond discussed the desire to work with the City of Port St. Joe

with the City of Wewahitchka to provide these services. Discussion followed regarding

Commissioner McCroan had no objection to submitting a letter. Discussion followed

OMB Director Herring requested Board approve payment of an invoice to the (Gulf Coast
Electric Cooperative) Coop. County Administrator Hammond noted the invoice is
