PUBLIC HEARING – ANIMAL CONTROL ORDINANCE
County Administrator Hammond provided facts and a timeline regarding the Animal
Control Ordinance (#2021-06), reporting it is a County Ordinance, not Humane Society
and there is no dog killing in this Ordinance. He stated the only change is that the County
will be responsible for all dog bites and dangerous animals. County Attorney Novak read
the proposed ordinance by title. Following discussion, Commissioner McCroan requested
Diana Burkett to appear, stating the intent was to take the dog-bites and aggressive dogs
off the plate of the (St. Joseph Bay) Humane Society. He noted he has agreed to sit-down
with the Humane Society and look at the proposed language. Commissioner McCroan
stated it is up to the Humane Society to how long they want to keep the dogs (at the
shelter). Commissioner Pridgeon discussed working with the Humane Society regarding
unadoptable dogs. Diana Burkett, of the St. Joseph Bay Humane Society appeared
before the Board to discuss the matter within each District regarding responsible owners
and the need to discuss the amended language within the proposed ordinance. She
discussed the operation of the shelter and the need to control the flow of these animals
getting spade or neutered. County Administrator Hammond discussed the need for the
Municipalities to adopt the animal control ordinance. Mrs. Burkett requested that the
Board table the proposed ordinance and have a sit-down with the Humane Society to
discuss the language. She volunteered to present the proposed ordinance to the
Municipalities for consideration. Chairman Quinn discuss assigning Commissioner
McCroan has the contact person for the sit-down with the Humane Society and bring back
a recommendation for the Board to consideration. Following discussion, Commissioner
McCroan made the motion. Chairman Quinn called the motion to table (adoption of the
proposed animal control ordinance). Commissioner McLemore seconded the motion.
Commissioner McCroan noted he will meet with the Humane Society. County
Administrator Hammond inquired about the wording of the motion and whether it is to be
tabled until the next meeting, noting the Public Hearing will need to be advertised, or does
the Board wish to table it indefinitely. County Attorney Novak discussed tabling today,
noting staff will readvertise for another Public Hearing with the new proposed language.
Chairman Quinn then called for public comment. Christy McElroy, of Port St. Joe
appeared before the Board to discuss her concerns regarding the Board agreeing to meet
with the Humane Society regarding this matter, providing copies of the meeting minutes
<copy provided to the Clerk for the record>. She asked the Board to rescind the vote and
hold a workshop. Chairman Quinn called for any further public comment. There being
none, the motion passed unanimously.

ADMINISTRATION UPDATE & REPORT

County Administrator Hammond discussed the deadline to submit the DOGE report,

regarding the DOGE report, noting staff submitted all data within the allotted time and

County Administrator Hammond noted he will ask the Board at the next meeting to set

working on the matter.
