JULY 21, 2025
SPECIAL MEETING
PORT ST. JOE, FLORIDA
The Gulf County Board of County Commissioners met this date in special session with
the following members present: Chairman Sandy Quinn, Jr., Vice Chairman Phil
McCroan, and Commissioners Christopher McLemore, Lloyd Jack Husband, III, and
Randy Pridgeon.
Others present were: Director of Finance & Management Leo Bebeau, Deputy Clerk
Leanna Cooper, County Administrator Michael L. Hammond, County Assistant
Administrator Clay Smallwood, III, Deputy Administrator Kari Summers, Building Official
Brad Bailey, County Consultant Brett Lowry, Gulf County E.M.S. Director Jody Daniels,
G.I.S. Coordinator Clay Kennedy, Grant Administrator Erin Kennedy, Grant Administrator
Laura Beech-Armstrong, Human Resource Director Kaci Rhodes, IT Department
Supervisor Dennis Barfield, Sr., Office of Management & Budget Director Sherry Herring,
Mosquito Control Director Austin Horton, County Planner Doug Crane, Property Appraiser
Mitch Burke, Property Appraiser IT Tech Dennis Barfield, Jr., Public Works Director Mark
Cothran, Assistant Public Works Director Raymond Hart, Sheriff Mike Harrison,
Supervisor of Elections Rhonda Pierce, and Tax Collector Ashley Forehand.
Chairman Quinn called the meeting to order at 9:00 a.m., E.T.
Chairman Quinn opened the meeting with prayer and then led in the Pledge of Allegiance
to the Flag.
TENTATIVE BUDGET FISCAL YEAR 2025-2026
County Administrator Hammond discussed the initial proposed budget that was provided
to the Board by July 15, 2025, as required, noting that it is a working document that is
subject to change until the Final Budget Hearing to be held in September 2025. He
requested the Board to set the maximum millage rates so staff can certify to the Property
Appraiser for the General Fund and each of the Fire Control Districts, set a zero rate for
the M.S.T.U.s, noting they were paid off last year, and set dates and times for the
proposed budget meetings. County Administrator Hammond reported the first date will
have to be included on the TRIM notice. He stated staff is proposing a 5.6 millage rate
Countywide, which is a 5.2 reduction overall. County Administrator Hammond reported
that by the Board reducing the current millage rate of 5.9 to 5.6 it will save $1,191,985.00
for the taxpayers, noting this will be the fifth year in a row the County has been able to
reduce the millage.
GULF COUNTY TAX COLLECTOR
Gulf County Tax Collector Forehand appeared before the Board to note her budget
request is asking for adjustments for pay raises and equipment for the tax department.

She discussed implementing some updates in the office. County Administrator Hammond
noted on page 11 (of the initial proposed budget) <copy provided to the Clerk for the
record> shows a 5% pay raise across the board. He stated the big change was that the
Tax Collector’s Office will be changing from 35 hours a week to 40 hours, noting there is
a 7% change for the projected insurance change.
GULF COUNTY SUPERVISOR OF ELECTIONS
Supervisor of Elections Pierce noted from the audience that she is good.
GULF COUNTY PROPERTY APPRAISER
Property Appraiser Burke appeared before the Board to report no increase in his budget,
stating his office is $35,000.00 less than last year. He reported on the tax roll, noting the
County is currently up 10.9%. He thanked the Board for lowering the millage rate, stating
the City of Port St. Joe is up 11.48%. Property Appraiser Burke reported new construction
is down 19% over last year. County Administrator Hammond encouraged Property
Appraiser Burke to explain to the public the difference between the County and the School
Board due to the caps. Property Appraiser Buke stated the School Board does not take
in the second $25,000.00 of the homestead exemption and those that are not homestead
there is no 10% cap, noting it goes straight to market value. Discussion followed regarding
capping and taxable value.
GULF COUNTY CLERK OF COURT
Director of Finance & Management Bebeau noted the Clerk is good with everything that
is presented. He thanked the Board for their support.
GULF COUNTY SHERIFF’S OFFICE
Sheriff Harrison appeared before the Board to discuss his budget, noting the LESA
funding coming from the State is offsetting law enforcement salaries. He stated he is
looking to open two more positions through the LEAP (law enforcement apprenticeship
program), discussing his concerns regarding recruiting. Sheriff Harrison discussed some
adjustments to his proposed budget and beach patrol.
INSURANCE
County Consultant Lowry appeared before the Board to report that the County is
expecting a 5% increase, plus whatever the workers comp payroll is based on payroll
increases. He stated the health insurance was recently changed from an October 1
renewal to a January 1 renewal date. County Administrator Hammond noted 7% was
calculated for healthcare and are hoping for a better rate for workers comp. Discussion
followed regarding healthcare coverage.
County Administrator Hammond stated the Countywide assessment is up 10.97%, values
of the Fire Control Districts and CRA/DRA, proposing a 5.6 millage rate that is a reduction
of 5.08%. He recommended all the Fire Control Districts stay at .5 mill and zero out the
M.S.T.U. for the Beachfront, Interior and Bayside. County Administrator Hammond noted
the Countywide millage would generate $22,250,000.00, St. Joseph Fire District,
$1,330,000.00, Tupelo Fire District $112,00.00, $57,000.00 for Overstreet Fire District

and Howard Creek Fire District $29,932.00. He provided an overview of the budget
recapitulation and millage levy. Discussion followed regarding the budget, various
projects, E.M.S., water department, holding a workshop in September to discuss fire
fighter stipends and a workshop in October to discuss E.M.S. Public Works Director Hart
discussed the matter of locating a supplier for the service flags and the American Flag at
Veterans’ Memorial Beacon Hill Park, noting the current supplier has changed the material
(fabric). OMB Director Herring discussed the parks fund. County Administrator Hammond
discussed the need for a canopy at the fueling depot, noting funds have been added into
the proposed budget for a pole barn type covering. Discussion followed regarding the
overview highlight of the proposed budget. County Administrator Hammond requested
the Board approve the Capital Outlay Reserves Plan. After discussion, Chairman Quinn
called for a motion to approve the updates on this last page (the last page titled FY2526
Budget – Capital Outlay Reverses for Future Projects). Commissioner Pridgeon made the
motion. Commissioner Husband seconded the motion. Chairman Quinn called for public
comment. There being no public comment, the motion passed unanimously. County
Administrator Hammond discussed an upcoming ribbon cutting at the County Country
Club Golf Course, thanking staff. He discussed the proposed animal control ordinance,
noting this is on the agenda for tomorrow. County Administrator Hammond reported the
only dogs the County is taking are dog bites and vicious dogs, all other animals picked
up will be handled by the Humane Society. Following discussion, County Administrator
Hammond noted the Port Authority finally has a full membership of their Board, inquiring
if the Board would be in favor to extend the deadline on the Port Authority’s loan and
negotiating with the Port Authority to get Maritime Academy on their land, across from
Eastern Shipbuilding, giving them a little working capital. The Board had no objection.
County Administrator Hammond noted he will work on this project and bring back an
agreement back before the Board for ratification. He concluded his discussion regarding
the proposed tentative budget. After discussion by members of the Board, Chairman
Quinn stated it has been five years since the Board negotiated a contract for the County
Administrator and inquired of the Board thoughts of providing Mr. Hammond a raise.
Commissioner Husband nominated Chairman Quinn to complete County Administrator
Hammonds evaluation. Commissioner McLemore inquired if this would be done annually.
Commissioner Husband stated yes. After discussion by members of the Board,
Commissioner Husband made a motion to nominate the Chairman. Commissioner
McCroan seconded the motion. Chairman Quinn called for public comment. Annette
Ryan, of Cape San Blas appeared before the Board to inquire where she could get a copy
of the current proposed budget and asked if at the next budget meeting the public be
provided copies. OMB Director Herring noted this is a working copy and it is not printed,
reporting that proposed budgets will be posted online according to Florida Statute. She
stated that she did create a summary sheet, but failed to announce to the public before
the meeting started <copy provided to the Clerk for the record>. Following discussion, the
motion then passed unanimously. Commissioner Pridgeon discussed placing a ‘hot sheet’
on the back on the TRIM notice this year so all voters will see the millage and County
goals. OMB Director Herring discussed her concerns regarding the deadline for providing
to the Property Appraiser, noting changes will be made up to the final budget meeting.
Property Appraiser Burke appeared before the Board to state the County information
would be on the backside of a separate letter that is mailed with the TRIM, not printed on

the actual TRIM notice. Chairman Quinn called for public comment. There was no public
comment. County Administrator Hammond requested the Board adopt the maximum
millage 5.6 mills Countywide. Commissioner Pridgeon so moved. Commissioner
McCroan seconded the motion. Chairman Quinn called for public comment. There being
none, the motion passed unanimously. County Administrator Hammond then asked the
Board to adopt .5 mill each for each of the four Fire Control Districts. Commissioner
McCroan so moved. Commissioner McLemore seconded the motion. Chairman Quinn
called for public comment. There being no public comment, the motion passed
unanimously. Upon request by County Administrator Hammond, Commissioner Husband
motioned to adopt a zero levy for the M.S.T.U.s. Commissioner McCroan seconded the
motion. Chairman Quinn called for public comment. There being no public comment, the
motion passed unanimously. County Administrator Hammond requested permission from
the Board to allow administration to certify to the Property Appraiser those millages just
adopted. Commissioner McCroan so moved. Commissioner McLemore seconded the
motion. Chairman Quinn called for public comment. There being none, the motion passed
unanimously. County Administrator Hammond discussed the proposed hearing date of
Wednesday, September 3, 2025, at 5:01 p.m., E.T. Commissioner Husband made the
motion to do it Wednesday, September 3, 2025, at 5:01 p.m., E.T. Commissioner
McCroan seconded the motion. Chairman Quinn called for public comment. There being
none, the motion passed unanimously. Upon discussion by County Administrator
Hammond and OMB Director Herring, Commissioner Pridgeon motioned to hold the final
budget hearing for September 16, 2025, at 5:01 p.m., E.T. Commissioner McLemore
seconded the motion. Chairman Quinn called for public comment. There being none, the
motion passed unanimously. County Administrator Hammond discussed final numbers
once staff received insurance information and Constitutional Officers salaries from the
State.
There being no further business, and upon motion by Commissioner McCroan, second
by Commissioner Husband, and unanimous vote, the meeting did then adjourn at 10:51
a.m., E.T.
