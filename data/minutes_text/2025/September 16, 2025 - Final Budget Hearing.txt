2025-26 BUDGET OVERVIEW
County Administrator Hammond stated there were very few changes to the proposed
budget, noting a small increase as follows: 1) Howard Creek and Overstreet Cash Carried
Forward; 2) increase of $175,920.00 in the Opioid Fund; 3) General Grant increase is
almost 100% for the underwater structures project; 4) Fuel Tax money; and 5) Cash
Carried Forward for actual ½ cent sales tax for the hospital. O.M.B. Director Herring noted
the changes that County Administrator Hammond mentioned will need Board approval.
She then stated the tentative aggregate millage rate is 5.9851, noting this is 2.02% greater
than the current year’s aggregate rollback rate of 5.8665. OMB Director Herring reported
that the tentative aggregate millage rate is made up of the Countywide millage rate of 5.6
mils and Dependent Fire District: St. Joseph, Tupelo, Overstreet and Howard Creek each
at .5 mils, noting the County is not levying the M.S.T.U. voted debt service millage for
2025. She also noted that these are the maximum millages that the Board may adopt.
OMB Director Herring stated a roll call vote is required; providing reasons for ad valorem

tax increase, as follows: 1) Sheriff’s Office; 2) other four Constitutional Officers; 3) E.M.S.;
4) Jail; 5) firefighter incentives; 6) insurance costs; 7) pay raises for employees; 8) park
renovations; and 9) Port St. Jospeh Downtown Redevelopment CRA. She reported that
today’s Hearing has been advertised in the newspaper and the tentative budget has been
posted on the County’s website. She noted on page 5 of the tentative budget book is the
balanced totals of each fund and reminded the Board any increases in expenditures will
require an equivalent reduction in a specified line-item. OMB Director Herring requested
approval of the Board for the following changes: 1) Fund 108 increase $908.00 in revised
revenue estimate and off-setting adjustments to reserve and expenditures; 2) Fund 109
increase $3,000.00 in revised revenue estimates and off-setting adjustments to reserve
and expenditures; 3) Fund 111 increase $175,920.00 revised revenue estimates and
adjustments to expenditures; 4) Fund 114 net increase $7.5 million; 5) Fund 124 increase
$600,000.00 revised revenue estimate and off-setting adjustments to reserve and
expenditures; 6) Fund 203 increase $35,245.00 to revised revenue and offsetting
adjustment s to reserves and expenditures; and 7) all funds to make line-item adjustments
to revenues, reserves, and expenditures within each fund prior to publishing the final
budget book to account for any personnel changes, contracts, revised state estimates,
the elected official salaries, and other necessary adjustments, not affecting ad valorem or
the total by fund. Chairman Quinn called for a motion to approve staff recommended
changes. Commissioner Husband made the motion. Commissioner McCroan seconded
the motion. Chairman Quinn called for public comment. There being no public comment,
the motion passed unanimously.
Chairman Quinn opened the Public Hearing to the public, calling for public comment. Skip
Marlin, representing Friends of the Library, appeared before the Board to request
additional funds in the amount of $32,590.00, discussing this year’s shortfall. He noted
the potential for losing staff due to the upcoming shortfall. Commissioner Pridgeon
discussed the increases received from the County over the past two years and the
statewide decrease in State funding for libraries. Discussion followed regarding
implementing a capital campaign. Chairman Quinn encouraged Mr. Marlin to reach out to
the Municipalities. Chairman Quinn called for any additional public comment. There being
no further public comment, Chairman Quinn closed the Public Hearing to the public.
County Administrator Hammond reported there were a few outside agencies that received
an increase, and the library was increased by 5%, noting that less than 14% of the budget
is funded by ad valorem tax dollars, the remaining is funded by grants and State share
revenue. Commissioner Pridgeon discussed State projecting future revenues going
down, discussing his concerns regarding next year’s budget. Members of the Board
expressed their concerns regarding decrease in permits, and changes coming from the
State. County Administrator Hammond stated the Board has cut taxes every year except
for the year after Hurricane Michael. He noted there is very little chance of cutting the
millage next year. Chairman Quinn thanked the grant department for the work they do to
get projects funded. OMB Director Herring stated the Port St. Joe Library’s initial request
was $8,000 and the library in Wewahitchka was $5,000.00.
