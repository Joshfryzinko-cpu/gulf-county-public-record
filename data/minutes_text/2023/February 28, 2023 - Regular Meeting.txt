MEMORANDUM OF UNDERSTATING – SACRED HEART HEALTH SYSTEM
County Administrator Hammond reported that the Hospital Trust Board requested an
addendum to the funding agreement and the Gulf County Health Trust Board met to bring
a recommendation to the Board for consideration. Kellie Beach, of Sacred Heart Hospital,
appeared before the Board. County Administrator Hammond stated that the Gulf County
Health Trust Board recommends to the Board to approve the amendment to the current
funding agreement. He reported that it is approximately $1.7 million with a four (4) year
change, stating this does not change the bottom-line total number of $21,522,460.00, but
may have an earlier termination date. Ms. Beach discussed the hospital’s request due to
the rising healthcare cost and contract labor following COVID. Commissioner McCroan
stated that he would like to see staff reach out to surrounding Counties for financial
support, discussing his concerns with Gulf County providing urgent care to other Counties
which eats into Gulf County’s funding. Ms. Beach discussed her concerns with looking at
the charity care for fiscal year 2022 and there are some opportunities, reporting she has
been charged with reinitiating that conversation (with the surrounding Counties).
Commissioner Farrell discussed the need to reach out to Franklin County and the
hospital. Ms. Beach reported the hospital (Scared Heart) is open and the mask mandate
has been lifted, reminding the community there is a fish fry every Friday. County
Administrator Hammond discussed primary care for the north end of the County and
marketing the hospital (Scared Hospital) as open and faster wait times, stating that this
is a County-wide tax. County Attorney Novak reported that he has the Resolution which
was adopted by the Health Trust Board to amend the pay scale <copy provided to the
Clerk for the record>. He then presented a proposed memorandum of understanding to
the Board for consideration <copy provided to the Clerk for the record>, reading the
highlights aloud. Commissioner Farrell so moved to approve. Commissioner Farrell
seconded the motion. Chairman Quinn called for public comment. There being none, the
motion passed unanimously (4-0).

BID #2223-12 – REGIONAL AIRPORT FACILITY
County Attorney Novak discussed the P3 (public private partnership) unsolicited
advertisement for the Gulf County Airport, reporting that the County has received one
response, being pages 7-9 of the packet. He requested a vote of the Board to allow
County Administrator Hammond to proceed with a preliminary agreement with The St.
Joe Company (only proposal submitted) on the P3. He stated, under Statute 255, the
County will place an advertisement in the Administrative Register up to two weeks
following today, reporting he will, with the assistance of the Clerk’s office, properly
advertise and in the meantime the County Administrator will move forward in good faith
to begin an outline with St. Joe Company. County Attorney Novak again requested a vote
of the Board to authorize the Administrator to move forward, with him advertising again in
the Register. Chairman Quinn called for a motion to move forward. Commissioner Farrell
so moved. Commissioner Rich seconded the motion. Chairman Quinn called for public
comment. Christy McElroy, of Port St. Joe appeared before the Board to discuss her
concerns regarding the airport, inquiring who the Board sees as clientele for this proposed
Regional Airport Facility and a timeline. County Administrator Hammond reported that
Gulf County is one of the only Counties in the State of Florida that does not have paved
runway, stating it is an economic development engine, a five (5) year process, and will
not be a Panama City Beaches/Northwest Florida International Airport. He stated the
facility will be a private, air cargo potentially, and things of that nature. Chairman Quinn
called for additional public comment. There was no further public comment. County
Attorney Novak noted this is a Public Hearing today, reading highlights from the bid
specifications. Chairman Quinn called for public comment, opening to the public. Francine
Vellanti, of Cape San Blass appeared before the Board in opposition to the airport facility.
Chairman Quinn called for additional public comment. There being no further public
comment, Chairman Quinn closed the Public Hearing to the public. The motion then
passed unanimously (4-0).

Planner Crane read aloud the recommendations from the P.D.R.B. regarding amendments

from agricultural to residential or mixed commercial/residential, maximum allowable density
would be lowered from the possible four (4) units per acre to two (2) units per acre which is
on page 66 in the LDR. P.D.R.B. Chairman Butler appeared before the Board to discuss the
first recommendation to the Board for the proposed change from four (4) to two (2) units per
acre. Discussion followed regarding the difference in terrain from the north to south ends of
the County. Upon inquiry by Chairman Quinn, County Administrator Hammond
recommended voting on these recommendations one at a time as there may be public
comment on some. Commissioner McCroan so moved to approve. Commissioner Farrell
seconded the motion. Chairman Quinn called for public comment. Coastal Community
Association President Pat Hardman appeared before the Board to discuss these proposed
changes and the diversity between south Gulf and the beaches to north Gulf. Chairman
Quinn called for additional public comment. Ralph Fisher, of Indian Pass appeared before
the Board to discuss his concerns regarding the diversity between the south and north ends
of the County. After discussion by members of the Board, Chairman Quinn called for public
comment. Francine Vellanti, of Cape San Blas appeared before the Board to discuss her
previous request for one (unit) per acre and her concerns regarding land clearing near her
residence. Chairman Quinn called for any additional public comment. There being no further

public comment, the motion passed unanimously (4-0). 2) When changing future land use to
mixed commercial/residential, allowable impervious area would be 30% for commercial
instead of the current 70% as stated on page 110 of the LDR. P.D.R.B. Chairman Butler
appeared before the Board to discuss this recommendation pertaining to commercial, noting
the previous vote by the Board was for residential. Commissioner Farrell moved to approve.
Commissioner McCroan seconded the motion. Chairman Quinn called for public comment.
There being none, the motion passed unanimously (4-0). 3) Add the following text to County
LDR Section 5.01.02, page 109, allowing the possibility of an engineered paver system to
be considered, statement would read: “If porous paving materials are used in accordance
with acceptable engineering practices, then the area or a percentage of the area covered
with porous paving materials shall not be counted as impervious surface. All other pavers
are counted as impervious.” Commissioner Farrell inquired what the percentage would be
for the pavers. Planner Crane discussed the recommended text, stating it would be a
percentage as the staff does not see a scenario where 100% would be given. P.D.R.B.
Chairman Butler stated it is all about stormwater, trying to maintain impervious areas at 30%.
County Administrator Hammond reported this will be rare and extremely expensive, if the
credit is granted, discussing his concerns regarding sticking to the 30% and stormwater
issues. Commissioner Rich moved to approve. Commissioner Farrell seconded the motion.

117, requiring additional parking spaces for dwelling units with more than four (4) bedrooms:

Community Association President Pat Hardman appeared before the Board to request
adding one more parking space beginning with four (4) bedrooms. She also discussed her

Remove the following statement from County LDR Section 3.02.04 from page 67, allowing
