SEPTEMBER 6, 2023
BUDGET HEARING
PORT ST. JOE, FLORIDA
The Gulf County Board of County Commissioners met this date in special budget session
with the following members present: Chairman Sandy Quinn, Jr., Vice Chairman Phil
McCroan, and Commissioners David C. Rich, Jack Husband, and Patrick Farrell.
Others present were: Director of Finance & Management Leo Bebeau, Deputy Clerk
Leanna Cooper, County Administrator Michael L. Hammond, County Assistant
Administrator Clay Smallwood, III, Deputy Administrator Kari Summers, Building Official
Brad Bailey, Gulf County E.M.S. Director Jody Daniels, Grant Administrator Kaci Rhodes,
Grant Administrator Erin Kennedy, Human Resource Director Brett Lowry, Office of
Management & Budget Director Sherry Herring, Mosquito Control Director Austin Horton,
County Planner Doug Crane, Property Appraiser Mitch Burke, Public Works Director Mark
Cothran, Assistant Public Works Director Raymond Hart, Sheriff Mike Harrison, Tax
Collector Shirley Jenkins, and T.D.C. Executive Director Silvia Williams.
Chairman Quinn called the meeting to order at 5:06 p.m.,E.T.
TENTATIVE 2023-24 OVERVIEW
County Administrator Hammond stated that not much had changed since the last time,
noting insurance (property casualty) came in worse than anticipated. He reported the
State has not yet released the Constructional Officer’s salary numbers. OMB Director
Herring reported the proposed budget is posted on the County’s website with a proposed
aggregate millage rate of 6.5883 that is 11.55% above the current aggregate rolled back
rate of 5.9064. She stated the proposed aggregate millage rate is: 1) County-wide millage
at 6.2; 2) The four (4) Dependent Fire Districts at .5 mills each; and 3) the proposed
M.S.T.U. Voted Debt Service millage rates are (Gulfside) Beachfront at .5502, Gulfside
Interior at .4758, and Bayside at .4706. OMB Director Herring reported this is the
maximum millage rates, noting the Board may go down but not go up. She provided an
overview of the reasons for the increase in Ad Valorem tax dollars, as follows: 1) FRS
Retirement cost; 2) insurance cost; 3) pay raises; 4) capital outlay; 5) Sheriff’s Office; 6)
County Jail; 7) Gulf County E.M.S.; 8) 911 Emergency Management services; and 9)
County services. OMB Director Herring noted the tentative budget has been provided to
each Board member and posted on the County’s website for the public. She then directed
members of the Board to page 4 of the proposed budget, stating page 4 provides totals
by fund. OMB Director Herring stated the Board may amend the budget at tonight’s
hearing, noting she is requesting the ability to make line-item adjustments between now
and the final hearing. She then requested Board approval of a net increase to General
Fund of $34,570.00, noting this is a $50,800.00 increase to the Sheriff’s Office for their
law enforcement contracts, but there are other revenues that are decreased by
$16,230.00. Chairman Quinn called for a motion to move forward with one change.

Commissioner McCroan so moved. Commissioner Husband seconded the motion.
Chairman Quinn called for public comment. There being no public comment, the motion
passed unanimously. OMB Director Herring stated her second request is for the Board to
authorize her the ability to make those line-item changes within funds. Chairman Quinn
called for a motion to move forward with those changes. Commissioner Husband so
moved. Commissioner Farrell seconded the motion. Chairman Quinn called for public
comment. There being none, the motion passed unanimously. Upon inquiry by Chairman
Quinn, none of the Constitutional Officer’s present at the hearing had any questions or
comments. County Administrator Hammond requested the Board approve the presented
proposed budget. Chairman Quinn stated if District IV Park Fund is not spent, then he
would like for the balance to roll over to the new budget year. County Administrator
Hammond stated that before the next budget hearing, staff will figure out the estimate and
the Board can vote on it at the Final Budget Hearing. Chairman Quinn called for public
comment. Randy Pridgeon, of WindMark appeared before the Board to inquire if staff
could provide a ‘hot sheet’ for the budget going forward. OMB Director Herring reported
the County is required to state the reasons for an increase in millage. County
Administrator Hammond discussed noting the top ten biggest changes starting next year’s
budget session. Chairman Quinn called for additional public comment. There was no
further public comment.
