JULY 31, 2023
SPECIAL MEETING
PORT ST. JOE, FLORIDA
The Gulf County Board of County Commissioners met this date in special session with
the following members present: Chairman Sandy Quinn, Jr., Vice Chairman Phil
McCroan, and Commissioners David C. Rich, Jack Husband, and Patrick Farrell.
Others present were: County Attorney Jeremy Novak, Clerk Rebecca L. Norris, Director
of Finance & Management Leo Bebeau, Deputy Clerk Leanna Cooper, County
Administrator Michael L. Hammond, County Assistant Administrator Clay Smallwood, III,
Deputy Administrator Kari Summers, Building Official Brad Bailey, County Consultant Don
Butler, County Consultant Warren Yeager, Jr., Gulf County E.D.C. Director Jim McKnight,
Gulf County E.M.S. Director Jody Daniels, Grant Administrator Kaci Rhodes, Grant
Administrator Erin Kennedy, Human Resource Director Brett Lowry, Human Resource
Staff Assistant Heather Byrd, Office of Management & Budget Director Sherry Herring,
Mosquito Control Director Austin Horton, County Planner Doug Crane, Public Works
Director Mark Cothran, Assistant Public Works Director Raymond Hart, Sheriff Mike
Harrison, T.D.C. Executive Director Silvia Williams, and VSO/S.H.I.P./Central Services
Director Lynn Lanier.
Chairman Quinn called the meeting to order at 9:01 a.m., E.T.
Chairman Quinn then opened the meeting with prayer and led in the Pledge of Allegiance
to the Flag.
SWEARING-IN DISTRICT II – HUSBAND
The Oath of Office was administered to Jack Husband, County Commissioner District II
by County Judge Timothy McFarland.
The meeting did then recess at 9:05 a.m., E.T.
The meeting did reconvene at 9:12 a.m., E.T.
BID #2223-29 – CESSNA DRIVE WATER LINE
County Administrator Hammond directed the Board to pages 1-4 of the Administrative
Update and Report Packet <copy provided to the Clerk for the record>, recommending
the low bid of Williams Plumbing Services, Inc. (for Bid #2223-29 * Cessna Drive Water
Line), total price is $170,611.70 but requesting permission, if possible, to do direct
purchase on the water line material. Chairman Quinn called for a motion to move forward.
Commissioner Farrell so moved. Commissioner McCroan seconded the motion.

Chairman Quinn called for public comment. There being no public comment, the motion
passed unanimously.
ADMINISTRATIVE UPDATE & REPORT
Referencing page 5 of the packet, Assistant County Administrator Smallwood requested
permission from the Board to advertise to receive sealed bids for repaving the roads in
Highland View, to be paid from one of the F.E.M.A. projects. Commissioner McCroan
inquired about which roads are to be paved. Assistant County Administrator Smallwood
reported it will be all the roads within Highland View with a $4.2 million budget. Chairman
Quinn called for a motion to move forward with advertising. Commissioner Farrell so
moved. Commissioner Rich seconded the motion. Chairman Quinn called for public
comment. There being none, the motion passed unanimously.
OMB Director Herring discussed page 6 of the packet as a request to apply for an F.D.L.E.
JAG grant along with a Certificate to Participate. Chairman Quinn called for a motion to
move forward with applying. Commissioner McCroan motioned to approve.
Commissioner Farrell seconded the motion. Chairman Quinn called for public comment.
There being no public comment, the motion passed unanimously.
Bringing attention to pages 7-8 of the packet, OMB Director Herring requested permission
from the Board to apply for the beach grant (FL D.E.P. * Beach Management Funding
Assistance Program), noting this for the Fiscal Year 2024-25 grant application. She stated
this grant will be for the construction and monitoring of the Breakwaters Project. OMB
Director Herring noted if the Board approvals the grant application, then there is a
proposed resolution for the Board to consider. Commissioner McCroan inquired if the
resolution could be included in one vote. County Attorney Novak said yes, then read the
proposed resolution by title. Chairman Quinn called for a motion to move forward with
applying and accepting the Resolution. Commissioner McCroan so moved.
Commissioner Farrell seconded the motion. Chairman Quinn called for public comment.
There being none, the motion passed unanimously, as follows:
RESOLUTION NO. 2023-22
THE BOARD OF COUNTY COMMISSIONERS, GULF COUNTY,
FLORIDA, IS AUTHORIZING THE COUNTY TO PROCEED WITH THE
SUBMITTAL OF THE “FISCAL YEAR 2024-2025 LOCAL GOVERNMENT
FUNDING REQUEST APPLICATION” UNDER THE PROVISIONS OF
CHAPTER 161.091, FLORIDA STATUTES, TO THE FLORIDA
DEPARTMENT OF ENVIRONMENTAL PROTECTION, BEACH
MANAGEMENT FUNDING ASSISTANCE (BMFA) PROGRAM, TO BE
USED FOR THE PRESERVATION OF THE ST. JOESPH PENINSULA
BEACHES.
*Complete Resolution on file with Clerk*
(End)

OMB Director Herring requested, under page 9 of the packet, permission of the Board to
apply for a NOAA (National Oceanic and Atmospheric Administration) grant, noting this
request would be for Salinas Park improvements. Chairman Quinn called for a motion to
move forward with applying. Commissioner McCroan so moved. Commissioner Rich
seconded the motion. Chairman Quinn called for public comment. There being no public
comment, the motion passed unanimously.
County Administrator Hammond inquired if the Board would like to hold a workshop to
discuss ongoing projects or whether the Board preferred an update at the next meeting.
Upon inquiry by Chairman Quinn regarding the wishes of the Board, Commissioner
McCroan stated the next meeting would be fine. The Board agreed to discuss this at the
next meeting.
Commissioner McCroan welcomed Commissioner Husband to the Board. Commissioner
Husband thanked the Governor and Board for the opportunity to serve District II.
Chairman Quinn shared encouraging words and welcomed Commissioner Husband to
the Board.
There being no further business, and upon motion by Commissioner McCroan, second
by Commissioner Farrell, and unanimous vote, the meeting did then adjourn at 9:20 a.m.,
E.T.
SANDY QUINN, JR.
