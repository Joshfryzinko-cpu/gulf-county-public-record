OCTOBER 31, 2023
EMERGENCY MEETING
PORT ST. JOE, FLORIDA
The Gulf County Board of County Commissioners met this date in emergency session
with the following members of the Board: Chairman Sandy Quinn, Jr., Vice Chairman Phil
McCroan, and Commissioners David C. Rich, Jack Husband, and Patrick Farrell.
Others present were: County Attorney Jeremy Novak, Director of Finance & Management
Leo Bebeau, Deputy Clerk Leanna Cooper, County Administrator Michael L. Hammond,
County Assistant Administrator Clay Smallwood, III, Deputy Administrator Kari Summers,
Building Official Brad Bailey, Grant Administrator Kaci Rhodes, Office of Management &
Budget Director Sherry Herring, Mosquito Control Director Austin Horton, County Planner
Doug Crane, Public Works Director Mark Cothran, Assistant Public Works Director
Raymond Hart, Sheriff Mike Harrison, and Tax Collector Shirley Jenkins.
Chairman Quinn called the meeting to order at 9:03 a.m., E.T.
Chairman Quinn then led in the Pledge of Allegiance to the Flag.
COUNTY/CITY SUB-RECIPIENT AGREEMENT
County Attorney Novak presented a memorandum of understanding for the Board to
consider, noting the County has received authorization from the City of Port St. Joe to
execute the subrecipient agreement for the submission to the (Gulf) Consortium. He
stated they arrived with a two-page document presented to the Board today, reading it by
title. County Attorney Novak discussed the County advancing the funds to the City of Port
St. Joe to purchase Gulf Aire Sewer System and now the County is looking to receive
reimbursement through the Consortium, but the City of Port St. Joe is required to sign a
subrecipient agreement. He stated that the City Commission voted to authorize the Mayor
to sign the subrecipient agreement once this proposed memorandum of understanding
has been signed. County Administrator Hammond thanked County Attorney Novak for his
work regarding this matter and apologized to the Board for his exuberance to get this deal
completed. Chairman Quinn called for a motion to move forward with the agreement.
Commissioner Husband so moved. Commissioner Rich seconded the motion, and
Chairman Quinn called for public comment. There being none, the motion passed 4-0,
with Commissioner Farrell abstaining <FORM 8B on file with Clerk>. County Attorney
Novak discussed the Board approving the Memorandum of Understanding amendment,
recommending the Board give Chairman Quinn the authority to sign the sub-recipient
agreement. Chairman Quinn called for a motion to move forward to allow him to sign.
Commissioner Husband so moved. Commissioner McCroan seconded the motion.
Chairman Quinn called for public comment. There being no public comment, the motion
passed 4-0, with Commissioner Farrell abstaining <FORM 8B on file with Clerk>.

There being no further business, and upon motion by Commissioner Farrell, second by
Commissioner Husband, and unanimous 5-0 vote, the meeting did then adjourn at 9:10
a.m. E.T.
