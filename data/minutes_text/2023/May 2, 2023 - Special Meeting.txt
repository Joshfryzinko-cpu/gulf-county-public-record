MAY 2, 2023
PORT ST. JOE, FLORIDA
SPECIAL MEETING
The Gulf County Board of County Commissioners met this date in special session with
the following members present: Chairman Sandy Quinn, Jr., Vice Chairman Phil
McCroan, and Commissioners David C. Rich and Patrick Farrell.
Others present were: Director of Finance & Management Leo Bebeau, Deputy Clerk
Leanna Cooper, County Administrator Michael L. Hammond, County Assistant
Administrator Clay Smallwood, III, Deputy Administrator Kari Summers, Grant
Administrator Kaci Rhodes, Office of Management & Budget Director Sherry Herring,
Mosquito Control Director Austin Horton, and Sheriff Mike Harrison.
Chairman Quinn called the meeting to order at 4:00 p.m., E.T.
Chairman Quinn led in the Pledge of Allegiance to the Flag.
D.O.T. GRANT
OMB Director Herring requested Board approval to accept the grant agreement with
F.D.O.T. (FL Department of Transportation) for a feasibility study on the proposed airport
and the adoption of a proposed resolution. She then read the proposed resolution by title
for the Board to consider. Discussion followed regarding the grant agreement. Chairman
Quinn then called for a motion to move forward with the grant. Commissioner Rich so
moved. Commissioner Farrell seconded the motion. Chairman Quinn called for public
comment. There being no public comment, the motion passed unanimously (4-0). OMB
Director Herring called for clarification of the motion as approving the grant agreement
and adopting the Resolution. Commissioner Rich had no objection to adding the adoption
of this Resolution to his motion. Commissioner Farrell’s second stands. The motion then
passed unanimously (4-0), as follows:
RESOLUTION NO. 2023-15
A RESOLUTION OF THE GULF COUNTY BOARD OF COUNTY
COMMISSIONERS AUTHORIZING THE CHAIRMAN OF THE BOARD TO
ENTER INTO A LOCAL TRANSPORTATION PROJECT STATE
FINANCIAL ASSISTANCE AGREEMENT (SFGA) WITH THE FLORIDA
DEPARTMENT OF TRANSPORTATION.
*Complete Resolution on file with Clerk*
(End)
JAG GRANT
OMB Director Herring requested permission from the Board to purchase AV
(Audio/Visual) equipment using JAGC grant (8C176) funds with an alternate contract

source. She reported the Gulf County Sheriff's Office received three (3) quotes using
SUNCOM with a recommendation to award to Inspired Technologies of North Florida,
Inc., in the amount of $24,974.37. OMB Director Herring stated this grant (JAGC) is
funding $24,300.50 with the Sheriff’s budget paying the remainder. She requested Board
approval to use this purchase method, noting it is an exception to the Bid Policy and the
Gulf County Sheriff will execute the contract, contingent upon OMB review. Chairman
Quinn called for a motion to move forward. Commissioner McCroan so moved.
Commissioner Farrell seconded the motion. Chairman Quinn called for public comment.
There being none, the motion passed unanimously (4-0).
ADMINISTRATIVE UPDATE & REPORT
County Administrator Hammond requested permission from the Board to negotiate with
sole-bidder (John Howell) for Bid #2223-21 (Sell of Lots 9 & 10 Wimico Place Subdivision)
on a reasonable price and bring a recommendation back before the Board at the next
meeting. Commissioner Farrell so moved. Commissioner Rich seconded the motion.
Chairman Quinn called for public comment. There being no public comment, the motion
passed unanimously (4-0).
County Administrator Hammond discussed receiving word from Representative Shoaf
that the T.D.C. Bill will be on the floor (Legislative Session). He reported the Jetty money
got axed at the last minute, but the airport money is in at $2.5 million. He also reported
that the correctional officer money (salary increase) and the Sheriff were funded.
OMB Director Herring presented a proposed resolution for the Board to consider
regarding Charles Avenue and Stebel Drive, reading it by title. Chairman Quinn called for
a motion to accept that Resolution. Commissioner Farrell moved to approve.
Commissioner Rich seconded the motion. Chairman Quinn called for public comment.
There being none, the motion passed unanimously (4-0), as follows:

County Administrator Hammond stated the County received word that approval from the
Federal (Government) regarding Cessna Drive will be received next week. Discussion
followed regarding the building and the project.
Assistant County Administrator Smallwood reported that it is looking like F.E.M.A. is going
to approve a $4.3 million roadway project in the Highland View area.

Commissioner McCroan inquired about Indian Pass and the bridge. Assistant County
Administrator Smallwood stated the geotech is finished and now the bridge design can
be finalized, reporting the road is at 70% and should be ready to advertise later this year.
County Administrator Hammond discussed the need for money and permitting in District
I, noting an update will be provided at the next Board meeting. Upon inquiry by
Commissioner Rich, County Administrator Hammond stated it is in regard to Byrd Parker
Drive.
Commissioner McCroan discussed the clearing at the ‘Y’ (C-30 and Highway 98) for the
new station (Rish Market), inquiring about the bypass. County Administrator Hammond
stated the County is having continued talks with D.O.T. regarding the proposed bypass.
Commissioner Rich inquired about the culvert just before Indian Pass Road. County
Administrator Hammond stated the culvert is fixed, but there are other issues that will
require road closure.
Commissioner McCroan discussed receiving phone calls regarding the erosion at the
Cape, stating the State will armor the road regardless of what happens. Assistant County
Administrator Smallwood stated that D.O.T. is fully aware of the road and where the water
and sand are. County Administrator Hammond stated he does not think it is in the
taxpayers’ best interest to dump any more sand until the mitigation project is
implemented. He reported when Hurricane Opal hit, Sunrise Sunset was falling in and the
water was under Sunrise Sunset, stating the road is still there and D.O.T. will keep this
road open no matter what. County Administrator Hammond discussed the County’s
portion spent in the last 25 years placing sand in this section of the Cape, reporting in the
last 13 years, the County has spent $40 million. He discussed his concerns regarding
their homes, noting these individuals, due to the ECL line, can place sand back that are
in danger of losing their house.
County Administrator Hammond discussed putting together an update on some of the
County’s bigger projects for the next Board meeting. He reported the County will be doing
three separate big projects at the same time, Jones Homestead, White City, and the line
connecting the water system. Discussion followed regarding the workforce rental project.
There being no further business, and upon motion by Commissioner Farrell, second by
Commissioner McCroan, and unanimous 4-0 vote, the meeting did then adjourn at 4:21
p.m., E.T.
