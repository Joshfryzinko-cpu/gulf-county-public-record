TROPICAL STORM IDALIA
County Administrator Hammond noted that Emergency Management Director Herring will
provide an update before he presents his recommendations and request to adopt the
Local State of Emergency. Emergency Management Director Herring appeared before
the Board to report Coastal Gulf County is currently under a hurricane warning and a
tropical storm watch for Mexico Beach. He discussed the projected 4-foot storm surge at
Indian Pass with a potential 6-foot storm surge; noting the projected arrival around
Horseshoe Beach at 7:00 a.m. Wednesday morning. County Administrator Hammond
discussed his concerns with Gulf County going under the warning; recommending to the
Board to have a mandatory evacuation for all RVs (recreational vehicles) in south Gulf
County. He noted the ones that are in those zones will lose their grandfather privileges if
they do not get out. County Administrator Hammond then discussed implementing a
mandatory evacuation for the visitors north of the Stumphole. Commissioner McCroan
discussed his concerns regarding the mandatory evacuation and inquired about the storm
surge. Emergency Management Director Herring reported the storm surge prediction for
Mexico Beach is 1-3 feet which just shown up at this last advisory. Upon inquiry by
Commissioner McCroan, Emergency Management Director Herring stated the tropical
forced winds should arrive at 6:00 pm tomorrow. Following discussion, County
Administrator Hammond suggested the Board leave everything open on the County side
and reevaluate tomorrow; requesting the Board recess the meeting. Chairman Quinn
called for public comment. There was no public comment. County Attorney Novak then

read aloud the Local State of Emergency for the Board to consider. County Administrator
Hammond recommended a mandatory evacuation north of the Stumphole for all visitors,
mandatory evacuation for all RVs (recreational vehicles) in the Coastal Gulf County from
Franklin/Gulf County line to Bay County line, and for those areas that are grandfathered
in and do not remove the RV be subject to losing their grandfather status; requesting that
his recommendation be included in the motion. Commissioner Farrell inquired when the
County would reopen. County Administrator Hammond stated it would be up to the Board
and if this passes it will be in effect until the Board rescinds it or seven days, whichever
comes sooner. Commissioner Farrell inquired if the Board would have to come back to
meet to reopen it. County Administrator Hammond stated technically yes; noting the
Board could recess each time. Chairman Quinn called for a motion to approve the Local
State of Emergency. Commissioner McCroan so moved. Commissioner Farrell seconded
the motion. Chairman Quinn called for public comment. There being no public comment,
the motion passed unanimously. County Administrator Hammond stated once the County
knows it is safe, the roads are safe and we have facilities, then the doors can open back
up, but it will be the decision of the Board. Deputy Clerk Cooper inquired for clarification
of the motion if County Administrator Hammond’s recommendation was included in the
motion. Chairman Quinn then called for a motion, to go along with the Local State of
Emergency, for the Board to go along with County Administrator Hammond’s
recommendations. Commissioner Farrell so moved. Commissioner McCroan seconded
the motion. Chairman Quinn called for public comment. There being none, the motion
passed unanimously. County Administrator Hammond requested this meeting be
recessed. Clerk Norris reported there is a Value Adjustment Board scheduled for
Wednesday at 3:30 p.m., E.T.; inquiring if Chairman Quinn would like to cancel or
postpone this meeting. Chairman Quinn requested this meeting be rescheduled.
The meeting recess at 5:37 p.m., E.T. on August 28, 2023

Emergency Management Director Herring appeared before the Board to report Hurricane
Idalia is strengthening with a prediction of a high category three when it makes landfall.
He stated landfall can be from Cedar Key to Perry or closer westward towards Gulf
County; reporting Gulf County is projected to receive a 3 to 5-foot possible storm surge
at Indian Pass and 45 mph wind gusts. County Administrator Hammond stated Leon
County has been listed under a hurricane warning inland. He recommended all visitors at
Cape San Blas, Simmons Bayou, and Indian Pass to mandatory evacuation; noting the
side road (Painted Pony) is open. County Administrator Hammond then discussed his
concerns regarding the Stumphole and the water and sewer line at the Stumphole.
Discussion followed regarding rain and wind. County Administrator Hammond
recommended the Board allow County employees to leave at 3:00 p.m., E.T. today and
close tomorrow for non-essential personnel. Commissioner Husband inquired if the
County’s essential personnel would be available. County Administrator Hammond stated
yes; noting Emergency Management Director will have his command staff at the E.O.C.
and Maintenance, Public Works, Law Enforcement, Jail, and E.M.S. will all be working.
Discussion followed regarding concerns of water, road damage, and the Bay. Sheriff
Harrison inquired about the E.O.C. activation. Emergency Management Director Herring
reported the E.O.C. is at a level two activation, which is just command staff, but is subject
to change. County Administrator Hammond stated there is no intention of opening the
shelter at this time. Chairman Quinn called for public comment. There was no public
comment. Upon inquiry by Commissioner Husband, County Administrator Hammond
suggested the Board close the beaches 24 hours; requesting this be added to the
recommendation. Clerk Norris stated the Clerk’s Office will have to remain open based
on Judicial direction; reporting there is a trial today but will contact the Chief Judge
(Patterson) regarding (closure for) tomorrow. Chairman Quinn called for a motion to move
forward with the recommendations that County Administrator Hammond stated.
Commissioner Farrell so moved. Commissioner Husband seconded the motion.
Chairman Quinn called for public comment. There being no public comment, the motion
passed unanimously (4-0). County Administrator Hammond stated staff recommends the
Board recess the meeting.
The meeting did recess at 11:44 a.m., E.T. on August 29, 2023
