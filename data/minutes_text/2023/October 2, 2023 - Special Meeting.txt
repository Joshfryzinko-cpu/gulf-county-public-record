AWARD BID #2223-34 – COUNTY ROADWAY PROJECT, PHASE II
County Administrator Hammond reported that the recommendation of staff is to award
(Bid #2223-34) to the second lowest-bidder, Roberts & Roberts, Inc., $3,180,310.30. He
stated that based on staff review, this is the lowest and best bid. Commissioner Husband
discussed there being driveway miscalculations in the low bid (Southeast Construction
Company, LLC). Chairman Quinn called for a motion to accept the recommendation of
the County Administrator. Commissioner Farrell so moved. Commissioner Husband
seconded the motion. Chairman Quinn called for public comment. There being no public
comment, the motion passed unanimously.
LISTER LANDING
County Administrator Hammond stated the County has a willing seller on the west side
(of Lister Landing) and the property owner has paid for the appraisal. He reported the
appraisal came back at $200,000.00 for ten (10) acres, stating if the Board wishes to
move forward with this purchase, then the next vote will be to amend the MYIP to use BP
funds for the purchase. County Administrator Hammond discussed the location being on
the left side that the County has been leasing for several years, noting this will allow water
and river access. Upon inquiry by Commissioner McCroan, County Administrator
Hammond reported it is not where the original boat ramp is located, but there is a
makeshift boat ramp in the river. He stated the east side is owned by Kay Whitfield and
she does not want to sell but wants to indefinitely lease to the County and that is where
the small canal is, calm water, and where the dirt boat ramp is located. County

Administrator Hammond noted that what is called Lister Landing is not part of this
purchase, it is the west side, which is ten (10) acres where the public parking is located
and the makeshift boat ramp. He stated for clarification, this location borders the City of
Port St. Joe property from the Fresh Water Canal to the west, back to the far east side of
the County paved road, Lister Landing Road. Upon inquiry by Commissioner McCroan,
County Administrator Hammond stated the purpose of this purchase is for parking and
public access. Following additional discussion by Commissioner Rich regarding public
access, Commissioner Husband stated he will have to abstain due to being related to the
property owners. Chairman Quinn called for a motion to move forward. Commissioner
Rich motioned to move forward. Commissioner Farrell seconded the motion. Chairman
Quinn called for public comment. There being none, the motion passed 4-0, with
Commissioner Husband abstaining <FORM 8B on file with Clerk>. County Administrator
Hammond stated the County Attorney will draft a purchase contract and then staff will
start the process of obtaining the grant funding to make the purchase, noting it will be a
several-month process.

USCOE GULF COUNTY CANAL SPOILAGE EASEMENT
County Administrator Hammond reported the Raffield family has requested that the
County reach out to the Corps of Engineers and request that the Corps abandon or give
back to the County the little speck which the County has no use for. He stated when the
spoilage easement was changed from one side of the Gulf County Canal to the other
side, this was just a left-off little spot. County Administrator Hammond stated if the Board
is willing to give up that spot, then County Attorney Novak will reach out to the Corps of
Engineers and request this be relinquished to the County and the County will then
abandon it. County Attorney Novak reported it was conveyed back to Gulf County 40-50
years ago, noting it was a spoilage easement and in order to abandon it, the County will
need to reach out, advertise it, hold a public hearing, and then the County would adopt a
proposed resolution formally abandoning that and then recording in the Official Records.

Following discussion, Chairman Quinn called for a motion to allow County Attorney Novak
to move forward with proper procedures. Commissioner Husband so moved.
Commissioner Rich seconded the motion. Chairman Quinn called for public comment.
There being no public comment, the motion passed 4-0, with Commissioner McCroan
abstaining due to family relations <FORM 8B on file with Clerk>.
