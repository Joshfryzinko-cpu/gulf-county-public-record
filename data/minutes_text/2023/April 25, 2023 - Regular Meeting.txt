PUBLIC HEARING – UNSOLICITED PROPOSAL #2223-12
County Attorney Novak discussed Unsolicited Proposal #2223-12 for the land pledge and
transfer for the proposed Gulf County Regional Airport Facility as being advertised per
Florida Statutes, inquiring of the Clerk’s Office if any additional proposals have been
received. Deputy Clerk Cooper stated no new proposals were received. County Attorney

Novak discussed moving forward on negotiations with St. Joe Company (sole proposal
received). He recommended the Board allow County Administrator Hammond and himself
to move forward to draft a comprehensive plan to present to the Board for consideration.
Chairman Quinn opened the Public Hearing to the public, calling for public comment. Mike
O’Neal, President of Gulf Rifle and Pistol Club appeared before the Board to discuss their
concerns regarding the location of the proposed airport. Chairman Quinn called for any
additional public comment. There being none, Chairman Quinn closed the Public Hearing
to the public. Regarding Mr. O’Neal’s concerns, County Administrator Hammond reported
that just because the proposed airport is 10,000 feet does not mean the runway will be
10,000, but for clear space, the County is asking for 10,000 feet. He stated the proposed
airport will be closer to White City, not the gun range, reporting the rough parameters will
be the Dynamite Doc, White City Canal on the north side, and County Road 382 (Industrial
Road) on the St. Joe side. County Attorney Novak stated with a vote of the Board the
Public Hearing will be closed and instructing the County Administrator to move forward in
working with St. Joe Company on a comprehensive agreement. Chairman Quinn called
for public comment. Francine Vellanti, of Cape San Blas appeared before the Board to
state she does not agree with the airport. Upon inquiry by Chairman Quinn, County
Attorney Novak stated the vote today is to authorize the County Administrator to move
forward with the private entity on the next step of the P3 (Public Private Partnership)
process to develop a comprehensive plan. Sarah Hodgson, of St. Joe Beach appeared
before the Board to inquire who the private entity is. County Administrator Hammond
reported that Gulf County has zero money in this project. He stated the proposal is for St.
Joe Company to give the County 488 acres of land, and then the County will apply for
grants, noting it will be tax funds at some juncture along with Federal and State funds, or
Triumph funds, to construct this proposed airport. County Attorney Novak discussed the
process of a P3 (Public Private Partnership). Chairman Quinn called for a motion to move
forward on the unsolicited proposal (#2223-12) for the plan, design, and construction.
Commissioner Rich so moved to move forward. Commissioner Farrell seconded the
motion. Chairman Quinn called for public comment. There being no public comment, the
motion passed unanimously (4-0).
PUBLIC HEARING – AMENDING COMPREHENSIVE PLAN ORDINANCE
County Attorney Novak read the proposed ordinance by title regarding amending the
Comprehensive Plan. Chairman Quinn opened to the public, calling for public comment.
George Mitchell, of Wewahitchka appeared before the Board to discuss his concerns
regarding this land use change. Upon motion by Commissioner Farrell, second by
Commissioner McCroan, and a unanimous 4-0 vote, the Board extended his time to
speak. Mr. Mitchell continued to discuss his concerns regarding this development, the
stormwater impact, and utilities. Chairman Quinn called for further public comment. There
being none, Chairman Quinn closed the Public Hearing to the public. Planner Crane
reported this is the second reading of this proposed ordinance, it did go before the
P.D.R.B. and it was submitted to the State after the first reading at which the County
received no comments back from any of the State agencies. Discussion followed
regarding density in this area. Mr. Mitchell reappeared before the Board to inquire if the
Board has thought about this. Following discussion, Chairman Quinn called for any
additional public comment. There being no further public comment, Commissioner Farrell

moved to approve. Commissioner Rich seconded the motion. Chairman Quinn called for
public comment. There being no public comment, the motion passed unanimously (4-0).
