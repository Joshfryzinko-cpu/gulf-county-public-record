STATUS UPDATE – BAYSAVERS
Dusty May, of Baysavers appeared before the Board to discuss the requested support
letter to the Corps of Engineers regarding the feasibility study for the Gulf County Canal

and Intercoastal Waterway, Apalachicola River, Apalachicola Bay St. Joseph Bay, and
St. Andrew’s Bay. He stated he sent an email to members of the Board <copy not provided
to the Clerk for the record>, reporting this support letter is not a contract, obligation for
financial obligations, or a risk to the County. Mr. May stated the letter is a statement of
local support that the Corps of Engineers is able to use to move this study forward, noting
until Northwest Florida Water Management District commits to being the non-federal
sponsor it will not move forward. Commissioner Farrell discussed his concerns regarding
the cost to build a block. Mr. May reported that the canal will not be blocked or any of the
other waterways, there may however be a gate. He stated that if the Board does not like
what is being read in the study, then it can be nixed as the County will be in the driver’s
seat. Commissioner McCroan inquired if verbiage could be added regarding access to
the canal. County Attorney Novak discussed the proposed draft support letter including
canal access and the funding source being from the State. Chairman Quinn called for a
motion to approve the letter. Commissioner McCroan so moved. Commissioner Rich
seconded the motion. Chairman Quinn called for public comment. There being no public
comment, the motion passed unanimously (4-0).

QUASI-JUDICIAL HEARING – REVIEW & CONSIDERATION OF P.D.R.B.
VARIANCE REQUEST – GULF COAST STATE COLLEGE
Planner Crane presented a variance request from Gulf Coast State College, Parcel ID
#03794-005R and #03794-010R. Chairman Quinn called anyone wishing to speak on this
matter to step forward. Miranda Rollins, of 98 Real Estate Group representing the buyer
of this property (applicant) appeared before the Board. Chairman Quinn inquired if Mrs.
Rollins had any objection to waiving Quasi-Judicial Hearing. With no objection,
Commissioner McCroan motioned to waive. Commissioner Farrell seconded the motion.
Chairman Quinn called for public comment. There being none, the motion passed
unanimously (4-0). Mrs. Rollins stated her client is requesting a variance for the ability to
build a more traditional style home, which the property currently will only accommodate
16-foot. She discussed a letter from the neighbor from behind in support of this variance
request. Planner Crane stated the P.D.R.B. voted 2-0, with one abstention to deny this
variance, reporting these two lots together are .117 acres, which is 5,096 square feet for
a total buildable square footage of 152,895. Discussion followed regarding the buildable
property. Upon inquiry by Chairman Quinn, Mrs. Rollins reported Gulf Coast State College
is the current owner and seller which has granted permission for the potential buyer to
request this variance on their behalf. Commissioner McCroan inquired about the letter
from the college. P.D.R.B. Chairman Butler appeared before the Board to state there is a
letter, not on letterhead, noting the current owner, based on the County website, is Gulf
Coast State College Foundation, Inc. Discussion followed regarding concerns in granting
a variance to applicants that are not owners of the property. Mr. Butler stated between
(Veterans’ Memorial) Beacon Hill Park and County Road 386 (Overstreet Road) there are
sixty lots on the land side of U.S. Highway 98, forty have been improved and twenty have
not, noting if this house is allowed to be built bigger than regulations allow, it projects out
beyond the 30-foot line that the other forty have to adhere to. He discussed the option of
building a shorter house or a two-story house without any variance or blocking any
property owner’s view, noting this is the main reason for the denial from P.D.R.B. Mrs.

Rollins provided a packet to each member of the Board to review <copy provided to the
Clerk for the record>. Chairman Quinn inquired if the Board has ever allowed an applicant
to apply for a variance on behalf of the property owner. Mr. Butler stated not to his
knowledge. County Administrator Hammond discussed the need to show a hardship and
if you do not own the property, you cannot show a hardship of why the variance is needed,
noting the Board may need to tighten down on this. Discussion followed regarding
showing hardship and variance requests. County Attorney Novak discussed conditions
within contracts subject to approval in purchasing the property, stating this is a reversed
situation. Chairman Quinn discussed his concerns regarding the applicant not being the
property owner and the letter not on college letterhead, stating he is in agreement with
the P.D.R.B. Commissioner Farrell discussed his concerns regarding the twenty lots and
setting a precedent. Upon inquiry by Chairman Quinn, Planner Crane stated voting 2-0,
with one abstaining, the P.D.R.B. denied this variance. Chairman Quinn called for a vote
to waive the formal reading. Commissioner Farrell so moved. Commissioner McCroan
seconded the motion. Chairman Quinn called for public comment. There being none, the
motion passed unanimously (4-0). Commissioner Rich motioned to deny. Commissioner
McCroan seconded the motion. Chairman Quinn called for public comment. There being
no public comment, the motion passed unanimously (4-0).
