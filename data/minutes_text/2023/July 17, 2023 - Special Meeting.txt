ADMINISTRATIVE UPDATE & REPORT
County Administrator Hammond directed the Board to page 4 of his packet, being a
proposed resolution regarding child support. Chairman Quinn called for a motion to
accept this Resolution. Commissioner Farrell so moved. Commissioner McCroan

seconded the motion. Chairman Quinn called for public comment. There being none, the
motion passed unanimously (4-0), as follows:
RESOLUTION NO. 2023-18
CLERK OF COURT CHILD SUPPORT FUND RESOLUTION
WHEREAS, pursuant to Section 61.181(1), Florida Statutes, the
Clerk of the Court operates the County’s child support depository; and
WHEREAS, pursuant to 42 U.S.C. 8658.48 C.F.R. 8302.52, the
Federal Government will provide to the State of Florida, Department of
Revenue, as the State’s Title IV-D agency, incentive payments received
from and in connection with operation of the State’s Title IV-D Child Support
Program; and
WHEREAS, pursuant to 42 U.S.C. 8658.45 C.F.R. 8302.5 and
C.F.R. 8302.52, the State of Florida, Department of Revenue, is to provide
to the local governmental entity, i.e. the County, the local governmental
entity’s share of such incentive payments; and
WHEREAS, the Clerk of the Court, as the operator of the child
support depository for the County, is entitled to receive the incentive
payments made to the County as well as the Federal reimbursements for
child support expenditures;
NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED, by the Board of County
Commissioners of Gulf County, Florida, that all Federal Financial
Participation Payments received by the Clerk of Court and any incentive
payments received by the County from the State of Florida, Department of
Revenue (or any subsequent State Title IV-D agency), relative to the
operation of the County’s child support collection and enforcement shall be
passed through to the Clerk of Court as the County Support Depository and
such support incentive payments shall be exempted from Section
218.36(2), Florida Statutes.
DULY PASSED AND ADOPTED by the Board of County
Commissioners of Gulf County, Florida this 17th day of July 2023.
(End)
From page 5, County Administrator Hammond requested Board approval of an
emergency purchase of a 50,000-gallon water tank, in the amount of $32,600.00, noting
it includes everything except the foundation and piping. He stated this water tank would
be installed at the LUCI I site on Cape water system. Upon inquiry by Commissioner
McCroan if this will be compatible with the new system, County Administrator Hammond
stated long-term it would be extra storage and short-term give the County lead-way in the

water demand in peak season as well as fire protection. Discussion followed regarding
the foundation and location. Commissioner McCroan motioned to approve.
Commissioner Rich seconded the motion. Chairman Quinn called for public comment.
There being no public comment, the motion passed unanimously (4-0).
Dwight Van Lierop, of Acentria Public Risk appeared before the Board to discuss the
projected 50% increase in the property (casualty) premium. Discussion followed
regarding the increase. County Administrator Hammond stated the County has set-aside
for an increase scenario. Mr. Van Lierop discussed the proposed Worker’s Comp
premium. County Administrator Hammond discussed an incentive package for no lost
time work accidents.
County Administrator Hammond reported the recommendation can be found on page 9
but directed the Board to view page 10 of his packet, recommending the Board go with
United Healthcare, a decrease from the current County plan. He discussed the County
being a benefactor of the pool for several years with a 79%-80% lost rate. County
Administrator Hammond discussed the pros with going with United Healthcare: 1) -0.16%
increase if the County goes with the vision plan also; 2) provide a $10,000.00 wellness
benefit; and 3) allow a profit-sharing plan fully insured. He stated staff is recommending
the Board switch to United Healthcare, HSA plan is essentially similar to the current plan.
County Administrator Hammond reported the dental insurance is the same being in a
contract with Florida Combined Life for at least another year, in the second year of a two-
year rate guarantee for life insurance with United Healthcare Life, recommending the
vision be switched to United Healthcare. Discussion followed regarding vision coverage.
County Administrator Hammond stated if the Board accepts the proposal with United
Healthcare verse the renewal rate with Blue Cross, it would save the individual $19.00
per month. Discussion followed regarding hospital coverage and benefits. County
Administrator Hammond noted Aetna was lower than United Healthcare and Blue Cross
but did not offer the profit-sharing plan. Commissioner Rich requested the Board vote on
this matter at tomorrow’s regular Board meeting. County Administrator Hammond stated
that it is his intent to negotiate with the awarder to get back on a January – December 31
cycle.
TENTATIVE 2023-24 BUDGET
County Administrator Hammond stated staff is recommended to the Board a budget
millage rate reduction, needing to certify it to the Property Appraiser and set the first
budget hearing date along with a proposed date for the final hearing. He then hit the
highpoints within the proposed tentative 2023-24 budget booklet <copy was not provided
to the Clerk for the record>. He reported staff is proposing a -0.3000 reduction in ad
valorem tax at 6.2000, no change in the Fire Control Districts at 0.5000 mill each, and a
reduction in the M.S.T.U.s due the value at the beach. County Administrator Hammond
stated if the Board adopted the proposed millage, then the Countywide levy would levy
$19,011,105.00 in County taxes, St. Joseph Fire Control District would levy
$1,035,960.00, Tupelo Fire Control District $90,512.00, Overstreet Fire Control District
$41,091.00, Howard Creek Fire Control District $23,204.00, and the combined M.S.T.U.s
$400,000.00 which is the yearly payment on that bond. He noted the County was able to

pay-off the budget appropriated bond in November and the only one left is the devoted
debt service MSTU at the Cape. County Administrator Hammond noted the proposed
budget is $103,298,895.00 providing overall totals of the revenue sources. He reported
that the County has not received the final State numbers, discussing his concerns
regarding the deadlines and the need for State numbers in a timely manner. County
Administrator Hammond then went through the proposed budget beginning on page 13.
There being no additional comments from the Constitutional Officers, Chairman Quinn
called for public comment regarding the budget. There was no public comment. County
Administrator Hammond requested that the Board set the tentative millage rate at 6.2 and
staff will certify to the Property Appraiser. Discussion followed regarding lowering the
millage and the price increases in fuel and utilities. Chairman Quinn called for a motion
to accept. Commissioner Farrell so moved. Commissioner McCroan seconded the
motion. Chairman Quinn called for public comment. There being none, the motion passed
unanimously.
County Administrator Hammond recommended holding the First Budget Hearing on
September 6, 2023 at 5:01 p.m., E.T. The Board had no objection. County Administrator
Hammond stated the hearing date will be advertised on the TRIM notice. He then
recommended September 18, 2023, for the Final Budget Hearing, advertising by
September 14, 2023. The Board had no objection.
There being no further business, and upon motion by Commissioner McCroan, second
