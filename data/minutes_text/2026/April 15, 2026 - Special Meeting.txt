APRIL 15, 2026
SPECIAL MEETING
PORT ST. JOE, FLORIDA
The Gulf County of Board of County Commissioners met this date in special session with
the following members present: Chairman Sandy Quinn, Jr., Vice Chairman Phil
McCroan, and Commissioners Christopher McLemore, Lloyd Jack Husband, III, and
Randy Pridgeon.
Others present were: County Attorney Jeremy Novak, Chief Deputy Leanna Cooper,
Director of Finance & Management Leo Bebeau, Deputy Clerk Elanie Bland, County
Administrator Michael L. Hammond, County Assistant Administrator Clay Smallwood, III,
Deputy Administrator Silvia Williams, Building Official Brad Bailey, Emergency
Management Director/Fire Coordinator Matt Herring, G.I.S. Coordinator Clay Kennedy,
Grant Administrator Erin Kennedy, Grant Administrator Laura Beech-Armstrong, Human
Resource Director Kaci Rhodes, Office of Management & Budget Director Sherry
Herring, Mosquito Control Director Austin Horton, Public Works Director Mark Cothran,
Assistant Public Works Director Raymond Hart, Sheriff Mike Harrison, and T.D.C.
Executive Director Blake Rish.
Chairman Quinn called the meeting to order at 1:00 p.m., E.T.
Chairman Quinn led in the Pledge of Allegiance to the Flag.
BID PROTEST – GULF COUNTY RFP #2526-09
County Attorney Novak reported that following the award of Bid #2526-09 (2026 Road
Paving Project) the County received a bid protest from Anderson Columbia. He noted the
County does not have a formal process but did notify the Protestor of today’s special
meeting. County Attorney Novak stated that a letter was received yesterday from
Anderson Columbia’s general counsel withdrawing their bid protest, noting this meeting
is no longer needed. He thanked members of the Board for their availability, noting that
the County can move forward with the bid award (to Roberts & Roberts, Inc.).
County Administrator Hammond discussed the recent award of Bid #2526-07 to Monolith
with option 1, noting after further review, staff would like to go with the base bid in lieu of
the alternate. He stated the alternate was $98,293.00, requesting the Board approve the
base bid of $117,094.25. County Administrator Hammond noted option 1 is too narrow for
the fuel cell location. Commissioner Husband made the motion. Commissioner Pridgeon
seconded the motion. Chairman Quinn called for public comment. There being none, the
motion passed 4-0, with Commissioner McCroan abstaining due to his business
relationship with Monolith Construction <FORM 8B on file with Clerk>.

County Attorney Novak discussed the unsolicited proposal for workforce housing, noting
a formal advertisement will publish in the Florida Register and in the local newspaper.
Upon recommendation by County Attorney Novak, Commissioner Pridgeon motioned to
place a $4,000.00 application fee for the workforce housing proposal. Commissioner
McCroan seconded the motion. Chairman Quinn called for public comment. After
discussion by staff and members of the Board, Chairman Quinn again called for public
comment. There being none, the motion passed 4-0, with Commissioner Husband
abstaining due to completing the engineer work on this proposed workforce housing
project <FORM 8B on file with Clerk>.
Commissioner Pridgeon discussed the burn ban, requesting the County post weekly
announcements to educate the weekly renters. County Administrator Hammond
discussed flashing sign regarding the burn ban on the Cape, noting signage is needed in
the St. Joe Beach area.
Stephen Wich, of Cape San Blas appeared before the Board to discuss developing a new
platform to replace social media, noting his corporate headquarters will be in Gulf County.
He discussed the platform by using AI.
There being no further business, and upon by Commissioner McCroan, second by
Commissioner Husband, and unanimous vote, the meeting did then adjourn at 1:00 p.m.,
