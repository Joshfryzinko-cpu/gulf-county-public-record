Chairman Quinn called the meeting to order at 1:01 p.m., ET
Chairman Quinn led in the Pledge of Allegiance to the Flag.
BID #2526-09 – 2026 ROAD PAVING
Upon recommendation by County Administrator Hammond, Commissioner McLemore
motioned to award Bid #2526-09 for the 2026 Road Paving Project to the best bid,
Roberts & Roberts, Inc., in the amount of $2,830,622.50. Commissioner Pridgeon
seconded the motion. Discussion followed. Chairman Quinn called for public comment.
There being none, the motion passed 4-1, with Commissioner McCroan voting no. County
Administrator Hammond noted the bid came in under budget, requesting that members
of the Board provide staff with additional roads to include in the change order.
ADMINISTRATIVE REPORT & UPDATE
MEMORANDUM OF UNDERSTANDING – SEWER SERVICE HOWARD CREEK
WORKCAMP
County Administrator Hammond reported that the FL Department of Corrections has
accepted the County’s offer (sewer service connection at Howard Creek Workcamp),
noting that the County Attorney has drafted an MOU (Memorandum of Understanding) for
the Board to consider. He noted the highlights of the MOU are, as follows: 1) April 1, 2028
the County will run a sewer line and lift station connection to the Workcamp between
Howard Creek and White City; 2) the County will not charge DOC any impact fees or tap
fees; 3) DOC will pay the regular sewer service fees; 4) DOC will provide property and
easement for the lift station location; 5) DOC will open the Workcamp no later than April

1, 2028; and 6) the County will have first priority of workcrews going forward. County
Administrator Hammond requested Board approve the MOU, contingent upon DOC’s
attorney approval. Discussion followed. Upon inquiry by Commissioner Pridgeon, County
Administrator Hammond requested to approve these terms and allow him to execute this
MOU, contingent upon any major changes from DOC’s attorney. Commissioner Pridgeon
made the motion. Commissioner McLemore seconded the motion. Chairman Quinn called
for public comment. There being no public comment, the motion passed unanimously.

GOLF CARTS – U.S. HIGHWAY 98
Commissioner Pridgeon discussed his concerns regarding a 6-seat golf cart loaded with
children that drove from WindMark to Highland View. He requested that Sheriff Harrison
post signs that golf carts are not allowed on U.S. Highway 98 in Gulf County.
ELECTRIC SCOOTERS – CAPE SAN BLAS ROAD
Commissioner McCroan noted his concerns to Sheriff Harrison regarding electric
scooters darting across the Cape San Blas Road.
GOLF CARTS – U.S. HIGHWAY 98
Commissioner Husband discussed the signage language regarding golf carts on the
sidewalks in St. Joe Beach. County Administrator Hammond requested permission to
purchase ten locking blocks to provide to the Sheriff’s Office for use on golf carts found
in violation, discussing the need to adopt an emergency ordinance. Discussion followed.
Sarah Hobson, of St. Joe Beach appeared before the Board to discuss her concerns
regarding the matter of signage, requesting more of a presence from law enforcement in
St. Joe Beach. Discussion followed. Sheriff Harrison appeared before the Board to
discuss enforcement, noting law enforcement cannot issue a citation without a driver
present. He discussed staffing shortage, noting he currently has two vacancies and three
people in the academy program. Upon inquiry by Commissioner McCroan, Sheriff
Harrison noted once the three cadets complete the LEAP academy, he will have three
vacant positions. Commissioner Pridgeon inquired on a date to adopt a proposed

emergency ordinance. County Attorney discussed advertising to hold a Public Hearing to
consider the proposed emergency ordinance at the meeting in April 2026.
