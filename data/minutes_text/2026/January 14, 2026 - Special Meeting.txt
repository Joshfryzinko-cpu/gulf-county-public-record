JANUARY 14, 2026
SPECIAL MEETING
PORT ST. JOE, FLORIDA
The Gulf County Board of County Commissioners met this date in special session with
the following members present: Chairman Sandy Quinn, Jr., Vice Chairman Phil
McCroan, and Commissioners Christopher McLemore, Lloyd Jack Husband, III, and
Randy Pridgeon.
Others present were: County Attorney Jeremy Novak, Interim Clerk Tonya A. Knox, Chief
Deputy Clerk Leanna Cooper, Deputy Clerk Casi Campbell, County Administrator Michael
L. Hammond, County Assistant Administrator Clay Smallwood, III, Deputy Administrator
Kari Summers, Deputy Administrator Silvia Williams, Building Official Brad Bailey,
Emergency Management Director/Fire Coordinator Matt Herring, Gulf County E.M.S.
Director Jody Daniels, G.I.S. Coordinator Clay Kennedy, Grant Administrator Erin
Kennedy, Office of Management & Budget Director Sherry Herring, County Planner Doug
Crane, Public Works Director Mark Cothran, Assistant Public Works Director Raymond
Hart, and T.D.C. Executive Director Blake Rish.
Chairman Quinn called the meeting to order at 8.45 a.m., E.T.
Chairman Quinn opened the meeting in prayer and led in the Pledge of Allegiance to the
Flag.
CHANGE ORDER – WHITE CITY WATER PLANT
County Administrator Hammond recommended Board approve Change Order #3 with
Empire Pipe & Supply for the White City Water Treatment Plant with an increase of
$104,127.01. Commissioner Pridgeon made the motion. Commissioner McCroan
seconded the motion. Chairman Quinn called for public comment. There being no public
comment, the motion passed unanimously.
ADMINISTRATIVE REPORT & UPDATE
AWARD BID #2425-29 – EMERGENCY VEHICLE TECHNICIAN
From page 1 of the Administrative Report & Update Packet <copy provided to the Clerk
for the record>, Emergency Management Director Herring appeared before the Board to
recommend Board award bid to Ten-8 Fire & Safety and Tactical Fire, LLC as qualified
mechanics to perform maintenance and repairs for all County fire apparatuses (Bid
#2425-29 * Emergency Vehicle Technician). He requested the policy be if the work is
under $500.00, then fire chiefs will proceed with the maintenance/repairs, but if the
amount is over $500.00, then a verbal quote will be required from one of the two qualified
mechanics, and a decision will be made between the fire chief and himself. Emergency
Management Director Herring stated if the amount is $3,000.00 or more, then a written
quote from both qualified mechanics will be obtained before making a decision to approve

the work. Commissioner Husband inquired if this procedure follows Board policy
regarding obtaining quotes. After discussion regarding the bidders, Commissioner
Husband made the motion to award. Commissioner McLemore seconded the motion.
Chairman Quinn called for public comment. There being no public comment, the motion
passed unanimously.
PURCHASE REQUEST – CAT LOADER / TRANSFER STATION
Mosquito Control Director Horton presented a request found on page 2 of the packet to
ratify the purchase of a CAT loader with attachments for the Transfer Station, noting the
purchase was paid from the Small County Solid Waste Grant (#CW011) and Capital
Improvement Funds, noting this purchase is an exception to the County Procurement
Policy due to the County going through the Florida Sheriff’s Association and the
equipment being compatible with the existing goods. He then requested Board approval
to purchase some of the attachments that were non-contract options. Commissioner
Husband motioned to approve. Commissioner Pridgeon seconded the motion. Chairman
Quinn called for public comment. There being none, the motion passed unanimously.

AGREEMENT MODIFICATION – EMS CONSULTANTS
Assistant County Administrator Smallwood referencing page 7 as a request to modify the
contract with EMS Consultants (Bid #1415-11) by removing the billing software
component and to procure another vendor for this service. Commissioner Husband so
moved. Commissioner Pridgeon seconded the motion. Chairman Quinn called for public
comment. There being no public comment, the motion passed unanimously.
BID #2526-06 – SALE FIVE ACRES
County Attorney Novak discussed five (5) acres (Parcel ID #03495-000R) adjacent to the
Chateau Project, noting the Board approved obtaining the legal description and
advertisement to receive sealed bids. He reported the bids will be opened during the
January 27, 2026, Board meeting, noting during that meeting he will then request
approval from the Board to negotiate a contract.
ORDINANCE – RECESSION OF SIXTH CENT
County Attorney Novak reported that the recession of the sixth cent has been advertised
to rescind Ordinance #2025-04. He stated the Public Hearing will be held on January 27,
2026. County Administrator Hammond reported that the interlocal agreement will end in
2032, noting the City of Port St. Joe will receive their 20% of the gas tax directly as they
declined the interlocal with the County.
MEETING – DEPARTMENT OF TRANSPORTATION & CORRECTIONS
County Administrator Hammond discussed upcoming meetings with secretaries from
D.O.T. (Department of Transportation) and D.O.C. on January 21, 2026, in Tallahassee,
noting an agenda will be provided to the Board next week.
STATUS REPORT – EROSION CONTROL BREAKWATER PROJECT
Commissioner McCroan requested an update on the breakwater project. Assistant
County Administrator Smallwood discussed the delay, noting pumping sand will begin in
March 2026. He stated as soon as the pumped sand is dry, then the breakwater portion
of the project will begin.
MORATORIUM – PERMITS ON GARRISON AVENUE
Commissioner McCroan requested a hold on issuance of permits on Garrison Avenue
until the County can obtain clarification from the developer. He then made a motion to do
that. Commissioner Pridgeon seconded the motion. Commissioner McCroan stated the
hold will be until County staff can meet with the developer to discuss this project.

Chairman Quinn called for public comment. There being no public comment, the motion
passed unanimously.
LEGISLATION CHANGES – PROPERTY TAXES
Commissioner Pridgeon requested that the Board work with Property Appraiser Burke
regarding the upcoming legislative changes regarding property taxes. County
Administrator Hammond discussed a synapsis issued by F.A.C. regarding what the
projected property taxes would be from 2020 to 2025. Discussion followed. Upon request
by Commissioner Pridgeon, OMB Director Herring stated she has had discussion with the
property appraiser but will follow up with Property Appraiser Burke on the matter.
There being no further business, and upon motion by Commissioner McCroan, second
by Commissioner Husband, and unanimous vote, the meeting did then adjourn at 9:02
