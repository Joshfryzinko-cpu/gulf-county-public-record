JANUARY 14, 2026
JOINT WORKSHOP
PORT ST. JOE, FLORIDA
The Gulf County Board of County Commissioners met this date in a joint workshop with
the Port St. Joe City Commissioners with the following members present: Chairman
Sandy Quinn, Jr., Vice Chairman Phil McCroan, and Commissioners Christopher
McLemore, Lloyd Jack Husband, III, and Randy Pridgeon.
City of Port St. Joe Commissioners and City staff present were as follows: City Mayor Rex
Buzzett, City Commissioners Steve Kerigan, Brett Lowry, and Scott Hoffman, City
Attorney Clinton McChill, City Manager Jim Anderson and Clerk Charlotte Pierce <City
Commissioner Eric Langston appeared at 12:34 p.m., E.T.>.
Others present were: County Attorney Jeremy Novak, Chief Deputy Leanna Cooper,
Deputy Clerk Casi Campbell, County Administrator Michael L. Hammond, County
Assistant Administrator Clay Smallwood, III, Deputy Administrator Kari Summers, Deputy
Administrator Silvia Williams, Building Official Brad Bailey, Gulf County E.D.C. Director
Jim McKnight, G.I.S. Coordinator Clay Kennedy, Office of Management & Budget Director
Sherry Herring, Mosquito Control Director Austin Horton, County Planner Doug Crane,
Public Works Director Mark Cothran, Assistant Public Works Director Raymond Hart, and
T.D.C. Executive Director Blake Rish.
County Chairman Quinn called the meeting to order at 11:00 a.m., E.T.
CITY AND COUNTY
City Mayor Buzzett discussed holding another meeting in one month to discuss any
additional topics to be held at the City’s meeting hall. He discussed the City’s workforce
housing project, inquiring if the County would join the City and the developer to construct
the road. Upon inquiry by County Chairman Quinn on the cost to build the road, City
Mayor Buzzett reported the construction cost is approximately $1.5 million. Discussion
followed regarding contacts with the State for funding. County Chairman Quinn discussed
having the County’s lobbyist to contact the FL Department of Commerce regarding
funding this project. City Mayor Buzzett discussed the need for a larger recreational
facility (Field of Dreams), noting the City of Port St. Joe now has enough beds to facilitate
tournaments. Upon inquiry by County Commissioner Pridgeon, City Mayor Buzzett noted
the location of the recreational facility, known as Field of Dreams. County Commissioner
Pridgeon discussed seeking support from the City of Port St. Joe for the Back Beach
Road Project, noting the County is in communication with D.O.T. (Department of
Transportation). City Mayor Buzzett stated the City of Port St. Joe provided a letter of
support to the County last year in favor of this project. County Commissioner McCroan
noted the County will be assisting the City of Port St. Joe with funding a new concession,
up to $75,000.00. County Commissioner Pridgeon noted the City could utilize the

ballfields at Donnie Wayne Brake Memorial Park for practices. City Mayor Buzzett stated
the City has access to the ballfields at the Washington Gym Recreational Center. County
Commissioner Pridgeon discussed his concerns regarding property owners renting
without paying bed tax, inquiring if the City of Port St. Joe inspect homes within the City.
City Manager Anderson appeared before the Board to discuss the City’s procedure
regarding short-term rentals within the City limits, noting there are few areas that allow
RBO (rent by owner). Discussion followed between the City and County working together
on this matter. County Chairman Quinn discussed working with the City of Port St. Joe
with demolition of derelict property, requesting City Manager Anderson provide a revised
list of properties. He discussed the renovations at the Washington Gym Recreational
Center and the upcoming assist with the soccer fields. County Commissioner Pridgeon
discussed the County’s desire to create a training facility, noting members from Tom P.
Hanely Technical College toured the proposed maritime academy (old Arizona Chemical
site). County Commissioner Pridgeon inquired what the City’s concerns are with the
floating dry dock. City Mayor Buzzett stated his concern is with regards to the amount of
taxpayers funds for a floating dry dock when there are no guarantee of work, noting with
the upgrade to the Tyndall Bridge there will be no more need for boats to dock at Eastern
Shipbuilding in Gulf County and the recent sell by Eastern Shipbuilding on a piece of
property across from the old Arizona Chemical site. He also discussed his concerns
regarding the (St. Joseph) Bay. Discussion followed. County Commissioner Pridgeon
discussed the floating dry dock and the economic impact for Gulf County. County
Commissioner McCroan discussed creating a buffer around Bird Island. County
Commissioner Pridgeon discussed creating a bird sanctuary at Bird Island. City Mayor
Buzzett had no objection to the proposed bird sanctuary at Bird Island. City Commissioner
Hoffman discussed his concerns with the floating dry dock and with the recent sale by
Eastern Shipbuilding of a portion of their property to a concrete company. He discussed
the rescinding of the support letter of the floating dry dock and his concerns with protecting
the Bay. City Commissioner Hoffman stated he is not in support of any dredge. He
inquired what would be the reason to place rocks around Bird Island. County
Commissioner Pridgeon stated it would help the floating dry dock, but another part to
preserve is creating a living shoreline in Highland View. He stated the more structure in
the Bay will supply more fish and the betterment of the Bay. County Chairman Quinn
inquired to the City of a job solution for 18-year-old graduates. City Commissioner
Hoffman discussed supporting jobs in training students at the high school. Discussion
followed regarding creating jobs, taking care of the Bay and the proposed floating dry
dock. City Mayor Buzzett discussed the need for workforce housing and assistance in
building this road. County Commissioner Pridgeon stated the County is building a
Wastewater Treatment Plant, requesting the City of Port St. Joe to join the County in
sewer services and creating a utility service board. City Mayor Buzzett reported that the
City of Port St. Joe has received $25 million to rehab the City’s Wastewater Treatment
Plant, requesting the County join their finances with the City of Port St. Joe. County
Commissioner McCroan noted the cost to clean the lagoon will take $100 million. He
discussed the City and County working together to get the Plant off the Canal. City
Commissioner Hoffman discussed getting the Plant off the Bay and discussed his
concerns regarding the County providing sewer service to the City of Wewahitchka.
County Commissioner McCroan discussed the discharge location for the City of

Wewahitchka and the benefits of offering sewer service to both Municipalities. Discussion
followed. Upon inquiry by County Chairman Quinn, City Mayor Buzzett stated staff will
provide available dates to the County for the next meeting. Discussion followed regarding
the City of Port St. Joe in support of joining the County in getting the City’s Wastewater
Treatment Plant off the canal. County Commissioner Pridgeon inquired what material
could the County provide to the City of Port St. Joe before their next joint workshop
regarding the projects discussed at today’s meeting. City Commissioner Lowry requested
information on the dredge project for the floating dry dock. City Commissioner Kerigan
discussed meeting regularly with the County to discuss various projects, thanking the
Board for holding this workshop. <City Commissioner Eric Langston entered the meeting
at 12:34 p.m., E.T.> City Commissioner Kerigan continued by discussing his concerns
with regards to the bill set to reconstruct the Port Authority board. County Chairman Quinn
noted City Commissioner Langston was late to this Workshop due to being at the school,
thanking him for taking care of the students. City Commissioner Langston thanked law
enforcement for keeping the students safe regarding an incident at the school today.
There being no further discussion, the meeting did then adjourn at 12:36 p.m., E.T.
