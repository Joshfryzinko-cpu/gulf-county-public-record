MAY 29, 2026
SPECIAL MEETING
PORT ST. JOE, FLORIDA
The Gulf County Board of County Commissioners met this date in special session with
the following members present: Chairman Sandy Quinn, Jr., Vice Chairman Phil
McCroan, and Commissioners Christopher McLemore and Randy Pridgeon.
Commissioner Lloyd Jack Husband, III appeared via telephone.
Others present were: County Attorney Jeremy Novak, Clerk James M. Terry, Chief
Deputy Leanna Cooper, Director of Finance & Management Leo Bebeau, County
Administrator Michael L. Hammond <appeared via telephone>, County Assistant
Administrator Clay Smallwood, III, Deputy Administrator Silvia Williams, Emergency
Management Director/Fire Coordinator Matt Herring, G.I.S. Coordinator Clay Kennedy,
Office of Management & Budget Director Sherry Herring, Property Appraiser Mitch Burke,
Property Appraiser IT Tech Dennis Barfield, Jr., Sheriff Mike Harrison, Sheriff's
Department Captain Chris Buchanan, Supervisor of Elections Rhonda Pierce, Tax
Collector Ashley Forehand, and T.D.C. Executive Director Blake Rish.
Chairman Quinn called the meeting to order at 10:03 a.m., E.T.
Chairman Quinn then led in the Pledge of Allegiance to the Flag.
GOVERNOR’S ANNOUNCEMENT HOMESTEAD EXEMPTION
Chairman Quinn noted that Commissioner Husband and County Administrator Hammond
are appearing telephonic. Property Appraiser Burke appeared before the Board to provide
a highlight of the Senate joint resolution with regards to the Governor’s proposed
homestead exemption and potential impacts to Gulf County. Clerk Terry noted the State
was in discussions of creating a trust for Counties that are in a deficit due to non-
homestead properties, inquiring if this was included in the joint resolution. Property
Appraiser Burke confirmed. Discussion followed regarding this proposed trust fund.
Property Appraiser reported that Gulf County’s total ad-valorem revenue under the current
system, in rough numbers, is $24,114,063.00, with the $150,000.00 exemption the total
is reduced to $22,661,330.00, reducing to $21,816,807.00 with the $250,000.00
exemption. He discussed his concerns with the impact to the City of Wewahitchka with
regards to this proposed exemption. Property Appraiser Burke noted this change does
not begin until January 1, 2027. Commissioner Pridgeon inquired if Property Appraiser
Burke would be posting this information on his website for the public. Property Appraiser
Burke stated he would have to review the matter further being that he holds a political
office, noting his office will be educating the public through information listed on the TRIM
notices. Commissioner Pridgeon discussed not taking a side but encouraging the County
to educate the public. County Attorney Novak discussed holding this special meeting
which is public records and the data presented at today’s meeting is also of public record,

noting the County may be able to create a link on the County’s website. Chairman Quinn
was in agreement to create a link to provide the data to the public for review.
ADMINISTRATIVE REPORT & UPDATE
ST. JOSEPH BAY HUMANE SOCIETY
Upon inquiry by Commissioner McCroan regarding the Humane Society, County
Administrator Hammond discussed the County policy, suggesting that the Board pay the
St. Joseph Bay Humane Society through May 2026 until the County receives their
financials. Commissioner McCroan made the motion. Chairman Quinn stated the motion
is to go ahead and pay the Humane Society. Commissioner McLemore seconded the
motion. Chairman Quinn called for public comment. There being none, the motion passed
unanimously.
GOVERNOR’S ANNOUNCEMENT HOMESTEAD EXEMPTION
County Administrator Hammond discussed the need for the County to travel to
Tallahassee next week. Commissioner Pridgeon stated that he is available Tuesday to
travel to Tallahassee, but not on Monday. County Administrator Hammond noted once the
hearings are scheduled, he will notify the members of the Board. Sheriff Harrison inquired
if the deadline to submit budget requests will be extended until clarification is made with
the joint resolution. OMB Director Herring stated the Statutory deadline to submit budget
requests is June 1st, noting future discussions can follow as new developments arise.
Upon inquiry by Commissioner Pridgeon, Sheriff Harrison stated that the Florida Sheriff’s
Association is not taking a stance on the topic. Discussion followed.
There being no further business, and upon motion by Commissioner McLemore, second
by Commissioner McCroan, and unanimous vote, the meeting did then adjourn at 10:23
a.m., E.T.
