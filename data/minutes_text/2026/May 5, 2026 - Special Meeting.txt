MAY 5, 2026
SPECIAL MEETING
PORT ST. JOE, FLORIDA
The Gulf County Board of County Commissioners met this date in special session with
the following members present: Chairman Sandy Quinn, Jr., Vice Chairman Phil
McCroan, and Commissioners Christopher McLemore, Lloyd Jack Husband, III, and
Randy Pridgeon.
Others present were: County Attorney Jeremy Novak, Clerk James M. Terry, Chief
Deputy Leanna Cooper, Director of Finance & Management Leo Bebeau, County
Administrator Michael L. Hammond, County Assistant Administrator Clay Smallwood, III,
Deputy Administrator Kari Summers, Deputy Administrator Silvia Williams, Building
Official/County Planner Doug Crane, Emergency Management Director/Fire Coordinator
Matt Herring, Grant Administrator Erin Kennedy, Grant Administrator Laura Beech-
Armstrong, Office of Management & Budget Director Sherry Herring, Mosquito Control
Director Austin Horton, Public Works Director Mark Cothran, Assistant Public Works
Director Raymond Hart, Sheriff Mike Harrison, and T.D.C. Executive Director Blake Rish.
Chairman Quinn called the meeting to order at 3:30 p.m., E.T.
Chairman Quinn then led in the Pledge of Allegiance to the Flag.
AWARD BID #2526-10 – WATER TRANSMISSION MAIN INSTALLATION
Assistant County Administrator Smallwood presented page 1 from the Administrative
Update and Report packet <copy provided to the Clerk for the record> for the Board to
consider staff’s recommendation to award Bid #2526-10 for the Water Transmission Main
Installation to the lowest-responsive bidder, 850 Construction Services, in the amount of
$546,451.05. Commissioner McCroan so moved. Commissioner McLemore seconded
the motion. Chairman Quinn called for public comment. There being no public comment,
the motion passed unanimously.
REJECT BID #2526-12 – SALINAS PARK DUNE RESTORATION
On page 2 of the packet, Assistant County Administrator Smallwood recommended to the
Board to reject Bid #2526-12 for the Salinas Park Dune Restoration and readvertise to
receive bids. Commissioner McCroan made a motion to reject these bids. Commissioner
Pridgeon seconded the motion. Chairman Quinn called for public comment. Georgio
Bisoglio, of Cape San Blas appeared before the Board to discuss the project,
recommending the Board split the work into three phases: 1) dune restoration; 2)
landscaping and; 3) the culvert portion. Discussion followed regarding possibly removing
the landscaping portion from the dune restoration. Chairman Quinn called for any
additional public comment. There being no additional public comment, the motion passed
unanimously.

AWARD BID #2526-14 – CYPRESS CREEK BRIDGE REPAIR
From page 3 of the packet, Assistant County Administrator Smallwood recommended
Board award Bid #2526-14 for the Cypress Creek Bridge Repair to lowest responsive
bidder, Webber Infrastructure Management, Inc., in the amount of $73,590.00, noting that
FL Department of Transportation has agreed to provide additional grant funding for this
project. He stated staff will be bringing the Board a supplemental agreement for approval
at a later date with regards to the additional grant funds. Chairman Quinn called for a
motion to award. Commissioner Husband made the motion. Commissioner McLemore
seconded the motion. Chairman Quinn called for public comment. There being no public
comment, the motion passed unanimously.
ADMINISTRATIVE REPORT & UPDATE
AWARD BID #2526-15 – PURCHASE BUNKER GEAR
Assistant County Administrator Smallwood discussed pages 4-6 of the packet being a
recommendation from Fire Coordinator Herring to award Bid #2526-15 for the purchase
of bunker gear to Sunbelt Fire, in the amount of $3,322.00 per set, noting Sunbelt Fire
was the low bidder. Commissioner McCroan so moved. Commissioner Husband
seconded the motion. Chairman Quinn called for public comment. There being none, the
motion passed unanimously.
CHANGE ORDER #3 – HENRY AVENUE
Assistant County Administrator Smallwood reported that the project on Henry Avenue is
complete, presenting Change Order #3 from Roberts & Roberts, Inc. (Bid #2425-25) with
a decrease of $33,899.86 (found on page 7 of the packet). Commissioner McLemore
made the motion. Commissioner Pridgeon seconded the motion. Chairman Quinn called
for public comment. There being no public comment, the motion passed 4-0, with
Commissioner Husband abstaining due to his business completing the CEI services for
this project <FORM 8B on file with Clerk>.
CHANGE ORDER #2 – COUNTY ROADWAY PROJECT, PHASE IV
Assistant County Administrator Smallwood presented a change order (#2) request on
page 8 of the packet from Roberts & Roberts, Inc. with a decrease of $9,510.54 regarding
the County Roadway Project, Phase IV (Bid #2324-04). Commissioner Pridgeon made
the motion. Commissioner Husband seconded the motion. Chairman Quinn called for
public comment. There being none, the motion passed unanimously.
CHANGE ORDER #1 – SUBMERSIBLE WELL PUMP
Located on page 9 of the packet, Assistant County Administrator Smallwood requested
the Board approve a change order (#1) with Rowe Drilling, noting this contractor is
installing the well pump at White City and additional materials were needed. He reported
the increase amount is $1,573.00 to be paid with grant funds. Chairman called for a
motion to move forward. Commissioner McCroan so moved. Commissioner Husband
seconded the motion. Chairman Quinn called for public comment. There being no public
comment, the motion passed unanimously.

GRANT AMENDMENT #1 – E-911 RECORDER MAINTENANCE
Assistant County Administrator Smallwood noted that page 12 is a request to allow the
Chairman to sign a grant agreement (Amendment #1 * FL Department of Management
Services * 12-10-07) with the State for E-911 recorder maintenance, increasing funds
from $64,452.69 to $68,940.69. Chairman Quinn called for a motion to approve this
amendment. Commissioner Husband so moved. Commissioner McCroan seconded the
motion. Chairman Quinn called for public comment. There being none, the motion passed
unanimously.
APPOINTMENT – CAREERSOURCE BOARD
County Administrator Hammond discussed page 13, requesting the Board to appoint
Cecil Jackson to serve on the CareerSource Gulf Coast board. Chairman Quinn noted he
has no objection to the appointment of Mr. Jackson, calling for a motion to move forward
with this recommendation. Commissioner Husband made the motion. Commissioner
McCroan seconded the motion. Chairman Quinn called for public comment. There being
no public comment, the motion passed unanimously.
PLAN – PUBLIC FACILITIES PROJECTS
County Administrator Hammond directed the Board to pages 14-15, recommending that
the Board accept the recommendation of the T.D.C. of this plan (Public Facilities Projects)
and move forward. Commissioner Pridgeon discussed the number of bathrooms in
District III, and the possibility of the Board considering the renovation of County public
restrooms next year. T.D.C. Director Rish discussed valuating each facility throughout the
County and then present a cost estimate based on the repair needs. Discussion followed.
Commissioner McCroan discussed his concerns regarding the recent vandalism in the

bathrooms. Discussion followed. Chairman Quinn called for a motion to go with the
Administrator’s recommendation from the T.D.C. Commissioner McLemore so moved.
Commissioner Husband seconded the motion. Chairman Quinn called for public
comment. There being none, the motion passed unanimously.
ROAD SIGNS – CHARLIE KIRK WAY
County Administrator Hammond reported that staff met with State Attorney Larry Basford,
noting the violators that stole the stop and road signs (Charlie Kirk Way) will return to Gulf
County to enter a plea. He stated the individuals will make restitution, appear before the
Board to apologize and complete 40 (forty) hours of community service with T.D.C. and
Public Works. Discussion followed. County Administrator Hammond noted the restitution
is approximately $3,000.00.
COUNTY HEALTH FAIR
County Administrator Hammond reported that the health fair is scheduled for tomorrow,
noting the big red bus will be on site for anyone wishing to donate blood. He asked that
all department heads encourage their staff to attend the health fair.
DRINKING WATER – CITY OF PORT ST. JOE
Commissioner Pridgeon noted the City water is under a boil notice, discussing his
concerns that customers are confused between the County and City water services. He
requested that a letter be submitted to the City of Port St. Joe requesting the City contact
the County when there is a boil water notice.
GOLF CARTS ORDINANCE
Commissioner Pridgeon inquired when will the locking device for the golf carts be
delivered. County Administrator Hammond noted the projected delivery date should be
today, but he will follow up after today’s meeting. Upon inquiry by Commissioner Pridgeon
regarding a second advertisement, County Administrator Hammond stated the Golf Cart
Ordinance (#2026-02) is in effect now, but the County has advertised again in case there
is a need to amend. Commissioner Pridgeon discussed his concerns with golf cart drivers
crossing U.S. Highway 98. Public Works Director Cothran discussed placing an order for
twelve locking devices. Upon inquiry by Commissioner Pridgeon regarding accepting
online payment, Clerk Terry stated the Clerk’s Office has the capability of accepting online
payments for infractions but waiting on the vendor to set up the software.
DRINKING WATER – CITY OF PORT ST. JOE
Commissioner Husband discussed the City of Port St. Joe’s water issue that took place
over the weekend, noting he contacted Emergency Management Director Herring to place
a notice on AlertGulf. He stated, with no objections of the Board, the City of Port St. Joe
could use the County’s AlertGulf system to broadcast notices. County Administrator
Hammond state he will send a letter to the City of Port St. Joe, noting the City did issue
a press release, but was a bit confusing on the service areas affect by the boil water
notice.

There being no further business, and upon motion by Commissioner Husband, second
by Commissioner McCroan, and unanimous vote, the meeting did then adjourn at 3:56
p.m., E.T.
