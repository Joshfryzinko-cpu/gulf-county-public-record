14. Resolution – Adoption of the Gulf County Local Mitigation Strategy Task Force, as
follows:
RESOLUTION NO. 2026-12
WHEREAS, the Gulf County Board of County Commissioners
created a Local Mitigation Strategy Task Force comprised of County and
Municipal representatives, private citizens, local and regional agencies
involved in hazard mitigation activities, and agencies having authority to
regulate development including businesses and other private and non-profit
interests; and

WHEREAS, the Board charged the Task Force with the responsibility
to assess the hazards facing the County and to identify initiatives designed
to reduce the impact of those hazards; and
WHEREAS, the Task Force has completed the hazard assessment
and has identified numerous initiatives designed to reduce the impact of
future disasters; and
WHEREAS, the Task Force has incorporated their findings and
recommendations into the Gulf County Local Mitigation Strategy; and
WHEREAS, the Board is committed to reducing the impact of
hazards for all County residents; and
WHEREAS, the Gulf County Local Mitigation Strategy supports
hazard mitigation actions throughout the entire County;
NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED, the Board adopted the Gulf
County Local Mitigation Strategy to reflect the current need and citizen
desire to identify and implement hazard mitigation initiatives that will reduce
the County’s susceptibility to numerous hazards. And, at the appropriate
time, the Board will develop and submit funding proposals to the appropriate
agencies to implement the hazard mitigation initiatives identified in the Gulf
County Local Mitigation Strategy.
ADOPTED this 31st day of March 2026
(end)

ECONOMIC DEVELOPMENT AGREEMENT – AIRPORT ROAD
From pages 7-12 of the packet, County Attorney Novak discussed an amended economic
development agreement regarding the conveyance of the airport road, requesting Board
approval. Commissioner Pridgeon discussed his concerns regarding a letter from Tyndall
Air Force Base regarding the County’s proposed airport project, suggesting the Board
pause this project. Commissioner McCroan discussed the recent restriction at Tyndall Air
Force Base, noting this is his only concern. County Administrator Hammond stated that
Tyndall did not object during the designated period, receiving a letter of approval and FAA
drew the airspace. He discussed his concerns regarding pausing this project, noting the
County will lose over $1 million from D.O.T. County Administrator Hammond noted the
request today is for the Board to approve the conveyance of the road, noting staff will still
need the Board to approve the paving of the road. Upon inquiry by Chairman Quinn,
County Administrator Hammond noted that if the County pauses the project in the future,
the road will revert to St. Joe Company. Discussion followed. Commissioner Pridgeon
stated for the record that he thinks this is detrimental to our County going forward with an

airport when the County has been said by a person that trains people how to fly airplanes
that this is not a good idea. Chairman Quinn discussed the airport project, noting that the
County should negotiate an agreement with St. Joe Company to maintain some of the
property for emergency management even if there is no airport. Commissioner McCroan
discussed his concerns regarding building a road and the possibility that the airport
project is not developed. Discussion followed. Commissioner Pridgeon inquired if County
Attorney Novak could modify the proposed addendum to address possible future airport
but definite plans for emergency management. County Attorney Novak discussed the
Board moving in the direction of funding, permitting, P3, transfer of 1,000 acres, noting
he will go back to St. Joe Company that the project is for an airport and if not, then the
County can address building an emergency management facility at this location. After
discussion by members of the Board, County Administrator Hammond stated staff will not
pave the road until the Board directs staff to proceed, noting the County needs to own the
road to finish the permitting. Discussion followed. Commissioner Pridgeon made a motion
to push this and let him (County Attorney Novak) come back with what the Board
discussed or a reverter clause that if the airport does not happen, it is still our (County)
road. Commissioner McCroan inquired if Commissioner Pridgeon wanted to accept the
conveyance and put that clause in the addendum agreement. Commissioner Pridgeon
had no objection. Commissioner McCroan seconded the motion. Chairman Quinn called
for public comment. There being no public comment, the motion passed unanimously.
County Administrator Hammond requested clarification of the motion, inquiring if the
Board is accepting the road with the stipulation that Commissioner Pridgeon added.
Commissioner McCroan stated the stipulation with the 1,000 acres is the reverter. County
Administrator Hammond inquiring either way (with or without an airport) the County would
keep the road. Commissioner McCroan stated yes.
SIGN REMOVAL – CHARLIE KIRK WAY
Public Works Director Cothran reported that some spring breakers removed the Charlie
Kirk Way signs at both ends of the road, one of the stop signs, and the pole in the middle
of the night. He discussed contacting the Gulf County Sheriff’s Office to report the
incident, reporting the Sheriff’s Office identified six (6) subjects. Public Works Director
Cothran noted it is a crime to steal a sign and a felony to steal a stop sign. He discussed
his concerns regarding individuals breaking the law. Commissioner McCroan stated the
Sheriff’s Office received the warrants, inquiring about the process. Sheriff Harrison
reported that his office secured warrants for five (5) individuals, noting the sixth individual

that all five (5) subjects were arrested and booked at the local jail in Missouri, noting there
was a change in the bond, and the bond reflected a $1,500.00 per charge to bond out.
He stated the subjects bonded out by paying $3,000.00 each and will appear in Gulf
County. County Administrator Hammond discussed the issuance of the warrants, noting
the warrants were recalled because State Attorney Larry Basford personally called the
Judge to state Gulf County did not want to spend the money to transport. He stated the
Sheriff had already made arrangement for transportation, discussion followed regarding
his concerns regarding the removal of the signs. County Administrator Hammond stated

that he requested that the County Attorney draft a letter (found on pages 13-14 of the
packet) to request Governor DeSantis to appoint a special prosecutor in this case.
Discussion followed. Chairman Quinn called for a motion to send this letter.
Commissioner Husband made the motion. Commissioner McCroan seconded the motion.
Chairman Quinn called for public comment. There being none, the motion passed
