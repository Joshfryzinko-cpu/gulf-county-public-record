Northwest Regional Library System and the grant funds provided to the libraries due to

Cities to assist financially. County Administrator Hammond discussed working with the
Municipalities to contribute to the libraries. He discussed his concerns regarding the hours

BUILDING HEIGHT LIMIT – ST. JOE BEACH
Commissioner Pridgeon discussed receiving resident complaints regarding height of
newly constructed homes at St. Joe Beach, noting the Building Department did review
these homes and the height does meet the County’s LDR. He noted the County does not
have zoning, discussing recent SB180 and legal challenges. Planner Crane discussed
the height limit within the County, noting the current height limit is 50 feet. Discussion
followed. County Attorney Novak discussed complaints received over the years within the
County regarding the height limit, noting the home rule and Legislative changes moving
forward. He stated the County has a conservative approach to development. County
Administrator Hammond stated the County has a three habitable floor limit since the
1990’s when the Comprehensive Plan came into effect. He provided various scenarios
throughout the County regarding height limits due to flood zones. Discussion followed.
After Mr. King appeared before the Board to discuss development of his subdivision
(Overstreet Heights), Planner Crane stated for the record that Mr. King had never stated
at any point or time that his intention was to install pole barns (in Overstreet Heights
Subdivision). Discussion followed regarding these lots and the contingency. Further
discussion followed regarding obtaining clarification of the developers’ intent during the
application process. County Attorney Novak discussed guidelines and regulations
established in Gulf County. Discussion followed. County Administrator Hammond called
for clarification of the requested LDR change, inquiring if the Board intends to, with the
exception of P.D.P.s (Planned Development Projects), with residential and mixed
commercial land use there must be a prior structure before receiving an accessory
structure permit. Commissioner McCroan inquired of the areas. County Administrator
Hammond stated residential and mixed commercial areas would apply to the proposed
LDR change, noting County Attorney Novak will draft an ordinance to hold Public
Hearings for the Board to consider. He recommended the Board exempt areas already
exempted. Discussion followed. County Administrator Hammond requested that
members of the Board meet with Administration and County Attorney to provide their
ideas and staff will draft a proposal to bring back before the Board for consideration. He
discussed the need for grandfathering. Discussion followed.
