JANUARY 14, 2026
JOINT WORKSHOP
PORT ST. JOE, FLORIDA
The Gulf County Board of County Commissioners held a joint workshop with the City of
Wewahitchka with the following County Board members present: Chairman Sandy
Quinn, Jr., Vice Chairman Phil McCroan, and Commissioners Christopher McLemore,
Lloyd Jack Husband, III, and Commissioner Randy Pridgeon.
Others present were: County Attorney Jeremy Novak, Chief Deputy Leanna Cooper,
Deputy Clerk Casi Campbell, County Administrator Michael L. Hammond, County
Assistant Administrator Clay Smallwood, III, Deputy Administrator Kari Summers, Deputy
Administrator Silvia Williams, G.I.S. Coordinator Clay Kennedy, Office of Management &
Budget Director Sherry Herring, County Planner Doug Crane, Assistant Public Works
Director Raymond Hart, and T.D.C. Executive Director Blake Rish.
City Mayor Phillip Gaskin, City Commissioner Charlie Pettis and City Manager Michael
Gortman were also present.
County Chairman Quinn called the meeting to order at 1:01 p.m., E.T.
CITY AND COUNTY
City Commissioner Pettis thanked the Board for helping the City Commission of
Wewahitchka. City Mayor Gaskin also thanked the Board, noting the City of Wewahitchka
has no new needs at this time. He discussed the County’s efforts to renovate the Old Gulf
County Courthouse in Wewahitchka. County Commissioner Husband provided a status
update regarding the Old Gulf County Courthouse renovation project, noting the County
needs to get this building on the National Historic Registry. Discussion followed regarding
applying for grant funds for this project. County Commissioner McLemore thanked City
staff of Wewahitchka for their working relationship with the County. County Administrator
Hammond discussed the recent change in the libraries’ hours of operation, noting that a
recommendation will be provided to the Board at the next meeting. He discussed the
funding the County provided to the libraries, inquiring if the City of Wewahitchka could
assist with any additional funding. City Mayor Gaskin discussed the amount and time the
City of Wewahitchka spends at the Wewahitchka Library. Discussion followed. Upon
inquiry by County Administrator Hammond, Chief Deputy Clerk Cooper provided an
update regarding the online advertisement portal project. County Administrator Hammond
noted he will notify the Municipalities and the School Board once the County goes live
with the online advertisement portal, noting these agencies are participants. Upon inquiry
by County Administrator Hammond, City Manager Gortman provided an update on the
lighting project at T. L. James Park. Discussion followed regarding the new fire truck at
the City of Wewahitchka and the newly built fire station. County Administrator Hammond
discussed working with the City of Wewahitchka on the sewer services, noting the County

has a desire to run a sewer line to the Howard Creek Workcamp. County Commissioner
Husband discussed the County applying for a grant to install a sidewalk on Lake Grove
Road. Discussion followed. County Administrator Hammond stated that the light pole will
have to be moved to make room for the sidewalk. He reported the County will be taking
bids for the Road Bond Paving Project 2026 to the end of the month and open in March
2026.
There being no further discussion, the meeting did then adjourn at 1:17 p.m., E.T.
