BANNED – GULF COUNTY SENIOR CITIZENS BUILDING
Gulf County United Community Development, Inc. President Amy Rogers appeared
before the Board to discuss a lease agreement between the County and a private
organization. She stated Gulf County United Community Development desires to continue
hosting a mobile food drive at the Old Gulf County Courthouse property in Wewahitchka
and hold social-economical programs at the Stiles Brown Senior Citizens Building, noting
conflicts with their last events in using these facilities and inquired why she was banned
from the building. She presented a letter to the Board <copy provided to the Clerk for the
record>. Chairman Quinn noted Ms. Rogers’ conflict is not with Gulf County Commission,
reported the County does not own the Senior Citizens Building in Wewahitchka. He stated
the County leases the property that the building is on. Chairman Quinn stated the County
did not issue a no trespassing on Ms. Rogers. County Administrator Hammond reported
it is a 75-year lease, noting the County could not sell or donate the property due to the
deed restrictions on the property from St. Joe Company. He stated the County cannot tell
the Senior Citizens how to run their day-to-day operations, suggesting Ms. Rogers go to
the Senior Citizens’ board of directors and make your case. County Administrator
Hammond stated that the City of Wewahitchka owns the senior citizens building in
Wewahitchka, but for the building in Port St. Joe she will need to address the Senior
Citizens board. Discussion followed. Ms. Rogers inquired how much of taxpayers’ dollars
are being used for the senior citizens program. OMB Director Herring reported the amount
is $59,808.00. Ms. Rogers discussed her concerns regarding the County giving funds to
a private individual that bans taxpayers. Discussion followed.
