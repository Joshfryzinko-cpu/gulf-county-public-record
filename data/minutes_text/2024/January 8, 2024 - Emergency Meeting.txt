JANUARY 8, 2024
EMERGENCY MEETING
PORT ST. JOE, FLORIDA
The Gulf County Board of County Commissioner met this date in emergency session with
the following members present: Chairman Sandy Quinn, Jr., Vice Chairman Phil
McCroan, and Commissioners David C. Rich, Jack Husband, and Patrick Farrell.
Others present were: County Attorney Jeremy Novak, Director of Finance & Management
Leo Bebeau, Deputy Clerk Leanna Cooper, County Administrator Michael L. Hammond,
County Assistant Administrator Clay Smallwood, III, Deputy Administrator Kari Summers,
Building Official Brad Bailey, County Consultant Warren Yeager, Jr., Emergency
Management Director/Fire Coordinator Matt Herring, Emergency Management Staff
Assistant Tim Wood, Grant Administrator Kaci Rhodes, Grant Administrator Erin Kennedy,
Grant Administrator Carla Ferrell, Human Resource Director Brett Lowry, Mosquito
Control Director Austin Horton, County Planner Doug Crane, Assistant Public Works
Director Raymond Hart, Sheriff Mike Harrison, and T.D.C. Executive Director Silvia
Williams.
Chairman Quinn called the meeting to order at 4:00 p.m., E.T.
Chairman Quinn then led in the Pledge of Allegiance to the Flag.
WINTER STORM
Emergency Management Director Herring appeared before the Board to provide an
update regarding Winter Storm Finn, noting the County should receive 30-40 mph
sustained winds and up to 70 mph wind gust once the storm reaches our area. County
Administrator Hammond stated staff is not recommending any evacuations but does need
to provide voluntary notifications at Presnell’s and the two Indian Pass Campgrounds. He
discussed the possibility of closing County offices to non-essential personnel, noting the
schools will be closed. Commissioner Rich reported the river will reach flood stage on
Friday. Discussion followed regarding rainfall and the projected upcoming rain. Upon
inquiry by Commissioner McCroan, Assistant County Administrator Smallwood stated
D.O.T. will be monitoring the Stumphole throughout the night and tomorrow.
Commissioner Husband discussed closing the Courthouse tomorrow, allowing only
emergency personnel, public works, and E.M.S. to report to work. Commissioner Farrell
agreed. The Board had no objection. County Attorney Novak then read a proposed local
state of emergency regarding Winter Storm Finn <copy provided to the Clerk for the
record> for the Board to consider. Discussion followed regarding voluntary evacuations
for the two campgrounds at Indian Pass and at Presnell’s and opening Painted Pony
Road as an alternate route, if needed. Commissioner Rich requested Public Works to
remove a leaning tree on U.S. Highway 71 North, discussing his concern regarding public
safety. Chairman Quinn called for a motion to move forward with the Local State of

Emergency. Commissioner Rich so moved. Commissioner McCroan seconded the
motion. Chairman Quinn called for public comment. There being none, the motion passed
unanimously. Sheriff Harrison reported the Courts will have a delayed opening tomorrow
at 1:00 p.m., E.T.
There being no further business, and upon motion by Commissioner Farrell, second by
Commissioner Husband, and unanimous vote, the meeting did then adjourn at 4:09 p.m.,
E.T.
