SEPTEMBER 4, 2024
SPECIAL MEETING
PORT ST. JOE, FLORIDA
The Gulf County Board of County Commissioners met this date in special session with
the following members present: Chairman Sandy Quinn, Jr., Vice Chairman Phil
McCroan, and Commissioners Jack Husband and Patrick Farrell <Commissioner David
C. Rich was absent>.
Others present were: County Attorney Jeremy Novak, Clerk Rebecca L. Norris, Director
of Finance & Management Leo Bebeau, Deputy Clerk Leanna Cooper, County
Administrator Michael L. Hammond, County Assistant Administrator Clay Smallwood, III,
Deputy Administrator Kari Summers, Emergency Management Director/Fire Coordinator
Matt Herring, Gulf County E.M.S. Director Jody Daniels, Grant Administrator Erin
Kennedy, Grant Administrator Laura Beach-Armstrong, Human Resource Director Kaci
Rhodes, Office of Management & Budget Director Sherry Herring, Mosquito Control
Director Austin Horton, Public Works Director Mark Cothran, Sheriff Mike Harrison, and

Chairman Quinn called the meeting to order at 4:30 p.m., E.T.

ADMINISTRATIVE UPDATE & REPORT
Kim Bodine, of CareerSource Gulf Coast appeared before the Board to present a
subaward agreement with the Florida Department of Commerce <copy was not provided
to the Clerk for the record>, noting this is a required agreement for workforce funding.
Assistant County Administrator Smallwood noted staff received the proposed agreement
last week and County Attorney Novak needs to review it, recommending the Board
approve, contingent upon County Attorney’s review. Chairman Quinn called for a motion
to move forward with it, contingent upon County Attorney review. Commissioner McCroan
so moved. Commissioner Farrell seconded the motion. Chairman Quinn called for public
comment. There being no public comment, the motion passed unanimously (4-0).

ADMINISTRATIVE UPDATE & REPORT
ONLINE LEGAL ADVERTISEMENT
Clerk Norris discussed a bill that passed by Legislator a few years ago allowing for
government agencies to advertise bids and public notices on publicly available websites
in lieu of though newspaper print. She noted staff found a vendor that provides this service
and will be a viable option for the County. Clerk Norris reported a recommendation will be
presented to the Board at the September (regular) meeting. Commissioner Husband
inquired if the ads will still be posted on the Administration Building doors. Clerk Norris
noted yes, if the Board still wishes for staff to post on the facility doors. County Attorney
Novak discussed the policy regarding posting notice on the annex building, noting it will
continue. Following discussion, County Administrator Hammond discussed bringing a
recommendation back before the Board at the September meeting for approval. He noted
going in this direction could be a huge cost savings to the County.
