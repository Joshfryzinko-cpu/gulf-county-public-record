NOVEMBER 26, 2024
SPECIAL MEETING
PORT ST. JOE, FLORIDA
The Gulf County Board of County Commissioners met this date in special session with
the following members present: Chairman Sandy Quinn, Jr., Vice Chairman Phil
McCroan, and Commissioners Christopher McLemore, Lloyd Jack Husband, III, and
Randy Pridgeon.
Others present were: County Attorney Jeremy Novak, Clerk Rebecca L. Norris, Deputy
Clerk Leanna Cooper, County Administrator Michael L. Hammond, County Assistant
Administrator Clay Smallwood, III, Deputy Administrator Kari Summers, Building Official
Brad Bailey, Code Enforcement Officer Robert Knowles, Emergency Management
Director/Fire Coordinator Matt Herring, Emergency Management Staff Assistant Tim
Wood, Gulf County E.M.S. Director Jody Daniels, Grant Administrator Erin Kennedy,
Grant Administrator Laura Beech-Armstrong, Human Resource Director Kaci Rhodes,
Office of Management & Budget Director Sherry Herring, Mosquito Control Director Austin
Horton, County Planner Doug Crane, Public Works Director Mark Cothran, Assistant
Public Works Director Raymond Hart, Road Department Superintendent Chris Wood,
Sheriff Mike Harrison, T.D.C. Executive Director Silvia Williams, and VSO/S.H.I.P./Central
Services Director Lynn Lanier.
Sheriff Harrison called the meeting to order at 8:30 a.m., ET
Jeremy Pridgeon opened the meeting with prayer. Commissioner Quinn led in the Pledge
of Allegiance to the Flag.
SWEARING-IN NEWLY ELECTED COUNTY COMMISSIONERS
Judge Timothy McFarland appeared before the Board to administer the Oath of Office to
Phil McCroan, County Commissioner for District V, Lloyd Jack Husband, III, County
Commissioner for District II, and Christopher “Spike” McLemore, County Commissioner
for District I. The Oath of Office was then administered to Randy Pridgeon, County
Commissioner for District III by Dr. Jeremy Pridgeon.
Commissioner McLemore thanked his Lord and Savior, Jesus Christ, his family and
friends for their support during the campaign. He extended thanks all that voted for him,
noting he is looking forward to working with the Board for what is best for Gulf County.
Commissioner Husband thanked District II for their support, looking forward to continuing
his service as their County Commissioner. He discussed listening and helping the
constituents and thanked the past and current members of the Board. Commissioner
Husband then thanked County staff, his family and the hard work his wife contributed
during his campaign.

Commissioner Pridgeon thanked God, his supporters, and his wife. He stated he is going
to do all he can to help the people of Gulf County.
Commissioner McCroan also thanked his Lord and Savior, Jesus Christ, his family, and
his wife for her support. He thanked Chairman Quinn and the Board, welcoming the new
Commissioners to the Board. Commissioner McCroan continued thanking the residents
of Gulf County and the County staff.
Chairman Quinn thanked all in attendance and the families of the Board members. He
stated each Commissioner represents our Lord and Savior, and discussed working
together and loving one another.
There being no further business, and upon motion by Commissioner Pridgeon, second
by Commissioner McLemore, and unanimous vote, the meeting did then adjourn at 8:49
a.m., E.T.
SANDY QUINN, JR.
