JANUARY 3, 2024
SPECIAL MEETING
PORT ST. JOE, FLORIDA
The Gulf County Board of County Commissioners met this date in special session with
the following members present: Chairman Sandy Quinn, Jr., Vice Chairman Phil
McCroan, and Commissioners David C. Rich, Jack Husband, and Patrick Farrell.
Others present were: County Attorney Jeremy Novak, Deputy Clerk Leanna Cooper,
County Administrator Michael L. Hammond, County Assistant Administrator Clay
Smallwood, III, Deputy Administrator Kari Summers, Emergency Management
Director/Fire Coordinator Matt Herring, Grant Administrator Erin Kennedy, Grant
Administrator Carla Ferrell, Office of Management & Budget Director Sherry Herring,
Mosquito Control Director Austin Horton, County Planner Doug Crane, Public Works
Director Mark Cothran, Assistant Public Works Director Raymond Hart, Sheriff's
Department Captain Chris Buchanan, and T.D.C. Executive Director Silvia Williams.
Chairman Quinn called the meeting to order at 12:00 p.m., E.T.
Chairman Quinn then led in the Pledge of Allegiance to the Flag.
AWARD BID #2324-03 – WATER MAIN INSTALLATION NORTH PHASE
County Administrator Hammond recommended <copy provided to the Clerk for the
record> to the Board to award to the low bidder, ECSC, LLC, in the amount of
$3,691,207.01 for the Water Main Installation North Phase (Bid #2324-03). Discussion
followed regarding the bidder and the received bid amounts. County Administrator
Hammond reported there will be a couple of caveats about the review by the grant agency
and submittal of the bidder’s performance bond. Chairman Quinn called for a motion to
move forward with the low bidder. Commissioner McCroan so moved. Commissioner
Farrell seconded the motion. Chairman Quinn called for public comment. There being no
public comment, the motion passed unanimously. County Administrator Hammond
discussed the project taking one year, noting the pipe will be delivered in three different
areas, some in White City, some will be behind the Courthouse, and some placed in Jones
Homestead.
ADMINISTRATIVE REPORT & UPDATE
County Administrator Hammond reported the contractor has mobilized in Highland View
(Bid #2223-34) but does not have an update on the repaving of Madison Street (Bid
#2324-02). Commissioner McCroan reported that the City of Port St. Joe is going to
mobilize on January 4, 2024 for the section in front of the High School and discussed his
concerns regarding the County starting their road project on Madison Street during the
same time frame.

County Administrator Hammond reported Legislative Week is coming up, noting the
Board meeting will be held the week after.
There being no further business, and upon motion by Commissioner Farrell, seconded by
Commissioner Rich, and unanimous vote, the meeting did then adjourn at 12:04 p.m.,
E.T.
