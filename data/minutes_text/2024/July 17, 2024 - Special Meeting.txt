INSURANCE
County Administrator Hammond addressed page 10 of the Administrative Update and
Report Packet, noting the Board approved at yesterday’s regular Board meeting to do the

ORDINANCE #2005-13 / GARBAGE RECEPTACLES TOURIST CORRIDORS
Code Enforcement Officer Knowles appeared before the Board to discuss meeting with
BCC Waste Solutions regarding the proposed changes to trash services in the tourist
corridor. Requesting a vote of the Board, Code Enforcement Officer Knowles reported
staff is looking to change the fee (fine amount), a two trash can minimum for all rentals
with house numbers marked on all trash cans, and adding language in the proposed
ordinance stating that all trash must be in the cans instead of on the ground. Discussion
followed regarding the revised fine amounts. The Board had no objection to first offence
fine of $100.00, $250.00 for second offense, and then going to the Special Magistrate for
the third. Building Official Bailey recommended the Board approve the extra language to
the proposed ordinance to require extra trash cans per bedroom. The Board had no
objection. County Administrator Hammond noted that if the Board is going to wait a month
to implement, there is no need for an emergency ordinance and therefore, staff will go
through the regular process of adopting the proposed ordinance. Commissioner Husband
inquired if BCC Waste Solution has the cans on-hand and how long will it take to
implement the additional cans. Code Enforcement Officer Knowles reported BCC Waste
Solutions will need a minimum of two weeks. Inquiring of the wishes of the Board, County
Administrator Hammond discussed educating the public over the next month and
recommended the Board go through the regular process in adopting the proposed
ordinance. The Board had no objection going through the regular normal procedure in
adopting the proposed ordinance. Commissioner Farrell then made a motion to impose
the $100.00, $250.00 fine amounts and (Special) Magistrate, stencil (house numbers) on
all of them (trash cans) and will send out to all rental companies. Commissioner McCroan
seconded the motion. County Administrator Hammond inquired if the Board is going to
add the language about the minimum number of cans, starting with two and adding
additional trash can thereafter for every odd number starting at five bedrooms.
Commissioner Farrell amended his motion for that. Commissioner Farrell inquired if it
needs to be added in the motion to have it on the site plan. Building Official Bailey reported
staff will handle that in-house. Upon inquiry by Chairman Quinn, Commissioner McCroan
stated his second stands. Chairman Quinn called for public comment. There being no

public comment, the motion passed unanimously. County Administrator Hammond
requested staff discuss pickup days. Code Enforcement Officer Knowles reported BCC
Waste Solutions has agreed to start working at St. Joe Beach, moving pickup to Mondays
just on U.S. Highway 98 (tourist corridor). Upon inquiry by Commissioner McCroan, Code
Enforcement Officer Knowles stated BCC Waste Solutions will pickup the entire tourist
corridor on Mondays. Brian Cox, of BCC Waste Solutions appeared before the Board to
discuss moving the tourist corridor in St. Joe Beach from Tuesday to Monday with a need
to amend the start time. He requested Board approval to start at 4:00 a.m., E.T. instead
of 6:00 a.m., E.T. The Board had no objection. County Administrator Hammond noted it
is in the contract that BCC Waste Solutions cannot begin before 6:00 a.m., E.T.,
requesting a vote of the Board for this change. Commissioner Farrell so moved.
Commissioner McCroan seconded the motion. Chairman Quinn called for public
comment. There being none, the motion passed unanimously.
County Administrator Hammond discussed the deadline for two F.R.D.A.P. (Florida

BUDGET
County Administrator Hammond requested the Board set the maximum millage rate
today, allowing staff to certify to the Property Appraiser for the Countywide Independent
Fire Districts, and MSTUs. He reported this is the last year for the MSTUs’ Gulf-front,
Interior and Bayside. County Administrator Hammond stated staff recommendation is to
impose a 5.9 millage rate Countywide, which is a 5% across the board reduction. He
stated that every single homestead property will receive a decrease in property tax under
this proposal. County Administrator Hammond discussed the highlights of the proposed
2024-25 budget <copy provided to the Clerk for the record> and the increase to various
fire control districts, noting all MSTUs are capped at $400,000.00. He stated the drivers
of the new money are mostly geared towards public safety and emergency services. After
discussion by County Administrator Hammond, Property Appraiser Burke appeared
before the Board to discuss an apartment complex (Gateway Manor) on the north side
being denied an exemption, noting the tax on this parcel was $53,000.00 and that is why
the County is seeing an increase on the CRA from last year. Discussion followed
regarding the increase to the library. County Administrator Hammond requested the Board
cap the millage at 5.9 for County operating, noting the Board will need to set dates for the
budget hearings. Chairman Quinn called for a motion to set that millage at 5.9.
Commissioner Rich so moved. Commissioner Farrell seconded the motion. Chairman
Quinn called for public comment. There being no public comment, the motion passed
unanimously. County Administrator Hammond then requested the Board set each Fire
Control District at .5 mill each. Chairman Quinn called for a motion to set that.

Commissioner McCroan so moved. Commissioner Husband seconded the motion.
Chairman Quinn called for public comment. There being no public comment, the motion
passed unanimously. County Administrator Hammond requested the Board set the
following MSTUs Gulfside Beachfront 0.4897, Gulfside Interior 0.4059, and Bayside
0.3981. Chairman Quinn called for a motion to set these. Commissioner Farrell so moved.
Commissioner McCroan second the motion. Chairman Quinn called for public comment.
There being none, the motion passed unanimously. Property Appraiser Burke thanked
the Board for decreasing the millage rate. Sheriff Harrison appeared before the Board
and thanked them for the increase, discussing the two new positions. He discussed an
apprentice program that will allow the Sheriff’s Office to hire someone and send them to
school to become a law enforcement officer, noting the Deputy Sheriff’s Association will
be paying for one of the positions, totaling three positions. Sheriff Harrison requested
assistance with grant funding to help fund the purchase of body cameras. He thanked the
Board for the raise to employees, noting this will raise the start-out salary for a law
enforcement officer to $51,700.00. Sheriff Harrison requested permission to roll-over any
remaining funds at the end of this budget session. Discussion followed regarding the body
cameras. Upon inquiry by Commissioner McCroan regarding the fine and forfeiture
money, County Administrator Hammond stated this is restricted money. Sheriff Harrison
noted some of these funds will be used for improvements to the Gulf County Sheriff’s
Office Building (St. Joe location), $10,000.00 went to Crime Stoppers and the remaining
is for training. He discussed the beach patrol, noting deputies will be taking the beach
items left on the beach to the St. Joesph Bay Humane Society to sell in their thrift store.
The Board had no objection. Upon inquiry by Commissioner Husband regarding assisting
the Sheriff with applying for a grant to purchase the body cameras, County Administrator
Hammond noted the easiest way is to get a direct appropriation to the Sheriff’ Office,
requesting the Sheriff have the Sheriff’s Association contact OMB Director Herring to work
out the details. He discussed his concerns regarding the stricter requirements by the
County verses if the Sheriff applied for the grant funds. OMB Director Herring stated the
matter is only for this one program. Chairman Quinn called for public comment regarding
the proposed budget. Lorinda Gingell, of the PSJ Library appeared before the Board to
request additional funding for the library, discussing her concerns regarding the cut in
grant funding this year. Upon inquiry by Chairman Quinn, Ms. Gingell reported the State
funding was cut by $26,000.00, noting all the reserves are gone. Following discussion,
Commissioner Rich stated the County is giving the library $30,000.00 more over last year,
asking what additional amount is being sought. Chairman Quinn called for any additional
public comment. There was no further public comment. County Administrator Hammond
stated staff is recommending holding the first budget hearing on September 4, 2024, at
5:01 p.m., ET. Chairman Quinn called for a motion to set that meeting for September 4,
2024, at 5:01 p.m., E.T. Commissioner McCroan so moved. Commissioner Farrell
seconded the motion. Chairman Quinn called for public comment. There being none, the
motion passed unanimously. County Administrator Hammond discussed the possible
dates for the second budget hearing. Chairman Quinn called for a motion to set that
meeting for September 16, 2024, at 5:01 p.m., E.T. Commissioner Husband so moved.
Commissioner Farrell seconded the motion. Chairman Quinn called for public comment.
There being no public comment, the motion passed unanimously. County Administrator
Hammond thanked staff, noting the Board has a good conservative balanced budget. Ms.

Gingell appeared before the Board and gave the Chairman the amount the library is
asking for. The Board told Ms. Gingell the library is getting the full requested amount.
There being no further business, and upon motion by Commissioner Farrell, seconded by
Commissioner Husband, and unanimous vote, the meeting did then adjourn at 10:00
a.m., E.T.
