NOVEMBER 26, 2024
REGULAR MEETING
PORT ST. JOE, FLORIDA
The Gulf County Board of County Commissioners met this date in regular session with
the following members present: Chairman Sandy Quinn, Jr., Vice Chairman Phil
McCroan, and Commissioners Christopher McLemore, Lloyd Jack Husband, III, and
Randy Pridgeon.
Others present were: County Attorney Jeremy Novak, Clerk Rebecca L. Norris, Deputy
Clerk Leanna Cooper, County Administrator Michael L. Hammond, County Assistant
Administrator Clay Smallwood, III, Deputy Administrator Kari Summers, Building Official
Brad Bailey, Emergency Management Director/Fire Coordinator Matt Herring, Gulf
County E.M.S. Director Jody Daniels, Gulf County Extension Service Director Ray
Bodrey, Grant Administrator Erin Kennedy, Grant Administrator Laura Beech-Armstrong,
Human Resource Director Kaci Rhodes, Office of Management & Budget Director Sherry
Herring, Mosquito Control Director Austin Horton, County Planner Doug Crane, Public
Works Director Mark Cothran, Assistant Public Works Director Raymond Hart, Sheriff
Mike Harrison, and T.D.C. Executive Director Silvia Williams.
Chairman Quinn called the meeting to order at 9:03 a.m., ET.
CONSENT AGENDA
Chairman Quinn called for public comment regarding the Consent Agenda. Chirsty
McElroy, of Port St. Joe appeared before the Board to discuss the request to purchase a
tractor from Jerry Pate Turf and Irrigation (item #11, request to purchase), in the amount
of $62,585.84, inquiring if this item went out for bid. She noted that Mr. Pate is the Vice
Chair of Northwest Florida Water Management District (N.W.F.W.M.D.) and Mr. Pate did
a sprinkler system for the County golf course last year. She stated that she would like to
make a public records request to inquire if it was bid out. County Administrator Hammond
read aloud the remaining purchase request found in the Consent Agenda on pages 72-
73, noting the purchase is a State contract type deal, going through the cooperative
program. Ms. McElroy discussed her concerns regarding N.W.F.W.M.D. issuing permits
to the County and City and for the Vice Chair (Jerry Pate, of N.W.F.W.M.D.) to be doing
business with the County and the City. Robert Branch, of Port St. Joe appeared before
the Board to inquire about pages 90-92 of the Consent, a request to N.W.F.W.M.D. to
reduce permit application fees, asking if this was for an individual or if it was for the whole
entire County. Assistant County Administrator Smallwood noted this request lowers the
application fee for every permit application the County submits, stating it does not include
the Cities, this is just for permitting of County projects. Mr. Branch discussed his concerns
regarding the language of hardship, noting the County has only been issued twelve
permits in the last seventeen years. County Administrator Hammond stated small
Counties, like Gulf County, across the State of Florida with a certain population as of

October 1, 2024, are eligible, noting it is not just N.W.F.W.M.D., it includes D.E.P.
(Department of Environmental Projection) and any other State agency that have a permit
fee. He noted this is a savings to the taxpayer by requesting this waiver. Mr. Branch
inquired if an individual would qualify for the hardship waiver for an application fee.
Following discussion, Commissioner McCroan stated he will need to abstain due to his
business relationship with Monolith (page 67). Commissioner Pridgeon requested page
67 be pulled for discussion. Chairman Quinn pulled page 67 (item 9 for a lease extension
with Monolith Construction) from the Consent Agenda for discussion. Following
discussion, Commission Husband moved to approve the Consent Agenda. Commissioner
McLemore seconded the motion. Chairman Quinn called for public comment. Upon
inquiry by Deputy Clerk Cooper, Chairman Quinn noted page 67 was placed back in the
Consent for approval. Christy McElroy, of Port St. Joe appeared before the Board to
inquire if the lease agreement (with Monolith) needs to be for three (3) years or if the
lease will expire in one year and then be renegotiated based on the market. After further
discussion, Chairman Quinn called for any additional public comment. There being no
further public comment, the motion passed 4-0, with Commissioner McCroan abstaining
due to business relations with Monolith Construction <FORM 8B on file with Clerk>, as
follows:
