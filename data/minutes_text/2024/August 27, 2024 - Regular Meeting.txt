INSURANCE – PROPERTY CAUSALITY, AUTO LIABILITY & WORKERS COMP
Dwight Van Lierop, of Acentria Insurance appeared before the Board to discuss the
upcoming insurance rates. He noted the spread sheet provided to the Board for review
<pages 5-7 of the Administrative Update and Report Packet> has incorrect information

under the general liability and the total premium for last year verses this year, reporting
there is only an 8.98% increase overall. County Administrator Hammond inquired of the
total liability. Mr. Van Lierop provided the correct amounts for liability and cybersecurity.
Discussion followed regarding workers comp rates and storm deducible options. County
Administrator Hammond and Mr. Van Lierop recommended that the Board keep the 5%
(windstorm deductible). County Administrator Hammond stated he would like to place a
caveat, capping some of the buildings insurance coverages. County Administrator
Hammond requested Board approval of this proposal, keeping the 5% windstorm
deductible. Commissioner Farrell moved to accept with the 5% cap on the deducible.
Commissioner McCroan seconded the motion. Chairman Quinn called for public
comment. There being no public comment, the motion passed unanimously.
MK RANCH UPDATE
Jon Creamer, of FWC appeared before the Board to provide the Board with some updates
to the MK Ranch Restoration Project, stating the road coming down to Brown’s Landing
will be open and will not be impacted by the improvements being made. Billy Webster, of
Ducks Unlimited appeared before the Board to discuss the grant funded project,
presenting an informational packet for the Board to review <copy provided to the Clerk
for the record>. Geoff Schafer and Shahbaz Mirza, of WSP both appeared before the
Board to discuss the provided presentation regarding the MK Ranch Restoration Project.
Upon motion by Commissioner Farrell, second by Commissioner McCroan, and
unanimous vote, the Board extended their time to speak. Mr. Mirza finished his section of
the presentation and Charlene Stroehlen discussed the design specifics regarding the
water crossings. Discussion followed regarding the material used. Mr. Mirza concluded
the presentation with the model setup. Upon motion by Commissioner McCroan, second
by Commissioner Farrell, and unanimous vote, the Board extended his time to speak. Mr.
Mirza continued to discuss the 2-D model regarding rainfall and flood stages. County
Administrator Hammond noted the County cannot compute the Sumatra gauge, because
the County goes by the Blountstown gauge. He inquired if WSP could compute the
Sumatra gauge to the Blountstown gauge. Mr. Mirza stated he does not have that
information readily available but can provide it. Commissioner Husband inquired if there
are any models of an eight (8) hour or 100-year storm water event. Mr. Mirza noted the
sever model was the 100-year, but no eight-hour event. Chairman Quinn called for public
comment. There was no public comment. County Administrator Hammond stated the
Board will need to approve this number two (#2), which is the fourth low water crossing,
noting the Board will have to execute a Memorandum of Understating with FWC. He
recommended that FWC keep up with the maintenance. Randy Pridgeon, of WindMark
appeared before the Board to request a meeting to be held in Howard Creek to discuss
this project. Mr. Creamer discussed relating the model to the Blountstown flood stages
and to look at low water crossings as a concrete structure. After further discussion, County
Administrator Hammond inquired if the Board was in support of executing an MOU with
FWC to install a second water crossing. He discussed negotiating and bringing something
back before the Board that will hold the County hold harmless and get this project moving.
The Board had no objections.

ADMINISTRATIVE UPDATE & REPORT
UPDATE REPORT – ECONOMIC DEVELOPMENT
E.D.C. Director McKnight appeared before the Board to provide an economic
development update, discussing the Eastern Shipbuilding, floating dry dock application
with Triumph, Project Safety proposal, and affordable housing. He discussed the
proposed project of building eleven affordable houses on Knowles Avenue, as County
owned property. E.D.C. Director McKnight noting four of the houses will be owned by the
County and leased to County employees as a recruitment incentive or a retention and the
other seven houses would partner with Citizen for Gulf County Recovery Team to
construct the houses. He stated seven would be sold and four houses the County would
own, noting the conditions of the seven houses: 1. three of the houses reserved
exclusively for sale to first responders, teachers, nurses, and/or County or City
employees; 2. the remaining three houses available to this group and people working and
living in Gulf County; 3. the process will be a lottery system; 4. resale prices will be deed
restricted or second mortgage; 5. resale prices will be tied to annual CPI (consumer price
index) increase, an additional amount to be determined; 6. purchaser will retain an
increasing portion each year and own the house, but the Citizen for Gulf County Recovery
Team will retain the balance of the sale price for administrative fees to be determined; 7.
reinvestment in additional houses; and 8. rental or leasing of the houses will not be
prohibited. E.D.C. Director McKnight noted the estimated price is $280,000.00, does not
include the property and the County has some downpayment assistance proposals with
Capital City Bank, Jesse DuPont and funds from S.H.I.P., making the mortgage price
$230,000.00 on the seven houses. He requested Board approval to plat the eleven lots.
After discussion, Commissioner Rich motioned to plat those eleven lots. Commissioner
Husband seconded the motion. Chairman Quinn called for public comment. Francine
Vellanti, of Cape San Blas appeared before the Board to oppose any more building,
suggesting a moratorium on all the building companies. Christy McElroy, of Port St. Joe
appeared before the Board to inquire if there will be a series of workshops to be held to
discuss this project. She discussed the needs of the Sheriff’s Office as the County grows
and her concerns regarding setting aside four houses for Gulf County workers.
Responding to Ms. McElory, E.D.C. Director McKnight discussed the County taking
property already owned and creating affordable housing, noting that Gulf County does
include the Sheriff’s Office and all entities of Gulf County. The motion passed
unanimously. E.D.C. Director McKnight requested Board approval of the seven and four
housing split of the eleven lots. After discussion by Commissioner Husband regarding his
concerns of the four houses, the Board agreed to obtain more information before voting
on this request. E.D.C. Director McKnight inquired if the Board had any objections to the
Gulf County Recovery Team being the designee and moving forward with formalizing an
agreement to be the entity that administers the number of houses to be sold. Chairman

Quinn inquired if E.D.C. Director McKnight was going to provide the Board with details to
consider. E.D.C. Director McKnight agreed. Upon inquiry by Chairman Quinn, E.D.C.
Director McKnight reported that the three ships currently at Eastern Shipbuilding will be
there for one year and more ships and tugboats are coming in, allowing for work over the
next three years.

GRANT EXTENSION – BUDDY FLOORE BRIDGE
Referencing page 2 of the packet, OMB Director Herring requested Board approval to
submit a time extension request to FL Department of Transportation for a S.C.O.P.-B
(Small County Outreach Program Bridge) grant (G2Q37) regarding Buddy Floore Bridge,
extending the grant to March 31, 2025. <Commissioner Rich left the meeting at 10:06
a.m., E.T.> Chairman Quinn called for a motion to extend. Upon inquiry by Commissioner
Farrell, Assistant County Administrator Smallwood stated D.O.T. is reviewing the design
work and out of abundance of caution the County would like to request a three-month
extension. Commissioner McCroan discuss his concerns regarding the recent sale and
the purchaser knowing the condition of the bridge, noting the County is using S.C.O.P.
funds to replace the bridge. County Administrator Hammond stated this project will not
solve the new homeowner’s problem, noting the bridge project is minor repairs. Assistant
County Administrator Smallwood noted this bridge was on the County’s list for repairs
before this recent purchase. Commissioner Farrell moved to approve. <Commissioner
Rich returned to the meeting at 10:09 a.m., E.T.> After discussion, Commissioner Rich
seconded the motion. Chairman Quinn called for public comment. Christy McElroy, of Port
St. Joe appeared before the Board to request that the Board wait until the new
Commission is seated before voting, discussing her concerns regarding Commissioner
Rich not being in the room while this matter was being discussed. Robert Branch, of Port
St. Joe appeared before the Board to discuss his concerns regarding this project and
Commissioner Rich leaving the room. Commissioner Rich noted he left to go to the
restroom. Francine Vellanti, of Cape San Blas appeared before the Board to request the
Board stop all the projects until the new Commission is seated and requested that the
Board replace County Administrator Hammond. She discussed her concerns regarding
the Board approving the development (recent purchase) and the County repairing the
bridge. County Administrator Hammond noted that the Buddy Floore Bridge has been on
the deficiency list for ten years, stating that since Hurricane Michael this bridge has been
under water at least three times and is structurally deficient and dangerous. He stated
that this is a county bridge, there are other residents that reside in the area, the County

has the means to use grant funding to repair the bridge, and this bridge project has
