P.D.R.B. RECOMMENDATIONS
VARIANCE REQUEST – GABY MAALOUF
Planner Crane presented a variance request from Gaby Mallouf, Parcel ID #06378-100R,
requesting a variance of the Gulf County LDR (Land Development Regulations) of Article
V, Section 5.01.03 building setback requirements to allow a zero-lot line setback on the
right side of the property for construction of a new home. He stated the P.D.R.B. had a
recommendation, by 4-0 vote, to approve this variance request, contingent upon the
applicant changing to a six-foot (6’) variance leaving a 3.5-foot right side setback.
Chairman Quinn called anyone wishing to speak on this issue to step forward. No one
stepped forward from the audience. Planner Crane reported that the adjacent lot is a HOA
lot that contains a few septic tanks and is a non-buildable lot. After discussion by members
of the Board, Chairman Quinn called for a motion to waive the Quasi-Judicial Hearing.
Commissioner McCroan so moved. Commissioner Husband seconded the motion.
Chairman Quinn called for public comment. There being none, the motion passed
unanimously. Upon inquiry by Chairman Quinn, Planner Crane stated the P.D.R.B.
recommend to the Board, by 4-0 vote, to approve this variance with the new change by
the applicant. He noted when granting a variance there are four options, reporting the
P.D.R.B. went with option one, for hardship and special conditions. Planner Crane
requested the Board include in the motion which option is their reason for granting this

variance. Commissioner McCroan motioned to approve. Commissioner Husband
seconded the motion. Chairman Quinn inquired if Commissioner McCroan had any
objections to including reasoning option one (hardship and special conditions).
Commissioner McCroan had no objection. Chairman Quinn then called for public
comment. There being no public comment, the motion passed unanimously.
NON-RESIDENTIAL DEVELOPMENT – CREEKSIDE PARTNERS, LLC
Planner Crane then presented a non-residential development from Creekside Partners,
LLC/BJ Heard, Parcel ID #03358-000R & #03364-000R, proposing a 220 lot RV
(recreational vehicle) park to include a clubhouse, pool, RV Storage area, bathhouses,
roadway, parking and other recreational facilities. Chairman Quinn called anyone wishing
to speak on this issue, to step forward. Tyler Marsh, of Southeastern Engineer Consulting,
appeared before the Board to speak on this project. Chairman Quinn inquired if Mr. Marsh
had any objection to the Board waiving Quasi-Judicial Hearing. Upon no objection by Mr.
Marsh, Chairman Quinn called for a motion to waive. Commissioner McCroan so moved.
Commissioner McLemore seconded the motion. Chairman Quinn called for public
comment. There being none, the motion passed unanimously. Mr. Marsh, representative
of the property owner, discussed the proposed development project, noting the park will
be self-contained regarding water and sewer. He then requested Board approval of the
development order for this RV park. Planner Crane stated the recommendation of the
P.D.R.B. to the Board is, by 4-0 vote, to approve this non-residential development,
contingent upon reaching an agreement with the Board on the replacement of the
roadway and bridge leading into this development. He noted this is Buddy Floore Road
with a one-lane wooden bridge. Commissioner Pridgeon discussed today’s vote is to
allow the developer to move forward with planning and permitting, inquiring if the
developer will bring back a plan of how the road and bridge will look making sure it meets
County’s requirements. Mr. Marsh confirmed, noting they are in the process of obtaining
survey data before designing and obtaining permits. Commissioner McLemore inquired if
the plan was for the property owner to pay for the road and bridge or if the County is going
to pay for the road and bridge. Mr. Marsh stated that the developer wants to asphalt the
entire length of the Buddy Floore Road, from Overstreet down to the development site,
noting the bridge is something that will have to be discussed with the County. Discussion
followed regarding it being a County road and bridge. Commissioner McCroan discussed
his concerns regarding the County paying for paving the road and bridge work. County
Administrator Hammond stated he agrees with the recommendation of the P.D.R.B. to
approve, contingent upon the developer paying for the road and bridge and working that
out with the County. Discussion followed regarding the development order. County
Administrator Hammond suggested if the Board moves forward with the conditional
approval, then the County engineer determines what the cost will be, the developer will
provide the funds to the County and the County will build the road and bridge.
Commissioner McCroan had no objections. Building Official Bailey inquired if the County
will be issuing building permits during this time. County Administrator Hammond stated
no building permits until there is an agreement regarding the cost and the funds are in
escrow. Chairman Quinn requested Planner Crane to state again the recommendation of
the P.D.R.B. Planner Crane stated the P.D.R.B. recommended, by 4-0 vote, to approve
the non-residential development, contingent upon reaching an agreement with the Board
on the replacement of the roadway and bridge. Commissioner Pridgeon moved to

approve. Commissioner McLemore seconded the motion. Commissioner McCroan
inquired if the contingency was added into his motion. Commissioner Pridgeon agreed to
add the stipulations. Commissioner McLemore noted his second stands. County
Administrator Hammond inquired when the Board would like for this matter to come back
before the Board. Chairman Quinn stated as soon as the developer has it ready to go.
County Administrator Hammond asked if the Board is directing him to negotiate with the
developer, come up with a number that the County engineer agrees with, and bring a final
packet to the Board for consideration. Members of the Board agreed with this direction.
Chairman Quinn then called for public comment. Robert Branch, of Port St. Joe appeared
before the Board to discuss his concerns regarding this development, inquiry if this site
will be an RV park or would it be residential. Planner Crane reported this development is
a commercial RV park, noting the property owner came before the Board to request the
change from residential to mixed commercial/residential. Chairman Quinn called for
public comment. Chirsty McElory, of Port St. Joe appeared before the Board to discuss
her concerns regarding the area, noting the property is near a creek and the possibility of
lifting the bridge. She discussed an environmental study and inquired when the public
might view this study. Chairman Quinn called for any additional public comment. Planner
Crane discussed the role of the Planning and Building Department before an application
is presented to the Board for consideration, noting records can be obtained through a
public records request or viewable in the Planning Department. Chairman Quinn again
calling for public comment. There being no further public comment, the motion passed 4-
0, with Commissioner Husband abstaining due to business relations on this project
<FORM 8B on file with Clerk>.

S.H.I.P. L.H.A.P. POLICY CHANGE
S.H.I.P. Director Lanier appeared before the Board to discuss page 4 of the packet,
requesting Board approval to make a policy change to the L.H.A.P. (Local Housing
Assistance Plan), noting this change will be for plan years SFY2021-22 through
SFY2026-27. She requested to change the amount of funding, changing the maximum
amount from $40,000.00 to $60,000.00 regarding owner occupied rehabilitation,
demolition/reconstruction and disaster mitigation and recovery. Discussion followed
regarding the delay in spending. Commissioner Husband discussed allowing past
applications resubmit for assistance rather than raise the rate. OMB Director Herring
requested the Board allow staff to draft a recommendation to present to the Board if the
Board is considering amending the L.H.A.P. to allow repeat applicants. Members of the
Board discussed tabling this matter until January to allow staff time to bring a
recommendation for consideration. Discussion followed regarding an applicant that would
currently benefit from the policy change (Bid #2425-02 * Eunice Manuel). Commissioner
Pridgeon inquired if the Board could grant a one-time waiver for this applicant (Eunice
Manuel), County Attorney Novak discussed setting a precedent, recommending the
Board make a comprehensive change to the policy. Discussion followed regarding the bid
(#2425-02). Members of the Board discussed tabling the proposed policy change and the
request to modify Bid #2425-02 until January. Commissioner Husband made a motion to
table pages 4 and 5, holding a workshop with staff presenting information to identify
possible issues and come up with recommendations for the Board to consider. Upon
noting the Board cannot vote at a workshop, Chairman Quinn inquired if Commissioner

Husband would mend his motion to table and bring it back at a special meeting.
Commissioner Husband agreed to amend his motion. Commissioner McCroan seconded
the motion for discussion. After discussion, Chairman Quinn called for public comment.
There being no public comment, the motion passed unanimously.

ON MOTON of Commissioner McCroan, seconded by
Commissioner Husband, the following Resolution was adopted;
WHEREAS, the State of Florida Department of Transportation
proposes to construct or improve County Road No. 30B, Financial Project
No. 4411851, in Gulf County, Florida; and

WHEREAS, it is necessary that certain lands now owned by Gulf
County be used temporarily by the State of Florida Department of
Transportation; and
WHEREAS, said use is in the best interest of the County; and
WHEREAS, the State of Florida Department of Transportation has
made application to said County to execute and deliver to the State of

NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED by the Board of County
Commissioners of Gulf County, that the application of the State of Florida
Department of Transportation for a temporary easement, or easements, is

of the State of Florida Department of Transportation, in Gulf County, should
be drawn and executed by this Board of County Commissioners.

BE IT FURTHER RESOLVED that a certified copy of the Resolution
be forwarded forthwith to the State of Florida Department of Transportation
at 1074 Highway 90, Chipley, Florida 32428;

Resolution adopted by the Board of County Commissioners of Gulf County,
Florida at a meeting held on the 17th day of December 2024.
