County Administrator Hammond discussed his concerns regarding the need for

SUNRISE/SUNSET SEAWALL – LOCAL EMERGENCY DECLARATION REQUEST
Jeremy Anderson, Attorney for Sunrise/Sunset Homeowners Association appeared
before the Board to provide material for review <copy provided to the Clerk for the
record>, noting this property consists of ten residential units protected by a seawall. He
reported the wave action from Hurricane Helene caused failure of the wall, directing the
Board members to review the provided photos of the breached seawall. Attorney
Anderson discussed the residents receiving notice from the Building Department (Code
Enforcement) to remove the seawall. He discussed this seawall type being similar to other
walls in the area, requesting Board approval to continue to have a seawall. Upon motion

by Commissioner McCroan, second by Commissioner Farrell, and unanimous vote, the
Board extended Attorney Anderson’s time to speak. Attorney Anderson reported D.E.P.
will permit a seawall in accordance with their specifications, but the HOA (Sunrise/Sunset)
needs approval from the Board to reinstall the seawall. He discussed policy 1.9 under the
Comprehensive Plan if deemed an emergency that the Board could grant an emergency
approval to allow for the installation. Discussion followed regarding past requests
regarding this seawall. Commissioner McCroan inquired of the seawall mentioned by
Attorney Anderson, requesting clarification from Building Official Bailey. Discussion
followed that those walls Attorney Anderson referenced were not installed below sea level.
Commissioner McCroan discussed his concerns regarding the erosion to the north of
Sunrise/Sunset over the past few years. County Administrator Hammond stated the
County has never permitted a seawall at Cape San Blas, noting the County has done
some zeros (sea-levels). He stated the difference between the wall in 2002 and the last
wall installed was not at zero, noting he does not think the Board will find where the
County permitted the last wall installed. Building Official Bailey provided aerial photos to
the Board for review <copy provided to the Clerk for the record>. County Administrator
Hammond discussed the presented photos, noting Sunrise/Sunset is the furthest house
that sticks out along the coastline. He discussed other options, encouraging the Board to
consider a variance to allow the homeowners to move the units closer to the road and get
in-line with the other houses. County Administrator Hammond noted to allow another
seawall to be installed below zero sea level is going to cause problems for either neighbor.
He stated, from his knowledge, in the last 32 years the County has never allowed a below
sea level seawall on Cape San Blas. County Administrator Hammond noted it is against
the County’s rule and not in the best interest to the neighboring property on either side,
the road, or the County’s infrastructure/water line, restating there are other options.
Commissioner Husband reported this property is in the thirty-year erosion projection line
of D.E.P. construction control line, noting in his opinion the process has not been followed
appropriately: 1. Attorney Anderson referred to policy 1.9.1 and 1.9.2, which does allow
for a caveat that does allow for certain situations, however in 1.9.2 the coastal protection
permitting is due to the technical expertise of the judicial agencies; 2. there is no permit
for this seawall and the two previous permits are after-the-fact permits. He discussed his
concerns regarding the amount of erosion north of this wall that is impacting the
neighboring adjacent property owners. Commissioner Husband noted the most recently
permit is dated December 7, 2011 and there is no permit for 2002. Discussion followed
regarding the comments from D.E.P. noted on the 2011 permit. Commissioner Husband
discussed his concerns regarding setting a precedent in bringing in seawalls, stating he
is not in support. Attorney Anderson reported D.E.P. will not issue a permit until the Board
submits approval, stating a letter from the Board will have to be submitted to obtain the
D.E.P. permit. Discussion followed regarding the D.E.P. permitting process. Attorney
Anderson inquired if the Board would agree to no action be taken on proceeding forward
with code enforcement seeking removal, asking for the Board to grant a local emergency
order to allow the property owners to do anything that is permitted by D.E.P. to secure the
wall. Following discussion, County Attorney Novak noted the questions by Attorney
Anderson are: 1. is the County going to force the property owners to remove it; and 2.
can the property owners take emergency repairs to preserve it. He discussed concerns
regarding the County exasperating the problem to the north and that neighboring property

owners have now requested public records regarding this matter. County Attorney Novak
stated the adjacent property owners should be placed on notice with the opportunity to
attend before the Board. Commissioner Husband inquired why Sunrise/Sunset could not
temporarily replace the portion of wall that had been destroyed, going to elevation zero in
the interim as a temporary repair, noting this action would be legal and can be done
without Board approval. After discussion by Attorney Anderson, Commissioner Husband
noted it would be temporary. Attorney Anderson requested that the Board retrack the
variance application and provide a refund. Commissioner McCroan and County
Administrator Hammond had no objection to the refund request. County Administrator
Hammond noted the property owners may still need a variance, stating the wall is not
protecting but damaging neighboring properties and the County’s infrastructure. He stated
if the Board is going to provide relief, then the Board will need to vote on it, noting staff
will never recommend that the wall go below zero. Attorney Anderson revised his request
to not refund the variance application fee but to table until the November meeting.
Commissioner Husband stated he has no objection to refunding the variance application
fee and allowing the homeowners (Sunrise/Sunset) to reapply at the $10,000.00 price.
Commissioner McCroan had no objection. Chairman Quinn inquired if the Board needed
to make a motion to refund the money. Commissioner McCroan so moved. Chairman
Quinn called the motion to refund them their money. Commissioner Husband seconded
the motion. Chairman Quinn called for public comment. There being none, the motion
passed unanimously. Following discussion by Commissioner McCroan regarding the
County having a policy in place, County Administrator Hammond stated if the Board does
not want staff to proceed with tearing down the wall, then the Board will need to grant the

ROAD ABANDONMENT – RISH HARBOR ROAD
James Rish, of Wewahitchka then moved on to discuss roads in Iola, providing the Board
members with a copy of an aerial map of the location <copy provided to the Clerk for the
record>. He discussed his concerns regarding no markers posted for emergency
vehicles, requesting the Board ask the new landowners to post new numbers or signs.
Mr. Rish then discussed an area on the map called Rish Pasture and the dirt road that is
at the northwest corner that leads to the entrance of Rish Pasture. He also discussed the

County using Rish Harbor Road as an alternate route during seasons of flooding as this
road is on higher ground. Upon motion by Commissioner Farrell, second by
Commissioner McCroan, and unanimous vote, the Board extended Mr. Rish’s time to
speak. Mr. Rish requested that the Board either abandon the road, 265 feet into the county
road, or grade it. County Administrator Hammond stated the timber company
(Apalachicola River Timberlands/Teal) thinks they own it when it was transferred from
Neal Land and Timber, noting he does not believe the County owns it but has no objection
to the County abandoning any interest the County may have in it. He noted Mr. Rish may
want to speak to someone regarding gaining access to this property being that his parcel
is completely surrounded by the timber company. Mr. Rish noted this road has always
been a county road. County Administrator Hammond stated Neal Land and Timber
transferred the road (Rish Harbor Road) as if they owned it, and during his tenure, the
County has not maintained this road. He reported to the Board that staff does not have
any objection of the Board abandoning any interest the County has, noting staff can
advertise the abandonment at the next meeting. County Attorney Novak discussed the
abandonment policy and process, noting if the Board votes, then staff will start the
process to advertise, go through the resolution, waive the fee, and go through the
advertisement process. Commissioner Rich motioned to approve to advertise to abandon
this for Mr. Rish, as requested. Commissioner Husband seconded the motion for
discussion. After discussion, County Attorney Novak discussed needing a vote of the
Board to suspend the procedure/policy which would waive all the additional requirements
that goes through the Clerk’s Office, noting once the policy is suspended, then staff will
move forward and there will still be a Public Hearing for public comment. Commissioner
Rich inquired if Mr. Rish has guaranteed deeded access to his property. Mr. Rish stated
no he does not. Following discussion, Chairman Quinn inquired if Commissioner Rich
wanted to add to his motion the recommendation by County Attorney Novak. County
Attorney Novak noted the motion is to suspend the County’s right-of-way abandonment
policy, expedite it, waive the fees, and move forward to hold a Public Hearing and the
County will release any interest and as public records it will be in the meeting minutes
and a resolution will be presented at the November 26, 2024 meeting. After discussion,
the Chairman Quinn called for any additional public comment. There being no further
public comment, the motion passed unanimously.
