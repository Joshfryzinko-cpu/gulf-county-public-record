SEPTEMBER 4, 2024
BUDGET HEARING
PORT ST. JOE, FLORIDA
The Gulf County Board of County Commissioners met this date in special budget session
with the following members present: Chairman Sandy Quinn, Jr., Vice Chairman Phil
McCroan, and Commissioners David C. Rich, Jack Husband, and Patrick Farrell.
Others present were: County Attorney Jeremy Novak, Clerk Rebecca L. Norris, Director
of Finance & Management Leo Bebeau, Deputy Clerk Leanna Cooper, County
Administrator Michael L. Hammond, County Assistant Administrator Clay Smallwood, III,
Deputy Administrator Kari Summers, Emergency Management Director/Fire Coordinator
Matt Herring, Gulf County E.M.S. Director Jody Daniels, G.I.S. Coordinator Clay Kennedy,
Grant Administrator Erin Kennedy, Grant Administrator Laura Beach-Armstrong, Human
Resource Director Kaci Rhodes, Office of Management & Budget Director Sherry
Herring, Mosquito Control Director Austin Horton, Property Appraiser Mitch Burke,
Property Appraiser IT Tech Dennis Barfield, Jr., Public Works Director Mark Cothran,
Sheriff Mike Harrison, and T.D.C. Executive Director Silvia Williams.
Chairman Quinn called the meeting to order at 5:01 p.m., E.T.
TENTATIVE 2024-25 OVERVIEW
Chairman Quinn noted that this is the first public hearing. County Administrator Hammond
provided a handout to the Board for review <copy provided to the Clerk for the record>,
noting this is the fourth year the Board has cut ad valorem taxes. He cautioned the Board,
barring another double-digit year, that cutting taxes and funding everyone’s request is
coming to a halt. Discussion followed regarding continuing to cut taxes and growth.
County Administrator Hammond reported the proposed aggregate millage rate is 5.69%
over the roll-back rate, the total levy this year is $22,503,000.00 and this is the last year
for M.S.T.U.s. He noted receiving calls after the TRIM notices were mailed regarding the
difference between the County levy and the School Board levy, that there are different
caps on the County that the School Board does not have. County Administrator Hamond
stated the key increases are due to E.M.S, Sheriff, Constitutional Officers, Insurance, Jail,
Firefighter Incentives, County Park Renovations, City of Port St. Joe Summer Program,
Downtown Redevelopment, and Library Operations. OMB Director Herring reported the
proposed aggregate millage rate is 6.2850 that is a 5.69% above the current aggregate
rolled back rate of 5.9466. She noted the aggregate rate is made up of the operating
millage Countywide of 5.9, each Dependent Fire District of .50, and the proposed
M.S.T.U. Voted Debt Service is Beachfront at 0.4897, Gulfside Interior is 0.4059 and
Bayside at 0.3981. OMB Director Herring then stated the millage rates are at the
maximum, noting they can decrease but cannot increase (the millage rate) at tonight’s
hearing. She repeated the reasons for Ad Valorem increases are related to: Sheriff’s
Office, Constitutional Officer, E.M.S., Insurance Cost, Jail, Firefighter Incentives, Park

Renovations, City of Port St. Joe Summer Program, Downtown Redevelopment, Library,
and Pay Raises for Employees. OMB Director noted each Board member has been
provided a budget book and it was posted in advance on the County’s website as required
by TRIM. She requested Board approval for the ability to make line-item adjustments as
long as the fund total does not change and does not affect Ad Valorem. Chairman Quinn
called for a motion to allow OMB Director Herring to make those changes. Commissioner
Farrell so moved. Commissioner Husband seconded the motion. Chairman Quinn called
for public comment. There being no public comment. The motion passed unanimously.
OMB Director Herring discussed a funding request from the City of Wewahitchka, noting
the initial request was for $95,000.00 which included $10,000.00 for fireworks, but now
requests an additional $20,000.00 to provide a total of $30,000.00 for fireworks. Following
discussion, Commissioner Husband motioned to give the City of Wewahitchka an
additional $10,000.00 from the T.D.C. funds for the 4th of July Celebration. Commissioner
Rich seconded the motion. Chairman Quinn called for public comment. There being none,
the motion passed unanimously. County Administrator Hammond recommended to take
the funds from Infrastructure, reducing it by $10,000.00 and increase T.D.C. by
$10,000.00. Commissioner McCroan so moved. Commissioner Husband seconded the
motion. Chairman Quinn called for public comment. There being no public comment, the
motion passed unanimously.
CLERK OF COURT
Clerk Norris thanked the Board for a great budget session and for approving the funding
requests.
SHERIFF’S OFFICE
Sheriff Harrison discussed his pay-out budget, noting he is expecting at least four officer
to retire this budget season. He requested any remaining funds be rolled over to the next
fiscal year. Sheriff Harrison discussed his concerns regarding the need for a new beach
vehicle, noting that one is ordered but will not be delivered until December 2024,
requesting Board approval to roll the balance of this fund over to next year.
COMMISSIONERS
Chairman Quinn inquired if members of the Board were good with rolling over their parks’
money, requesting his park’s money be rolled over into next year’s budget. County
Administrator Hammond discussed tracking the final balance and bringing a
recommendation before the Board at the final budget meeting. OMB Director Herring
discussed drafting a budget amendment once the final numbers are in. Members of the
Board had no objection.
PUBLIC COMMENT
Chairman Quinn called for public comment regarding any questions or concerns in
reference to the proposed budget. There being no public comment, Chairman Quinn then
closed the Public Hearing to the public.

FINAL BUDGET HEARING – SEPTEMBER 16, 2025
OMB Director Herring noted the Final Budget Hearing date was originally set for
September 16, 2024, at 5:01 p.m., E.T., asking if the Board was in agreement with this
date. The Board had no objection. OMB Director Herring noted the hearing notice will be
advertised in the local newspaper. Chairman Quinn called for a motion to set that date.
Commissioner Farrell so moved. Commissioner Husband seconded the motion.
Chairman Quinn called for public comment. There being none, the motion passed
unanimously.
