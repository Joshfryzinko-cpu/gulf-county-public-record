to the Flag.
TROPICAL STORM HELENE
Chairman Quinn noted the meeting time changed due to the change of the storm
<meeting was originally scheduled for noon>. County Administrator Hammond stated
overnight the storm has continually tracked towards Gulf County and now is projected to
hit near Carrabelle, discussing his concerns regarding wind and the amount of rainfall
predicted for Gulf County. Emergency Management Director Herring appeared before the
Board to report that as of yesterday afternoon, Gulf County was placed under a Hurricane
Warning and the latest forecast is predicting Tropical Storm Helene to intensify and make
landfall around Carrabelle. He stated Gulf County will start experiencing tropical storm
force winds tomorrow morning around 8:00 a.m., ET, noting the storm surge is expected
to be 5-10’ (foot) at Indian Pass and 1-3’ (foot) at Cape San Blas, St. Joe Beach and St.
Joseph Bay with 6-8” (inches) of rain and 12” (inches) in other areas. County
Administrator Hammond discussed his concerns regarding the amount of rainfall and
potential drainage issues. He recommended to the Board a mandatory evacuation of the
nursing home (Gulf Shores Care Center), mandatory evacuation at Cape San Blas, Indian
Pass, Simmons Bayou and Oak Grove, a mandatory evacuation of all RV (recreational
vehicles) Parks south of the Intercoastal Waterway that was not already included (in
previous mandatory evacuation), voluntary evacuation Countywide of low-lying areas and
mobile homes, and to close the Gulf County Courthouse at noon today, remaining closed

Thursday and Friday. He added that the shelter will open at 8:00 a.m., E.T. tomorrow at
Honeyville Community Center, noting the shelter is a last resort for those that cannot go
anywhere else. Chairman Quinn noted for the record that Commissioner Rich is
appearing by telephone. Following discussion, Chairman Quinn called for public
comment. Port St. Joe City Mayor Buzzett appeared before the Board to discuss calling
a meeting to declare a local state of emergency, noting City staff is monitoring the storm.
Clerk Norris stated the Courts will be closing the entire 14th Judicial Circuit at noon today
and will remain closed through Friday. Upon inquiry by Clerk Norris regarding the noon
scheduled meeting, County Administrator Hammond noted he will suggest the Board
recess this meeting and not meet at noon. Chairman Quinn called for a motion to move
forward with those recommendations. Commissioner McCroan so moved. Commissioner
Farrell seconded the motion. Chairman Quinn called for public comment. There being no
public comment, the motion passed unanimously. County Administrator Hammond then
reported that the north Cape will be monitored closely, and the water (utility) will be turned
off, noting there is enough water at the pump station for emergency purposes only.
Discussion followed regarding people evacuating the Cape area due to having no water,
sewer and possibly no power. Upon inquiry by Commissioner Husband regarding placing
an ambulance at the Stumphole, County Administrator Hammond stated no, noting the
ambulances will be moved to White City. E.M.S. Director Daniels stated additional
ambulances will be staffed at the north and south end of the County, however services
will be suspended once winds reach 45 mph. Discussion followed regarding rainfall in the
Bay, the debris company on standby and provisions after the storm. County Administrator
Hammond requested the Board recess this meeting and not meet at 12:00 p.m., E.T.
Chairman Quinn called for public comment. Austin Byrd appeared before the Board to
inquire about the process of those that are evacuated and how to get back into the County.
County Administrator Hammond noted the County wants the residents to return as soon
as possible but it depends on working water, sewer, power and if it is safe to the public.
Sheriff's Department Captain Buchanan appeared before the Board to remind returning
residents to have their Florida identification card available to show proof or documentation
of ownership along with a government issued ID.
The meeting did then recess at 8:50 a.m., E.T.
The meeting did reconvene at 5:46 p.m., E.T.
The following Board members were present: Chairman Sandy Quinn, Jr., Vice Chairman
Phil McCroan, and Commissioners Jack Husband Patrick Farrell. Commissioner David

Emergency Management Director Herring appeared before the Board to provide a status
update regarding Hurricane Helene, noting it is predicted to be a category 4 major
hurricane by noon tomorrow with landfall around Eastpoint, Franklin County. He reported
a 6’ (foot) storm surge is predicted at the Cape, St. Joe Beach and St. Joseph Bay, and
12’ (foot) plus for Indian Pass with 8-12” (inches) of rain on the south end of the County
and possibility 16” (inches) of rain at the north end of the County. County Administrator
Hammond recommended, out of abundance of caution, a mandatory evacuation for
Highland View, St. Joe Beach and Beacon Hill to the County line and mandatory
evacuation up to Garrison Avenue in the City of Port St. Joe. City of Port St. Joe Mayor
Buzzett and City Manager Anderson appeared before the Board to report that the City is
in agreement with the mandatory evacuation. City Manager Anderson stated, out of
abundance of caution, he would recommend the City of Port St. Joe follow suit. Chairman
Quinn called for a motion for a mandatory evacuation for all of St. Joe Beach, City of Port
St. Joe up to Garrison Avenue, Highland View, Beacon Hill to the County line. County
Administrator Hammond noted this would mean everything on the coast: WindMark,
Highland View, WindMark Phase II, Beacon Hill, Gulf Aire, all the way to the Bay County
line and City of Port St. Joe up to Garrison Avenue. Upon inquiry, County Administrator
Hammond stated the County is not recommending Jones Homestead due to its location,
noting it is voluntary. After discussion, Chairman Quinn discussed adding mandatory
evacuation of all mobile homes in Jones Homestead. Commissioner McCroan agreed.
Discussion followed regarding making voluntary evacuation for mobile homes. Chairman
Quinn called for a motion. Commissioner Farrell so moved. Commissioner Husband
seconded the motion. Chairman Quinn called for public comment. There being none, the
motion passed unanimously. County Administrator Hammond stated he gave Danny
Collins, with Duke Energy permission to stage trucks at Veterans’ Memorial Beacon Hill
Park, noting there will be some utility vehicles at the school.
The meeting did then recess at 5:54 p.m., E.T.
