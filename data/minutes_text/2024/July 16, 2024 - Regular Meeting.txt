COUNTY INSURANCE – LIFE/HEALTH/DENTAL
As referenced on pages 1-2 of the Administrative Update & Report Packet <copy provided
to the Clerk for the record>, Reid Torgersen, of Torgersen Causey appeared before the
Board to discuss the renewal of health benefits and recommendations, reporting open
enrollment is October 1, 2024. He stated the health insurance premium for the current
plan will increase by approximately 18%, recommending the Board offer two HSA (Health
Savings Account) plans to County employees. Mr. Torgersen reported that United
Healthcare has approved a 15-month contract, meaning the next renewal will be January
1, 2026. He also recommended the Board contribute an additional $10.00 per month
towards HSA (health savings account) with a maximum match of $115.00.
Mr. Torgersen reported the Combined Life Dental renewal will increase by 11.3%, which
calculates approximately $2.50 per employee a month for the employee only rate.
Mr. Torgersen then stated the increase to United Healthcare Life Insurance is 14.2%,
which is $80.00 per month for the entire County, noting there is no change to the voluntary
life insurance.
Mr. Torgersen noted the voluntary products have no change to rates or benefits.
Mr. Torgersen then provided detailed information to the Board regarding the two proposed
HSA health plans. Upon motion by Commissioner McCroan, seconded by Commissioner
Farrell, and unanimous vote, the Board extended Mr. Torgersen time to speak. Mr.
Torgersen recommended the Board contribute 100% of the employee rate of the new
alternate plan. Discussion followed regarding the new 15-month contract. County
Administrator Hammond discussed the dental rate increase, noting it is not unreasonable.
County Consultant Lowry appeared before the Board to request Board approval on the
provided recommendations. County Administrator Hammond stated staff is
recommending the Board move forward today, and if moving forward, then staff will
announce the new opening enrollment dates. Commissioner Farrell motioned to approve.
Commissioner Husband seconded the motion. Chairman Quinn called for public

comment. There being no public comment, the motion passed unanimously. County
Consultant Lowry directed Human Resource Director Rhodes to announce the opening
enrollment dates, reporting staff will meet with the Constitutional Officers to discuss the
health insurance changes. Human Resource Director Rhodes reported the dates for
opening enrollment are August 1-15, 2024, noting Mr. Torgersen will be present August
6-7, 2024.
AMEND ORDINANCE 2005-13 – GARBAGE RECEPTACLE TOURIST CORRIDOR
Code Enforcement Officer Knowles appeared before the Board to discuss the current
Ordinance No. 2005-13 regarding trash receptacles in the tourist corridor, reporting that
the County is having issues with excessive trash, creating safety, health and nuisance
issues. He discussed amending the Ordinance by increasing the fees and including
additional language for enforcement. Code Enforcement Officer Knowles recommended
the Board increase the fine to a minimum of $100.00 for the first offence, $250.00 for the
second offence and a third offense would go before Special Magistrate. He noted an
additional recommendation would be to place the house number on the trash cans, stating
there needs to be no trash left on the ground as BCC Waste Solution will not pick it up.
Code Enforcement Officer Knowles suggested the Board make it mandatory for rentals
to maintain a minimum of two trash cans and after every four bedrooms there be an
additional trash can requirement. Commissioner Farrell stated he is in support of the
additional trash cans, discussing concerns regarding houses on the Cape and St. Joe
Beach that are eight to ten bedrooms. Commissioner McCroan discussed his concerns
and noted his support of amending the current Ordinance. After further discussion, Code
Enforcement Officer Knowles also suggested to the Board to consider, for larger rental
companies, to obtain a front loader dumpster and the rental company collect the trash
from the trash cans and dispose in the dumpster for BCC Waste Solutions to pick up. He
reported BCC Waste Solutions is willing to change the trash pickup day to Monday at St.
Joe Beach along the tourist corridor. County Administrator Hammond stated the Board
will need to set the first and second fee and to send to Special Magistrate after the third
offence, which is a staff recommendation, noting the Board will need to decide if this will
process as an emergency ordinance or need to go through the formal procedure.
Commissioner Farrell motioned to go with the recommendation of $100.00, $250.00, and
Special Magistrate and go ahead and do it as an emergency ordinance. Upon inquiry by
Commissioner McCroan, Commissioner Farrell clarified his motion to be $100.00,
$250.00 and after that go to the Special Magistrate. Commissioner McCroan seconded
the motion for discussion. Following discussion, members of the Board agreed to table
this matter until staff can meet with BCC Waste Solutions. Commissioner Farrell withdrew
his motion. County Administrator Hammond discussed drafting the language regarding
the penalty fee and fill in the timeline of enforcement after speaking with BCC Waste
Solutions. Commissioner Rich inquired if the draft language would include the number of
trash cans to number of bedrooms. County Administrator Hammond said yes. Chairman
Quinn called for a motion to table. Commissioner Farrell made the motion. Commissioner
McCroan seconded the motion. Chairman Quinn called for public comment. Sarah
Hodgson, St. Joe Beach appeared before the Board to request the Board to consider in
addition to the fees that an additional trash can be added after every offense that trash is

left on the ground. Chairman Quinn called for public comment. There being no further
public comment, the motion passed unanimously.
