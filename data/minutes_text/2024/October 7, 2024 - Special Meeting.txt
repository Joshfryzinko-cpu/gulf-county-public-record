OCTOBER 7, 2024
SPECIAL MEETING
PORT ST. JOE, FLORIDA
The Gulf County Board of County Commissioners met this date in special session with

Commissioners David C. Rich, Jack Husband, and Patrick Farrell.
Others present were: County Attorney Jeremy Novak, Director of Finance & Management
Leo Bebeau, Deputy Clerk Leanna Cooper, County Administrator Michael L. Hammond,
County Assistant Administrator Clay Smallwood, III, Deputy Administrator Kari Summers,
Building Official Brad Bailey, Emergency Management Director/Fire Coordinator Matt
Herring, Emergency Management Staff Assistant Time Wood, Grant Administrator Erin
Kennedy, Human Resource Director Kaci Rhodes, Office of Management & Budget
Director Sherry Herring, Mosquito Control Director Austin Horton, County Planner Doug
Crane, Public Works Director Mark Cothran, Assistant Public Works Director Raymond
Hart, Sheriff Mike Harrison, and T.D.C. Executive Director Silvia Williams.
Chairman Quinn called the meeting to order at 2:15 p.m., E.T.
Chairman Quinn then led in the Pledge of Allegiance to the Flag.
ADMINISTRATIVE UPDATE & REPORT
STATUS REPORT – STORM
Emergency Management Director Herring appeared before the Board to provide an
update regarding Hurricane Milton, noting this storm is a Category 5 hurricane and
forecasted to make landfall in the Tampa, Florida area Wednesday evening. He stated
Gulf County could start experiencing tropical force winds Wednesday morning and a 3’
(foot) storm surge for Indian Pass is expected, reporting staff will keep tracking this storm.
County Administrator Hammond discussed the minor damages from Hurricane Helene at
the Stumphole and Indian Pass. Assistant Public Works Director Hart discussed working
through some issues with several of the new generators throughout the County facilities.
County Administrator Hammond discussed getting the generators back online, noting
White City Fire Department is the E.M.S. station on the South end during hurricanes.
Upon inquiry by County Administrator Hammond regarding the total numbers for F.E.M.A.
reimbursement, Emergency Management Staff Assistant Wood stated he will not have
the final numbers until after all the damage assessments are submitted, noting F.E.M.A.
has declared Gulf County therefore, Gulf County is eligible for reimbursements.
Discussion followed regarding the generator at White City Fire Department.

PURCHASE TANKER FIRE TRUCK – SOUTH GULF FIRE DEPARTMENT
County Administrator Hammond directed the Board to the Administrative Update & Report
Packet, page 1 <copy provided to the Clerk for the record>. Emergency Management
Director Herring discussed a request (page 1) from South Gulf Fire Department to
purchase a tanker fire truck through State contract, not to exceed $500,000.00, noting
there will be a price increase before delivery and the funds are in their budget. He
recommended to the Board to allow South Gulf Fire Department Chief Barrett start
working on this, reporting delivery is expected in 6 to 9 months. Commissioner McCroan
motioned to approve. Commissioner Farrell seconded the motion. Chairman Quinn called
for public comment. There being none, the motion passed unanimously. County
Administrator Hammond stated the County will be putting in $100,000.00 to South Gulf
Fire Department’s $400,000.00 for the purchase.
INDIAN PASS LAND PURCHASE
County Administrator Hammond noted the Board gave him authorization to negotiate a
contract to purchase property on Indian Pass Road for the bridge replacement project,
stating the County has $150,000.00 to purchase approximately 15 acres consisting of two
parcels: Parcel ID #03185-003R containing 11.404 acres and Parcel ID #03184-001R
containing 4.38 acres. Public Works Director Cothran discussed the Indian Lagoon Bridge
project, just past the Indian Pass Raw Bar. County Administrator Hammond discussed
the awarded grant funds to elevate Indian Pass Road and a D.O.T. (Department of
Transportation) grant to replace this (Indian Lagoon) bridge and the need for a bypass
bridge while the road and bridge are being constructed. He reported that there is one
parcel listed for $349,00.00 and a second parcel listed at $134,900.00, recommending
the Board go forward with $150,000.00 for both pieces. County Administrator Hammond
noting there are some stipulations by the seller that the County would not develop the
property commercially or for residential. He stated the County, with this property
purchase, will have access for a temporary bridge and future access off of C-30 to the
Lagoon. Discussion followed regarding condemnation and eminent domain.
Commissioner McCroan discussed his concerns regarding efforts to gain access to
Painted Pony Road while Indian Pass Road was under construction. Commissioner
McCroan inquired if these two parcels are enough land for this project. County
Administrator Hammond stated D.O.T. is trying to obtain land on the other side (of the
bridge), noting it will be tight, but the County can make it work. Discussion followed
regarding the project and the land purchase price. County Attorney Novak discussed the
experience incurred and the highlights regarding eminent domain. Commissioner Farrell
moved to approve. Commissioner Rich seconded the motion. Chairman Quinn called for
public comment. There being no public comment, the motion passed unanimously.

There being no further business, and upon motion by Commissioner McCroan, second
by Commissioner Farrell, and unanimous vote, the meeting did then adjourn at 2:33 p.m.,
