2017-18 BUDGET OVERVIEW
Chairman McDaniel noted for the record that this is the second and final Public Hearing
for the 2017-18 Tentative Budget for Gulf County. Clerk Norris advised the Board of the
following: (1) the aggregated tentative millage rate is 7.6357, which is a 6.90% above the
current year aggregate rolled back rate of 7.1426; (2) the Countywide Operating Tentative
Millage Rate is 7.2442; (3) the Dependent Special Fire Control Districts Tentative Millage
Rates are .5000 for each District; (4) the MSTU Voted Debt Services Tentative Millage
Rates are: (a) Gulfside Beachfront 1.2542; (b) Gulfside Interior is 1.0869; and (c) Bayside
is 1.0727; (5) adoption of the Final Millage Rates and Budgets will require a majority vote
of the Board with these being the maximum rates, but the Board may adopt a lesser rate
with a majority vote; (6) compliance with the voting requirements will be measured tonight
on the final adoption of the levy by a roll-call vote; (7) General Fund Budget includes total
Cash Carry Forward of $6,805,352.00 ($4,350,434.00 Unrestricted and $2,454,948.00
Restricted Cash) all being budgeted as a source of funds for 2017-18 Budget; and (8)
some reasons for the increases over the rolled back rate are: (a) Board professional
service agreements; (b) cost of living pay increases; (c) health insurance elections; (d)
Sheriff’s transfer; € County Jail; (f) Clerk to Board transfer; (g) Park funding; (h) landfill
long term care cost; (i) retirement cost; (j) Elected Official’s pay increases; and (k)
Worker’s Comp and Property Insurance. Clerk Norris advised the Board that the Port St.
Joe Downtown Redevelopment Agency obligation is $167,340.00 based on the tentative

millage rate. Clerk Budget & Finance Officer Herring reported that the following changes
are being requested by staff for non-ad valorem adjustments to revenues and
expenditures for the Board’s approval: (a) decrease of $19,000.00 in Fund 127 Disaster
Fund affecting Cash Carry Forward revenue and disaster overtime expenditures; this
decrease is due to current year expenditures exceeding the prior projections; (b) increase
of $90,000.00 in Fund 301 Capital Projects Fund affecting Cash Carry Forward revenue
and Public Works equipment expenditures; this increase is due to the vendor unable to
meet fiscal year delivery requirements on three (3) pieces of equipment. Expenditures
include the addition of $64,500.00 for two (2) workcrew vans and $25,500.00 for one (1)
fleet truck; (c) line item adjustments to allocate the lump sum COLA pay increase; (d) line
item adjustments to consolidate the Building and Planning Departments at the direction
of County Administration; total consolidated budgets will equal the tentative budgets; and
(e) line item adjustments to appropriately budget new and transitioning employees into
their new roles. Commissioner Rogers motioned to approve these changes and
adjustments. Commissioner McCroan seconded the motion. Chairman McDaniel called
for public comment. There being no public comment, the motion passed unanimously (4-
0). Clerk Budget & Finance Officer Herring reminded the Board that any increases
approved by the Board tonight will require offsetting decreases in other specified line
items. Chief Administrator Butler stated that Clerk Budget & Finance Officer Herring has
done a great job on the budget. After discussion by Clerk Norris, Chairman McDaniel
called for public comment. Zebe Schmitt, Rosemary Lewis, and Andrea De La Vega, of
Port St. Joe appeared before the Board in support of the increased funding requests for
the Gulf County Library. Chairman McDaniel called for public comment. John Nagy, of
Howard Creek and Ann Tison, of Port St. Joe appeared before the Board to show their
support for the Gulf County Library. Chairman McDaniel again called for public comment.
Russell Scholz, Director of Gulf County Senior Citizens appeared before the Board to
request additional funding for the Senior Citizens Program. Chairman McDaniel called for
public comment. There being no further public comment, Chairman McDaniel closed the
meeting to the public comment. After discussion by Clerk Norris, Commissioner McCroan
motioned to increase the Gulf County Library by $6,000.00. Commissioner Quinn
seconded the motion for discussion. After discussion by members of the Board,
Commissioner Quinn stated that he is willing to giving up money from District IV’s Parks
and Recreation Fund; requesting that the other Commissioners agree to give up funds
from their Parks and Recreation Fund. Commissioner McCroan agreed to take funds from
District V Parks and Recreation Fund. Assistant Administrator Hammond reported that
546001 is the fund code for Parks and Recreation. Chairman McDaniel stated that he is
in agreement. Chairman McDaniel stated that the motion on the floor by Commissioner
McCroan is to give the Library an increase of $6,000.00. The motion then passed
unanimously (4-0). Commissioner McCroan motioned to reduce Parks and Recreation
Fund by $6,000.00. Commissioner Quinn seconded the motion. Chairman McDaniel
called for public comment. There being no public comment, the motion passed
unanimously (4-0). Chairman McDaniel called for a motion for the Senior Citizens. There
was no motion.
