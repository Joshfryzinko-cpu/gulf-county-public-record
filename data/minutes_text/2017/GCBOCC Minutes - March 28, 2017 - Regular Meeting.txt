PROCLAMATION – NATIONAL LIBRARY WEEK
Nancy Brockman with the Gulf County Libraries appeared before the Board to discuss
annual celebration of National Library Week. Ms. Brockman presented a newsletter to
the Commissioners <copy provided to the Clerk for the record> and requested that the
Board adopt a proclamation for National Library Week. She then read the proposed
proclamation for consideration. Commissioner McCroan motioned to adopt this
proclamation. Commissioner Rogers seconded the motion. Chairman McDaniel called for
public comment. There being no public comment, the motion passed unanimously.

REIMBURSEMENT – WASTE PRO
Ralph Mills, Regional Vice President of Waste Pro, appeared before the Board and
presented two (2) checks; one (1), in the amount of $13,500.00, for fees for the use of
the County’s facilities while the transfer station was being built, and the second (2nd)
check, in the amount of $17,620.00 representing the percentage the County received
from Out-of-County tipping fees. He stated that Waste Pro would like to pay the fees
quarterly instead of annually. Upon inquiry by Chairman McDaniel, County Attorney
Novak reported there is no conflict within the contract regarding paying the fees on a
quarterly basis. Commissioner Whitfield motioned that the County take these (fees from
Waste Pro) on a quarterly basis. Commissioner Quinn seconded the motion. Chairman
McDaniel called for public comment. There being no public comment, the motion passed
unanimously.
ROAD PAVING – FIVE POINTS ROAD
Ralph Mills, Regional Vice President of Waste Pro, discussed the paving of the transfer
station road (Five Points Road); reporting that the County was in the process of closing
Five Points Landfill and it was agreed to delay the paving until all heavy trucks where
finished hauling into the Landfill. He stated that Waste Pro has received three (3) bids (for
the paving project) and will need a few days to allow the engineers (for Waste Pro) to
review these bids. Mr. Mills reported that he will come back before the Board to provide
a time-table as to when the construction will begin. Upon inquiry by Chairman McDaniel,
Public Works Director Cothran reported that the County is through with the heavy dump
trucks on Five Points Road.
THANK YOU – SOLID WASTE SERVICES
Ralph Mills, Regional Vice President of Waste Pro thanked the Board for allowing Waste
Pro to service Gulf County; stating that he is looking forward to a long relationship.

OPENING BID #1617-15 – GULF COUNTY HEALTH DEPARTMENT PAVILION
Deputy Clerk Roberts stated that we are here today to open sealed bids for Bid #1617-
15 for the Gulf County Health Department Pavilion; reporting that there was only one (1)
sealed bid received, that being Cathey Construction. Central Service Director Lanier
stated aloud Cathey Construction’s bid, in the amount of $78,407.00. She reported that
the Gulf County Health Department’s budget for this project is only $18,000.00; stating
that the bid will be submitted to them for review. After discussion by members of the
Board, Chairman McDaniel called for a representative from the Gulf County Health

Department. There was no representative in the audience. Chief Administrator Butler
reported that this is a request from the Gulf County Health Department for the County to
run their project through the County’s procurement process and it was their design bid
specs for this project. Upon inquiry by Chairman McDaniel, Chief Administrator Butler
recommended that the Board table the bid. After discussion, Commissioner Quinn
motioned to table Bid #1617-15 for the Gulf County Health Department Pavilion.
Commissioner McCroan seconded the motion. Chairman McDaniel called for public
comment. There being no public comment, the motion passed unanimously.

Commissioner McCroan discussed his concerns regarding public parking on the side of
the road on C30 being a safety matter; requesting that Chief Administrator Butler contact

specifically South of Treasure Bay; reporting that it is narrow in that area. Commissioner
McCroan stated that it is North of Treasure Bay (not south). Chief Administrator Butler
reported that he will contact D.O.T. and hopefully they can map it out and the soft
approach would be to place plastic posts. He stated that if this does not work, then the
Board will need to consider adopting an ordinance to allow the Sheriff to ticket those that
do park there.

ROAD CLOSURE – POMPANO STREET
Stacey Hanlon, of Highland View appeared before the Board to discuss his concerns
regarding the safety concerns due to the road closure of Pompano Street on Wednesday
nights. He reported that there is no need for the road to be closed off to public use, as the
church has a large play area with basketball goals located behind the church. Upon
motion by Commissioner McCroan, second by Commissioner Whitfield, the Board
allowed Mr. Hanlon additional time to speak. Mr. Hanlon stated that his family supports
the youth ministry and do not object to the children, but there is no need in blocking a
portion of Pompano Street which blocks access to his driveway and restricts access to
public safety and healthcare personnel in emergencies. After discussing his concerns,
Mr. Hanlon, on behalf of himself, family, and neighbors requested the Board reconsider
the closer of Pompano Street on Wednesday nights. Upon inquiry by Commissioner
Quinn, Mr. Hanlon stated that he was not in support of the road closure. Upon inquiry by
Chairman McDaniel, Mr. Hanlon reported during the time that the road is closed on
Wednesday nights he cannot gain access to his property without moving the barricades.
Mr. Hanlon provided photos to the Board for review <provided to the Clerk for the record>.
Upon inquiry by Commissioner McCroan, Mr. Hanlon reported that he was originally
involved with the youth program, but has gotten away from it. He stated that this matter
has nothing to do with that, he just wants to be able to have access to his property.
Commissioner Rogers reported that he has people promising to help fund a privacy fence
for the back of the church and the church has promised that if there was a way to get the
children away from the dogs from the back lot, then they will move the children off of the
road. He stated that hopefully in the next week or so we will see fencing delivered and
the temporary road closure on Wednesday nights will go away. After discussion by
members of the Board, Dennis Barfield, Jr., representative of the Highland View Church
of God appeared before the Board to report that the church has tried many measure to
rectify the situation. He reported that they do not allow the children to go anywhere near
the road and the barricades have not been in place for the last three (3) weeks. Mr.
Barfield reported that the children are being relocated to the fellowship hall or the play
area out front. He reported that when the youth program began three (3) years ago, the
attendance was approximately 10 and now the program tops out at eighty-three (83) on
a Wednesday night service. Mr. Barfield stated that the church has no issue if the Board
decides to take the ordinance away. Discussion followed. Upon inquiry by Chairman
McDaniel, Mr. Barfield stated that the Highland View Church of God is open to all. Upon
inquiry by Chairman McDaniel, Mr. Barfield reported that there was a time when the street
was blocked off that they would allow the children to throw a football and play tag in the
street, but there would be representatives at each end of the barricades. He reported that
the church will not be opposed to any measure that the Board takes; stating that if the
Board deems to remove the ordinance the church will not oppose. Commissioner Quinn
thanked Mr. Barfield for what the church does for the community and picking up children
to attend. Upon inquiry by Commissioner Quinn, Mr. Barfield stated that the church would
not be opposed to the Board taking away the road closer. After discussion by members
for the Board, County Attorney Novak reported that there is no ordinance; stating that the
prior Board permitted this temporary road closure by vote. Commissioner Rogers
motioned to rescind the previous temporary road closure of Pompano Street on
Wednesday nights. Commissioner Whitfield seconded the motion. Chairman McDaniel

called for public comment. There being no public comment, the motion passed
unanimously.
