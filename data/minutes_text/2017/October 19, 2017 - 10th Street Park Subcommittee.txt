GULF COUNTY PARKS SUBCOMMITTEE
10TH STREET PORT ST. JOE
OCTOBER 19, 2017
9:00 AM
Attendees: County Commissioner Sandy Quinn, County Attorney Jeremy Novak, County
Administrator Don Butler, Assistant Administrator Michael Hammond, Public Works Director
Mark Cothran, Gulf County Building Official Lee Collinsworth, BOCC Grants Writer Lianna Sagins,

OPENING OF MEETING
Chairman Quinn called the meeting to order at 9:03 a.m., E.T.
REVIEW OF NOTICE AND PUPLIC MEETING
County Attorney Novak handed out the past meetings minutes and recommended that the
committee read over during the meeting.
STATUS AND REPORT FROM DEWBERRY REPRESENTATIVE
Clay Smallwood explained that he is going to try to answer the list of questions that have been
asked. He began with explaining the piping project and the plan that Dewberry has laid out. He
also reached out to some vendors for an estimated cost on the piping project, which came out
at $180,000. Mr. Smallwood believed that this is the high estimate and the price could come
down when Dewberry actually starts the project. There was discussion throughout the
committee about the project including permitting, phases, and footprint.
REPORT BY GULF COUNTY TOURIST DEVELOPMENT COUNCIL
T.D.C. Director Kelli Godwin gave a presentation on possible ways the T.D.C could market the
10th Street Park. She read a mission statement that could be used for marketing as well
recommending branching from HWY 71 to 22nd Street. There would be one name with several
sub brands. The name she gave as a suggestion was The Port City Park. She also gave out a
small survey on what the committee members would want more as elements inside the park
including a dog park, fitness elements and more. Mr. Yeager noted that any information the
T.D.C. could give on cost of different elements would be helpful to the committee. Chairman
Quinn commented that if the committee could identify some of those areas that could go
ahead and have smaller elements added as they are working on the main sports complex part
of the park that that would be a good idea.

AVAILABLE GRANT OPPURTINITES
Mrs. Pierce explained that there are grant opportunities available but she needs to know
elements before she is able to fully start applications. There was some discussion about grants
that would be available for extra funds.
MASTER PLAN AND BUDGET
County Attorney Novak stressed that this meeting, being the 5th meeting and then heading into
the 6th meeting, that the committee comes up with a collective goal and has recommendations
that could be put forth to the City and County Commissioners. These recommendations could
be a phased approached plan, a tentative master plan, and a possible budget. Nr. Novak also
stressed the 5th cent that is collected through Tourist Development Tax would come up for re-
vote soon. Mr. Novak suggested that the next time the committee meets that it would be a
master plan workshop.
GENERAL COMMENTARY FROM COMMITTEE MEMEBERS
T.D.C. Executive Director Kelli Godwin asked if Dewberry could consult when it comes to the
tournaments that would be held at the ball field aspect. Mrs. Jerri asked if the September
collections for Tourist Development Tax had been received yet and Mrs. Godwin told her that
they would be announced in the next week. Mr. Lacour stressed that we make sure we do not
create a flooding problem when it comes to the piping project.
ESTABLISH THE NEXT MEETING DATE
It was decided to meet on December 7th 9:00 in the Gulf County Emergency Management
Building.
The minutes from the past three meetings were motioned for a adoption by Member Grantland
and seconded by Member Lacour, and it passed Unanimously.
There being no further business, the meeting was adjourned at 9:40 a.m., E.T.
