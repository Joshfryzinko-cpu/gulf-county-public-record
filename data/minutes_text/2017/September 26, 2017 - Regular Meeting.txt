PRESENTATION – VETERANS’ MEMORIAL BEACON HILL PARK
Chief Administrator Butler recognized people in the audience that have been working on
the Veterans’ Memorial Beacon Hill Park Project. He requested that the Board: 1)
recognize this group (George Duren, Rodney Herring, Brian Cahill, Rick Lamberson,
Ralph Rish, Joe Paul, Billy Traylor, and Jessica Susich) as the group to work on Veterans’
Memorial Beacon Hill Park; 2) approve the direction that the group is going; and 3) apply
for grants when they become available. George Duren appeared before the Board to
present a map <copy provided to the Clerk for the record> and discuss the honor walk.
He requested the Board create and support the honor walk. Rodney Herring appeared
before the Board to discuss the memorial at Veterans’ Memorial Beacon Hill Park. Brian
Cahill appeared before the Board to discuss the proposed plans for the honor walk and
the flags within the pentagon. Chief Administrator Butler reported that the group has a
video to present to the Board and the public. The video did then play over the television
screens. Veterans’ Service Officer Paul appeared before the Board to discuss the
committee and the plan; reporting that the committee has partnered with Blue Water
Outriggers Inshore/Offshore Charity Classic, Inc., which is a 501(c)(3), and have been
approved to use the 501(c)(3) for fundraising and apply for grants. After discussion, Chief
Administrator Butler recommended the Board recognize this group as the committee and
the plan, and to allow for the applying of grants as they come available. He also reported
that the committee has set their meetings for Wednesday before the fourth Tuesday of
each month at 9:00 a.m., E.T. Commissioner Rogers motioned to approve this
recommendation. Commissioner Quinn seconded the motion. Chairman McDaniel called

for public comment. There being no public comment, the motion passed unanimously (4-
0).
ROAD REPAIR – MUSCOGEE ROAD
Chief Administrator Butler discussed a letter submitted regarding the condition of
Muscogee Road; requesting direction from the Board as to how to proceed. He stated
that the County fixes the road and then four-wheelers (ATVs) come in and tear up the
road; reporting that as of today, you cannot drive down this road (Muscogee) in a four-
wheel drive. Chairman McDaniel stated that the landowner, Neal Lumber Company,
wants to take the road back; reporting that they will repair the road. He discussed his
concerns regarding the road; stating that maintaining this road is a lot of expense to the
County. Public Works Director Cothran appeared before the Board to report that staff
went up to the road using a four-wheel drive four-wheeler with a wrench and was not able
to drive down the road. He reported that the damage to the road is not due to rain events,
but to bog-trucks; presenting pictures of the damaged road <copy provided to the Clerk
for the record>. Upon inquiry by Commissioner McCroan, Public Works Director Cothran
stated that there are (18) eighteen parcels effected by the closer. Chairman McDaniel
recommended that Chief Administrator Butler and County Attorney Novak work with Neal
Lumber Company. Chief Administrator Butler asked the Chairman whether the Board
would like staff to come back with options regarding Muscogee Road; stating that it will
take a public hearing to abandon a roadway. Chairman McDaniel inquired whether the
County really owned Muscogee Road and Commissioner McCroan asked if the County
had a maintenance agreement. Chief Administrator Butler reported that there is not a
