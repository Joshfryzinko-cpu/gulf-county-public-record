SEPTEMBER 12, 2017
PORT ST. JOE, FLORIDA
SPECIAL MEETING
The Gulf County Board of County Commissioners met this date in special session with
the following members present: Chairman Ward McDaniel, Vice Chairman Sandy Quinn,
Jr., and Commissioners Jimmy Rogers and Phil McCroan <District I seat vacant; awaiting
appointment by Governor Scott>.
Others present were: County Attorney Jeremy Novak, Clerk Rebecca L. Norris, Clerk
Director of Finance & Management Rhonda Woodward, Clerk Budget & Finance Officer
Sherry Herring, Deputy Clerk Leanna Roberts, Chief Administrator Don Butler, Assistant
Administrator Michael L. Hammond, Deputy Administrator Kari Summers, Interim Building
Official Lee Collinsworth, Central Services Director Lynn Lanier, Gulf County E.D.C.
Director/RESTORE Act Coordinator Warren Yeager, Jr., Emergency Management
Director Marshall Nelson, E9-1-1 Coordinator Ben Guthrie, G.I.S. Coordinator Jessica
Susich, HR/Risk Management Director Denise Manuel, County Planner Brett Lowry,
Dewberry l Preble-Rish/County Engineer Clay Smallwood, III, Public Works Director Mark
Cothran, Assistant Public Works Director Lee Collinsworth, Sheriff Mike Harrison, T.D.C.
Marketing Manager Kelli Godwin, and Veterans' Service Officer & S.H.I.P. Administrator
Joe Paul.
Chairman McDaniel called the meeting to order at 4:45 p.m., E.T.
Commissioner Quinn led in the Pledge of Allegiance to the Flag.
BIDS
After discussion by Assistant Administrator Hammond, Commissioner Quinn motioned to
reject Bid #1617-26 for the purchase of a Jet Ski and Trailer for the South Gulf County
Fire Department. Commissioner Rogers seconded the motion. Chairman McDaniel called
for public comment. There being no public comment, the motion passed unanimously (4-
0).
Upon request by Assistant Administrator Hammond, Commissioner McCroan motioned
to reject Bid #1617-27 for the (Bear) Kits for the Trash Can Modification. Commissioner
Rogers seconded the motion. Chairman McDaniel called for public comment. There being
no public comment, the motion passed unanimously (4-0).
Upon recommendation by Interim Building Official Collinsworth, Commissioner McCroan
motioned to reject Bid #1617-32 for the Roof Replacement at South Gulf County Fire
Department and to re-advertise with specifications. Commissioner Rogers seconded the
motion. Chairman McDaniel called for public comment. There being no public comment,
the motion passed unanimously (4-0).

Upon request by Assistant Administrator Hammond, Commissioner Rogers motioned to
reject Bid #1617-31 for Managing and Maintaining Services for the T.D.C. Website.
Commissioner Quinn seconded the motion. Chairman McDaniel called for public
comment. There being no public comment, the motion passed unanimously (4-0).
T.D.C.
Assistant Administrator Hammond discussed the need to go out for advertisements for
new website/hosting (advertise to receive sealed bids). Commissioner McCroan
motioned to approve this request. Commissioner Quinn seconded the motion. Chairman
McDaniel called for public comment. There being no public comment, the motion passed
unanimously (4-0).
Assistant Administrator Hammond reported that Public Works is going to transfer a truck
from Public Works to T.D.C.; stating that T.D.C. will be purchasing this used vehicle.
Public Works Director Cothran discussed handing-off one of the Public Works’ trucks that
is three (3) years old to T.D.C.; requesting to purchase a new vehicle for Public Works,
through State Contract, and moving money forward out of this year’s budget into next
year’s budget to cover the cost. Chairman McDaniel clarified that T.D.C. will buy a truck
from Public Works and that money, plus funds from Public Works will be used to purchase
a new truck for Public Works. Commissioner McCroan so moved. Commissioner Quinn
seconded the motion. Chairman McDaniel called for public comment. There being no
public comment, the motion passed unanimously (4-0).
There being no further business, and upon motion by Commissioner Rogers, second by
Commissioner Quinn, and unanimous 4-0 vote, the meeting did then adjourn at 4:51 p.m.,
E.T.
