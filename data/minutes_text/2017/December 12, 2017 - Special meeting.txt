POLICY AMENDMENT – PROCUREMENT
County Attorney Novak discussed the need to amend the procurement policy due to
Statute changes and the new C.C.N.A. (Competitive Consultants Negotiation Act)
language to be included; requesting that the Board include C.C.N.A. (Competitive
Consultants Negotiation Act) language and the P3 (public-private partnership) Statutes in
the Procurement Policy. Commissioner McCroan motioned to move forward.
Commissioner Rich seconded the motion. Chairman McDaniel called for public comment.
There being no public comment, the motion passed unanimously.
PUBLIC HEARING – FL STATUTE 255 PRIVATE PARTNERSHIP PROPOSAL /
FLOATING DRY DOCK
County Attorney Novak reported that the County received one proposal; reporting the
proposal notice had been properly advertised. He stated that the applicant has paid the
application fees. County Attorney Novak stated for the record that the County has not
received any other unsolicited proposals for the floating dry dock. Chairman McDaniel
opened the Public Hearing; calling for public comment. Tom Sims, of Wewahitchka

appeared before the Board to discuss his concerns regarding the tangible tax on the dry
dock and the County being responsible for the maintenance/upkeep for the dry dock.
County Attorney Novak stated that under the agreement and lease, Eastern Shipbuilding
will be responsible for the maintenance and insurance on the floating dry dock; reporting
that the lease will be structured around job growth. Assistant Administrator Hammond
reported that the tax will be charged based on the value of the lease. Mr. Sims discussed
the life of the lease and inquired whether the County intends to recover all the money that
will be put into this dry dock. After discussion by County Attorney Novak, and Assistant
Administrator Hammond reported that there is no intent to recoup money from this
investment directly to the Board; stating that the hope is Eastern Shipbuilding will create
enough jobs that they will not have to pay a penny. Chairman McDaniel called for public
comment. There being no additional public comment, Chairman McDaniel closed the
Public Hearing. Following Board discussion, Commissioner Rogers motioned to accept
and approve the unsolicited proposal. Commissioner McCroan seconded the motion.
Chairman McDaniel called for public comment. There being no public comment, the
motion passed unanimously. County Attorney Novak reported that staff will draft an
agreement to bring back before the Board for consideration. Commissioner McCroan
motioned to authorize staff to advertise to receive sealed bids for engineering services to
design the floating dry dock. Commissioner Rogers seconded the motion. Chairman
McDaniel called for public comment. There being no public comment, the motion passed
unanimously. Commissioner Quinn motioned to allow staff to advertise to receive sealed
bids for engineering services to inspect the plans and to oversee the construction of the
floating dry dock. Commissioner Rich seconded the motion. Chairman McDaniel called
for public comment. There being no public comment, the motion passed unanimously.
Commissioner Quinn motioned to allow staff to draft and finalize an economic
development grant for site improvements and authorize the Chairman’s signature.
Commissioner McCroan seconded the motion. Chairman McDaniel called for public
comment. There being no public comment, the motion passed unanimously.

PUBLIC HEARING – ROAD ABANDONMENTS
Pursuant to advertisement to hold a Public Hearing to consider various road
abandonments, County Attorney Novak discussed the proposed road abandonment of
County Line Road, GT-14, Muscogee Road, and Rish Harbor Road. Chairman McDaniel
called for public comment regarding the abandonment of County Line Road. There being
no public comment, Chairman closed the Public Hearing. Chairman McDaniel opened
and called for public comment regarding the abandonment of GT-14. There being no
public comment, Chairman McDaniel closed the Public Hearing. County Attorney Novak
stated for the record that the proposed abandonment of GT-14 will be from Williams Bay
Road. Commissioner Rich motioned to proceed with the closing of County Line Road.
Commissioner McCroan seconded the motion, and it passed unanimously.
Commissioner Quinn motioned to move forward with the closure of GT-14. Commissioner
Rogers seconded the motion, and it passed unanimously. Chairman McDaniel opened
the Public Hearing to the public regarding Muscogee Road. Gary Roberts, of
Wewahitchka appeared before the Board to discuss his concerns regarding the road
closure; requesting the Board secure the road, not abandon it. Chairman McDaniel
discussed the damage to Muscogee Road; reporting the road is not going to be
abandoned. Neal Timberland Company has agreed to maintain and give key access to
the property owners, stated Chairman McDaniel. After discussion by Commissioner
McCroan, County Attorney Novak stated that a proposed resolution will be brought back
to the Board for consideration, if the Board votes to proceed. Chairman McDaniel called
for public comment regarding Rish Harbor Road. There being no public comment,
Chairman McDaniel closed the Public Hearing. Commissioner Quinn motioned to move
forward with the closer of Muscogee Road. Commissioner Rich seconded the motion.
After discussion by Commissioner McCroan, the motion passed unanimously.
Commissioner Rogers motioned to move forward with the abandonment of Rish Harbor
Road. Commissioner Rich seconded the motion, and it passed unanimously.

REPORT – BEACH RESTORATION PROJECT
Gulf County E.D.C. Director/RESTORE Act Coordinator Yeager, Jr. appeared before the
Board to report that staff is close to having a recommendation for the Board to consider
regarding the Beach Restoration Project. He noted one of the options to consider is to
reverse bid the project, giving the bidders a dollar amount to bid from. Assistant
Administrator Hammond suggested holding a workshop on this matter to receive public
input. Gulf County E.D.C. Director/RESTORE Act Coordinator Yeager, Jr. reported that
part of the funds in Pot III is for erosion control; stating the County has committed
approximately $3 million. Assistant Administrator Hammond reported the County is trying
to set up a meeting with our lobbyist regarding the Eglin property. After discussion,
Commissioner McCroan motioned to hold a workshop on January 23, 2017 before the
regular scheduled Board meeting. Commissioner Quinn seconded the motion. Chairman
McDaniel called for public comment. Roland Wilson, of Cape San Blas appeared before
the Board to inquire about who will responsible for rebuilding the highway after hauling
the sand to the Cape. Chairman McDaniel discussed a dredging option verses hauling
sand. Mr. Wilson reported that the Florida Department of Transportation was supposed
to do a study on installing a bridge at the Stumphole; discussing his concerns regarding
the erosion. Commissioner McCroan motioned to allow Mr. Wilson additional time to
speak. Commissioner Quinn seconded the motion. After discussion by Mr. Wilson,
Assistant Administrator Hammond discussed meeting with D.O.T. (Florida Department of
Transportation); stating D.O.T. has taken the bridge design and land acquisition off the
table and is now looking into a non-elevated causeway. Pat Hardman, President of the
Coastal Community Association appeared before the Board to thank staff for their time
and effort that has gone into finding a solution. She suggested to structure the project in
phases; opposed just saying the County has so much money (for the project). After
discussion by Ms. Hardman and Chairman McDaniel, Butch Kline, of Cape San Blas
appeared before the Board to discuss his concerns regarding this project; stating that

time is of the essence. Chairman McDaniel called for public comment. There being no
further public comment, the motion passed unanimously.

PUBLIC HEARING – LIGHTHOUSE UTILITIES
Representing Lighthouse Utilities, Michael McKenzie, CPA of Roberson and Associates
appeared before the Board to report that Lighthouse Utilities is requesting to come under
the regulatory authority of Gulf County instead of the State of Florida; reporting the County
would have jurisdiction over the Lighthouse Utilities. County Attorney Novak discussed
holding the Public Hearing; stating that if the Board accepts, then the next step would be
to advertise for a Public Hearing to consider adoption of an ordinance. Commissioner
McCroan motioned to start the procedure. Commissioner Rogers seconded the motion.
Upon inquiry by Chairman McDaniel regarding what the regulatory authority entails,
Assistant Administrator Hammond stated the County would take complaints and petitions
for rate changes. Chairman McDaniel called for public comment. Roland Wilson, of Cape
San Blas appeared before the Board to discuss his concerns regarding the transfer;
asking why Lighthouse Utilities is requesting the County take over instead of the State of
Florida. He reported that the State is equipped, understands utilities, and knows how to
regulate them. Upon inquiry by Mr. Wilson about the benefit to the County, Assistant
Administrator Hammond reported that the major benefit to the County is the collection of
funds, in the amount of $40,000.00. He stated there has been a change in the Law;
reporting the Public Service Commission wants to get out of the smaller operations
(utilities). Commissioner McCroan stated that his main concern is the public and wishes
to hear from the public regarding this matter. Pat Hardman, of Cape San Blas appeared
before the Board discuss her concerns regarding the Lighthouse Utilities structure and
the cost of maintenance. After discussion by Commissioner McCroan, Chairman
McDaniel called for public comment. There being no further public comment, the motion
passed unanimously.
