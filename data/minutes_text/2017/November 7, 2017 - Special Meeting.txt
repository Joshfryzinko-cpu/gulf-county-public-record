<Commissioner Quinn did not return to the meeting after the recess.>

REJECT BID #1617-40 - BEACH RENOURISHMENT PROJECT
Chief Administrator Butler reported that Staff’s recommendation to the Board is to reject
Bid #1617-40 for the St. Joseph Peninsula Beach Renourishment Project. He stated that
if the Board rejects the bid, Staff will come back in December with additional options for
the Board to consider. Commissioner McCroan motioned to reject the bids for the St.
Joseph Peninsula Beach Renourishment Project (Bid #1617-40). Commissioner Rogers
seconded the motion. Chairman McDaniel called for public comment. Pat Hardman,
President of Community Coastal Association appeared before the Board to discuss the
rejection of the bids and the renourishment project. She presented a hand-out to the
Board <copy provided to the Clerk for the record> for review regarding the renourishment
project. After discussion by members of the Board, the motion passed unanimously (4-
0).
F.S. 255 PUBLIC PRIVATE PARTNERSHIP
County Attorney Novak discussed the P3 Statute (public private partnership); reporting
that under the P3 Statute the County received an unsolicited proposal from Eastern
Shipbuilding Group. He stated that under the Statute the proposal is protected under the
public records statute 255 and 815; reporting that he has provided each Commissioner a
copy of the unsolicited proposal as well as staff. County Attorney Novak requested
permission to advertise through a public notice in the Florida Register and the local
newspaper for a public comment period. He reported that this unsolicited proposal has
been presented to the Board with an effort to establish a public private partnership where
Gulf County would construct a floating dry dock, which would be owned by the County,
and would be operated by the private contractor. Upon request by County Attorney
Novak, Commissioner McCroan motioned to deem the unsolicited proposal submitted by
Eastern Shipbuilding Group a qualifying project under the P3 Statute. Commissioner
Rogers seconded the motion. Chairman McDaniel called for public comment. There being
no public comment, the motion passed unanimously (4-0). County Attorney Novak
recommended an initial administrative review fee of $2,500.00. Chairman McDaniel called
for a motion to start the process. Commissioner Rogers so moved. Commissioner Rich
seconded the motion. Chairman McDaniel called for public comment. There being no
public comment, the motion passed unanimously (4-0). After discussion by County
Attorney Novak, Commissioner McCroan motioned to move forward with the initial steps
under the Statute. Commissioner Rich seconded the motion. Chairman McDaniel called
for public comment. There being no public comment, the motion passed unanimously (4-
0).
TRIUMPH APPLICATION SUBMITTAL
Gulf County E.D.C. Director/RESTORE Act Coordinator Yeager appeared before the
Board to report that Staff is ready to submit a pre-application to Triumph; stating that the
submittal will be by Gulf County and the Port St. Joe Port Authority. Commissioner Rogers
motioned to proceed with the submittal of the pre-application. Commissioner McCroan
seconded the motion. Chairman McDaniel called for public comment. There being no
public comment, the motion passed unanimously (4-0).

INDUSTRIAL PARK LEASE/PURCHASE – CHEROKEE AIR, INC
County Attorney Novak reported that staff met with Michael Lawson, of Cherokee Air, Inc.
and there is a proposed three (3) year lease agreement at a rate of $2,200.00 per month
for the Industrial Park Building. He stated that the County has offered Cherokee Air, Inc.
the first right of refusal. After discussion by County Attorney Novak regarding the terms
of the proposed lease, Commissioner Rogers motioned to approve the right of first refusal
of the property (Industrial Park Building). Commissioner Rich seconded the motion.
Chairman McDaniel called for public comment. County Attorney Novak requested a clear
motion that the Board is approving the lease as well as the right of first refusal. Chairman
McDaniel inquired to any objection. The Board had no objection. Chairman McDaniel
called for public comment. There being no public comment, the motion passed
unanimously (4-0).
OPIOID LITIGATION – SPECIAL COUNSEL ENGAGEMENT
Upon recommendation by County Attorney Novak, Commissioner McCroan motioned to
move forward with the Opioid Litigation. Commissioner Rich seconded the motion.
Chairman McDaniel called for public comment. There being no public comment, the
motion passed unanimously (4-0).
UNION CONTRACT
Chief Administrator Butler discussed the proposed three (3) year union contract that was
presented to the Board <copy provided the Clerk for the record>; reporting that there are
no changes to the contract. He stated that the only item that will need to be negotiated in
year two (2) and three (3) is to consider pay. Upon staff recommendation, Commissioner
Rich motioned to ratify the Union Contract. Commissioner Rogers seconded the motion.
Chairman McDaniel called for public comment. There being no public comment, the
motion passed unanimously (4-0).
