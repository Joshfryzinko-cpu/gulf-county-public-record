ACQUISITION & PROCUREMENT POLICY
At the request of County Attorney Novak, the Board had no objection to allowing County
Attorney Novak to present a proposed amended procurement policy to the Board at their
next monthly meeting.
LEASE – INDUSTRIAL PARK
County Attorney Novak reported the County has received an interest in the lease of the
Industrial Park for two (2) years at $2,200.00 per month; providing a handout for Board’s
review <copy provided to the Clerk for the record>. He requested permission to negotiate
lease terms with this company, but continue to accept other offers from companies with
interest in leasing this building. Gulf County E.D.C. Director/RESTORE Act Coordinator
Yeager appeared before the Board to discuss three (3) interests in leasing the Industrial
Park. He stated that staff will come back with a recommendation as to which vendor would
be the best option for the County. Commissioner Rogers motioned to allow Gulf County
E.D.C. Director/RESTORE Act Coordinator Yeager and County Attorney Novak to
proceed with working with all three (3) vendors regarding the leasing of the Industrial
Park. Commissioner Quinn seconded the motion. Chairman McDaniel called for public
comment. There being no public comment, the motion passed unanimously (4-0).
OPIATE LITIGATION
County Attorney Novak reported that several Counties over the State of Florida have
pursued some opiate litigation against pharmaceutical companies; requesting permission
to bring back some recommendations to the Board at the next regular meeting as to how
to move forward on possible opioid litigation.
MONTHLY STATUS REPORT – GULF COUNTY E.M.S.
E.M.S. Director Houston appeared before the Board to provide the monthly status report
for the month of September, 2017 <copy provided to the Clerk for the record>.
F.E.M.A. MODIFICATION AGREEMENT – STUMPHOLE PROJECT
Gulf County E.D.C. Staff Assistant Sagins appeared before the Board to report that
F.E.M.A. has awarded the entire Stumphole Project, totaling 500 feet; providing a copy
of the award letter to the Board <copy provided to the Clerk for the record>. She reported
that there is an agreement with T.R.I.P. (Transportation Regional Incentive Program) for
the match, but the County will be short just under $100,000.00; noting she is working with
D.O.T. to obtain additional funding. Upon request by Gulf County E.D.C. Staff Assistant
Sagins, Commissioner McCroan motioned to approve the Chairman to sign the

modification agreement with F.E.M.A. (for the Hazard Mitigation Grant #DR-4068-15-R
for the Stumphole Revetment Project, Phase II). Commissioner Rogers seconded the
motion. Chairman McDaniel called for public comment. There being no public comment,
the motion passed unanimously (4-0).
M.Y.I.P. UPDATE
Gulf County E.D.C. Director/RESTORE Act Coordinator Yeager appeared before the
Board to report that the committee previously approved various projects under the
M.Y.I.P. (Multi-Year Implementation Plan); some of which have been completed,
recommending it be rewritten and begin the 45-day public comment period on the
revisions. He requested permission to work with staff and the Grant Consultants
(Dewberry | Preble-Rish) to draft a five (5) year plan so that the County will not have to
go through this process annually. Commissioner Quinn motioned to approve this request.
Commissioner McCroan seconded the motion. Chairman McDaniel called for public
comment. There being no public comment, the motion passed unanimously (4-0).
LIGHTHOUSE UTILITIES
Representing Lighthouse Utilities, Michael McKenzie, CPA of Robertson and Associates
appeared before the Board to report that Lighthouse Utilities Company would like to come
under the regulatory authority of Gulf County; requesting permission from the Board to
allow the County Attorney to work with their attorney to discuss this option moving
forward. Upon inquiry by Chairman McDaniel, Mr. McKenzie stated that Lighthouse
Utilities Company would like to come out from under the Public Service Commission and
come under the umbrella of Gulf County; reporting that the regulatory fees will then be
paid to the County, in the amount of approximately $40,000.00 annually. County Attorney
Novak reported that public hearings will be held and an ordinance will need to be adopted;
stating the Board would sit as the Utility Commission; addressing complaints and rate
increases. After discussion by Chairman McDaniel, Commissioner McCroan stated he
would like to hear from the public; requesting a public hearing be scheduled. County
Attorney Novak stated that with a vote of the Board to move forward, a public hearing
notice will be advertised in the newspaper. Commissioner McCroan motioned to allow
staff to move forward in publicly advertising to hold a public hearing to consider placing
Lighthouse Utilities Company under the regulatory authority of Gulf County.
Commissioner Rogers seconded the motion. Chairman McDaniel called for public
comment. There being no public comment, the motion passed unanimously (4-0).
ROAD ABANDONMENTS
County Attorney Novak discussed two (2) aerial maps provided to the Board for review
<copy provided to the Clerk for the record> regarding Muscogee Road, Rish Harbor
Road, and County Line Road (section between Gulf and Bay County that starts at U.S.
Highway 22 and terminates at Pleasant Rest Road). He reported that the 45 (forty-five)
day process will begin by placing an ad in the newspaper for Muscogee Road and Rish
Harbor Road and posting signs to notify the neighboring property owners; requesting the
Board to waive the requirements in the County’s policy to enable staff to move forward.
County Attorney Novak stated that the County will acquire Odena Boat Landing from
Deseret Ranches and in turn the County will abandon County Line Road from U.S.

Highway 22 to Pleasant Rest Road. He requested the authority to advertise, post signs,
work with Deseret Ranches, and require Dewberry | Preble-Rish to create a legal survey
on Odena Boat Landing; bringing a final package back in late November or early
December for the Board’s consideration. Commissioner McCroan so moved.
Commissioner Rogers seconded the motion. After discussion by Commissioner Rogers,
Chairman McDaniel called for public comment. There being no public comment, the
motion passed unanimously (4-0).

QUASI-JUDICIAL HEARING – REVIEW & CONSIDERATION OF P.D.R.B.
RECOMMENDATIONS
VARIANCE REQUEST – TIMOTHY & TERRY COLWELL
Planner Lowry presented a variance request from Timothy and Terry Colwell * Parcel ID
#03806-025R; stating that they are requesting a 20 (twenty) foot Roadside Building
Setback located on U.S. Highway 98 in Beacon Hill. He reported that the P.D.R.B. voted
unanimously, by a (4-0) vote, to accept this variance request. Planner Lowry requested
that the Chairman inquire if there are any objections to the waiving of the Quasi-Judicial
Hearing. Chairman McDaniel called for any objection to the waiving of the Quasi-Judicial
Hearing. There being no objection, Commissioner Quinn motioned to waive.
Commissioner Rogers seconded the motion, and it passed unanimously (4-0). Jack
Husband, of Southeastern Consulting Engineers appeared before the Board to discuss
the variance request submitted by his clients, Mr. and Mrs. Colwell. Planner Lowry
reported that this request is one of those typical variances that the Board has approved
several times. He stated that the Hearing was properly noticed, there was a waiver of
Quasi-Judicial Hearing, Planning and the P.D.R.B. deems the substantial completeness
of this application. Commissioner Rogers so moved. Commissioner McCroan seconded
the motion. Chairman McDaniel called for public comment. There being no public
comment, the motion passed unanimously (4-0).

BID #1617-40 – ST. JOSEPH PENINSULA BEACH RENOURISHMENT PROJECT
James Taunton, of James Taunton Construction appeared before the Board to discuss
his concerns regarding the St. Joseph Peninsula Beach Renourishment Project Bid;
presenting a proposal for this project by using an upland sand source <copy provided to
the Clerk for the record>. He requested that the Board make a motion to allow him to work
with County staff to present a proposal to provide an alternative solution for this project.
Mr. Taunton again asked for a motion. Chairman McDaniel noted no motion was offered
from the Board. Following discussion, Commissioner McCroan reported that the County
has its own upland sand source should the County decide to consider this option. Chief
Administrator Butler discussed opening the bids today and bringing a recommendation
back before the Board, noting at that point in time the other option of using an upland
sand source, which Mr. Taunton is discussed, could be considered. He stated that first
the County has to go through the process that has already started. County Attorney Novak
stated that the County didn’t prohibit anyone from submitting a bid; reporting that bidders
needed to attend the pre-bid conference and ideally need to meet the bid specifications
that was developed by the County’s engineer.
