TENTATIVE 2017-18 OVERVIEW
Chairman McDaniel began the meeting by reporting that the second and final Budget
Hearing will be held on September 28, 2017 at 5:01 p.m., E.T. For the record Clerk Norris
addressed the budget highlights as follows: (1) The aggregate proposed millage rate is
7.6357, which is 6.90% above the current year aggregate rolled back rate of 7.1426, (2)
the county-wide proposed tentative millage rate is 7.2442 which is the maximum county-
wide millage rate unless the Board votes to adopt a lesser rate, (3) the General Fund
Cash Carry Forward amount is $6,805,352 as balance brought forward-cash and has
been budgeted as a source of funds in the 2017-18 General Fund Budget (unrestricted is
$4,350,434, with the remaining $2,454,948 being restricted cash in the General Fund),
(4) some key reasons for the increases in the rolled back rate are: a) Board professional
service agreements, b) Cost of living pay raises, c) health insurance elections, d) Sheriff’s
transfer, e) County Jail, f) Clerk to Board’s transfer, g) parks funding, h) landfill long term
care cost, i) retirement cost, j) Elected Official’s pay increases, and k) worker’s
compensation and property insurance, (5) the Port St. Joe Downtown Redevelopment
Agency’s obligation this year, based on the current millage rate, is $167,340.00, (6) the
Dependent Districts proposed tentative budgets include .5000 mill each, and (7) the
MSTU voted debt service proposed tentative millage rates: a) Gulfside Beachfront is

1.2542, b) Gulfside Interior is 1.0869, and c) Bayside is 1.0727. Clerk Norris noted there
have been various changes included in the proposed tentative budget to balance to the
millage rate as directed and set by the Board on July 18, 2017. Clerk Norris informed the
Board that changes associated with the updated Elected Official salary schedules and
changes associated with grants, special revenue and designated funds have been
included in the proposed budget presented; advising the Board that any increases
approved by the Board at tonight’s Hearing will require equivalent reductions specified by
line item.
Chairman McDaniel called for public comment. Erin Payner, of Windmark Beach and
Andrea De La Vega, of Cape San Blas both appeared before the Board in support of
increased funding for the Public Library ($10,000.00); both expressing the benefit of
programs available at the Gulf County Library. Chairman McDaniel again called for public
comment. Director of the Senior Citizens Association Russell Scholz appeared before the
Board to address their request for increased funding ($12,000.00); noting the additional
funds would take two (2) senior citizens off the waiting list. Chairman McDaniel stated
that the Board will take these requests under advisement. Chairman McDaniel called for
public comment. There was no additional public comment. Chairman McDaniel then
closed the hearing to public comment. Clerk Budget & Finance Officer Herring discussed
the Board’s vote in a prior meeting for cash carried forward funding of a new vehicle for
Public Works; stating the majority of the funding ($30,000.00) is included in the proposed
tentative budget. She requested Board approval of an additional $5,000.00 be added to
Capital Project Fund 301 (30139800-398000 & 30154100-564000); reporting this
increase does not affect ad valorem. Commissioner Quinn so moved. Commissioner
Rogers seconded the motion. Chairman McDaniel called for public comment. There being
no public comment, the motion passed unanimously (4-0).
