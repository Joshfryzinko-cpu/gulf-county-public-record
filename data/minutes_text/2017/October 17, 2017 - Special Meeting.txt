OCTOBER 17, 2017
PORT ST. JOE, FLORIDA
SPECIAL MEETING
The Gulf County Board of County Commissioners met this date in special session with
the following members present: Chairman Ward McDaniel, Vice Chairman Sandy Quinn,
Jr., and Commissioners Jimmy Rogers and Phil McCroan <District I seat vacant; awaiting
appointment by Governor Scott>.
Others present were: County Attorney Jeremy Novak, Clerk Rebecca L. Norris, Clerk
Director of Finance & Management Rhonda Woodward, Deputy Clerk Leanna Roberts,
Chief Administrator Don Butler, Assistant Administrator Michael L. Hammond, Building
Official Brad Bailey, Gulf County E.D.C. Director/RESTORE Act Coordinator Warren
Yeager, Jr., Gulf County E.M.S. Director Houston Whitfield, Assistant Mosquito Control
Director Austin Horton, Sheriff’s Office Captain Chris Buchanan, and Interim T.D.C.
Executive Director Kelli Godwin.
Sheriff’s Office Captain Buchanan called the meeting to order at 9:59 a.m., E.T.
Chairman McDaniel opened the meeting with prayer, and Commissioner Rogers led the
Pledge of Allegiance to the Flag.
PUBLIC HEARING – PROPOSED MEDICAL MARIJUANA MORATORIUM
EXTENSION ORDINANCE
County Attorney Novak reported that the Public Hearings were advertised in the
newspaper and a proof of publication is on file. He stated that Staff’s recommendation is
to extend the current moratorium for twelve (12) months. Commissioner McCroan
motioned to extend the medical marijuana moratorium for twelve (12) months.
Commissioner Rogers seconded the motion. Chairman McDaniel called for public
comment. There being no public comment, the motion passed unanimously (4-0).
PUBLIC HEARING – PROPOSED PARKS POLICY & APPLICATION ORDINANCE
Pursuant to advertisement to consider adoption of a proposed ordinance, County Attorney
Novak summarized the proposed parks policy and application ordinance for the Board’s
consideration. After discussion, Commissioner Rogers motioned to adopt the following
Ordinance. Commissioner Quinn seconded the motion. Chairman McDaniel called for
public comment. There being no public comment, the motion passed unanimously (4-0),
as follows:
ORDINANCE NO. 2017-04

AN ORDINANCE OF GULF COUNTY, FLORIDA; ESTABLISHING GULF
COUNTY PARKS POLICY AND APPLICATION ORDINANCE AND
SHALL BE COMMONLY REFERRED TO AS THE “GULF COUNTY
PARKS POLICY” ORDINANCE WHEREIN GULF COUNTY ADOPTS,
INSTITUTES AND IMPLEMENTS THE GULF COUNTY PARKS
POLICIES, GUIDELINES, APPLICATION FOR RESERVATION AND USE
OF COUNTY PARKS AND OR COUNTY FACILITY USAGES
PROVIDING FOR PENALTIES; PROVIDING FOR REPEALER,
PROVIDING FOR ADMINISTRATIVE OVERSIGHT; SEVERABILITY AND
MODIFICATIONS THAT MAY ARISE FORM CONSIDERATION AT
PUBLIC HEARINGS; PROVIDING FOR CODIFICATION AND AN
EFFECTIVE DATE.
* Complete Ordinance on file with Clerk*
(End)

Pursuant to advertisement to consider adoption of a proposed ordinance, County Attorney

“GULF COUNTY TOURIST DEVELOPMENT PLAN, POLICY AND

GRAY ROBINSON CONTRACT EXTENSION
Upon recommendation by Assistant Administrator Hammond, Commissioner Rogers
motioned to extend the Gray Robinson contract (Lobbyist Service) for one (1) year.
Commissioner McCroan seconded the motion. Chairman McDaniel called for public
comment. There being no public comment, the motion passed unanimously (4-0).

T.D.C. WEBSITE RECOMMENDATION / BID #1617-41
Interim T.D.C. Executive Director Godwin recommended to award Bid #1617-41 for the
T.D.C. Website to the low bidder (Curiosity Marketing); requesting permission to negotiate
a contract with this company. Commissioner McCroan so moved. Commissioner Quinn
seconded the motion. Chairman McDaniel called for public comment. There being no
public comment, the motion passed unanimously (4-0).
ST. JOE BEACH FIRE DEPARTMENT VEHICLE BID – NJPA
Chief Administrator Butler reported that the Beaches Fire Department is requesting
approval to purchase a fire truck on contract; stating that County Attorney Novak has
vetted the process and the County’s ability to use this existing contract (NJPA). He
requested Board approval to use: 1. the St. Joe Fire Control District .1 Mill Funds from
FY2016-17; 2. the St. Joe Fire Control District .1 Mill Funds from FY2017-18; 3. St. Joe
Fire Control District Windmark Funds; 4. St. Joe Fire Control District – Gulf County
Beaches Fire Department FY2017-18 equipment budget to purchase a new fire truck for
Beaches Fire Department from V.T. Hackney as described in Quote #E3306-0003 for a
total of $288,858.55 through the NJPA (National Joint Powers Alliance) Contracting
Agency. Chief Administrator Butler reported that the Beaches Fire Department will be
giving up the yellow mini-pumper (#745) and the white Ford truck (#749) to another fire
department within the County. Commissioner Rogers so moved to adopt. Commissioner
Quinn seconded the motion. After discussion, Chairman McDaniel called for public
comment. There being no public comment, the motion passed unanimously (4-0). After
discussion by Chief Administrator Butler regarding bringing a proposed budget
amendment before the Board and the next meeting, County Attorney Novak stated that
the County will need to be a member of NJPA (National Joint Powers Alliance); reporting
there is no cost to the County for this membership. Commissioner McCroan so moved.
Commissioner Quinn seconded the motion. Chairman McDaniel called for public
comment. There being no public comment, the motion passed unanimously (4-0).
BUCHANAN INGERSOLL CONTRACT EXTENSION
Following discussion by County Attorney Novak, Commissioner McCroan motioned to
extend the contract with Buchanan Ingersoll regarding C.B.R.A. consulting services.
Commissioner Quinn seconded the motion for discussion. After discussion, Chairman
McDaniel called for public comment. Pat Hardman, President of Coastal Community
Association appeared before the Board to encourage the Board and to thank them for
their support. Chairman McDaniel called for any additional public comment. There being
no public comment, the motion passed unanimously (4-0).
FLYERS CLUB LICENSE APPROVAL
County Attorney Novak provided an aerial map to the Board for review <copy provided to
the Clerk for the record> of the Five Points Landfill site; stating that Five Points RC Flying
Club has requested from the Board continued support in allowing them to have access to
an area at Five Points Landfill site. He stated that he has drawn up a license agreement
for the Board’s consideration; reporting that the map shows the area that the Club will
have access for use. Commissioner McCroan motioned to approve a Five (5) Year
License Agreement and Memorandum of Understanding. Commissioner Rogers

seconded the motion. Chairman McDaniel called for public comment. There being no
public comment, the motion passed unanimously (4-0).
HURRICANE IRMA DECLARATION OF PERMIT WAIVER & RELIEF
County Attorney Novak discussed the proposed Hurricane Irma declaration of permit
waiver and relief. After discussion by Chairman McDaniel, Commissioner McCroan
motioned to approve a sixty (60) day Declaration of Permit Waiver and Relief due to
Hurricane Irma. Commissioner Rogers seconded the motion. Chairman McDaniel called
for public comment. Pat Hardman, President of Coastal Community Association appeared
before the Board to inquire if this waiver includes structures. Chief Administrator Butler
reported that this is a protective measure to protect the property owner’s structure; stating
that you can safeguard what you have. Chairman McDaniel called for public comment.
There being no further public comment, the motion passed unanimously (4-0).
S.H.I.P. DISBURSEMENT TO PACES – GATEWAY MANOR
County Attorney Novak reported that Gateway Manor is accepting applications; stating
that the complex is half-full. He also stated that the County has finalized the agreements
with the PACES Foundation and their subsidiary, The Gateway Manor of Port St. Joe;
reporting that the PACES Foundation has executed the Mortgage, Promissory Note, and
Agreements, which are ready to be recorded in the Official Records (in the Clerk’s Office).
County Attorney Novak stated that S.H.I.P. Administrator Paul has submitted the requests
for the first two (2) years funding of $87,500.00, for a total of $175,000.00; reporting next
year, per the agreement, if the funds are available the additional funding request will be
submitted, as the County’s pledge is $300,000.00. Commissioner Quinn so moved.
Commissioner Rogers second the motion. Chairman McDaniel called for public comment.
There being no public comment, the motion passed unanimously (4-0).
TRIUMPH / RESTORE FUND REPORT
Gulf County E.D.C. Director/RESTORE Act Coordinator Yeager discussed the Triumph
Board and various projects that would bring jobs to Gulf County. He requested the Board
to approve authorization for staff to work with stakeholders on the pre-application.
Commissioner McCroan motioned to allow staff to work with all the stakeholders to
prepare and submit the pre-application to the Triumph Board. Commissioner Rogers
seconded the motion. Chairman McDaniel called for public comment. There being no
public comment, the motion passed unanimously (4-0). Gulf County E.D.C.
Director/RESTORE Act Coordinator Yeager reported that the County has a little more
money in Pot I and will receive additional money in April, 2018; stating that acquisition
projects were put in the original M.Y.I.P. (Multi-year Implementation Plan) and some
appraisals have been completed. He reported that it is getting close to time for the Board
to make a decision; stating this needs to be discussed further at the next regular Board
meeting as to how the Board would like to proceed with the proposed projects.

There being no further business, and upon motion by Commissioner Quinn, second by
Commissioner Roger, and unanimous (4-0) vote, the meeting did then adjourn at 10:49
a.m., E.T.
