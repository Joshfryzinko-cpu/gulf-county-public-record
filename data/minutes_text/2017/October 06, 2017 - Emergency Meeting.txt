OCTOBER 6, 2017
PORT ST. JOE, FLORIDA
EMERGENCY MEETING
The Gulf County Board of County Commissioners met this date in emergency session
with the following members present: Chairman Ward McDaniel, Vice Chairman Sandy
Quinn, Jr., and Commissioners Jimmy Rogers and Phil McCroan <District I seat vacant;
awaiting appointment by Governor Scott>.
Others present were: County Attorney Jeremy Novak, Deputy Clerk Leanna Roberts,
Chief Administrator Don Butler, Assistant Administrator Michael L. Hammond, Deputy
Administrator Kari Summers, Gulf County E.D.C. Director/RESTORE Act Coordinator
Warren Yeager, Jr., Emergency Management Director Marshall Nelson, G.I.S.
Coordinator Jessica Susich, Public Works & Mosquito Control Director Mark Cothran, and
Sheriff Mike Harrison.
Chairman McDaniel called the meeting to order at 4:01 p.m., E.T.
Commissioner Quinn led in the Pledge of Allegiance to the Flag.
TROPICAL STORM NATE
Chairman McDaniel discussed the need for calling an emergency meeting to discuss
Tropical Storm Nate and possibly pass a local state of emergency. Emergency
Management Director Nelson appeared before the Board to provide an update on
Tropical Strom Nate; stating that there is a slight trend of shifting. He reported that this
storm is really moving fast; at the last forecast it was traveling at 21 (twenty-one) mph.
Emergency Management Director Nelson discussed the affects for Gulf County regarding
winds, possible tornadoes, and storm surge. Upon request by Emergency Management
Director Nelson, Commissioner McCroan motioned to issue a Local State of Emergency
due to Tropical Storm Nate. Commissioner Rogers seconded the motion. Chairman
McDaniel called for public comment. Pat Hardman, from the audience inquired about the
roads at the Cape and Indian Pass, and at what point the public will know when Painted
Pony could be used. Emergency Management Director Nelson stated they will know more
Monday morning. Emergency Management Director Nelson reported that the State Park
issued an evacuation due to Gulf County being placed under the Tropical Strom Watch.
Chairman McDaniel called for public comment. There being no further public comment,
the motion passed unanimously (4-0).
Assistant Administrator Hammond requested Board approval to give the Administrator
the authority to make decisions based on Emergency Management Director Nelson’s
recommendation, if needed, regarding evacuation so that the Board will not have to come
back for an emergency meeting. Commissioner Quinn motioned to approve this request.
Commissioner McCroan seconded the motion. Chairman McDaniel called for public

comment. There being no public comment, the motion passed unanimously (4-0).
Commissioner Rogers inquired whether the Board needs to recess this meeting. County
Attorney Novak stated that the Board has adopted the seven (7) day declaration and gave
the Administrator the authority to make the declaration, so there is no need to recess this
meeting. Upon inquiry by Commissioner McCroan, Emergency Management Director
Nelson stated the next update will be coming out in about 45 (forty-five) minutes. He
reported that as of now, E.O.C. is not activated, but might activate at a Level II depending
on how the storm sets up. Emergency Management Director Nelson stated that Duke
Energy has already setup in Apalachicola. After discussion by Chairman McDaniel,
Emergency Management Director Nelson discussed the automated phone system at
E.O.C. and information available on their Facebook page and website.
There being no further business, upon motion by Commissioner McCroan motioned,
second by Commissioner Quinn, and unanimous 4-0 vote, the meeting did then adjourn
at 4:17 p.m., E.T.
