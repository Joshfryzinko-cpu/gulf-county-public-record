GULF COUNTY PARKS SUBCOMMITTEE
10TH STREET PORT ST. JOE
JUNE 14, 2017
Attendees: County Commissioner Sandy Quinn, County Attorney Jeremy Novak, County
Administrator Don Butler, Assistant Administrator Michael Hammond, Deputy Administrator Kari
Summers, Public Works Director Mark Cothran, Assistant Public Works Director Lee Collinsworth,
T.D.C. Staff Assistant Kelli Godwin, Restore Coordinator/E.D.C. Director Warren Yeager, City
Manager Jim Anderson, City Budget and Finance Director Mike Lacour, and City Public Works
Director John Grantland.
Chairman Sandy Quinn called the meeting to order at 9:03 a.m., E.T.
City Mayor led the pledge of allegiance to the flag.
Chairman Quinn thanked everyone for attending the meeting.
10TH STREET PARK
County Attorney Novak discussed the meeting was publicly noticed and posted on the doors and
Courthouse and Administration Building. County Attorney discussed the process of forming the
Subcommittee, stating that the subcommittee will need to draft a plan, a project budget. He
discussed that this committee is only for the 10th Street project and T.D.C. needs to start
marketing for the park. City Manager Anderson discussed that the name of the park will be Benny
Roberts Park. Chairman Quinn discussed that the committee needs to have a master plan and
vision for the park. Discussion followed. City Manager Anderson discussed some of the issues are
the old existing pipe, parking, timing, field size and direction. City Public Works Director
Grantland discussed that the storm water issue needs to be addressed and the issue of parking
on the pipe. Upon inquiry by County Attorney Novak, City Manager Anderson discussed that the
park can be extended to 16th Street. Upon inquiry by City Public Works Director Grantland,
County Administrator Butler discussed that the City can apply for a FRDAP grant to help with the
funding. County Attorney Novak stated that he will work with City Attorney Clint McCahill on a
Interlocal Agreement. Restore Coordinator Yeager discussed it would be nice if the park could
be extended from the school to Highway 71. Chairman Quinn presented pictures of the Franklin
County Park for review. <Assistant Administrator Hammond entered the meeting at 9:27>. Public
Works Director Cothran discussed that the pipe replacement will be a major project. City Public
Works Director Grantland requested permission to work on a layout draft of the park for the next
meeting. The committee had no objections. County Attorney Novak discussed the need to have
a pledge of funds in the Interlocal Agreement for the City and County to be statutorily compliant,
stating that the fifth penny sunsets in 2 ½ years. Restore Coordinator Yeager discussed that the
new Grant Consultant can help find available grant funds. County Administrator Butler discussed
this is a long park, stating that we need to get the plans together, a list of the amenities and
permitting for pipes. City Manager Anderson discussed that he will contact Water Management
District for the possibility of available funding. County Attorney Novak discussed the possibility
of tying the City waterfront to the 10th Street park. He then read F.S. which provides connectivity

from bayside to parks. Chairman Quinn reported that the next meeting will be on Wednesday,
July 5th at 9:00 a.m., E.T. in the E.O.C. building.
ADJOURNMENT
There being no further business, the meeting was adjourned at 9:51 a.m., E.T.
