OPENING OF MEETING
Chairman Quinn was not able to attend. Vice Chairman Rex Buzzet called the meeting to order
at 9:07 a.m., E.T.
REVIEW OF NOTICE AND PUPLIC MEETING
Meeting minutes for October 19th, 2017 were reviewed. Mr. Collingsworth motioned for an
approval of the minutes presented, Mr. Anderson seconded, and it passed unanimously.
STATUS AND REPORT FROM DEWBERRY REPRESENTATIVE
Dewberry Representative Clay Smallwood explained that he was able to meet with The
Northwest Florida Water Management District. There were no initial issues seen, no
roadblocks, and they should be able to attain permits. The mitigation should also not be an
issue. Mr. Buzzet asked if a soil and muck survey had been done and Mr. Smallwood explained
that once a final footprint has been decided on that a survey will be done.
REPORT BY GULF COUNTY TOURIST DEVELOPMENT COUNCIL
T.D.C. Director Kelli Godwin and T.D.C. Parks and Recreations Director Billy Traylor went over
cost for some top elements that were decided on from a survey that was handed out at the
October 19th meeting. The top elements were baseball fields, pickleball courts, basketball
courts and pavilions.
AVAILABLE GRANT OPPURTINITES
Port St. Joe City Manager Jim Anderson noted that along with BOCC Grants Writer Lianna
Sagins, the City and County are working on the grant that will allow lighting along the trail. He
also stressed that once elements have been decided that the City and County can work on
grants.
MASTER PLAN AND BUDGET
The conceptual plan presented by Dewberry Engineers includes seven ball fields of varying
sizes, six pickle ball courts, two parking lots, three sets of batting cages, two bathrooms, press
boxes, and an area for mitigation. Mr. Buzzett commented he liked the conceptual plan
presented he also asked if there was anything else on the wish list as far as elements. Mrs.

Godwin noted that pavilions were high on the survey wish list. The discussion led to closing 10th
street. Mr. Buzzett explained that there have been commentaries on closing the road due to
the poor quality of the road. Mr. Buzzett commented that he would like to see a conceptual
plan from Dewberry if 10th street and potentially 8th street were to be closed. The committee
agrees that the plan needs to be in a phased approached so that people can still play on the
fields. The first step would be to start building the new aspects of the park from 10th street to
16th street then to work on the existing elements from 8th street to 10th street. Mr. Buzzett
commented that he thought the older part on 8th street would be removed and the new side
would be where tournaments would be held. Mr. Traylor agreed that having a new facility next
to older fields would be an eye sore. The question is if that would be enough fields to play on
for a tournament. Gulf County Public Works Assistant Director Lee Collingsworth noted that for
baseball and softball tournaments that the remaining four fields in the new facility would not
be enough. Mr. Buzzett asked the committee if they were okay with the elements presented in
the conceptual plan. The only element that was not included on conceptual plan from the
T.D.C. survey is basketball court. The next steps would be a new conceptual plan from
Dewberry with some of the new elements discussed during the meeting.
GENERAL COMMENTARY FROM COMMITTEE MEMEBERS
ESTABLISH THE NEXT MEETING DATE
It was decided to meet on January 16th, 9:00 in the Gulf County Emergency Management
Building.
There being no further business, the meeting was adjourned at 9:42 a.m., E.T.
