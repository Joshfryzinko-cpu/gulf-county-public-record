10TH STREET PORT ST. JOE

Administrator Don Butler, Assistant Administrator Michael Hammond, Deputy Administrator Kari
Summers, Public Works Director Mark Cothran, Assistant Public Works Director Lee Collinsworth,
T.D.C. Director Jennifer Adams, Restore Coordinator/E.D.C. Director Warren Yeager, City

Chairman Quinn called the meeting to order at 9:06 a.m., E.T.
City Mayor Patterson led the pledge of allegiance to the flag.
APPROVAL OF MINUTES
County Attorney Novak reported that the minutes will be email to all members and get approval
at the next committee meeting.
10TH STREET PARK
County Attorney Novak discussed the meeting was publicly noticed and posted on the doors of
the Courthouse and Administration Building. Chairman Quinn discussed hiring a consultant for
the 10th Street Park. City Commissioner Buzzett discussed that he does not want to spend the
money for a consultant when we already have plans for the Field of Dreams park. Chief
Administrator Butler discussed that some things must be done to move forward. City
Commissioner Buzzett stated that he had a conversation with City Public Works Director
Grantland concerning the location being in a residential area, and suggested looking at the Field
of Dreams plans that are already in place. Restore Coordinator/E.D.C. Director Yeager discussed
that it needs to be in a location that is good for the tourist and for the community. County
Attorney Novak discussed the letter received from Don Schumacher concerning building a sports
complex, stating that Mr. Schumacher suggested use the funds to improve parks with an
emphasis on enlarging boat ramps, docks and parking areas. County Attorney Novak discussed
that the City and County have pledged $600,000.00 each for the park. City Commissioner Buzzett
discussed that he would like to know what the difference would be on the 10th Street Park and
the Field of Dreams Park. County Attorney Novak discussed the cost of the feasibility study
ADJOURNMENT
There being no further business, the meeting was adjourned at 9:51 a.m., E.T.
