Chairman McDaniel called the meeting to order at 6:00 p.m., E.T.
Commissioner Rogers led in the Pledge of Allegiance to the Flag.
HURRICANE IRMA
Chairman McDaniel reported for the residents of Indian Pass, the County has staged sand
bags at the Indian Pass Raw Bar. He noted to the Board and to Emergency Management
Director Nelson his intent to not adjourn this meeting, but recess and reconvene at 8:00
a.m., E.T. tomorrow. Chairman McDaniel stated this will be addressed a little later (in the
meeting). Emergency Management Director Nelson reported that the storm has shifted a
little more to the West; going up the Westside of the State at the present moment. He
stated that if Hurricane Irma remains on the same track, Gulf County will receive minimum
impact, but will receive some tropical storm force wind action beginning Sunday night,
noting there will be some beach erosion, and the beach area storm surge is projected to
be 1-3 feet. Emergency Management Director Nelson reported that the water will pile up
in the Indian Pass Lagoon and as the storm goes by and when the wind shifts there will
be some build-up inside the Bay. He discussed concerns should the storm continue
moving toward Gulf County, noting things will then change. Emergency Management
Director Nelson reported that the County has things in place if the Board wants to look at
any type of evacuation or sheltering. Chairman McDaniel called for public comment. Pat

Hardman inquired about the rocks at the Stumphole. Emergency Management Director
Nelson stated that if the storm remains on track, water will be coming over the rocks with
possible asphalt damage. City of Port St. Joe Commissioner Thursbay, from the audience
inquired how employees will be notified if the bridges are closed. Emergency
Management Director Nelson reported that the bridges are closed when winds reach
45mph or when they become unsafe to pass over. He stated that a press release will go
out through E.O.C., but the Sheriff will make the determination if and when to close the
bridges. After discussion about various media outlets used to get the word out to the
public, Emergency Management Director Nelson discussed the possible flooding issue
that may occur two (2) weeks after the storm should Atlanta receive a lot of rainfall. Regina
Washabaugh asked if the Nursing Home has a plan. <Commissioner Quinn left the
meeting at 6:15 p.m., E.T.> Emergency Management Director Nelson stated that the
Nursing Home does have a plan; <Commissioner Quinn returned to the meeting at 6:16
p.m., E.T> reporting that they have a contract with the State Hospital in Chattahoochee,
and if residents have to be moved, they also have contracts with the Gulf County School
Board to use school buses and other vendors that supply transportation. Terri Pearce,
from the audience inquired about any backup in the Bay. Emergency Management
Director Nelson stated that he is not worried about it, and doesn’t know if it will happen.
Upon inquiry by Clerk Director of Finance & Management Woodward, Emergency
Management Director Nelson reported that today the E.O.C. went to a Level II activation.
Assistant Administrator Hammond recommended evacuating the State Park, Indian Pass,
Presnell’s Campground, and Indian Pass Campground. Emergency Management
Director Nelson stated that being that it is late in the day, he will contact those people so
they can start evacuating. He reported that Gulf County School Board Superintendent
has offered a place at the Port St. Joe Elementary School and Wewahitchka High School
for campers to park until after the event of the hurricane. Chairman McDaniel stated that
they can stage at the Dead Lakes Park at no charge. Upon inquiry by Commissioner
McCroan, Assistant Administrator Hammond stated that if the Board desires to evacuate
north of the Stumphole tomorrow, then it would be our recommendation to open the
shelter at that time; also discussing tomorrow the possibility of the County buildings
closing. Upon inquiry by Chairman McDaniel, Assistant Administrator Hammond stated
that Staff will have a recommendation in the morning regarding the shelter and closure of
the Gulf County Courthouse. <No Board action on the recommendation regarding the
evacuation>. Chairman McDaniel stated that the Board will reconvene tomorrow at 8:00
a.m., E.T.
The meeting did then recess at 6:25 p.m., E.T. on September 8, 2017.
The meeting did reconvene at 8:00 a.m., E.T. on September 9, 2017.
Emergency Management Director Nelson appeared before the Board to provide an
update regarding Hurricane Irma; reporting that the National Hurricane Center update
puts the storm track shifting a little more to the West. He stated for planning purposes,
his office is going to say that the storm will continue shifting West; reporting that on its

current tract, Gulf County will start receiving sustained tropical force winds Sunday night.
Emergency Management Director Nelson stated that Staff is looking for direction
regarding Government Offices closing, opening of shelters, and evaluations. He then
recommended that the Board close Government Offices on Monday (September 11,
2017). Upon motion by Commissioner McCroan, second by Commissioner Quinn, and
unanimous 4-0 vote, the Board closed Government Offices on Monday September 11,
2017, requesting the Constitutional Officers contact their staff regarding the closure.
Emergency Management Director Nelson requested the Board to consider making it
mandatory for all RVs (recreational vehicles) throughout the tourist corridor, which will
include Indian Pass, Simons Bayou, the Cape, and the tourist corridor at St. Joe Beach
up through Windmark. Emergency Management Director Nelson requested a momentary
recess.
The meeting then did recess at 8:04 a.m., E.T.
The meeting reconvened at 8:06 a.m., E.T.
Emergency Management Director Nelson addressed the mandatory evacuation of RVs
(recreational vehicles) along the tourist corridor; requesting approval to do a voluntary
evacuation of the general population in Zone A (anything on the waterside of U.S.
Highway 98, County Road 30A, State Road 30A which will include the Cape San Blas
area), and a mandatory evacuation North of the Stumphole of all residents and visitors.
Upon inquiry by Commissioner McCroan, Emergency Management Director Nelson
stated that this is his recommendation to the Board. Commissioner Rogers so moved.
Commissioner McCroan seconded the motion. Chairman McDaniel called for public
comment. Marsha Cassie, from the audience asked where the Stumphole is located.
Emergency Management Director Nelson provided a marker location so Ms. Cassie
would know where the Stumphole area was located. The motion then passed
unanimously (4-0). Emergency Management Director Nelson reported that Calhoun
County is planning on opening their shelter in Blountstown to general population at 4:00
p.m., E.T. today. He recommended the Board not open the Gulf County shelter at this
time, but discuss this topic again at the next meeting. Chairman McDaniel reported that
this meeting will shortly recess and the Board will reconvene at 6:00 p.m., E.T. this
evening and will address the Honeyville Community Center Shelter then. Upon
recommendation by Emergency Management Director Nelson, Commissioner Rogers
motioned to issue a voluntary evacuation for all people in mobile homes and people in
low lying areas. Commissioner Quinn seconded the motion. Chairman McDaniel called
for public comment. There being no public comment, the motion passed unanimously (4-
0). After discussion, Emergency Management Director Nelson reported that for the RVs
(recreational vehicles) there will be staging areas available at the Port St. Joe Elementary
School, Wewahitchka High School, and Dead Lakes Park. Clerk Norris reported that the
Gulf County Clerk’s Office and Court Operations have been suspended for Monday, as
Chief Judge Smiley, though order of the Supreme Court, closed all Court Operations for
the 14th Judicial Circuit. Emergency Management Director Nelson provided an update

regarding Hurricane Irma; reporting that the storm has had a minor shift back to the West.
He stated that National Hurricane Center’s latest projection will put the storm going in
somewhere around Key West and then right up the (West) Coast. After discussion by
Emergency Management Director Nelson, Marsha Cassie inquired about shelters where
they can go with pets. Emergency Management Director Nelson stated that Calhoun and
Gulf do not have pet friendly shelters.
The meeting then did recess at 8:21 a.m. on September 9, 2017.
The meeting reconvened at 5:59 p.m., E.T. on September 9, 2017.
Chairman McDaniel called Emergency Management Director Nelson to appear before the
Board to provide an update regarding Hurricane Irma. Emergency Management Director
Nelson presented a map for review <copy provided to the Clerk for the record> of the
possible track of the storm. He reported that once the storm reaches Georgia, there is
good news for our river system; stating that he will discuss the river system at a later date.
Emergency Management Director Nelson stated, as of right now, Gulf County is okay but
to expect winds to pick up and become gusty tomorrow afternoon with tropical storm force
winds arriving around midnight and lasting through Monday afternoon. After discussion
by Emergency Management Director Nelson, Chairman McDaniel stated there is no
significant change from this morning’s update. E.M.S. Director Whitfield appeared before
the Board to report that unless there are any changes, E.M.S. will stay where they are, if
there is bridge closures, then one ambulance will be left on this side of the bridge and
one in the White City area. He stated that Sacred Heart will be open. A woman from the
audience asked if the ambulance would take them should a patient want to go to Bay
County. E.M.S. Director Whitefield stated that the E.M.S. probably will not transport to
Bay; patients will be taken to Sacred Heart to stabilize and when the weather gets better
transport the patient out; noting there are exceptions to that. Emergency Management
Director Nelson reported that the shelter in Calhoun County remains open and has space
available; stating that the address is on the Emergency Management’s Facebook Page.
Commissioner Quinn discussed his concerns regarding the storm; encouraging people to
not let their guard down, be prepared, and continue to watch the storm. Upon inquiry by
Chairman McDaniel, Public Works Cothran discussed the reinforcement efforts at the
Stumphole. Chairman McDaniel called for public comment. Pat McGuire, from the
audience inquired about a possible storm surge in the Bay. Emergency Management
Director Nelson stated not inside the Bay; noting that the wave height is going to be on
the outside beach, Indian Pass down to Cape San Blas, and on down the Peninsula. He
reported that it will be choppy inside the Bay. Upon inquiry by Commissioner McCroan,
Emergency Management Director Nelson stated that the tides are built into the storm
surge predictions. Marsha Cassie, of the audience asked if the County has a storm surge
map. Emergency Management Director Nelson stated that the County does have storm
surge maps available online at www.flooddisaster.org; requesting they get together after
the meeting to discuss her specific situation. Employee of the T.D.C. inquired from the
audience regarding the electric companies. Emergency Management Director Nelson

discussed the two different electric companies within the County and their stage plan;
reporting that Duke Energy will not turn the power off unless it is due to safety issues.
Lamar Morris, from the audience inquired how many businesses have self-contained
capability in the effect of no power. Emergency Management Director Nelson stated that
he does not know how many, but there are some businesses that have generators and
some that have the ability to bring generators in. Pat Hardman, Coastal Community
Association President, from the audience inquired if the power will be turned off in the
mandatory evacuation areas. Emergency Management Director Nelson stated the power
will not be turned off. Ms. Hardman then asked at what point will the rocks (Stumphole)
be closed to traffic. Emergency Management Director Nelson reported it will be closed
when it becomes impassable. Discussion followed. Upon inquiry by Ms. Hardman,
Emergency Management Director Nelson reported that the County is not looking at
placing any additional areas under mandatory evacuation; noting the shelter in Gulf
County will not be opened but evacuees can utilize Calhoun County’s shelter. After
discussion, Mike Barrett, from the audience asked about F.E.M.A. and Urban Search &
Rescue. Emergency Management Director Nelson stated that Urban Search & Rescue
doesn’t necessarily come from F.E.M.A., they come from interlocal agreements between
Counties and groups in the State as well as between groups from other States. He
reported that Gulf County’s Urban Search & Rescue is in the State of Florida
(Jacksonville, Tampa, and Miami), but are being pulled from other States and are staged
in Tallahassee. Upon inquiry by Chairman McDaniel, Emergency Management Director
Nelson stated for routine questions please call the E.O.C.; reporting staff is available 24
hours by calling 850-229-9110. Following discussion and upon inquiry by Chairman
McDaniel, Emergency Management Director Nelson reported that the next full update
from the National Hurricane Center will be tomorrow at 11:15 a.m., E.T. Chairman
discussed recessing this meeting. County Attorney Novak recommended they recess this
meeting again. Emergency Management Director Nelson discussed his concern should
the Board decide to reconvene tomorrow; requesting they do it in the morning because
tomorrow afternoon is when the gusty winds are expected to start and he would rather
the public not be out on the roads. Emergency Management Director Nelson stated that
if a meeting is needed, he would be calling the Chairman. Chairman McDaniel stated that
he will allow Emergency Management Director Nelson decide if the Board needs to
reconvene tomorrow.
The meeting did then recess at 6:29 p.m., E.T. on September 9, 2017.
The meeting reconvened at 4:52 p.m., E.T. on September 12, 2017.
Emergency Management Director Nelson appeared before the Board to report that the
Local State of Emergency will expire tomorrow and recommended to let it lapse out. He
stated that the E.O.C. is back to a Level III Activation; reporting that all the special needs
patients are back within the County. He recommended that the Board close (adjourn) out
this meeting. Emergency Management Director Nelson stated that the cost at this time is
approximately $42,000.00; requesting Board approval for paying the salaries of personnel

expense out of the 127 Fund, which is the Emergency Disaster Fund, and all other cost,
such as, equipment and supplies will be paid out of each individual department’s budget.
Commissioner Rogers so moved. Commissioner McCroan seconded the motion. Chief
Administrator Butler reported that the money paid for personnel cost will be reimbursed
by F.E.M.A. that will go back into that fund (127). Chairman McDaniel called for public
comment. There being no public comment, the motion passed unanimously (4-0). Upon
discussion by Assistant Administrator Hammond, Commissioner McCroan motioned to
allow a one-time storm debris pickup, in house by Public Works, to be advertise to start
in two (2) weeks. Commissioner Quinn seconded the motion. Chairman McDaniel called
for public comment. Ms. Hardman, from the audience inquired about a start date.
Members of the Board stated this will start two (2) weeks from today. The motion passed
unanimously (4-0). Assistant Administrator Hammond reported that Staff will advertise
the start date regarding the one-time storm debris pickup.
There being no further business, and upon motion by Commissioner Quinn, second by
Commissioner McCroan, and unanimous 4-0 vote, the meeting did then adjourn at 4:58
p.m., E.T.
