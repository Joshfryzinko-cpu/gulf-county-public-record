T.D.C. BUDGET AMENDMENTS & RECOMMENDATIONS
Chief Administrator Butler discussed the Board’s decision regarding the expenses from
the 5th (fifth) Penny Bed Tax; stating that these expenses will be moved to the first three
(3) Pennies. He reported that Staff does not have a proposed budget for the Board to
consider today, but plan to have a budget amendment to present to the Board at the June
26th meeting. Chief Administrator Butler discussed the need to curtail some of the outside
marketing expenses and start doing some of the marketing in-house in order to make the
budget work. He stated that Staff would like to bring a proposed amended budget to the
Board based on these changes. Commissioner Whitfield motioned to accept Chief
Administrator Butler’s recommendation. Commissioner Quinn seconded the motion.
Chairman McDaniel called for public comment. Assistant Administrator Hammond
requested that part of the motion include terminating the No Luggage Contract and
formally start the process to provide a termination notice. Upon inquiry by Commissioner
McCroan, Assistant Administrator Hammond agreed that this is his recommendation.
Commissioner Whitfield agreed to amend his motion to add Assistant Administrator
Hammond’s recommendation. Chairman McDaniel called for public comment. T.D.C.
Chairman Warriner appeared before the Board to discuss his concerns regarding the 5th
(fifth) Penny; suggesting T.D.C. bring recommendations to the Board regarding these
changes, and the services provided by No Luggage. Commissioner Whitfield discussed
his concerns regarding the percentage spent on marketing and overhead. After
discussion, T.D.C. Chairman Warriner stated that the T.D.C. is charged by Statute to put
heads in beds. After further discussion, Assistant Administrator Hammond discussed
moving expenses from the 5th (fifth) Penny to the first three (3) Pennies, cutting out No
Luggage, not replacing Towan’s position (T.D.C. Deputy Director), and hiring two (2) local
people. County Attorney Novak reported that there is three (3) rule areas that the 5th (fifth)
Penny is being spent: (1) run the 5th Penny, which is administrative; (2) in the Ordinance
and Statute the County has to promote and market the 5th Penny for the purpose in which
it was adopted, which is a sports complex, and parks and recreation; and (3) development
of the project (Field of Dreams) across the hospital that the City of Port St. Joe owns. He
stated the primary focus for the past two (2) years has been two of the three items, which
is marketing and promotion, and administrative; with the discussion by the Board over the
last few months being what are the plans and development of the third (3rd) aspect of the
5th Penny. County Attorney Novak stated the expenditures have been compliant and
lawful, and in accordance with what the Statute and Ordinance requires. He reported that
the consultant agreement (with No Luggage) has an outstanding balance of $17,000.00
and the contract runs through September, 2017; stating that the vendor still has services
to provide and is entitled to the remaining balance under the contract. Discussion
followed. T.D.C. Chairman Warriner requested the Board allow T.D.C. to bring a
recommendation back to the Board before a decision is made. After discussion by
members of the Board, Chairman McDaniel called for public comment. City of Port St.
Joe Mayor Patterson appeared before the Board to report that the City Commissioners
voted to work with the County on the 10th Street Park Project, and that he has been
appointed to serve on the T.D.C. Board. Chairman McDaniel called for additional public
comment. There was no further public comment. After discussion by Commissioner
Whitfield, Chairman McDaniel requested that the motion be read back for the record.
Clerk Norris read the motion as: Commissioner Whitfield motioned to accept Chief

Administrator Butler’s recommendation to allow Staff to come back with a budget proposal
at the next regular Board meeting and then an amendment was made to take Assistant
Administrator Hammond’s recommendation to suspend the No Luggage Contract, which
Commissioner Whitfield had no objection to adding this recommendation to his motion.
Clerk Norris noted that we need to obtain an agreement to the amended motion by
Commissioner Quinn who seconded the motion. Commissioner Quinn voiced he had no
objection to the amended motion. The motion then passed unanimously. Commissioner
Quinn recommended holding a meeting with T.D.C. Executive Director Adams and
County Staff to come up with a plan to move forward. He then motioned to establish a
sub-committee with County Staff and County and City Commissioners to help chair this
vision. Commissioner McCroan seconded the motion for discussion. He then
recommended that Commissioner Quinn be appointed Chairman of this sub-committee.
The Board had no objections. Commissioner Quinn requested that Staff put something
together so the sub-committee can hold a meeting next week. Chairman McDaniel called
for public comment. There being no public comment, the motion passed unanimously.

SUSPENSION OF COUNTY PROCUREMENT POLICY – BEAR TRASH CAN GRANT
PROGRAM
Chief Administrator Butler discussed the grant with FWC to fund bear proof containers.
He reported that FWC gave the County direction to obtain quotes, which the County did,
and then FWC requested that the County advertise to receive sealed bids. He reported
that after advertising, no bids were received for the trash can modification kits (Bid #1617-
27) and the grant deadline is at the end of June, 2017. Chief Administrator Butler stated
that FWC will honor the grant if the County has the money encumbered by the end of the
month. He stated that in order for the money to be encumbered the Board would need to
agree to waive the normal bidding process and accept the best quote <copy provided to
the Clerk for the record>. Chief Administrator Butler reported that the County does not
have time to re-advertise to receive sealed bids before the grant deadline. Commissioner
Whitfield motioned to abandon the whole project and do whatever the County needs to
do to drop out of this program. Commissioner McCroan discussed his concerns regarding
the bear problem in District V. Commissioner Quinn discussed his concerns regarding the
funds being grant money and the presence of bears in District IV. Upon inquiry by
Chairman McDaniel, Chief Administrator Butler reported that the Gulf Aire Homeowners
Associations agreed to be the pilot program and every trash can in the subdivision will be
assigned a kit. Central Services Director Lanier reported that as long as the homeowner
opens the trash can by removing the hasps on the morning of pickup, then Waste Pro
would pick up the trash at no extra charge. Upon inquiry by Chairman McDaniel, Central
Services Director Lanier stated that if the trash can is not unlocked at the time of pickup,

Waste Pro will not collect the trash. Upon inquiry by Commissioner Rogers, Central
Services Director Lanier reported that the grant states that the homeowner will install the
hasps, except for the elderly and those that cannot install it themselves. She stated that
the County has asked some of the fire departments to volunteer to assist with these
installations. After further discussion, Chairman McDaniel called for public comment.
Coastal Community Association President Pat Hardman appeared before the Board to
discuss the program. Chairman McDaniel called for any additional public comment. There
was no further public comment. Chairman McDaniel called for a second to the motion.
The motion failed for a lack of a second. Commissioner McCroan motioned accept the
low quote (for the trash can modification kits). Commissioner Rogers seconded the
motion. County Attorney Novak noted that the County advertised to receive sealed bids,
following the Purchasing and Procurement Policy, but no bids were received (Bid #1617-
27). He stated the Board needs to entertain a motion, in the interest of time in having less
than a month to spend these grant funds, to suspend the County’s Purchasing and
Procurement Policy by reason of: (1) waste of the money; and (2) time being of the
essence. Commissioner McCroan so moved. Commissioner Rogers seconded the
motion. Chairman McDaniel asked Commissioner McCroan if he was pulling his motion
back and adding County Attorney Novak’s request to his motion. Commissioner McCroan
agreed in the affirmative. Upon inquiry by Chairman McDaniel, Central Services Director
Lanier stated that for the pilot program, residents (of Gulf Aire) will go to the Gulf Aire
Homeowners Association to collect the kits. She reported that the low quote is from
Bayside Lumber for 2,252 kits, in the amount of $25,413.79. Central Services Director
Lanier also reported that the funds for this grant came from the FWC bear kill permits.
Chairman McDaniel called for public comment. There being no public comment, the
motion passed unanimously.

CODE ENFORCEMENT – 111 GRIFFIN ROAD REPORT
Chairman McDaniel presented photos to the Board for review regarding a code
enforcement matter on 111 Griffin Road <copy provided to the Clerk for the record> in
Stone Mill Creek. Staff Assistant (former Code Enforcement Officer) Aiken appeared
before the Board to discuss the history of the case filed against the property owner;
reporting that Special Magistrate (Grover) signed an Order in favor of the Board of County
Commission. Staff Assistant Aiken reported that the property owner didn’t clean the site
within the allocated time; discussing options the County can take to clean the site.
Chairman McDaniel discussed his concerns regarding the cleanup cost and time at a
different site in the Williamsburg area. He stated that the County needs a policy that
addresses a legal way the County can go in and clean these sites. After discussion by
members of the Board, Commissioner Whitfield recommended that the County clean the
site (111 Griffin Road) and add the expense to the homeowner’s tax bill. He then
requested that County Attorney Novak draft a policy that would address this matter
moving forward. After discussion by Chairman McDaniel, County Attorney Novak stated
that he will advertise a Notice of Hearing to consider a proposed ordinance and policy to
be held at the June 26, 2017 Board meeting. Chairman McDaniel called for public
comment. There was not public comment.
