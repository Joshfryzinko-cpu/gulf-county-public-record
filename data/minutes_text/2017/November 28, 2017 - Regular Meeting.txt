PRESENTATION – BEACH RENOURISHMENT PROJECT
Michael Dombrowski, of MRD Associates appeared before the Board to provide a status
presentation regarding the Beach Renourishment Project <copy provided to the Clerk for
the record>; discussing five (5) options for the Board to consider, as follows: 1. Increase
funding; 2. Re-bid the project; 3. Use of offshore sand sources; 4. A Cape San Blas
borrow area; and 5. An upland borrow area. He recommended the Board consider an
ongoing MSTU over a period of time. Chairman McDaniel discussed the project;
suggesting the possible creation of a structure for purposes of healing or repairing the
shoreline itself. Members of the Board discussed their concerns regarding C.B.R.A.
(Coastal Barrier Resources Act), additional funding, the proposed structure, and upland
sand options. Chief Administrator Butler requested permission from the Board for to
obtain quotes on soil, to sale the over-burden soil at the Honeyville location, and to go
out for bid specifications on the removal the over-burden on the additional acreage (at the
Honeyville location). Upon request by Chief Administrator Butler, Commissioner McCroan
motioned to allow staff to obtain quotes and sale the over-burden soil that has already
been removed. Commissioner Rogers seconded the motion. Chairman McDaniel called

for public comment. There being no public comment, the motion passed unanimously.
Commissioner Quinn motioned to allow staff to form bid specifications and advertise for
sealed bids for someone to remove the additional over-burden. Commissioner Rogers
seconded the motion. After discussion by Commissioner McCroan, Chairman McDaniel
called for public comment. There being no public comment, the motion passed
unanimously.
ACQUISITION – CAPE SAN BLAS / EGLIN AIR FORCE BASE
Chief Administrator Butler discussed the possibility of beginning a conversation with Eglin
(Air Force Base) regarding acquisition of a piece of the old Coast Guard property, south
of the Stumphole under the beach renourishment project. Commissioner McCroan
motioned to allow staff to begin the conversation with Eglin regarding this acquisition.
Commissioner Rogers seconded the motion. Chairman McDaniel called for public
comment. There being no public comment, the motion passed unanimously.
