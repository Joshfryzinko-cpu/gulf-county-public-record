UPDATE / REPORT / PREPAREDNESS – HURRICANE IRMA
Emergency Management Director Nelson appeared before the Board to provide an
updated report regarding Hurricane Irma; reporting there has not been much change, but
it has drifted somewhat to the East. He stated, at this point, they cannot determine the
impact on Gulf County. Chairman McDaniel discussed listening to Emergency
Management Director Nelson direction regarding the actions for Gulf County. Upon
inquiry by Chairman McDaniel, Emergency Management Director Nelson stated that he
will have a more definitive answer by Friday; reporting the County will make the call in
plenty of time if an evacuation is needed. He encouraged the residents and business
owners to go ahead and prepare by putting a family plan in place, tapping-off their fuel
tanks, and having a ‘go kit’ ready. Chairman McDaniel discussed not opening any shelters
at this time based on what was discussed at today’s meeting. Emergency Management
Director Nelson discussed shelter options for in-county and out-of-county depending on
Hurricane Irma’s intensity and ultimate landfall. Chairman McDaniel asked about Tropical
Storms Jose and Katia. Emergency Management Director Nelson reported that Staff is
watching all three storms. Commissioner Quinn inquired as to where the shelters would

be located if there was an evacuation. Emergency Management Director Nelson reported
that the out-of-county shelters will be in Liberty, Calhoun, and Jackson Counties and the
special needs shelter is in Gadsden County at the State Hospital. Commissioner McCroan
directed Public Works Director Cothran to make sure the ditches on the South-end of the
County are clean and clear. Chairman McDaniel called for public comment. There was
no public comment.
DECLARATION OF LOCAL STATE OF EMERGENCY – HURRICANE IRMA
Emergency Management Director Nelson requested approval from the Board to declare
a local state of emergency; reporting that Gulf County is under an Emergency Declaration
by Governor Scott, which covers all 67 Counties, but would still request a Declaration of
a Local State of Emergency. Port St. Joe City Commissioner William Thursbay appeared
before the Board to inquire if the City of Port St. Joe needed to blanket the same as the
County. County Attorney Novak stated that the County’s jurisdiction, under the Statute,
gives the County the ability to declare a local state of emergency that effects the entire
County. He then read the proposed declaration of a local state of emergency for the
Board’s consideration. Commissioner McCroan motioned to declare a Local State of
Emergency for Gulf County due to Hurricane Irma. Commissioner Quinn seconded the
motion. Chairman McDaniel called for public comment. There being no public comment,
the motion passed unanimously (4-0).
There being no further business, Chairman McDaniel then adjourned the meeting at 4:20
