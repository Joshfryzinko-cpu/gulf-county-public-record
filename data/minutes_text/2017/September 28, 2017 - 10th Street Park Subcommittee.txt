GULF COUNTY PARKS SUBCOMMITTEE
10TH STREET PORT ST. JOE
SEPTEMBER 28, 2017
Attendees: County Commissioner Sandy Quinn, County Attorney Jeremy Novak, County
Administrator Don Butler, Deputy Administrator Kari Summers, Public Works Director Mark
Cothran, Gulf County Building Official Lee Collinsworth, BOCC Grants Writer Lianna Sagins, Gulf
County Clerks Office Representative Sherry Herring, T.D.C. Director Kelli Godwin, T.D.C. Parks &
Rec Director Billy Traylor, Restore Coordinator/E.D.C. Director Warren Yeager, Port St. Joe
Redevelopment Agency Director Bill Kennedy, Park Committee Member Jeri Ashcraft, Dewberry
Representative Clay Smallwood, City Manager Jim Anderson, City Commissioner Brett Lowry,
Port St. Joe Grant Writer Charlotte Pierce, and City Attorney Clinton McCahill.
OPENING OF MEETING
Chairman Quinn called the meeting to order at 9:00 a.m., E.T. Chairman Quinn commented that
the Board of County Commissioners approved moving forward with piping for 10th Street Park.
REVIEW OF NOTICE AND PUPLIC MEETING
County Attorney Novak reported that T.D.C. would now be in charge of notices and minutes
based on a request by Chairman Quinn. County Attorney Novak also introduced a new member
to the committee: Jeri Ashcraft. He also gave an updated committee list with addition of new
appointee.
INTRODUCTIONS AND COMMENTS FROM PSJDRA
PSJDRA Director Bill Kennedy discussed how he is trying to connect the waterfront with
downtown and ultimately Port City Trail and 10th Street Park. He also explained that some of
the ponds, including Buck Griffin Lake were paid for by environmental finds because the ponds
help collect water instead of it flowing all the way to St. Joseph Bay.
STUDY AND REPORT FROM DEWBERRY REPRESENTATIVE
Clay Smallwood explained that Dewberry would begin piping project at 8th street by the stack
house. The first thing will be assessing the project and developing cost. County Attorney Novak
discussed that the committee wanted to know what kind of footprint to they would be working
with to feasibly work towards a master plan and budget. Smallwood explained that there are
different thresholds for permitting and that it is better to stay in less than 1 acre of wetland
impact. County Attorney Novak requested that as soon as Dewberry finished with their studies,
mostly for the underground work that Dewberry works with city and county grant writers to
begin applying for grants.
REPORT BY GULF COUNTY TOURIST DEVELOPMENT COUNCIL
T.D.C. Director Kelli Godwin explained she reached out to a few different cities and counties
that have similar plans and budgets as Gulf County. The T.D.C. website has been updated to
reflect what the Port St. Joe City website says about 10th street park. There are also plans on
working with digital marketing to point people to website. The T.D.C. may be able to give some

funding for signs. Mrs. Godwin wanted to make sure that the park is branded under one name.
County Attorney Novak explained that the T.D.C. was given the job to recommend a name for
the park. Mrs. Godwin explained that name would be “Benny Roberts Park” for the time being.
She completed her report by explaining there are many opportunities in and out of market
through print and digital media as well as local kiosk for the park. Sherry Herring commented
that the biggest thing that needs to be done is a master plan to be able to start planning for
funding and an overall budget.
GENERAL COMMENTARY FROM COMMITTEE MEMEBERS
Mrs. Jeri Aschraft excited to be on board and thanked Chairman Quinn for the opportunity.
Parks and Recreations Director Billy encouraged cost estimates and solid numbers before the
planning really starts. County Attorney Novak discussed phased approaches and short-term list
of goals for the next 12 months. He commented on possible phases beginning with cleaning and
leveling so that the T.D.C. can start spending the fifth cent as well as marketing for the park
based on what phase one is. Kennedy agreed with leveling and cleaning and then let the land
surcharge for a year. Novak commented that the committee should focus on a 5 year plan and
have a calendar in place soon so that the County Commissioners can decide if they want to roll
the fifth penny past the 5-year sunset in 2019. There was some discussion on what needs to be
done before the next meeting to decide different phases. Parks & Recreations Director Billy
Traylor stressed that the committee needs to decide what they would like to be in the park and
then the T.D.C. will go and find costs for those elements. Mrs. Jeri Ashcraft noted the
committee should also focus on elements that could be used during shoulder season for visitors
as well as locals for example Pickleball courts. Mark Cothran commented that the placement of
the ditch and piping would determine where major structures would be able to be. There was
discussion about locations that could be used for Pickleball in the immediate future including
the high schools old tennis courts.
ESTABLISH THE NEXT MEETING DATE
It was decided to meet on October 19th 9:00 in the Gulf County Emergency Management
Building.
There being no further business, the meeting was adjourned at 9:40 a.m., E.T.
