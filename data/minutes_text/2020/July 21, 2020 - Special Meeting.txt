McCroan, and Commissioners David C. Rich and Ward McDaniel. Commissioner Jimmy
Rogers appeared by telephone.

Management Rhonda Woodward, Deputy Clerk Leanna Roberts, County Administrator

Jim McKnight, Gulf County E.M.S. Director Jody Daniels, County Engineer Clay
Smallwood, III, Grant Administrator Kaci Rhodes, Office of Management & Budget

PROPOSED BUDGET
County Administrator Hammond reported that the County does not have 100% of our
revenues and have waited until the last minute (to present); stating that the State has
been slow on getting their revenue estimates out on state shared revenues. OMB Director
Herring stated that each Commissioner has been provided a proposed budget book,
together with replacement pages correcting the mileage rate of the MSTUs <copy was
not provided to the Clerk for the record>. She discussed the proposed budget; reporting
that there may be changes and updates as they become available. OMB Director Herring
also discussed the new rates for FRS contributions, cost of living increases, and pay
adjustments that are already included; noting adjustments will need to be made for
elected officials’ salaries once that have been finalized and provided from the State. She
reported that the base request for all Constitutional Officers have been funded, which
includes the new FRS rates, insurance, HSA contributions, workers’ comp rate changes,
and a 3% cost of living for full time employees. OMB Director Hearing also reported that
the Sheriff’s budget includes a supplemental request of $50,000.00 (over the base
budget) for computer equipment and position overlap due to the upcoming retirement of
his Finance Director; noting the Sheriff’s budget also includes an additional $9,500.00 for
beach patrol which aligns the funding with projected revenue. She discussed the Property
Appraiser’s budget request, noting the budget request includes his retention of the funds
budgeted from last year’s overlap position of his Chief Deputy, and additional funds for a
two (2) year purchase agreement for a new software system (GSACAMA) that is
budgeted in the Capital Equipment Fund (301). OMB Director Herring requested Board
approval on revisions related to cash carried forward projections across all funds in order
to provide the best possible estimate, revisions related to the REC revenue estimates
across all funds, creation of a new Solid Waste Enterprise Fund, line item adjustments
for various expenditures in Reserves in order to appropriately account for everything and
balance those funds, revisions related to (cost of living) pay adjustments once evaluations
have been finalized, and the insurance once open enrollment is complete. Commissioner
McDaniel so moved. Commissioner Rich seconded the motion. Chairman Quinn called
for public comment and emails. There being none, the motion passed unanimously.

Chairman Quinn called for comments from the Constitutional Officers. County
Administrator Hammond reported that the Clerk (Norris) is in court this morning, unable
to attend today’s meeting, but she did not have any changes. Sheriff Harrison appeared
before the Board to discuss his request of $100,000.00 for additional servers for the entire
system, including E.M.S. and Fire Departments and $75,000.00 for a position overlap due
to his Finance Director retiring; requesting the full amount, if possible. County
Administrator Hammond discussed Sheriff Harrison’s request for the full amount; stating
that unless the Board wants to cut on the expenditure side and keep the same mileage
rate, staff is going to have to look harder. He reported that he has not heard from
Supervisor of Elections (Hanlon), but Tax Collector Jenkins said she was good. County
Administrator Hammond reported that all outside agencies were either the same as last
year or went down, no additional requests were funded from outside agencies. OMB
Director Herring stated that only one was FRS related for the change and then there was
some for court technology. County Administrator Hammond recommended that the Board
set the exact millage at 7.100 for the Board (County-wide) rate, St. Joe Fire Control
District at .5000 mill, Tupelo Fire Control District at .5000 mill, Overstreet Fire Control
District at .5000 mill, and Howard Creek Fire Department District at .5000 mill. He reported
that the MSTU for Gulf Front went down to 1.0232, 0.7785 for Interior, and 0.8175 for Bay
Side. Commissioner McDaniel discussed the need for the County to look at having a
(E.M.S.) vehicle in the Cape area during the summer months and the need to hire more
full time paramedics. E.M.S. Director Daniels appeared before the Board to discuss
staffing schedules. Commissioner McDaniel discussed the daily trips made outside of the
County; <Commissioner Rich left the meeting at 10:25 a.m., E.T.> stating the County
needs to hire more paramedics. Discussion followed by members of the Board regarding
this matter. County Administrator Hammond was in agreement for the need of another
paramedic; bringing the Board’s attention to the E.M.S. revenue and expenses projected
in next year’s budget. He reported that the County is projected to lose $410,000.00, not
counting ambulances, utilities, overhead, or liability insurance. <Commissioner Rich
returned to the meeting at 10:27 a.m., E.T.> County Administrator Hammond discussed
the contract with Bay County for E.M.S. coverage for the City of Mexico Beach.
Discussion followed regarding this agreement with Bay County. Commissioner McDaniel
inquired if Chairman Quinn would agree to reach out to Bay County to negotiate a new
contract. County Attorney Novak reported that the contract requires a notification;
requesting permission from the Board to send them notice under the agreement and then
start working on negotiations. Chairman Quinn called for a motion that the County send
a letter via County Attorney Novak regarding the ambulance contract. Commissioner
McDaniel made the motion. Commissioner McCroan seconded the motion. After
discussion, Chairman Quinn called for public comment and emails. There being none, the
motion passed unanimously. Commissioner McDaniel recommended the Board keep the
millage rate the same at 7.100 mills for FY2020-21 (County-wide). Chairman Quinn called
a motion. Commissioner McDaniel moved to leave it where it is. Commissioner Rich
seconded the motion. Chairman Quinn called for public comment and emails. There being
none, the motion passed unanimously. County Administrator Hammond requested the
Board to approve setting all the Fire Control Districts at half mill each (.5000).
Commissioner McDaniel so moved .5000 mill. Commissioner McCroan seconded the
motion. Chairman Quinn called for public comment and emails. There being none, the

motion passed unanimously. County Administrator Hammond requested the Board set
the MSTU Gulfside Beachfront at 1.0232 mills. Commissioner McCroan so moved.
Commissioner McDaniel seconded the motion. Chairman Quinn called for public
comment and emails. There being none, the motion passed unanimously. County
Administrator Hammond requested that the Board set the MSTU Gulfside Interior rate at
