JUNE 17, 2020
SPECIAL MEETING
PORT ST. JOE, FLORIDA
The Gulf County Board of County Commissioners met this date in special session with
the following members present: Chairman Sandy Quinn, Jr., Vice Chairman Phil
McCroan, and Commissioners David C. Rich, Ward McDaniel, and Jimmy Rogers.
Others present were: County Attorney Jeremy Novak, Clerk Rebecca L. Norris, Clerk
Director of Finance & Management Rhonda Woodward, County Administrator Michael L.
Hammond, County Assistant Administrator Warren Yeager, Jr., Deputy Administrator Kari
Summers, County Engineer Clay Smallwood, III, Office of Management & Budget Director
Sherry Herring, and Assistant Public Works Director Raymond Hart.
Chairman Quinn called the meeting to order at 2:00 p.m., E.T.
Chairman Quinn led in the Pledge of Allegiance to the Flag.
GOLF COURSE INVOICE
Assistant County Administrator Yeager appeared before the Board to discuss the closure
of the Hombre Golf Course; reporting they have excess equipment that Gulf County
needs and the course owners have offered the County the opportunity to purchase all
surplus equipment for $25,000.00. County Attorney Novak advised that if the Board
deems this purchase an emergency, then a motion would be in order to suspend the
procurement (policy) for the purpose of the emergency condition of the (Golf) Course.
Commissioner McCroan inquired if the funds would come from the Country Club fund.
Assistant County Administrator Yeager reported that the Country Club does have the
money to make this purchase. Chairman Quinn called for a motion to suspend the
(Purchase and Procurement/Bid) Policy. County Attorney Novak requested that the Board
include this equipment being sole-source. Chairman Quinn then called for a motion to
suspend the Policy. Commissioner McDaniel made the motion. Commissioner Rogers
seconded the motion. Chairman Quinn called for public comment and emails. There being
none, the motion passed unanimously. Chairman Quinn called for a second motion to
purchase the equipment, sole-source and emergency for preservation of the (Golf)
Course. Commissioner Rich made the motion. Commissioner McCroan seconded the
motion. Chairman Quinn called for public comment and emails. There being none, the
motion passed unanimously. County Administrator Hammond asked if the record reflects
from whom the County is making the purchase. Clerk Norris confirmed Hombre
Development, LLC is who the County will be purchasing the equipment from. County
Administrator Hammond discussed the progress at the Golf Course; reporting that they
are looking to have a Grand Re-Opening on September 11, 2020.

ADMINISTRATIVE UPDATE & REPORT
County Administrator Hammond requested to hold a special meeting on July 1, 2020 to
consider proposed ordinances regarding a land use change, County Administrator
(appointment, powers, duties and responsibilities), and a small-scale map amendment;
stating there will be at least four (4) to consider on this date. Chairman Quinn called for a
motion to hold a special meeting on July 1, 2020 at 9:00 a.m., E.T. Commissioner
McCroan so moved. Commissioner McDaniel seconded the motion. Chairman Quinn
called for public comment and emails. There being none, the motion passed unanimously.
County Administrator Hammond reported that the County has been approached to take
over Lighthouse Water Utilities; stating that Water Management wants to help with this
matter. He requested permission from the Board the ability to negotiate (with the owners)
and bring recommendations back before the Board. Commissioner McCroan discussed
his concerns about the County getting in the water business, but is willing to see what
recommendations County Administrator Hammond brings before the Board to consider.
After discussion regarding the water wells and usage, Commissioner McDaniel inquired
about whether a study had been done regarding future customer base. County
Administrator Hammond reported that currently there are approximately 2,000 customers
and the H.C.P. (Habitat Conservation Plan) study shows the potential 2,000 lots to be
developed. Commissioner McDaniel motioned to allow the County Administrator, County
Attorney and whatever staff look into this and report back to the Board. Commissioner
Rich seconded the motion. Chairman Quinn called for public comment and emails. There
being none, the motion passed unanimously. County Administrator Hammond stated that
in the event the County does get back in the water business, this is not something that is
going to be subsidized by the Ad Valorem Taxpayer; reporting this will be an enterprise
fund, stand alone, the users and customers will be paying the associated cost. County
Attorney Novak stated that once a recommendation is brought before the Board a
proposed resolution will be presented for consideration.
Commissioner McDaniel congratulated Clerk Norris and Commissioners McCroan and
Rich on their re-election.
Commissioner McCroan discussed the wake problems around Raffield’s and Wood’s
Fisheries; questioning if County Administrator Hammond could move forward with the No
Wake Zones, including Overstreet and White City. Commissioner McDaniel discussed
the locations being in Highland View, near Raffield’s and Wood’s Fisheries, White City
and Overstreet. County Administrator Hammond discussed getting a vote to advertise (for
a proposed ordinance) to present at the July regular meeting; stating that FWC (Fish and
Wildlife Commission) is the only agency to enforce it and the need to work with the FWC’s
attorney regarding the wording of the proposed ordinance. Commissioner McCroan made
the motion. Commissioner Rogers seconded the motion. Chairman Quinn called for public
comment. There being no public comment, the motion passed unanimously.
County Engineer Smallwood appeared before the Board to provide updates regarding
various projects within the County. County Administrator Hammond provided updates to
the Eastern Shipbuilding Project and the Veterans’ Memorial Beacon Hill Park Project.

There being no further business, and upon motion by Commissioner McDaniel, second
by Commissioner McCroan, and unanimous vote, the meeting did then adjourn at 2:28
p.m., E.T.
