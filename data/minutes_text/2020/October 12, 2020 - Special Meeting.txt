OCTOBER 12, 2020
SPECIAL MEETING
PORT ST. JOE, FLORIDA
The Gulf County Board of County Commissioners met this date in special session with
the following members present: Chairman Sandy Quinn, Jr., Vice Chairman Phil
McCroan, and Commissioners David C. Rich, and Jimmy Rogers <Commissioner Ward
McDaniel was absent>.
Others present were: County Attorney Jeremy Novak, Clerk Rebecca L. Norris, Deputy
Clerk Leanna Roberts, County Administrator Michael L. Hammond, County Assistant
Administrator Warren Yeager, Jr., Deputy Administrator Kari Summers, Code
Enforcement Officer Trevor Seay, Gulf County E.D.C. Director Jim McKnight, Emergency
Management Director/Fire Coordinator Matt Herring, County Engineer Clay Smallwood,
III, Grant Administrator Kaci Rhodes, Office of Management & Budget Director Sherry
Herring, Mosquito Control Director Austin Horton, Public Works Director Mark Cothran,
Assistant Public Works Director Raymond Hart, Sheriff Mike Harrison, T.D.C. Executive
Director Kelli Godwin, and Veterans' Service Office Director & S.H.I.P. Director Joe Paul.
Sheriff Harrison called the meeting to order at 4:00 p.m., E.T.
Chairman Quinn opened the meeting with prayer and led in the Pledge of Allegiance to
the Flag.
OPEN BID #1920-48 – INSTALL IRRIGATION PUMP STATION / GOLF COURSE
Deputy Clerk Roberts opened the sealed bids for Bid #1920-48 for the installation of a
irrigation pump station for the Golf Course (Country Club), with Grant Administrator
Rhodes reading aloud the proposals, as follows: 1) Premier Golf Group, in the amount
of $70,000.00; 2) Premier Golf Group, in the amount of $77,000.00; 3) Storm
Construction, LLC, in the amount of $142,000.00; 4) Hub Diving Services, in the amount
of $43,600.50 for dredging and $84,659.55 for the irrigation & pump station; and, 5) North
Florida Construction, Inc., in the amount of $427,885.00. County Administrator Hammond
recommended that staff be giving time to review the submitted bids and bring a
recommendation back before the Board later in the meeting.
REJECT & READVERTISE BID #1819-37 – DEAD LAKES PARK CAMPGROUND
OPERATIONS, DEVELOPMENT & ADMINISTRATION
County Administrator Hammond recommended to reject (rescind) Bid #1819-37 for the
Dead Lakes Park Campground Operations, Development and Administration and re-
advertise; and to include Howard Creek (Lower Landing). Commissioner McCroan
motioned to reject bid and re-advertise. Commissioner Rogers seconded the motion.
Chairman Quinn called for public comment and emails. There being none, the motion
passed unanimously (4-0).

RESTORE ACT GRANT AGREEMENT – ACQUISITION OF HOWARD CREEK
LOWER LANDING
OMB Director Herring requested the Board ratify the acceptance of the RESTORE Act
Grant for the acquisition of Howard Creek Lower Landing. Commissioner Rogers so
moved. Commissioner Rich seconded the motion. Commissioner McCroan inquired
whether staff wanted to read submitted emails. After confirmation by Chairman Quinn,
County Attorney Novak then read an email from Pat Hardman, of Cape San Blas
questioning the acquisition of Howard Creek Lower Landing <copy on file with the Clerk
for the record>. County Administrator Hammond provided the answers to Ms. Hardman’s
questions regarding the purchase. County Attorney Novak then read aloud an email from
Lissa Dulany, of Cape San Blas with questions regarding the assessed value. Discussion
followed regarding having three (3) appraisals completed for this parcel. Chairman Quinn
called for public comment. Tom Semmes, of Wewahitchka appeared before the Board to
discuss the last appraisal (assessed value) from the Property Appraiser, the condition of
the property, and the ownership of the property. He stated the Board needs to delay the
purchase and find out who owns this property. Chairman Quinn called for a motion to
extend Mr. Semmes time. No motion was placed on the floor. County Administrator
Hammond stated that there is 15 acres of upland, the purchase price is $610,000.00, and
he believes no one would sale their property at the Property Appraiser’s appraisal. He
also stated that the County Attorney Novak can discuss the title; reporting the reason it
changed over was because Billy Joe Rish passed away. Following discussion, County
Attorney Novak discussed the title owners and documents to U.S. Treasury; stating the
title has been examined. Commissioner McCroan asked that it be stated where the funds
were coming from for this purchase. County Administrator Hammond reported that the
funds are coming from BP Pot 1 money. Chairman Quinn called for public comment and
emails. There being no further public comment, the motion passed unanimously (4-0).
H.H.R.P. / S.H.I.P.
S.H.I.P. Director Paul appeared before the Board to report after reverifying Marjorie
Phillips’ income, she is over the very low income and now in the low income category. He
requested that the Board continue to approve the $45,000.00 for this applicant under the
low income category. OMB Director Herring discussed her concerns regarding having
more applicants than available funding in the low income category and if this is approved,
then there will be an applicant in the low income that will not receive funding. S.H.I.P.
Director Paul stated that there are more applications in low income than there is funding
for this category. OMB Director Herring voiced her concerns of over funding the low
income category; noting it can only come from the moderate level. After discussion,
Chairman Quinn directed S.H.I.P. Director Paul to sit down with OMB Director Herring
and bring a recommendation back before the Board at the special meeting to be held on
Thursday.
S.H.I.P. Director Paul requested the Board to approve a maximum of $60,000.00 for the
S.H.I.P. Project for Bertha Quinn (Bid #1920-39 * awarded to Gulf 2 Bay Construction);
stating he misspoke at a previous meeting regarding a maximum allowance of
$40,000.00. Commissioner Rogers so moved. Commissioner Rich seconded the motion.
Chairman Quinn called for public comment and emails. There being none, the motion

passed unanimously, 3-0 with Chairman Quinn abstaining due the applicant being his
aunt by marriage <FORM 8B on file with Clerk for the record>.
ADMINISTRATIVE UPDATE & REPORT
After discussion by County Administrator Hammond about the woodchip operation at the
mill site, E.D.C. Director McKnight appeared before the Board to first report that members
of the Board will soon receive an invitation to attend an announcement by federal
authorities next Tuesday morning at 10:00 a.m. at the Methodist Church. Regarding the
woodchips update, he stated the woodchips will be here by midnight; noting there are
three (3) barges on the Intercoastal waterway headed to Port St. Joe that will eventually
be shipped to Honduras. E.D.C. Director McKnight also provided a status update on
various other projects (Sky Borne Technology and Gulf-Franklin Nursing Simulation
Center) within the County.
County Administrator Hammond introduced Matt Herring as the new Emergency
Management Director and Fire Coordinator. Emergency Management Director and Fire
Coordinator Herring appeared before the Board to thank the Board for the opportunity to
come aboard; reporting on his plan to have meetings with the fire departments in the
upcoming months.
County Administrator Hammond recommended that the Board give Bay County Board of
County Commissioners two (2) weeks to answer the County’s proposal regarding E.M.S.
Service for Mexico Beach; stating that if they do not want to agree to the terms, then they
need to provide their own services. He stated that if the Board has no objections, then
staff will send a letter requesting their answer within two (2) weeks. Commissioner
McCroan requested that Assistant Administrator Yeager call one more time before a
decision is made.
County Administrator Hammond requested the Chairman to call a Special Meeting on
Thursday (October 15, 2020) at 11:00 a.m., E.T. to close the bond (purchase Lighthouse
Utilities-LUCI). Chairman Quinn called for a motion to hold a Special Meeting on Thursday
at 11:00 a.m., E.T. Commissioner Rich so moved. Commissioner McCroan seconded the
motion. Chairman Quinn called for public comment and emails. There being none, the
motion passed unanimously (4-0).
AWARD BID #1920-48 – INSTALL IRRIGATION PUMP STATION / GOLF COURSE
Assistant Administrator Yeager appeared before the Board to request the Board award
tentatively to the low bidder, Premier Golf Group and negotiate the amount down within
the budgeted range. Commissioner McCroan so moved. Commissioner Rogers
seconded the motion. Assistant Administrator Yeager asked that the motion include
allowing the Chairman to sign all necessary documents. Upon inquiry by Chairman Quinn,
Commissioner McCroan had no objection to the addition. Chairman Quinn called for
public comment and emails. There being none, the motion passed unanimously (4-0).

ADMINISTRATIVE UPDATE & REPORT
T.D.C. Director Godwin appeared before the Board to provide a status report regarding
T.D.C.; noting that the months of July and August are the highest the County has ever
had. County Administrator Hammond requested that T.D.C. help with the dedication at
the Beacon Hill Veterans’ Memorial Park. Discussion followed regarding the dedication
and (Highway 98) road name changes on the same date (Willis Rowan and John C.
Gainous). At the direction of Commissioner McCroan, County Administrator Hammond
agreed to reach out to Rodney Herring.
There being no further business, and upon motion by Commissioner Rogers, second by
Commissioner McCroan, and unanimous 4-0 vote, the meeting did then adjourn at 4:56
p.m., E.T.
