SEPTEMBER 14, 2020
BUDGET HEARING
PORT ST. JOE, FLORIDA
The Gulf County Board of County Commissioners met this date in special budget session
with the following members present: Chairman Sandy Quinn, Jr., Vice Chairman Phil
McCroan, and Commissioners Ward McDaniel and Jimmy Rogers. <Commissioner David
C. Rich appeared by telephone.>
Others present were: County Attorney Jeremy Novak, Clerk Rebecca L. Norris, Deputy
Clerk Leanna Roberts, County Administrator Michael L. Hammond, County Assistant
Administrator Warren Yeager, Jr., Deputy Administrator Kari Summers, County Engineer
Clay Smallwood, III, Grant Administrator Kaci Rhodes, Office of Management & Budget
Director Sherry Herring, Mosquito Control Director Austin Horton, Public Works Director
Mark Cothran, Assistant Public Works Director Raymond Hart, Sheriff Mike Harrison, and
T.D.C. Executive Director Kelli Godwin.
Chairman Quinn called the meeting to order at 5:03 p.m., E.T.
PUBLIC COMMENT
County Administrator Hammond reported the meeting is being live streamed and there is
also available seating to attend in person; stating that no one has signed up to speak. He
requested the Board wait a few minutes before starting to allow the public to connect to
the live stream. Chairman Quinn had no objections; confirming that the public could attend
this meeting as seats are available. Upon inquiry by Chairman Quinn, County
Administrator Hammond stated that public comment can be submitted by email; providing
the email address for the public.
TENTATIVE 2020-21 OVERVIEW
OMB Director Herring stated that each member of the Board received a copy of the
proposed budget book and proposed resolutions <copy provided to the Clerk for the
record>. She addressed the Board with the following information: 1) the proposed
aggregate millage rate is 7.4935, which is 7.89% over the current year aggregate rolled
back rate of 6.9452; 2) the county-wide proposed tentative millage rate is 7.1000; 3) the
Dependent Fire Districts; St. Joseph, Tupelo, Overstreet, and Howard Creek are all at
0.5000 mills; and 4) the proposed M.S.T.U. Debt Service millage rates are: Gulfside
Beachfront at 1.0232, Gulfside Interior at 0.7785, and Bayside at 0.8175. OMB Director
Herring stated the following reasons the ad valorem tax revenues are increasing: 1) the
Triumph agreement to keep millage rates the same as prior year lowered the rolled back
rate; 2) Interlocal Agreement with City of Port St. Joe for the first (1st) of three (3) additional
stipend payments; 3) Property Appraiser capital outlay for the first (1st) of a two (2) year
contribution for a new software system; 4) first (1st) full year funding of County Engineer;
5) Sheriff’s Office increases; 6) Jail termination leave payouts; 7) legal services; 8) FRS

retirement cost; 9) health insurance cost and; 10) pay raises. She reported that the
proposed tentative budget has been provided to each Commissioner <copy provided to
the Clerk for the record> and posted on the County’s website as required for this Public
Hearing. OMB Director Herring then provided additional information, as follows: 1) page
2 provides the balanced totals of each fund; 2) Hurricane Michael Funding Relief from
Triumph and the State of Florida helped to mitigate the financial impacts of revenue loss
and initial recovery, but these funds will end on September 30, 2020; 3) insurance
settlement funds have been carried forward and reserved until those expenditures are
incurred due to the unknown timing of many repair projects; 4) growth and stability of
reserves are necessary to maintain and improve credit worthiness on current and future
bond issues. Additionally, the County must continue to cash flow the anticipated
reimbursements for insurance deductibles, F.EM.A. waiver for local match and
improvements not covered by F.EM.A. or insurance with our reserves. She requested a
motion of the Board to allow her to make clerical adjustments and changes from recent
open enrollment and contract services that would change line items, but not change the
funds before the Final Hearing. Commissioner McDaniel so moved. Commissioner
Rogers seconded the motion. Chairman Quinn called for public comment and emails.
There being none, the motion passed unanimously. County Administrator Hammond
discussed the completed proposed budget; asking if members of the Board had questions
regarding the proposed budget. There were not questions from members of the Board.
After discussion by County Administrator Hammond, Chairman Quinn called for public
comment from the audience and online emails. There were none. County Administrator
Hammond recommended the Board close time for comment to the public and move on to
the adoption of the millage rates. Chairman Quinn closed the tentative 2020-21 budget
hearing to the public.

FINAL BUDGET HEARING – SEPTEBMER 23, 2020
OMB Director Herring reminded the Board that the Final Budget Hearing will be held on
Wednesday, September 23, 2020 at 5:01 p.m., E.T. She stated that there will be an
