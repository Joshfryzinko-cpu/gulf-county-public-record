LIGHTHOUSE UTILITIES
County Consultant Butler appeared before the Board to present recommendations <copy
provided to the Clerk for the record> regarding the acquisition of Lighthouse Utilities. He
discussed his recommendation to the Board for consideration, as follows: 1) Purchase
Lighthouse Utilities, including land and all assets associated with the operation, for a sum
of $3 million, contingent upon an appraisal before closing; 2) Move forward with bonding
to allow for the $3 million purchase, plus $1 million for improvements; 3) Order and
consider a proposal from Dewberry Engineers to provide bid procurement documents and
oversight for the project; 4) Immediately upon receipt of bid documents, advertise to
received sealed bids for necessary work; and, 5) Approve signatory authority to allow
either the Chairman or the County Administrator to sign documents related to this project.
Upon inquiry by Commissioner McCroan regarding the timeline, County Consultant Butler
explained that the County cannot legally back a private loan (loan guarantee), as originally
intended, therefore the acquisition timeline was moved from three (3) years to three (3)
months. County Attorney Novak discussed Article 7, Section 10 of the Florida Constitution
that prohibits the pledge of public dollars to back a private enterprise. He noted that in
order to be in control and meet the immediate infrastructure and capital improvement
repairs needed, the County had to step away from the original timeline and find a way to
do it lawfully under a conditional procurement. Discussion followed regarding regulatory
authority, water capacity and pressure, system funding, and Board member’s concerns
regarding the desire to have both the Chairman and the County Administrator sign all

related documents. County Attorney Novak then read the proposed resolution, with the
desired signatory authority changes, for the Board to consider. Commissioner McDaniel
motioned to move forward. Commissioner McCroan seconded the motion for discussion;
inquiring about the signatory authority changes and the regulatory authority. County
Attorney Novak confirmed the changes to signatory authority; noting due diligence and
his intent to come back before the Board for approval prior to a letter being sent (to the
regulatory authority). Commissioner McCroan confirming his second stands. Chairman
Quinn then called for public comment and emails. There being none, the motion passed
unanimously, as follows:

ADMINISTRATIVE UPDATE & REPORT
Building Inspector Bailey appeared before the Board to report that the Coast Guard wants
to replace the beacon at Veterans’ Memorial Beacon Hill Park that was destroyed during
Hurricane Michael; stating the location is on County property. County Consultant Butler
appeared before the Board to report that the County assumed ownership of Beacon Hill
Park in the mid 1980’s with the beacon on it. He noted the desire of the Coast Guard to
replace the beacon at its original location; discussing the property lines, the LDR’s, and
the P.D.R.B.’s recommendation to bring this matter before the Board. Commissioner
McDaniel discussed his desire to preserve the history of the beacon; noting his support
to replace it. County Attorney Novak requested for the Board to include in their motion for
approval the conditional approval of the Chairman to sign a temporary construction
easement. Commissioner McCroan so moved to what County Attorney Novak said.
Commissioner McDaniel seconded the motion. Chairman Quinn called for public

comment. After discussion, Chairman Quinn called for public comment and emails. There
being none, the motion passed unanimously.
There being no further business and upon motion by Commissioner McDaniel, second by
