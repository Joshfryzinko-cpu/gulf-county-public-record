PUBLIC HEARING – GULF COUNTY NO WAKE ZONE ORDINANCE
Pursuant to advertisement to hold a Public Hearing to consider a proposed no wake zone
ordinance, County Attorney Novak read the proposed ordinance by title and highpoints.
County Administrator Hammond stated that he assumes the County will ask Florida
Marine Patrol (Florida Fish and Wildlife Commission) to enforce this, even though it says
the Sheriff; noting the Sheriff does not have a fleet of boats to patrol. Commissioner
McCroan said yes. After discussion, Commissioner McDaniel inquired about the purchase
of buoys. County Administrator Hammond stated that staff will ask to work with Florida
Marine Patrol (FWC), but the County may have to purchase them. Discussion followed
regarding posting signs and enforcement. <Commissioner Rich left the meeting at 10:14
a.m., E.T.> Sheriff Harrison appeared before the Board to inquiry if the language
regarding the Sheriff’s Office could be removed and just include FWC (Florida Wildlife
Commission) until the Sheriff’s Office has the mechanism to enforce it. <Commissioner
Rich returned to the meeting at 10:16 a.m., E.T.> Discussion followed regarding
amending the language regarding enforcement. County Attorney Novak read the section
regarding speed limits into the record. Chairman Quinn opened the Public Hearing to the
public; calling for any emails or comments from the audience. There being none,
Chairman Quinn closed the Public Hearing to the public. Commissioner McDaniel so
moved to move forward with this Ordinance. Commissioner McCroan seconded the
motion. Chairman Quinn called for public comment and emails. There being none, the
motion passed unanimously, as follows:
