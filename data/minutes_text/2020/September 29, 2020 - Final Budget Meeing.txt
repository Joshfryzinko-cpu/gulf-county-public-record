2020-21 BUDGET OVERVIEW
County Administrator Hammond stated that staff will wait a few minutes to allow for the
public to attend (by connecting to) live stream online; reporting that questions can be
sent in via email. He reported that staff has received one (1) email thus far to read aloud
during public comment. For the record, OMB Director Herring stated the following
regarding the 2020-21 Budget: 1) the tentative aggregate millage rate is 7.4935, which
is 7.89% above the current year aggregate roll-back rate of 6.9452; 2) the tentative
aggregate millage rate includes the following millage rates: Countywide at 7.1000 mills
and each Dependent Fire District are at 0.5000 mills and; 3) the tentative M.S.T.U.
voted debt service millage rates are: a) Gulfside Beachfront at 1.0232; b) Gulfside
Interior at 0.7785 and, c) Bayside at 0.8175. She reported that the stated millage rates
are the maximum the Board may adopt, and compliance requirements will be measured
with a vote of each adoption, therefore there will be a roll call. OMB Director Herring
then reported the reasons for the increase, as follows: 1) Triumph agreement requiring
the County to keep millage rates the same as prior year lowered the rolled back rate; 2)
Interlocal Agreement with the City of Port St. Joe for the first (1st) of three (3) additional
stipend; 3) Property Appraiser’s capital outlay for the first (1st) of a two (2) year
contribution for new software system; 4) first full year funding for the County Engineer;
5) Sheriff’s Office; 6) legal services; 7) FRS retirement cost; 8) health insurance cost
and; 9) pay raises. She reported that the tentative budget has been provided to the
Board members and posted on the County’s website as required for this Public Hearing.
OMB Director Herring further reported that Hurricane Michael funding relief from
Triumph and the State of Florida helped to mitigate the financial impacts of revenue loss
and initial recovery, but these funds will end September 30, 2020, the insurance
settlement funds have been carried forward and reserved until those expenditures are

incurred due to the unknown timing of many repair projects and, the growth and stability
of reserves are necessary to maintain and improve the County’s credit worthiness on
current and future bond issues. Additionally, the County must continue to cash flow the
anticipated reimbursements for insurance deductible, F.E.M.A. waiver for local match
and improvements not covered by F.E.M.A. or insurance, with the Reserves. She stated
the Board may amend the Budget during this Hearing, but any increases in
expenditures will require equivalent reductions specified by line item. OMB Director
Herring then requested the Board allow additional time for public to have the ability to
submit comments via email prior to any measures being adopted. She also requested
the following be approved by the Board: 1) decrease of $1,000.00 in Fund 107 affecting
Cash Carry Forward, Reserves, and Expenditures; 2) increase of $40,000.00 in Fund
301, affecting Cash Carry Forward and Capital Expenditures; 3) increase of $21,565.00
in Fund 404, affecting Revenues and Reserves; 4) line item adjustments in Fund 001 to
adjust Reserves, Expenditures, and transfers out to Capital Projects Fund.
Commissioner McDaniel motioned to adopt these changes. Commissioner Rogers
seconded the motion. Chairman Quinn called for public comment and emails. County
Attorney Novak read an email from Annette Ryan, of Georgia <copy provided to the
Clerk for the record> asking that the taxing authorities not do any level of increase in
property tax based. Commissioner McCroan stated that the Board has not increased the
millage from last year, but there could have been an increase in value by the Property
Appraiser. County Administrator Hammond stated the millage rates remained the same
and the M.S.T.U.’s had a reduction this year. Chairman Quinn called for any additional
emails. There being none, the motion passed unanimously. Upon recommendation from
County Administrator Hammond, Chairman Quinn opened the Public Hearing for public
comment. County Administrator Hammond stated for the record that there is seating
capacity and a waiting list if needed. Chairman Quinn then closed the Public Hearing to
the public comment. OMB Director Herring again reported that the Countywide Millage
is 7.1000, with the Aggregate Millage Rate at 7.4935, which is greater than Aggregate
Roll-back Rate of 6.9452 by 7.89%.
