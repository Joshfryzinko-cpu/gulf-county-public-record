SEPTEMBER 1, 2020
SPECIAL MEETING
PORT ST. JOE, FLORIDA
The Gulf County Board of County Commissioners met this date in special session with
the following members present: Chairman Sandy Quinn, Jr., Vice Chairman Phil
McCroan, and Commissioners David C. Rich, Ward McDaniel, and Jimmy Rogers.
Others present were: County Attorney Jeremy Novak, Clerk Rebecca L. Norris, Clerk
Director of Finance & Management Rhonda Woodward, County Administrator Michael L.
Hammond, County Assistant Administrator Warren Yeager, Jr., Deputy Administrator Kari
Summers, Gulf County E.D.C. Director Jim McKnight, E9-1-1 Coordinator Ben Guthrie,
County Engineer Clay Smallwood, III, Grant Administrator Kaci Rhodes, Office of
Management & Budget Director Sherry Herring, Public Works Director Mark Cothran,
Assistant Public Works Director Raymond Hart, Sheriff's Department Captain Chris
Buchanan, and Veterans' Service Office Director & S.H.I.P. Director Joe Paul.
Sheriff's Department Captain Buchanan called the meeting to order at 4:00 p.m., E.T.
Chairman Quinn led in the Pledge of Allegiance to the Flag.
GULF COUNTY HEALTH DEPARTMENT CORE CONTRACT
County Attorney Novak discussed the proposed core contract (Florida Department of
Health/Gulf County Health Department); requesting permission for Board approval,
contingent upon adding language within the contract regarding the rental revenue
received from renting out office space. Commissioner McCroan so moved. Commissioner
Rogers seconded the motion. Chairman Quinn called for public comment and emails.
There being none, the motion passed unanimously.
STATE 911 GRANT
County Attorney Novak discussed a proposed letter presented to the Board for
consideration <copy of file with Clerk for the record> authorizing sole-source provider,
Data Mark GIS for the NG 911 Region One Routing Project. He discussed adding a fifth
reason to the proposed letter regarding the sole-source designation. County Attorney
Novak requested permission to add the fifth provision; allowing County Administrator
Hammond to sign the letter. Chairman Quinn called for a motion to move forward; adding
the fifth sentence. Commissioner McDaniel so moved. Commissioner Rich seconded the
motion. Chairman Quinn called for public comment and emails. There being none, the
motion passed unanimously.

AWARD BID #1920-37 – CARES ACT CONSULTING SERVICES
County Administrator Hammond recommended to award Bid #1920-37 for the CARES
Act Consulting Services and Program Management to Roberson & Associates, P.A., the
highest ranked bidder; allowing staff to negotiate a contract (agreement). Commissioner
McDaniel so moved. Commissioner Rogers seconded the motion. Chairman Quinn called
for public comment and emails. There being none, the motion passed unanimously.
AWARD BID #1920-41 – PURCHASE MOBILE HOMES
S.H.I.P. Director Paul appeared before the Board to recommend <copy provided to the
Clerk for the record> the Board award Bid #1920-41 for the purchase of mobile homes
(H.H.R.P. Funding * Hurricane Michael) to Timberline Homes, the low bidder. Chairman
Quinn called for a motion to accept the bid. Commissioner Rich so moved. Commissioner
McCroan seconded the motion. Chairman Quinn called for public comment and emails.
There being none, the motion passed unanimously.
AWARD BID #1920-42 – PORT CITY TRAIL
County Engineer Smallwood appeared before the Board to report that BCL Civil
Contractors, Inc. was the low bidder for this project and the City of Port St. Joe would like
to move forward with the low bidder, in the amount of $147,113.16, contingent upon the
approval from FL Department of Environmental Protection. Chairman Quinn called for a
motion to move forward. Commissioner McCroan so moved. Commissioner McDaniel
seconded the motion. Chairman Quinn called for public comment and emails. There being
none, the motion passed unanimously.
ADMINISTRATIVE UPDATE & REPORT
County Administrator Hammond recommended awarding Bid #1920-39 for S.H.I.P.
Project – Bertha Quinn to Gulf 2 Bay Construction, sole-bidder, contingent upon getting
the dollar amount to $40,000.00 or below and the S.H.I.P. Director, homeowner and
contractor signing off. Chairman Quinn passed the Chair to Vice Chairman McCroan.
Chairman McCroan called for a motion. Commissioner McDaniel made the motion.
Commissioner Rogers seconded the motion, and the motion passed 4-0, with
Commissioner Quinn abstaining <FORM 8B on file with the Clerk for the record>.
<Commissioner Quinn resumed the Chair.>
County Administrator Hammond presented a memorandum <copy provided to the Clerk
for the record> regarding the submitted applications for the H.H.R.P. Funding (Hurricane
Michael); requesting Board approval. Chairman Quinn called for the motion.
Commissioner McDaniel made the motion. Commissioner Rogers seconded the motion.
After discussion regarding the set aside and the remaining balance, Chairman Quinn
called for public comment and emails. There being none, the motion passed unanimously.
County Administrator Hammond reported that a representative from Duke Energy will be
attending the Board meeting at the end of this month to make a major announcement.
County Administrator Hammond discussed the need to hold a special meeting next week
on September 9, 2020 at 9:00 a.m., E.T.

Chairman Quinn stated that he has received notification that the Live Stream is not
available.
The meeting did then recess at 4:16 p.m., E.T.
The meeting reconvened at 4:17 p.m., E.T.
IT Department Steve Cherry appeared before the Board to report on the technology;
reporting that the meeting is live streaming and those having viewing errors need to
refresh their browser. County Attorney Novak discussed for future meetings the need to
pause once the meeting is open to allow for the lag time.
County Administrator Hammond stated that he needs the Board to call a special meeting
on September 9, 2020 at 9:00 a.m., E.T. Chairman Quinn called for a motion to call a
special meeting on September 9, 2020 at 9:00 a.m., E.T. Commissioner Rich so moved.
Commissioner McCroan seconded the motion. Chairman Quinn called for public
comment and emails. There being none, the motion passed unanimously.
County Administrator Hammond reported that the Budget Hearing is scheduled for
Monday, September 14, 2020 at 5:01 p.m., E.T. and a special meeting at 4:30 p.m., E.T.
beforehand. He stated that the Final Budget Hearing will be held on September 23, 2020
at 5:01 p.m., E.T. County Administrator Hammond also reported that the regular meeting
has been rescheduled to September 29, 2020 at 9:00 a.m., E.T.
There being no further business and upon motion by Commissioner McDaniel, second by
Commissioner McCroan, and unanimous vote, the meeting did then adjourn at 4:20 p.m.,
E.T.
