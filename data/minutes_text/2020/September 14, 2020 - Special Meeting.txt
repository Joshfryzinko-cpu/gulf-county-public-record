SEPTEMBER 14, 2020
SPECIAL MEETING
PORT ST. JOE, FLORIDA
The Gulf County Board of County Commissioners met this date in special session with
the following members present: Chairman Sandy Quinn, Jr., Vice Chairman Phil
McCroan, and Commissioners Ward McDaniel and Jimmy Rogers <Commissioner David
C. Rich appeared by telephone>.
Others present were: County Attorney Jeremy Novak, Clerk Rebecca L. Norris, Deputy
Clerk Leanna Roberts, County Administrator Michael L. Hammond, County Assistant
Administrator Warren Yeager, Jr., Deputy Administrator Kari Summers, County Engineer
Clay Smallwood, III, Grant Administrator Kaci Rhodes, Office of Management & Budget
Director Sherry Herring, Mosquito Control Director Austin Horton, Public Works Director
Mark Cothran, Assistant Public Works Director Raymond Hart, Sheriff Mike Harrison, and
T.D.C. Executive Director Kelli Godwin.
Sheriff Harrison called the meeting to order at 4:30 p.m., E.T.
Chairman Quinn opened the meeting with prayer and led in the Pledge of Allegiance to
the Flag.
AWARD BID #1920-43 – CEI SERVICES COUNTY ROAD 387
County Engineer Smallwood appeared before the Board to recommend the award of Bid
#1920-40 for the CEI (Construction Engineering Inspection) Services for County Road
387/Doc Whitfield Road in Howard Creek to sole-bidder, Southeastern Consulting
Engineers, Inc.; allowing staff to negotiate a contract and sign said contract. He reported
that D.O.T. (Department of Transportation) is fine with only one bidder; stating that this
will be paid from a D.O.T. grant. Chairman Quinn then called for a motion to go with the
recommendation. Commissioner McCroan so moved. Commissioner Rogers seconded
the motion. Chairman Quinn called for public comment and emails. There being none, the
motion passed unanimously.
MEMBERS OF THE BOARD
Chairman Quinn reported that Commissioner Rich is appearing by phone and
Commissioner Rogers is present, sitting to the right of the Board Room.
OPEN BID #1920-43 – RESURFACE COUNTY ROAD 387
Clerk Norris opened sealed bids for Bid #1920-43 for the resurfacing of County Road
387/Doc Whitfield Road in Howard Creek, while Grant Administrator Rhodes called the
bids, as follows: 1) C.W. Roberts, Inc., in the amount of $7,990,462.87; and 2) Roberts
& Roberts, Inc., in the amount of $5,689,165.31. County Administrator Hammond request

the Chairman allow staff to review these bids and bring a recommendation back before
the Board. The Chairman had no objection.
OPEN BID #1920-45 – WHITE CITY BOAT RMAP IMPROVEMENTS
Clerk Norris opened sealed bids received for Bid #1920-45 for the White City Boat Ramp
Improvements, as Grant Administrator Rhodes read the bids aloud, as follows: 1) Storm
Construction, LLC, in the amount of $242,470.10; 2) Inland Construction & Engineering,
Inc., in the amount of $375,990.00; 3) Michael L. Anderson, Inc., in the amount of
$303,000. 00; 4) BCL Civil Contractors, Inc., in the amount of $335,672.20; and 5) H. G.
Harders & Son, Inc., in the amount of $258,568.00. County Administrator Hammond
requested that the Board allow staff to review these bids and bring a recommendation
back before the Board. Chairman Quinn was in agreement.
OPEN BID #1920-46 – IRRIGATION PUMP STATION
Clerk Norris reported that no bids were received for Bid #1920-46 for the installation of
an irrigation pump station at the St. Joseph Bay Golf Club (Country Club Golf Course).
Assistant Administrator Yeager appeared before the Board to request permission from
the Board to rebid. Commissioner McDaniel motioned to rebid. Commissioner McCroan
seconded the motion. Chairman Quinn called for public comment and emails. There being
none, the motion passed unanimously.
GRANT AGREEMENT – BRIDGE REPAIR / COUNTY ROAD 30A
Grant Administrator Rhodes appeared before the Board to request the Board approve a
S.C.O.P. Grant Agreement, a proposed resolution, and to allow the Chairman to sign any
necessary documents for the County Road 30A (Money Bayou) Bridge repair. She
reported that the award amount is $148,310.00 through D.O.T. (Department of
Transportation). Grant Administrator Rhodes then read the proposed resolution by title.
Commissioner McCroan so moved to approve. Commissioner McDaniel seconded the
motion. Chairman Quinn called for public comment and emails. There being none, the
motion passed unanimously, as follows:
RESOLUTION NO. 2020-22
A RESOLUTION OF THE GULF COUNTY BOARD OF COUNTY
COMMISSIONERS AUTHORIZING THE CHAIRMAN OF THE BOARD TO
ENTER INTO A S.C.O.P. JOINT PARTICIPATION AGREEMENT (J.P.A.)
WITH THE FLORIDA DEPARTMENT OF TRANSPORATION.
*Complete Resolution on file with Clerk*
(End)
ADMINISTRATIVE UPDATE & REPORT
Assistant Administrator Yeager appeared before the Board to provide a storm report
(Hurricane Sally) to the Board; discussing the rain and erosion expected within Gulf
County. Commissioner McCroan discussed his concerns regarding the beach erosion.

The meeting did recess at 4:45 p.m., E.T.
The meeting did then reconvene at 4:58 p.m., E.T.
AWARD BID #1920-43 – RESURFACE COUNTY ROAD 387
County Engineer Smallwood recommended the Board award Bid #1920-43 for the
resurfacing of County Road 387 (Doc Whitfield Road) in Howard Creek to Roberts &
Roberts, Inc., contingent upon negotiating the number down to the budget amount and
D.O.T. approving this award. Commissioner Rogers so moved to accept the
recommendation by County Engineer Smallwood. Commissioner McCroan seconded the
motion. Chairman Quinn called for public comment and emails. There being no public
comment, the motion passed unanimously.
AWARD BID #1920-45 – WHITE CITY BOAT RAMP IMPROVEMENTS
County Engineer Smallwood recommended the Board award Bid #1920-45 for the White
City Boat Ramp Improvements to low bidder, Storm Construction, LLC, contingent upon
getting the dollar amount within budget and F.B.I.P. (FL Fish & Wildlife Commission)
approval of this bidder. Chairman Quinn called for a motion to approve County Engineer
Smallwood’s recommendation. Commissioner McDaniel so moved. Commissioner
Rogers seconded the motion. Chairman Quinn called for public comment and emails.
There being none, the motion passed unanimously.
There being no further business and upon motion by Commissioner McDaniel, second by
Commissioner McCroan, and unanimous vote, the meeting did then adjourn at 5:01 p.m.,
E.T.
