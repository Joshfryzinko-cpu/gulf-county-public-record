MEETING – DEPARTMENT OF INTERIOR
Commissioner McCroan requested a report from the meeting held with the Secretary of
the Interior (David Bernhardt) regarding C.B.R.A. (Coastal Barrier Resources Act).
Assistant County Administrator Yeager reported that the push began back in March with
Department of Interior and the Director of U.S. Fish and Wildlife; stating these discussions
have been phenomenal and totally different than those in the past. He also reported that
with the help of U.S. Congressman Dunn, this was the first time a Cabinet level person
has visited Gulf County; referencing the Secretary of Interiors visit last week to Cape San
Blas. Assistant County Administrator Yeager stated this is the best opportunity Gulf
County has had in removing 948 acres, over and beyond the 40 acres that have already
removed (from C.B.R.A.). County Administrator Hammond discussed the meetings held,
years involved, and money spent on this project; commending former Congressman
Southerland who set up the meetings in March. He sated the Secretary and Director are
common sense folks who will be looking at the facts when making a decision.
Commissioner McCroan and Chairman Quinn discussed the role County Attorney Novak
has played in this effort; thanking him for his service and commitment. Commissioner
McCroan discussed the need of a lobbyist for this project; commending the work of former
U.S. Congressman Steve Southerland (Capital Hill Consultants) and his role in getting us
to this point. County Attorney Novak stated this could be a transformational change for
Gulf County; noting what a big deal it was to have a Cabinet level official such as
Secretary Bernhardt to come and look at our issue. Commissioner Rich noted Secretary
Bernhardt’s positive comments on our area and his desire to come back with his family.
TRANSFER STATION – FIVE POINTS LANDFILL
Public Works Director Cothran appeared before the Board to discuss concerns regarding
the contractor (Highland Wake Construction, LLC) and their sub-contractors at the

Transfer Station at Five Points Landfill; recommending the Board allow him to get with
County Attorney Novak regarding the contract and how to move forward. County Attorney
Novak reported that the performance date under the contract was February 4, 2020;
discussing delays to the completion of the project. Upon recommendation by Public
Works Director Cothran and discussions with County Administrator Hammond, County
Attorney Novak requested the ability to examine the contract, look at the performance,
and speak with the vendor to see where they stand. He stated in the event of a breach of
contract, the contractor will need to be notified, and if there are grounds to terminate the
contract, then he needs to work with the Administrator and Department Heads to work in
that direction. County Attorney Novak requested permission from the Board the
opportunity to review this matter and move forward under the contract; authorizing him to
submit the appropriate notice under the contact with the Administrator’s guidance.
Chairman Quinn called for a motion to move forward with it. Commissioner McDaniel so
moved with Public Works Director Cothran recommendation. Commissioner McCroan
seconded the motion. Chairman Quinn called for public comment. There was no public
comment. County Administrator Hammond discussed his concerns regarding this project,
the contractor, and the subcontractors. Chairman Quinn called for comments by email.
There being none, the motion passed unanimously.
S.H.I.P. & H.H.R.P. FUNDING
S.H.I.P. Director Paul appeared before the Board; presenting and discussing two (2)
handouts (reports) regarding S.H.I.P. and H.H.R.P. <copy provided to the Clerk for the
record>. He reported that one report shows items not yet funded, by district; giving a few
reasons why they are pending. S.H.I.P. Director Paul discussed the 30% set-a-side
requirement by income level, with very low being at $18.36%, low at 32%, and moderate
at 26.07%., further reporting that 20% of the total moneys must be spent on special needs
(persons with disabilities) and the County is currently $832,467.00 behind in meeting this
requirement. He requested a motion to take applications for the very low income
(category) only, for all five (5) districts; recommending the Board hold another lottery
drawing as they did in the past. S.H.I.P. Director Paul then discussed the other handout,
a list of current S.H.I.P. applications on file (47) that are not included in the H.H.R.P.
funding; noting if the very low income applicants (18 in total) wished to apply for H.H.R.P.
funding, and meet the criteria, that would give the County room in the S.H.I.P. program.
He stated that when there is a waiting list of applicants sufficient to meet the upcoming
funding of $350,000.00, they are not required to advertise, per Florida Statute. S.H.I.P.
Director Paul again requested a motion to allow staff to take applications for the very low
income only, throughout all five (5) districts, and put together a lottery drawing.
Discussion followed regarding the required funding percentages across income
categories, where these levels currently stand, and whether they could be shifted to other
income levels. Upon inquiry by Chairman Quinn, S.H.I.P. Director Paul stated he could
contact the applicants on the S.H.I.P. funding list; notifying them of the H.H.R.P. funding
application opportunity. Upon inquiry by Chairman Quinn regarding receipt of the new
year funding, S.H.I.P. Director Paul discussed the process; noting that current applicants
would need to re-apply if the County opens up the application process for the new year
funding. County Administrator Hammond asked the Board to consider, based on staff’s
recommendation, to fill the current waiting list before accepting new applications for the

new year S.H.I.P. funding. After discussion regarding the special needs requirement,
Commissioner McDaniel asked for S.H.I.P. Director Paul’s recommendation on the very
low (income category) regarding the H.H.R.P. funds. S.H.I.P. Director Paul recommended
the Board accept applications in all five (5) districts for the very low income category only.
Commissioner McDaniel so moved to go with S.H.I.P. Director Paul’s recommendation
for H.H.R.P. Commissioner Rogers seconded the motion. Chairman Quinn called for
public comment and emails. There being none, the motion passed unanimously. S.H.I.P.
Director Paul then requested direction regarding the current forty-seven (47) applications
on the S.H.I.P. fund waiting list. County Administrator Hammond recommended the Board
keep the current list until they have exhausted it; recommending they not advertise (for
new applications). Commissioner McCroan made the motion, with a second by
Commissioner Rogers. S.H.I.P. Director Paul presented, upon request by Chairman
Quinn, an expenditure report for both the S.H.I.P. and H.H.R.P. programs <copy provided
to the Clerk for the record>. Chairman Quinn then called for public comment and emails.
There being none, the motion passed unanimously.

RECOMMENDATIONS
VARIANCE REQUEST – RALPH & ELIZABETH MATTHEWS

Planner Collinsworth presented a variance request from Ralph and Elizabeth Matthews,
Parcel ID #03798-550R; reporting the P.D.R.B. voted 4-0 to approve the variance.
Chairman Quinn called for public comment on this item; requesting they step forward.
Tom Graney, of St. Joe Beach appeared before the Board. Chairman Quinn then inquired
about any objections to the waiving of the Quasi-Judicial Hearing. Mr. Graney stated he
has not objection. Commissioner McDaniel motioned to waive. Commissioner Rogers
seconded the motion. Chairman Quinn called for public comment. There being none, the
motion passed unanimously. Mr. Graney discussed the variance request for the two (2)
townhouses, Section 31, Range 11, referred to as the Gulf Aire Townhouse; stating he
has no objection to the variance request. Chairman Quinn stated the Board has not
addressed this parcel yet, but he can speak on the matter once that parcel is presented.
Chairman Quinn called for anyone else wishing to speak on this variance (request). There
was none. Upon inquiry by Chairman Quinn, Planner Collinsworth reported that the
P.D.R.B. voted 4-0 to approve the variance. County Attorney Novak discussed the
P.D.R.B. recommendation; calling for consideration of the Board to accept, deny, or table
the P.D.R.B. recommendation, and waive the reading of the substantial and competent
evidence that was presented to the P.D.R.B., and accept that and the Planner’s
representation and staff’s recommendation to move forward with the variance. Chairman
Quinn called for a motion to waive the formal reading. Commissioner McDaniel so moved.
Commissioner Rogers seconded the motion. Chairman Quinn called for public comment.
There being none, the motion passed unanimously. Commissioner McDaniel motioned to
accept the recommendation of the P.D.R.B. Commissioner Rich seconded the motion.
Chairman Quinn called for public comment or emails. There being none, the motion
passed unanimously.
VARIANCE REQUEST – BOB & GINNY LEGETTE
Planner Collinsworth presented a variance request from Bob and Ginny Legette, Parcel
ID #03798-655R. Chairman Quinn called for public comments on this issue (variance).
Tom Graney acknowledged he would like to speak. Chairman Quinn asked if Mr. Graney
had any objections to the waiving of the Quasi-Judicial Hearing. Mr. Graney had no
objection. Commissioner McDaniel so moved to waive. Commissioner Rogers seconded
the motion. Chairman Quinn called for public comment. There being none, the motion
passed unanimously. Mr. Graney, of St. Joe Beach then discussed this variance; stating
there is no objection to the request, but wanted to make the Board aware of some things.
He stated there is an issue with contractors trespassing on the adjacent beach property,
dune damage, and traffic problems along Highway 98. County Attorney Novak reported
at the public hearing before the P.D.R.B., Mr. Graney came on behalf of the Gulf Aire
Homeowners Associations; stating that he thinks Mr. Graney is here today on that
capacity. Mr. Graney was in agreement. County Attorney Novak reported that Mr. Graney
is allotted five (5) minutes, in lieu of three (3); requesting the record and the Clerk to
provide Mr. Graney an additional two (2) minutes. Mr. Graney noted his comments are
the concerns of the HOA and they are happy to have the townhouses back, and will
cooperate with the variance, but wanted to make the Board aware of their concerns.
Chairman Quinn called for the recommendations of the P.D.R.B. Planner Collinsworth
stated the P.D.R.B., by a 4-0 vote, approved the variance. Chairman Quinn called for any
objections to the waiving of the formal reading. When there were none, Chairman Quinn

called for a motion to waive the formal reading. Commissioner McCroan so moved.
Commissioner Rogers seconded the motion. Chairman Quinn called for public comment.
There being none, the motion passed unanimously. Commissioner McDaniel recommend
that the Board accept the recommendation of the P.D.R.B. to approve. Commissioner
Rogers seconded. Chairman Quinn called for public comment or emails. There being
none, it passed unanimously.

DITCH CLEANING – N.R.C.S.
County Administrator Hammond reported that beginning on July 6, 2020, the County will
start the N.R.C.S. (Natural Resources Conservation Services) ditch cleaning and debris
removal (wet debris removal due to Hurricane Michael); stating that some property
owners would not sign the waivers to allow the County on their property to clean the
ditches. He reported the County will be able to get the houses out of the ditches in
Highland View and Simmons Bayou; thanking Director Moskowitz (Director of Florida
Division of Emergency Management) for assisting in obtaining the matching grant funds.
He reported the project is scheduled to begin on July 6, 2020.
