project at the Cape (San Blas); stating the battery will be used to offset the high voltage
use for customers in this area. He reported that Duke Energy purchased property on 7921
Cape San Blas Road near Spinnaker Way, a two acre tract. Upon inquiry by
Commissioner McDaniel, Mr. Collins stated that this battery system will assist with voltage
during high pecks; covering the Indian Pass Substation outward. Following discussion
regarding the battery system, County Administrator Hammond requested that Mr. Collins
provide a ballpark estimate for this project. Mr. Collins reported that this project will be in
the range of $10-$20 million.

COVID-19 RELIEF FUND
S.H.I.P. Director Paul appeared before the Board to present and discuss the Coronavirus
Relief Fund (CRF) under the CARES Act; reporting that Gulf County’s portion of these
funds is $175,000.00 to assist residents with rental assistant, mortgage payments, and
buy-downs; recommending the Board approve the proposed agreement with A.R.P.C.
(Apalachee Regional Planning Council) to administer this funding program and to allow
the County Administrator to sign this agreement as an exception under the Procurement
Policy, Section 4(7) due to the timeframe required in the expenditure of these funds (no
later than 12/30/2020). He also requested the Board approve the agreement with Florida
Housing; stating that once signed the funds will be deposited in the County’s S.H.I.P.
account. S.H.I.P. Director Paul discussed the critical delivery schedule, availability of
service, unique technical requirements, and legislative direct, and prior experience of the
vender; resulting in a cost savings to the County. He explained the program has a ten
percent (10%) administrative fee allowance for the administration of this program, but the
proposal is for only eight percent (8%); leaving a two percent (2%) administrative for use
by his department in serving as liaison. County Administrator Hammond affirmed that staff
needs several separate motions; disclosing that he has a conflict because he sits on the
A.R.P.C. Board, as the Governor’s appointee. Commissioner McDaniel stated that he will
abstain due to representing this board and Gulf County on A.R.P.C. County Administrator
Hammond suggested the first motion be that the County accept, from Florida Housing,
the grant agreement. Chairman Quinn called for a motion to accept the money from
Florida Housing, CARES Act funding. Commissioner McCroan so moved. Commissioner
Rich seconded the motion. Chairman Quinn called for public comment and emails. There
being none, the motion passed unanimously. Upon recommendation by County
Administrator Hammond, Chairman Quinn then called for a motion to accept A.R.P.C. to
administrator this program; for an 8% administrative fee. S.H.I.P. Administrator Paul
confirmed the agreement would commence August 1, 2020. After discussion regarding
County Administrator Hammond serving on the A.R.P.C. Board, County Attorney Novak
stated that if wanted, the Chairman could sign. County Administrator Hammond was fine
with (the Chairman signing the agreement). Chairman Quinn clarified by saying that the
Board accepts this A.R.P.C. proposal and he will sign all the necessary documents as
Chairman. Commissioner Rich so moved. Commissioner McCroan seconded the motion.
Chairman Quinn called for public comment and emails. There being none, the motion
passed 4-0, with Commissioner McDaniel abstaining <Form 8B on file with the Clerk for
the record>. County Administrator Hammond addressed the need for the Board to set
some parameters; discussing proposed qualifications, requirements, and maximum
funding limits. Discussion followed regarding holding a special meeting next week to
discuss and adopt a plan; noting the need for a lottery, not first come first serve, following
the appropriate advertising. Upon Chairman Quinn clarifying that the Board does not need
a motion at this time, County Administrator Hammond agreed; stating that staff will come
back before the Board with a recommendation next week.

NO WAKE ORDINANCE – INTERCOASTAL WATERWAY
Commissioner McCroan inquired about the progress on the proposed no wake ordinance.
County Attorney Novak discussed his review of the matter; reporting it is not the County’s
jurisdiction for enforcement, but can vote as a Board to support the State authorities to
enforce the No Wake Zone around the fisheries and areas that the Board has identified
throughout the County. Upon inquiry by Commissioner McCroan regarding the need for
an ordinance in order for FWC to enforce, County Attorney Novak gave an example of a
portion of the Leave No Trace Ordinance where the FWC came back to the County stating
the County does not have jurisdiction over the waters. He stated the County can put the
language out there, but it is not enforceable; discussing a proposed resolution in support
of the FWC maintaining No Wake Zones near the fisheries and areas of concern.
Commissioner McCroan was in agreement. County Attorney Novak stated he will offer
the proposed resolution, but if an ordinance will support that under their (FWC) General
Counsel then he will follow up with that and do the advertisements.

Hill Park to the low bidder, Michael L. Anderson, Inc., in the amount of $118,003.00 to be
paid from the money that F.E.M.A. obligated for that park. Commissioner McDaniel so
moved with the recommendation with Michael L. Anderson. Commissioner Rich
seconded the motion. Chairman Quinn called for public comment and emails. There being
none, the motion passed unanimously. County Administrator Hammond reported that the
County will be reimbursed by F.E.M.A., but it may take a while to receive these funds. He
reported that site completion will be around mid-October with a plan to hold a dedication
of the park on November 11, 2020 for Veterans’ Day. Chairman Quinn inquired on any
information from Mediacom, County Administrator Hammond and County Attorney Novak
reported that they have not heard from Mediacom.
SPECIAL MEETING
County Administrator Hammond inquired if the Chairman was ready to set the Special
Meeting date. Chairman Quinn stated he would get back with County Administrator
Hammond on a date. Discussion followed regarding available dates to hold the special
meeting.
ROAD DEDICATION/U.S. HIGHWAY 98 MEMORIAL DESIGNATION
Commissioner McCroan reported that Mr. Rodney Herring received approval to rename
these highways (sections of U.S. Highway 98) and would like to hold a road dedication
tentatively on October 26, 2020, if the Board has no objection. County Administrator
Hammond inquired whether the plan was to unveil the signs at the site; noting one would

begin at the base of the Highland View Bridge and end at either the entrance to WindMark
Beach or the end of the City limits at St. Joe Beach, indicating there are two (2) different
sections. County Administrator Hammond stated that Governor DeSantis approved this
last month (June 30, 2020) through the 2020 Legislative session (CS/CS/SB 78) as
supported by Representative Jason Shoaf (Willis V. Rowan Memorial Highway between
Pine Street to County Road 386 on St. Joe Beach, and John C. Gainous Memorial
Highway between County Road 382 and Pine Street on St. Joe Beach). Commissioner
McCroan reported that Mr. Herring wanted to do it at the amphitheater at Veterans’
Memorial Beacon Hill Park. Members of the Board had no objection. Commissioner
McCroan requested that Assistant County Administrator Yeager touch base with Mr.
Herring to work out the details.

by Commissioner Rich, and unanimous vote, the meeting did then adjourn at 11:43 a.m.,
