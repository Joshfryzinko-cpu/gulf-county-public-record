SEPTEMBER 15, 2020
EMERGENCY MEETING
PORT ST. JOE, FLORIDA
The Gulf County Board of County Commissioners met this date in emergency session
with the following members present: Chairman Sandy Quinn, Jr., Vice Chairman Phil
McCroan, and Commissioners Ward McDaniel and Jimmy Rogers. <Commissioner David
C. Rich appeared by telephone.>
Others present were: County Attorney Jeremy Novak, Deputy Clerk Leanna Roberts,
County Administrator Michael L. Hammond, County Assistant Administrator Warren
Yeager, Jr., Deputy Administrator Kari Summers, Building Official Brad Bailey, Code
Enforcement Officer Trevor Seay, Gulf County E.D.C. Director Jim McKnight, Emergency
Management Director Marshall Nelson, County Engineer Clay Smallwood, III, Grant
Administrator Kaci Rhodes, Office of Management & Budget Director Sherry Herring,
Mosquito Control Director Austin Horton, and Public Works Director Mark Cothran.
Chairman Quinn called the meeting to order at 12:50 p.m., E.T.
Chairman Quinn led in the Pledge of Allegiance to the Flag.
HURRICANE SALLY
County Attorney Novak presented a proposed Local State of Emergency declaration to
the Board to consider <copy provided to the Clerk for the record>. Emergency
Management Director Nelson appeared before the Board to provide a status update
regarding Hurricane Sally; reporting that the Governor has signed an Emergency Order
and Gulf County has been included in this Order. He then recommended the Board enter
into a Local State of Emergency due to Hurricane Sally. County Attorney Novak reported
that the Local State of Emergency is good for seven (7) days. Chairman Quinn called for
a motion to approve the recommendation. Commissioner McCroan so moved.
Commissioner Rogers seconded the motion. Chairman Quinn called for public comment
and emails. There being none the motion passed unanimously.
ADMINISTRATIVE UPDATE & REPORT
County Administrator Hammond discussed his concerns regarding the Chief Judge
closing the Gulf County Courthouse. He noted the Chief Judge can close the Court
System; explaining that there was some confusion that went out over the airwaves. He
stated that most of the Constitutional Officers are closed based on that information.
County Administrator Hammond discussed his concerns regarding County staff working
and not being compensated. He stated that he does not see a need to close County
Offices tomorrow, but if the Board desires to close the Courthouse or County buildings,
then his recommendation would be to give the Chairman the authority, if the need arises.
County Administrator Hammond reported that Commissioner Rich is appearing by

telephone. Commissioner McCroan made the motion (giving the Chairman the authority
to close the County Offices tomorrow, if needed). Commissioner Rich seconded the
motion. Chairman Quinn called for public comment and emails. There being none, the
motion passed unanimously. County Administrator Hammond discussed the rainfall and
drainage within the City of Port St. Joe.
The meeting recessed at 12:57 p.m., E.T.

RIVER FLOODING
E911 Coordinator Guthrie appeared before the Board to provide a status report regarding
river flooding and watching another tropical disturbance in the Gulf of Mexico. He stated
that arrangements have been made for the use of equipment from the Army Corps of
Engineers that will produce sandbags; reporting once the sandbags are made, they can
be picked up at City of Wewahitchka, Dalkeith Fire Department or Howard Creek Fire
Department. Upon inquiry by County Administrator Hammond, E911 Coordinator Guthrie
reported that bottled water is already staged at these locations. Discussion followed
regarding National Guard staging. Public Works Director Cothran appeared before the
Board to discuss the placement of barricades. County Administrator Hammond reported
that Public Works will be working the weekend; authorizing overtime for staff. Upon inquiry
by County Administrator Hammond, Public Works Director Cothran stated that sandbags
will be placed outside the fence at City of Wewahitchka, at Dalkeith Fire Department and
Howard Creek Fire Department. County Administrator Hammond stated that the County
is still good through next Tuesday (on the Local State of Emergency) and if the Board has
no objection, he has authorized overtime for Public Works and E.O.C. staff. He requested
the Board extend the declaration (Local State of Emergency) for seven (7) days <copy
not provided to the Clerk for the record>. Chairman Quinn then called for a motion to
extend for an additional seven (7) days. Commissioner McCroan so moved.
Commissioner Rogers seconded the motion. Chairman Quinn called for public comment.
There being no public comment, the motion passed unanimously.
The meeting recessed at 4:39 p.m., E.T.

ADMINISTRATIVE UPDATE & REPORT
County Administrator Hammond discussed his concerns regarding FWC’s (Fish Wildlife
Commission) inclusion of Gulf County and Indian Lagoon in the five (5) year closer of
Apalachicola Bay (for oyster harvesting); stating that Gulf County was not notified and
there was no public hearing held. He discussed the need to have a draft a resolution for
the Board to consider at the next meeting opposing the closer to Indian Lagoon. County
Administrator Hammond noted harvesting oysters in Indian Lagoon could be managed in
Gulf County with minimal rules for winter months, harvesting by hand, and size regulation.
Commissioner McCroan motioned to direct the County Attorney to draft a resolution to
present at Tuesday’s Board meeting. Commissioner McDaniel seconded the motion.
Chairman Quinn called for public comment and emails. There being none, the motion
passed unanimously.
There being no further business, and upon motion by Commissioner McDaniel, second
by Commissioner McCroan, and unanimous vote, the meeting did then adjourn at 4:58
p.m., E.T.
