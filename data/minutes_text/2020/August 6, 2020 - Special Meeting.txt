AUGUST 6, 2020
SPECIAL MEETING
PORT ST. JOE, FLORIDA
The Gulf County Board of County Commissioners met this date in special session with
the following members present: Chairman Sandy Quinn, Jr., Vice Chairman Phil
McCroan, and Commissioners David C. Rich, Ward McDaniel, and Jimmy Rogers.
Others present were: County Attorney Jeremy Novak, Deputy Clerk Leanna Roberts,
County Administrator Michael L. Hammond, County Assistant Administrator Warren
Yeager, Jr., Deputy Administrator Kari Summers, Gulf County E.D.C. Director Jim
McKnight, Emergency Management Director Marshall Nelson, Gulf County E.M.S.
Director Jody Daniels, County Engineer Clay Smallwood, III, Grant Administrator Kaci
Rhodes, Property Appraiser Office IT Tech Dennis Barfield, Jr., Public Works Director Mark
Cothran, Assistant Public Works Director Raymond Hart, Sheriff Mike Harrison, and
Veterans' Service Office Director & S.H.I.P. Director Joe Paul.
Sheriff Harrison called the meeting to order at 9:01 a.m., E.T.
Chairman Quinn opened the meeting in prayer and led in the Pledge of Allegiance to the
Flag.
FLORIDA HOUSING COVID ASSISTANCE
S.H.I.P. Director Paul appeared before the Board to discuss an agreement with A.R.P.C.
(Apalachee Regional Planning Council) to administer the COVID Relief Aid (CARES Act)
and the information sheet showing what can be done with this funding <copy of both
documents on file with the Clerk for the record>. He then requested that the Board
consider signing an agreement with A.R.P.C. to administer this fund at 8% (eight percent);
giving permission to allow the Chairman to sign, as County Administrator Hammond has
a conflict (County appointee to the A.R.P.C. Board). Chairman Quinn called for a motion
to move forward. Commissioner McCroan so moved. Commissioner Rich seconded the
motion. Chairman Quinn called for public comment and emails. There being none, the
motion passed 4-0, with Commissioner McDaniel abstaining <Form 8B on file with the
Clerk>. S.H.I.P. Director Paul then discussed the proposed COVID Relief Funds Plan
<copy on file with the Clerk for the record>; allowing each applicant an award of a
maximum of $2,000.00 towards mortgage payments, rental assistance, including back
rent, deposits and utilities. He requested the Board to approve this presented proposed
plan. Discussion followed regarding the implementation of the plan, disbursement of
funds, and advertisement. Commissioner McDaniel stated that he wants the check cut to
the utility company or the landlord/mortgage company, not the applicant to ensure
safeguards. County Attorney Novak recommended the Board make it as open, fair, and
transparent as possible, recommending they start the advertisement and do it from that
date forward, making it equal and equitable across the board. County Administrator

Hammond explained that applications received prior to the advertisement will be placed
in holding until the deadline to ensure no unfair advantage of one applicant over another.
After discussion about a possible lottery and how recipients will be selected, Chairman
Quinn called for a motion to accept the COVID Relief Aid Plan with a deadline to receive
applications by close of business on August 24, 2020; the maximum award being
$2,000.00 that will go towards mortgage, rental assistance, including back rent, deposits,
utilities, and foreclosure/eviction. Commissioner McDaniel so moved. Commissioner
Rogers seconded the motion. Chairman Quinn called for public comment. Vicki Abrams,
of Port St. Joe appeared before the Board to report that CareerSource has funds for
emergency assistance primarily for utilities and deposits. She discussed the need to pay
back rent and mortgage payments. Chairman Quinn called for any additional public
comment and emails. There being none, the motion passed unanimously.
ADMINISTRATIVE UPDATE & REPORT
Upon request by County Administrator Hammond, Commissioner McDaniel motioned to
extend accepting the very low income applications in all Districts for the H.H.R.P. funding
to September 30, 2020. Commissioner McCroan seconded the motion. Chairman Quinn
called for public comment and emails. There being none, the motion passed unanimously.
County Administrator Hammond reported that the County has been approved for a Florida
Department of Emergency Management Hurricane Loss Mitigation Program, in the
amount of $194,000.00 to help 12 or 13 (twelve or thirteen) Gulf County homeowners;
recommending they take applications and hold a lottery. He reported the funds are to help
with roofs and shuttering; requesting the Board accept this grant from D.E.M.
Commissioner McCroan so moved. Commissioner Rogers seconded the motion.
Chairman Quinn called for public comment and emails. There being none, the motion
passed unanimously.
E.D.C. Director McKnight appeared before the Board to request approval for a private
practice physician to lease office space in the Port St. Joe Health Department Building;
presenting an authorization <copy provided to the Clerk for the record> for the Board to
consider. Commissioner McDaniel so moved. County Attorney Novak requested to add
in the motion that the Administrator be given the authority to negotiate and set the lease
rate and terms; authorizing him to sign-off on the lease and in addition to the public health
aspect of E.D.C. Director McKnight’s recommendation that there is a public health and
safety benefit, and there is a 60 (sixty) day termination clause at the County’s discretion.
Commissioner McDaniel agreed to add this to his motion. Commissioner Rich seconded
the motion. Chairman Quinn called for public comment and emails. There being none, the
motion passed unanimously.
Grant Administrator Rhodes appeared before the Board to request approval of Grant
Agreement Amendment #1 with Florida Fish and Wildlife Commission for the White City
Boat Ramp <copy provided to the Clerk for the record>. Commissioner McCroan so
moved to accept. Commissioner Rogers seconded the motion. Chairman Quinn called
for public comment and emails. There being none, the motion passed unanimously.

Grant Administrator Rhodes requested permission to advertise to receive sealed bids for
the White City Boat Ramp. Commissioner Rogers so moved. Commissioner McCroan
seconded the motion. Chairman Quinn called for public comment and emails. There being
none, the motion passed unanimously.
Grant Administrator Rhodes requested approval to accept the National Fish and Wildlife
Grant <copy provided to the Clerk for the record> for the St. Joseph Peninsula Dune
Enhancement Project (in the amount of $3,700,000.00); allowing the Chairman to sign
the agreement and any necessary documents, contingent upon administration review.
Commissioner McCroan so moved. Commissioner Rogers seconded the motion.
Chairman Quinn called for public comment and emails. There being none, the motion
passed unanimously. Assistant County Administrator Yeager reported that the project is
a little over $5 million, the grant being around $3.7 million, and the County has received
pre-approval for the match. He reported that the County will wait for the agreement with
the State regarding the agreement (for match) before executing the grant agreement with
the National Fish and Wildlife. Upon inquiry by Commissioner McCroan regarding the
need to make it contingent upon getting the match funds, County Administrator Hammond
stated the agreement (with National Fish & Wildlife) will not be signed until the County
receives (approval for the match funds from the State); inquiring whether there is a need
to go ahead and advertise this project. Assistant County Administrator Yeager reported
that staff will put that request in the next Consent Agenda for the August 25, 2020
meeting, along with some other items to consider.
Emergency Management Director Nelson appeared before the Board to discuss a food
pantry program through the First United Methodist Church of Port St. Joe; reporting that
since COVID-19 there has been an increase of distribution. Vicki Abrams, of First United
Methodist Church appeared before the Board to discuss the various food programs
offered. She reported that the church is in the process of establishing a Two Fish Food
Pantry; providing an information sheet to the Board to review regarding the Two Fish
Food Pantry <copy provided to the Clerk for the record>. Discussion followed regarding
the food and security needs within the County. E.D.C. Director Nelson reported that the
County can help through F.E.M.A.; stating no money will be spent from the County. He
stated the County can go under an agreement with the food pantry to provide this service
and under the Category A, which is an emergency protective measures under F.E.M.A.,
submit to F.E.M.A. for reimbursement. He requested the Board’s approval to enter into
an agreement with First United Methodist Church for food distribution, contingent upon
County Attorney and Administrative review. Vicki Abrams, of First United Methodist
Church appeared before the Board to report that the church is keeping very detailed
records of experiences related to COVID-19 and pre-COVID-19. Commissioner McCroan
so moved. Commissioner McDaniel seconded the motion. Chairman Quinn called for
public comment and emails. There being none, the motion passed unanimously. County
Administrator Hammond stated that if it is okay with the Board, he will sign-off on the
monthly expense reports and will place a copy in the agenda or information packet for the
Board to review. The Board had no objections. Emergency Management Director Nelson
reported that once the agreement is drafted, it will be submitted to F.E.M.A. for approval,
once approved by F.E.M.A., the program can commence.

County Administrator Hammond presented a recommendation to appoint Shannon Gant
to serve on the P.D.R.B.; replacing Eric Langston <copy provide to the Clerk for the
record>. Commissioner McDaniel so moved. Commissioner Rich seconded the motion.
Chairman Quinn called for public comment and emails. There being none, the motion
passed unanimously.
Assistant County Administrator Yeager reported that staff is working through the process
with Bay County Board of County Commissioners regarding negotiations for E.M.S.
services for the City of Mexico Beach.
Commissioner McDaniel discussed looking into purchasing a rapid COVID test to be used
by the Gulf County Health Department, to be paid from CARES Act funds; requesting that
the County earmark funds to purchase a rapid test machine. County Administrator
Hammond reported that he can research the cost, see if it meets the criteria (under the
CARES Act), and report back to the Board. After discussion, County Administrator
Hammond requested Board authority, assuming the County can get it on State contract,
to move forward, then staff will submit a request to D.E.M. for their approval to cover the
cost, and purchase the machine. He stated the Board will need to waive the County’s
procurement rules; allowing him to purchase, if the County can get it under the grant and
at a reasonable price. Chairman Quinn called for a motion to waive the Procurement
Policy. Commissioner McDaniel so moved. Commissioner McCroan seconded the
motion. County Attorney Novak requested that the Board state the reasons for the
waiving. He then stated it is for the public health and safety and emergency.
Commissioner McDaniel said he is good. Chairman Quinn called for public comment and
emails. There being none, the motion passed unanimously. Chairman Quinn called for a
motion to move forward. Commissioner McDaniel so moved to get a rapid test machine.
Commissioner Rich seconded the motion. Chairman Quinn called for public comment and
emails. There being none, the motion passed unanimously. County Administrator
Hammond stated that if he can find one (rapid test machine); reasonably priced,
reasonably quick, and the State will pay for it, then he will move forward.
