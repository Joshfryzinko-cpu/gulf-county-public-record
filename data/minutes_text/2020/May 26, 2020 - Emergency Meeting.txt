MAY 26, 2018
PORT ST. JOE, FLORIDA
EMERGENCY MEETING
The Gulf County Board of County Commissioners met this date in an emergency session
at the E.O.C. Building with the following members present: Chairman Sandy Quinn, Jr.,
Vice Chairman Jimmy Rogers, and Commissioners David C. Rich, Ward McDaniel, and
Phil McCroan.
Others present were: Clerk Rebecca L. Norris, County Administrator Michael L.
Hammond, Gulf County E.D.C. Director Warren Yeager, Jr., Emergency Management
Director Marshall Nelson, E9-1-1 Coordinator Ben Guthrie, G.I.S. Coordinator Jessica
Susich, Assistant Mosquito Control Director Austin Horton, Assistant Public Works
Director Raymond Hart, Sheriff Mike Harrison, and Sheriff's Department Captain Chris
Buchanan.
Chairman Quinn called the emergency meeting to order at 6:16 p.m., E.T.
DECLARATION OF LOCAL STATE OF EMERGENCY – SUB-TROPICAL STORM
ALBERTO
Emergency Management Director Nelson reported that Governor Rick Scott declared a
State of Emergency for the entire State of Florida but recommended the Board also
declare a Local State of Emergency. Upon his recommendation, Commissioner McCroan
motioned to declare a Local State of Emergency due to Sub-Tropical Storm Alberto.
Commissioner Rogers seconded the motion. Chairman Quinn called for public comment.
There being no public comment, the motion passed unanimously. County Administrator
Hammond reported that staff is recommending that the Board waive the enforcement of
the removal of recreational vehicles (RV) which is required under the RV Ordinance,
being that no evacuations are being called at this time. Commissioner McDaniel motioned
to waive the RV (Recreational Vehicle) Ordinance. Commissioner Rich seconded the
motion. Chairman called for public comment. There being no public comment, the motion
passed unanimously. Upon inquiry by County Administrator Hammond, Emergency
Management Director Nelson reported that the next statewide update will be held on May
27th at 9:30 a.m., E.T. County Administrator Hammond requested that the Board stand in
recess. Emergency Management Director Nelson reported that tomorrow morning the
E.O.C. will go to a Level II activation which will go through Monday. The Board had no
objection to the Level II activation.
The meeting did then recess at 6:19 p.m., E.T.
The meeting did reconvene on June 26, 2018 at 8:58 a.m., E.T.

Commissioner McDaniel motioned to adjourn. Commissioner Rich seconded the motion.
Chairman Quinn called for public comment. There being no public comment, the motion
passed unanimously.
SANDY QUINN, JR.
