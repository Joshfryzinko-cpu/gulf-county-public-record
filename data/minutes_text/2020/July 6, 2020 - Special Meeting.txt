JULY 6, 2020
SPECIAL MEETING
PORT ST. JOE, FLORIDA
The Gulf County Board of County Commissioners met this date in special session with
the following members present: Chairman Sandy Quinn, Jr., Vice Chairman Phil
McCroan, and Commissioners David C. Rich, Ward McDaniel, and Jimmy Rogers.
Others present were: County Attorney Jeremy Novak, Clerk Rebecca L. Norris, Clerk
Director of Finance & Management Rhonda Woodward, County Administrator Michael L.
Hammond, County Assistant Administrator Warren Yeager, Jr., Deputy Administrator Kari
Summers, County Engineer Clay Smallwood, III, Grant Administrator Kaci Rhodes, Office
of Management & Budget Director Sherry Herring, Mosquito Control Director Austin
Horton, Public Works Director Mark Cothran, and Sheriff Mike Harrison.
Chairman Quinn called the meeting to order at 9:02 a.m., E.T.
Chairman Quinn led in the Pledge of Allegiance to the Flag.
TRANSFER STATION
County Administrator Hammond provided an overview regarding the Transfer Station.
County Attorney Novak reported that the County will take possession of the facility;
recommending the Board to authorize the Chairman and County Administration to work
with the County Attorney and staff to finalize the contract amendment (with BCC Waste
Solutions * Bid #1819-05 * Solid Waste Services); removing their obligation from the
contract to operate the Transfer Station. He then requested a motion to amend the
contract; giving the Chairman authorization to sign it and remove that obligation, with the
second step being to authorize the Chairman with the County Administrator to
immediately terminate both leases (with BCC Waste Solutions). County Attorney Novak
further requested Board authority to allow the County Administrator to take the necessary
steps required for the County to run this facility with the suspension of the Procurement
Policy for the necessary expenditures. Chairman Quinn called for a motion to amend the
contract (with BCC Waste Solutions) and authorize the Chairman to sign. Commissioner
McCroan so moved. Commissioner McDaniel seconded the motion. Chairman Quinn
called for public comment. County Attorney Novak requested that in the motion it state
that the substantive change in the contract is the removal of one of the two obligations,
that BCC Waste Solutions will not be operating the Transfer Station. Upon inquiry by
Chairman Quinn, Commissioner McCroan had no objection to adding this to his motion.
Chairman Quinn called for public comment and emails. There being none, the motion
passed unanimously.

Chairman Quinn then called for a motion to terminate both leases for the Transfer Station
and the Scale House. Commissioner McDaniel so moved for the motion. Commissioner
McCroan seconded the motion. Chairman Quinn called for public comment and emails.
There being none, the motion passed unanimously.
County Attorney Novak discussed the need for a motion to authorize the County
Administrator, under an emergency situation for public health and safety to suspend the
Procurement (bid) Policy, to make decisions to get staff and equipment to get the Transfer
Station running. Commissioner Rogers so moved. Commissioner Rich seconded the
motion. Chairman Quinn called for public comment and emails. There being none, the
motion passed unanimously.
County Administrator Hammond reported that the County will have to find a hauler to haul
the waste away and also purchase a big piece of equipment; stating the County has the
money, but it is not budgeted. He stated this is a specialized tractor, as a sole-source
item, in the amount of $212,871.00.

proposed resolution regarding landscape improvements in front of Veterans’ Memorial

Grant Administrator Rhodes appeared before the Board to request acceptance of the
FDLE JAG (Edward Byrne Memorial Justice Assistance) Grant for the Gulf County
Sheriff’s Office mobile messaging board, in the amount of $22,901.00; reporting the
Sheriff’s Office will pay the matching funds <copy provided to the Clerk for the record>.
Commissioner Rogers so moved to accept. Commissioner McCroan seconded the
motion. Chairman Quinn called for public comment and emails. There being none, the
motion passed unanimously.

Mosquito Control Director Horton appeared before the Board to request permission to
obtain the Chairman’s signature on annual reports for the Florida Department of
Agriculture <copy not provided to the Clerk for the record> and any other necessary
documents for annual reporting. Commissioner McDaniel so moved. Commissioner
Rogers seconded the motion. Chairman Quinn called for public comment and emails.
There being none the motion passed unanimously.
County Administrator Hammond thanked staff for their work in getting the facility (Transfer
Station) up and running over the weekend.
County Administrator Hammond reported that he received a positive call from DEM;
discussing the various projects through DEM (Division of Emergency Management).
County Administrator Hammond requested permission to change the time for the Special
Budget Meeting from 9:00 a.m., E.T. to 9:30 a.m., E.T. for July 21, 2020; reporting that
staff will have the Board a budget book by Thursday and expecting to set a maximum
millage rate at this meeting.
Commissioner McDaniel inquired if the County had a piece of equipment that could be
used (at the Transfer Station) until one is purchased. Public Works Director Cothran
reported that the County will be purchasing a CAT 926M, discussing some of the features
of the tractor.
There being no further business, and upon motion by Commissioner McCroan, second
by Commissioner Rich, and unanimous vote, the meeting did then adjourn at 9:17 a.m.,
