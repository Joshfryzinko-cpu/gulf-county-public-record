JUNE 3, 2020
SPECIAL MEETING
PORT ST. JOE, FLORIDA
The Gulf County Board of County Commissioners met this date in special session with
the following members present: Chairman Sandy Quinn, Jr., Vice Chairman Phil
McCroan, and Commissioners David C. Rich, Ward McDaniel, and Jimmy Rogers.
Others present were: County Attorney Jeremy Novak, Clerk Rebecca L. Norris, Deputy
Clerk Leanna Roberts, County Administrator Michael L. Hammond, Deputy Administrator
Kari Summers, County Engineer Clay Smallwood, III, Mosquito Control Director Austin
Horton, Public Works Director Mark Cothran, Assistant Public Works Director Raymond
Hart, and Sheriff Mike Harrison.
Sheriff Harrison called the meeting to order at 9:01 a.m., E.T.
Chairman Quinn opened the meeting with prayer and led in the Pledge of Allegiance to
the Flag.
COVID-19 EMERGENCY RESPONSE
County Attorney Novak requested the Board to continue the extension of the declaration
<copy not provided to the Clerk for the record>. Chairman Quinn called for a motion.
Commissioner McCroan so moved. Commissioner Rogers seconded the motion.
Chairman Quinn called for public comment or emails. There being none, the motion
passed unanimously.
BURN BAN
County Administrator Hammond asked for the wishes of the Board on whether to drop
the burn ban or just drop the burn ban north of the Intercoastal (Canal) Waterway. He
requested the Board allow him and the Chairman the authority to drop the ban when there
is measurable rain (on the south end of the County). Commissioner McDaniel stated he
has no objection to allow the Chairman and County Administrator Hammond to drop the
burn ban on the south end of the County once we receive (measurable) rain. After
discussion regarding proper permitting, the cost, and financial responsibility for those who
violate, Commissioner McDaniel motioned that the Board lift the burn ban north of the
Intercoastal Canal (Waterway) and remain in effect south of the Intercostal Canal; giving
the Chairman and the County Administrator the authority to lift the ban south of the Canal
when appropriate. Commissioner McCroan seconded the motion. Chairman Quinn called
for public comment. After discussion from Commissioner Rogers, Chairman Quinn called
for public comment or emails. There being none, the motion passed unanimously.

ADMINISTRATIVE UPDATE & REPORT
County Administrator Hammond reported that the S.H.I.P. Administrator (Paul) is
requesting the Board increase by their policy for the H.H.R.P. maximum allowable
purchase price for existing homes; increasing it from $187,000.00 to $225,000.00, but
maintaining the 35% (thirty-five percent) affordability test. He stated that it does not
change the amount of funds the County is applying to it, being 50% (fifty percent) up to
$60,000.00. County Administrator Hammond stated it allowable by the State. Upon
inquiry by Commissioner McCroan regarding the cap, County Administrator Hammond
confirmed the funding cap is still in place. Commissioner Rogers so moved.
Commissioner McCroan seconded the motion. Chairman Quinn called for public
comment or emails. There being no none, the motion passed unanimously.
County Administrator Hammond requested permission from the Board to execute a
contract (agreement) with Southeastern Consulting Engineers for the CEI Services
(Construction Engineering Inspection Services) on County Road 386 (Overstreet Road)
and Pineda Street (Bid #1920-30), which is over his normal spending authority.
Commissioner McDaniel so moved to go with the Administrator’s recommendation.
Commissioner Rogers seconded the motion. Chairman Quinn called for public comment
or emails. There being none, the motion passed unanimously.
County Administrator Hammond discussed the progress made at Beacon Hill Veterans’
Memorial Park. He requested permission to replace both the dune walkover on the beach
side of U.S. Highway 98 and the handicap access ramp; needing permission to bid this
out. He stated that the Board expects to get some F.E.M.A. money for this. Commissioner
McDaniel commended Duke Energy for placing the powerlines underground; requesting
that Mediacom be contacted to work with them in getting their utility lines moved. Upon
inquiry by Chairman Quinn, County Administrator Hammond stated that he will have the
County Attorney send Mediacom a letter regarding this request. Commissioner Rogers
so moved (for the dune walkover and handicap access ramp). Commissioner Rich
seconded the motion. Chairman Quinn called for public comment or emails. There being
none, the motion passed unanimously.
County Administrator Hammond reported that he authorized a repair to an emergency
break-down of a mosquito control truck. Mosquito Control Director Horton appeared
before the Board to report that there was an equipment malfunction and they were not
able to spray; stating that Adapco is the only provider of the equipment that the County
owns. Upon inquiry by Commissioner McDaniel, Mosquito Control Director reported that
the equipment has already been replaced. County Administrator Hammond stated that
the amount is $27,854.63, dated March 30, 2020; reporting that this is a sole-source item
and the County has upgraded to the next (version) technology due to our current system
being outdated. He requested the Board approve this as an emergency repair. Mosquito
Control Director Horton reported that this will be paid from a State grant. Commissioner
McCroan motioned to approve. Commissioner McDaniel seconded the motion. Chairman
Quinn called for public comment or emails. There being none, the motion passed
unanimously.

County Administrator Hammond requested approval of the Board to install signs
designating that no golf carts are allowed on the multi-use path from Pine Street to Sea
Shores Drive; noting they can have (use) that section from Sea Shores Drive and the
north side of Beacon Hill Veterans’ Memorial Park. After discussion, Commissioner
Rogers motioned to follow the recommendation of the County Administrator.
Commissioner McDaniel seconded the motion. Commissioner McCroan stated, for
clarification, this is just from Sea Shores (Drive) to Beacon Hill Veterans’ Memorial Park.
County Administrator Hammond stated that it is from Sea Shores (Drive) to the north
entrance to Beacon Hill Veterans’ Memorial Park. Chairman Quinn called for public
comment or emails. Upon inquiry by Commissioner McDaniel regarding the name of the
street that goes into the park, County Administrator Hammond stated it is Lightkeepers
Drive. Chairman Quinn again called for public comment or emails. There being none, the
motion passed unanimously.
County Administrator Hammond discussed following up with the libraries regarding
reopening; stating that he will provide the members of the Board an update.
Mosquito Control Director Horton appeared before the Board to request approval to
purchase a heavy duty truck and a goose-neck trailer outside of the State contract, to be
paid from Small County Consolidated Solid Waste Grant; reporting that the Florida
Department of Environmental Protection has no objections. Upon inquiry by
Commissioner McDaniel, Mosquito Control Director Horton stated his request to purchase
off State contract is just for the (goose-neck) trailer. Commissioner McCroan so moved.
Upon inquiry by Clerk Norris, Mosquito Control Director Horton stated that the exception
requesting would be product quality. Commissioner Rich seconded the motion. Chairman
Quinn called for public comment or emails. County Administrator Hammond stated the
County is doing this due to public health and emergency situation, under the Bid Policy
and being paid from the Solid Waste Grant; requesting that this be added to the motion.
Commissioner McCroan had no objection to add to his motion. The motion then passed
unanimously.
County Administrator Hammond reported that he will be meeting with Clerk Norris today;
requesting the Board give him and the Chairman the authority to do whatever we need to
do get the Courts going regarding cleaning (requirements). Commissioner McDaniel so
moved. Commissioner Rogers seconded the motion. Chairman Quinn called for public
comment or emails. There being none, the motion passed unanimously.

There being no further business, and upon motion by Commissioner McCroan, second
by Commissioner McDaniel, and unanimous vote, the meeting did then adjourn at 9:36
a.m., E.T.
