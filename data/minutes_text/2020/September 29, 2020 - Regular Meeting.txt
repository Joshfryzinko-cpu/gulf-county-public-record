SMALL-SCALE LAND USE MAP AMENDMENT – CHARLES E. GUILFORD
Planner Collinsworth presented a small-scale land use map amendment from Charles E.
Guilford, Parcel ID #03515-030R; stating the applicant is requesting an approximate 8.58
acre parcel land use designation change from residential low density to mixed
commercial/residential. He reported that the P.D.R.B. voted 4-0 to deny. Chairman Quinn
called for anyone wishing to speak on this item to step forward. Three (3) people from the
audience were recognized. County Attorney Novak discussed having all step forward, as
there is no waiving of the Quasi, and have the Clerk swear them all in at the same time
and then have them go back to their seats. Chairman Quinn called those wishing to speak
to please step forward. With four (4) people stepping forward, County Attorney Novak
requested the Chairman have them state their name and address for the record and
entertain the waiving of Quasi. The following people stated their name and address for
the record: 1) Yvonne Guilford Guillot, of Overstreet; 2) George Robert Guilford, of
Overstreet; 3) Tee Bridges, of Stone’s Home Centers; 4) Adrian Welle, of Century 21
Coast Properties; and 5) John Bridges, of Oracle Design. Chairman Quinn then called for
any objections to the waiving of the Quasi-Judicial Hearing. There being no objection,
Commissioner McDaniel motioned to waive. Commissioner McCroan seconded the
motion. Chairman Quinn called for public comment and emails. There being none, the
motion passed unanimously. Tee Bridges, of Stone’s Home Centers, speaking on behalf
of Charles Guilford appeared before the Board to present material <copy provided to the
Clerk for the record> and discussed the request for a small-scale land use map
amendment from residential low density to mixed commercial/residential; requesting that
the Board overturn the recommendation of the P.D.R.B. He then called Adrian Welle, of
Century 21 Coast Properties to appear to discuss the current status of this and the
surrounding properties. Mr. Welle discussed looking for suitable property for Stone’s
Home Centers to expand their business into Gulf County; pursuing the purchase of
property owned by Charles Guilford in the Overstreet area. He reported that he has letters
of no opposition from neighboring landowners <copy provided to the Clerk for the record>.
John Bridges, Technical Advisor for Stone’s Home Centers appeared before the Board
to discuss the site plan change due to the concerns presented at the P.D.R.B. meeting.
Tee Bridges, of Stone’s Home Centers discussed environmental concerns and
enhancements to take place to the property site, the upcoming bypass to the area, and
the economic impact this business will bring to Gulf County and the State of Florida. Upon
inquiry by Commissioner McDaniel regarding ownership of the property, Tee Bridges
reported that the property owner is Charles Guilford and Stone’s Home Centers has a
contract to purchase the property pending the land use change. Commissioner McDaniel
asked if the property was under the Green Belt (agricultural). Planner Collinsworth stated
the land is residential/low density, not agricultural. Discussion followed regarding the
property and the small-scale map land use request. Don Butler, member of the P.D.R.B.
appeared before the Board to discuss this request; reporting that there were family
members opposing this land use change, therefore the P.D.R.B. voted to deny. Planner
Collinsworth reported that certified letters were sent out to the property owners that abut
this property and some of the property owners were present at the P.D.R.B. meeting and
another (Alice Morehead) sent a letter opposing the request; noting no letters of support
were received at the P.D.R.B. meeting. Yvonne Guilford Guillot, of Overstreet appeared
before the Board to discuss her concerns regarding the land use change request; reading

a letter from her Aunt, Alice Guilford Moorhead, of Overstreet opposing the designation
change <copy was not provided to the Clerk for the record>. Mrs. Guillot stated she is not
in support of this designation change; discussing concerns regarding development in this
area. Commissioner McCroan so moved to extend time. Commissioner McDaniel second
the motion, and it passed unanimously. Mrs. Guillot discussed the property use in the
future; reporting that the letters presented to the Board today are property owners that do
not touch this property line. She requested that the Board not vote to rezone this parcel
of land. Bobby (Robert) Guilford appeared before the Board to discuss the family history
of the parcel; stating there is a better piece of property close to Health Check on either
side of the roadway (County Road 386/Overstreet Road). Planner Collinsworth provided
some answers to some of the comments made by reporting that he has not seen any
development plans regarding the Intercoastal; stating anyone that develops property has
to have stormwater on their own property, the turn lanes/traffic analysis has to be done
before permitted, and there are no trees on this property, they have been clear-cut down.
He stated that if this request goes through then an ordinance will be presented to the
Board at their next regular meeting. County Attorney Novak discussed the substantial and
competent evidence on the record; stating when the Board makes a decision on a small-
scale map amendment, the Board has it clear and referred to page 3 of the packet
received from the applicant of the property owners’ references. He pointed out to the
Board, for the record, that the adjacent property owners that were notified and who is in
support and who opposed this small-scale land use map change. <Commissioner Rich
left the meeting at 10:46 a.m., E.T.> Bobby Guilford appeared before the Board to discuss
the adjacent property owners and the letters submitted. <Commissioner Rich returned to
the meeting at 10:49 a.m., E.T.> Chairman Quinn called for additional public comment.
There being none, Chairman Quinn closed the Public Hearing for comment. Upon request
by Chairman Quinn, Planner Collinsworth stated that the P.D.R.B. recommended by a 4-
0 vote to deny the land use change. County Attorney Novak summarized the formal
reading aloud to the Board <the Finding form on file with the Clerk>. Upon inquiry by
Chairman Quinn, County Attorney Novak stated there will be two (2) votes; one to accept
and close substantial and competent evidence as presented before the Board and second
after the Board deems the record complete and competent evidence, then the Board will
make a determination to grant or deny this map amendment. Chairman Quinn called for
a motion to accept. Commissioner McDaniel so moved. Commissioner Rogers seconded
the motion. Chairman Quinn called for public comment and emails. There being none, the
motion passed unanimously. Commissioner McDaniel motioned to deny based on the
facts that was heard and the recommendation of the P.D.R.B. Commissioner McCroan
seconded the motion for discussion. After discussion, Chairman Quinn called for public
comment and emails. There being none, the motion passed unanimously.

ADVERTISE TO RECEIVE SEALED BIDS – CONTRACTORS / PROPERTY CLEANUP
County Administrator Hammond discussed going out for bids, like the County did for the
F.E.M.A. requirements for the insurance (Hurricane Michael) adjustments; stating the
County will want to pre-qualify contractors, but will still be getting a bid on each specific
property. He requested Board approval to advertise to receive sealed bids to pre-bid
qualified contractors to do this clean up (nuisance ordinance violations). Commissioner
McCroan so moved. Commissioner Rich seconded the motion. Chairman Quinn called
for public comment and emails. There being none, the motion passed unanimously.
ADVERTISE PROPOSED ORDINANCE – GULF COUNTY FLOOD MAPS
County Administrator Hammond discussed the recent changes to the flood maps in
various areas within the County; reporting that the County has to update the Flood
Ordinance to satisfy F.E.M.A. County Attorney Novak presented material for the Board to
review regarding the draft language amending the Ordinance (#2012-08 & #2018-03) and
recommendations made from F.E.M.A. <copy provided to the Clerk for the record>. He
requested the Board to allow the advertisement to hold a Public Hearing amending both
Ordinances at the next regular meeting. Chairman Quinn called for a motion to move
forward with it. Commissioner Rogers so moved. Commissioner Rich seconded the
motion. Chairman Quinn called for public comment and emails. There being none, the
motion passed unanimously. County Administrator Hammond stated it is the
recommendation of staff that the County stick with the two feet (2’) above base flood;
acknowledging it will be a Board decision; asking them to think about it before it is brought
back before the Board probably in February, 2021.
