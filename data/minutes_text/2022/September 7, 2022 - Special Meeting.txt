SEPTEMBER 7, 2022
SPECIAL MEETING
PORT ST. JOE, FLORIDA
The Gulf County Board of County Commissioners met this date in special session with
the following members present: Chairman Sandy Quinn, Jr., Vice Chairman Phil
McCroan, and Commissioners David C. Rich, Ward McDaniel, and Patrick Farrell.
Others present were: County Attorney Jeremy Novak, Clerk Rebecca L. Norris, Director
of Finance & Management Director Leo Bebeau, Deputy Clerk Leanna Cooper, County
Administrator Michael L. Hammond, County Assistant Administrator Clay Smallwood, III,
Deputy Administrator Kari Summers, Building Official Brad Bailey, Gulf County E.M.S.
Director Jody Daniels, Grant Administrator Kaci Rhodes, Grant Administrator Erin
Kennedy, Office of Management & Budget Director Sherry Herring, Mosquito Control
Director Austin Horton, Public Works Director Mark Cothran, Assistant Public Works
Director Raymond Hart, and Sheriff Mike Harrison.
Chairman Quinn called the meeting to order at 4:00 p.m., E.T.
Chairman Quinn led in the Pledge of Allegiance to the Flag.
CITY OF PORT ST. JOE SEWER PLANT CONTAMINATION/HIGHLAND VIEW
CONTAMINATION
County Administrator Hammond reported that at the request of Commissioner Farrell this
topic was placed on today’s agenda, stating that every year when the algae blooms and
dies it causes a problem. Commissioner Farrell discussed concerns regarding the number
of calls received over the years. He stated that if the City of Port St. Joe needs help, then
the County is willing to help find a solution. City of Port St. Joe Mayor Rex Buzzett
appeared before the Board to provide a background history of the Port St. Joe Sewer
Plant. He stated that the City of Port St. Joe is doing all they can, reporting that the City
is not ignoring it and calls are coming into the City as well. After discussion by
Commissioner McDaniel, City Mayor Buzzett stated that the City of Port St. Joe is working
with Deseret Ranches to spray their pasture and to allow Deseret to put cows on the
City’s property for pastureland. He stated that the main thing is to get D.E.P. (FL
Department of Environmental Protection) to allow the City to expand their spray field and
find a way to pump up to the spray fields more efficiently. City Mayor Buzzett discussed
the issues with the current filters installed. Commissioner McCroan discussed the City’s
decision to go solar to cut costs to the citizens of Port St. Joe and the current City Board
members inheriting a situation at which they are doing the best they can with it. He stated
that he supports the City and County working together to find a solution. Chairman Quinn
inquired of a temporary solution. County Administrator Hammond discussed the funds
provided from The St. Joe Company in 2005 for the Water Plant and Advance Wastewater
Treatment Plant, stating this was the solution, to go from an industrial plant to an AWT

(Advanced Wastewater Treatment), and in the last twenty years that AWT has gone
away. He reported that the County has received a $25 million grant to do an AWT and
would be perfect to team with the City of Port St. Joe to do that. County Administrator
Hammond discussed his concerns regarding the smell that Highland View endures every
year. Following discussion, Chairman Quinn called for public comment. John Strayer, of
Highland View appeared before the Board to discuss his concerns regarding the smell
from the Wastewater Treatment Plant that occurs every year. Commissioner McDaniel
stated that it hits during the hot months. After further discussion, Chairman Quinn called
for additional public comment. J.D. Strayer, of Highland View appeared before the Board
to discuss his concerns regarding the smell. Chairman Quinn called for public comment.
There was no further public comment.
ADVERTISE FOR WINDOW – WASHINGTON GYM
County Administrator Hammond reported that Congressman Dunn’s Office called to say
that the $500,000.00 grant request has made it to the next round and in the event, they
do Omnibus bill, it has a good potential to get funded. He requested permission to go out
for advertisements to replace the windows at the Washington Gym to obtain a cost.
Chairman Quinn called for a motion to go ahead and move forward to advertise for the
windows for Washington Gym. Commissioner Farrell so moved. Commissioner McCroan
seconded the motion. Chairman Quinn called for public comment. There being none, the
motion passed unanimously.
GRANTS
County Administrator Hammond discussed page 1 <copy of the packet was provided to
the Clerk for the record> from his packet as being the grant for the purchase of an
ambulance. OMB Director Herring requested permission from the Board to purchase a
demo ambulance for the Addiction Stabilization Unit Pilot Program (Opioid) through the
FL Department of Health, in the amount of $251,979.00. She stated that due to the critical
delivery schedule and an allowance with this program, a procurement exemption has
been provided due to the timing. Chairman Quinn called for a motion to move forward to
purchase this ambulance. Commissioner McDaniel motioned to move forward.
Commissioner Rich seconded the motion. Chairman Quinn called for public comment.
There being no public comment, the motion passed unanimously.
OMB Director Herring discussed pages 15-22 of the packet; noting this is the equipment
to go along with the demo ambulance which includes a stretcher and monitor, providing
two quotes. She stated that due to the timing and trying to obtain the equipment that is
compatible with what the County already has; noting this is under the same program with
the same exemption. Discussion followed regarding the cost of the equipment and using
grant funds for the purchase. Commissioner Rich moved to approve. Commissioner
Farrell seconded the motion. After a discussion regarding the location of the ambulance,
Chairman Quinn called for public comment. There being none, the motion passed
unanimously.

ADMINISTRATIVE UPDATE & REPORT
Assistant County Administrator Smallwood discussed page 23 of the packet,
recommending the Board to award the F.E.M.A. Emergency Berm (Dune) Project (Bid
#2122-26) to the lowest bidder, North Florida Construction, Inc., in the amount of
$4,858,837.00. He stated that this portion is from Rish Park North at the Cape (St. Joseph
Peninsula). Discussion followed regarding the location and scope of work. Chairman
Quinn called for a motion to award the bid. Commissioner McCroan so moved.
Commissioner McDaniel seconded the motion. Chairman Quinn called for public
comment. There being no public comment, the motion passed unanimously.
From page 24, Assistant County Administrator Smallwood requested Board approval to
amend the contract with Roberts and Roberts, Inc. (Bid #1920-06 * Honeyville Borrow
Pit) that extracts sand from the County’s pit and to do the project (Bid #2122-26 * F.E.M.A.
Emergency Berm Dune Project at St. Joseph Peninsula segment) for $16.00 per cubic
yard. Following discussion, Commissioner Farrell so moved. Chairman Quinn called the
motion to move forward. Commissioner Rich seconded the motion. Chairman Quinn
called for public comment. There being none, the motion passed unanimously.
County Administrator Hammond reported that the County did not get funding for the sand
project that was submitted, stating the County did not receive any funding out of this latest
round (Legislative Budget Commission). Commissioner McCroan discussed this funding
request was for money from the State to place emergency sand for the south end.
Discussion followed regarding the Fiscal Year 2022-23 Local Support Grants List <copy
provided to the Clerk for the record> from the (LBC) Legislative Budget Commission.
There being no further business, and upon motion by Commissioner McCroan, second
by Commissioner Farrell, and unanimous vote, the meeting did then adjourn at 4:32 p.m.,
