AUGUST 25, 2022
JOINT WORKSHOP
WARD RIDGE, FLORIDA
The Gulf County Board of County Commissioners held a joint workshop with the City of
Port St. Joe at their City Commission Meeting Room in Ward Ridge. The following Board
members were present: Chairman Sandy Quinn, Jr., Vice Chairman Phil McCroan, and
Commissioners Ward McDaniel and Patrick Farrell <Commissioner David C. Rich was
absent>.
City Commission Board members present were: Mayor Rex Buzzett, and City
Commissioners Scott Hoffman, David Ashbrook, and Eric Langston <City Commissioner
Brett Lowry was absent>.
Also present from the County were: County Attorney Jeremy Novak, Director of Finance
& Management Director Leo Bebeau, Deputy Clerk Leanna Cooper, County Administrator
Michael L. Hammond, County Assistant Administrator Clay Smallwood, III, Deputy
Administrator Kari Summers, and Grant Administrator Kaci Rhodes.
City staff members present were: City Attorney Clinton McCahill, City Manager Jim
Anderson, Chief of Police Jake Richards, Financial Analyst Mike Lacour, and Public
Works Director John Grantland.
City Mayor Buzzett called the meeting to order at 5:00 p.m., E.T.
Chairman Quinn opened the meeting with prayer, and the Port St. Joe Little League
Baseball Team led in the Pledge of Allegiance to the Flag.
FIELD OF DREAMS
City Mayor Buzzett discussed the need for a safe facility for the children, reporting today’s
sole purpose is to discuss the Field of Dreams and the discussion regarding E.S.A.D. will
be a separate meeting. He stated that the City of Port St. Joe set aside $1 million seed
money and would like the County to assist in this project. Following discussion by City
Mayor Buzzett, County Chairman Quinn discussed the need to set a date to discuss
E.S.A.D. (Beacon Hill Sewer), setting it for September 21, 2022, at 5:00 p.m., E.T. in the
County Commission Board Room. County Chairman Quinn then discussed the need for
a facility for the children and his desire to partner with the City of Port St. Joe to
accomplish this goal. He stated that a project in Bay County for seven (7) fields is a $11
million project and his understanding is that the City’s plan is for more than seven (7)
fields. County Chairman Quinn stated that the Board is here today to hear the City’s plans
for funding and the project. County Commissioner McDaniel discussed the various
ballfields in the Wewahitchka area, providing a timeline up to present day regarding the
proposed Field of Dreams. He discussed his concerns regarding the County going into

another joint venture with the City of Port St. Joe and something going afoul, stating that
he wants the County and City to go into this the proper way. He inquired about how the
children would get to the proposed Field of Dreams location, stating it would be nice if the
ballpark was in a more central location. Following discussion by County Commissioner
McDaniel, City Commissioner Hoffman discussed drafting a plan and focusing on what
can be done for the children in Gulf County. He stated that most of the children that are
involved in sports activities live outside the City limits, expressing the importance of the
County partnering in this project. Discussion followed regarding funding this project and
the spending plan from the COVID funding. City Commissioner Hoffman discussed the
possibility of creating an economic community fund where the City and the County pledge
$50,000.00 for the next ten years based on the increase of ad valorem; pledging his
support in this funding source for the next ten years. He inquired if the County is willing
to find the funds and match the City of Port St. Joe’s pledge for this project. City
Commissioner Langston stated that something needs to be done for the community as a
group, find the funding, work together, and get this project done. Discussion followed
regarding this project and various possible funding sources. City Mayor Buzzett called for
public comment. Port St. Joe Skate Group appeared before the Board to ask the City of
Port St. Joe for support of a skate park near the Goodwill store. Discussion followed
regarding the 16th Street Park location, or the location owned by the Gulf County School
Board near the soccer field and tennis courts. Christy McElroy, of Port St. Joe appeared
before the Board to discuss her concerns regarding building a skate park at 16th Street
and 10th Street Park due to wetlands, asking the City Commissioners to take this in
consideration in determining a location for the skate park. City Mayor Buzzett called for
additional public comment. Gentleman (Josh) from the audience came before the Board
to discuss his support in this project. Tim Peterson, of Cape San Blas appeared to
encourage the City and the County to review the location and consider whether this
location (near the College) is the best spot for The Field of Dreams. County Chairman
Quinn discussed this meeting, the vision for this project, and the need for a facility for the
children; stating the County is in support. Robert Branch, of Port St. Joe appeared to state
that he was one of the parties in the lawsuit regarding the 10th Street Park, discussing his
concerns regarding the proposed park project. He encouraged the City and County to
move forward and inquired if T.D.C. funds have been approved for a sports complex.
County Commissioner McCroan stated only the fifth penny. City Mayor Buzzett reported
that the fifth penny has not been obligated to the sports complex (Field of Dreams).
Discussion followed regarding regulations with spending the fifth penny (T.D.C. funds).
County Attorney Novak stated that the State statute and case law lays it down very clearly
on how this penny can be spent and the Board votes on how these funds are to be used
to market the County. After further discussion by Mr. Branch, City Mayor Buzzett thanked
all for attending and stated that another workshop needs to be scheduled for further
discussions regarding The Field of Dreams.

There being no further discussion, the meeting did then adjourn at 6:12 p.m., E.T.
