BUTLER BAY ABANDONMENT RESOLUTION
County Administrator Hammond discussed the Butler Bay Abandonment Resolution
(#2021-17); stating the Board set several criteria before it would go through with the
abandonment, as follows: 1) the property owner would convey some property to the
County; 2) property owner would build a handicap accessible ramp down to the beach;
and 3) the property owner would repave the front section (Butler Bay Road) up to the
bridge where the County would be doing the abandonment. He reported that the property
owner has not performed, it is his understanding the property is up for sale, and unless
the Board tells him different, this abandonment will be in limbo until the property owner
performs or notifies the Board they don’t want to, and it will quash that abandonment.
Commissioner McCroan stated that the property owner (developer) never signed the
agreement. County Administrator Hammond inquired of the Board, if the current property
owner or whomever he sells it to performs all of those things, then the Board will honor
the agreement that the Board approved to abandon that portion of the road (Butler Bay
Road). Commissioner McCroan stated that is fare. County Administrator Hammond
stated that staff will proceed that way.

BUDGET – ANIMAL CONTROL OFFICER
County Administrator Hammond reported the County budgeted for one-half of an
additional animal control officer position, stated that most of the calls come from the City.
He stated that is it time to hire another animal control officer and the Cities (Port St. Joe
and Wewahitchka) need to help pay for it. County Administrator Hammond suggested
that staff request the Cities to contribute $7,500.00 each for the remaining of this fiscal
year and put another officer on. Chairman Quinn inquired about sending a letter
requesting funding for this position. Following discussion, Chairman Quinn called for a
motion to send a letter requesting the Cities to help; by contributing $7,500.00 from each
Municipality (Port St. Joe and Wewahitchka) for a new animal control officer.
Commissioner McDaniel so moved. Commissioner McCroan seconded the motion.
Chairman Quinn called for public comment. There being none, the motion passed
unanimously.
