APRIL 7, 2022
SPECIAL MEETING
PORT ST. JOE, FLORIDA
The Gulf County Board of County Commissioners met this date in special session with
the following members present: Chairman Sandy Quinn, Jr., Vice Chairman Phil
McCroan, and Commissioners David C. Rich, Ward McDaniel, and Patrick Farrell.
Others present were: Director of Finance & Management Director Leo Bebeau, Deputy
Clerk Leanna Cooper, County Administrator Michael L. Hammond, County Assistant
Administrator Clay Smallwood, III, Deputy Administrator Kari Summers, Emergency
Management Director/Fire Coordinator Matt Herring, Gulf County E.M.S. Director Jody
Daniels, Grant Administrator Kaci Rhodes, Grant Administrator Maegen Conners, Office
of Management & Budget Director Sherry Herring, Mosquito Control Director Austin
Horton, Public Works Director Mark Cothran, Assistant Public Works Director Raymond
Hart, and Sheriff Mike Harrison.
Sheriff Harrison called the meeting to order at 11:45 a.m., E.T.
Chairman Quinn led in the Pledge of Allegiance to the Flag.
AWARD BID #2122-11 – OLD BAY CITY ROAD, PHASE IV
County Administrator Hammond stated that staff is ready to award Bid #2122-11 (Old Bay
City Road, Phase IV), in the amount of $261,778.60. Chairman Quinn called for a motion
to award the bid. Commissioner Rich motioned to approve. Commissioner Farrell
seconded the motion. Upon inquiry by Commissioner McDaniel, County Administrator
Hammond stated that the contractor is Roberts & Roberts, Inc. Chairman Quinn called
for public comment. There being no public comment, the motion passed unanimously.
ADMINISTRATIVE REPORT & UPDATE
Grant Director Rhodes discussed page 1 from Administration’s packet <copy provided to
the Clerk for the record>, requesting Board approval to submit two (2) beach project
applications for the NFWF (National Fish & Wildlife Foundation) National Coastal
Resilience Fund Grant (2022). Commissioner McCroan so moved. Commissioner Farrell
seconded the motion. Chairman Quinn called for public comment. There being no public
comment, the motion passed unanimously.
OMB Director Herring presented page 2 of the packet, stating that Citizens of Gulf County
Recovery Team will be providing the gap funding for three (2) H.H.R.P. applicants as:
Kandi Rollins, Windham Meredith and James Littrell. She requested Board approval to
award $50,000.00 purchase assistance for these applicants; reporting that the agreement
will be between the applicant and the long-term recovery group. Chairman Quinn called
for a motion to move forward with awarding it. Commissioner McDaniel so moved.

Commissioner McCroan seconded the motion. Chairman Quinn called for public
comment. There being no public comment, the motion passed unanimously.
Assistant County Administrator Smallwood discussed page 3 of the packet for the Board
to consider Change Order (#1) for the Washington Gym Bathroom Revocations (Bid
#2122-08 * Godfrey Builders, LLC), an increase of $3,750.00 to replace all the galvanized
waterlines. He reported that this amount will be split with the City of Port St. Joe.
Commissioner Farrell so moved. Commissioner McCroan seconded the motion.
Chairman Quinn called for public comment. There being none, the motion passed
unanimously.
County Administrator Hammond stated page 4 is his recommendation to set aside in a
separate fund $100,000.00 from the lot sale (Bid #2122-10 * sale vacant lot 7 Cape Isle
Preserve, Phase I) and $89,000.00 from the net insurance proceeds for the Salinas Park
rebuild project. Chairman Quinn called for a motion to move forward. Commissioner
McCroan so moved. Commissioner Farrell seconded the motion. Chairman Quinn called
for public comment. There being none, the motion passed unanimously.
County Administrator Hammond stated that staff is still working on specifications for the
fueling depot and the County will be taking quotes. He reported that the project will remain
at or under the $400,000.00 budget.
Public Works Director Cothran reported that BCC Waste Solutions will hold two (2)
Amnesty Days, one is set for Saturday, May 28, 2022, from 8:00 a.m. to 4:00 p.m., E.T.
at the Courthouse parking lot. He stated that after the summer, BCC Waste Solutions will
hold the second scheduled date, noting signs will be posted, and a notice will be placed
in customer’s billing.
Commissioner McDaniel noted that some of the money from the sale of the property in
the Salinas Park area will remain in this area. Commissioner McCroan reported that
initially the County was to receive funds from the trail (Recreation Trail Program) grant
but was cut out of the money, which drove the County to use these funds for Salinas Park.
There being no further business, and upon motion by Commissioner McDaniel, second
by Commissioner McCroan, and unanimous vote, the Board did then adjourn at 11:53
a.m., E.T.
SANDY QUINN, JR.
BOCC CHAIRMAN
