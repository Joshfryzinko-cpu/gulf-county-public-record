NOVEMBER 16, 2022
SPECIAL MEETING
PORT ST. JOE, FLORIDA
The Gulf County Board of County Commissioners met this date in special session with
the following members present: Chairman Sandy Quinn, Jr., Vice Chairman Phil
McCroan, and Commissioners David C. Rich, Commissioner Ward McDaniel, and
Commissioner Patrick Farrell.
Others present were: Director of Finance & Management Director Leo Bebeau, Deputy
Clerk Leanna Cooper, County Administrator Michael L. Hammond, County Assistant
Administrator Clay Smallwood, III, Emergency Management Director/Fire Coordinator
Matt Herring, Grant Administrator Kaci Rhodes, Grant Administrator Erin Kennedy, Office
of Management & Budget Director Sherry Herring, Public Works Director Mark Cothran,
and Sheriff Mike Harrison.
Chairman Quinn called the meeting to order at 3:00 p.m., E.T.
Chairman Quinn then led in the Pledge of Allegiance to the Flag.
CITY SEWER / ESAD AGREEMENT
County Administrator Hammond discussed pages 1-4 of the administration packet <copy
provided to the Clerk for the record>, being the proposed memorandum of understanding
with the City of Port St. Joe, stating this agreement is contingent upon the City of Port St.
Joe purchasing the private solid waste system at Gulf Aire (owned by ESAD). He reported
that the County will give the City of Port St. Joe $750,000.00 of BP money toward the
purchase and rehabilitation (of this sewer system) and the County will convey the
perpetual easements for the lift station at (Veterans Memorial) Beacon Hill Park, and the
existing lift stations (Integres System) that currently is being run by the City of Mexico
Beach. County Administrator Hammond stated this agreement is that the City of Port St.
Joe will work with the County, the County will front the money, and then get reimbursed.
He reported that it will close either on or before September 30, 2023. County Administrator
Hammond requested Board approval of the proposed memorandum of understanding
with the City of Port St. Joe and for the Chairman to sign. Chairman Quinn called for a
motion to move forward with the agreement. Commissioner McDaniel so moved to move
forward. Commissioner Rich seconded the motion. Chairman Quinn called for public
comment. Randy Pridgeon, of St. Joe Beach, appeared before the Board to thank the
Board for proceeding and inquired about the Beacon Hill area. County Administrator
Hammond stated the plan now is to convey this easement to the City of Port St. Joe in
order to put in a lift station for Beacon Hill, noting the City of Port St. Joe has a grant for
this project and Beacon Hill will be on (sewer system) in two years. The motion passed
4-0, with Commissioner Farrell abstaining <FORM 8B on file with Clerk>.

ADMINISTRATIVE UPDATE & REPORT
Emergency Management Director Herring reported that the former 911 Coordinator
applied for a grant for the Region 1 NG911 Project, and this grant has been rescinded.
He stated that new quotes have been requested and the best, cheapest quote also
provided a sole-source letter. Emergency Management Director Herring requested
permission from the Board to reapply for this grant and to use the bid exception for this
sole-source vendor (INdigital). Chairman Quinn called for a motion to move forward and
apply. Commissioner Farrell so moved. Commissioner McCroan seconded the motion.
Chairman Quinn called for public comment. There being none, the motion passed
unanimously.
OMB Director Herring discussed page 5 of the administration packet, as a budget
amendment request for the (Gulf County) Sherriff for the old year (FY2021-22) in the
amount of $65,500.53. Commissioner McDaniel so moved. Commissioner Rich seconded
the motion. Chairman Quinn called for public comment. There being no public comment,
the motion passed unanimously.
From pages 7-9 of the packet, OMB Director Herring requested Board approval on bid
(#2223-05) for the S.H.I.P. rehab for Schelley Lester, in the amount of $58,039.00. Upon
inquiry by Commissioner McDaniel, OMB Director Herring stated that the bid award is to
Raymond Driesbach, sole bidder. Chairman Quinn called for a motion to move forward
with Mr. Driesbach. Commissioner Rich so moved. Commissioner Farrell seconded the
motion. Chairman Quinn called for public comment. There being none, the motion passed
unanimously.
County Administrator Hammond noted for members of the Board to pay close attention
to the Consent Agenda (November 22, 2022) for the change regarding H.H.R.P. Round
1 funding. He stated that the proposal will be to use S.H.I.P. applicants for H.H.R.P. funds,
which have been approved by the State.
County Administrator Hammond discussed receiving a thank you letter from Senior
Citizens Executive Director <copy was not provided to the Clerk for the record> Eddie
Fields regarding the contribution increase this year. He reported that he will place a copy
of this letter in the next information packet.
There being no further business, and upon motion by Commissioner McDaniel, second
by Commissioner Farrell, and unanimous vote, the meeting did adjourn at 3:14 p.m., E.T.
