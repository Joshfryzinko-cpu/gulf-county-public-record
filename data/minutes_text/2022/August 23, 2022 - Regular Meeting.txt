memorandum, Dwight Van Lierop, of Acentria Public Risk appeared before the Board to
discuss the proposed renewal premiums. After discussion of the price break-down and
coverage, County Administrator Hammond recommended these changes, understanding
the fact that the County’s deducible is going up to $25,000.00 per occurrence and having
removed quite a number of small items, property in the open (bleachers, playground,
equipment) to reduce the number. Following discussion, County Administrator Hammond
then presented a list to the Board of the items removed, stating it’s mostly decks and
docks <copy provided to the Clerk for the record>. He requested the Board to ratify the
changes made collectively, mainly the deductible changes and the deductions, and then
approve the overall coverage. After discussion by members of the Board, Commissioner
McDaniel made the motion. Chairman Quinn called the motion to move forward.
Commissioner McCroan seconded the motion. Chairman Quinn called for public
comment. There being no public comment, the motion passed unanimously.

RATE CHANGE – BEACH DRIVING PERMITS
From page 4 of his packet, County Administrator Hammond recommended to the Board
to: 1) make all permits (beach driving) effective January 1, 2023; 2) all permits (beach
driving) will be sold from January 1-December 31; 3) all permits (beach driving) will be
uniform, same color, no piece-mill permits; 4) change will be $100.00 for non-homestead
property owner fee; 5) $200.00 for commercial permit fee; 6) $500.00 for non-property
owner out-of-County fee and limited to the first 200 per calendar year; 7) no changes to
homestead residents, disabled or senior citizen property owners or permit fees and; 8)
permits that are not affixed to the vehicle will be confiscated by the Officer and subject to
fines as applicable. Commissioner McCroan inquired regarding the quota. County
Administrator Hammond stated that it only applies to out-of-County $500.00 fee permits
that will be limited to 200; there is no quota for locals, non-local property owners, senior
citizens, or the disabled. Upon inquiry by Commissioner Farrell regarding rental agents
leaving the permit for renters use, County Administrator Hammond stated the intention is
to seize that floating permit, reporting it does not matter if it is a local, renter, or vacationer,
if they have a permit that is loose, he encourages the Officer to seize it and it will be void,
and no refund will be given. Following discussion, County Attorney Novak stated that the
Board just recently amended the Beach Driving Ordinance (#2022-13), giving the Board
and the Administrator the ability to set these rates and permitting without making a change
of language in the Ordinance. He stated that the purpose of affixing the permit to a
particular vehicle, as Gulf County Tax Collector Jenkins receives these applications, is
that they sign an affidavit that they understand the Beach Driving Ordinance, and the
requirements and they are insured. Upon inquiry by Chairman Quinn about the amount
for a beach driving permit for locals, County Administrator Hammond stated that it is
$30.00. Chairman Quinn called for a motion to move forward. Commissioner McDaniel
so moved. Commissioner McCroan seconded the motion. Chairman Quinn called for
public comment. Clerk Norris inquired of the effective date regarding these changes.
County Administrator Hammond stated that his intent is for it to take effect January 1,
2023. Pat Hardman, President of Coastal Community Association appeared before the
Board to inquire if they can pre-buy in December for January permits. County
Administrator Hammond discussed meeting with Tax Collector Jenkins to see what she
suggests, stating he thinks that is a great idea. Chairman Quinn called for additional public
comment. There being none, the motion passed unanimously.

PUBLIC HEARING – ABANDONMENT ALLEYWAY / COWRIES & HELMET
County Attorney Novak stated that the County has advertised the abandonment on
Cowries and Helmet up in Beacon Hill and the recommendation of staff is to kick it back
to the Planning Department and P.D.R.B. to fill out the completion of the application. He
reported that the Board would need to have a public and safety review for access and
proper easement when there is an abandonment of these alleyways. County Attorney
Novak stated that once the applicant has provided the information, and after the Planning
Department reviews it, then it can come back to the Board at a later date. He requested
whether the Board would agree to this recommendation and table the issue for now.
Commissioner McDaniel stated that he would like this to bounce back to the P.D.R.B. to
review further and bring back (a recommendation) to the Board. Chairman Quinn called
for a motion to table this and send it back to the P.D.R.B. Planner Collinsworth stated that
he does not believe abandonment applications go before the P.D.R.B., according to
policy, road abandonments go straight to the Board. He reported that the P.D.R.B. has
not looked at this application per the policy of the Board, stating if the Board wants it to
go to P.D.R.B., then he thinks there needs to be some changes (to the policy). County
Administrator Hammond stated that if the Board tables this, he thinks abandonment
applications need to go before the P.D.R.B. He stated it would allow an extra Public
Hearing and not necessarily for easements for drainage, but the County has gotten bitten
very badly, especially on St. Joe Beach on the alleys between Pine Street and Court
Street in Yon’s Addition, and for years they were active drainage ditches and people
encroached in them, built sheds in them, and put septic tanks in them. County
Administrator Hammond reported that now the County is having massive drainage
problems with no way to get the water out. He stated that it would not hurt for P.D.R.B. to
review these abandonment applications to determine if the County needs to retain
easement for drainage, if necessary, and give the public one more opportunity to review
and possibly object. County Administrator Hammond stated that he is not opposed to this
one but does believe the Board needs to change the policy to send all abandonments
directly to the P.D.R.B. Chairman Quinn called for a motion to table. Commissioner
McDaniel motioned to table. Commissioner Farrell seconded the motion. Chairman Quinn
called for public comment. Sean McBrearty, of Beacon Hill appeared before the Board to
discuss the abandonment request, stating that this is the first he’s hearing of it. County
Attorney discussed the procedure in notification of an abandonment applications. After
further discussion, Commissioner McCroan stated that the applicants request is not near
Mr. McBrearty’s property. Planner Collinsworth reported that it was advertised in the
paper, signs are posted, and all the adjacent property owners that would be affected have
been sent certified letters with return receipt (requested). Chairman Quinn called for any
additional public comment. There being none the motion passed unanimously.
