APPROVAL OF PREVIOUS REQUESTS – CR 30A
Fran Vellanti, of Cape San Blas, appeared before the Board to present a letter <copy
provide to the Clerk for the record>, discussing her previous concerns regarding erosion
on East 30A and the RV (Recreational Vehicle) Ordinance. She also requested that the
Board consider capping the millage for homestead properties to provide relief to those
owners. County Administrator Hammond stated the Board has already voted on the
majority of her inquiries and does not intend to vote on the others. He reported it (RV
Ordinance) is not within a mile of the Gulf (of Mexico), it is zero to a mile of Indian Lagoon,
no different than St. Joe Bay is not the Gulf of Mexico. Following discussion, County
Administrator Hammond discussed the timeline regarding the RV Ordinance and stated
that it was a 3-2 vote. He reported the Board will not stop through traffic on C30 or build
an animal walk-over or walk-under on the road because the County can’t make the wildlife
walk on the designed trail (walk-over). County Administrator Hammond stated the Board
looked at the public’s opinion regarding RVs and the Board decided 3-2 to adopt an RV
Ordinance that took the highest risk rates which were the ones right on the Coast and
eliminated the ones covered by the Bay and Indian Lagoon, reporting that Highland View,
Oak Grove, Indian Pass, past the Raw Bar are out and places like Cape San Blas, St.
Joe Beach, and Beacon Hill are in.

OLD GULF COUNTY COURTHOUSE – WEWAHITCHKA
Ann Johnson, President of Wewahitchka Historical Society appeared before the Board to
inquire if the Board would hold a town hall meeting in Wewahitchka to discuss the Old
Gulf County Courthouse repairs. She reported that the Honeyville Community Center is
available on August 4, 2022, requesting this meeting be held in the evening to allow more
public attendance. Chairman Quinn suggested that if the Board holds a town hall meeting,
it be held in the Board Room so it can be recorded and broadcast. Mrs. Johnson stated
that she doesn’t believe there would be as good of turnout if held in Port St. Joe. County
Administrator Hammond discussed holding a town hall meeting with one (1)
Commissioner, stating when you have multiple Commissioners then it needs to be in a
professional setting with the Clerk present. He discussed the group (Wewahitchka
Historical Society) holding a meeting and then bringing a recommendation before the
Board. Discussion followed regarding what the ask is regarding the Old Gulf County
Courthouse. Commissioner Rich reported that the Old Gulf County Courthouse was the
first roof repaired after Hurricane (Michael), stating the County maintains 300 roofs. Mrs.
Johnson stated that tearing down the back part, the old jail, is a good idea, saving the
front part of the building. Commissioner Rich discussed his concerns regarding the Old
Gulf County Courthouse and stated the County will maintain the yard and upkeep the
paint and keep it looking good from the outside until the Board comes up with a game
plan and funding. Discussion followed. Commissioner McDaniel suggested that Mrs.
Johnson hold a meeting with her board and bring a recommendation back before the
Board to consider. Following discussion, Mrs. Johnson inquired if anything has been done

to keep the wind from blowing rain into the building since the windows have been left
open for ventilation. After a discussion regarding the open windows, Commissioner Rich
stated that the open windows are on the back part of the building (old jail area). Mrs.
Johnson inquired if the old part of the building can be sealed off from the Courtroom.
Commissioner McDaniel stated that the County can address this, stating that Public
Works Assistant Hart can handle it. Commissioner McCroan discussed the roof repair
and the desire to repair the building. After discussion by County Administrator Hammond
regarding tearing off the old part of the building, he then inquired what purpose will this
building serve once it is restored, as there will be upkeep and utilities, stating that no
County offices will go in it. Chairman Quinn suggested that Mrs. Johnson meet with her
board and bring a list of ideas to the Board to review. Discussion followed regarding
holding a meeting with the Board.
