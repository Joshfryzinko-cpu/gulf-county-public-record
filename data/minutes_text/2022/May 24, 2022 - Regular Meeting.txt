BEACH EROSION – STUMPHOLE
Commissioner McCroan discussed receiving calls from residents regarding the beach
erosion past ‘The Rocks’ (Stumphole), stating the County is applying for all available
funding regarding beach erosion. Following discussion, Assistant County Administrator
Smallwood provided a timeline of the beach projects the County has undertaken
beginning in 2008, reporting the County has put $36 million of sand on the beach the past
14 years. He stated that in addition, the County has the F.E.M.A. Emergency Berm (Dune)
project currently being constructed which is a $16 million project. Assistant County
Administrator Smallwood reported that the County has an application in with F.E.M.A. for
Hurricane Sally which is close to $3 million worth of sand and the County has applied for
multiple NFWF (National Fish and Wildlife Foundation) applications for another $3 plus
million for sand. He stated that at the last meeting the Board approved a $15.5 million
D.E.P. project for the erosion control structures which is a $20 plus million project where
some of the funds will come from RESTORE Act and NFWF and the County does have
an active permit application with D.E.P. and Army Corps of Engineers.
