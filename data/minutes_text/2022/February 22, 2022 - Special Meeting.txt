FEBRUARY 22, 2022
SPECIAL MEETING
PORT ST. JOE, FLORIDA
The Gulf County Board of County Commissioners met this date in special session with
the following members present: Chairman Sandy Quinn, Jr., Vice Chairman Phil
McCroan, and Commissioners David C. Rich, Ward McDaniel, and Patrick Farrell.
Others present were: County Attorney Jeremy Novak, Deputy Clerk Leanna Cooper,
County Administrator Michael L. Hammond, County Assistant Administrator Clay
Smallwood, III, Deputy Administrator Kari Summers, County Consultant Don Butler,
Emergency Management Director/Fire Coordinator Matt Herring, Grant Administrator
Kaci Rhodes, Office of Management & Budget Director Sherry Herring, Mosquito Control
Director Austin Horton, Public Works Director Mark Cothran, Assistant Public Works
Director Raymond Hart, and Sheriff Mike Harrison.
Chairman Quinn called the meeting to order at 8:00 a.m., E.T.
INTERVIEWS BID #2122-07 – DISASTER RECOVERY & DEBRIS REMOVAL
SERVICES
Each representative from the top three ranked bidders from Bid #2122-07 for Disaster
Recovery and Debris Removal Services appeared before the Board to discuss their
company’s services, experience, and proposed goals for Gulf County: 1) AshBritt, Inc.
(Matt Gierden); 2) Crowder Gulf (Joe Hass) and 3) D & J Enterprises, Inc. (Chip Starr).
Following each presentation, members of Board asked questions and received responses
from each company represented. County Administrator Hammond stated for the record
that Governor Scott took that contract on day five (5) of Hurricane Michael, but the County
got saddled with the D.O.T. contractor monitor, Metric. He stated the next project for the
Board will be to select and contract with a debris monitor for Gulf County.
ADMINISTRATIVE REPORT & UPDATE
County Attorney Novak discussed a packet provided to the Board from County
Administration <copy provided to the Clerk>, which included a letter from the City Attorney
of Port St. Joe regarding Gulf County Ordinance 2021-05, establishing Gulf County Water
Service District. He then read the letter aloud for the public. County Administrator
Hammond discussed the maps attached to the letter; stating the County purchased
Lighthouse (Utilities) and the service area. He reported that over the years the City of Port
St. Joe has encroached into this service area, but the County continues to service this
area. County Administrator Hammond stated that Gulf County is not taking a single
customer the City of Port St. Joe has today, but areas between U.S. Highway 71 and
Industrial Road, where the City of Port St. Joe has no line, and never had a line, the City
of Port St. Joe wants to include these and areas on C30 that never have been in the City
and encroaching around the hospital (Sacred Heart Hospital). Following discussion,

County Administrator Hammond recommended the Board allow the County Attorney to
respond to the letter stating that County Ordinance (2021-05) stands, and the County is
not taking anything from the City that they didn’t already possess. Discussion followed by
members of the Board and County Administrator Hammond. County Attorney Novak
discussed the County meeting with the City of Port St. Joe and amending the County’s
Ordinance per the City’s request, adding in the agreement. He stated that he does not
know when the vote came to send a letter to the County giving a ten (10) day deadline to
respond. County Administrator Hammond requested permission from the Board to allow
the County Attorney to respond. Commissioner Farrell motioned to let the attorney
respond. Commissioner McDaniel seconded the motion. Chairman Quinn called for public
comment. There being none, the motion passed unanimously. County Administrator
Hammond stated that County Attorney Novak will send a response letter of no change in
our agreement or Ordinance, the City of Port St. Joe keeps their customers, and the
County keeps what area that has been described at the County’s, including the service
area purchased from Lighthouse (Utilities).
County Administrator Hammond reported that the City of Port St. Joe received funds to
extend sewer into Beacon Hill, septic to sewer. He stated that it has been County policy
that Gulf Aire Sewer System come off-line, and the County needs to let the City of Port
St. Joe know if the City does not take Gulf Aire Sea Shores off-line, they cannot skip them
and go on to the County line. County Administrator Hammond discussed drafting an
interlocal agreement to allow the City of Port St. Joe to continue to run sewer to Beacon
Hill, but they must take Gulf Aire off-line. He requested the Board send a letter to the City
of Port St. Joe and County staff will work out a plan with the City of Port St. Joe. Chairman
Quinn called for a motion to send a letter. Commissioner McDaniel so moved.
Commissioner Rich seconded the motion. Commissioner Farrell stated that he will have
to abstain from the motion. Following discussion, Chairman Quinn called for public
comment. There being no public comment, the motion passed 4-0, with Commissioner
Farrell abstaining due to having a small interest in this system <FORM 8B on file with
Clerk>.
There being no further business, and upon motion by Commissioner McDaniel, second
by Commissioner Farrell, and unanimous vote, the meeting did then adjourn at 8:55 a.m.,
