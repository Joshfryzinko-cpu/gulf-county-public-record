ROADSIDE WILDFLOWER PROGRAM
County Administrator Hammond stated that there are a few people with the Garden Club
in the audience today regarding Item #12 on the Agenda, providing a copy of Resolution
(#2017-19) and the letter sent to D.O.T. for the Board to review. Chairman Quinn called
Nancy Jones, of the Port St. Joe Garden Club to step forward to speak. Liz Sparks, of the
Florida Wildflower Foundation appeared before the Board to discuss the wildflower
Resolution that was passed in 2017 and provide a presentation regarding the wildflower
program. Upon motion by Commissioner Farrell, second by Commissioner McCroan, and
unanimous vote, the Board allowed Ms. Sparks additional time to speak. Ms. Sparks then
concluded her presentation discussing the need for a roadside wildflower program in Gulf
County. Discussion followed regarding grass cutting on rights-of-ways. Ms. Sparks
requested the Board follow through with County Administrator Hammond’s letter. After
discussion, Commissioner McDaniel made the motion to submit a letter to D.O.T.
Commissioner McCroan discussed supporting a plan that could be worked out with the
Garden Club. Discussion followed regarding areas to be left for the wildflower program.
Nancy Jones, of the Garden Club stated that the Garden Club is willing to pay for the
signs recognizing the wildflower areas. Commissioner Farrell discussed meeting with the
Garden Club to identity the areas for this program. County Administrator Hammond
inquired, if after they meet to designate these areas, then staff will come back before the
Board for approval to submit the letter to D.O.T. Following discussion, Ms. Jones
requested the Board approve the proposed designed area on U.S. Highway 98 to the
Franklin County line as a pilot program and work on other areas within the County with
Commissioner Farrell. Commissioner McCroan stated he has no objection to
Commissioner Farrell working with the Garden Club to select designed areas within the
County but requested that each Commissioner have input regarding their District. Upon
inquiry by County Administrator Hammond, Commissioner McCroan agreed with the pilot

program being between Twine (Drive) East to the Franklin County line and sending a
letter. County Attorney Novak stated that the Board has already adopted a Resolution,
the letter is the Board’s commitment to the program. Commissioner McCroan seconded
the motion to send a letter. Chairman Quinn called the motion as to send a letter to D.O.T.
and include to allow Commissioner Farrell to work with the Garden Club to identify roads
for the wildflower program. County Administrator Hammond stated the letter will be from
Twine to the Franklin County line. Commissioner McDaniel added this in his motion.
Chairman Quinn called for public comment. There being no public comment, the motion
passed unanimously.

STATUS REPORT – GULF CORRECTIONAL INSTITUTION
GCI Warden Scott Payne appeared before the Board to provide a status update regarding

at Gulf Coast Campus (Gulf/Franklin site) for certification. Warden Payne discussed

unanimous vote, the Board allowed additional time for Warden Payne to speak. Warden

BOARD POLICY – LAND DEVELOPMENT REGULATIONS
Planner Collinsworth reported that in the LDR (Land Development Regulations) it states
on these plat subdivisions that you cannot split minor replat lots as of 2019, but the County
has some older subdivisions where the lots have already been split up. He stated that
when the Ordinance was originally written in 1998 that any of those platted subdivision
prior to April 1998 if they have at least one (1) acre parent parcel that they are allowed to
minor replat those lots. Planner Collinsworth reported that the County can insert this
provision the next time the LDR is amended, as long as staff received Board approval to
allow those subdivisions prior to April 1998 and they meet the one (1) acre parent parcel
to allow them to minor replat these lots and grandfather those in. He stated that he will
need permission from the Board to address that. County Administrator Hammond stated
that: 1) time and date prior to April 1998, and 2) be at least an acre. Discussion followed.
Planner Collinsworth stated that the piece that is split-off still must meet today’s density
requirements. Chairman Quinn called for a motion. Commissioner Farrell so moved.
Commissioner McDaniel seconded the motion. Chairman Quinn called for public
comment. There being none, the motion passed unanimously.
