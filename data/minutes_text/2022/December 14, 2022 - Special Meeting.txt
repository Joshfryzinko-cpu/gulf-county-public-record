DECEMBER 14, 2022
SPECIAL MEETING
PORT ST. JOE, FLORIDA
The Gulf County Board of County Commissioners met this date in special session with
the following members present: Chairman Sandy Quinn, Jr., Vice Chairman Phil
McCroan, and Commissioners David C. Rich, and Patrick Farrell.
Others present were: County Attorney Jeremy Novak, Director of Finance & Management
Director Leo Bebeau, Deputy Clerk Leanna Cooper, County Administrator Michael L.
Hammond, County Assistant Administrator Clay Smallwood, III, Building Official Brad
Bailey, Emergency Management Director/Fire Coordinator Matt Herring, Grant
Administrator Kaci Rhodes, Grant Administrator Erin Kennedy, Office of Management &
Budget Director Sherry Herring, Mosquito Control Director Austin Horton, County Planner
Doug Crane, Assistant Public Works Director Raymond Hart, Sheriff Mike Harrison, and
T.D.C. Executive Director Kelli Godwin.
Chairman Quinn called the meeting to order at 4:00 p.m., E.T.
Chairman Quinn then opened the meeting with prayer and led in the Pledge of Allegiance
to the Flag.
AWARD BID #2223-01 – HOWARD CREEK BOAT RAMP IMPROVEMENTS
From page 1 of the Administrative Update and Report packet <copy provided to the Clerk
for the record>, Assistant County Administrator Smallwood stated that working with low-
bidder, H. G. Harders & Son, Inc., there is a reduction in price for Bid #2223-01 for Howard
Creek Boat Ramp Improvements, reporting that one of the items to assist in this price
reduction was to only build a one-lane boat ramp at this time. He stated that the basin will
be the same width to build a second concrete ramp in the future if needed. Assistant
County Administrator Smallwood reported that the price has been lowered to
$603,912.00, recommending to award to H. G. Harders & Son, Inc., Bid #2223-01 for the
Howard Creek Boat Ramp Improvements. Commissioner Farrell inquired about the
construction time, discussing his concerns regarding the downtime for the boat ramp.
Assistant County Administrator Smallwood stated there will be a lead time on materials,
reporting that the County only gave the contractor three months to build it. Chairman
Quinn called for a motion to award this bid. Commissioner Farrell so moved.
Commissioner Rich seconded the motion. Chairman Quinn called for public comment.
There being none, the motion passed unanimously 4-0>

ADMINISTRATIVE UPDATE & REPORT
Grant Administrator Rhodes discussed page 4 of the packet, requesting the approval of
Change Order #1 with Roberts & Roberts for Pine Street (Bid #2122-14), in the amount
of $4,507.17 to be paid from Secondary Road & Bridge Fund. Commissioner McCroan
so moved. Commissioner Farrell seconded the motion. Chairman Quinn called for public
comment. There being none, the motion passed unanimously 4-0.
Referencing page 6 of the packet, Grant Administrator Rhodes requested Board approval
to advertise to receive sealed bids for construction and CEI services for Stone Mill Creek
Road, S.C.O.P. (Small County Outreach Program) Project and Borders Road, S.C.R.A.P.
(Small County Road Assistance Program) Project. Chairman Quinn called for a motion to
apply and move forward. Commissioner Rich so moved. Commissioner Farrell seconded
the motion. Chairman Quinn called for public comment. There being no public comment,
the motion passed unanimously 4-0.
Assistant County Administrator Smallwood presented page 7 of the packet as a request
for permission from the Board to advertise to receive sealed bids for a new kitchen hood
system at the golf course (County Country Club). Following discussion, Chairman Quinn
called for a motion to move forward. Commissioner Farrell so moved. Commissioner
McCroan seconded the motion. Chairman Quinn called for public comment. There being
none, the motion passed unanimously 4-0.
County Administrator Hammond called the Board to page 11 of the packet. Assistant
Public Works Director Hart discussed the proposed plan for Gulf County Sheriff’s Office
repairs (Bid #2223-04). County Administrator Hammond recommended the full amount,
in the amount of $191,482.50 which includes the Choice 1 add-on, requesting Board
approve this change and for the Chairman to sign the contract with the understanding he
will have to shuffle some funds to cover this amount, to be paid from ARP (AMERICAN
RECOVERY ACT) money. Chairman Quinn called for a motion to approve this contract

bid. Commissioner Farrell so moved. Commissioner Rich seconded the motion. Chairman
Quinn called for public comment. There being no public comment, the motion passed 3-
0, with Commissioner McCroan abstaining due to a business relationship with the
awarded contractor <FORM 8B on file with Clerk>.
County Administrator Hammond discussed the need to fix the Gulf County Sheriff’s
generator. Assistant Public Works Director Hart reported that the purchase price for a
new generator will be around $50,000.00, stating he is waiting on additional quotes.
County Administrator Hammond discussed the possibility of purchasing under State
contract. He discussed staff trying to link the County’s big generator with the E.O.C.
generator, but there are concerns regarding costs. County Administrator Hammond
suggested that the Board purchase a smaller generator that will handle the emergency
operations for the Courthouse Complex, requesting permission from the Board to
purchase a generator, but if they cannot purchase on State Contract, then requested
permission to advertise to receive sealed bids. Upon inquiry by Chairman Quinn, County
Administrator Hammond requested permission from the Board to bid it if the County
cannot purchase generators on State contract for both the Sheriff’s Office and the backup
at the Port St. Joe Courthouse. Commissioner McCroan so moved. Commissioner Farrell
seconded the motion. Chairman Quinn called for public comment. There being no public
comment, the motion passed unanimously 4-0.
County Administrator Hammond stated that he intended to bring a recommendation to
award Charles Avenue and Stebel Drive (Bid #2223-02), but the County has not received
the agreement from the State and is still over budget, reporting that a recommendation
will be presented in January 2023.
Fire Coordinator Herring discussed two asset disposal requests <pages 8-10 of the
packet>, one from South Gulf Fire Department of a 24-foot enclosed trailer (Asset #295-
60) that is unrepairable and a 1992 tanker firetruck (Asset #321-20) that belongs to
Wetappo Creek Fire Department, requesting Board approval to remove these items from
inventory. Chairman Quinn called for a motion to remove these from inventory.
Commissioner Farrell so moved. Commissioner Rich seconded the motion. Chairman
Quinn called for public comment. There being none, the motion passed unanimously 4-
0.
County Administration Hammond reported that Commissioner Farrell requested him to
reach out to Representative Jimmy Patronis regarding coming down or doing a resolution
for David’s (last name was not provided) retirement. He stated that he will work on getting
this scheduled for the January 2023 Board meeting.
County Administrator Hammond stated that Commissioner McCroan had asked him to
get with the County Attorney regarding the RV (recreational vehicles) situation with
multiple RVs on one lot; reporting that he will get with the Gulf County Sheriff’s Office
regarding writing individual citations to the violators onsite and then will follow up with
property owners through the civil/code enforcement side. He stated the County Attorney
said the way it (ordinance) is written, it can be written as a criminal citation for that

particular day and can also do the civil side for the property owner for the code
enforcement and put it on the tax bill if there is a fine or penalty. County Administrator
Hammond stated if Commissioner McCroan has no objections, then he will get with the
Gulf County Sheriff’s Office to establish a plan. Commissioner McCroan had no objection.
Upon discussion by County Administrator Hammond, Commissioner Rich nominated
Commissioner Quinn to serve as Chairman and Commissioner McCroan as Vice
Chairman for 2023. Chairman Quinn clarified Commissioner Rich as making the motion.
Commissioner Farrell seconded the motion. After discussion, Chairman Quinn called for
public comment. There being none, the motion passed unanimously 4-0.
Commissioner McCroan discussed receiving calls regarding striping on Jones
Homestead Road, inquiring if the County has plans to pave this road. County
Administrator Hammond stated that the County will pave both Jones Homestead Road
and Rutherford Road. He reported that it will be 24 months before completion but will try
to do some striping. Commissioner McCroan stated that if the County is going to pave,
then there is no need to strip it at this time. Discussion followed regarding pricing and
scheduling road paving.
There being no further business, and upon motion by Commissioner Farrell, second by
Commissioner McCroan, and unanimous 4-0 vote, the meeting did then adjourn at 4:26
p.m., E.T.
