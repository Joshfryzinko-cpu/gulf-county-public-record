SEPTEMBER 7, 2022
BUDGET HEARING
PORT ST. JOE, FLORIDA
The Gulf County Board of County Commissioners met this date in special budget session
with the following members present: Chairman Sandy Quinn, Jr., Vice Chairman Phil
McCroan, and Commissioners David C. Rich, Ward McDaniel, and Patrick Farrell.
Others present were: County Attorney Jeremy Novak, Clerk Rebecca L. Norris, Director
of Finance & Management Director Leo Bebeau, Deputy Clerk Leanna Cooper, County
Administrator Michael L. Hammond, County Assistant Administrator Clay Smallwood, III,
Deputy Administrator Kari Summers, Building Official Brad Bailey, Gulf County E.M.S.
Director Jody Daniels, Grant Administrator Kaci Rhodes, Grant Administrator Erin
Kennedy, Human Resource Director Brett Lowry, Office of Management & Budget
Director Sherry Herring, Mosquito Control Director Austin Horton, Property Appraiser
Mitch Burke, Public Works Director Mark Cothran, Assistant Public Works Director
Raymond Hart, and Sheriff Mike Harrison.
Chairman Quinn called the meeting to order at 5:01 p.m., E.T.
TENTATIVE 2022-23 OVERVIEW
County Administrator Hammond discussed the proposed budget, stating that it reduces
taxes, funds everyone in a prudent way, provides increases for County employees/cost
of living rates, increases across the board for Constitutional Officers, and increases some
outside agencies. OMB Director Herring provided each member of the Board with a
proposed budget book, reporting that this book is also posted on the County’s website,
as required by TRIM <copy provided to the Clerk for the record>. She stated this proposed
budget book has: 1) a proposed aggregate millage rate of 6.8934 mills, which is 13.76%
above the current aggregate rolled back rate of 6.0596; 2) operating millage rate for
County-wide at 6.5 mills; 3) each Dependent Fire District: St. Joseph, Tupelo, Overstreet,
and Howard Creek at .5 mills; and 4) proposed M.S.T.U. Debt Service millage rates are
Gulfside Beachfront at .6786, Gulfside Interior at .5689, and Bayside at .5723. OMB
Director Herring stated this is the maximum millage rate that the Board may adopt;
providing a few reasons for the increase in Ad Valorem Revenues as: 1) Sheriff’s Office;
2) Clerk to the Board; 3) County Jail (Detention Facility); 4) FRS Retirement rate
increases; 5) insurance cost increases; 6) pay raises; 7) Capital Outlay Projects; 8)
E.M.S. services; and 9) Road and Street Facilities. She reported that today’s meeting is
the first and tentative Public Hearing for this budget, noting that page 4 of the proposed
budget book is the balanced totals for each fund. OMB Director Herring stated: 1) Fund
301, the insurance settlements from Hurricane Michael, are a carry forward balance and
reserved for those expenditures and do not have a set time on when repair projects will
take place; 2) growth and stability of reserves are necessary to maintain and improve our
credit worthiness for future bond issues; 3) cash flow anticipated reimbursements from

F.E.M.A. and a lot of large grant projects that are in place that Reserves is funding until
reimbursements are received. She stated that any amendment made during tonight’s
meeting will need to have offsetting reductions to keep everything in balance. OMB
Director Herring requested permission from the Board to allow her to make line-item
adjustments, keeping the funds in balance from any changes from tonight’s meeting.
Chairman Quinn opened the Public Hearing to the public, calling for public comment
regarding the proposed budget. There being no public comment, Chairman Quinn closed
the Public Hearing to the public. County Administrator Hammond discussed COLA (cost-
of-living adjustment) and merit raises based on evaluations; requesting the Board allow
staff to make minor changes, add payroll changes, and open enrollment changes to the
budget which will not change any of the numbers other than the individual line-items.
Chairman Quinn called for a motion for the Board to allow staff to make those changes.
Commissioner McDaniel so moved. Commissioner Farrell seconded the motion.
Chairman Quinn called for public comment. There being no public comment, the motion
passed unanimously. Chairman Quinn called again for public comment. There being
none, Chairman Quinn then closed the Public Hearing to the public.
RESOLUTION – TENTATIVE 2022-23 MILLAGE RATES
OMB Director Herring read the proposed resolution adopting the tentative millage rates
for Board consideration. Commissioner McDaniel discussed Tupelo and Overstreet Fire
Districts’ desire to roll over funds; inquiring if this is automatic. OMB Director Herring
stated that the projections are in this tentative budget book; having worked with each Fire
Chiefs to confirm those numbers for rollover. Upon request by Commissioner McCroan,
County Administrator Hammond stated it is a reduction from 6.8 to 6.5 mills. Chairman
Quinn called for a motion to adopt this resolution. Commissioner McDaniel so moved.
Commissioner McCroan seconded the motion. Chairman Quinn called for public
comment. There being no public comment, the motion passed unanimously at 5:11 p.m.,
E.T., as follows:

FINAL BUDGET HEARING – SEPTEMBER 26, 2022
After discussion and staff recommendation, Chairman Quinn called for a motion to set the
last hearing date for September 26, 2022, at 5:01 p.m., E.T. Commissioner McCroan so
moved. Commissioner Farrell seconded the motion. Chairman Quinn called for public
comment. There being no public comment, the motion passed unanimously.
