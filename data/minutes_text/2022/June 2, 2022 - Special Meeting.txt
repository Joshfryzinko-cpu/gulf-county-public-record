JUNE 2, 2022
SPECIAL MEETING
PORT ST. JOE, FLORIDA
The Gulf County Board of County Commissioners met this date in special session with
the following members present: Chairman Sandy Quinn, Jr., Vice Chairman Phil
McCroan, and Commissioners David C. Rich, Ward McDaniel, and Patrick Farrell.
Others present were: County Attorney Jeremy Novak, Clerk Rebecca L. Norris, Director
of Finance & Management Director Leo Bebeau, County Administrator Michael L.
Hammond, County Assistant Administrator Clay Smallwood, III, Deputy Administrator
Kari Summers, Building Official Brad Bailey, Emergency Management Director/Fire
Coordinator Matt Herring, Grant Administrator Kaci Rhodes, Grant Administrator Maegen
Conners, Office of Management & Budget Director Sherry Herring, Mosquito Control
Director Austin Horton, and Assistant Public Works Director Raymond Hart.
Chairman Quin called the meeting to order at 9:00 a.m., E.T.
Chairman Quinn then opened the meeting with the Pledge of Allegiance to the Flag.
RESILIENT FLORIDA GRANT
County Administrator Hammond discussed page 1 of his packet <packet provided to the
Clerk for the record>; recommending the Board accept a D.E.P. Resilient Florida Grant
(for Gulf County Roads and Stormwater Improvements) and allow the Chairman to sign
all agreements, identifying the match to come from Secondary Road and Bridge, in the
amount of $47,167.00. Commissioner Farrell so moved. Commissioner McDaniel
seconded the motion. Chairman Quinn called for public comment. There being none, the
motion passed unanimously.
MRD TASK ORDER #5
OMB Director Herring, referencing page 2, requested Board approval to ratify Task Order
#005 with MRD Associates for additional Sea Turtle Monitoring (Bid #1920-36 * Coastal
Engineering Consulting Services), to be paid from the NFWF (National Fish & Wildlife
Foundation) grant. Commissioner McCroan so moved. Commissioner Farrell seconded
the motion. Chairman Quinn called for public comment. There being none, the motion
passed unanimously.
ADMINISTRATIVE UPDATE & REPORT
Referencing page 3 of the packet, OMB Director Herring requested the Board to approve
an extension request of the NFWF project previously discussed (Bid #1920-36 * Coastal
Engineering Consulting Services), reporting it is currently set to expire July 2023 and the
request would extend it to December 31, 2023, which will allow the required (Sea Turtle)
monitoring to be completed. Commissioner McDaniel so moved. Commissioner Rich
seconded the motion. Chairman Quinn called for public comment. There being none, the
motion passed unanimously.

County Administrator Hammond recommended to award Bid #2122-13 (Gulf County
Water System Improvements) to the low-bidder, Marshall Brothers Construction, in the
amount of $1,069,200.00, contingent upon Florida D.E.P. for the State revolving fund loan
agreement being approved (page 4 of the packet). Commissioner McCroan made the
motion. Commissioner Rich seconded the motion. Chairman Quinn called for public
comment. There being none, the motion passed unanimously.
Mosquito Control Director Horton recommended to award the Waste Hauling Services
(Bid #2122-23) to the sole-bidder Pritchett Trucking, Inc. Following discussion, Mosquito
Control Director Horton reported that this will be a one-year contract with an option of two
(2) one-year renewals. County Administrator Hammond noted this bid is a significant
increase compared to what the County is currently paying, therefore it will have to be
monitored. Following discussion about the current fuel prices, Chairman Quinn called for
a motion to move forward with it. Commissioner McDaniel so moved. Commissioner
Farrell seconded the motion. Chairman Quinn called for public comment. There being
none, the motion passed unanimously.
County Administrator Hammond discussed a recent drowning, stating the County
received a request for assistance for the Search and Rescue regarding a County loaned
trailer that will be stored at the City (of Wewahitchka) yard.
Emergency Management Director Herring appeared before the Board to provide a status
update regarding the (tropical) disturbance in the Gulf of Mexico, reporting that South
Florida is looking to receive eight inches (8”) of rain over the weekend. He stated that Gulf
County will experience rip-currents.
Emergency Management Director Herring stated that the Search and Rescue trailer is
inventoried under Dalkeith (Fire Department), but a plan was created to store the trailer
and Search and Rescue will store their equipment in it.
County Administrator Hammond reported that the County’s Lobbyist says the Governor
will sign the (State) budget today at 3:30 p.m., E.T. Chairman Quinn stated that once the
Governor signs it Board members cannot file Form 6 (Financial Disclosure) electrically.
There being no further business, and upon motion by Commissioner McDaniel, second
by Commissioner Farrell, and unanimous vote, the meeting did then adjourn at 9:08 a.m.,
E.T.
SANDY QUINN, JR.
