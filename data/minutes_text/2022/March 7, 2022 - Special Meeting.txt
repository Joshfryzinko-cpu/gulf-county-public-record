MARCH 7, 2022
SPECIAL MEETING
PORT ST. JOE, FLORIDA
The Gulf County Board of County Commissioner met this date in special session with the
following members present: Chairman Sandy Quinn, Jr., Vice Chairman Phil McCroan,
and Commissioners David C. Rich, Ward McDaniel, and Patrick Farrell.
Others present were: County Attorney Jeremy Novak, Clerk Rebecca L. Norris, Director
of Finance & Management Director Leo BeBeau, Deputy Clerk Leanna Cooper, County
Administrator Michael L. Hammond, County Assistant Administrator Clay Smallwood, III,
Emergency Management Director/Fire Coordinator Matt Herring, Grant Administrator
Kaci Rhodes, Office of Management & Budget Director Sherry Herring, Mosquito Control
Director Austin Horton, Public Works Director Mark Cothran, Assistant Public Works
Director Raymond Hart, Sheriff's Department Lieutenant Tim Wood.
Chairman Quinn called the meeting to order at 3:30 p.m., E.T.
Chairman Quinn then led in the Pledge of Allegiance to the Flag.
BURN BAN
County Administrator Hammond reported that after consultation with the Chairman, the
County implemented a burn ban on Saturday (March 5, 2022) and received a Declaration
on a State of Emergency from the Governor which included Gulf County. Emergency
Management Director Herring provided an update regarding the wildfire at Bertha
Swamp. He stated that the fire is in Gulf, Bay and Calhoun Counties and moving north,
reporting that rains are expected on Wednesday. County Administrator Hammond
requested the Board continue the burn ban, recommending the Board’s extension, or the
dropping it, and could be left up to him and the Chairman. He then recommended a seven
(7) day burn ban extension to the Board. Chairman McDaniel inquired of the effective
date for the seven-day extension. County Administrator Hammond stated it would be
effective today and would allow him and the Chairman decide to drop it if the County
receives a lot of rain, or extend it without the need to call the Board back in. Commissioner
McDaniel made the motion approving County Administrator Hammond’s
recommendation. Commissioner Farrell seconded the motion. Chairman Quinn called for
public comment. There being none, the motion passed unanimously. County
Administrator Hammond reported that Gulf County E.MS. participated in the evacuation
of the Sims Veterans’ Nursing Home. He stated that E.M.S. Director Daniels will provide
an update at the next meeting.
INSURANCE SETTLEMENT AGREEMENT
County Administrator Hammond reported that a settlement has been met with the
insurance company (damage from Hurricane Michael), recommending the Board allow

the Chairman to sign the settlement and release. Chairman Quinn called for a motion to
move forward. Commissioner McCroan so moved. Commissioner Rich seconded the
motion. Chairman Quinn called for public comment. There being none, the motion passed
unanimously.

Grant Administrator Rhodes stated page 8 of the packet is a request to advertise to
receive sealed bids for construction and CEI services for Pine Street S.C.R.A.P. Project.
Commissioner McCroan noted the need to stick pretty hard on the condition that they pull
a permit on that building, before doing the road. County Administrator Hammond agreed,
stating the County will not allow the contractor to start work without pulling a building
permit. He stated the agreement with the Federal Government is that the County will not
start construction on that (Cessna Drive) road or infrastructure which is includes power
and water. Chairman Quinn called for a motion. Commissioner Farrell so moved (Pine
Street S.C.R.A.P. Project). Commissioner Rich seconded the motion. Chairman Quinn
called for public comment. There being none, the motion passed unanimously.
Grant Administrator Rhodes stated page 9 of the packet is what Commissioner McCroan
was speaking about, requesting permission to advertise to receive sealed bids for
construction of Cessna Drive Improvements. County Administrator Hammond reported
that the County will not award until a building permit is pulled. Chairman Quinn called for
a motion to move forward to request to advertise. Commissioner McCroan so moved.
Commissioner Farrell seconded the motion. Chairman Quinn called for public comment.
There being none, the motion passed unanimously.

Grant Administrator Rhodes discussed page 10 being a request to the Board to advertise
to received sealed bids for construction of an In-line Booster Station for the Gulf County
Water Department. Commissioner Farrell inquired of the location. County Administrator
Hammond stated this will be on the lease property that the County received from the State
around the Troy Deal property. Discussion followed. Chairman Quinn called for a motion
to move forward with the request to advertise. Commissioner Rich so moved.
Commissioner Farrell seconded the motion. Chairman Quinn called for public comment.
There being none, the motion passed unanimously.
Assistant County Administrator Smallwood provided an update on the pipe (culvert)
project on C30 close to the (Indian Pass) Raw Bar, reporting that the contractor mobilized
last week and is starting to line the pipe. Upon inquiry by Commissioner McCroan,
Assistant County Administrator Smallwood reported that traffic is open both ways.
County Administrator Hammond discussed the increase cost of fireworks, inquiring if the
Board would like for staff to find some T.D.C. funds to try and mitigate the increase.
Members of the Board had no objection. County Administrator Hammond stated that staff
will look to find funds for this year’s fireworks for July.
Commissioner McCroan inquired if staff had any update regarding the T.D.C. bill for the
Sheriff (fire and E.M.S.). County Administrator Hammond stated that he has not heard
anything in the last week, but it is in the House side in budget negotiations. He reported
that the Airport Bill did make it through and now they just need to check for the veto.
Commissioner McDaniel inquired whether staff heard anything further on the Senate
redistricting. County Administrator Hammond reported that he has not heard anything
recently but understands this is in a flux.
There being no further business, and upon motion by Commissioner McDaniel, second
by Commissioner Farrell, and unanimous vote, the meeting did then adjourn at 3:43 p.m.,
