FEBRUARY 10, 2022
SPECIAL MEETING
PORT ST. JOE, FLORIDA
The Gulf County Board of County Commissioners met this date in special session with
the following members present: Chairman Sandy Quinn, Jr., Vice Chairman Phil
McCroan, and Commissioners David C. Rich, Ward McDaniel, and Patrick Farrell.
Others present were: County Attorney Jeremy Novak, Clerk Rebecca L. Norris, County
Administrator Michael L. Hammond, County Assistant Administrator Clay Smallwood, III,
Deputy Administrator Kari Summers, Gulf County E.D.C. Director Jim McKnight, Grant
Administrator Kaci Rhodes, Grant Administrator Maegen Conners, Office of Management
& Budget Director Sherry Herring, Mosquito Control Director Austin Horton, Public Works
Director Mark Cothran, Assistant Public Works Director Raymond Hart, Sheriff Mike
Harrison, and T.D.C. Executive Director Kelli Godwin.
Sheriff Harrison called the meeting to order at 3:01 p.m., E.T.
Chairman Quinn led in the Pledge of Allegiance to the Flag.
PIGGY-BACK PORT AUTHORITY – GOURAS CONTRACT
County Administrator Hammond stated that the Port Authority is requesting, if deemed
appropriate, to piggy-back on the County’s C.D.B.G.-DR contract with Gouras (Bid #1920-
32 * C.D.B.G.-Disaster Recovery & Economic Development Grant Writing Services) and
other contracts that have been done in such a way that can be piggy-backed. He stated
that this would be specific to the Raise Grant, which is based on the old Tiger Grant.
County Administrator Hammond recommended to allow the Port Authority to piggy-back
on the County’s contract. Chairman Quinn called for a motion to allow the Port Authority
to piggy-back on the County’s contract. Commissioner Farrell so moved. Commissioner
McDaniel seconded the motion. Chairman Quinn called for public comment. There being
none, the motion passed unanimously. County Administrator Hammond requested the
Board to approve a one (1) year extension (with Gouras) and allow staff to do new work
orders, added scope of work as needed. Chairman Quinn called for a motion to extend
the contract for one (1) year. Commissioner Farrell so moved. Commissioner Rich
seconded the motion. Chairman Quinn called for public comment. There being none, the
motion passed unanimously.
AWARD BID #2122-08 – WASHINGTON GYM BATHROOM RENOVATIONS
County Administrator Hammond reported that the City (of Port St. Joe) has agreed to the
increase in bid amount, therefore the County will increase about $8,000.00 on this
contract, purchase a shed, and a water heater. He stated this project with be split 50/50
with the City (of Port St. Joe). Upon inquiry by Commissioner Farrell regarding who the
awarded bidder would be, Assistant County Administrator Smallwood stated Godfrey

Builders, LLC. Commissioner McCroan so moved. Commissioner McDaniel seconded the
motion. Chairman Quinn called for public comment. There being none, the motion passed
unanimously.
C.D.B.G.-DR GRANT AGREEMENT
Grant Director Rhodes discussed the recommendation <copy provided to the Clerk for
the record> by staff to allow the Board to accept and allow the Chairman to sign the grant
agreement for D.E.O. (Department of Economic Opportunity) C.D.B.G.-DR for the water
transmission and booster station project. Chairman Quinn called for a motion to move
forward. Commissioner Rich so moved. Commissioner McCroan seconded the motion.
Chairman Quinn called for public comment. There being none, the motion passed
unanimously.
AMENDMENT #1 – D.E.P. RED TIDE GRANT AGREEMENT
Grant Director Rhodes recommended the Board to accept and allow the Chairman to sign
Amendment #1 of the D.E.P. Red Tide Grant Agreement. Following discussion,
Commissioner McDaniel so moved. Commissioner McCroan seconded the motion.
Chairman Quinn called for public comment. There being none, the motion passed
unanimously.
COUNTY ROAD 387 – H.M.G.P. GRANT AGREEMENT
Assistant County Administrator Smallwood recommended <copy provided to the Clerk for
the record> that the Board accept and allow the Chairman to sign the agreement with
D.E.M. (FL Division of Emergency Management) for County Road 387 (Doc Whitfield
Road Howard Creek) H.M.G.P., stating these funds will be used to add additional box
culverts along this road. Chairman Quinn called for a motion to move forward.
Commissioner Rich so moved. Commissioner Farrell seconded the motion. Chairman
Quinn called for public comment. There being none, the motion passed unanimously.
ADMINISTRATIVE REPORT & UPDATE
County Attorney Novak reported that the County Health Trust Board has authorized for
the next five (5) years to support it (dialysis center) for $100,000.00 per year. He
requested Board approval and authorization for the Chairman to sign the final
Memorandum of Understanding. Commissioner McDaniel inquired if the County had
received a commitment from Franklin County. E.D.C. Director McKnight appeared before
the Board to report that Franklin County is a little reluctant, discussing negotiations with
Franklin County regarding their support of this facility. He stated that he does not believe
the County will receive upfront money from Franklin County but is hopeful the County will
receive funds at the end of the year. Following discussion, Chairman Quinn called for a
motion to allow himself to sign the final agreement once reviewed by the County Attorney.
Commissioner McCroan so moved. Commissioner McDaniel seconded the motion.
Chairman Quinn called for public comment. There being none, the motion passed
unanimously. County Administrator Hammond discussed his concerns with attempts to
team with Franklin County on various projects but got clapped in the face each time.
Following discussion, Commissioner McCroan inquired about a timeframe on the dialysis

center. E.D.C. Director McKnight discussed the need to execute the agreement, and that
April 1, 2022 is a possibility.
County Administrator Hammond discussed the mitigation regarding insurance claims
(regarding Hurricane Michael), requesting a closed-door executive session meeting after
the next regular Board meeting (February 22, 2022). County Attorney Novak requested a
vote and instruction from the Board to advertise and setup the necessary cost of a court
reporter. Chairman Quinn called for a motion to move forward. Commissioner Rich so
moved. Commissioner McCroan seconded the motion. Chairman Quinn called for public
comment. There being none, the motion passed unanimously.
County Administrator Hammond requested <copy provided to the Clerk for the record>
the Board call a special meeting for 8:00 a.m., E.T. before the regular meeting to conduct
interviews with AshBritt, Inc., Crowder Gulf, and D & J Enterprises, Inc. for the Disaster
Recovery and Debris Removal, Bid #2122-07. Chairman Quinn called for a motion to
have a special meeting before the regular Board meeting on February 22, 2022, at 8:00
a.m., E.T. Commissioner McDaniel so moved. Commissioner Farrell seconded the
motion. Chairman Quinn called for public comment. There being none, the motion passed
unanimously.
County Administrator Hammond presented a request <copy provided to the Clerk for the
record> to reject Bid #2122-09 for the deep well installation at Beaches Fire Department.
Chairman Quinn called for a motion to reject Bid #2122-09. Commissioner McDaniel
made the motion. Commissioner Farrell seconded the motion. Chairman Quinn called for
public comment. There being none, the motion passed unanimously.
OMB Director Herring presented a request <copy provided to the Clerk for the record>
for permission to submit an extension request on three (3) beach grants with D.E.P.,
which are 18GU1, 19GU1 and 20GU1 for the beach renourishment project. Chairman
Quinn called for a motion to extend these requests. Commissioner McCroan so moved.
Commissioner Farrell seconded the motion. Chairman Quinn called for public comment.
There being none, the motion passed unanimously.
County Administrator Hammond provided a status update regarding the Legislative
meeting, discussing the T.D.C. tax bill.
County Administrator Hammond discussed proposed projects at the Jetty in front of the
Old Mill Site on the Bird Island Spoil Site and the dredge project coming in July 2022.
E.D.C. Director McKnight appeared before the Board to report that a dredge barge
contract is to follow the Staten Island Ferry project, and they waiting to see if Eastern
Shipbuilding will receive the contract for the Coast Guard Cutters.

There being no further business, and upon motion by Commissioner Farrell, second by
Commissioner McCroan, and unanimous vote, the meeting did then adjourn at 3:25 p.m.,
