The Gulf County Board of County Commissioners met this date in regular session with
the following members present: Chairman Sandy Quinn, Jr., Vice Chairman Phil
McCroan, and Commissioners David C. Rich, Ward McDaniel, and Patrick Farrell.

Chairman Quinn opened the meeting with prayer and led in the Pledge of Allegiance to
the Flag.
CONSENT AGENDA

public comment. Clerk Norris presented a request to the Board regarding corrections to
the minutes <copy provided to the Clerk for the record>. County Attorney Novak then

Federal Holiday. Chairman Quinn called for a motion to accept the Consent Agenda with
those corrections (minutes) and those changes. Commissioner McDaniel so moved.
Commissioner Farrell seconded the motion. Chairman Quinn called for public comment.

RECOGNITION – WARD MCDANIEL
Chairman Quinn discussed the years of service Commissioner McDaniel has provided to
Gulf County, requesting County Attorney Novak to read the proposed proclamation
acknowledging Ward McDaniel’s years of service to the citizens of Gulf County <copy
provided to the Clerk for the record>. County Attorney Novak then read the proposed
proclamation aloud for the Board to consider. Chairman Quinn called for a motion to adopt
this proclamation. Commissioner McCroan so moved. Commissioner Rich seconded the
motion. Chairman Quinn called for public comment. There being no public comment, the
motion passed 4-0, with Commissioner McDaniel abstaining <FORM 8B on file with
Clerk>. Members of the Board then presented Commissioner McDaniel with a plague and
provided comments regarding Commissioner McDaniel and his service. Commissioner
McDaniel discussed the current Board members and the management, thanking
Chairman Quinn, County staff, and Constitutional Officers. County Administrator
Hammond discussed the past 12 years with members of the Board, thanking
Commissioner McDaniel. County Attorney Novak stated that it has been an honor to work
with Commissioner McDaniel over the past 12 years. Clerk Norris thanked Commissioner
McDaniel for his service to the community and the people of Gulf County, stating it has
been an honor serving with him.
