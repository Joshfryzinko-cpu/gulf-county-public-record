JULY 18, 2022
SPECIAL MEETING
PORT ST. JOE, FLORIDA
The Gulf County Board of County Commissioners met this date in regular session with
the following members present: Chairman Sandy Quinn, Jr., Vice Chairman Phil
McCroan, and Commissioners David C. Rich, Ward McDaniel, and Patrick Farrell.
Others present were: County Attorney Jeremy Novak, Director of Finance & Management
Director Leo Bebeau, Deputy Clerk Leanna Cooper, County Administrator Michael L.
Hammond, County Assistant Administrator Clay Smallwood, III, Deputy Administrator
Kari Summers, Building Official Brad Bailey, Gulf County E.M.S. Director Jody Daniels,
Grant Administrator Kaci Rhodes, Grant Administrator Maegen Conners, Office of
Management & Budget Director Sherry Herring, Mosquito Control Director Austin Horton,
Property Appraiser Mitch Burke, Property Appraiser Office IT Tech Dennis Barfield, Jr.
Public Works Director Mark Cothran, Assistant Public Works Director Raymond Hart,
Sheriff Mike Harrison, Supervisor of Elections John Hanlon, and T.D.C. Executive
Director Kelli Godwin.
Chairman Quinn called the meeting to order at 9:01 a.m., E.T.
Chairman Quinn then led in the Pledge of Allegiance to the Flag.
ADMINISTRATIVE UPDATE & REPORT
County Administrator Hammond presented an email thread passed to the County from
Tax Collector Jenkins <copy provided to the Clerk for the record>, a request to waive
interest and penalties on personal property located at the old MTI (Material Transfer, Inc.)
site. He recommended that the Board deny this request. Upon request by Chairman
Quinn, County Administrator Hammond reported $224,476.69 is the total amount due of
which $118,144.55 is interest and the balance of the taxes and fees are $85,065.71 and
$21,266.43. Upon inquiry by Chairman Quinn, County Administrator Hammond stated
that they (Far Away Port, LLC) are asking for a waiver of $118,144.55, reporting that this
amount would be split between the County, School Board, and other taxing authorities,
including (Northwest Florida) Water Management (District). Commissioner McCroan
inquired if the recommendation from County Administrator Hammond was to deny.
County Administrator Hammond confirmed it is his recommendation to deny this waiver
request. Discussion followed regarding the percentage of the interest and fees that is
County funds. County Administrator Hammond stated that he does not think a partial
waive is an option, as the funds would be split-out the way the taxes are paid. Discussion
followed regarding delinquent taxes and the rules that are set in place. Upon inquiry by
Commissioner McDaniel regarding whether any taxes have been paid, County
Administrator Hammond reported that they (Fare Away Port, LLC) purchased the old MTI
property in a distressed sale knowing that there was a tax lien on the property.

Commissioner Rich motioned to deny. Commissioner McDaniel seconded the motion.
Chairman Quinn called for public comment. There being no public comment, the motion
passed unanimously.
Assistant County Administrator Smallwood discussed meeting with the Developer (Scott
Seymour of Palm Point Development, LLC) that is working on the property around Butler
Bay Road and stated that one of the requirements before the County will abandon this
road is for the Developer to repair the bridge. He reported that the Developer has provided
plans to the County and is ready to submit permits (for the bridge repairs). Assistant
County Administrator Smallwood stated that since the County owns the road, the County
should be the permittee for this project, requesting Board approval. He also stated there
will be no cost to the County by placing our name on the permit. Upon inquiry by
Commissioner McCroan regarding abandoning this road, County Attorney Novak
discussed the Resolution (#2021-17) the Board passed which has conditions and terms,
that the Developer must honor, and once these conditions are all complete, the Board will
finalize the abandonment. Following discussion, Chairman Quinn called for a motion to
move forward with the permitting. Commissioner Rich so moved. Commissioner McDaniel
seconded the motion. Chairman Quinn called for public comment. There being none, the
motion passed unanimously.
Assistant County Administrator Smallwood discussed the price to pave a portion of Dairy
Farm Road, recommending moving forward at the amount of $96,600.00, to be paid from
the balance of the (2015 Series Gas Tax Bond) Road Bond and the remaining to be paid
from Secondary Road and Bridge. County Administrator Hammond reported that the
Board previously approved this project at the cost of $70,000.00 plus, but due to the price
increases the amount has increased, requesting Board to approve the difference.
Commissioner McDaniel motioned to approve the increase. Commissioner Farrell
seconded the motion. Chairman Quinn called for public comment. There being no public
comment, the motion passed unanimously.
PROPOSED 2022-23 BUDGET
County Administrator Hammond provided the highpoints to the Board regarding the
proposed 2022-23 budget <copy provided to the Clerk for the record>, reporting staff
requested the Board reduce the mileage by 1/3 of a mil, for the proposed 6.5 mils, and
keeping the Fire Control Districts the same at .5 mil each. County Administrator
Hammond reported that the County is in the last year of the $409,000.00 payment for the
Downtown Redevelopment (CRA/DRA). E.M.S. Director Daniels reported the State has
allocated funds for Gulf County to start an opioid epidemic program, if approved, the
County will use these funds to purchase a new ambulance with equipment. County
Administrator Hammond discussed the Industrial Park property, stating the Board will
need to decide, since the County is out of that threshold, if they want to pursue a release
from EDA (Economic Development Agency) so the County can sell the Industrial Park in
Wewahitchka or continue to lease it in the future. He stated this topic can be discuss
further at tomorrow’s regular Board meeting. Commissioner McDaniel discussed his
desire to obtain control of this facility. Following discussion, OMB Director Herring
discussed the plan to pay off the Non-Ad Valorem Revenue Bond, Series 2016 early,

using potential bed tax funds, by November 1, 2022. County Administrator Hammond
reported that $50,000.00 has been added to the Parks and Recreational Fund, inquiring
how the Board would like to allocate these funds. Commissioner McCroan suggested to
pay the utilities off the top and then divide the remainder between the five Districts.
Commissioner Farrell agreed with Commissioner McCroan’s suggestion. Commissioner
McDaniel discussed the purchase of flags for the Veterans’ Memorial Beacon Hill Park
and the utilities in District II. Chairman Quinn agreed with taking the park utilities out first,
inquiring if the flags were being taken out of District II’s Parks & Recreational Budget.
County Administrator Hammond reported that funds for the purchase of the flags for
Veterans’ Memorial Beacon Hill Park are taken out of each District. OMB Director Herring
stated that last year, the Board took $3,000.00 from each District’s Parks & Recreational
Budget to cover the cost of these flags. County Administrator Hammond stated the
County is trying to get this cost down by being more proactive in taking the flags down
before a bad wind event and reducing the replacement to 5-6 times per year. He reported
that the County set aside $15,000.00, but if it is the will of the Board, staff will come back
with the next proposed budget book with the utilities coming out of the $50,000.00; noting
that if it runs over, the additional funds will be taken from the individual District’s budget.
Assistant County Administrator Smallwood reported that in the T.D.C. budget there will
be funding to replace the irrigation system at the Country Club Golf Course, stating this
will be over a $600,000.00 project. County Administrator Hammond discussed his
concern regarding rising fuel costs, stating that if the County does not receive relief, then
tipping fees will have to be increased. He reported the County will not be able to break
even at the current rate. Public Works Director Cothran reported that due to an increase
in fuel cost, Pritchett Trucking, Inc. has increased the cost to haul the waste from the
Transfer Station to Spring Hill Landfill, and the County will have to increase the tipping
fees eventually just to break even. County Administrator Hammond reported that the
County is looking at a large water customer (for Gulf County Water Department) and will
be bringing back final numbers and a recommendation to the Board. He also stated that
the County will need to set aside Reserves to pay the money back from SRF (State
Revolving Fund). OMB Director Herring stated the proposed budget presented is a
balanced budget, reporting the County does not have all the data due to waiting on
revenue projections to come from the State. She requested permission from the Board to
make necessary changes as data is received. OMB Director Herring reported that the
proposed budget book is based off the mileage rate of 6.5 Countywide, .5 mils for each
of the Fire Districts, and the M.S.T.U. Gulf Front is 0.6786, Gulf Interior is 0.5689 and
Bayside is 0.5723. She then reminded the Board she will need their permission to make
those changes as necessary between now and the First Public Hearing. County
Administrator Hammond reported that there is a $13.11 per month per employee
reduction on the health insurance premium and the proposed match contribution has
increased by $10.00 (for H.S.A.). Discussion followed regarding the proposed budget,
T.D.C. permitting, and Leave No Trace. Chairman Quinn called for a motion to allow OMB
Director Herring to make the necessary changes as the numbers come in. Commissioner
McDaniel so moved. Commissioner McCroan seconded the motion. Chairman Quinn
called for public comment. There being none, the motion passed unanimously.

Director of Finance & Management Director Bebeau reported that the Clerk’s Office has
no changes or comments at this time, stating that the Clerk is happy with what was
submitted and the County’s reply.
Supervisor of Elections Hanlon appeared before the Board to state that the Supervisor of
Elections Office is good with the current budget, needing no changes. He thanked the
Board for always being understanding and accommodating of the changes and increases.
Property Appraiser Burke appeared before the Board to thank the Board for lowering the
mileage rate, stating this will help the taxpayers and the County will still get revenue. He
stated that his office does not have anything else for the budget.
Sheriff Harrison appeared before the Board to state that he requested a 10% budget
increase, requesting time to review the numbers. He reported that funding is coming from
the State, but the County still has an obligation to the citizens of Gulf County. He thanked
the Board for what they have done this far and again requested time to review the
numbers presented. Sheriff Harrison then discussed his concerns regarding the increase
of operational costs and fuel, cost of supplies, and the cost and shortage of vehicles.
Chairman Quinn called for Tax Collector Jenkins. She was not present.
County Administrator Hammond recommended setting the Mileage Rate at 6.5 for Board,
.5 mil for each of the Fire Control Districts, and M.S.T.U.’s Gulf Front at 0.6786, Gulf
Interior at 0.5689, and Bay Side at 0.5723. With Chairman Quinn calling for a motion,
Commissioner McDaniel motioned to set the cap Mileage at 6.5 mills for the Fiscal Year
2022-23. Commissioner McCroan seconded the motion. Chairman Quinn called for public
comment. There being no public comment, the motion passed unanimously. County
Administrator Hammond requested one motion for the Dependent Fire Districts and the
M.S.T.U.’s. Commissioner Rich so moved. Commissioner Farrell seconded the motion.
Chairman Quinn called for public comment. There being none, the motion passed
unanimously. County Administrator Hammond recommended holding the First Public
Hearing on Wednesday, September 7, 2022, at 5:01 pm., E.T. The Board had no
objection. Chairman Quinn called for a motion to set the First Hearing on September 7,
2022, at 5:01 p.m., E.T. Commissioner McCroan so moved. Commissioner Farrell
seconded the motion. Chairman Quinn called for public comment. There being none, the
motion passed unanimously. County Administrator Hammond reported that the Board
does not have to set the second date today, but OMB Director Herring is recommending
that the Second/Final Public Hearing be held on September 26, 2022. County
Administrator Hammond stated that the Board will officially set the Second Public Hearing
at the First Public Hearing, but if the Board has no objections to the September 26, 2022,
date, then that is the date the County will shoot for. The Board had no objection.
Commissioner McDaniel so moved. Chairman Quinn stated that a motion is not needed
at this time.

There being no further business, and upon motion by Commissioner McDaniel, second
by Commissioner Farrell, and unanimous vote, the meeting did then adjourn at 10:19
a.m., E.T.
