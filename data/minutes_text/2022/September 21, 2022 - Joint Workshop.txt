SEPTEMBER 21, 2022
JOINT WORKSHOP
PORT ST. JOE, FLORIDA
The Gulf County Board of County Commissioners held a joint workshop with the City of
Port St. Joe at their County Board Meeting Room. The following Board members were
present: Chairman Sandy Quinn, Jr., Vice Chairman Phil McCroan, and Commissioners
David C. Rich, Ward McDaniel, and Patrick Farrell.
City Commissioner Board members present were: City Mayor Rex Buzzett, and City
Commissioners Scott Hoffman, David Ashbrook, Brett Lowry, and Eric Langston.
Also present from the County were: County Attorney Jeremy Novak, Deputy Clerk
Leanna Cooper, County Administrator Michael L. Hammond, County Assistant
Administrator Clay Smallwood, III, Deputy Administrator Kari Summers, Emergency
Management Director/Fire Coordinator Matt Herring, Grant Administrator Kaci Rhodes,
Grant Administrator Erin Kennedy, Office of Management & Budget Director Sherry
Herring, Mosquito Control Austin Horton, Public Works Director Mark Cothran, and Sheriff
Mike Harrison.
City staff members present were: City Attorney Clinton McCahill, City Clerk Charlette
Pierce, City Manager Jim Anderson, and Public Works Director John Grantland.
Chairman Quinn called the meeting to order at 5:00 p.m., E.T.
Chairman Quinn then opened the meeting with the Pledge of Allegiance to the Flag.
BEACON HILL SEWER/E.S.A.D.
Chairman Quinn called for public comment. Aaron Little, of Port St. Joe appeared before
the Board to inquire what the hold up is for the City of Port St. Joe to take over the Beacon
Hill Sewer System, expressing his support in this transition.
The meeting did recess at 5:03 p.m., E.T.
The meeting did then reconvene at 5:07 p.m., E.T.
City Mayor Buzzett stated that the City of Port St. Joe has been working with the County
on a Memorandum of Understating which was submitted to the County in July but has not
received it back. He reported that the City is working with Dewberry and waiting to receive
the task order. Chairman Quinn called for public comment. There was no additional public
comment. County Administrator Hammond presented a letter of recommendation <copy
provided to the Clerk for the record>, stating that he will be making this recommendation
to the Board at Tuesday’s meeting (September 27, 2022): to have a timeline for the City

of Port St. Joe to reach an agreement to purchase the system and to have it online by
June 1, 2023. Upon inquiry by County Commissioner McDaniel, County Administrator
Hammond stated that this would be approximately 378 additional hook-ups for the City of
Port St. Joe in the Gulf Aire, Sea Shores (subdivisions), and would include the Dollar
General Store. City Mayor Buzzett stated that the engineers (Dewberry) have said that
without the lift station and the customers of Beacon Hill, there is not enough flow to just
take on the E.S.A.D. customers. After discussion by County Administrator Hammond,
County Commissioner McDaniel discussed the monetary commitment by the County
contingent upon the City of Port St. Joe purchasing the (E.S.A.D.) system. Chairman
Quinn inquired about a clarification on the engineer saying it will not work. City Mayor
Buzzett stated that the first opinion was that it would not work, reading the engineer’s
recommendation <copy provided to the Clerk for the record> aloud. Following discussion,
County Assistant Administrator Smallwood stated that from an engineering standpoint it
is very straightforward to upgrade one of the exciting lift stations, to take the flow from
E.S.A.D. and get it online, reporting the City does not need the Beacon Hill customers for
it to work. County Attorney Novak stated that within the memorandum presented,
numbers one through 4 (1-4) are what was in the Memorandum of Understanding, and
the only new thing is number five (5), which is the recommendation of County
Administrator Hammond that in the next thirty (30) days the City of Port St. Joe agrees to
sign the agreement, and the City of Port St. Joe has nine (9) months to get it in order, and
close on it. He then asked Assistant County Administrator Smallwood if nine months was
sufficient time to bring upgrades in terms of a lift station to Beacon Hill. Assistant County
Administrator Smallwood discussed lead time on materials, once advertised, stating it is
certainly doable. After further discussion, County Attorney Novak discussed placing a
contingency in the agreement with E.S.A.D. City Public Works Director Grantland
discussed the proposed project, inquiring why the County is in a rush. Chairman Quinn
asked how much time the City of Port St. Joe will need. City Public Works Director
Grantland stated that the upfront agreement was for the City of Port St. Joe to purchase
E.S.A.D., and once the City secured the property and built the lift station in Beacon Hill,
then the City would take Integras off the Mexico Beach system and the County would
then convey it to the City of Port St. Joe. Chairman Quinn again asked the City of Port St.
Joe how much time they would need. County Commissioner McCroan inquired if the
County Attorney could build in some contingency for the City of Port St. Joe as far as
signing a contract. County Attorney Novak stated that item number five (5) of County
Administrator Hammond’s memorandum was to put parameters on the pledge of the
County’s funding and his recommendation is to narrow the scope. County Administrator
Hammond stated that the County will not wait three (3) years for the City of Port St. Joe
to build a lift station and get all of Beacon Hill online. He discussed the scope of work and
the timeline to complete the project. Discussion followed regarding the contingency to
purchase E.S.A.B. before the City can receive the easement at Beacon Hill for the lift
station. City Public Works Director Grantland reported that the recommendation from
Dewberry was 12-13 months to build the lift station in Beacon Hill. Discussion followed
regarding the need for an easement from the County, D.E.P. (Department of
Environmental Protection) funding, obtaining plans from Dewberry, and the City working
on both Beacon Hill and E.S.A.D. projects at the same time. Commissioner McCroan
inquired if an agreement was signed on the 31st would County Administrator Hammond

be okay with thirteen (13) months (instead of the recommended nine months). County
Administrator Hammond stated that it would be up to the Board, it is just a
recommendation from him. He reported that until the City of Port St. Joe agrees to buy
E.S.A.D., which has dragged out for ten (10) years, the Board will lose their leverage to
make the City purchase the plant when the Board gives the City the lift station at Beacon
Hill. County Administrator Hammond discussed the need for the City to decide if they are
going to purchase E.S.A.D., and if not, then the County will move forward with a different
plan. Following discussion, City Commissioner Hoffman discussed his concerns
regarding the recommendation from the engineer (Dewberry) and the pressure from the
County to purchase this private sewer plant. He stated that if the County sets a deadline
to purchase E.S.A.D., then his vote will be no, but if things can get worked out, getting
the property at Beacon Hill pledged/deeded over to the City of Port St. Joe, and get the
engineering plans completed, then the City will make it happen in a reasonable timeframe.
City Commissioner Lowry discussed the City taking the deadline to a vote based on these
terms or bring alternate term back to the County to consider. Commissioner McDaniel
inquired if the City needs a guarantee from the County regarding Beacon Hill before the
City will commit to the purchase of E.S.A.D. City Mayor Buzzett stated he does not know,
he will need to talk to the experts to find out if the City needs Beacon Hill. Discussion
followed regarding the Memorandum of Understanding. County Attorney Novak
discussed the timeline regarding the Memorandum of Understanding, stating if the Board
accepts the City’s changes to the MOU, then his recommendation, at next Tuesday’s
meeting, will be to add an additional provision based on the County Administrator’s
recommendations regarding timelines. Chairman Quinn inquired about whether the
revised MOU will be introduced Tuesday for the Board to consider. County Administrator
Hammond said yes, along with his recommendations; noting the deal is whether the City
is going to purchase E.S.A.D. and put these customers on as quickly as reasonable, but
not after Beacon Hill customers are on, and not on an indefinite timeline. Discussion
followed regarding the revision to the Memorandum of Understanding and the deadline.
City Public Works Director Grantland stated that all the City is asking is the deed to the
Beacon Hill property, to allow the City to get all that going (at Beacon Hill) and in the
meantime the City still work on E.S.A.D. Chairman Quinn inquired whether this is all
contingent upon E.S.A.D. going offline. Members of the City Commission agreed, stating
that is their goal. City Commissioner Hoffman stated that he would be comfortable with a
contingency to the purchase of E.S.A.D. if Dewberry comes back with very clear and
specific language that it can happen without Beacon Hill and the assurance from City staff
that it is doable, then the City will set a timeframe. Chairman Quinn called for public
comment. There was no public comment.

There being no further discussion, the meeting did then adjourn at 5:53 p.m., E.T.
