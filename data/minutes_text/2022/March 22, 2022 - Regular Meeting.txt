NGA911 OVERVIEW PRESENTATION
E9-1-1 Coordinator Guthrie appeared before the Board to present an overview
presentation of NGA911, providing a packet for Board to review <copy provided to the
Clerk for the record>. He discussed the 911 calls rerouted during Hurricane Michael,
stating that the Counties in Region 1 have gotten together and are currently working on
a Region 1 Project, reporting if calls go down then the calls will remain in the Region. E9-
1-1 Coordinator Guthrie stated that grant funds will need to be applied for to fund this
project by the April 22, 2022 deadline, requesting that the Board review the packet
provided and contact him with any questions. He then requested permission to apply for
the State 911 Board grant, reporting that it will be a five (5) year agreement, and an
experiment for the rest of Florida. Upon inquiry by Commissioner Farrell, E9-1-1
Coordinator Guthrie stated that five (5) Counites in the State of Florida signed up for this

forward. Following discussion, Commissioner McDaniel discussed visiting Jackson
County to see the system in operation. County Attorney Novak inquired if the County
would need to join that purchasing group (MiCTA). E9-1-1 Coordinator Guthrie stated that
the County will, and the fee will be $200.00 per year that is reimbursable by the Board.
County Administrator Hammond stated that staff thought that Bay County would have
already decided before today’s meeting if they were going with this service. He discussed
his concerns regarding the old server system, stating that a workshop may need to be
held for further discussion about getting on the Cloud. County Administrator Hammond
stated that he would like for the Board to wait and see what Bay County decides, inquiring
if the Board wants to give staff permission to apply, contingent upon Bay County accepting

QUASI-JUDICIAL HEARING – REVIEW & CONSIDERATION OF P.D.R.B.
Planner Collinsworth presented a non-residential development request from Old Salt
Works, LLC * Parcel ID #06292-002R, #06292-003R, #06292-004R, #06292-105R &
#06292-115R, reporting that Old Salt Works, LLC is requesting to combine these parcels

for future non-residential development. Chairman Quinn called for anyone wishing to
speak on this matter, to stand. Several from the audience stood in acknowledgement.
County Attorney Novak discussed the process of waiving the Quasi-Judicial Hearing to
enter into a Public Hearing. Chairman Quinn inquired if anyone standing had any
objections to waiving Quasi-Judicial Hearing. After discussion, Chairman Quinn called for
any objection to waive Quasi-Judicial Hearing. There were no objections to waive.
Chairman Quinn then called for a motion to waive Quasi-Judicial Hearing process.
Commissioner McDaniel so moved. Commissioner McCroan seconded the motion.
Chairman Quinn called for public comment. There being none, the motion passed
unanimously. County Administrator Hammond stated that the vote today is not for a
development order, only voting to combine the lots. County Attorney Novak read the
project description aloud for the Board. Planner Collinsworth reported that the P.D.R.B.
recommended by 3-1 vote to approve the combining of the parcels for future land use
development. He stated that the P.D.R.B. will allow for those parcels since it is already
zoned as mixed commercial/residential to allow for the combining before continuing with
further developments of stormwater, wetlands, and traffic studies. Lance Watson, of
Southeastern Consulting Engineers appeared before the Board to state that today is not
a request for a development order, only to confirm and receive preliminary approval to
move forward with combining the lots for commercial use. He stated the adjacent cabins
have been used commercially and for vacation rentals, reporting that these lots are
surrounded by either commercial property or commercial use. Upon inquiry by
Commissioner McDaniel, Mr. Watson confirmed this property is already zoned mixed
commercial/residential. Chairman Quinn called for public comment. Cindy Stack, of Cape
San Blas appeared before the Board to provide material for the Board to review <copy
provided to the Clerk for the record>, discussing her opposition to combine these lots.
Discussion followed regarding egress and ingress easement access to the property and
property owners. County Attorney Novak stated that the Board has received an advisory
opinion from the P.D.R.B., reporting that there is a conceptual plan presented, but no
development being approved today. He stated that egress/ingress, setback requirements,
density is not changing, it is not a platted subdivision, not an HOA so there is not anything
that is prohibiting the Board from accepting the recommendation by the P.D.R.B.
Discussion followed. Kim Colon, of Cape San Blas appeared before the Board to discuss
her opposition to the future development, providing material to the Board for review <copy
provided to the Clerk for the record>. Commissioner McDaniel discussed laws against
land-locking property owners. Upon inquiry by Chairman Quinn, Planner Collinsworth
reported that the P.D.R.B. recommended, by 3-1 vote, to approve combining the lots for
future non-residential development. He stated that this request is only to combine the lots,
but all these parcels have different owners and Salt Works, LLC is waiting for today’s
outcome before deciding. Mr. Watson, of SCE appeared before the Board to state the
sale is pending contingent upon today’s approval and this request does meet the LDR
allowance of combining mixed commercial/residential lots for commercial use. He stated
that the two easements will remain and everything off these two easements per the LDR
will be maintained. Upon inquiry by Chairman Quinn, Planner Collinsworth reported the
land use has been since day one, since 1992, that this area has a land use of mixed
commercial/residential. County Attorney Novak stated that there is no application in front
of the Board for a land use change in this area; reporting for the last thirty years it has

been MCR (mixed commercial/residential). After further discussion by County Attorney
Novak, Commissioner McCroan motioned to waive the formal reading. Commissioner
Farrell seconded the motion. Chairman Quinn called for public comment. There being
none, the motion passed unanimously. Commissioner McDaniel motioned to approve
combining these lots. Commissioner Rich seconded the motion. After discussion,
Chairman Quinn called for public comment. County Attorney Novak stated the question
before this Board is whether or not you can combine these MCR properties for the
purpose of future potential application of commercial development. He recommended to
the Board that if they do pass the motion and second, he recommended they strike the
second part of that and answer the question for them to move forward. Commissioner
McDaniel modified his motion. Chairman Quinn stated to strike that second part.
Commissioner Rich stated his second stands. Chairman Quinn called for public comment.
There being none, the motion passed 4 to 1, with Commissioner McCroan voting no.
