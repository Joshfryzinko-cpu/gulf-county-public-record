PORT ST. JOE, FLORIDA
The Gulf County Board of County Commissioners met this date in regular session with
the following members present: Chairman Sandy Quinn, Jr., Vice Chairman Phil
McCroan, and Commissioners David C. Rich, and Patrick Farrell <newly elected Billy E.
Traylor was absent>.

of Finance & Management Director Leo Bebeau, Deputy Clerk Leanna Cooper, County

Management & Budget Director Sherry Herring, Mosquito Control Director Austin Horton,

Harrison, and T.D.C. Executive Director Kelli Godwin.
Sheriff Harrison called the meeting to order at 9:00 a.m., E.T.
Chairman Quinn opened the meeting with prayer and led in the Pledge of Allegiance to
the Flag.
“SWEARING-IN” CEREMONY – NEW COMMISSIONERS
The Oath of Office was administered to Sandy Quinn, Jr., County Commissioner for
District IV, by Circuit Judge Devin Collier. County Administrator Hammond reported that
staff was notified this morning that newly elected Billy E. Traylor is ill and not able to
attend today’s swearing-in. He stated that he has requested County Attorney Novak to
research the situation regarding Commissioner Traylor not taking the Oath this morning
and upon County Attorney Novak’s finding, he will report to the Chairman accordingly.
CONSENT AGENDA

public comment. OMB Director Herring requested Board approval to replace pages 27-

Consent Agenda with those necessary changes. Commissioner Rich so moved.
