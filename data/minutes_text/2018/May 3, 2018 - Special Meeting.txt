MAY 3, 2018
PORT ST. JOE, FLORIDA
SPECIAL MEETING
The Gulf County Board of County Commissioners met this date in special session with
the following members present: Chairman Sandy Quinn, Jr., Vice Chairman Jimmy
Rogers, and Commissioners David C. Rich, Ward McDaniel, and Phil McCroan.
Others present were: County Attorney Jeremy Novak, Clerk Rebecca L. Norris, Deputy
Clerk Leanna Roberts, County Administrator Michael L. Hammond, Building Official Brad
Bailey, Central Services Director Lynn Lanier, Gulf County E.D.C. Director Warren
Yeager, Jr., Emergency Management Director Marshall Nelson, E9-1-1 Coordinator Ben
Guthrie, Assistant Mosquito Control Director Austin Horton, County Planner Lee
Collinsworth, Dewberry/County Engineer Clay Smallwood, III, Assistant Public Works
Director Raymond Hart, Sheriff's Department Captain Chris Buchanan, and T.D.C.
Executive Director Kelli Godwin.
Sheriff’s Department Captain Buchanan called the meeting to order at 8:59 a.m., E.T.
Chairman Quinn led in the Pledge of Allegiance to the Flag.
SALINAS PARK
Following discussion, Commissioner McCroan motioned to allow County Staff to clean
out the underbrush at Salinas Park immediately. Commissioner McDaniel seconded the
motion. Chairman Quinn called for public comment. Mary Ann Mathias, of Port St. Joe
appeared before the Board to discuss the fire at Salinas Park; thanking the Fire
Departments and the Division of Forestry. Chairman Quinn called for further public
comment. There being none, the motion passed unanimously.
AWARD BID #1718-13 – ST. JOSEPH PENINSULA BEACH RENOURISHMENT
PROJECT
Michael Dombrowski, of MRD Associates appeared before the Board to discuss the
project and the bids received; recommended the Board award Bid #1718-13 to Manson
Construction. Upon inquiry by Commissioner McDaniel, he reported that the County
received approval from D.E.P. (Department of Environmental Protection) and U.S. Army
Corps of Engineers to re-nest/relocate the turtle nests. Discussion followed. County
Administrator Hammond stated for the record the bid (in sand volume) was 705,882.35
cubic yards; requesting that the bid be awarded to Manson Construction Co with the
stipulation that the County will not provide a notice to proceed until U.S. Treasury has
approved the notice to proceed, and to allow County Administrator Hammond to sign any
change orders to increase the sand based on the amount of money. Commissioner
McCroan motioned to move forward with the recommendation of the County
Administrator. Commissioner Rogers seconded the motion. Chairman Quinn called for

public comment. Pat Hardman, Coastal Community Association President appeared
before the Board to discuss the County’s effort regarding this project. She inquired about
the distance up/down the coast will it carry; asking if the sand will get to Rish Park. Mr.
Dombrowski stated that the plan is to start the sand at the ‘rocks’ and go down to Rish
Park. Chairman Quinn called for additional public comment. There being no further public
comment, the motion passed unanimously. Mr. Dombrowski reported the company
(Manson) is planning to mobilize prior to August 1, 2018, but will start pumping August
1st; estimating it would take 45 days, finishing at the latest the end of September, 2018.
There being no further business, and upon motion by Commissioner Ward, second by
Commissioner Rich and unanimous vote, the meeting did then adjourn at 9:13 a.m., E.T.
