OCTOBER 31, 2018
PORT ST. JOE, FLORIDA
EMERGENCY MEETING
The Gulf County Board of County Commissioners met this date in an emergency session
with the following members: Chairman Sandy Quinn, Jr., Vice Chairman Jimmy Rogers,
and Commissioners David C. Rich, Ward McDaniel, and Phil McCroan.
Others present were: County Attorney Jeremy Novak, Clerk Rebecca L. Norris, Deputy
Clerk Leanna Roberts, County Administrator Michael L. Hammond, County Assistant
Administrator Warren Yeager, Jr., Deputy Administrator Kari Summers, Building Official
Brad Bailey, Staff Assistant – E.D.C. Johanna White, G.I.S. Coordinator Jessica Susich,
Office of Management & Budget Director Sherry Herring, Assistant Mosquito Control
Director Austin Horton, County Planner Lee Collinsworth, and Sheriff Mike Harrison.
Chairman Quinn called the meeting to order at 9:00 a.m., E.T.
Chairman Quinn led in the Pledge of Allegiance to the Flag.
EXTEND LOCAL STATE OF EMERGENCY – HURRICANE MICHAEL
Following discussion by County Administrator Hammond, Commissioner McCroan
motioned to extend the Local State of Emergency (due to Hurricane Michael) for an
additional seven (7) days <copy provided to the Clerk for the record>. Commissioner
Rogers seconded the motion. Chairman Quinn called for public comment. There being
no public comment, the motion passed unanimously.
UPDATE – HURRICANE MICHAEL
Planner Collinsworth requested that the Board suspend the Bid Policy for contractors;
reporting the County has 218 buildings that have damage. He recommended that the
Board suspend the Bid Policy, noting the County will still obtain three (3) quotes, and staff
will review the quotes to expedite repairs (to the County buildings due to Hurricane
Michael). Commissioner McDaniel motioned to approve his recommendation.
Commissioner Rich seconded the motion. Chairman Quinn called for public comment.
Clerk Norris requested staff to provide three (3) quotes with the invoice for the record
(pursuant to policy). There being no public comment, the motion passed unanimously.
Planner Collinsworth clarified that this suspension of the Bid Policy is only for County
buildings. He then reported that homeowners are tearing down their houses without
obtaining a demolition permit, not calling for their locates, and gas lines are getting busted;
stating that the Building Department is requiring a demolition permit that will include the
property owner to call to have the underground lines cut-out. Upon inquiry by
Commissioner McDaniel, Planner Collinsworth stated that this will apply to the County
and the City of Wewahitchka, and until further notice the County is assisting the City of
Port St. Joe. County Administrator Hammond recommended that the Board submit a letter

to the City of Port St. Joe offering to take over their building services again. After
discussion, Commissioner McDaniel motioned to submit a letter to the City of Port St. Joe
to offer support (regarding building services). Commissioner Rich seconded the motion.
Chairman Quinn called for public comment. There being no public comment, the motion
then passed unanimously. County Administrator Hammond reported that he will be
presenting scenarios to the Board next week regarding the budget forecast (due to
Hurricane Michael); stating that he will be requesting the Chairman to call a special
meeting next week. Commissioner McDaniel requested clarification regarding contractors
going onto private property to clean-up (storm debris) at no cost to the property owner;
stating the County needs to pursue it. County Administrator Hammond reported that
private roads have been approved and he understands that it has been approved for
private property cleanup. He noted this will be limited to 100 feet around the house;
requiring a waiver that will have to be signed by the homeowner. Commissioner McCroan
discussed his concern regarding the rumors pertaining to F.E.M.A. trailers; stating that
this program has been approved (Transitional Sheltering Assistance/TSA). After
discussion, County Administrator Hammond reported that he is going to sign an
emergency contract with Consolidated Communications for a backup (communication)
services, in the amount of $400.00 per month. He stated that the Board, with County staff,
will hold a joint meeting with the City (of Port St. Joe) and Horne (LLP) to sign individual
contracts. County Administrator Hammond also reported that the Supervisor of Elections
will be voting straight through Election Day at the two (2) voting sites; the Supervisor of
Elections Office and the Wewahitchka Library from 7:00 a.m. to 7:00 p.m., E.T.
Discussion followed regarding debris pickup on the State and County right-of-way’s and
concerns over leaning trees. Upon inquiry by Commissioner Rich, County Administrator
Hammond reported on electrical restoration in Overstreet.
There being no further business, and upon motion by Commissioner McCroan, second
by Commissioner Rogers, and unanimous vote, the meeting did then adjourn at 9:22 a.m.,
E.T.
