TROPICAL STORM MICHAEL
Upon recommendation by Emergency Management Director Nelson, Commissioner
McCroan motioned to declare a Local State of Emergency (due to Tropical Storm
Michael). Commissioner Rogers seconded the motion. Chairman Quinn called for public
comment. There being no public comment, the motion passed unanimously (4-0).
Emergency Management Director Nelson discussed the approaching storm; reporting
that Gulf County will be largely impacted. He reported that they will meet again in the
morning and after this meeting the E.O.C. will go to a Level II (partial) activation; with a
Level III (full) activation to begin on Tuesday. County Attorney Novak discussed the
proclamation that the Board motioned to adopt; reporting that per Statute it will be
renewed every seven (7) days. Upon inquiry by Commissioner McCroan regarding sand
bags, Public Works Cothran stated if the Board agrees, Public Works will haul sand
outside of the Public Works gate for public use. County Administrator Hammond
discussed recessing until tomorrow at 10:00 a.m.; stating that the County will need to do
mandatory evacuations on the coast. Commissioner Rogers reported that Roberts &
Roberts is getting ready to stage their stuff at the Stumphole. County Assistant
Administrator Yeager discussed Manson (Construction Company); reporting the County
will provide a location at Public Works for Manson to store their equipment. Upon inquiry
by Commissioner McCroan regarding the gate on Painted Pony Road, County
Administrator Hammond stated we need to get the gate open.
The meeting did then recess at 6:05 p.m., E.T.
The meeting did reconvene at 10:01 a.m., E.T. on October 8, 2018.

that the E.O.C. went to a Level II (partial activation) this morning and will activate a Level
I (return to monitoring) tomorrow. Public Works Director Cothran reported that sand, bags,
and shovels are available to the public outside the Public Works gate. Commissioner
McCroan discussed having sand available at the Cape by the Raw Bar (Restaurant).

everything out. After discussion by Commissioner McCroan, County Administrator
Hammond discussed the road at the Cape, water, power, and sewer after the storm;
reporting the County does not want to risk the lives of first responders to retrieve residents

that chose to stay. Following discussion, County Assistant Administrator Yeager reported
that Waste Pro is going to pick up today out at Cape San Blas; requesting residents to
pull their carts back once the trash has been picked up. County Administrator Hammond
noted that this information will be posted on the T.D.C. website. He requested the Board
recess this meeting and come back (reconvene), if needed. Chairman Quinn called Jim
Norton to appear. Jim Norton, Gulf County School Board Superintendent appeared before
the Board to report that the School Board has an emergency meeting at 1:00 p.m. today
and he will recommend they close the schools for Tuesday, Wednesday, and Thursday.

Port St. Joe getting prepared for the storm; reporting they will hold another emergency
meeting this afternoon at 3:00 p.m. Sheriff Harrison appeared before the Board to report

adhere the warnings. City of Port St. Joe Chief of Police Matt Herring appeared before
