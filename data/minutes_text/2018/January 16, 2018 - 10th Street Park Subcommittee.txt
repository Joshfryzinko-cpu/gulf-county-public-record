MASTER PLAN AND BUDGET
After going over updates from option #1 to option #2 the committee discussed additonal
updates before presenting the plans to City and County Commissioners. The updates discussed
were an additional t-ball field, a walking trail or path around the fields and connecting with the
Port City Trail, parking lot coming off 8th street next to stack house, playground by 8th street,
and extra restrooms. Mr. Smallwood commented that he could have the changes done on the
conceptual plan before the City Commissioner’s meeting later that evening. Mr. Buzzett
motioned to approve option #2 with the updates discussed, Mr. Yeager seconded the
motioned, and it passed unanimously. Chairman Quinn noted that the GCTDC fifth cent has
been frozen for this project and that the plans need to be presented to the Board of County
Commissioners as soon as possible for any extra funding. Mr. Smallwood explained that there
could be grant funding through Water Management District in the fall for piping the ditch. Next
steps after both City and County Commissioners approve the plan would include diving into the
details and design so that the project can submit a request for permit from The State of Florida.
