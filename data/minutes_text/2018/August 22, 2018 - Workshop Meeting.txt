AUGUST 22, 2018
WORKSHOP
PORT ST. JOE, FLORIDA 32456
The Gulf County Board of County Commissioners met this date in a workshop with the
following members present: Chairman Sandy Quinn, Jr., Vice Chairman Jimmy Rogers,
and Commissioners David C. Rich, Ward McDaniel, and Phil McCroan.
Others present were: County Attorney Jeremy Novak, Deputy Clerk Leanna Roberts,
County Administrator Michael L. Hammond, Deputy Administrator Kari Summers,
Building Official Brad Bailey, Central Services Director Lynn Lanier, Gulf County E.D.C.
Director Warren Yeager, Jr., Gulf County E.D.C. Staff Assistant Lianna Sagins, Human
Resource Director Brett Lowry, Assistant Mosquito Control Director Austin Horton, Parks
& Recreational Director Billy Traylor, County Planner Lee Collinsworth, Public Works &
Mosquito Control Director Mark Cothran, and T.D.C. Executive Director Kelli Godwin.
Chairman Quinn called the meeting to order at 10:03 a.m., E.T.
Chairman Quinn led the Pledge of Allegiance to the Flag.
LEAVE NO TRACE
County Attorney Novak stated for the record that today’s workshop was publicly posted
on the Courthouse and the Administration Building. Chairman Quinn stated that there will
be no votes taken today. Mark Costin, on behalf of Smiley’s Beach Rentals appeared
before the Board to discuss his concerns regarding the Leave No Trace Ordinance and
his business (Smiley’s Beach Rentals). He asked if there could be a concession for
businesses. Pat Hardman, President of Costal Community Association appeared before
the Board to present an informational flyer <copy provided to the Clerk for the record>;
discussing community meetings held to discuss the Leave No Trace Ordinance. She
discussed tourists visiting our beaches and the need for rules. Commissioner McCroan
motioned to allow Ms. Hardman two (2) additional minutes to speak. The motion failed for
a lack of a second. Nick Vaughn, of Cape San Blas appeared before the Board to suggest
that the (Leave No Trace) Ordinance be studied more carefully before any changes are
made. Randy Pridgeon, representing West Gulf Beaches appeared before the Board to
request that the Board appoint a committee that will meet yearly to review the (Leave No
Trace) Ordinance. Julia Cunningham, of Cape San Blas appeared before the Board to
discuss the need for a Leave No Trace Ordinance; requesting a moratorium on any action
to redline or diminish the Ordinance and have a committee to determine what each beach
needs. Christy McElroy, of Port St. Joe appeared before the Board to discuss her
concerns regarding the County protecting our assets; providing a handout from the HCP
(Habitat Conservation Plan) Workshop from August 18, 2016 <copy provided to the Clerk
for the record>. Robyn Rennick, of Cape San Blas appeared before the Board to present
and discuss a survey conducted by the Coastal Community Association <copy provided

to the Clerk for the record>. Patrick Foy, from the audience, asked if he could forgo his
time to Pat Hardman. Upon inquiry by Chairman Quinn, County Attorney Novak stated it
would be at the pleasure of the Board; reporting there is nothing in the rules to prevent
that. Pat Hardman, of Cape San Blas appeared before the Board to discuss appointing a
committee prior to the September meeting and have the committee meet annually to look
at the Ordinance. Davis Motil, of Cape San Blas appeared before the Board to thank the
County for what they have done; requesting that the County continue in moving forward.
Janna Rinehart was called to appear. Ms. Rinehart, from the audience, stated she would
concede her time to the next person; reporting that she is only here to protect the turtles.
John Ehrman, of Cape San Blas appeared before the Board to discuss his concerns
regarding personal property left on the beach and the lack of enforcement. Upon inquiry
by Chairman Quinn regarding the location of the canopies being blown into the surf, Mr.
Ehrman stated it occurs in vaiours areas. Ron Shaeffer was called to appear to speak.
Mr. Shaeffer, from the audience, stated he wished to concede his time. Dorothy Rogers
was called to appear before the Board to speak. Ms. Rogers, from the audience, stated
that she is conceding her time. Dusty May, of Port St. Joe appeared before the Board to
discuss his concerns regarding the Leave No Trace Ordinance. MaryAnn Mathias, of
Cape San Blas appeared before the Board to discuss her concerns regarding the Leave
No Trace Ordinance; suggesting the Board implement random removals (spot checks).
Jane Gringinger, with Happy Cabanas appeared before the Board to discuss her
concerns with the Leave No Trace Ordinance. Gene Behage was called before the Board
to speak. Mr. Behage, from the audience, stated that he wished to forgo his time. County
Attorney Novak reported that the Board has legally advertised for the First Public Hearing
to be held next Tuesday at 9:00 a.m., E.T.; inviting the public to attend. He then read for
the record the purpose of the Leave No Trace Ordinance. County Administrator
Hammond reported that a letter was submitted by someone that was not able to attend
the meeting; stating County Attorney Novak will read aloud. The Board had no objection.
County Attorney Novak then read the letter from Benny Williams aloud to the members
of the Board regarding the Leave No Trace Ordinance <copy not provided to the Board
for the record>. Chairman Quinn thanked the public for their time; stating that the Board
will take their comments inconsideration.
There being no further discussion, and upon motion by Commission McCroan, second by
Commissioner McDaniel, and unanimous vote, the meeting did then adjourn at 11:00
a.m., E.T.
SANDY QUINN, JR.
