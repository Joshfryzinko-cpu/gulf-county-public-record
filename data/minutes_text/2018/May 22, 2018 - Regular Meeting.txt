TRIUMPH UPDATE
Chairman Quinn discussed his concerns with the newspaper article regarding Triumph
funding and projects; asking staff to respond to questions raised in regards to the Triumph
application. Chairman Quinn requested that the County’s representative on the Triumph
Board, Jason Shoaf appear before the Board at the next regular meeting. County
Administrator Hammond reported that Gulf County has $15 million reserves and $63
million total, at a minimum; noting that Gulf County’s minimum amount of money is safe.
County Administrator Hammond stated that staff worked two (2) years on this (dry dock)
project and the official application was submitted last night. Upon inquiry by Chairman
Quinn, County Administrator Hammond stated that all elected bodies (County, City of Port
St. Joe, City of Wewahitchka, and Gulf County School Board) supported the one project
for this first year. Chairman Quinn noted his support of other applications deemed good
for Gulf County. Commissioner McCroan discussed the multi-purpose use of the road (old
Mill site); stating that Gulf County needs jobs. Discussion followed by members of the
Board regarding the (dry dock) project. County Administrator Hammond provided a
history of the County’s efforts to create jobs and the creation of Triumph. Upon inquiry by
Commissioner McCroan, County Administrator Hammond stated that the dry dock will be
joint ownership between Gulf County and The Port Authority. He reported that staff will
invite the County’s representative to the Triumph Board meeting which is scheduled June
12th; suggesting each Board member contact the Triumph Board members to encourage
them to look favorable on our application.

INTER-LOCAL AGREEMENT – BAY COUNTY / E.M.S. TRANSPORT
County Attorney Novak discussed a recent policy adopted by Bay County (Commission)
regarding (E.M.S.) transport for residents; reporting that this new policy will have a

negative effect on what Gulf County can provide to the residents of Mexico Beach and
change how the County will be reimbursed. He stated that Bay County is proposing an
interlocal agreement; reporting once drafted the Chairman will sign it. County Attorney
Novak stated that any cost above and beyond what is not reimbursed through Medicare,
Indigent Care, on their transports from Mexico Beach, Bay County will pay the difference.
After discussion, Commissioner McDaniel motioned to authorize the Chairman to sign an
interlocal agreement with Bay County regarding the E.M.S. transport of residents of
Mexico Beach. Commissioner Rogers seconded the motion. Chairman Quinn called for
public comment. There being no public comment, the motion passed unanimously.

recommendation of the Planning Department that the Board move forward with this

OIL DRILLING ALONG APALACHICOLA RIVER
Rebecca Jetton, representative of the Apalachicola Riverkeeper Board of Directors
appeared before the Board to discuss the concerns of oil drilling along the Apalachicola
River <handout provided to the Clerk for record>; reporting that the Florida Department
of Environmental Protection issued a permit for an exploratory oil well near Kinard above
the Apalachicola River in Calhoun County. She requested that the Board consider
amending the County’s Comprehensive Plan and Land Development Regulations (LDRs)
to prohibit oil drilling in the wetlands and in the one hundred year floodplain along the
Apalachicola River and to prohibit the disposal of toxic chemicals, brines, solid waste
leachate and other industrial wastes utilizing shallow or deep injection wells.
