SEPTEMBER 25, 2018
PORT ST. JOE, FLORIDA
SPECIAL MEETING
The Gulf County Board of County Commissioners met this date in special session with
the following members present: Chairman Sandy Quinn, Jr., Vice Chairman Jimmy
Rogers, and Commissioners David C. Rich, Ward McDaniel, and Phil McCroan.
Others present were: County attorney Jeremy Novak, Clerk Budget & Finance Officer
Sherry Herring, Deputy Clerk Leanna Roberts, County Administrator Michael L.
Hammond, Deputy Administrator Kari Summers, Central Services Director Lynn Lanier,
Gulf County E.D.C. Director Warren Yeager, Jr., Gulf County E.D.C. Staff Assistant
Lianna Sagins, G.I.S. Coordinator Jessica Susich, Assistant Mosquito Control Director
Austin Horton, County Planner Lee Collinsworth, Public Works & Mosquito Control
Director Mark Cothran, Sheriff Mike Harrison, T.D.C. Executive Director Kelli Godwin, and
Veterans’ Services Officer & S.H.I.P. Administrator Joe Paul.
Chairman Quinn called the meeting to order at 8:00 a.m., E.T.
F.E.M.A. FLOOD MAP REVISIONS
County Administration Hammond discussed a meeting that was held yesterday (with
Northwest Florida Water Management District); stating they have come to a potential
resolution. Planner Collinsworth presented maps to the Board for review; discussing the
various communities within the County that are coming out of flood zones. County
Administrator Hammond reported that 90% of the existing built homes on the north side
of the bridge in White City are going to come out of the flood zone, and there are parcels
on the south-side of the bridge that will be coming out of the unnumbered A Zone. Jerrick
Saquibal, of Northwest Florida Water Management District appeared before the Board to
provide an update from the meeting held yesterday with the County Administrator. He
reported that the agreement was move forward with the inland panels that are going from
A and AE to X Zones. Mr. Saquibal discussed the process regarding the zoning changes
to the inland panels; reporting that there are sixty-three (63) inland panels and forty (40)
coastal panels. Upon motion by Commissioner McDaniel, second by Commissioner
McCroan, and unanimous vote, the Board extended Mr. Saquibal time to speak. Mr.
Saquibal discussed re-running the study on the coastal panels; reporting that the process
is going to take two & half to three (2½ to 3) years. After discussion by Commissioners
McDaniel and McCroan, Mr. Saquibal stated that they will still have to discuss funding
with F.EM.A., as this resolution was agreed upon last night; reporting that the funds are
secured for the inland process. County Administrator Hammond reported that the
Northwest Florida Water Management District is going to do a mass LOMA (Letter of Map
Amendment) for those inland areas at no cost to the resident. He discussed the study to
be completed on the coastal panels. Upon inquiry by Commissioner Rich regarding when

residents should expect a letter notifying them of the zone change, County Administrator
Hammond stated that the process will start immediately and within six (6) months
residents will be notified of the zone change (for the inland panels). Chairman Quinn
called for public comment. There was no public comment. County Administrator
Hammond requested direction from the Board to move forward on the non-coastal panels
and a mass LOMA (Letter of Map Amendment) on those lots that are coming out of the A
or AE flood zone. Commissioner Rich made the motion to move forward. Commissioner
Rogers seconded the motion. Chairman Quinn called for public comment. There being
no public comment, the motion passed unanimously. County Administrator Hammond
requested a motion from the Board to request that Northwest Florida Water Management
District do additional studies on the coastal areas (40 panels). Commissioner McCroan
so moved. Commissioner Rogers seconded the motion. Chairman Quinn called for public
comment. There being no public comment, the motion passed unanimously. County
administrator Hammond requested that the Board draft a letter to Northwest Florida Water
Management District. Commissioner Rogers motioned to submit a letter to the Northwest
Florida Water Management District. Commissioner McDaniel seconded the motion.
Chairman Quinn called for public comment. There being no public comment, the motion
passed unanimously.
There being no further business, and upon motion by Commissioner Rogers, second by
Commissioner Rich, and unanimous vote, the meeting did then adjourn at 8:17 a.m., E.T.
