OPENING OF MEETING
Chairman Quinn called the meeting to order at 9:01 a.m., E.T.
CHAIRMAN QUINN COMMENTS
Chiarman Quinn noted one small change to the site plan that was approved. The large 180’
fields were changed to 200’ fields, based on typical tournament regulations.
APPROVAL OF AGENDA AND MINUTES
The committee reviewed the agenda and minutes from January 16, 2018. Mike Lacour
motioned for an approval as presented, Jim Anderson seconded, and it passed unanimously.
STATUS AND REPORT FROM DEWBERRY REPRESENTATIVE
Clay Smallwood presented a preliminary budget based on the site plan. There were some things
that were not included in the budget such as phasing of the project, revenues, and work that
will be done within the City and County employees. Local Public Clare Morris spoke to the
committee about when the park plan is finalized to please consider the residents in the area.
She requested that there be some money in the budget for beautification and landscaping to
help keep light pollution and traffic away from back yards. Rex Buzzett requested an estimate
on collections from the Tourist Development Council’s 5th penny since freezing the spending.
Sherry Herring noted that the end of the fiscal year would project the penny to collect
$825,000. Jeremy Novak noted that this would be a good time to discuss the penny and if the
committee wished for the Gulf County Commissioners to continue to collect the 5th penny since
it was due to sunset after 5 years. After some discussion, Mr. Buzzett motioned a
recommendation to the County Commissioners from the 10th Street Subcommittee to extend
collection of the 5th penny for another 5 years, Mr. Lacour seconded it, and it passed
unanimously.

REPORT BY GULF COUNTY TOURIST DEVELOPMENT COUNCIL
T.D.C. Director Kelli Godwin explained that the next step would be a brand identity process and
from that process form a logo. Mrs. Godwin provided a quote from Curiosity Marketing, who
has been working with the T.D.C. on their website and marketing, for $2,750. Warren Yeager
motioned for an approval for Curiosity Marketing to perform the brand identity, John Grantland
seconded it, and it passed unanimously. There was some discussion about acquiring land near
HWY 71 owned by the St. Joe Company. Jeremy Novak formed a committee to look at land
details and feasibility.
GENERAL COMMENTARY FROM COMMITTEE MEMEBERS
Jeri Ashcraft noted that there might be grant funding available from the Garden Club for
beautification and landscaping. Rex Buzzett explained that Dixie Youth might also be able to
provide funding up to $5,000 for a few years. Jeremy Novak stressed that the next steps should
be how the project will be phased. This would include a proposed timeline for each phase as
well as a budget. Mr. Novak also explained that if Dewberry is going to be the official designer
and engineer for the project there needs to be an official agreement and contract.
ESTABLISH THE NEXT MEETING DATE
It was decided to meet on March 20, 2018 9:00 in the Gulf County Emergency Management
Building.
