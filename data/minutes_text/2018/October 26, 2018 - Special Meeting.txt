OCTOBER 26, 2018
PORT ST. JOE, FLORIDA
SPECIAL MEETING
The Gulf County Board of County Commissioners met this date in special session with
the following members present: Chairman Sandy Quinn, Jr., Vice Chairman Jimmy
Rogers, and Commissioners David C. Rich, Ward McDaniel, and Phil McCroan.
Others present were: County Attorney Jeremy Novak, Clerk Rebecca L. Norris, Deputy
Clerk Leanna Roberts, County Administrator Michael L. Hammond, Deputy Administrator
Kari Summers, Building Official Brad Bailey, Gulf County E.D.C. Director Jim McKnight,
Emergency Management Director Marshall Nelson, G.I.S. Coordinator Jessica Susich,
Human Resource Director Brett Lowry, Office of Management & Budget Director Sherry
Herring, County Planner Lee Collinsworth, Property Appraiser Mitch Burke, Public Works
& Mosquito Control Director Mark Cothran, Sheriff Mike Harrison, and Tax Collector
Shirley Jenkins.
Chairman Quinn called the meeting to order at 12:01 p.m., E.T.
Chairman Quinn led in the Pledge of Allegiance to the Flag.
BID #1819-02 – DISASTER RECOVERY CONSULTANT SERVICES
Upon recommendation by the Scoring Committee, Commissioner McDaniel motioned to
award Bid #1819-02 for the Disaster Recovery Consultant Services to Horne, LLP.
Commissioner Rich seconded the motion. Chairman Quinn called for public comment.
There being no public comment, the motion passed unanimously.
UPDATE – HURRICANE MICHAEL
County Administrator Hammond reported that there is no truth to the rumor that Gulf
County or any County regulations are prohibiting F.E.M.A. trailers in the County. He
stated the City of Port St. Joe has relaxed their rules regarding one (1) RV (recreational
vehicle) being placed in the front yard while working on homes and on modular/mobile
homes in certain areas in the City of Port. St. Joe. Gulf County E.D.C. Director McKnight
appeared before the Board to provide a report regarding job opportunities within Gulf
County and applicants that have applied for an S.B.A. (Small Business Administration)
loan. Emergency Management Director Nelson appeared before the Board to encourage
residents to apply for F.E.M.A. Sheriff Harrison provided an update regarding law
enforcement; reporting that the curfew has been changed to midnight to 5:00 a.m., E.T.
DISASTER OVERTIME REIMBURSEMENT FOR CONSTITUTIONAL OFFICERS
Office of Management & Budget Director Herring provided the Board a memorandum
<copy provided to the Clerk for the record> recommending the County reimburse the
Constitutional Officers for their employees’ disaster related overtime due to Hurricane

Michael, to be paid from Disaster Fund 127. Commissioner Rogers so moved.
Commissioner McCroan seconded the motion. Chairman Quinn called for public
comment. There being no public comment, the motion passed unanimously.
BID #1819-02 – DISASTER RECOVERY CONSULTANT SERVICES
County Administrator Hammond requested Board approval to allow him to execute the
contract (for Bid #1819-02), if the County can come to an agreement. County Attorney
Novak recommended that the Board allow the Chairman and County Administrator
Hammond to sign the contract for the disaster recovery consultant services (with Horne,
LLP), contingent upon his review. Commissioner McDaniel motioned to approve his
recommendation. Commissioner Rogers seconded the motion. Chairman Quinn called
for public comment. There being no public comment, the motion passed unanimously.
UPDATE – HURRICANE MICHAEL
Commissioner McDaniel provided an update regarding the North-end of the County. After
discussion by Commissioners Rogers and Rich, Port St. Joe City Manager Jim Anderson
appeared before the Board to provide an update regarding the water and sewer for Cape
San Blas. Commissioner McCroan reported that the County is working on the matter
regarding F.E.M.A. and S.B.A. (Small Business Administration) assistance for those that
reside in the C.B.R.A. (Coastal Barrier Resource Act) area.
There being no further business, and upon motion by Commissioner McCroan, second
by Commissioner Rich, and unanimous vote, the meeting did then adjourn at 12:27 p.m.,
E.T.
