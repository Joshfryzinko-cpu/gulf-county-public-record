LAND DONATION – KENNY LAKE PROPERTY / YON’S ADDITION TO BEACON HILL
County Administrator Hammond reported that Trustees of Yon’s Addition to Beacon Hill
have agreed to convey the Kenny Lake Property to the County as a Passive Park. He
stated over the next year, as Public Works has time, they will fill in the Lake.
Commissioner McDaniel so moved. Commissioner Rogers seconded the motion. After
discussion by members of the Board and staff, Chairman Quinn called for public
comment. There being no public comment, the motion passed unanimously.
ADVERTISE PUBLIC HEARING – PROPOSED TOWING RATES ORDINANCE
Upon request by County Attorney Novak, Commissioner Rogers motioned to advertise to
hold a Public Hearing at the September (regular) Board Meeting to consider the adoption
of an ordinance regarding towing rates. Commissioner Rich seconded the motion.
Chairman Quinn called for public comment. There being no public comment, the motion
passed unanimously.
MEDICAL MARIJUANA DISPENSARY
County Attorney Novak requested authorization to proceed in presenting proposed
language for Gulf County regarding possible land development regulations and
restrictions that the County would consider on medicinal marijuana dispensary consistent
with State Law. Commissioner McDaniel so moved to move forward. Commissioner
Rogers seconded the motion. Chairman Quinn called for public comment. There being
no public comment, the motion passed unanimously.
10th STREET PARK – CITY OF PORT ST. JOE RESOLUTION
County Attorney Novak discussed the City of Port St. Joe’s Resolution regarding the 10th
Street Park that is in the County’s Information Packet, pages 6-9; reporting that he is
working with T.D.C. to bring a recommendation and his legal review back before the
Board.
ENGINEERING PROPOSAL – MILL SITE DREDGING
E.D.C. Director Yeager discussed an engineering proposal for the Mill Site Dredging
Project; reporting that this can be paid though the J.P.A. (Joint Participation Agreement).
He requested Board approval of this proposal (Dewberry * Port St. Joe Bulkhead Dredge

Project * Sampling and Sediment Testing Professional Services * not to exceed
$102,480.00) <copy provided to the Clerk for the Record>; stating that it will be presented
to D.O.T. (Florida Department of Transportation) and upon their approval, the County will
proceed with this project. Commissioner McCroan so moved. Commissioner Rogers
seconded the motion. Chairman Quinn called for public comment. There being no public
comment, the motion passed unanimously.
SALINA’S PARK – N.R.D.A. PROJECT
E.D.C. Director Yeager reported that the N.R.D.A. (Natural Resource Damage
Assessment) Project for Salina’s Park is underway with a Pickleball court scheduled to
be built within the next 90-120 days, and the elevated boardwalk on Bayside is going
through the permitting process which should be completed by next summer.
HIGHLAND VIEW BOAT RAMP – N.R.D.A. PROJECT
E.D.C. Director Yeager stated that the Highland View Boat Ramp Project will be totally
funded, permitted, and constructed before next spring.
WINDMARK PARK PIER – N.R.D.A. PROJECT
E.D.C. Director Yeager reported that the WindMark Park Pier Project has grown in scope;
stating that D.E.P. (Department of Environmental Projection) needs to provide handicap
access. He reported that their (D.E.P.) cost estimate is $3.3 million and only $1.27 million
is funded. He provided options for the Board to consider: 1) the County can ask for other
grant funds from other outlets; 2) go a few years out and ask for Pot I or Pot III funding;
or 3) return the project back to D.E.P. and resubmit for full N.R.D.A. (Natural Resource
Damage Assessment) Funding; reporting that D.E.P. is recommending option 3.
HIGHLAND VIEW BOAT RAMP – N.R.D.A. PROJECT
County Administrator Hammond discussed his concerns regarding the Highland View
Boat Ramp Project; stating that the County could have done this project quicker and for
less.
GRANT APPLICATIONS – WHITE CITY BOAT RAMP & ODENA BOAT RAMP
E.D.C. Director Yeager stated that the County applied for two (2) grants, one (1) for White
City Boat Ramp and one (1) for Odena Boat Ramp (Florida Fish & Wildlife Commission);
reporting that these applications have been awarded, in the amount of $200,000.00 (for
each project). He stated that both grants are matching grants, but the County has funds
available in the Building Improvement Fund. E.D.C. Director Yeager reported that the
agreements should be submitted to the County in the next few weeks.
FLOATING DRY DOCK – TRIUMPH
E.D.C. Director Yeager discussed meeting with the Triumph Board regarding the Floating
Dry Dock Project; reporting that Triumph is ready to move this project forward. He stated
that the Triumph Board has requested a meeting with Chairman Quinn and County staff
next Friday; noting that the County will know more after this meeting. E.D.C. Director
Yeager discussed looking for other funding sources for this project; reporting that a grant
application has been submitted for the Governor’s Job Growth Funds Grant.

LEASE – INDUSTRIAL PARK / SKYBORNE TECHNOLOGY
E.D.C. Director Yeager discussed the leasing of the Industrial Park to Skyborne
Technology, Inc. (formally known as Cherokee Air, LLC); reporting that a press release is
scheduled for September regarding the company. He stated that this company will
provide twenty (20) jobs immediately and up to 50-75 jobs in the near future. E.D.C.
Director Yeager reported that the technology has to do with drones and airships. He
stated that staff has met with Triumph’s education representative to discuss an education
piece to drones.
BEACH RESTORATION PROJECT – CAPE SAN BLAS
E.D.C. Director Yeager reported that the County still has not received notice from U.S.
Treasury if they will allow $2.8 million of RESTORE money to be used on the Beach
Restoration Project (Bid #1718-13 * St. Joseph Peninsula Beach Renourishment Project)
being that it is in a C.B.R.A. (Costal Barrier Resources Act) area. He stated that U.S.
Treasury received a letter from U.S. Fish & Wildlife and there is ongoing discussions
between these agencies. E.D.C. Director Yeager reported that the County will need to
know something in the next two (2) weeks in order to maintain the contractor; stating that
a request will be made to the Board to hold a meeting in September to discuss how to
move forward with this project. He then called Melissa Beaudry, of Langton Consulting to
appear before the Board to provide an update. Ms. Beaudry appeared before the Board
to discuss working to move the Beach Restoration Project forward as well as the next
MYIP (Multi-Year Implementation Plan) projects with Pot I money. She discussed the
grant process regarding the Beach Restoration Project with the U.S. Treasury; reporting
that Langton Consulting is committed to assist the County with Triumph funds and the
C.B.R.A. designation issue. Commissioner McDaniel discussed his concerns regarding
the lack of support from State representatives; encouraging the public to write their
Senator and Congressman.
COASTAL BARRIER RESOURCES ACT
E.D.C. Director Yeager stated that the County has spent a lot of time on C.B.R.A. (Coastal
Barrier Resources Act); reporting that forty (40) acres made it through the committee to
be removed, but has not yet been approved by the House of Representatives, the Senate,
or the U.S. President. Commissioner McCroan referenced the letter in 2008 from U.S.
Fish & Wildlife that cost the County $15 million. E.D.C. Director Yeager discussed
Hurricane Gustav of 2008, the $15 million worth of sand lost due to this storm, and the
letter of objection from U.S. Fish & Wildlife.

UPDATE – MOSQUITO CONTROL SPRAYING
Upon request by County Administrator Hammond, Assistant Mosquito Control Director
Horton provided an update regarding the scheduled mosquito control spraying times and
days; reporting weather permitting they are spraying seven (7) nights a week.
Commissioner McDaniel stated that with the mosquito spraying trucks having a GPS, the
County can track the routes. He reported that the trucks are quiet and the spray is
calibrated based on the speed of the vehicle. Upon request by Commissioner Rich,
Assistant Mosquito Control Director Horton discussed the new spraying machines and
the chemicals used for spraying for the public. He asked if the public would call the office
with their complaints at (850) 227-1401.
