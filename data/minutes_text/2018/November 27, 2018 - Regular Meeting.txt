EMERGENCY ORDINANCE – F.E.M.A. FLOOD MAP ORDINANCE ADOPTION
Brett Cyphers, Northwest FL Water Management District Executive Director appeared
before the Board to offer assistance and discuss the flood mapping process. County
Administrator Hammond discussed meeting with N.W.F.W.M.D. to come up with a
solution to assist residents that want to rebuild immediately, due to Hurricane Michael.
He inquired whether the Board wanted to go one (1) foot above base flood or two (2) feet
for the preliminary maps. Following discussion, County Attorney Novak provided the
Board with the proposed emergency ordinance for review <copy provided to the Clerk for
the record> and then read aloud Florida Statute 125.66 paragraph 3 for the record. He
then requested the Board entertain a motion establishing both the waiver of the notice
requirements that the County would follow under Statute, as well as the immediacy and
the emergency that exists for the consideration of this proposed ordinance. Commissioner
McCroan so moved. Commissioner Rogers seconded the motion. Chairman Quinn called
for public comment. Pat Hardman, President of the Coastal Community Association
appeared before the Board to recommend the Board consider going up one (1) foot
(above base flood). After discussion by County Attorney Novak, Chairman Quinn called
for any opposition to the motion. There being no opposition, the motion passed
unanimously. County Attorney Novak stated for the record that with the administration’s
recommendation before the Thanksgiving holiday, notices were posted more than a week
