QUASI-JUDICIAL HEARING – REVIEW & CONSIDERATION OF P.D.R.B.
RECOMMENDATIONS
VARIANCE REQUEST – WILLIAM & SUSAN JOHNSON
Planner Collinsworth presented a variance request from William and Susan Johnson *
Parcel ID #03185-027R; stating their request is to build a six (6) foot wide dune walkover.
Upon inquiry by Chairman Quinn, Planner Collinsworth reported that it was a 4-0 vote by
the P.D.R.B. to approve. Chairman Quinn asked for the public wishing to speak on this
variance request to please step forward. Chairman Quinn called for any objection to the
waiving of the Quasi-Judicial Hearing. There being no objection, Commissioner McCroan
motioned to waive the Quasi-Judicial Hearing. Commissioner Rogers seconded the
motion, and it passed unanimously. Lance Watson of Southeastern Consulting Engineers
who represents William & Susan Johnson appeared before the Board to discuss the
variance request; reporting that if this property changes hands and the walkover has to
be replaced, the walkover will revert back to a width of four (4) foot. Upon inquiry by
Commissioner Rich regarding handicap accessibility, Mr. Watson stated that it is not an
ADA requirement, but more for Mrs. Johnson’s physical disability. After discussion by
Planner Collinsworth, Chairman Quinn called for public comment. There was no further
public comment. County Attorney Novak reported that the P.D.R.B. voted 4-0 to grant this
variance; noting that granting the variance requested will not confer on the applicant any
special privilege that is denied by these regulations to other lands, structures or buildings
in the same district. He advised that the P.D.R.B. established that competent and
substantial evidence had been presented, that proper notice has been complied with by
the Planning Department and with Florida Statute, and recommended the Board approve
this variance request. Commissioner McCroan motioned to waive the reading.
Commissioner Rogers seconded the motion, and it passed unanimously. Commissioner
McDaniel motioned to accept the recommendations of the P.D.R.B. on this variance
request. Commissioner Rich seconded the motion. Chairman Quinn called for public
comment. There being no public comment, the motion passed unanimously.
PRELIMINARY/FINAL PLAT – OAK TRACE SUBDIVISION
Planner Collinsworth presented a preliminary/final plat for Oak Trace Subdivision by JR
& JR Holdings, LLC * Parcel ID #06076-011R; stating this is a 31 (thirty-one) lot

subdivision off of Oak Grove Avenue. Chairman Quinn called those wanting to speak on
this item to please step forward. Chairman Quinn asked for any objection to the waiving
of the Quasi-Judicial Hearing. There being no objection, Commissioner McDaniel
motioned to waive of the Quasi-Judicial Hearing. Commissioner Rich seconded the
motion. Chairman Quinn called for public comment. There being no public comment, the
motion passed unanimously. Dina Bautista of Dewberry, representing JR & JR Holdings,
LLC appeared before the Board to discuss the preliminary/final plat for Oak Trace
Subdivision. Planner Collinsworth reported that the P.D.R.B. recommended, by a 4-0
vote, to approve this plat. Chairman Quinn called for public comment. There was no public
comment. Commissioner Rich motioned to approve. Commissioner McDaniel seconded
the motion. Chairman Quinn called for public comment. There being no public comment,
the motion passed unanimously. County Attorney Novak referenced the P.D.R.B.
Subdivision Preliminary/Final Plat Public Hearing and Findings Form, presented to the
Board for review <copy provided to the Clerk for the record>. He referenced the section
of Ordinance 2005-16 that establishes the standards for granting the subdivision and
preliminary/final plat approval requirements. County Attorney Novak stated the P.D.R.B.
recommends, by a 4-0 vote, for the Board to approve; noting the P.D.R.B. has accepted
the representations of both the applicant and the Planner. He confirmed the adherence
to the Public Hearing Notice and requirements in accordance with the LDR and
Subdivision Ordinance #2005-16. Commissioner Rogers so moved. Commissioner Rich
seconded the motion. Chairman Quinn called for public comment. There being no public
comment, the motion passed unanimously.
10th STREET PARK – SPORTS COMPLEX / T.D.C. PENNY
Christy McElroy, of Port St. Joe appeared before the Board to discuss the amount of
money from T.D.C. that has been given to the City of Port St. Joe for the parks in 2015,
2016, 2017, and part of 2018; reporting the total is $39,584.00 <copy provided to the

RV ORDINANCE – POLE BARNS
Chris Wahl, of Oak Grove appeared before the Board to discuss his concerns regarding
the lack of enforcement of the RV (Recreational Vehicle) Ordinance, construction of pole
barns, and RVs in residential areas. He discussed the property values and collection of
property taxes.
CODE ENFORCEMENT & INFRASTRUCTURE – BUILDING DEPARTMENT
Lorinda Gingell, of Oak Grove appeared before the Board to discuss her concerns
regarding code enforcement and infrastructure; requesting that the Board increase the
Building Department’s budget in order to hire another code enforcer. She also requested
that the Sherriff’s Office have enough deputies to tell people with RVs to evacuate when
a storm is coming. Commissioner McCroan discussed the need to address this matter.
Chairman Quinn noted that the Board will need to come up with a solution. Upon inquiry
by Commissioner McDaniel, Building Official Bailey appeared before the Board to report

that Gulf County does not require a permit for bringing in dirt or smaller pre-built
structures. He discussed the process of a property owner not being in compliance of
pulling permits. Members of the Board discussed their concerns regarding this matter.
