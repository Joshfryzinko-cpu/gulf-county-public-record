BEEKEEPERS – TUPELO HONEY
Commissioner McDaniel called County Extension Service Director Bodrey and Jeff
Pippin, of the Florida Department of Agriculture to appear before the Board. He discussed
his concerns regarding complaints about the harvest of the tupelo honey due to over-
crowding. County Extension Service Director Bodrey report that there is a problem with
the tupelo trees due to the lack of water coming down from the Apalachicola River. He
then introduced Jeff Pippin, of the Florida Department of Agriculture who appeared to
discuss the history of beekeeping in Florida. He discussed the overcrowding of
beekeepers harvesting tupelo and the recent condition of the tupelo trees. Mr. Pippin
reported that the majority of the complaints are bees in pools, which they are working on
the best they can. He informed the Board to notify him of any out-of-state beekeepers,
and he will make sure they are registered for inspection; discussing diseases that bees
pickup and bring into the State. Mr. Pippin discussed the population, overcrowding of bee
stands, and working with the beekeepers under the Law; stating that he provided the
Board with a copy for review <copy on file with the Clerk for the record>. After discussion,
Commissioner Rich asked if the State could reduce the number of days (for out-of-state
beekeepers). Mr. Pippin stated he would have to talk to his boss about it. Upon inquiry by
Commissioner McCroan, Mr. Pippin stated that the beekeepers on the barge are locals.

RECREATIONAL VEHICLE ORDINANCE
Commissioner McCroan reported that he received calls regarding pole barns and RVs
(recreational vehicles); stating he also received a petition of people in the community
mostly in the Oak Grove area. He requested the Board allow County Attorney Novak to
look into this matter for possible solutions. County Attorney Novak discussed the timeline
regarding the adoption of the RV (recreational vehicle) Ordinance and the amendments.
He reported that presently Oak Grove, Jones Homestead, and Highland View are not
under the County Ordinance, but are dealt with under the Land Development Regulations.

County Attorney Novak stated in order for him to go back, review, and notice public
hearings, a vote of the Board will be needed. After discussion by Commissioner McCroan,
he motioned to allow County Attorney Novak to look into it and bring back a
recommendation. Commissioner Rich seconded the motion. After Board discussion,
Commissioner McDaniel asked if the motion was for County Attorney to review and
research Oak Grove primarily and bring a recommendation back to the Board.
Commissioner McCroan agreed. County Attorney Novak stated that once the issue is
opened the other areas will be on the table as well as Oak Grove. He recommended that
the Board deal with all areas, not just one. Chairman Quinn called for public comment.
Chris Wahl, of Oak Grove appeared before the Board to discuss his concerns regarding
the RV (recreational vehicle) Ordinance; reporting that it is not just an Oak Grove issue.
He stated that pole barns are being advertised as homes at St. Joe Beach. County
Attorney Novak stated the Ordinance applies to St. Joe Beach. Upon motion by
Commissioner McCroan, second by Commissioner Rich, the Board extended Mr. Wahl’s
time to speak. Mr. Wahl continued to discuss his concerns regarding the property value
due to the pole barns, installation of pole barns without a permit, and using it as rental
property. Chairman Quinn called for public comment. Laura Angel, of Oak Grove
appeared before the Board to discuss her concerns regarding road conditions in Oak
Grove, RV usage, stormwater and culvert issues, as well as the cutting of trees. County
Administrator Hammond inquired as to what the Board wishes staff to review; stating the
RV Ordinance does not address pole barns. He reported that Oak Grove is a residential
area and they cannot legally rent RV slots. County Administrator Hammond stated that
the County is noticing the violators, and the Building Department is working on the building
of pole barns illegally. After discussion by County Administrator Hammond,
Commissioner McCroan stated the peoples’ concern is those who just throw up a pole
barn on a vacant lot without a structure or a permit. Commissioner McDaniel discussed
reviewing the matter and bringing back a recommendation to the Board. Commissioner
McCroan stated for the record that he will file the petition with the Clerk of the Court.
Chairman Quinn called for additional public comment. Chris Wahl, of Oak Grove again
appeared before the Board to state that they are not asking the County to abolish the RV
(recreational vehicle) Ordinance; noting there are different needs. Chairman Quinn called
for public comment. There being no further public comment, Chairman Quinn stated the
motion by Commissioner McCroan is to go back and review the (RV) Ordinance; which
will open up all areas with Commissioner Rich second. The motion passed unanimously.

VARIANCE REQUEST – DAVID AND HEIDI REHAK
Planner Collinsworth presented a variance request from David and Heidi Rehak * Parcel
ID #03249-005R; stating they are requesting a roadside setback variance to build 9’ 6”
(nine foot; six inches) into a 10’ (ten foot) egress easement. County Attorney provided a
recap of the County’s procedure summary. He reported that the Board has provided
proper notice, it complies with the LDR (Land Development Regulations), it was properly
posted, and publicly advertised. Chairman Quinn requested those wishing to speak to
state their name for the record before the Clerk swears them in. Jack Husband appeared,
representing David and Heidi Rehak to state his name for the record, as well as Attorney
Patrick Floyd, representing Karen and Jim Biddy. Upon request by County Attorney
Novak, Chairman Quinn called for any objection to the waiving of the Quasi-Judicial
Hearing. There being no objection, Commissioner McDaniel motioned to waive the Quasi-
Judicial Hearing. Commissioner McCroan seconded the motion, the motion passed
unanimously. Jack Husband, of Southeastern Consulting Engineering, Inc. discussed the
variance request application for Parcel ID #03249-005R; reporting that the P.D.R.B. voted
to deny the variance request based on lack of information. He reported that an agreement
has been met between the two parties (Rehak’s & Biddy’s) to be 5’ (five foot) off of the
easement, which is 15’ (fifteen feet) from the property line. Attorney Floyd appeared
before the Board to discuss the variance and the agreement agreed upon. After
discussion by members of the Board, County Attorney Novak reported that the P.D.R.B.
voted, 3-0, to deny based on the fact for further evidence and testimony heard.
Commissioner McDaniel motioned to confirm this variance request is of special conditions
and circumstances existing which are peculiar to the land, structure or buildings involved
and which are not applicable to other lands, structures, or buildings in the same district.
Commissioner McCroan seconded the motion. After discussion by County Attorney
Novak, Chairman Quinn called for public comment. There being no public comment, the
motion passed unanimously. Upon request by County Attorney Novak, Commissioner
McCroan motioned to accept the P.D.R.B. statement of completeness for the variance
application; that Gulf County deems the record submitted as competent and substantial
evidence to support the decision of this Commission for the variance application; and Gulf
County accepts the representations and the proper notice of today’s hearings and
previsions specified under special exemptions under the Gulf County LDRs (Land
Development Regulations) for variance applications pursuant to a Public Notice for Quasi-
Judicial Hearing affixed to the County Courthouse and Administration Building, policy
effective November 19, 2014 which provides public notice to all parties should be
permitted to present evidence. Commissioner Rich seconded the motion. Chairman
Quinn called for public comment. There being no public comment, the motion passed
unanimously. After discussion by County Attorney Novak, Commissioner Rich motioned
to approve this variance. Commissioner McDaniel seconded the motion. Chairman Quinn
called for public comment. There being no public comment, the motion passed
unanimously.

Peninsula Beach Renourishment Project, as Central Services Director Lanier read aloud

discussion, the motion passed unanimously.

HOUSE BILL 631 – BEACH RESTRICTIONS
Upon inquiry by Chairman Quinn, County Attorney Novak reported that House Bill 631
was passed regarding beach restrictions; stating that this Law goes into effect this
summer. He reported going forward that the sandy shores belong to all people; stating
should they (local governing boards) want to adopt a customary use ordinance, the Law
will now impose upon Counties or Cities that they go through the Courts to justify it.
County Attorney Novak also stated that Gulf County does not have a customary use
ordinance, but we are under the same State doctrine as the other 67 Counties that line
the coast. He reported that the County will continue to drive on our beaches and enforce
the County Ordinances.
