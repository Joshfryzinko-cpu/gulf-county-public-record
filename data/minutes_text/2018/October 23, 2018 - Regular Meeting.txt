OCTOBER 23, 2018
PORT ST. JOE, FLORIDA
REGULAR MEETING
The Gulf County Board of County Commissioners met this date in regular session with
the following members present: Chairman Sandy Quinn, Jr., Vice Chairman Jimmy
Rogers, and Commissioners David C. Rich, Ward McDaniel, and Phil McCroan.
Others present were: County Attorney Jeremy Novak, Clerk Rebecca L. Norris, Clerk
Director of Finance & Management Rhonda Woodward, Deputy Clerk Leanna Roberts,
County Administrator Michael L. Hammond, County Assistant Administrator Warren
Yeager, Jr., Deputy Administrator Kari Summers, Building Official Brad Bailey, Gulf
County E.D.C. Director Jim McKnight, Emergency Management Director Marshall
Nelson, E9-1-1 Coordinator Ben Guthrie, Gulf County E.M.S. Director Houston Whitfield,
Dewberry/County Engineer Clay Smallwood, III, G.I.S. Coordinator Jessica Susich,
Human Resource Director Brett Lowry, Assistant Mosquito Control Director Austin
Horton, Parks & Recreational Director Billy Traylor, County Planner Lee Collinsworth,
Public Works & Mosquito Control Director Mark Cothran, Sheriff Mike Harrison,
Supervisor of Elections John Hanlon, Veterans' Service Officer & S.H.I.P. Administrator
Joe Paul, and VSO/S.H.I.P./Central Services Director Lynn Lanier.
Sheriff Harrison called the meeting to order at 9:00 a.m., E.T.
Chairman Quinn opened the meeting with prayer, and led in the Pledge of Allegiance to
the Flag.
UPDATE – COUNTY STAFF / HURRICANE MICHAEL
County Administrator Hammond discussed staff working together during the disaster
(Hurricane Michael).
FUNERAL SERVICE – PRICE
County Administrator Hammond reported that Fire Coordinator Brad Price’s funeral
service will be held tomorrow at the High School Football Field in Wewahitchka.
POLICY – ANNUAL LEAVE
Upon request by County Administrator Hammond, Commissioner McCroan motioned to
waive the County Policy regarding leave sell back; allowing eighty (80) hours of annual
leave to be sold from October 23, 2018 to December 31, 2018. Commissioner Rogers
seconded the motion. Chairman Quinn called for public comment. There being no public
comment, the motion passed unanimously.

SPECIAL MEETING – OCTOBER 26, 2018
Upon recommendation by County Administrator Hammond, Commissioner Rogers
motioned to call a Special Meeting for Friday, October 26, 2018 at 12:00 p.m., E.T. to
award the public assistance contract (Bid #1819-02). Commissioner McDaniel seconded
the motion. Chairman Quinn called for public comment. There being no public comment,
the motion passed unanimously.
UPDATE – SUPERVISOR OF ELECTIONS / HURRICANE MICHAEL
Supervisor of Elections Hanlon appeared before the Board to report that early voting will
begin Saturday, October 27, 2018. He discussed the Executive Order received from the
Governor concerning the election plan. Supervisor of Elections Hanlon stated that voters
can go to the Supervisor of Elections office located in Port St. Joe or at the Wewahitchka
Library to cast their vote; discussing the absentee voting procedures. Upon inquiry by
County Administrator Hammond, Supervisor of Elections Hanlon discussed the media
coverage regarding the voting locations.
PURCHASE – DISASTER FUND / HURRICANE MICHAEL
Emergency Management Director Nelson appeared before the Board to request Board
approval to use the Disaster Fund to pay for items that are F.E.M.A. reimbursable.
Commissioner McDaniel so moved. Commissioner McCroan seconded the motion.
Chairman Quinn called for public comment. There being no public comment, the motion
passed unanimously.
UPDATE – EMERGENCY OPERATIONS CENTER / HURRICANE MICHAEL
Following discussion by Emergency Management Director Nelson, he introduced Incident
Commander Les Hallman, of the IMT (Incident Management Team). Mr. Hallman
appeared before the Board to provide an executive summary <copy provided to the Clerk
for the record> and introduce his team.
INTERLOCAL AGREEMENT – DISASTER DEBRIS SERVICES / MUNICIPALITIES
County Attorney Novak reported that both municipalities (City of Port St. Joe and the City
of Wewahitchka) have executed an Interlocal Agreement with Ashbritt, which will fall
under the County’s contract (Bid #1516-13).
BID #1819-02 – DISASTER RECOVERY CONSULTANT
County Attorney Novak stated that after review by the F.E.M.A. Attorneys, staff requested
that the Clerk’s Office not open the bids received for Bid #1819-02 for the Disaster
Recovery Consultant. He reported that the RFPs (request for proposals) will be amended
with approval of the Board. Commissioner McCroan motioned to suspend and amend the
Request for Proposals, and to extend the bid until Thursday at noon. Commissioner Rich
seconded the motion. Chairman Quinn called for public comment. There being no public
comment, the motion passed unanimously.
UNLICENSED CONTRACTORS – HURRICANE MICHAEL
County Attorney Novak discussed the matter of unlicensed contractors and price gouging;
reporting that the County is working to identify these individuals and will pursue them.

ECONOMIC OPPORTUNITY – SMALL BUSINESSES / HURRICANE MICHAEL
Assistant Administrator Yeager reported that FL Department of Economic Opportunity
Executive Director Cissy Proctor came to Gulf County to brief the small businesses
regarding their options to rebuild.
BEACH CLEANUP – ASHBRITT / DEBRIS REMOVAL CONTRACT
Assistant Administrator Yeager discussed that the beach cleanup may be under the
contract with Ashbritt; stating that the County Attorney will review the contract. He
requested that the business communities not try to move things until the County can
acknowledge that AshBritt, Inc. can do this service through the F.D.O.T. (Florida
Department of Transportation) contract. Assistant Administrator Yeager stated that the
County is working with MRD Associates regarding emergency berms for the beaches.
UPDATE – F.EM.A. / HURRICANE MICHAEL
John Ford, from F.E.M.A. appeared before the Board to report that a meeting will be held
today to discuss housing; stating that TSA (Transitional Sheltering Assistance has been
approved.
BIRTHDAY – PAUL & COTHRAN
Deputy Administrator Summers recognized Public Works Director Cothran and Veterans'
Service Officer/S.H.I.P. Administrator Paul’s birthdays.
REPORT – DAMAGE ASSESSMENT / HURRICANE MICHAEL
Planner Collinsworth provided a damage assessment report to the Board; stating that the
Planning Department will be providing the data to the Property Appraiser’s Office.
UPDATE – AMERICAN RED CROSS / HURRICANE MICHAEL
Heidi O’Shan, of the American Red Cross appeared before the Board to provide an
update to the Board.
UPDATE – BUILDING DEPARTMENT / HURRICANE MICHAEL
Planner Collinsworth reported that staff is checking every meter (electrical) box;
requesting that residents be patient. He also discussed the matter of unlicensed
contractors.
UPDATE – DUKE ENERGY / HURRICANE MICHAEL
County Administrator Hammond provided an update to the Board regarding Duke
Energy’s progress.
UPDATE – LIGHTHOUSE UTILITIES / HURRICANE MICHAEL
Jay Rish, of Lighthouse Utilities appeared before the Board to provide an update
regarding Lighthouse Utilities.

MOSQUITO CONTROL – HURRICANE MICHAEL
Assistant Mosquito Control Director Horton stated that due to the debris on roadways, he
is trying to get an aerial (mosquito) spray. Upon request by Assistant Mosquito Control
Director Horton, Commissioner McCroan motioned to allow the Chairman to sign a letter
requesting aerial spraying. Commissioner Rich seconded the motion. Chairman Quinn
called for public comment. There being no public comment, the motion passed
unanimously.
UPDATE – HEALTHCARE / HURRICANE MICHAEL
Gulf County Health Department Director Hinds appeared before the Board to provide an
update regarding healthcare and Sacred Heart Hospital.
UPDATE – TOURIST DEVELOPMENT / HURRICANE MICHAEL
T.D.C. Director Godwin provided an update to the Board; reporting that staff is assessing
the damage to the County’s parks and beaches.
UPDATE – COUNTY PARKS / HURRICANE MICHAEL
Parks & Recreational Director Traylor appeared before the Board to provide an update
regarding the T.D.C. Building and the County parks. He reported that eighty (80%)
percent of the timber at the Dead Lakes Park is destroyed; discussing the possibility of
harvesting the damaged timber.
UPDATE – EMERGENCY MEDICAL SERVICES / HURRICANE MICHAEL
E.M.S. Director Whitfield provided an update to the Board regarding E.M.S. (Emergency
Medical Services).
FLORIDA HOUSING FINANCE CORPORATION – S.H.I.P. / HURRICANE MICHAEL
S.H.I.P. Administrator Paul requested Board approval and for the Chairman to sign a
letter <copy provided to the Clerk for the record> to be submitted to the Florida Housing
Finance Corporation requesting relief funds, in the amount of $750,000.00 to help rebuild
homes of the very low to low and moderate income families. Commissioner McDaniel so
moved. Commissioner Rogers seconded the motion. Chairman Quinn called for public
comment. There being no public comment, the motion passed unanimously.
WAIVER REQUEST – FINANCIAL REPORT / CONSTITUTIONAL OFFICERS
Upon request by Clerk Norris, on behalf of the Constitutional Officers, Commissioner
McCroan motioned to approve a financial report waiver and extension to November 30,
2018. Commissioner Rich seconded the motion. Chairman Quinn called for public
comment. There being no public comment, the motion passed unanimously.
UPDATE – GULF COUNTY SHERRIFF’S OFFICE / HURRICANE MICHAEL
Sheriff Harrison provided an update to the Board regarding the Sheriff’s Office.
UPDATE – CITY OF PORT ST. JOE / HURRICANE MICHAEL
Port St. Joe City Mayor Patterson appeared before the Board to provide an update
regarding the water and sewer utilities.

BID #1819-02 – DISASTER RECOVERY CONSULTANT / HURRICANE MICHAEL
County Attorney Novak reported that electronic filing (of proposals) will be allowed, per
the F.E.M.A. attorneys, which will be included in the Notice to Receive Sealed Bids (Bid
#1819-02 * Disaster Recovery Consultant).
MEETING – DEPARTMENT OF CORRECTIONS
Commissioner McDaniel motioned for County Attorney Novak to contact the Region I
Director of the Florida Department of Corrections to schedule a meeting with
Commissioner McDaniel, County Attorney Novak, County Administrator Hammond, and
Wewahitchka City Commission. Commissioner Rich seconded the motion. Chairman
Quinn called for public comment. There being no public comment, the motion passed
unanimously.
UPDATE – DISTRICT III / HURRICANE MICHAEL
Commissioners Rogers provided an update regarding District III.
INVENTORY – PROPERTY DAMAGE / HURRICANE MICHAEL
Human Resource Director Lowry appeared before the Board to provide an update
regarding risk management and insurance; reporting that a second insurance adjustor
will be coming. He stated that he has requested that all department heads to compile a
list of items that are damaged, and any structural damage.
DISASTER RECOVERY ASSISTANCE – S.B.A. / HURRICANE MICHAEL
Tamim Choudhury, of U.S. Small Business Administration appeared before the Board to
discuss the S.B.A. and assistance options for residents.
PORTABLE FACILITIES – CAPE SAN BLAS / HURRICANE MICHAEL
Bill McGee, of Cape San Blas appeared before the Board to inquire whether the County
could obtain portable laundry and (bathing) trailers to utilize with a water truck for a
temporary period.
REBUILD/RECOVERY – HURRICANE MICHAEL
Pat Hardman, President of the Coastal Community Association appeared before the
Board to discuss the community getting ‘back online’; thanking County staff.
PORT ST. JOE LIBRARY – HURRICANE MICHAEL
Nancy Brockman, of the Gulf County Public Libraries appeared before the Board to report
that the library in Port St. Joe is currently not open and are waiting on Duke Energy to
restore the electric (power). She stated that she is working on obtaining Wi-Fi hot spots
for public use.
CAPE SAN BLAS – HURRICANE MICHAEL
Mike Beard, Deputy Chief of South Gulf Fire Department appeared before the Board to
encourage the public.

SPECIAL MEETING – OCTOBER 26, 2018
Chairman Quinn reminded everyone of a Special Meeting to be held on Friday (October
26, 2018) at 12:00 p.m., E.T.
There being no further business, and upon motion by Commissioner Rogers, second by
Commissioner Rich, and unanimous vote, the meeting did then adjourn at 10:27 a.m.,
E.T.
SANDY QUINN, JR.
CHAIRMAN
