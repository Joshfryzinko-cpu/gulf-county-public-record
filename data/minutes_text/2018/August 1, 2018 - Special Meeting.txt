AUGUST 1, 2018
SPECIAL MEETING
PORT ST. JOE, FLORIDA
The Gulf County Board of County Commissioners met this date in special session with
the following members present: Chairman Sandy Quinn, Jr., Vice Chairman Jimmy
Rogers, and Commissioners David C. Rich, Ward McDaniel, and Phil McCroan.
Others present were: County Attorney Jeremy Novak, Clerk Rebecca L. Norris, Clerk
Director of Finance & Management Rhonda Woodward, Clerk Budget & Finance Officer
Sherry Herring, Deputy Clerk Leanna Roberts, County Administrator Michael L.
Hammond, Deputy Administrator Kari Summers, Building Official Brad Bailey, Central
Services Director Lynn Lanier, Gulf County E.D.C. Director Warren Yeager, Jr., Gulf
County E.M.S. Director Houston Whitfield, Human Resource Director Brett Lowry,
Assistant Mosquito Control Director Austin Horton, County Planner Lee Collinsworth,
Property Appraiser Mitch Burke, Gulf County Property Appraiser IT Tech Dennis Barfield,
Jr. Public Works & Mosquito Control Director Mark Cothran, Assistant Public Works
Director Raymond Hart, Sheriff Mike Harrison, Supervisor of Elections John Hanlon,
T.D.C. Executive Director Kelli Godwin, and Veterans' Service Officer & S.H.I.P.
Administrator Joe Paul.
Sheriff Harrison called the meeting to order at 10:00 a.m., E.T.
Chairman Quinn led the Pledge of Allegiance to the Flag.
BEACH RESTORATION – ST. JOSEPH PENINSULA
E.D.C. Director Yeager made an announcement that the beach restoration project was
supposed to start mobilizing in the month of August; reporting that he has received an
email from the coastal engineer that Manson (Construction Company * Bid #1718-13), in
the absence of any news on the grant award within the next couple of days, are prepared
to temporary resign the dredge for the Beach Restoration Project to another job. He stated
that the other job is estimated to take 45 (forty-five) days, which will set back the
restoration project 45 (forty-five) to 60 (sixty) days. E.D.C. Director Yeager discussed his
concerns regarding the grant being approved for the U.S. Treasury and the RESTORE
Act portion of these dollars. County Attorney Novak stated for the record that this topic is
relevant to today’s public notice and meeting in discussing the County’s budget. County
Administrator Hammond reported that the County has picked up 22,000 yards of
additional sand due to the State agreeing to pay 100% on the lots owned by FL D.O.T.
(Department of Transportation).

2018-19 BUDGET
Clerk Norris presented the new proposed budget set at a rate of 7.1 mills; stating the Fire
Control Districts are set at .5000 mill each <copy provided to the Clerk for the record>.
County Administrator Hammond reported that the proposed budget is a reduction in the
Millage Rate of .1442 mills, which will bring in $12,345,753.00 in tax money. He requested
Board approval to allow Administration to adjust the numbers in rounding; stating that it
will not change the bottom line, but will change line items. Chairman Quinn called for
comment from the Constitutional Officers.
Sheriff Harrison appeared before the Board to discuss his concerns regarding the
proposed budget cuts involving the fleet exchange program; asking for the Board’s
consideration.
SHERIFF JAG GRANTS
Sheriff Harrison requested permission from the Board to allow the Chairman to sign two
JAG (Justice Assistance Grant) applications, one for the secure access control and the
other for a security camera system for the new Sheriff’s Building and to allow County
Attorney Novak and County Administrator Hammond to sign the necessary documents.
Commissioner McDaniel motioned to allow the Chairman to sign the two (2) JAG (Justice
Assistance Grant) applications for the Sheriff’s Office. Commissioner Rich seconded the
motion. Chairman Quinn called for public comment. There being no public comment, the
motion passed unanimously.
2018-19 BUDGET
Chairman Quinn called for Henry Flack, Gulf County Senior Citizens Executive Director
to appear before the Board <public speaking form not provided to the Clerk for the
record>. Lynn Lanier, President Pro-tim of the Gulf County Senior Citizens Associations
appeared before the Board to discuss the budget request for the Senior Citizens Program
<copy provided to the Clerk for the record>; introducing three (3) new members to the
board, Gina Tindsley and Loretta Watkins, as well as herself. She discussed the handout
provided regarding the program and financial needs and various fundraisers scheduled
for the upcoming year. Mr. Flack appeared before the Board to thank them for their
support. Commissioner McCroan motioned to extend Mr. Flack’s time to speak.
Commissioner Rogers seconded the motion, and it passed unanimously. Mr. Flack
discussed the services provided through the Gulf County Senior Citizens and thanked the
Board for their past support; requesting that the Board pass the increase request, in the
amount of $16,265.00. Chairman Quinn called for public comment. There were no
additional public comment. County Administrator Hammond stated that if the Board is
going to make changes, now is the time; recommending, if no changes are to be made,
the Board to adopt the 7.10 cap. Commissioner McDaniel motioned to set the Millage
Rate at 7.10. Commissioner Rich seconded the motion. After discussion by Chairman
Quinn, he then called for public comment. There being no public comment, the motion
passed unanimously. Following discussion by County Administrator Hammond and Clerk
Norris, Commissioner McCroan motioned to approve .5000 mill each for the Fire Control
Districts. Commissioner Rogers seconded the motion. Chairman Quinn called for public
comment. There being no public comment, the motion passed unanimously. Upon

discussion by County Administrator Hammond, Commissioner McDaniel motioned to set
the MSTU Gulfside Beachfront at 1.1935 mills. Commissioner McCroan seconded the
motion. Chairman Quinn called for public comment. There being no public comment, the
motion passed unanimously. After discussion by County Administrator Hammond,
Commissioner McDaniel motioned to set the MSTU Gulfside Interior at 0.9838 mills.
Commissioner McCroan seconded the motion. Chairman Quinn called for public
comment. There being no public comment, the motion passed unanimously. After
discussion by County Administrator Hammond, Commissioner McDaniel motioned to set
the MSTU Bayside at 0.9894 mills. Commissioner McCroan seconded the motion.
Chairman Quinn called for public comment. There being no public comment, the motion
passed unanimously. Upon discussion by Clerk Norris, Commissioner McCroan motioned
to hold the first public hearing on September 4th and the second/final public hearing on
September 20th both to be held at 5:01 p.m., E.T. Commissioner Rogers seconded the
motion. Chairman Quinn called for public comment. There being no public comment, the
motion passed unanimously. County Administrator Hammond noted for the September
meetings, staff will have to have dollar-for-dollar cuts for any additions. Upon request by
Clerk Norris, Commissioner Rich motioned to allow staff to round (the numbers).
Commissioner Rogers seconded the motion. Chairman Quinn called for public comment.
There being no public comment, the motion passed unanimously.
There being no further business, and upon motion by Commissioner McDaniel, second
by Commissioner Rich, and unanimous vote, the meeting did then adjourn at 10:24 a.m.,
E.T.
