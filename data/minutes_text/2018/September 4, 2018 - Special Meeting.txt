SEPTEMBER 4, 2018
SPECIAL MEETING
PORT ST. JOE, FLORIDA
The Gulf County Board of County Commissioner met this date in special session with the
following members present: Chairman Sandy Quinn, Jr., Vice Chairman Jimmy Rogers,
and Commissioners David C. Rich, Ward McDaniel, and Phil McCroan.
Others present were: County Attorney Jeremy Novak, Clerk Budget & Finance Officer
Sherry Herring, Deputy Clerk Leanna Roberts, County Administrator Michael L.
Hammond, Deputy Administrator Kari Summers, Building Official Brad Bailey, Gulf
County E.D.C. Director Warren Yeager, Jr., Assistant Mosquito Control Director Austin
Horton, County Planner Lee Collinsworth, Public Works & Mosquito Control Director Mark
Cothran, and Veterans' Service Officer & S.H.I.P. Administrator Joe Paul.
Chairman Quinn called the meeting to order at 4:50 p.m., E.T.
Chairman Quinn led in the Pledge of Allegiance to the Flag.
CULVERT – CHARLES AVENUE
Public Works Director Cothran appeared before the Board to report that there was a
massive culvert failure on Charles Avenue in White City last week. He stated that Public
Works temporary patched the seam and replaced the dirt. He discussed his concerns
regarding the water, gas, sewer, and fiber optic lines that run across this ditch; stating if
the County loses the road, all these utilities will be exposed and the water and sewer lines
will break. He stated that due to recent rainfall, the culvert is still compromised; reporting
that the patch did not work and additional dirt has been hauled in. Public Works Director
Cothran discussed the magnitude of this project; reporting that by the end of the week he
should have some price options for pipe material. He stated that it is his recommendation
that the County do something. Commissioner Rogers reported that this ditch was dug by
the State to drain U.S. Highway 71; stating he asked staff to contact the State for possible
help with the pipe. County Administrator Hammond reported that everything from Doc
Whitfield (Road) to the Canal runs through this ditch; stating the road will be closed 3-4
(three to four) more weeks. He requested permission from the Board to allow him to use
the remaining funds from District III Road Paving Bond, in the amount of $60,000.00, and
up to $75,000.00 out of Secondary Road and Bridge to fix this problem <Commissioner
McDaniel left the meeting at 4:56 p.m., E.T.> <Commissioner McDaniel returned to the
meeting at 4:56 p.m., E.T.>. Commissioner Rich made the motion. Commissioner
McCroan seconded the motion for discussion. After discussion, Chairman Quinn called
for public comment. There being no public comment, the motion passed unanimously.

There being no further business, and upon motion by Commissioner McDaniel, second
by Commissioner McCroan, and unanimous vote, the meeting did then adjourn at 4:59
p.m., E.T.
