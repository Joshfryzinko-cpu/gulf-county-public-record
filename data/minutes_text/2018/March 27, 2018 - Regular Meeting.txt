LAND ACQUISITION – 418 CECIL G. COSTIN, SR BLVD. / BANK BUILDING
County Attorney Novak reported that the County was notified by the U.S. Treasury that
under the MYIP (Multi-Year Implantation Plan) the County will need to go out for additional
comment period on another project (Beach Restoration Project) and as a result, the
funding date for the MYIP and use of the RESTORE funds will be pushed back. He stated
that the sellers have been notified of this delay. County Attorney Novak requested a
motion of the Board to allow County Administrator Designate Hammond, County Attorney
Novak, and E.D.C. Director/RESTORE Act Coordinator Yeager to work with the sellers
to negotiate a short-term lease on the facility and allow the Chairman to sign an amended
contract and lease. He reported that this will require the County to put additional earnest
money down, beyond the initial $10,000.00. Commissioner McDaniel made the motion,
and Commissioner McCroan seconded it. Chairman Quinn called for public comment.
There being no public comment, the motion passed unanimously.

VEHICLE BEACH ACCESS – CAPE SAN BLAS / INDIAN PASS
County Attorney Novak reported that Commissioner McCroan received correspondence
regarding vehicle beach access from Higgins Street and Oak Street; stating that all
throughout McNeil Subdivision there have been vehicle access points along that beach.
He reported that the Board held an emergency meeting back in 2015 and discussed
access points being unsafe due to erosion and these access points would be monitored.
County Attorney Novak stated that since 2015 the access point has grown and is now
safe to use; reporting the County placed beach access signs and removed the barricades.
He clarified for the record that they are all open and have been since 1953; stating that it
would take a vote of the Board to officially close down any of those vehicle access points.

FLOOD ZONE – DISTRICT III
Commissioner Rogers discussed his concerns regarding flood insurance in District III;
reporting there was a promise to be removed from the high price on flood insurance. He
requested that County Attorney Novak or Assistant Administrator Hammond review this

matter to see what can be done to get this process on the way. Assistant Administrator
Hammond reported that a good portion of White City is coming out (X Zone) under the
proposed map and a good number of Zone A in Overstreet is going away. Human
Resource/Risk Management Director Lowry reported that staff is waiting on new data to
be submitted regarding elevation changes within the County. He stated with direction from
the Board, staff can speed up the process and make Northwest Florida Water
Management Board aware of the County’s standing. Commissioner Rogers motioned for
staff to contact the Northwest Florida Water Management Board requesting that they
move forward with the new data received. Commissioner McDaniel seconded the motion.
Commissioner McDaniel requested an explanation for the public on the different zoning
types. Human Resource/Risk Management Director Lowry discussed the different types
of zones and which ones require flood insurance. Chairman Quinn called for public
comment. Pat Hardman, Chairman of Gulf County FEMA Committee appeared before the
Board to discuss her concerns regarding the data and analysis; stating that anything the
Board can do to speed this up would be appreciated. Chairman Quinn called for an
additional public comment. There being no further public comment, the motion then
passed unanimously. Assistant Administrator Hammond reported that he will direct staff
to draft a letter for the Chairman to sign to submit to the Northwest Florida Water
Management District.
ROAD PAVING – SOUTH DUCK AVENUE
Upon discussion by Commissioner Rich, Public Works Director Cothran appeared before
the Board to discuss paving South Duck Avenue in Howard Creek from Doc Whitfield
Road to Bass Street; reporting that it could possibly be paved for under $30,000.00.
Commissioner Rich motioned to pave this road (South Duck Avenue). Public Works
Director Cothran reported that Public Works will do the clay base and a contractor will do
the asphalt portion. After discussion by Commissioner McDaniel, Assistant Administrator
Hammond stated that if Commissioner Rich wants to form his motion to pay it out of
Secondary Road & Bridge, then staff will do a change order to the current Road Bond.
Discussion followed. Commissioner Rich then motioned to pave this road (South Duck
Avenue) out of Secondary Road & Bridge money. Commissioner McDaniel seconded the
motion. Chairman Quinn called for public comment. There being no public comment, the
motion passed unanimously.
