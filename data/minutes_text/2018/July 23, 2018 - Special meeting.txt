JULY 23, 2018
SPECIAL MEETING
PORT ST. JOE, FLORIDA

following members present: Chairman Sandy Quinn, Jr., Vice Chairman Jimmy Rogers,
Commissioners David C. Rich, Ward McDaniel, and Phil McCroan.
Others present were: County Attorney Jeremy Novak, Clerk Rebecca L. Norris, Clerk
Director of Finance & Management Rhonda Woodward, Clerk Budget & Finance Officer
Sherry Herring, Deputy Clerk Leanna Roberts, County Administrator Michael L.
Hammond, Deputy Administrator Kari Summers, Building Official Brad Bailey, Emergency
Management Director Marshall Nelson, E9-1-1 Coordinator Ben Guthrie, Gulf County
E.M.S. Director Houston Whitfield, G.I.S. Coordinator Jessica Susich, Information
Technology Dennis Barfield, Sr., Assistant Mosquito Control Director Austin Horton,
County Planner Lee Collinsworth, Public Works & Mosquito Control Director Mark
Cothran, Property Appraiser Office IT Tech Dennis Barfield, Jr., Property Appraiser Office
Staff Samantha Naylor, Sheriff's Department Captain Chris Buchanan, Supervisor of
Elections John Hanlon, Tax Collector Shirley Jenkins, T.D.C. Executive Director Kelli
Godwin, and Veterans' Service Officer & S.H.I.P. Administrator Joe Paul.
Sheriff’s Department Captain Buchanan called the meeting to order at 10:00 a.m., E.T.
Chairman Quinn led the Pledge of Allegiance to the Flag.
BUDGET
County Administrator Hammond reported that today’s meeting is the first budget meeting
(for the upcoming fiscal year); stating that Staff have been working on the proposed
budget since March, 2018. Clerk Norris stated that members of the Board were provided
an initial proposed budget on July 17th; reporting it represents the initial requests and/or
obligations of the County agencies, departments, and Constitutional Officers. She noted
that outside agencies have been included at the FY2018-19 budget proposal but do not
reflect any requested increases; reporting that a list has been provided to the Board of
these agencies requests <copy provided to the Clerk for the record>. Clerk Norris
reported that the initial budget stands at a proposed millage rate of 7.4921 as compared
to the current year of 7.2442 mils. She stated that a portion of the increase can be
contributed to the final adoption of last year’s budget; reporting that when the final taxable
values came in they had a reduction resulting in approximately $115,000.00 loss in the
proposed ad valorem tax assessment. After discussion by Clerk Norris, County
Administrator Hammond suggested the Board start with the Constitutional Officers.

Property Appraiser’s Office IT Tech Barfield, Jr. appeared before the Board to request a
3% increase for pay, $19,750.00 for a new server; noting the cost will be split with the
Tax Collector’s Office, and funds for a new GIS position to cross-train for the replacement
of an employee that will be retiring.
Supervisor of Elections Hanlon appeared before the Board to request an increase of
$23,143.00. He noted that $15,000.00 of this is for cyber security and the remainder is
for maintenance and equipment for the new precinct (in Highland View).
Tax Collector Jenkins appeared before the Board to state that she has a part-time
employee that will be transitioning to full-time employment who will work between T.D.C.
and property taxes; requesting a $20,000.00 increase for this change, a 3% pay increase,
and funds ($19,750.00) for the new server that will be shared with the Property Appraiser.
Sheriff’s Department Captain Buchanan appeared before the Board to request funds for
an IT services for Sheriff’s operations, in the amount of $79,956.00. He reported that the
Sheriff’s Office will be contracting with an IT company. Sheriff’s Department Captain
Buchanan stated the other increase is for the safe schools program that is contracted
through the Gulf County School Board, in the amount of $222,759.00 that will provide the
additional school resource officers. County Administrator Hammond stated that the actual
change to the budget request is an approximate increase of $280,000.00 based on the
outside contracts with the School Board and the City of Port St. Joe. Clerk Budget &
Finance Officer Herring discussed the increases by line items within the Sheriff’s
proposed budget.
Clerk Norris reported that she does not have an increase for this year; noting that one line
item shifted from the County’s budget to the Clerk’s, in the amount of $6,000.00 (Rentals
& Leases).
County Administrator Hammond stated that in order to get back to the current millage
rate, the Board would need to cut $409,000.00 to get back to the 7.2442. He stated that
the proposed budget includes a 3% across the board pay increase for the Constitutional
Officers and County employees, a new building inspector and code enforcement officer,
and a new officer of management and budget. County Administrator Hammond went
through each line item of the County’s budget and outside agencies.
Commissioner McDaniel motioned to have the Administrator and Budget Committee to
bring a balanced budget back to the Board at the lowest possible millage rate.
Commissioner McCroan seconded the motion for discussion. After discussion, the Board
agreed to allow the Administrator to work towards a 7.10 millage rate. Chairman Quinn
called for public comment. There being no public comment, the motion passed
unanimously.
County Administrator Hammond advised the Board that now is the time if there is anything
a Commissioner wants to add. Commissioner Rogers mentioned a request for funds for
the Veterans’ Memorial Beacon Hill Park from Fund 123, in the amount of $50,000.00.

County Administrator Hammond stated at the direction of the Board, staff will include it in
the proposed budget. After discussion, Chairman Quinn discussed a request from the
Historical Society. County Administrator Hammond reported that he has received an
email from the Charlotte Pierce (Historical Society) this morning, but this change can be
made at the next meeting as it will not affect the ad valorem side. He stated that the Board
will need to set some dates for the next hearing. After discussion, the Chairman called for
recess.
The meeting did then recess at 10:39 a.m., E.T.
The meeting did reconvene at 10:43 a.m., E.T.
After discussion, Commissioner Rogers motioned to set a special tentative budget
meeting on August 1st at 10:00 a.m., E.T., the first Public Hearing on September 4th and
the final Public Hearing on September 20th, both to be held at 5:01 p.m., E.T.
Commissioner Rich seconded the motion. Chairman Quinn called for public comment.
There being no public comment, the motion passed unanimously.
There being no further business, and upon motion by Commissioner McDaniel, second
by Commissioner Rogers, and unanimous vote, the meeting did then adjourn at 10:46
a.m., E.T.
