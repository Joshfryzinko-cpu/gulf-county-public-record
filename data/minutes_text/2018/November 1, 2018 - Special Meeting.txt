NOVEMBER 1, 2018
JOINT SPECIAL MEETING
WARD RIDGE BUILDING, PORT ST. JOE, FLORIDA
The Gulf County Board of County Commissioners met this day in a Joint Special Meeting
with the City of Port St. Joe at the Ward Ridge Building with the following members
present: Chairman Sandy Quinn, Jr., Vice Chairman Jimmy Rogers, and Commissioners
Ward McDaniel and Phil McCroan <Commissioner David C. Rich arrived at 5:03 p.m.,
E.T.>.
Others present were: Deputy Clerk Leanna Roberts, County Administrator Michael L.
Hammond, County Assistant Administrator Warren Yeager, Jr., Gulf County E.D.C.
Director Jim McKnight, Staff Assistant – E.D.C. Johanna White, Emergency Management
Director Marshall Nelson, Emergency Management Staff Assistant Rachel Jackson,
Dewberry/County Engineer Clay Smallwood, III, Office of Management & Budget Director
Sherry Herring, County Planner Lee Collinsworth, Public Works & Mosquito Control
Sheriff Mike Harrison, and T.D.C. Executive Director Kelli Godwin.
City Mayor Bo Patterson called the meeting to order at 4:58 p.m., E.T.
City Mayor Patterson led in the Pledge of Allegiance to the Flag and County
Commissioner Chairman Quinn opened the meeting with prayer.
HURRICANE MICHAEL JOINT EFFORTS
City Manager Jim Anderson discussed his concerns regarding the City of Port St. Joe’s
Building Department regarding damage assessments due to effects of Hurricane Michael.
City Building Inspector Bo Creel appeared before the City Board to provide a status report
regarding the City Building Department. He encouraged residents to file a claim with
F.E.M.A. (Federal Emergency Management Agency). City Building Inspector Creel
discussed the color-coded cards being issued to residents within the City of Port St. Joe.
<Commissioner Rich entered the meeting at 5:03 p.m., E.T.> Upon inquiry by City Mayor
Patterson, City Building Inspector Creel reported there are currently three (3) inspectors
working Gulf County but he is waiting on a Notice to Proceed from the City of Port St. Joe
in order to submit to F.E.M.A for reimbursement prior to bringing five (5) additional
inspectors in. Following discussion regarding staffing, City Manager Anderson expressed
his concerns regarding getting permits issued and getting residents back in their homes.
Following discussion, City Commissioner Scott Hoffman stated that he would like one (1)
licensed inspector in the City Building Department during operational business hours to
answer questions and assist with the issuance of permits; requesting that there be five
(5) licensed inspectors working in the City of Port St. Joe daily, with the understanding
that if the County needs assistance these inspectors can be pulled, plus the one (1)
building inspector stationed in the office. He recommended that the City of Port St. Joe
work with the County. After discussion by members of the City Commission, City Building

Inspector Creel discussed sending over a letter to the County requesting an interlocal
agreement. County Commissioner Chairman Quinn stated that the County is here to
assist the City. City Mayor Patterson called for public comment. There was no public
comment. Emergency Management Director Nelson appeared before the City Board to
discuss working on a disaster housing program. Jason Shoaf, of St. Joe Natural Gas
appeared before the City Board to encourage the public to call the utility companies before
they dig, demolish or grind trees. Roni Coppock, of Port St. Joe Beach appeared before
the City Board to discuss her concerns regarding the need to get information out to the
communities. City Mayor Patterson called for public comment. There was no public
comment.
There being no further business, City Mayor Patterson adjourned the meeting at 5:30
p.m., E.T.
