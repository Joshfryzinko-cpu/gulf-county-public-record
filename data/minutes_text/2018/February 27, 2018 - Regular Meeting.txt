EASTERN SHIPBUILDING – FLOATING DRY DOCK PROJECT
William Harrison, of Panama City appeared before the Board to give a presentation to the
Board regarding Eastern Shipbuilding and the Floating Dry Dock Project <copy was not

ANIMAL CONTROL – ENFORCEMENT ON CAPE SAN BLAS
Dwayne Piergiovanni, of Cone Heads appeared before the Board to give a presentation
regarding animal control enforcement on Cape San Blas <copy not provided to the Clerk
for the record>. He discussed the beach, amount of visitors, and the Animal Control
Ordinance; reporting that there are many complaints about unleashed dogs on the beach
and waste being left behind. Commissioner McCroan motioned to extend Mr.

Piergiovanni’s time to speak. Commissioner Rich seconded the motion. Mr. Piergiovanni
discussed his concerns regarding pet waste being damaging to the beach. He discussed
possible solutions: 1. Use some of the bed tax to fund code enforcement; 2. Charge
visitors to the beach a dog tag to fund code enforcement; 3. Designate only certain areas
of the beach that are pet-friendly; or 4. Set time limits in which dogs can be on the beach.
Mr. Piergiovanni closed stating hopefully this presentation will implement some measures
with enforcement that will help with the matter of unleashed dogs on the beach. After
discussion by Chairman Quinn, Commissioner McCroan reported that the County has a
leash law; stating that the majority of the people abide by law. He stated that the County
only has one (1) animal control officer and there is always going to be a percentage of
people that are not going to abide by the law, but majority of the people do the right thing.
Commissioner McDaniel reported that he spoke with Parks and Recreation Director
Traylor regarding this issue; stating that the majority are well mannered. Commissioner
Rich thanked Mr. Piergiovanni for this presentation and encourage visitors to pick up their
loved one’s waste. After discussion by Assistant Administration Hammond, Interim T.D.C.
Executive Director Godwin appeared before the Board to discuss the matters of pets on
the beach; reporting that people traveling with pets are a huge part of T.D.C.’s market.

PROTEST BID #1718-08 – CONSULTING & ENGINEERING INSPECTION
OVERSIGHT SERVICES / FLOATING DRY DOCK PROJECT
County Attorney Novak provided a historical background regarding the floating dry dock
project. He reported that on February 1st the County received a response and intent to
protest Bid #1718-08 and #1718-09 from Technology Associations, Inc., followed by a
formal bid protest on February 10th. County Attorney Novak stated that staff held a
debriefing with the vendor to discuss the bids. He reported that the County does not have
a bid protest policy and Technology Associates has requested to speak to the Board,
which has been placed on today’s Agenda. County Attorney Novak provided an outline
of a packet provided to the Board for review <copy provided to the Clerk for the record>;
reporting that the formal bid protest is for Bid #1718-08 (Consulting and Engineering
Inspection Oversight Services). He called attention to the section Request for Relief of
the Vendor from the bidder’s protest letter. County Attorney Novak stated for the record
that the protestor specifically requested a ruling by Gulf County Board of County
Commissioners; reporting that in light of this request the Chairman and Administrator
placed it on today’s Agenda. He stated that the County does not have a policy, but the
Board does have a public speaking ordinance and it is placed on the Agenda;
recommending there are some guidelines from the State Statute under F.S. 120,
Administrative Procedure Act, as well as standing case law giving guidance in how to
proceed. After further discussion by County Attorney Novak, Chief Administrator Butler
provided a handout to the Board for review regarding the evaluation of Bid #1718-09
<copy provided to the Clerk for the record>. He then went over the evaluation scoresheet
in detail. After discussion by Commissioner McDaniel and Chief Administrator Butler,
County Attorney Novak stated again for the record there is no process, no policy or
procedure in which the Board is to follow, and per the request of the vendor (Technology
Associates, Inc.) the Board has afforded them time today to appear before the Board.
Miles Thomas, of Technology Associates, Inc. appeared before the Board to discuss the
bid specifications and bids submitted for consulting and engineering inspection oversight
services for the Floating Dry Dock Project. He requested permission to go through their
presentation that they have brought to present to the Board. Mr. Thomas discussed
Dewberry’s project history; stating that Dewberry has not designed a floating structure.
He requested permission to go over Dewberry’s proposal and to go through their
presentation. Commissioner Rogers motioned to allow Mr. Thomas additional time to
speak. Commissioner McCroan seconded the motion, and it passed unanimously. Mr.
Thomas discussed the Technology Associates, Inc. staff and their years of experience.
He then began going over the slides in his presentation; <copy provided to the Clerk for
the record> discussing project experience and understanding of the project.
Commissioner McCroan motioned to allow additional time to speak. Commissioner
Rogers seconded the motion, and it passed unanimously. Mr. Thomas continued with his
presentation; discussing understanding oversight, Dewberry's proposal, and Technology
Associates’ approach to this project. Commissioner Rogers motioned to allow additional
time to speak. Commissioner McCroan seconded the motion, and it passed unanimously.
Mr. Thomas stated that Technology Associates clearly understands the project and has
a clear and precise project approach; reporting that based on the information provided to
Technology Associates, Dewberry has no experience with floating dry docks. He
discussed Technology Associates’ staff experience and Dewberry’s background work;

stating that Technology Associates has more project experience. Commissioner Rich
motioned to allow additional time to speak. Commissioner Rogers seconded the motion,
and it passed unanimously. Mr. Thomas continued with his presentation; continuing to
compare the work experience between both bidders. Commissioner McCroan motioned
to allow additional time to speak. Commissioner Rich seconded the motion, and it passed
unanimously. Mr. Thomas continued to discuss the compliance with the bid; stating that
the County needs someone to ask the questions and hold Eastern Shipbuilding
accountable. He compared work experience between the two (2) bidders. Chairman
Quinn called for a motion to extend time. There was no motion. County Attorney Novak
inquired if the Board had any questions regarding Mr. Thomas’ presentation or the papers
received. Chairman Quinn called for questions from the members of the Board. There
were no questions from the Board members. County Attorney Novak stated that the
County does not have a policy and that staff is at the will of the Board. He reported for
the record that the County received a protest for both Dewberry (Bid #1718-08) and
Herger (Bid #1718-09) bids, the County acknowledged and confirmed it with Technology
Associates within seven days after, the County received a formal protest regarding the
Dewberry contract at which time the County ceased our efforts to secure. County Attorney
Novak reported that Technology Associates has requested a public hearing for the
County’s re-evaluation of their vote, which was done today as well as completing their
public records request and debriefing. He reported that the public hearing was properly
noticed and the vendor was invited to attend to make a presentation and provide the
Board information. County Attorney Novak stated the paths to follow is: 1. hire an
independent third party to evaluate the bid proposals and bring a recommendation back
before the Board; 2. Start the bid process over; 3. Accept the reevaluation, award, and
direct staff to engage Technology Associates, Inc. to negotiate a contract; 4. Reaffirm the
Board’s prior vote accepting Chief Administrator Butler’s recommendation; basing your
vote on the analysis and information received today. Commissioner McDaniel motioned
to accept Chief Administrator’s recommendation and move forward with Dewberry.
Commissioner Rich seconded the motion. Chairman Quinn called for public comment.
There being no public comment, the motion passed unanimously.
