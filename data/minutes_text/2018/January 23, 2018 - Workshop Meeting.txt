Sherry Herring, Deputy Clerk Leanna Roberts, Chief Administrator Don Butler, Assistant

Chairman Quinn called the meeting to order at 8:15 a.m., E.T.
BEACH RESTORATION REPORT
Chief Administrator Butler announced that the County’s Coastal Engineer is present to
discuss options to renourish the beach at Cape San Blas; reporting the County is short
on funds to do what they want to do. Michael Dombrowski, of MRD Associates (the
County’s Coastal Engineer) appeared before the Board to provide a presentation and
recommendation regarding the Beach Restoration Project. He discussed possible volume
changes, proposed beach fill, and relocation of some homes inward. Mr. Dombrowski
reported that renourishment is not needed North of Rish Park as it is performing as
originally designed; stating that beach and dune erosion is present South of Rish Park.
Mr. Dombrowski made the following recommendation to the Board: 1. Remove North
Beach Fill and repair “hot-spots” by truck haul; 2. Construct the South Beach Fill “Feeder
Beach” by dredging offshore sand; 3. Re-bid this project at the available budget of $10.5
million; 4. Cape Shoals Relocation; 5. Advertise to receive sealed bids between March-
April ($16,400.00 – Re-bidding Service & $27,344.00 – Surveys); 6. Award the bid in May;
7. Construction project between June and November; and Seventy-five days versus one
hundred-twenty days of construction. Upon inquiry by Commissioner McCroan, Mr.
Dombrowski stated that the surveyor thinks he can start the end of this month and then
be able to go out for bids in early March. After discussion, Commissioner McCroan
discussed the budget for this project and asked if Mr. Dombrowski’s recommendation is
to do a reverse bid. Mr. Dombrowski agreed. Chairman Quinn called for public comment.
Pat Hardman, President of Coastal Community Association appeared before the Board
to thank staff for their efforts; discussing the State’s budget and the new approach to
advertising for bids. She requested the Board move forward with the reverse bid and look
at acquiring beach access to add another $250,000.00 worth of sand. After discussion by

Assistant Administrator Hammond, Chairman Quinn called for public comment. There
was no additional public comment.
There being no further business, and upon motion by Commissioner McCroan, second
by Commissioner Ward, the meeting did then adjourn at 8:48 a.m., E.T.
