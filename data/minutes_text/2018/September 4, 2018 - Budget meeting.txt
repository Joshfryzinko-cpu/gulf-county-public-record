TENTATIVE 2018-19 OVERVIEW
Clerk Budget & Finance Officer Herring addressed the budget highlights as follows: 1)
the aggregate proposed millage rate is 7.4947, which is 3.66% above the current year
aggregate rolled back rate of 7.2303; 2) the county-wide proposed tentative millage rate
is 7.1000; 3) the Dependent Fire Districts; St. Joseph, Tupelo, Overstreet, and Howard
Creek are all at 0.5000; and 4) the proposed MSTU Debt Service millage rates are:
Gulfside Beachfront at 1.1395, Gulfside Interior at 0.9838 and Bayside at 0.9894. Ms.
Herring reported that some reasons for the increases of 3.66% over the rolled back rate
are: a) Sheriff’s Office, b) Building and Planning Departments, c) Office of Management
& Budget and other departmental transitions, d) cost of living pay increases e) Property
Appraiser’s Office f) Elected Officials pay increases, g) Property and Causality Insurance
Premiums, and h) Retirement Rates. She noted that the stated millage rates are the
maximum; the Board may adopt a lesser rate at tonight’s Hearing with a majority vote.
Ms. Herring advised the Board of the Port St. Joe DRA obligation in the amount of
$183,078.00, based on the proposed millage rate. Additionally, Clerk Budget & Finance
Officer Herring stated that the General Fund Cash Carry Forward Budget amount is
$7,089,079 as balance brought forward-cash which has been budgeted as a source of
funds in the Fiscal Year 2019-20 Budget (restricted is $2,509,023 of Cash Carry Forward
and is not available for general operations). Ms. Herring noted that the proposed tentative
budget was provided to each Commissioner and posted on the County’s website, as
required for this Hearing. She reported that the budget includes Non-Ad Valorem
adjustments from the prior August 1, 2018 proposal, as follows: 1) to provide rounded

totals; 2) to provide coding and allocation corrections on identified items; and 3) to provide
revised cash carried forward estimates on identified items. Clerk Budget & Finance Officer
Herring stated that the Board may amend the budget, but any increases in expenditures
will require equivalent reductions specified by line item. After discussion by Clerk Budget
& Finance Officer Herring, Commissioner Rogers motioned to remove the $2.8 million
grant in Fund 119 (BP & RESTORE Act Fund) from the tentative budget. Commissioner
McDaniel seconded the motion. Chairman Quinn called for public comment. There being
no public comment, the motion passed unanimously. County Administrator Hammond
requested that the Chairman call a special meeting within the next ten (10) days to decide
a path forward for the Beach Renourishment Project (Bid #1718-13). After discussion,
Chairman Quinn called for public comment. Jeff Harvey, of Port St. Joe appeared before
the Board to discuss his support of the Port St. Joe Library; emphasizing the need to
nurture the Library. Chairman Quinn called for public comment. Nancy Brockman, of the
Port St. Joe Library appeared before the Board to provide information and a newsletter
for the Board to review <copy provided to the Clerk for the record>. She discussed various
services provided through the library. Commissioner McDaniel stated that the Board did
not touch the Library’s or Senior Citizen’s budget this year. Chairman Quinn called for
public comment. There being no further public comment, Chairman Quinn then closed
the time for public comment.
