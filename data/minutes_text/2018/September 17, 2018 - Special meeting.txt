SEPTEMBER 17, 2018
PORT ST. JOE, FLORIDA
SPECIAL MEETING
The Gulf County Board of County Commissioner met this date in special session with the
following members present: Chairman Sandy Quinn, Jr., Vice Chairman Jimmy Rogers,
and Commissioners David C. Rich, Ward McDaniel, and Phil McCroan.
Others present were: County Attorney Jeremy Novak, Clerk Rebecca L. Norris, Clerk
Budget & Finance Officer Sherry Herring, Deputy Clerk Leanna Roberts, County
Administrator Michael L. Hammond, Deputy Administrator Kari Summers, Building Official
Brad Bailey, Central Services Director Lynn Lanier, Gulf County E.D.C. Director Warren
Yeager, Jr., Gulf County E.D.C. Staff Assistant Lianna Sagins, G.I.S. Coordinator Jessica
Susich, Assistant Mosquito Control Director Austin Horton, County Planner Lee
Collinsworth, Dewberry/County Engineer Clay Smallwood, III, Public Works & Mosquito
Control Director Mark Cothran, Assistant Public Works Director Raymond Hart, Sheriff
Mike Harrison, T.D.C. Executive Director Kelli Godwin, and Veterans' Service Officer &

Sheriff Harrison called the meeting to order at 4:00 p.m., E.T.
Chairman Quinn opened the meeting with prayer and led the Pledge of Allegiance to the
Flag.
BEACH RENOURISHMENT PROJECT
E.D.C. Director Yeager discussed the Beach Renourishment Project; reporting that U.S.
Fish and Wildlife now concur with U.S. Treasury that this project is an exception to
C.B.R.A. (Coastal Barrier Resources Act). He stated that the County still has not received
grant approval from U.S. Treasury; advising that this is the last day to make a decision in
how to move forward with this project. After discussion, E.D.C. Director Yeager
recommended the Board to move forward with this project; executing a contract, and the
Notice to Proceed with the understating that the Board will give staff the ability, if the
money comes in from U.S. Treasury, to proceed with a change order for the $2.8 million.
After further discussion, Commissioner McCroan asked for E.D.C. Director Yeager’s
recommendation. E.D.C. Director Yeager stated his recommendation would be to
proceed with this project. Upon inquiry by Chairman Quinn, E.D.C. Director Yeager stated
staff would need a motion giving the Chairman authorization to sign the contract and the
Notice to Proceed, and to give staff the ability to do the change order once U.S. Treasury
approves the funds for this project. Commissioner McCroan motioned to proceed.
Commissioner McDaniel seconded the motion. After discussion, Chairman Quinn called
for public comment. Robyn Rennick was called to appear <public speaking form
submitted>. Mrs. Rennick stated from the audience that she will waive in support of staff’s
recommendation. Pat Hardman, President of Coastal Community Association appeared

before the Board to discuss the work by staff regarding this project; stating that if the
County does not move forward, then homes and sand will be lost. After discussion by
Commissioner McDaniel, Jim White was called to appear. After discussion by E.D.C.
Director Yeager., Mr. White, of Cape San Blas appeared before the Board to thank the
Board for their efforts regarding this project. Chairman Quinn called for public comment.
There being no further public comment, the motion passed unanimously.

REJECT BID #1718-23 – CEI DAVID B. LANGSTON DRIVE SIDEWALK
County Administrator Hammond reported that D.O.T. (Department of Transportation)
would not issue a waiver regarding Bid #1718-23, therefore this bid will need to be

F.E.M.A. FLOOD MAP REVISIONS
Planner Collinsworth reported that Northwest Florida Water Management District found a
few discrepancies in the preliminary maps; discussing an information packet provided to
the Board for their review <copy provided to the Clerk for the record>. He recommended
the Board move forward with what they have and should the N.W.F.W.M.D. find the funds,
then they can address these issues (with the discrepancy parcels). County Administrator
Hammond reported that this started in 1994; reporting that in 2016 F.E.M.A. notified the
County that they were going to take out approximately 900 lots in unincorporated Gulf
County, mainly White City, Overstreet, and St. Joe Beach. He stated that the County

needs to move this process forward. After discussion by Planner Collinsworth, Bo Spring,
of N.W.F.W.M.D. appeared before the Board to discuss the preliminary findings from the
LiDAR analysis; reporting that N.W.F.W.M.D. would like to meet with the Board and
present information. Upon inquiry by Commissioner McCroan regarding the process of
getting the 900 (parcels) out but also helping those going into a flood zone, Mr. Spring
stated that N.W.F.W.M.D. is working on that. Following discussion, Mr. Spring requested
a sit-down before the Board prior to taking any action. After discussion by County
Administrator Hammond, Pat Hardman, Chairman of the F.E.M.A. Committee appeared
before Board and discussed obtaining a group LOMA to help; stating that very little is
going to change on the Cape. She discussed her concerns with the County using the old
data with the possibility of property owners challenging the old data. Ms. Hardman
recommended that the Board let N.W.F.W.M.D. come and explain to the Board what the
numbers are and how they really work. Upon inquiry by Commissioner McCroan, Mr.
Spring reported that they have all the 2017 data but presently do not have the funding in
place to run the models. He stated that N.W.F.W.M.D. is moving forward with remodeling
the entire County. Upon inquiry by Commissioner McCroan regarding how N.W.F.W.M.D.
could do both, Mr. Spring reported one would be doing a mass LOMA project, which could
be done much faster. County Administrator Hammond reported that the LOMA would
remain in place until the next map. At County Administrator Hammond suggestion,
Commissioner McCroan motioned to hold a Special Meeting on September 25th at 8:00
a.m., E.T. Commissioner Rich seconded the motion. Chairman Quinn called for public
comment. Robyn Rennick was called to appear <public speaking form submitted>. Mrs.
Rennick stated from the audience that she will waive her time to speak. There being no
public comment, the motion passed unanimously.
AWARD BID #1718-28 – PURCHASE ADULTICIDES
Assistant Mosquito Control Director Horton appeared before the Board to recommend
they award Bid #1718-28 for the purchase of adulticides for Mosquito Control to the low
bidder, Univar. Commissioner McDaniel then made the motion. Commissioner Rogers
seconded the motion. Chairman Quinn called for public comment. There being no public
comment, the motion passed unanimously.
FLOATING DRY DOCK
Commissioner McDaniel requested staff to provide an overview to the public on the
floating dry dock project. E.D.C. Director Yeager discussed meeting with the Triumph
Board last week; reporting the County was awarded $13 million for the Floating Dry Dock
Project with a term sheet. He stated that Eastern Shipbuilding is coming to Gulf County
before the construction of the dry dock to outfit Staten Island ferries at the mill site.
