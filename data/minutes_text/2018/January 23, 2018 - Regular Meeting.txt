OFF LEASH DOG PARK – COURTHOUSE COMPLEX
Barbara Veldhuizen, of Port St. Joe appeared before the Board to present a petition
requesting an off-leash dog park on Gulf County property <copy provided to the Clerk for
the record>; recommending an off-leash dog park behind the Gulf County Library and
Senior Citizens building in Port St. Joe. Ms. Veldhuizen discussed the positives in locating
the dog park at this location; stating her concerns is that allowances will need to be made
for canine training and maintenance for the Sheriff’s Office. After discussion regarding
additional needs for the proposed park, Commissioner McCroan motioned to allow
additional time for her to speak. Commissioner Rogers seconded the motion. Ms.
Veldhuizen discussed appointing a 30-day or 60-day work group to work out the details
regarding this project; requesting Board approval to compile a work group to work out the
details and bring a recommendation back before the Board. After discussion by County
Attorney Novak, Chairman Quinn called for public comment. Marsha Cassie, of Port St.
Joe appeared before the Board to discuss the proposed dog park location. Sheriff
Harrison appeared before the Board to report the proposed site is being used as a canine
training field for the Gulf County Sheriff’s Office; stating that if the Board agrees to move
forward with the off-leash dog park, he will have to look for another canine training field.
After discussion by members of the Board, Chairman Quinn discussed handing this
matter over to staff for review and then have them bring a recommendation back before
the Board. Commissioner Rich asked Ms. Veldhuizen if she has spoken with the City
Commissioners on an area within the City limits for an off-leash dog park. Ms. Veldhuizen
stated that she had not because the proposed site is owned by the County. Commissioner
Rich then asked Commissioner Rogers if the City had any available property for this
project. After discussion by Commissioner Rogers, Commissioner McCroan stated that
he thinks there are other areas to consider; allowing staff to review this further. Chairman
Quinn directed staff to review this and bring back a recommendation. Upon inquiry by
someone (unknown) in the audience, Chairman Quinn stated that staff will review and
hopefully have a recommendation to the Board by the next regular meeting.
BEACH RESTORATION PROJECT
Chief Administrator Butler discussed the workshop held this morning, noting Michael
Dombrowski, of MRD Associates presented a recommendation to the Board during this
meeting. He recommended that the Board move forward with the process; allowing Mr.
Dombrowski to draft the bid documents, with new bid specifications, and to advertise for
sealed bids. Chief Administrator Butler also recommended the Board, following legal
review, purchase the piece of property (located near Salinas Park) as discussed in a
previous meeting; stating the purchase will up the ante for the State to keep more money
in this project. As the County’s Coastal Engineer, Michael Dombrowski, of MRD
Associates appeared before the Board to present his recommendation to the Board from
the Workshop: remove the north beach fill area, solely focusing on the southern fill limits
(between the Stumphole and Rish Park); proposing they reverse bid the project to give
contractors a maximum price and awarding the contract based on whoever comes in with
the most cubic yards, provided the volume is sufficient for the County’s purpose.

Commissioner McCroan so moved. Commissioner McDaniel seconded for discussion.
Upon inquiry by Commissioner Rogers, County Attorney Novak confirmed the legality of
the reverse bid process. Chairman Quinn called for public comment. Pat Hardman,
President of Coastal Community Association appeared before the Board in support of the
project; encouraging the Board to pass this today. She noted that (3) three million dollars
could possibly come forward through a legislative budget appropriation; asking they not
block that out of the project cost should it come to fruition. Commissioner McCroan added
to his motion, if the three (3) million dollars was to come in, then the County will leave that
option. Assistant Administrator Hammond asked the County to leave the number out and
allow staff to come up with a number; stating they are going to back it out if the Board so
chooses to purchase the lot for that amount.. He noted if the County gets more money,
then they can add more volume. Commissioner McCroan stated the lot is also in his
motion. Commissioner McDaniel stated his second still stands. Chairman Quinn called
for additional public comment. Janet Gransinger, of Cape San Blas appeared before the
Board to inquire about the property North of Rish Park that has paid (taxes through the
M.S.T.U.) into this project. Mr. Dombrowski addressed the Board, noting that part of the
recommendation was to repair ‘hot spots’ on the southern end through truck haul; stating
the County would not totally delete the northern section, it would be more of going out
and remediating ‘hot spots’ as they are needed. Commissioner McCroan reported that
the northern portion of the beach is increasing in several areas. Mr. Dombrowski agreed
with Commissioner McCroan’s statement; explaining the northern portion is accreting with
the sand coming from the southern end. Chairman Quinn called for public comment.
There was no further public comment. Assistant Administrator Hammond asked for
clarification of the motion; stating Chief Administrator Butler’s recommendation was to do
two (2) things: 1. Reverse bid the project, as recommended, and 2. purchase the lot,
contingent upon the County Attorney making sure the County can get (beach) access;
adding the County will only purchase this lot if it provides access to raise the State’s match
portion. Upon inquiry by Chairman Quinn, Commissioner McCroan agreed to add this to
his motion. The motion then passed unanimously.

RESTORE – MULTI-YEAR IMPLEMENTATION PLAN
Gulf County E.D.C. Director/RESTORE Act Coordinator Yeager, Jr. discussed the
proposed five (5) year plan to submit to U.S. Treasury; reporting that over the next five
(5) year’s, the County will accumulate another five (5) million dollars in the Pot. He stated
that the Board has committed $2.8 million of the first amount of money to the Beach
Restoration Project; reporting that the grant has been submitted for this project. Gulf
County E.D.C. Director/RESTORE Act Coordinator Yeager, Jr. discussed the welding
program (for Wewahitchka High School); reporting the County is still working with the
School system and Grant Consultant on this project. He discussed other projects within
the Five (5) Year Plan: (1) Sewer Rehab Project for District II; (2) Erosion Control; and
(3) Acquisitions; calling it Public Safety. He reported that this acquisitions project will
consolidate the Sheriff’s Office, 9-1-1, and Dispatch. Gulf County E.D.C.
Director/RESTORE Act Coordinator Yeager, Jr. reported that staff found a building
downtown and believe the County can use Pot I funds to purchase the building. He then
presented a request to purchase the property and office building located at 418 Cecil
Costin Boulevard, Port St. Joe <copy provided to the Clerk for the record>. Gulf County
E.D.C. Director/RESTORE Act Coordinator Yeager, Jr. reported that staff worked out the
details to do owner financing, being that there is not enough funds currently in Pot I to
purchase in full. County Attorney Novak reported that the proposed terms of the purchase
agreement are: (1) a 45-day feasibility period for the County; (2) $1.1 million purchase
terms; (3) escrow deposit of $10,000.00 down; (4) owner financing for three (3) years at
3.75%; and, (5) proposed closing date of June 1st with the first payment of $550,000.00

due at that time. Gulf County E.D.C. Director/RESTORE Act Coordinator Yeager, Jr.
reported that the BP Funds will be used for acquisition but there may be some funds that
the County will have to come up with for wiring and a few other things that will need to be
done. He stated that staff will bring back a report of a cost estimate. Gulf County E.D.C.
Director/RESTORE Act Coordinator Yeager, Jr. requested a motion to move forward on
this project. County Attorney Novak requested Board approval to ratify the Contract, move
forward with the feasibility period, authorize Chief Administrator Butler to sign, and direct
Clerk Norris to issue the earnest money deposit (escrow) to be held against the property
while the County moves towards closing. Commissioner Rogers motioned to move
forward with this purchase. Commissioner McCroan seconded the motion. After
discussion by Chairman McDaniel, Chairman Quinn called for public comment. There
being no public comment, the motion passed unanimously.

called for public comment. There being no public comment, the motion passed
unanimously.
