PORT ST. JOE, FLORIDA

The Gulf County Board of County Commissioners met this date in special budget session
with the following members present: Chairman Sandy Quinn, Jr., Vice Chairman Jimmy
Rogers, and Commissioners David C. Rich, Ward McDaniel, and Phil McCroan.
Others present were: County Attorney Jeremy Novak, Clerk Rebecca L. Norris, Clerk
Budget & Finance Officer Sherry Herring, Deputy Clerk Leanna Roberts, County
Administrator Michael L. Hammond, Deputy Administrator Kari Summers, Building Official
Brad Bailey, Central Services Director Lynn Lanier, Gulf County E.D.C. Director Warren
Yeager, Jr., Information Technology Dennis Barfield, Sr., Assistant Mosquito Control

Chairman Quinn called the meeting to order at 5:01 p.m., E.T.
Chairman Quinn led the Pledge of Allegiance to the Flag
2018-19 BUDGET OVERVIEW
Clerk Norris advised the Board of the following: 1) the aggregated tentative millage rate
is 7.4947, which is 3.66% above the current year aggregate rolled back rate of 7.2303; 2)
the tentative aggregated millage rate includes the following operating millage rates:
Countywide is 7.1000 and Dependent Fire Districts are 0.5000 each; and, 3) the tentative
MSTU voted debt service millage rates are: a) 1.1395 for Gulfside Beachfront, b) 0.9838
for Gulfside Interior and, c) Bayside is 0.9894. Clerk Norris noted some of the reasons
for the Ad Valorem increase of 3.66% over the rolled back rate include: a) Sheriff’s Office,
b) Building and Planning Departments, c) Office of Management & Budget and other
Departmental Transitions, d) cost of living pay increase, e) loss from prior year value
adjustment, f) Property Appraiser’s Office, g) property and casualty insurance, and h)
retirement rates. Clerk Norris informed the Board that the adoption of the Final Millage
Rates and Budgets will require a majority vote of the Board with these being the maximum
rates, noting the Board may adopt a lesser rate with a majority vote. Clerk Budget &
Finance Officer Herring reported the following: 1) the Port St. Joe DRA obligation is
$183,078.00 based on the tentative millage rate of 7.1000; 2) the General Fund Budget
does include Case Carried Forward of $7,089,079.00 as a source of funds for FY2018-
19, and $2,509,023.00 is Cash Carried Forward that is restricted for specific purposes;
and 3) the tentative budget has been posted on the County’s website, and advertised in
the newspaper as required for this Public Hearing. She requested Board approval to place
the following Non-Ad Valorem adjustments in the Final Budget: 1) line item adjustments
in Fund 001 * General Fund, 104 * Public Works, 112 * S.H.I.P., 114 * General Grants,

and 126 * E9-1-1, and to allocate the lump sum appropriations for approved pay rates
and to allocate those to the appreciate lines; 2) adjustments to expenditures, transfers,
reserves, and other lines to account for various cost allocations, grant funding, and
rounding adjustments; 3) increase Fund 002 * Fine & Forfeiture Fund by $12,680.00
affecting Cash Carried Forward and various restricted revenues and expenditures; 4)
increase Fund 115 * C.D.B.G. Fund by $10,000.00 affecting Cash Carried Forward
revenues and reserves for project funds; 5) increase Fund 117 * Industrial Park Fund by
$25,080.00 affecting rent revenues, reserves and expenditures; 6) decrease Fund 126 *
E9-1-1 Fund by $40,000.00 due to the reduction of Cash Carried Forward estimates; 7)
increase Fund 127 * Disaster Fund by $10,000.00 affecting Cash Carried Forward
revenues and disaster overtime expenditures; and, 8) increase Fund 128 * T.D.C. by
$263,200.00 affecting Cash Carried Forward revenues, reserves, and expenditures.
Commissioner Rogers so moved. Commissioner Rich seconded the motion. Chairman
Quinn called for public comment. There being no public comment, the motion passed
unanimously. After discussion by County Administrator Hammond and Chairman Quinn,
Chairman Quinn called for public comment. There being no public comment, the Hearing
was closed to the public. Clerk Norris reported that the Countywide General Fund is
7.1000, Aggregate Millage Rate is 7.4947, and this Aggregate Rolled-back Rate of 7.2303
and is an increase of 3.66%.
